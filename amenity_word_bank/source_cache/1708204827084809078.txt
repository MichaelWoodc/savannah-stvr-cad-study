=== FULL DESCRIPTION ===
Stay in a beautifully preserved 1892 Historic Landmark just three blocks from Forsyth Park. This spacious second-floor residence features two king bedrooms, a queen sleeper sofa, private parking, high-speed Wi-Fi, and timeless Savannah character throughout. Enjoy morning coffee from the upper porches overlooking Hall Street’s tree-lined canopy, then explore nearby restaurants, coffee shops, boutiques, and historic landmarks—all within one of Savannah’s most walkable and desirable neighborhoods.

The space
The Hall House | Balcony Residence

Welcome to The Hall House | Balcony Residence, a beautifully preserved second-floor residence in an 1892 Historic Landmark just three blocks from Forsyth Park.

Perched above historic Hall Street, this spacious 1,534-square-foot residence offers elevated views through Savannah's tree canopy, two private balconies, soaring ceilings, original heart pine floors, decorative fireplaces, elegant chandeliers, and timeless architectural character throughout.

Whether you're enjoying coffee on the front balcony, relaxing on the private bedroom balcony, exploring Savannah's historic squares, or gathering with family and friends after a day in the city, The Hall House offers an authentic Savannah experience in one of the city's most desirable historic neighborhoods.

The Space:

Originally constructed in 1892, The Hall House showcases the craftsmanship and architectural details that make Savannah's historic homes so beloved.

Spanning approximately 1,534 square feet, the Balcony Residence features oversized rooms, abundant natural light, original heart pine floors, decorative fireplaces, elegant furnishings, and gracious living spaces designed for gathering and relaxation.

The spacious living room welcomes guests with comfortable seating, a Smart TV, decorative fireplace, and beautiful historic details throughout. Adjacent to the living room, the formal dining room provides an inviting setting for shared meals, conversation, and memorable evenings together.

The fully equipped kitchen includes everything needed to prepare meals during your stay, whether you're enjoying breakfast before a day of sightseeing or dinner with family and friends.

Just beyond the kitchen, step onto the private front balcony overlooking picturesque Hall Street. Furnished with outdoor dining and lounge seating, it's the perfect place to enjoy Savannah's beautiful weather while watching the neighborhood come to life beneath the shade of mature crepe myrtles and magnolias.

Outdoor Living:

One of the most unique features of the Balcony Residence is its collection of outdoor spaces.

Enjoy meals and drinks on the private front balcony overlooking historic Hall Street, relax on the private rear balcony accessed from the bedrooms, or gather in the shared courtyard below.

The beautifully landscaped courtyard features outdoor dining areas, mature plantings, and a gas grill, offering a quintessential Savannah outdoor living experience.

• No pets, parties, events, or smoking are permitted on the property.
