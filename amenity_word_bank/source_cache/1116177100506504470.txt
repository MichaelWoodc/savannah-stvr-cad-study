=== FULL DESCRIPTION ===
Welcome to Whitaker Wonder, an immaculate one-bedroom, one-bathroom, second-floor apartment in the heart of Savannah, just minutes from the City Market. With custom finishes throughout, original hardwood floors, and exposed brick, the apartment blends modern design with classic Savannah charm that makes for a beautiful stay. This apartment is perfect for up to four guests, featuring one queen bed and a Murphy bed. Enjoy easy access to dining, pubs, attractions, and shopping.
SVR-02911

The space
Step into this freshly updated urban haven, where high ceilings, oversized windows, and exposed brick welcome you to a chic loft atmosphere. This second-floor unit boasts a spacious open concept, seamlessly blending kitchen, dining, and living areas.
Cooking is a breeze in the large custom kitchen, complete with a full appliance package, including a wine cooler. A high-top peninsula island offers seating for three, perfect for casual dining or socializing.
Relax in the spacious living room, featuring a comfortable sofa, reading chair, and large smart TV for entertainment. A built-in Murphy bed provides additional sleeping options, while large windows bathe the space in natural light.
A dedicated workspace and built-in shelves offer functionality for remote work, supported by our reliable Wi-Fi.
The primary suite awaits to the right of the living room. Unwind in the luxurious queen mattress adorned with crisp linens and ample custom-built storage. A washer and dryer are located behind the frosted glass closet doors just outside the primary suite.
The single bathroom boasts an oversized single vanity with double faucets, a storage cabinet, and a custom-built low-entry shower. Essentials such as towels, toiletries, and a hair dryer are provided for your convenience.
Experience urban luxury and convenience at its finest in this meticulously appointed Savannah retreat.

Guest access
The entire condo is setup for your use! You have a murphy bed in the living room that pulls down to be a full bed (please pull slowly/carefully).

Other things to note
No parties. This is a condo building where many of the residents live there full-time. Please be respectful of noise levels particularly after 10pm. 
Only the number of guests listed on the reservation is permitted at the property.
No illicit activities, peddling, or soliciting are allowed on the property.
Do not smoke on the property.  
Report all damaged property to the property owner immediately.
Treat the studio with respect as if it were your property.

Neighborhood highlights
Just minutes from the City Market and steps from Telfair Square, you will be located among everything you come to Savannah for.
Explore all of the flavors Savannah has to offer, all just steps from the apartment. Enjoy French-inspired dishes at Circa 1875, grab a traditional southern dish at The Lady & Sons, or crack a few oysters at Sorry Charlie’s Oyster Bar. Breakfast is easy and delicious. Check out Treylor Park Diner or the Little Duck Diner.
Enjoy Savannah's incredible nightlife with a stop in the Peacock Lounge, Totally Awesome Bar, Jens’s and Friends, Moon River Brewing, and many more. Transport to London by stopping into Six Pence Pub, a British-inspired pub, or bring out your inner Irish with a stop into O’Connell’s Irish Pub.
A trip to Savannah is complete with exploring a little of its historic colonial past. Fill your schedule by checking out the Telfair Academy, a beautiful art museum, or stroll through the Webb Military Museum. Relive the prohibition area by heading to the American Prohibition Museum. Don’t forget to grab a drink at their cocktail bar to celebrate freedom. Or fancy exploring Savannah’s ocean ties? Check out the Ships of the Sea Maritime Museum.
Not enough time to walk all the sights of Savannah? Take it all in at once while riding or biking. We are just minutes from Old Town Trolley Tours of Savannah and Savannah Bike Tours. Check out the city’s skyline from the water by jumping on one of the Savannah Belle’s ferries, with multiple stops all along Savannah’s riverfront.
