=== FULL DESCRIPTION ===
Step into this charming 2BR/2BA apartment in the heart of Savannah, facing the iconic Forsyth Park fountain! Fully renovated in 2026 with guests in mind, it features exposed brick accents and professional decor.  This space offers a sublime blend of modern comfort and historic charm. Ideal for two couples, it has spacious bedrooms with en suite full baths, stylish touches, and easy walking to Savannah’s best attractions. Enjoy a relaxing stay in this beautifully appointed home!

SVR 02813

The space
Walk in and feel immediately at home in this stylishly designed apartment.  You are welcomed by the open kitchen and living space with room to relax, cook, or eat-in some of the fabulous Savannah food.  We renovated the property in 2026 and included two full en-suite bathrooms and king bedrooms with brand new mattresses to fall into after a long day exploring.    Other custom elements abound throughout the space, some added by our builder and a few by us :). Step outside and right into the heart of Savannah's Crown Jewel - Forsyth Park!  As comfortable as we think the apartment is - make some time to explore the beauty of the city too!  The listing comes with one off-street driveway parking space.  There is a 240V outlet to help with recharging EVs too!

Guest access
The entire 2 bedroom apartment is yours!  You have private access on ground level.  The space includes a reserved off-street driveway parking space behind the building.  A quick walk around the house gets you to your front door.

If you want to eat in, grab your groceries at Kroger, just a blocks away across Forsyth.
