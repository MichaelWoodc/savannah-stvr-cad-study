=== FULL DESCRIPTION ===
The Lafayette Trio offers the rare opportunity to rent an entire historic townhouse building in the heart of Savannah’s Landmark District. Just one block from the iconic Cathedral Basilica of St. John the Baptist, this elegant property includes three full townhomes, each with 3 bedrooms, 2 bathrooms, a full kitchen, and dining space. Perfect for large groups, the home sleeps up to 24 guests and places you steps from Lafayette Square, Forsyth Park, and Savannah’s most beloved sights.

The space
Step inside The Lafayette Trio, a rare full-building rental featuring three private townhomes stacked across three floors. Each unit offers its own kitchen, living area, 3 bedrooms, and 2 bathrooms, creating the perfect balance of shared space and personal privacy. With room for up to 24 guests, this beautifully designed historic property offers stylish comfort, flexibility, and unmatched access to Savannah’s most iconic sites—all just a block from Lafayette Square and the Cathedral.

Guest access
Guests have a private access and will receive a key code prior to arrival to enter the home. This keycode is changed after every guest for your safety.

Other things to note
★ Key Features:

• Prime location near Lafayette Square

• 9 bedrooms (3 king, 6 queens, 3 sleeper sofas)

• 6 full bathrooms

• Sunlit bay windows & grand fireplaces

• 3 Full kitchen w/ stainless steel appliances

• Ceiling fans in bedrooms & living room

• 3 In-unit washer & dryer

• High-speed WiFi & smart TVs

• Elegant historic details throughout

• Walk to Forsyth Park, shops & dining
