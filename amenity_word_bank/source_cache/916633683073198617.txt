=== FULL DESCRIPTION ===
Stay in a private apartment on the top floor of a Victorian house in Savannah's Victorian District, a 6-8 minute walk from Forsyth Park. It has a large living room, equipped kitchen, renovated bathroom, Roku TV, Wi-Fi, washer, dryer and original heart pine floors. Ideal for couples.

STVR: SVR-03081

City of Savannah STVR Certificate #: SVR-03081

The space
This one-bedroom apartment features a comfortable queen size bed and a closet in the bedroom. The sofa offers extra sleeping space, and a queen size inflatable mattress is stored in the chaise section of the sofa. You will also find extra bedding in the bedroom closet for your convenience.

Guest access
Guests have private access to the entire upstairs apartment, which includes the bedroom, bathroom, living room, fully equipped kitchen, and washer/dryer. The backyard is shared, although it is rare for multiple guests to be there at the same time. The host may occasionally access the courtyard to perform maintenance tasks or move supplies.

Other things to note
This is a private apartment on the top floor of a historic Victorian house. To access the apartment, guests must go up a flight of stairs once they are inside. The back yard is shared, but it is usually quiet and rarely used by multiple guests at the same time. There is free street parking in front of the property.

The streets are cleaned on Monday and Thursday nights. Please pay close attention to the parking signs on the streets.
