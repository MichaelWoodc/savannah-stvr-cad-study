=== FULL DESCRIPTION ===
Experience the best of Savannah’s southern charm in this cozy, quaint cottage. Conveniently tucked behind Charlton St., this old-world home provides its guests with a quiet and relaxing stay. Built in 1850, its picturesque design captures your attention from the moment you step through the front door. An elegant, spiral staircase sits peacefully in the middle of the main floor, connecting all three levels. You don't want to miss out on one of the cutest cottages in all of Savannah!

SVR-02415

The space
Despite its old-fashioned appeal, the cottage hosts a modern kitchen, complete with stainless steel appliances and a sturdy, granite countertop. You’ll find everything you need to enjoy a home cooked meal, including a full set of kitchen knives, coffee maker, toaster and blender. Step out onto the courtyard for a relaxing evening under the stars after dinner. The patio area is surrounded by plant life and plentiful citrus trees. It also has a spacious patio table that seats 4, allowing you to enjoy the green oasis with loved ones. You’re guaranteed a good night’s rest in the main bedroom, decorated with a stonewall fireplace (non-working) and plenty of antiques. Step out onto the connected balcony in the morning for a cup of coffee or cocktail at night!

Guest access
Everything is yours! Aside from the rear patio on the ground floor, you can access the balcony off of the "master bedroom." We have a large smart TV downstairs and another in the first bedroom and GREAT WiFi! Again, all the rooms are connected via the spiral staircase, so please be careful going up and down especially on the top floor (watch your head!). There is no "real privacy" between the rooms because of this, so make sure you're bringing people you actually like! :)

Other things to note
This space is essentially a 2 bedroom, but the spiral staircase cuts through all floors, so there's no doors  It's great for a family or 2 couples that are open to a sharing space together. The bathroom is in the main bedroom on the second floor.

Neighborhood highlights
Located in the South Historic District, this warm, restful cottage allows its guests to enjoy greenery without sacrificing accessibility to downtown. Just north of Savannah’s expansive, 30-acre Forsyth Park, there’s plenty of green area for you to enjoy! Lafayette and Troup Square are also nearby, providing you with even more greenery to explore. With a walkability score of 93, this pleasant neighborhood is more than easy to get around. Enjoy Savannah’s serenity and warmth in this peaceful property.
Not even one block from the famous Clary's Cafe (in Midnight in The Garden of Good and Evil!), a few blocks from Fox & Fig (best all-vegan restaurant in town!), and so many more amazing local spots!
