=== FULL DESCRIPTION ===
Sun filled Parlor in an elegant mansion dating back to 1850. A true gem on Jones Street, referred to as "one of the most beautiful streets in the U.S". Soaring high ceilings, marble fireplaces, floor to ceiling windows looking out to a historic cobblestone street. Walking distance to all downtown has to offer, quiet and peaceful. Very lar tv with premium cable. New king bed. Laundry room with washer and dryer. Perfect to "work from home" with comfortable desk, high speed wifi. No pets. SVR-02203

The space
Sunshine flows throughout all the spaces, it’s as elegant as it gets.  The eat-in kitchen is fully equipped. RH leather sofa, large flat screen smart tv with cable in the living room. Bedroom has a very comfortable KING bed (new Aug 2022) a tv. Ensuite bathroom has a doorway to the bedroom and to the living room. Laundry room with a washer, dryer and utility sink. Spacious front parlor (2nd living room) with a desk overlooking Jones Street. Ample formal dining room.  Access to a back porch, a backyard garden, and a beautiful front courtyard with views onto Jones Street. Located in the Design District where all the most stylish home stores and chic boutiques are. Absolute best location you can get downtown. A GREAT “WORK FROM HOME” place, reliable fast wifi internet, front parlor has pockets doors that can be closed for work privacy, comfortable desk.

Guest access
You have access to two courtyards, a back porch, and a laundry room. 
Inside the apartment you can access all areas that are in the photos. Front balcony not to be accessed or used. To access the front courtyard garden guests must go out the front door, down the stairs in through the courtyard gates off the front of the property.

Other things to note
*****PARKING INFO********
Parking is on street. For the area around the apartment: Meters in effect Mon-Fri 8am to 5pm. Free after 5pm, free all weekend, free overnight any night. Parking on the same block as the apartment should be easy, you should have no problem finding a spot right outside the door on W Jones St. If you are coming for the weekend don't bother reading ahead: Parking is FREE from Friday at 5pm to Monday at 8am and no street cleaning on weekends and all good to park overnight. 

If you are parking during the week:
1) Park in any spot that has a number and pay the meter Mon-Fri 8am to 5pm. Pay at the muni meter with a credit card or cash or use the Parking Savannah Phone app and pay from your  mobile phone. Parking overnight is free. TIP: if you want to park for free on street, E Jones St just 3 blocks away has not meters, just be mindful of street sweeper sides/signs. 

2) If you don't want to deal with paying meters just go to the visitor's center  **Currently closed due to COVID**  about 5 mins away on MLK boulevard) and get a visitor's parking pass for the duration of your stay. Put it on your dashboard and park anywhere (pay attention to street sweeping signs). They cost $15 a day. **During COVID: you have to get the pass at the Parking and Mobility office on Bryan St. They want you to call ahead if you want to purchase them. Airbnb won't let me disclose the website or their phone number here but just search "Parking and Mobility Savannah" and go to the "Visitor's" tab. It's all there. 

3) Garage option: forget about meters and street sweepers: There is a very inexpensive parking garage 5 minute walking distance from the carriage house. Called the Liberty Street parking garage. It costs about $17 for 24 hours. It is covered and safe. 

4) Last option is park your car in streets nearby that have no meters: Ask me for the link to a map I created  that shows the nearest streets without meters, can't put link here airbnb does not allow links. These streets are E Jones St.  (east of Abercorn), and any street South of Gaston Street. Both about 5 minute stroll from the apartment. Just be mindful of the street sweeper nights.
