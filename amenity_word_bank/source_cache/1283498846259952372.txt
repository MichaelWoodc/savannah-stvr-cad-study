=== FULL DESCRIPTION ===
The historic Hamilton Estate was built 1868 and has been preserved in its beautiful antiquity, but with all the modern amenities.  There are 5 Bedrooms with memory foam mattresses, 3.5 bathrooms, ample living space, a large courtyard with plunge pool and outdoor dining space, garage parking for 2 cars, and all bathrooms have Public Goods products and soft Turkish towels.

The space
The home features 4 main bedrooms with king beds and a bonus bedroom with two twin beds. The bonus bedroom is through the master bedroom for a total of 6 beds.

This home is part of an estate which includes garden suites below and carriage houses behind. All potential guests must be aware of this and be willing to respect other guests on the property. Adhering to the house rules includes taking off shoes, respecting the city noise ordinance, etc.

The home is styled in era appropriate furniture and antiques, and much of the original character has been preserved while modernizing everything up to today's comforts and efficiency.

The kitchen is stocked with a complete set of Green Pan's highest grade cookware, full bar set, hand mixer, blender, American Standard bakeware, and all the odds and ends. 

Perfect place to have a family vacation. Baby monitor, pack and play, kid dishes, books and some toys ready to go. All TV's are smart TV's

There is off street parking in the garage for 2 cars.

STVR Certificate: SVR-03242

Guest access
The courtyard and cold plunge are shared for the whole property. There is also an outdoor shower and courtyard bathroom. You have 2 private balconies on the main and second floor of the house.

The garage parking is exclusive to the main house.
