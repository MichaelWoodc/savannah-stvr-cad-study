=== FULL DESCRIPTION ===
Welcome to “The Janie”,  our Victorian townhome in beautiful Downtown Savannah's Victorian District. Sophisticated, stylish, & comfortable décor compliment this historic retreat while you enjoy Savannah.

Located 2.5 blocks south of enchanting Forsyth Park, the largest park in all of Historic Savannah. Two designated off-street parking spaces included with ample on-street, non-metered, parking available.   

SVR-00089 | City of Savannah Short-Term Vacation Rental Certificate license holder.

The space
The entire home is yours to enjoy. Built in 1891, the home remains remarkably unchanged from years gone by with beautiful architectural details mixed with modern and antique furnishings making for a sophisticated, stylish, & comfortable décor for you to enjoy. 

Downstairs: Enjoy the spacious floor plan with a modern, fully equipped  kitchen that is open to the breakfast room with a table that seats 6; expansive double parlor with living room, flat screen TV and DVD player and formal dining room that seats an additional 6. One of the two vintage-styled bathrooms with clawfoot tub is located downstairs.

The home has a fenced courtyard with patio seating, grill, and wicker patio table and chaise lounge for our guest to enjoy. 
 
Upstairs: Enjoy the comfort of 4 bedrooms, 1 full bathroom, and a separate dressing/laundry room all upstairs. Sleeping arrangements consist of these 4 bedrooms on the 2nd floor (a King, a Queen, a large bedroom with 2 Full Size Beds, and the 'trunk room' with a Full bed). There is a Twin Rollaway bed in the closet downstairs which can be placed in any room. There is one full bathroom upstairs (with original clawfoot tub!) by the dressing/laundry room and one full (near identical) bathroom downstairs by the kitchen. 

Fully stocked kitchen with stainless appliances, electric stove/oven, microwave, refrigerator with ice/filtered water dispenser, dishwasher. Coffee maker with coffee stocked, blender, toaster. Ample cooking & kitchen wares, dishes and utensils.

Full size washer/dryer upstairs in separate sitting/dressing room with iron and ironing board provided.

Two designated off-street parking spaces provided with our rental home located just behind the fenced courtyard on the alley-way. 

Bedrooms: 
#1: King bed with seating area, large dresser, TV
#2: Two Full beds with dresser, TV, 2 slipper chairs
#3: Queen bed with dresser, TV, reading chair
#4: Full bed with dresser
Extra: 1 Twin Rollaway bed stored in home for guest use.

Our home is equipped with a combination of cable and/or Roku and free wifi.

Guest access
Why choose our circa 1891 Victorian home? Authenticity! We dearly love the beautiful city of Savannah that we call home and particularly love old houses and all things old...we love the stories and memories that antique architecture and vintage furnishings can tell (I am a 'talker', so if you ask me I will share them all!) The home is affectionately nicknamed "The Janie" for Miss Jane Strous who grew up and lived her adult life in the adjacent home. Her mother had the row of townhomes built as rental properties. Today our family continues this legacy and hopes to share Miss Janie's family's home with you as well.

Other things to note
Things to consider prior to booking:
Can guest in your party comfortable climb stairs or step into a claw-foot tub? While we do have a full bath on the first level of the home, all bedrooms are located upstairs as is the 2nd full bath. 

Can all guest be respectful of the home and neighbors? We welcome courteous and respectful guest and will not tolerate a 'party-house' (i.e. loud or drunken behavior). We are more than happy to answer questions, so please inquire. 

The home is 1.2 miles straight out the door down to the cobblestones of River Street, the most northern boundary of the Landmark Historic District. The home is situated near the corner of a main arterial street in Savannah, so traffic noise and the occasional siren is to be expected as downtown is an active urban center.

Neighborhood highlights
The Victorian District borders the Landmark Historic District. Enjoy the leisurely 5 minute walk to Savannah’s iconic Forsyth Park or spend the day strolling the 1.2 miles from our home under Spanish moss draped trees to River Street, the most northern border of historic downtown, exploring all our beautiful Savannah Squares, quaint shops, and eateries along the way!


Kroger, downtown's only main grocery store is only 5 blocks away with Brighter Day, a local health and organic market within 3 blocks of the house as is the weekly Saturday morning Forsyth Park Farmer's Market.


Locally owned coffee shops, bbq and slider joints, a Cuban inspired and separate All-American diner, a Mediterranean eatery, fine dining, craft-brew store, weekend Forsyth Farmer's Market, and more all within a few block stroll of our home as well.
