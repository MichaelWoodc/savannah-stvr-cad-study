=== FULL DESCRIPTION ===
This beautiful third floor townhome has everything you'll need to feel like a true Savannah local. This home features a remodeled kitchen, historic architectural details, and front door access to our city's most beautiful park.

The space
This beautiful third floor townhome includes access to a private rooftop deck, creating an ideal romantic spot to watch the sunset. You’ll feel like a true Savannahian with “front door” access to Forsyth Park, Savannah’s largest park and popular location for weddings, music and food festivals. 

Enjoy a little “me time” curled up on the couch with a good book, or catching up on your favorite shows. The living room is complete with a Queen size pull-out sofa bed, a 37" flat screen tv and DVD player - plus a bonus library of DVDs for your enjoyment. The bedroom has an additional seating area to enjoy reading a book or just watching the 50” television. When it’s time to get some shut-eye, slip between the sheets of the luxurious King sized bed. Come morning, you’ll be ready for a whole new day of exploring Historic Savannah.

No matter how you start your day - perhaps with a walk to nearby Sentient Bean for coffee and a scone, or some antique shopping at Alex Raskin Antiques on Monterey Square - you’ll want to end a night (or two) on the roof, enjoying a glass of wine and your private view. 

Why Lucky Savannah Loves this Property:

1) There is plenty of non-metered parking surrounding the home, so there's no need to park in a garage or pay parking meters! 
2) This home features a kitchenette so you won't miss out on the opportunity to pop some popcorn or reheat some leftovers from one of Savannah's great restaurants. 
3) The iconic Forsyth Park is just outside your front door. Enjoy the famous Fountain, the many events, and the shops and eateries surrounding the park

What is parking like for this home?
Savannah’s historic buildings, squares, and streets have been beautifully preserved through the dedicated efforts of the Historic Savannah Foundation. Founded before the invention of the automobile, parking can be limited. 

Non-metered Parking
This unit has free, on-street parking right outside the front door. Availability will depend on time of day and season. Our guests have not reported trouble parking near the home, even on the busiest of weekends.
