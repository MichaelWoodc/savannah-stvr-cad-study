=== FULL DESCRIPTION ===
The Madison Suite, one of the Jones Street Suites, is a stylish and elegant place to relax and enjoy your stay in Savannah. The light-filled rooms and floor to ceiling windows will delight you, not to mention the private balcony where you can enjoy your morning beverage and relax after a day of sightseeing!

Situated on the prettiest street in Savannah, you can walk to Forsyth Park, River Street, and all the attractions our beautiful city has to offer.
