=== FULL DESCRIPTION ===
Experience the charm of The Bolton Heritage Collection—a seamless blend of two beautifully renovated units into one unforgettable home that sleeps 12. Enjoy modern comforts and historic charm, a private front porch and backyard perfect for relaxation and socializing. Ideal for families or groups, this unified retreat invites you to savor every moment in a timeless setting. Located steps from Forsyth Park in Savannah’s iconic Victorian district, this home offers an unforgettable getaway.

The space
Nestled in the heart of the Savannahs' Victorian District, this Victorian duplex offers a delightful blend of historic charm and modern amenities. Built in 1912 and meticulously renovated in 2024, The interior of The Hearth at Bolton Heritage exudes a perfect balance of modern comfort and historic charm. The unit boasts three distinct bedrooms, each with its own unique theme—ranging from nautical elegance in the first bedroom to country and southwestern elements in the second, and Victorian-inspired furnishings in the third. Elegant hardwood floors and stylish furnishings throughout create a warm and inviting atmosphere, while four smart TVs ensure entertainment at every turn. The fully equipped kitchen offers all the conveniences needed to prepare meals, while the built-in bar area adds a touch of sophistication. Cozy, thoughtful design elements provide a welcoming ambiance, making each corner of the space feel like a true home away from home.



The duplex's outdoor spaces are equally charming. The front porch, with its rocking chairs, invites you to relax and enjoy a glass of wine as you watch the world go by. At the back, the rear shared patio features a propane grill and a dining table for six, perfect for alfresco meals. Beyond the deck, a small fenced-in area provides a safe and convenient space to hang out. Located less than three blocks from Forsyth Park, this duplex places you within easy reach of Savannah's most iconic attractions, making it an ideal home base for exploring the city's rich history and vibrant culture.

Guest access
Guests have a private access and will receive a key code prior to arrival to enter the home. This keycode is changed after every guest for your safety.

Other things to note
The duplex's prime location is a significant draw for guests. Being less than three blocks from Forsyth Park, visitors have easy access to one of Savannah's most iconic and picturesque spots. The surrounding Victorian District is a treasure trove of cultural and historical attractions, charming boutiques, art galleries, and over 100 restaurants. After a day of exploring the city's vibrant scene, guests can unwind on the inviting front porch, enjoy alfresco dining on the rear deck, or let their pets play in the fenced-in area. With its ideal mix of historic charm, modern amenities, and convenient location, The Hearth at Bolton Heritage offers a truly unique and memorable Savannah experience.
