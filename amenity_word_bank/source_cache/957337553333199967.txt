=== FULL DESCRIPTION ===
Welcome to The Blue Belle on Broughton, a charming historic condo nestled on the second floor, overlooking the picturesque Broughton Street. You will be just steps away from City Market and a two block walk to River Street and the Plant Riverside District. You will be surrounded by iconic Savannah restaurants, bars, and shopping. Stunning Southern charm awaits you in this luxury TWO bedroom one bathroom Broughton Street Condo!

The space
Experience Savannah from one of the city's most sought-after locations! Nestled above iconic Broughton Street in the heart of the Historic District, The Blue Belle places you just steps from Savannah's best boutiques, award-winning restaurants, rooftop bars, coffee shops, and local favorites. City Market is just around the corner, while River Street and the vibrant Plant Riverside District are only a short two-block stroll away.

Inside, this beautifully restored historic condo blends timeless Savannah character with the comforts of modern living. Exposed brick walls, soaring ceilings, abundant natural light, and thoughtfully curated furnishings create a space that feels both elegant and inviting. It is the perfect place to unwind after a day of exploring.

The spacious, fully equipped kitchen offers everything you need to prepare breakfast before heading out or enjoy a relaxing evening at home. The open living area is warm and welcoming, with comfortable seating that's perfect for planning your next adventure or simply relaxing after a day on your feet.

The condo comfortably accommodates up to four guests with two thoughtfully appointed bedrooms designed for a restful night's sleep. The full bathroom features updated finishes and a convenient tub/shower combination, while the second bedroom includes a dedicated workspace with a comfortable desk and chair, making it easy for remote workers or business travelers to stay connected.

One of the best parts of staying here is simply stepping outside your front door. Spend your days wandering Savannah's historic squares, shopping along Broughton Street, enjoying rooftop cocktails, discovering local art galleries, or strolling along the waterfront at River Street—all without needing a car.

Whether you're planning a romantic getaway, a weekend with friends, a family vacation, or an extended stay, The Blue Belle offers the perfect combination of historic charm, modern comfort, and an unbeatable downtown location.

We love helping our guests experience the city like a local. From our curated guidebook filled with favorite restaurants, coffee shops, boutiques, and hidden gems to personalized recommendations throughout your stay, we're here to help make your Savannah getaway one you'll never forget.

We can't wait to welcome you to Savannah!

SVR-02837

Guest access
You'll have exclusive access to the entire private condo throughout your stay.

Upon arrival, you'll enter through the secure entrance of the condominium building and take the elevator to your floor. From there, it's just a short interior walk to your private front door.

Once inside, you'll have the entire condo to yourself, including the fully equipped kitchen, comfortable living and dining spaces, two private bedrooms, a full bathroom, and an in-unit washer and dryer. There are no shared living spaces, allowing you to relax and enjoy the condo as your own home away from home. We do have one locked closet for owner storage.

Other things to note
***Please note that there may be more city noises that you may not be accustomed to.  Fridays and Saturdays can get noisy at night due to our close proximity to Savannah's lively night life.  We do provide a sound machine to help drown out noises you may not be used to stying in a city.
