Step back in time at this historic Victorian gem in Savannah, GA

This beautifully renovated home in the historic East Victorian district is a true gem. The house has been meticulously restored to preserve its original Victorian architectural details, and it has been professionally decorated to create a warm and inviting atmosphere.
SVR-02959

The space
With six bedrooms and three bathrooms, this home can comfortably accommodate up to 10 guests. The house is perfect for families or groups of friends who are looking for a unique and memorable vacation experience.

The house also has a fenced outdoor area with a hot tub, which is the perfect place to relax and unwind after a long day of sightseeing.

Key features:
Six bedrooms, three bathrooms
Professionally decorated
Original Victorian architectural details
Fenced outdoor area with hot tub
Two blocks away from Forsyth Park
Walking distance to many historic sites, museums, and restaurants

Other things to note
There is free on-street parking on Lincoln and Duffy St. Guests are advised to keep an eye out for posted Sweep Zone signs. 
Please note that the hot tub is for 6 people maximum.
