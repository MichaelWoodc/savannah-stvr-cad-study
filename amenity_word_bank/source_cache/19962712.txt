Wonderful pool open year round for your families enjoyment! If you're planning a Georgia vacation with a visit to Savannah, then the last thing you want to do is stay in a boring hotel. Savannah vacation rentals we offer give you the chance to stay downtown in the heart of the historic district while enjoying a fully furnished house with all the amenities.

The space
One such property among our Savannah vacation rentals is the Seona House property on West Charlton Street and Tattnall Street. It's just down the street from Pulaski Square, which is one of Savannah's many historic squares and very popular with visitors. This is one of our larger vacation rentals Savannah GA with the potential to sleep up to 8 guests in three bedrooms with four and a half bathrooms. The top floor offers a twin bed and two queen beds. On the middle floor, you'll find a queen bed and a king bed, with access to a private balcony. The garden level includes an additional queen bed. For extra sleeping space, the living room features a queen-size sleeper sofa. A great option for two families vacationing together, this house also has two full-size living rooms and two gourmet kitchens. Like all our Savannah vacation homes for rent, it's well-stocked with amenities and necessities. You'll find free Wi-Fi, several TVs throughout the house, two private parking spaces and a private pool. This property has everything you need for a great vacation stay.



Walking into one of our Savannah vacation rentals will make you feel right at home on your vacation and this one is no exception. The light and bright living room will greet you with glossy wood floors and comfortable seating. The decorative fireplace adds ambiance near the windows. Immediately adjacent is the dining room, also with a decorative fireplace and plenty of lighting. Another living room on the top floor offers a cozy seating area and is right off the kitchen. The kitchens have plenty of counter space and all necessary appliances. One of the kitchens offers seating for four at the bar. Overlooking the pool area is a charming bar and sunroom area. Bedrooms are all well-appointed with ceiling fans, comfortable beds and charming decor. Bedrooms with two beds have some separation between the sleeping areas, giving guests some privacy. A laundry room with washer and dryer is included in the unit, making this one of our vacation rentals Savannah GA perfect for longer stays.



Starting your itinerary is easy with our Savannah vacation homes for rent as you can simply walk outside your door. With this property, you'll be in walking distance to Pulaski Square to the north and not far from Chatham Square going the other direction. The neighborhood around Pulaski Square offers a good example of quintessential Savannah-style architecture. Many houses are four levels and have gorgeous gardens. The Chatham Square neighborhood has a number of multi-level brick townhouses framed by cast-iron railings.



Architecture viewing is just one of the fun things to do in Savannah. A multitude of museums, tours, shopping and dining await visitors to this beautiful Southern city. Must-visit places include Forsyth Park, River Street, the Old Town Trolley Tour and the Ships of the Sea Museum. Walking is generally the best way to get around Savannah and allows you to take in all the architecture and mossy-oak lined streets. Another option is the Chatham Area Transit shuttle in the Historic District. Pick up a comprehensive map at the visitor's center and explore the Savannah History Museum while you're there. If you love tours, you'll find no shortage of them in Savannah. Consider a ghost tour, horse and buggy tour or a riverboat cruise among others.
