=== FULL DESCRIPTION ===
Step into timeless Savannah charm in this beautifully restored 1872 historic building in the heart of the Landmark Historic District. This elegant top-floor 1-bedroom retreat blends original character with curated interiors for a romantic, relaxing stay. Wake up to natural light, tall historic windows, and views of Oglethorpe Avenue lined with iconic live oak trees, an unmistakably Southern experience.
SVR-03260

The space
Designed to feel both refined and welcoming, this private top-floor unit combines historic beauty with modern comfort. Wake up to natural light streaming through tall historic windows and enjoy the steeple view of the famous Presbyterian church from the opening scene of Forrest Gump! a setting that feels unmistakably Southern.

King Bedroom:
The spacious bedroom is one of the highlights of the home:
Plush king bed with upscale linens
Original hardwood floors
Decorative historic fireplace mantel (non-working)
Cozy reading chair for slow Savannah mornings
Writing desk by the windows - ideal for remote work or planning your day
Classic artwork and warm textures that create a calm, romantic atmosphere
Natural light fills the room throughout the day, giving the space a bright and airy feel while still maintaining historic charm.

Bathroom:
Fresh, elegant, and thoughtfully renovated:
Glass walk-in shower with designer tile accents
Brass fixtures for a refined, boutique feel
Modern vanity with clean lines and classic finishes
Bright natural light from the shower window
It feels more like a boutique hotel bathroom than a typical rental.

Living Area + Kitchen:
The open living space is designed for relaxing after exploring Savannah:
Comfortable sofa for movie nights
Decorative historic fireplace (non-working)
Smart TV
Fully equipped kitchen with stainless appliances
Charming dining nook for coffee or evening wine
The combination of historic details and soft modern furnishings creates an elevated but comfortable atmosphere.

Guest access
The entire unit is set up for your use. Please be respectful of your neighbors and keep noises down at night, etc.

Other things to note
Top-floor unit (2 sets of stairs required)
Decorative fireplaces are non-working
Located in a lively historic downtown area
Historic building — expect character and charm
