=== FULL DESCRIPTION ===
Step into a spacious 1st-floor apartment in a restored 150-year-old Victorian townhouse, just one block from Forsyth Park. Blending historic charm with modern comfort, this 1-bed/1-bath retreat features high ceilings, hardwood floors, a plush queen bed, full kitchen, free street parking, and walkable access to Savannah’s cafés, squares, and sights.

The space
Welcome to your Savannah getaway! A charming 1st-floor apartment in a beautifully preserved 150-year-old Victorian townhouse, perfectly situated just one block from Forsyth Park. Inside, 1,050 sq/ft of thoughtfully curated space combines old-world character with modern convenience. Original hardwood floors, high ceilings, and tall windows bathe the rooms in light, while plush furnishings invite you to relax after exploring.

The spacious bedroom offers a comfortable queen-sized bed and an en-suite bathroom. In the living area, unwind on the oversized sectional or enjoy a movie night on the large smart TV. The fully equipped kitchen includes everything you need to prepare meals, plus a Keurig coffee maker for your morning cup. For extended stays, a washer and dryer are available.

Guests love the quiet, tree-lined street, free and easy street parking right outside, and the ease of walking to cafés, restaurants, and boutique shops. Forsyth Park’s Saturday farmers market, historic squares, and DOT trolley stops are all steps away. Whether your plans include strolling River Street, visiting Tybee Island, or savoring Savannah’s dining scene, you’ll find this home an ideal base.

From first-time visitors to seasoned Savannah travelers, guests often say this is their favorite stay for its location, comfort, and welcoming feel. Experience the perfect blend of history, charm, and modern comfort. Your Savannah story starts here!

NOTE: Two adult guests are permitted per reservation overnight. Any additional overnight guests will be charged $50.00 per night, per guest.
