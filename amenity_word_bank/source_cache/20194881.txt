=== FULL DESCRIPTION ===
This beautiful open concept condo is in the perfect for your next trip to Savannah. Lorch street is located in the historic district only two blocks from Forsyth Park and perfect walking distance to downtown Savannah. The condo is completely open with bookshelves separating bedrooms each with brand new king size pillow top beds. The open concept provides guests with a lot of space and a modern industrial feel. SVR- 00925.

The space
Savannah is such an artistic city and we have the added bonus of Savannah College of Art and Design! There is so much local talent and we are very excited about giving artists another platform to showcase and sell their work! These artists have worked hard on their pieces so please be respectful of their work. Everything is for sale so message me if you are interested in purchasing a piece. There is so much talent in this city so support local and buy some art to stare at in case your phone dies! Lol. Again, my number is ((PHONE NUMBER HIDDEN) if you have any questions. Have a great stay and enjoy the beautiful city and the local talent we have discovered!

Guest access
The guest has access to the entire home! This is separated into levels with a common stairwell. Once you get to the second floor the whole unit is yours.  Again, this is an open concept large studio so there are no individual bedrooms making it ideal for families and friends traveling together. There is a large bookcase separating the two king size beds. The only room with a separated door is the restroom. We hope you enjoy the home!
