=== FULL DESCRIPTION ===
Stroll among the beautiful homes and gardens of downtown Savannah and soak in its rich history. Historic Sage Cottage (c. 1900) offers a peaceful three-bedroom haven in the heart of the Landmark Historic District.

The space
If Sage Cottage happens to be booked for your dates, our neighboring Geranium Cottage offers the same layout and amenities and may still be available. You’ll find a link to that listing further down in this description.

Sage Cottage is perfectly situated in Savannah's Historic District,  just steps from River Street, City Market, Forsyth Park, dining, shopping, museums, and the trolley stop at Troup Square, only one block away. Metered and free street parking are available right in front of the cottage.

Inside, you will find dinnerware for eight, a 50" Smart Roku TV equipped with guest mode so you can log into your own streaming accounts, and fast Wi-Fi. There are three bedrooms: one king, one queen, and two twins, all featuring luxurious Egyptian cotton linens. The spacious bathroom includes fluffy towels, soap, shampoo, conditioner, lotion, and a hair dryer.

The fully stocked kitchen is beautifully appointed with stainless steel appliances, including a dishwasher, microwave, toaster, and coffee maker. We provide complimentary coffee and tea. The laundry room includes an extra-capacity washer and dryer for your convenience.

Enjoy your morning coffee, afternoon drinks, or an al fresco meal in the private courtyard surrounded by tropical flowers and bamboo, with seating for four.

Special Note: Sage Cottage is attached to Geranium Cottage, which may also be available if you are traveling with a larger group. Each cottage has three bedrooms, allowing for a total of six bedrooms (2 kings, 2 queens, and 4 twins) when both are reserved.

While the cottages are separate units, the privacy fence between the courtyards includes a locked gate that we can open to combine the two private courtyards into one shared space.

We offer special pricing when both cottages are reserved together, so please feel free to reach out if you are interested. You can view the Geranium Cottage listing here:

From the time we made the reservation until the time we checked out, Libby and Patrick were friendly, helpful, and always responded very quickly. The cottage is so lovely, spotlessly clean, and quiet. The beds and linens were very comfortable. The neighborhood is safe and very walkable to historic district sites and restaurants. Parking was always easy to find, we just had to be mindful about which marked stalls were paid during what hours, and which side was getting street sweeping in the morning. Overall, this was a wonderful place for my 3 friends and I to reconnect. It was a charming space for making meals together,  drinking coffee or wine, and just sitting and talking, with easy access to lots of sites and restaurants. It was also a great value compared to similarly located stays. We would definitely stay here again if we find ourselves back in Savannah.
