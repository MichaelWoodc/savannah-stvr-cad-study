=== FULL DESCRIPTION ===
Rainbow Row at Washington Square is known for its vibrant exterior, among the row of identical row homes. Just off of Washington Square, and one block from Historic River Street, this two-bedroom homes is packed with charm and rich decor.

The space
Located in the National Landmark Historic District, Up to four Lucky guests will enjoy Rainbow Row at Washington Square. Guests can live like a local being steps from Historic River Street, and having impressive amenities such as a private courtyard with a koi pond, and dedicated parking space. 

Enter into your slice of Southern charm, where you'll find a sitting room and living room with ample seating on the first floor. In the morning, open the sliding glass door to the private patio and enjoy the sounds of the fountain streaming. At the end of the day, catch up in your new gathering spot as you wind down for the night in the living room, fitted with a flat screen television. 

Whether you are dining out, or preparing a home cooked meal, take advantage of having a dedicated dining space for four Lucky guests. The galley style kitchen offers high-end stainless steel appliances, granite countertops, and dark espresso cabinetry.

Upstairs, guests will find their two private suites, each with an ensuite bathroom. Both bedrooms offer a queen size bed, and a designated reading space for those rainy Savannah afternoons. Each ensuite offers a shower and tub combination, with plenty of room to get ready for your day out on the town. 

Venture out on the town, and explore what Savannah has to offer. Just around the corner is the world famous Pirate’s House, which first opened in 1753 as an inn for seafarers, and quickly became a rendezvous spot for pirates. Heading out for the night? Walking down Broughton Street will bring you past a myriad of Savannah’s dining options. For more suggestions on things to do around town, download our hospitality app!
