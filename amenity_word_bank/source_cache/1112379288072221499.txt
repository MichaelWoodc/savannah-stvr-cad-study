=== FULL DESCRIPTION ===
Welcome to our garden-level retreat! Located in the vibrant heart of the historic district, just steps from Forsyth Park, this cozy one-bedroom, one bathroom condo is the perfect space to call home! Historic charm abounds with original heart pine beams adorning the ceilings, exposed brick walls throughout, and a lovely courtyard right outside your door! If you are looking to immerse yourself in a historical property for a truly authentic Savannah experience...you've found it here!

SVR-02737

The space
Nestled within a stunning 19th-century Savannah row home, this charming condo boasts a prime garden-level location just around the corner from Forsyth Park, right off Monterey Square. Step through your private entrance into a spacious bedroom featuring a luxurious king-sized bed, a flat-screen TV, and ample storage space. For leisurely days in the park, a delightful picnic basket awaits, while those seeking activity can indulge in provided workout gear for yoga sessions.

The cozy living room showcases original architectural highlights, offering a perfect retreat after a day of exploration. Whip up a gourmet meal or a quick snack in the fully equipped eat-in kitchen, or take your morning coffee (or evening glass of wine!) outdoors to the enchanting courtyard space.

For extended stays, enjoy the convenience of in-unit laundry facilities. Need to attend to business matters? A dedicated desk space in the foyer area provides an ideal setting to tackle emails before immersing yourself in all that Savannah has to offer.

Guest access
The entire garden-level condo is all yours! The courtyard is a shared space.

Other things to note
Parking can be tricky in downtown Savannah! The parking options nearby are paid street parking, which can either be paid using the kiosks/meters, the Park Savannah App, or by purchasing a day pass from the city. We will share all the details regarding navigating parking with you upon booking! There are also some free parking options a few blocks away that we will also give you more info about!
