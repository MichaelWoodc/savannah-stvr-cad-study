=== FULL DESCRIPTION ===
Have it all!  Southern Elegance is a beautiful historic home located in the Savannah Historic District and near renowned Forsyth Park.  It has been professionally updated, designed and decorated and has everything you need to make your stay in Savannah one to remember. Offering four bedrooms and four bathrooms, this lovely house can sleep a total of eight guests in 3450 square feet. Southern Elegance also stands out in terms of what it offers for outdoor living

The space
The courtyard is shared with the one-bedroom carriage house on the back of the property. Create your own private compound by renting the carriage house in addition to the main house. 



Spacious & Fabulous: The first floor includes two separate living areas: a lovely front parlor, as well as a sitting room in the back of the home with television. The formal dining room can seat 14 comfortably. And the well-equipped gourmet kitchen includes a butler’s pantry, wine refrigerators, ice maker, two dishwashers, and more. There’s also a lovely half bath on the first floor. 

The second floor includes three bedrooms and two full baths. Each bedroom has a historic mantle with non-working fireplace. The master bath includes a claw-footed tub and a separate oversized shower with two shower heads. There is also a porch off the master bath. The second bath includes a tub with shower.



The third floor is its own suite – including a king-sized bed, living area, wet bar, and full bath.



Southern Elegance is steps away from one of Savannah’s best-rated coffee shops and bakeries and a short walk to the grocery store for all your last-minute needs. 



Parking: Southern Elegance includes a garage. There is also usually plenty of parking available right in front of the house.



Historic Charm: The house was built in 1892 for Frederick Wessells and maintains that Savannah historic charm, while providing you with space for everyone and modern décor and amenities you’ll cherish during your stay. Enjoy the five historic mantles (non-working fireplaces) throughout the house, the original duel fuel light fixtures in the living room and dining room, the original kitchen hutch now located adjacent to the butler’s pantry, and many other historic features that give Southern Elegance it’s wonderful charm.   



Savannah vacation rentals help bring the city to life for our guests. By staying in various historic neighborhoods, you can feel more at home versus staying in a hotel. They also offer fantastic locations and a ton of privacy. The moment you walk out the door, you become immersed in Savannah culture. At the Southern Elegance Main House, the first item on your itinerary may be exploring Forsyth Park. You can reach this park easily by walking west on East Hall Street from this rental. Once there, you can explore the trails, gardens and picnic areas. There's even a cafe near the park if you want a bite to eat. When it comes to exploring Savannah from vacation rentals Savannah GA, the easiest way is to walk. However, you can also drive or take the Chatham Area Transit. The Old Town Trolley Tour also offers on and off service through all of its stops. The following are some other popular attractions to visit in Savannah's historic district:



- Leopold's Ice Cream

- Telfair Museums

- Bonaventure Cemetery

- Flannery O’Connor Childhood Home

- Monterey Square & The Mercer House

- City Market

- River Street

- Colonial Park Cemetery



If you're looking for a unique look at Savannah, then be sure to try the Underground Savannah Tours. This collection of tours takes a closer look at the history of African Americans in the city. You can take a tour to the First African Baptist Church, or something more spooky with the Hain’t and Boo Hags tour.



When you stay in Savannah vacation rentals, you can easily do more than walk around Savannah. For example, the relaxing Atlantic beaches of Tybee Island are only 30 minutes away by car. Tybee Island is one of the top extra excursions for guests of our Savannah vacation homes. It's easy to see why the moment you arrive. Tybee Island is a cozy barrier island to the east of Savannah. It includes a long stretch of beach on the Atlantic Ocean as well as beaches at the Savannah River mouth and the banks on the west. You can generally explore the island in a day with the Tybee Lighthouse and Marine Science Center being the most popular attractions. There are other activities as well, including water sports, bike rentals, marsh treks and dolphin-watching charters.



During your stay in our Savannah vacation homes, you may have the opportunity to check out a special event or two. Several events and festivals take place in Savannah and on nearby Tybee Island over the year. Take a look at some of the following possibilities:
