=== FULL DESCRIPTION ===
Enjoy First Class living on the First Floor.  This first floor garden apartment was recently completely redesigned by a local architect. This home sleeps up to 4 people with the use of the queen size murphy bed in the living room.

The space
Located on the first floor of a historic home, Hall & Oaks has been redesigned and lovingly renovated by a local Savannah architect. A ton of style has been packed into this one-bedroom, one-bath garden apartment on East Hall Street, which can sleep up to four Lucky guests. 

The spacious kitchen space is a chef’s dream, complete with stainless steel appliances and exposed brick and reclaimed wood walls. Enjoy coffee at the breakfast bar or a glass of wine while cooking dinner. You’ll have everything you need to prepare a home cooked meal. The reclaimed wood wall continues into the living room, which has an inlaid beam ceiling, and sleek, mid-century furnishings. Relax on the large sectional sofa in front of the wall-mounted flat screen TV. The living area also features a writing desk, upright piano, and queen-size Murphy bed. The bright and cozy queen-size bedroom is anchored by a rich wood sleigh bed and dresser, and the adjacent bathroom has a large walk-in tile shower with rain shower head.

You’ll be just across the street from Kroger, the only full grocery store in the Historic District, but close enough to all of Savannah’s dining options to never need to cook! The area adjacent to Forsyth Park offers a charming array of restaurants within walking distance - from fine dining at Saint Bibiana or Local 11ten to casual fare at Collins Quarter at Forsyth or Alexander's Bistro.

Why Lucky Savannah Loves this Property:
This home has a washer and dryer, making it perfect for extended stays. 
The desk in the living room provides a comfortable spot for the digital nomad to get some work done.  

Sleeping Configuration:
Bedroom 1: Queen Bed
Living Room: Queen Murphy Bed

Parking: Non-metered Parking
This unit has free, on-street parking right outside the front door. Availability will depend on time of day and season. Our guests have not reported trouble parking near the home, even on the busiest of weekends.
