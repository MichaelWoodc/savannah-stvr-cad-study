=== FULL DESCRIPTION ===
Sunlit and cheerful, Shipwreck Cottage is tucked in a quiet neighborhood in Savannah’s Historic District where you’ll sleep soundly away from the raucous party that is City Market and Ellis Square, but your feet will take you there in just 10 minutes. Named for the original photographs of local shipwrecks that decorate the space, you’ll enjoy your morning coffee and evening cocktail in the private courtyard. 
City of Savannah Cert: SVR-02408

The space
Shipwreck Cottage is your relaxing getaway in beautiful Historic Savannah located a block and a half from Troup Square.  What was originally lodging for railway workers is now a bright space featuring a private courtyard that feels like an outdoor room--one where you can dine al fresco, enjoy your morning coffee, check your email and sip sweet tea or wine in the afternoon. With a nod toward modern design, Shipwreck Cottage is eclectically furnished and decorated with local, original photographs. 

Open the door and step into the living room. The heart pine floors are original and, near the kitchen is a table with seating for four. In the spacious kitchen you'll find the tools you need to cook a great meal along with a full-size stacked laundry center. The bathroom features a tub/shower combination. A door from the kitchen takes you out to the charmingly landscaped courtyard.

The bedroom offers a comfortable queen bed, two nightstands, reading lamps and a closet. Quality bed linens and towels are provided, along with soaps, shampoo, conditioner. You will also find a hair dryer, iron and ironing board. The heating and cooling system will keep you comfortable no matter what the weather brings.

For your entertainment, enjoy wifi and cable TV. Bring your codes for Netflix, etc., accessible through the Roku Streaming device.

Savannah is best experienced on foot. Park your car in front of the cottage in one of the free spaces, or across the street in a paid space, and explore the moss-draped streets, hidden gardens and beautiful architecture of our charming city. Everything you need is in walking distance—historic sites, restaurants, pubs and groceries.

We designed our charming Shipwreck Cottage with your comfort in mind. Come enjoy Savannah like a local.

Guest access
This 525 sq. ft. private cottage is at your complete disposal. There is room in the closets, drawers and bathroom shelves for you to unpack and feel at home.
