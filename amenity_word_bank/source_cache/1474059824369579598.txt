=== FULL DESCRIPTION ===
Unit A at The Hourglass Inn ⏳ is a stylish 2BR/1BA retreat with all-new furniture and modern comfort in a quiet residential setting—no late-night bar noise here! 🌙 Relax in the 14-ft heated Jacuzzi/swim spa 💦 after exploring Savannah’s charming streets. Walk 1.5 blocks to Kroger 🛒, 4 blocks to Forsyth Park 🌳, and just a mile to River Street. Perfect for couples, friends, or families seeking a peaceful, centrally located getaway ✨.


Savannah License # - SVR-03172

The space
⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ 
Over 500 five-star reviews from previous listings—now with even more upgrades! ✨ New owners are investing big time to keep the great vibes going. Enjoy 2 Queens beds, one bathroom, a fully equipped kitchen 🍳, washer/dryer, coffee station ☕, and a designated workspace 💻. Downstairs unit of a charming quadplex with 1 off-street spot + free street parking 🚗.

NO ADDITIONAL PET FEE, we just ask that you take care of our space! 😁

Guest access
The entire condo is for your use as well as the back yard.

Other things to note
Our electronic locks are managed by Airbnb and Yale Lock Company, and each stay receives a custom code based on the last four digits of the booker’s phone number 🔐.

Each unit has one designated off-street parking spot, and any additional vehicles are welcome to use the free street parking available nearby 🚗.

This is a downstairs unit within a charming quadplex, so you may occasionally hear normal sounds from above — if you’re a light sleeper, we’re happy to provide a white noise machine 🌙.

If you’re traveling with friends or family and would like to book more than one unit in the building, we can offer a discount for multi-unit reservations 💫.

We’re conveniently located about 16 miles (roughly 20 minutes) from Savannah/Hilton Head International Airport ✈️.

This is a new venture for us, and we’re still getting to know Miss Hourglass ⏳, so your feedback means the world! 💛
