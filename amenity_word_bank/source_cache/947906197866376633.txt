=== FULL DESCRIPTION ===
Welcome to The Tea Olive B, a classic 1940s Airey-style designed townhome in the historic district of Savannah. With three bedrooms, including a loft accessed by a spiral staircase, this townhome is perfect for eight guests. Enjoy a beautiful, shared courtyard with a large deck, shade, and unique charm. Located on a quiet side street with brick sidewalks, you will be just minutes from some of the best places in the Savannah downtown. 10 Minutes to Forsyth Park, a short drive to the Riverwalk!

The space
The Tea Olive B provides a spacious primary floor providing ample relaxing room. The living space has a large sofa, reading chairs, and Smart TV above the fireplace. Walking to the back of the home, find a powder room under the stairs and a fully equipped galley kitchen/dining room. The kitchen has everything you need to cook, including pots, pans, dishware, and utensils. A custom dining table is set for six.
Step out back to enjoy a gorgeous, shared courtyard. With a large deck, BBQ grill, plenty of greenery, and an old brick patio, this courtyard is perfect for enjoying sunny Savannah weather.  
Travel the main staircase to the second floor, where you will find two bedrooms with queen mattresses and fresh linens. Spacious rooms with custom features, smart TVs, and storage space provide a relaxing environment for a great night’s rest. Additionally, find two full bathrooms on the second floor, one with a combination shower/tub and the second with a low-entry shower. Both bathrooms are fully outfitted with the essentials.
Finally, take the incredible spiral staircase to the loft to find four twin beds. A perfect place for kids or friends, the loft is spacious and is filled with natural light from the window and skylight.
Are you working remotely during your stay? All homes have high-speed Wi-Fi and ample room to set up your home office.  
Love the townhome but need more space? You can rent the neighboring townhome and cottage in the backyard, hosting up to 16 guests! 
Check out our separate listing for the availability all three units together.

Neighborhood highlights
The Tea Olive B is located on the east side of the Historic District, just minutes from Forsyth Park, Mother Matilda Beasley Park (with a dog park!), and many of Savannah’s best attractions.
Start your day by grabbing your morning coffee and breakfast just a couple blocks down at Carly’s Café and enjoying a park bench in Calhoun Square or Whitefield Square.
Enjoy excellent dining options by heading towards the riverwalk and stopping at The Olde Pink House for fine southern dining. Grab some laid-back bites and brews at Spanky’s Pizza Galley & Saloon. Enjoy delicious southern dining with a river view at Huey’s or Vic’s on the River. A short drive will take you to the River Walk and City Market area off Broughton Street. With endless shopping, sites, and eateries, you’ll find adventure at every corner.
Start your bar/pub tour by stopping into some staples of Savannah, including Six Pence Pub, O’Connell’s Irish Pub, or Dub’s Public House.
Explore the rich history of Savannah by stopping into one of the many museums and historical sites. Just minutes from Blue on Broad are the Web Military Museum, Telfair Museums, Owens-Thomas House & Slave Quarters, and the American Prohibition Museum.
A stop in Savannah wouldn’t be complete without a walk-through of Forsyth Park, just a 20-minute walk away. Or enjoy Washington Square, another beautiful green space just minutes away.
Want to take it all in at once while riding or biking? We are just minutes from Old Town Trolley Tours of Savannah and Savannah Bike Tours.
Getting around is easy with walkable streets, bike rentals, and Uber service. Jump on Savannah’s public transit system with the Price & Gordon SB bus stop just a 3-minute walk from the townhome.
Ready for a little on-the-water adventure? Try out the Savannah Belles Ferries to see the beautiful skyline of Savannah from the riverfront.
