=== FULL DESCRIPTION ===
Experience Savannah like a local in this peaceful, renovated 1BR/1BA cottage in the Historic District. 

Just blocks from Forsyth Park, River Street, charming squares, museums, boutiques, and top restaurants and bars. 

Sleeps 2 and offers a relaxing retreat after a day of exploring. Includes off-street parking. City of Savannah STVR Certificate #SVR-00558.

The space
Welcome to Architect’s Cottage, a charming tiny home in Savannah’s Landmark Historic District. Built in 1900 as a former railroad cottage, this cozy 1BR/1BA retreat blends historic character with modern comfort and includes dedicated off-street parking. 

Renovated by an architect, the beautifully designed interior features a fully equipped kitchen, inviting living space, queen bed with quality linens, and a tiled shower with stained-glass window. Enjoy morning coffee on the shaded courtyard deck. Modern amenities include high-speed Wi-Fi, Roku TVs and a washer/dryer. Walk to historic sites, shops, and restaurants, then return for a quiet, restful stay.

Guest access
Enjoy access to everything in the house including the private courtyard, laundry, and the entire kitchen! You will have complete privacy during your stay, including easy self check-in with a unique code to make your arrival smooth, secure, and hassle-free. Best of all, enjoy an included reserved, off-street parking space.
