=== FULL DESCRIPTION ===
A Historic Cottage in Downtown Savannah with a Rare Splash Pool to beat the Heat! Located within walking distance of local restaurants and .9 miles from River St! A separate entrance gives access to this half of the duplex and inside you will find 2 Bedrooms (King Beds), Small, Functional Living Room, and Compact Kitchen including Washer/Dryer.  Enjoyment here is in Lounging in a Private Fenced Backyard with Unheated Splash Pool, after a day of sightseeing in our beautiful city.

The space
This historic cottage is an absolute Savannah gem! Located in the downtown historic district this sweet bungalow is part of a Two-Unit Townhouse. Both sides are completely private with a separate entrance and backyard. The location is less than 1 mile from all the amazing restaurants and sites our Southern city has to offer! 

Inside you’ll enter to find Two Stacked Bedrooms, each with a Comfy King-sized Bed. Follow the Hallway to a Cozy Living Room, where a Vintage Table unfolds for meals, if you decide to cook while on vacation! Additional chairs are provided. Continue to the Modest Sized and Fully Stocked Kitchen which includes a Complete Set of Appliances and Washer / Dryer. 

Enjoy sipping a cold beverage in the courtyard, taking a swim in the dipping pool during warmer weather or resting up after hard day of sightseeing!

Both sides of the Duplex are available for rental.

Guest access
Access to the property is by Keyless Entry. Six Steps Lead to the Private Entry of each side of the Duplex.  Guests have their own private, fenced backyard with Splash Pool.

Other things to note
You may either park your vehicle right on the street out front or on the nearby side streets if you prefer (E Harris or E Charlton). There are typically plenty of spaces available and it is free on the one side of E. Harris and the one side of E. Charlton.  The metered parking spaces are free after 5 PM until 8 AM and Sunday all day.
