=== FULL DESCRIPTION ===
Stay at The Hourglass Inn! 🕰️ This pet-friendly upstairs 2BR/1BA is just blocks from Forsyth Park and walkable to shops, dining, River Street, and the only grocery store downtown. Enjoy a peaceful street, new furniture, king & queen beds, full kitchen, washer/dryer, and off-street parking. Unwind in the 14’ heated swim spa after exploring Savannah’s cobblestone charm. Keyless entry for easy self check-in!


Savannah License # - SVR-03173

The space
⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐
Over 500 five-star reviews from previous listings—now with even more upgrades! ✨ New owners are investing big time to keep the great vibes going. Enjoy one king bed 🛏️, one queen bed 🛌, one bathroom, a full kitchen 🍳, washer/dryer, coffee station ☕, and a workspace 💻. This pet-friendly upstairs unit in a charming quadplex offers 1 off-street spot, free street parking 🚗, and a fenced-in backyard perfect for pups 🐾.

NO ADDITIONAL PET FEE, we just ask that you take care of our space! 😁

Guest access
Guests have access to the entire condo as well as the back yard.

Other things to note
Our electronic locks are managed by Airbnb and Yale Lock Company, and each stay receives a custom code based on the last four digits of the booker’s phone number 🔐.

Each unit includes one designated off-street parking spot, with additional free street parking nearby 🚗.

This is an upstairs unit within a charming quadplex, so you may occasionally hear normal sounds from below — if you’re a light sleeper, we’re happy to provide a white noise machine 🌙.

If you’re traveling with friends or family and would like to book multiple units, we offer discounts for multi-unit reservations 💫.

We’re conveniently located about 16 miles (roughly 20 minutes) from Savannah/Hilton Head International Airport ✈️.

As a pet-friendly property, we provide a dog bed cot, food and water bowls, and waste bags to help keep the space clean and comfortable for your furry friends 🐾.

This is a new venture for us, and we’re still getting to know Miss Hourglass ⏳, so your feedback means the world! 💛
