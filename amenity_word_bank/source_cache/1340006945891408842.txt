=== FULL DESCRIPTION ===
Two apartments at the top of an incredible historic home on the St. Patrick's Day Parade route. Incredible, walkable location and amazing views from two balconies. Three king rooms, all ensuite await your every wish for a fantastic Savannah getaway.

The space
Historic Charm Meets Modern Comfort in Savannah

Discover two beautifully appointed apartments perched atop a stunning historic home directly on Savannah's famous St. Patrick’s Day Parade route. This unique property offers an unbeatable walkable location, breathtaking views from two private balconies, and all the comforts you need for an unforgettable getaway. 

Inside, you’ll find three king bedrooms, each with its own ensuite bathroom, ensuring a luxurious retreat for every guest. With two fully equipped kitchens, two cozy living rooms, and two spacious balconies, this home is ideal for a couples’ weekend or an intimate gathering with friends. 

The rooftop patio provides the perfect setting for evening conversations under the stars, while the upstairs unit boasts a small screening room and sweeping views of Savannah’s charming skyline. Thoughtful touches like luxury bedding and elegant architecture enhance every moment of your stay. 

Leave the car behind and enjoy Savannah’s walkable streets, incredible attractions, and historic charm. Book your stay today for a memorable escape!
