This brand new townhouse will take your breath away as soon as you step inside.  It is over 1700 square feet, tastefully decorated with an eye for design, and fully equipped with modern amenities and appliances. Perfect for a family getaway, a girls weekend, a gathering with friends, or even a business destination! The house is located on beautiful Liberty St in the Landmark Historic District of Savannah.

SVR 02098

The space
This lovely townhouse features an open concept living room and kitchen, with dining space for six.  Upstairs, two master bedrooms await with spa-like en-suite, private baths. And in the living room, that just isn't any sleeper sofa...our couch pulls out into a KING sized, super luxe, amazingly comfortable bed...perfect for an extra couple! The entire two-story main house is all yours.

Neighborhood highlights
The BEST part of our home? The location! We are in the heart of the Downtown Historic District, making the best places to dine, drink, and explore super accessible! We are with a 15 minute walk from the Savannah Riverfront, City Market, The Olde Pink House, Clary's, The Fountain at Forsyth Park, Mrs. Wilkes, and the Colonial Park Cemetery! Better yet, all of Savannah's beautiful squares are right outside of your door...perfect for exploring both the beautiful live oaks with their hanging moss as well as the historic architecture that frames each one! Want to check out SCAD? We are within walking distance to almost all of their downtown campus buildings!

If you choose to stay with us and need lots of recommendations...don't worry! We are happy to provide personalized recommendations based on your interests and preferences...just ask us! We are passionate about our little Southern city and are happy to provide lots of awesome spots to check out! We also provide our guests a full welcome packet, full of our personal favorites, from quiet local spots to must-sees attractions! And we live right around the corner, so we are ALWAYS here if you need us!
