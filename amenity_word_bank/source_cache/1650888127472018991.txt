=== FULL DESCRIPTION ===
Step into The Stables, a beautifully reimagined historic retreat in the heart of Savannah’s Historic District. Blending old-world charm with modern comfort, this thoughtfully designed home features inviting living spaces, a private outdoor courtyard with a firepit and fountain, and garage parking. Walk to top landmarks, dining, and shops, then unwind in a peaceful, refined setting.

The space
Welcome to The Stables, a truly one-of-a-kind luxury retreat tucked away in the heart of Savannah’s Historic District. Once home to working stables, this thoughtfully transformed residence blends rich history with refined design, offering a timeless escape just steps from Lafayette Square. Designed to comfortably accommodate up to eight guests across four beautifully appointed bedrooms and three full bathrooms, this home delivers both space and sophistication in one of downtown Savannah’s most coveted locations. From the moment you arrive, you’ll feel the magic of this hidden gem where historic charm meets elevated, modern comfort.



An Effortless Blend of Indoor & Outdoor Living

Entering through the front gates, you’re transported into what feels like another country entirely. The Stables reveals itself as a private European-inspired oasis, quietly tucked away from the energy of the city yet perfectly positioned to enjoy it all. The expansive courtyard is nothing short of breathtaking—an experience in itself and the true heart of the home. At its center, you’ll find dining tables ideal for al fresco meals beneath the Savannah sky, surrounded by lush greenery, blooming flowers, and soft ambient lighting that transforms the space at night into something truly unforgettable. A custom-designed fire feature invites guests to gather and unwind, while a peaceful fountain adds a sense of calm and tranquility. With a comfortable lounge area and a grill for preparing meals, this outdoor sanctuary is designed for connection, relaxation, and memorable evenings that feel worlds away from downtown, yet you’re right in the middle of it.



Inside, the home continues to impress with a seamless flow and beautifully curated interiors that honor its historic roots while embracing a sophisticated, modern aesthetic. The main living space is designed for both comfort and conversation, effortlessly connecting the dining area, living room, and additional sitting spaces. The dining area offers seating for four, perfect for shared meals or a casual breakfast before heading out to explore the city. Just beyond, the living room invites you to settle in with a plush sofa, four comfortable sitting chairs, and ottomans arranged around a smart TV, creating a warm and welcoming space to unwind after a day of sightseeing.



At the base of the staircase, you’ll find a charming nook that adds both function and character to the home. This quaint sitting area, complete with a work desk, provides a quiet place to catch up on emails, journal, or simply enjoy a moment of stillness. It’s these thoughtful details that make The Stables feel not just like a place to stay, but a place to truly live.



The kitchen is a standout feature in its own right, beautifully designed with custom finishes, elegant tile work, and a refined aesthetic that feels both luxurious and inviting. Fully equipped with full-size stainless steel appliances, it offers everything you need to prepare meals at home, whether you’re cooking for the group or simply brewing your morning coffee. A tucked-away pantry houses a convenient coffee bar along with a stacked washer and dryer, making longer stays as effortless as they are enjoyable. Just off the kitchen, a cozy breakfast area with seating for two provides the perfect spot for a quiet start to the day.



Restful, Well-Appointed Accommodations

Thoughtfully designed for both gathering and privacy, The Stables offers a well-balanced layout that comfortably accommodates up to eight guests across four bedrooms and three full bathrooms. Each sleeping space has been carefully curated to provide a restful retreat, while still maintaining the home’s cohesive blend of historic character and refined style.



On the first floor, the primary guest suite offers a serene retreat with a king-size bed and its own en suite full bathroom. This room is filled with character, featuring a unique arched window that adds architectural interest, along with French doors that open directly onto the courtyard allowing you to step outside and enjoy the peaceful surroundings at any time of day. A charming bar area with a mini fridge adds an extra touch of convenience, perfect for evening refreshments. The en suite bathroom features a petite clawfoot tub with a handheld shower head, adding a vintage-inspired detail that reflects the home’s historic past.



As you make your way upstairs, you’ll notice one of the most distinctive reminders of the property’s history—original hardware still mounted along the walls, once used to tie up horses when the building functioned as a working stable. These preserved elements offer a tangible connection to the past, thoughtfully integrated into the home’s design.



The second floor hosts three additional guest rooms, each offering its own unique charm. The second guest suite is particularly spacious, featuring a king-size bed and a private en suite bathroom with a beautifully tiled walk-in shower. Down the hall, two additional guest rooms provide comfortable accommodations, one with a queen bed and the other with a double bed. These rooms share access to a nearby full bathroom, also featuring a tiled walk-in shower, ensuring that every guest has access to well-appointed, stylish amenities.



An Exceptional Location with the Ease of Private Parking and Security

Beyond the home itself, The Stables offers the rare convenience of a private three-car garage with EV charger, an exceptional feature in the Historic District. Additional paid street parking is also available nearby, making it easy for guests traveling with multiple vehicles. For added peace of mind during your stay, the property is equipped with exterior-facing security cameras located in the courtyard, garage, and lane outside the front gate.



One of the most compelling aspects of this property is its unbeatable location. Situated just off Lafayette Square, you are within walking distance of some of Savannah’s most iconic landmarks and attractions. Take a leisurely stroll to the stunning Cathedral Basilica of St. John the Baptist, explore the historic beauty of the Andrew Low House, or wander through the nearby Colonial Park Cemetery. The Sorrel Weed House and Savannah Theatre are also just moments away, offering a glimpse into the city’s rich history and vibrant culture.



When it comes to dining and nightlife, you’ll be spoiled for choice. Enjoy a relaxed brunch at J. Christopher’s, savor elevated cuisine at The Public or Emporium, or grab a quick and flavorful bite from Zunzi’s. In the evenings, head to Peregrin for rooftop cocktails with sweeping views, experience the refined ambiance of Artillery Bar, or enjoy a laid-back night at local favorites like Pinkie Masters or McDonough’s Lounge. For coffee and lighter fare, nearby cafés like Mirabelle, Gryphon Tea Room, and Gallery Espresso provide charming spots to start your day or take a midday break.



Whether you’re visiting Savannah for a weekend getaway, a special celebration, or an extended stay, The Stables offers an experience that is truly unmatched. It’s a place where history and luxury coexist effortlessly, where every detail has been thoughtfully considered, and where the beauty of the surroundings invites you to slow down and savor each moment. At The Stables, you’re not just booking a place to stay, you’re stepping into a story, a setting, and a level of comfort that will leave a lasting impression long after your visit ends.
