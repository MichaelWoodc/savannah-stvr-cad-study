The space
LOCATION, LOCATION, LOCATION!
Ideally situated between City Market and River Street, this 1,860 sq ft Bay Street Beauty offers the perfect place to stay when you want to be close to everything in Savannah's historic district while enjoying plenty of room to spread out. This entire condo rental is easily accessible with an elevator in the building and features 2 luxurious king-size bedroom suites, each with its own ensuite bathroom; one bathroom is equipped with grab bars for enhanced accessibility. Southern charm and comfort all in one! 
STVR Certificate: SVR-02342

Why Stay Here?

- Spacious and Comfortable: With 2 king-size bedroom suites, spacious walk-in closets, and plenty of fluffy blankets, this condo is perfect for families or friends seeking a relaxing retreat. Maximum occupancy is 4, as per the City of Savannah and our condo association.

- Accessible Features: One of the ensuite bathrooms is equipped with grab bars in both the shower and next to the toilet, providing enhanced safety and accessibility for all guests. The condo also features wide doorways for ease of movement, and the building is equipped with an elevator.

- Modern Amenities: The condo, built in 2007 after a complete restoration of the original 1800s building, blends history and modernity. Enjoy Smart TVs in the living room and both bedrooms, and feel at home with the in-unit laundry room equipped with a washer and dryer. A starter pack of laundry pods and dryer sheets is provided for your convenience.

- Stunning Kitchen: The modern kitchen shines with stainless steel appliances and is fully stocked with all the essentials, including coffee supplies. We provide a drip coffee maker with a starter pack of coffee grounds and filters, as well as a Keurig machine with a selection of K-cups, sugar, and dairy creamer.

Prime Location: Walkable to Everything
This condo is ideally located in the heart of Savannah's historic district, making it easy to explore the city's rich history and vibrant culture on foot:

- River Street: Stroll along the picturesque Savannah River, visit local shops, and dine at waterfront restaurants. Don't miss out on pralines from River Street Sweets or a visit to the captivating Plant Riverside District.

- City Market: Enjoy lively restaurants, boutiques, and live music just a short walk away. Vinny Van Go Go's, a beloved local pizza haven, is a must-try!

- Broughton Street & Forsyth Park: Discover charming shops and iconic sites, including the beautiful Forsyth Park, all within a few blocks.

Dining at Your Doorstep
Onsite Options: If you prefer to dine close by, you’ll love having Five Guys, YATAI ramen + yakitori, and Starbucks right in the same building. For a top-notch dining experience, Ruth's Chris Steakhouse is just next door.

Stocked for Your Comfort

- Plush Linens & Towels: We've thought of everything to make your stay comfortable, including high-quality linens and towels. You'll find sheet sets and quilts for all bed sizes, as well as white bath towels, hand towels, and washcloths.

- Well-Equipped Kitchens: Whether you’re cooking a family meal or just grabbing a snack, our kitchens have you covered. We’ve stocked them with supplies to cook a delicious meal, including a full set of pots and pans, cups, plates, bowls, measuring cups, mixing bowls, and a toaster, to name a some items. We also supply a starter pack of dish cloths, dish soap, dishwasher detergent, salt and pepper.

- Personal Care Items: We provide a starter pack of personal care items including shampoo, conditioner, and soap.

- Laundry & Cleaning Supplies: We provide a starter pack of what you need to keep things tidy during your stay, including laundry detergent, dryer sheets, bleach, trash bags for both the kitchen and bathroom, and paper towels.

Additional Amenities
- Balcony Views: Enjoy glimpses of the Savannah River and the bustling City Market from the standing balcony off the living room.
- Floral Arrangements: Our property manager, a talented local florist, can arrange exquisite floral bouquets to enhance your stay.
- Baby Equipment: Need baby gear? We can recommend trusted rental services to make your stay even more comfortable.

Parking & Security

- Parking: We recommend parking in the Whitaker St. Garage, located around the corner from the condo. A parking pass for 1 vehicle is provided for the duration of your stay. Please return the pass to the table at the entrance of the condo unit upon departure, or a $200 replacement fee will be charged. Street parking is also available at your own expense.

- Security: For your peace of mind, the HOA has cameras in the common areas and exterior of the building.

Explore Savannah’s Charm
The neighborhood surrounding this condo is a captivating destination for guests seeking to experience the essence of Savannah's historic district. With cobblestone streets, well-preserved buildings, and picturesque squares adorned with moss-draped oak trees, you’ll be immersed in Southern charm. Visit nearby museums like the Ships of the Sea Maritime Museum and the Telfair Museums, or take a leisurely stroll in Forsyth Park. The diverse culinary scene, featuring Southern specialties and international flavors, makes this area a food lover's paradise.

*No parties or third party bookings*
A rental agreement is required to be completed upon booking.

Guest access
We recommend parking in the Whitaker St. Garage around the corner from the condo.  We will allow you to use our parking pass (good for 1 vehicle) for the Whitaker St parking garage during your stay.  It must be returned to the table at the entrance of our condo unit when you leave or we will charge you a $200 replacement fee.

You may also find street parking at your own expense.

Other things to note
This luxurious 1,860 sq. ft. condo blends Southern charm with modern convenience. It boasts two spacious king-size bedroom suites with ensuite bathrooms, one featuring grab bars for enhanced accessibility. Enjoy a fully equipped kitchen with stainless steel appliances, in-unit laundry, Smart TVs in every room, and a standing balcony with views of the Savannah River and City Market. The building includes an elevator for easy access, and a parking pass is provided for nearby garage parking. Perfectly located in Savannah’s historic district, you’re steps away from River Street, City Market, and top dining options.
