=== FULL DESCRIPTION ===
This beautifully updated two story home has two private porches and lots of Southern charm.

The space
Tattnall Street is an often missed gem among the streets of Savannah, overshadowed by the cobblestones of Jones Street, which Southern Living Magazine dubbed “America’s prettiest street.” This quintessential Southern street will will make you feel like you’re coming home. 

You’ll enter into the foyer, which has stairs leading to the second floor, a hallway that leads back to the kitchen, and opens to the living room to your right. Past the living room is the dining room with seating for six. The upgraded kitchen has a convenient island with stools and a breakfast nook. Enjoy your morning coffee in a rocking chair on your private porch just off the kitchen. 

Upstairs you’ll find three beautiful bedrooms. The bedroom at the top of the stairs has two twin beds, perfect for families with children. The second bedroom has a queen-sized bed and its own private porch. These two bedrooms share a bath, which has a wonderful clawfoot tub. The primary bedroom has a king bed, television, and ensuite bath with a double vanity and huge shower with built-in seating.  

Nearby dining favorites include Mrs. Wilkes Dining House, where you’ll find some of the best Southern home cooking in the city, and the Crystal Beer Parlor, Savannah’s oldest restaurant. You’ll also be a short walk from Forsyth Park, Savannah’s largest park and popular location for weddings, music and food festivals. 
 
Why Lucky Savannah Loves this Property:
- There is plenty of non-metered parking surrounding the home, so there's no need to park in a garage or pay parking meters! 
- A washer and dryer is conveniently located in the kitchen area. 
- Built in 1871, this home has been fully updated to provide all the comforts you’ll need during your stay in Savannah. 

What is parking like for this home? Non-metered Parking
This unit has free, on-street parking right outside the front door. Availability will depend on time of day and season. Our guests have not reported trouble parking near the home, even on the busiest of weekends.
