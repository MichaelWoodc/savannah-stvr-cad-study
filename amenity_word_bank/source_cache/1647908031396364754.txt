=== FULL DESCRIPTION ===
The Upper Carriage House is a beautifully restored second-story residence within the historic Wayne Mansion Estate, located just one block from Forsyth Park. This private two-bedroom retreat blends classic charm with modern comfort.

The space
About the Space
Access your private top-floor residence via an exterior staircase. Inside, warm natural light and original hardwood floors create a welcoming, peaceful atmosphere. The layout is ideal for couples, friends, or small families who appreciate privacy and thoughtful design.

Bedrooms & Bathrooms
• 	Bedroom 1 – Queen bed, en-suite full bathroom, and smart TV
• 	Bedroom 2 – Queen bed, en-suite full bathroom, and walk-in closet 
Both bedrooms offer complete privacy with their own attached bathrooms—perfect for guests who value comfort and personal space.

Kitchen, Living & Laundry
• 	Fully equipped full kitchen with range, refrigerator, microwave, and essentials
• 	Comfortable living area with smart TV
• 	Dining nook suitable for meals or remote work
• 	In-unit washer and dryer for convenient laundry access
Location
Located one block from Forsyth Park, the Upper Carriage House offers effortless walkability to Savannah’s best cafés, restaurants, boutiques, and historic sites. Guests enjoy a short walk to the largest grocery store in downtown Savannah, making it easy to stock up during longer stays. The home is also near a stop for the north–south fare-free DOT shuttle, providing quick, car-free access to the rest of the Historic District. In addition, you’re within easy walking distance of several of Savannah’s beautiful historic squares, perfect for morning strolls or exploring the city’s charm at your own pace.

Please note that this listing does not include any outdoor living space or on-site parking.  Free street parking is available in the immediate area.
