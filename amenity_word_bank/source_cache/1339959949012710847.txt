Abercorn and York – Historic Charm Meets Modern Comfort

Immerse yourself in the beauty of Savannah at this stunning apartment where you can leave the car behind and explore the city on foot. Located in the heart of the Historic District, this home offers a king bedroom and two queen bedrooms, each designed for your comfort. Exposed brick adds a touch of historic charm throughout the space, while an outdoor patio on the St. Patrick’s Day parade route ensures your stay will be truly memorable. 

A Historic Legacy
Abercorn and York was built in 1853 by Captain Henry James Dickerson, a prominent Savannah figure. Born in Maryland in 1814, Dickerson came to Savannah at 19, beginning his career as a sailor before owning ships and pioneering large-scale stevedoring operations in the region. His contributions to Savannah’s maritime and economic development were significant, leaving a lasting legacy before his passing in 1883. 

What is Stevedoring?
Stevedoring involves loading and unloading cargo from ships, a vital part of the shipping and logistics industry. Captain Dickerson played a key role in modernizing these operations during his time, helping to expand the bustling Port of Savannah. 

Please note: Guests will need to be able to climb stairs to access the property.
