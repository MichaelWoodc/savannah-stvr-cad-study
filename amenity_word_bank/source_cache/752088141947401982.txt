=== FULL DESCRIPTION ===
Situated on East Taylor Street, this charming abode built in 1862 has been tastefully restored, and is the perfect Savannah hideaway.

The space
This historic home offers all of the amenities one could hope for while staying an extended period of time. Located in the quieter, residential area of the Downtown Historic District, you are surrounded by tree-line views.

Situated on East Taylor Street, this charming abode built in 1862 has been tastefully restored, and is the perfect Savannah hideaway. Entering into the main floor of the home,  you'll be greeted by impressive tall ceilings, original hardwood flooring that gleam with tons of character, and large windows that flood the gathering room with natural light. The furnishings and artwork tie this space together, adding to the historic charm of this town-home. Right off of this space, you'll find the large kitchen and dining space, where you'll have everything needed to prepare a home-cooked meal for your friends or family. 

On the second floor, you'll find two quaint and cozy bedrooms, both fitted with a Queen size beds, and an ample amount of closet space. The bathroom atop of the stairs offers a large vanity, and a bathtub/shower combination. 

Heading up-to the third floor, you will find the lofted bedroom, that is fitted with two queen beds, perfect for friends or family that visit. This floor offers two closets; Guests will love having plenty of storage, including a dresser, to un-pack their belongings in their new home away from home!

Just out back, you will find the private deck, and a large patio space. Enjoy the evenings sipping a glass of wine, or a morning cup of joe on the back porch! Just on the opposite side of the fence, you will find a private parking space designated to your home.

Just a short walking distance to the heart of downtown, this property is ideal for travelers looking to stay in a tree-lined, quieter residential part of town without sacrificing convenience. Head West and dine at some of the city's best restaurants, like 700 Drayton, Fox & Fig, and Hitch. Due North toward River Street, guests can shop til they drop inside various local retailers, like The Paris Market & Brocante, 24e, and shopSCAD to name a few. Guests are about a 15-20 minute walk to the River front, 10-15 to City Market, and a quick 8 minute walk to Forsyth Park.

What is parking like for this home?
This home offers one private parking space. All other parking is on-street, non-metered on East Taylor Street. We encourage all guests to pay close attention to street signs, as they state when the street cleaners come.

Sleeping Configuration:
Bedroom 1: Queen Bed
Bedroom 2: Queen Bed
Bedroom 3: 2 Queen Beds

We can welcome your dog companions at this pet friendly home, but ask that you find other arrangements for cats, birds, and other furry friends. There is a $130 pet fee (non-refundable) per dog, with a 35 lb. maximum weight limit per dog and a limit of 2 dogs per reservation. Some homes may allow larger dogs, please inquire for more information.
