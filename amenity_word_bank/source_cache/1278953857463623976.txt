=== FULL DESCRIPTION ===
This luxurious estate offers two private parking spaces and a refreshing plunge saltwater pool, making it an unparalleled retreat in one of the city’s most sought-after locations.

The space
Welcome to Southern Roots Estate, a hidden gem in the heart of Savannah's historic district. Just steps from River Street and half a block from Broughton Street, this luxurious estate offers two private parking spaces and a refreshing saltwater plunge pool, making it an unparalleled retreat in one of the city’s most sought-after locations.

What Makes Southern Roots Estate Special?

Unbeatable Location: Enjoy a leisurely 5-minute stroll to River Street, where you can take in breathtaking sunsets and watch majestic ships cruise by. Just behind the estate, explore the vibrant shops and restaurants along East Broughton Street. Plus, you’ll be nestled between two of Savannah’s iconic squares, Warren and Washington.

Exceptional Outdoor Space: Relax in the courtyard or take a dip in the saltwater plunge pool. Whether you’re basking in the summer sun or enjoying a cozy fall evening, the outdoor area is designed for ultimate relaxation.

Luxurious Interiors: The home’s interior, designed by a renowned local designer, leaves no detail overlooked. From the elegant living spaces to the plush beds, every corner has been thoughtfully curated to provide a luxurious and comforting experience.

In the main house, you'll find three generously sized bedrooms. The primary bedroom, located on the ground floor, opens to the courtyard through a sliding glass door and features a spa-like ensuite bathroom with a jetted tub, along with its own living area and two walk-in closets. Upstairs, two additional bedrooms each have their own full ensuite bathroom and walk-in closet, both offering king beds for restful nights.  There is even a gathering space in the wine cellar for a unique and festive experience.  

The carriage house, adjacent but separate from the main home, provides a private escape and you'll find the fourth bedroom here. Perfect for guests seeking privacy or adult children, the carriage house includes a full kitchen, a cozy living room with a queen sleeper sofa, and a bedroom with a queen bed and ensuite bathroom.

What is parking like for this home?
Guests will have access to two garage spaces. These spaces can accommodate full-size sedans and small SUVs. All larger vehicles must find on-street parking, which is metered on East Congress Street, but is free on Price Street, which runs parallel to the carriage house.

We can welcome your dog companions at this pet friendly home, but ask that you find other arrangements for cats, birds, and other furry friends. There is a $130 pet fee (non-refundable) with a 35 lb. maximum weight limit per dog and a limit of 2 dogs per reservation. Some homes may allow larger dogs, please inquire for more information.

*Please note that this is a plunge pool and not a hot tub or spa.*

*For the security of our guests and the safety of our home, we have an external security camera located in the courtyard facing the pool and gate.*
