=== FULL DESCRIPTION ===
Nestled in Savannah’s historic Victorian District from the 1800's, just 3.5 blocks from Forsyth Park, this carriage triplex offers a quiet, charming retreat in the heart of the city. Walk to River Street, SCAD, Broughton Street shopping, and the Starland Art District, or explore local restaurants, bars, and boutiques nearby. Popular with locals, students, and visitors, it’s ideal for couples, solo travelers, weekend getaways, and SCAD guests seeking comfort and convenience!

The space
This beautifully restored 1830s structure has been brought to life with artistic touches and modern amenities to provide you with a unique and comfortable stay. You have everything you need at Josephine's -- living space, flat screen TV, WIFI, kitchen (for light cooking and microwaving only -- no baking or frying), laundry room, work desk, bedroom -- all designed and furnished for comfort and visual interest. 

In addition to its prime location, Apt. A offers easy access to a range of local shops, restaurants, and nightlife. Be sure to check out the seasonal Farmer's Market at Forsyth, where you can sample locally grown and made foods on Saturday mornings. You’ll have easy access to I-16 for trips to the Outlet Mall or, head to Tybee Island Beach, about a 25 minute drive, with Fort Pulaski as a great stop along the way.

Whether you're exploring the city's rich history, enjoying the vibrant arts scene, or simply soaking up the Southern charm, Josephine's Carriage House, Apt. A is the ideal place to stay. Book your visit today and experience the best of Savannah!

Guest access
This is a private triplex, and guests have full access to their unit and the shared exterior property. For added security, exterior cameras are in place around the building.

Free parking is available on the side of the property, with one designated space per unit.

Other things to note
Please note that parking is available on a first-come, first-served basis, with one space per home. On-site parking is located immediately to the left of the building. Free street parking is also available on W. Park Ave. and Montgomery St. Guests are responsible for adhering to all posted parking signage and street-cleaning restrictions.

Neighborhood highlights
Nestled in Savannah’s celebrated Victorian District, just west of Bull Street and only 3.5 blocks from Forsyth Park, this home is the perfect base for exploring the city. Sitting midway between the lively River Street to the north and the creative energy of the Starland Art District to the south, you’re never far from Savannah’s best attractions.

Within walking distance, you’ll find the Railroad Museum, SCAD Art Museum, the Design District, and Broughton Street shopping. Restaurants, bars, and boutiques are just a few blocks away, and a Kroger grocery store sits conveniently on the opposite side of Forsyth Park.
The Victorian District itself is a vibrant mix of history and renewal. Stroll the streets to admire late-1800s cottages alongside grand Italianate and Queen Anne mansions, it often feels like an architectural tour at every turn.

This Carriage House is tucked along a quiet, historic lane, offering a more intimate perspective of the neighborhood. Just a block east, charming railroad cottages look like they’ve stepped straight out of a storybook. The area thrives as a true mixed-use neighborhood, blending owner-occupied homes, local businesses, SCAD buildings, and both long- and short-term rentals, just as it was meant to be.
