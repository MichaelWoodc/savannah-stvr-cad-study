=== FULL DESCRIPTION ===
One block from Savannah’s largest park, Forsyth, sits the Big Blue House. Our 6 bedrooms and 4 bathrooms can accommodate a family or group of friends. This listing is for the entire home. Please keep in mind that this home is over a 100 years old and isn't a luxury hotel.

We do allow dogs with a fee. No cats.

The space
My place is close to parks, restaurants and dining, the city center, and great views. You’ll love my place because of the high ceilings, the location, and the ambiance. My place is good for families (with kids) and big groups.

Guest access
During your stay, you'll have access to all the amenities our home offers, including WiFi, laundry facilities, TV with cable, complimentary towels, and bathroom essentials like soap, shampoo, and makeup wipes. We provide three rolls of toilet paper per bathroom and one roll of paper towels to get you started. If you're staying longer or planning to cook often, you may want to pick up additional supplies.

Other things to note
We are a dog friendly home. 2 dogs maximum. We do not allow dogs on furniture or beds. We require a $150 non-refundable dog fee. If there is evidence of dog hair on the furniture, excess dog hair on towels and sheets, or stains, you will be charged the cost to clean this. In the event that a security deposit is collected, we will retain all or part of the security deposit to cover any costs associated with additional cleaning due to pets. 

The house is over a hundred years old and sound does travel.  If your dog likes to bark a lot this house is not for them. 

Please be considerate of the neighborhood and our neighbors. City of Savannah Noise Ordinance for vacation rentals is between the hours of 9pm- 8am. Please remember that sound carries very well throughout Savannah, most especially in courtyards, porches, patios, and verandas.

If you have questions you need answered before securing your booking, or you simply want to talk with us first, please click my profile picture at the top of this listing and then click the "contact host" button to send an email directly to us without submitting a reservation request.

We've lived in Savannah for over 20 years each. We still live downtown but decided to rent our homes as vacation rentals because we want you to enjoy Downtown Savannah as much as we do. All of our homes are located in the Historic / Victorian district and in walking distance to Forsyth Park where we love to go on Saturday mornings for the Farmers Market or have a picnic on Sunday afternoon. Our kids love the playground and the grand yard. Our properties were decorated to be comfortable for you and your family/friends. We have a large sectional sofa in the living rooms. We want you to relax when you're at our homes and if that means lying on the couch and watching a movie then this is the right place for you! We are both Realtors for Salt Marsh Realty. So if you decide that you want to buy a home while visiting Savannah, please let us know.
