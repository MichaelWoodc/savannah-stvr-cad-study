=== FULL DESCRIPTION ===
A beautiful and historic 1888 Victorian second story unit in the heart of Savannah's East Victorian District.  Just a few minutes walk from Forsyth Park, delicious coffee shops / restaurants, and Savannah's College of Art and Design (SCAD). 
Enjoy complementary snacks and water at check in. Additionally each room has a smart TV and with complementary HBO and Paramount+

The space
The space is clean and modern, yet welcoming and warm.  It has soaring 10ft ceilings in the bedrooms and bath with 14ft ceilings in the living area. Enjoy this 130+ year old home with updated touches and design.

Guest access
Guests will have access to the entire upstair apartment which includes a separate entrance, kitchen, two bedrooms, living room, bathroom, and an upstairs porch.

Other things to note
We offer self check in and will send you the door code a few days before your arrival. Please message us if you have any questions! Please note we do not host anyone from the Savannah area or surrounding areas. Please reach out for questions. SVR-02028
