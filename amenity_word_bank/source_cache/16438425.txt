=== FULL DESCRIPTION ===
Fall for the charm of this loft-style flat with gleaming wood floors, brick walls, & a view of historic Savannah. With an elevator, king bed & pull-out sofa, this high-ceilinged, 100-year-old warehouse space sports tall windows that stream with sunlight. If you love the elegance, fun & buzz of downtown Savannah, Ellis Square Place is for you. Please note that because of its central location you will hear people having fun on the streets--especially weekends. Parking not included. 
SVR-00661

The space
Inside the well-appointed apartment building equipped with an elevator, sunlight streams through tall windows, into a high-ceilinged 100 year old warehouse space. Historic hardwood floors and exposed original brick walls recall Savannah’s trading past. A beautiful fully equipped kitchen opens onto a dining area, living and entertaining space. This flows into the bedroom area and bathroom. Exquisitely furnished, City Market Central is the perfect spot for up to 4 people to enjoy one of America’s most fun-living cities. 
Entertain in style from the fully stocked kitchen and bar cart or kick back and enjoy the 50" LED TV with internet access for Netflix (bring your own code), YouTube, etc., and local channels. The apartment offers a romantic king-sized bed, and a custom made leather queen sleeper sofa. The en suite bathroom features a Jacuzzi tub. Pamper yourself with high thread count organic cotton sheets and quilts and quality Turkish towels. Ample walk-in closets will take care of your party wardrobe. High-speed Wi-Fi for business and everything else. Washer/dryer for laundry. White noise machine to temper the noise of late-night revelers. Park at Whitaker Street Garage, a 3-minute walk from your front door. (Parking not included.)

**Please Note: You will hear street noise, which may be loud--especially on weekends. We provide two white noise machines and ear plugs.**

Guest access
This 750 sq. ft. private apartment is at your complete disposal. There is room in the closets, drawers and shelves for you to unpack and feel at home.
