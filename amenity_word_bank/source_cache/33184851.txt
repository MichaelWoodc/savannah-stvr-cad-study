=== FULL DESCRIPTION ===
Luxury three-bedroom, two-bath condo just four blocks away from Forsyth Park in the historic downtown (Adults only). Treat yourself to a bit of luxury with this stunning home, be it for yourself or a group of adult friends/family. Two private off-street parking spaces are also included. Looking for a larger space? If available, the downstairs unit can be booked as well, which offers an additional three bedrooms, a living room, a kitchen, and two bathrooms.

The space
This elegantly presented upper level Savannah vacation rental is located just 4 blocks from Forsyth Park on a beautiful tree-lined stretch of East Waldburg Street. It is conveniently close to many amenities, including a popular coffee shop, health food store, and the outstanding Local 11Ten restaurant for fine dining. A grocery store is also just two blocks away. 

This beautiful Savannah vacation home is comprised of a Master bedroom/bathroom, including a jetted tub and front balcony perfect for street view lounging, two additional bedrooms, and guest bath, a living room, a gourmet kitchen and much, much more.

Amenities include FREE high-speed Wi-Fi and standard cable; Savannah Dream Vacations has thought of everything in advance so guests can pack light. Enjoy Premium Amenities including: Linens, Towels, Washcloths, Soap, Shampoo, Conditioner & Body Lotion along with all disposable paper products and cleaning supplies including Laundry Detergent. Household Appliances including Hair Dryers, Iron & Board & Washer/Dryer, as well as coffee, tea, creamer, sugar, pots and pans, electric kettle, silverware, and dishes.

SVR-00379

Guest access
The entire upper level, the backyard is shared with the downstairs unit. You also have two off-street parking spaces behind the house.

Other things to note
For guests traveling with pets. "Dogs only", sorry. We welcome your Well-behaved dogs, max. of 2 dogs per unit. 
There is a non-refundable dog cleaning fee of $120 per stay. 
 Failure to inform us will result in a $ 1,000.00 penalty, with no exception.
