=== FULL DESCRIPTION ===
On the 200 East block of Jones Street, this historic two bedroom home is steps away from everything including the legendary Clary's Restaurant. With a garden meeting you at your front door, to unwind after a busy day on the town.

The space
Tucked away behind the massive oak trees is Jones Street Hideaway, a quaint garden-level apartment beneath a 200-year-old estate. Guests will enjoy waking to the sounds of horse-drawn carriages, and spending a slow afternoon walking to the famous Clary's Cafe for brunch.

Enter through the courtyard into your two-bedroom garden apartment. The living area is furnished for comfort with two leather sofas and a large flat-screen TV. A full size kitchen boasts stainless steel appliances and granite countertops, with a dining table fit for four.

Both bedrooms have Queen size beds, and each have access to the full bath with standing shower. This home is ideal for the couple looking to have a quiet respite after a long day of exploring downtown.

Bedroom Configuration:
Bedroom 1: Queen Bed
Bedroom 2: Queen Bed

What is parking like for this home?
Parking for this home is on-street and metered, which is enforced by the City of Savannah from Monday through Friday, 8am to 5pm. Guests may conveniently pay using the ParkSavannah app, feed the meter or parking kiosks directly, or park on a nearby non-metered street. Please take note of posted Sweep Zone signs when parking on the street.
