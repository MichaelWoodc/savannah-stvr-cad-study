=== FULL DESCRIPTION ===
Fully furnished 5 bedroom house waiting for your visit to Savannah. Seven blocks from Savannah’s largest park, Forsyth. This listing is for the entire home. Please keep in mind that this home is over a 100 years old and isn't a luxury hotel.

My place is close to parks, restaurants and dining, the city center, and great views. You’ll love my place because of the high ceilings, the location, and the ambiance. My place is good for families and big groups.

We do allow dogs with a fee. No cats.

Guest access
While staying with us, you may make use of everything our home has to offer: Wifi, laundry, TV, cable, complimentary towels, bathroom essentials (soap/shampoo/makeup wipe) and more are at your disposal. We provide a three toilet paper rolls per bathroom and one roll of paper towels to get you started, you may want to pick up some more if you plan on spending lots of time or cooking at the house.

Other things to note
We are a dog friendly home. 2 dogs maximum. We do not allow dogs on furniture or beds. We require a $150 non-refundable dog fee. If there is evidence of dog hair on the furniture, excess dog hair on towels and sheets, or stains, you will be charged the cost to clean this. In the event that a security deposit is collected, we will retain all or part of the security deposit to cover any costs associated with additional cleaning due to pets. 

The house is over a hundred years old and sound does travel. If your dog likes to bark a lot this house is not for them. 

Please be considerate of the neighborhood and our neighbors.

If you have questions you need answered before securing your booking, or you simply want to talk with us first, please click my profile picture at the top of this listing and then click the "contact host" button to send an email directly to us without submitting a reservation request.

Our home is in compliance with all Short-Term Vacation Rental laws in Savannah, Georgia. Our STVR permit number is SVR-00855

We've lived in Savannah for over 20 years each. We still live downtown but decided to rent our homes as vacation rentals because we want you to enjoy Downtown Savannah as much as we do. All of our homes are located in the Historic / Victorian district and in walking distance to Forsyth Park where we love to go on Saturday mornings for the Farmers Market or have a picnic on Sunday afternoon. Our kids love the playground and the grand yard. Our properties were decorated to be comfortable for you and your family/friends. We have a large sectional sofa in the living rooms. We want you to relax when you're at our homes and if that means lying on the couch and watching a movie then this is the right place for you! We are both Realtors for Salt Marsh Realty.
