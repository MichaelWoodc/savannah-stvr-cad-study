=== FULL DESCRIPTION ===
The Chippewa Suite, one of the Jones Street Suites, is light-filled and inviting with soaring ceilings and incredible views of Savannah's world-famous Jones Street. This suite is located on the 3rd floor and accommodates up to 4 guests. You'll be steps from dining, shops, Forsyth Park, River Street, and all that our wonderful city has to offer while staying on the most beautiful street in America!

The space
The Chippewa Suite, one of the Jones Street Suites, is light-filled and inviting with soaring ceilings and incredible views of Savannah's world-famous Jones Street. This apartment is located on the 3rd floor and accommodates up to 4 guests. The space has one bedroom with an en-suite bathroom, and there is a pull-out couch in the living room for guests three and four.

Guest access
Please note this suite is on the 3rd floor.  There is a flight of stairs outside to enter the property, and another flight of stairs once you enter the property to get to the suite.

Other things to note
There isn't a designated parking space for this suite, but there are typically plenty of parking spaces available on the street.  You may either park your vehicle right on the street out front or on the nearby side streets if you prefer. Parking on Jones Street is metered Monday – Friday 8 AM-5PM and they have a 2 hour time limit.  Parking is free after 5PM during the weekdays and all day on Saturday and Sunday, with no time limit.
