=== FULL DESCRIPTION ===
Experience historic Savannah in our elegant 2-bedroom, 2-bath condo for 4. Located in the heart of the historic district, this charming retreat blends Victorian architecture with modern comforts. Just one block from Forsyth Park and steps from the Mercer-Williams House Museum, our walkable location is unbeatable. Enjoy a fully equipped kitchen, Wi-Fi, and a unique stay in a beautifully restored 1870s building. Perfect for couples or small families seeking a stylish and central Savannah getaway.

The space
Welcome to Classic Quarters at the 101, your charming retreat in the heart of Savannah's historic district. This elegant two-bedroom condo beautifully blends timeless Victorian architecture with modern comforts, creating a stylish and welcoming ambiance for your stay.

Originally designed by the Meinhardt brothers in the 1870s, the space exudes historic charm with its soaring ceilings, warm hardwood floors, and intricate crown molding. The inviting living room features plush couches, cozy armchairs, and large windows overlooking downtown Savannah. Please note the fireplaces are decorative, adding to the home's historic character.

The condo comfortably accommodates up to four guests in two bedrooms:
*   Master Bedroom: King-size four-poster bed
*   Second Bedroom: Queen-size four-poster bed

Both bedrooms are outfitted with soft mattresses and fresh linens. Two full bathrooms add convenience, one with a walk-in shower and the other with a shower-and-bathtub combination.

The fully equipped kitchen boasts stainless steel appliances and all the essentials you need:
*   Full-size oven and stove
*   Refrigerator and dishwasher
*   Microwave, toaster, and coffee maker

Modern amenities for a comfortable stay include:
*   Whole-house Wi-Fi
*   Flatscreen television
*   Central air conditioning (AC)
*   In-unit washer and dryer
*   Hairdryer and ironing board

Guest access
Guests will have private and exclusive access to the entire two-bedroom condo. Entry is seamless with a keyless lock on the main exterior doors of the building. Classic Quarters at the 101 is Unit 3, located up one flight of stairs from the building's entrance.

Other things to note
*   This condo is professionally cleaned and thoughtfully stocked with essentials like soap, shampoo, and fresh towels for your arrival.
*   Please be aware that the unit is on the second floor and requires guests to walk up one flight of stairs to access it.
*   The fireplaces within the home are historic and for decorative purposes only; they are not functional.
*   City of Savannah Short-Term Vacation Rental Permit #: SVR-02744
