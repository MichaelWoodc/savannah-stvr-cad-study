=== FULL DESCRIPTION ===
Want a relaxing get away without compromising location? Steeple View Loft is on tree-lined Oglethorpe Avenue, in one of Savannah's most desirable locations. Recently renovated, you will enjoy all of the modern luxuries one could hope for!

The space
Looking for a relaxing get away without compromising location? Steeple View, on tree-lined Oglethorpe Avenue, is located in one of Savannah's most desirable locations in the heart of Savannah's downtown historic district. Recently renovated, you'll enjoy all of the modern luxuries one could hope for with remodeled kitchen, bathrooms, and stunning hardwood floors! Take the elevator to the top floor of this historic building, built in 1923. 

The tastefully designed kitchen offers plenty of cooking space with brand new stainless steel appliances. Just across from the kitchen, you'll find the designated dining space that opens up to the living room. The dining table seats four or is expandable for up to six Lucky guests. The living room is furnished with a sofa that converts into a comfortable Queen bed. A large flat screen television with App streaming access has also been conveniently placed in this space. A full bathroom with shower/tub combo sits just off of the living room. 

The primary bedroom can be found just past the living room and offers a King bed, flat screen television, writing desk, ample amount of closet space, and steeple views. Guests will enjoy an ensuite bathroom with shower/tub combo that has been tastefully remodeled. The second bedroom on the other side of the apartment has a beautifully planted window box under a large window that fills the room with natural light, and also has a comfy King size bed, a flat screen television, a writing desk, and a spacious closet.

Why Lucky Savannah Loves this Property:
- You’ll have a full sized washer and dryer, perfect for extended stays. 
- Stunning views with an ample amount of natural light!
- Nearby City Market is a hub of nightlife in Savannah, with live music every weekend. 

Parking: Metered Parking
Parking for this home is on-street and metered, with enforcement by the City of Savannah Monday through Saturday, 8am to 8pm. Guests may conveniently pay using the ParkSavannah app, feed the meter or parking kiosks directly, or utilize a nearby public parking garage. Pre-paid visitor parking passes are available for purchase at the Parking and Mobility Services office. Please take note of posted Sweep Zone signs when parking on the street.
