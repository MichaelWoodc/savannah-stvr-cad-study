=== FULL DESCRIPTION ===
A lovingly restored home near shops and restaurants, The Parsonage at Forsyth is the perfect choice in Savannah vacation rentals for up to seven guests. Its coveted address puts you in proximity to Forsyth Park, the heart of the Historic District. From evening strolls to morning coffee, you’ll enjoy all the wonders of Savannah without the worries of how to get around. Most of the area is walkable from The Parsonage.

The space
Your Savannah Vacation Rentals Accommodation



Turn down the quiet, tree-lined street to The Parsonage at Forsyth. As you approach, the covered front porch and carved pillars will work their unique charm, beckoning you in. In the formal foyer, you'll find gleaming hardwood floors and a graceful, curved staircase.



A spacious living room on the main floor provides plenty of comfortable seating, an entertainment center and a ceiling fan. A lovely period fireplace and antique mirror add an elegant ambiance to the space. Several windows provide a wash natural light. The sofa opens into a bed if needed.



Step through the open archway to the formal dining room. Pocket doors provide privacy as needed. The finely wrought wood dining set offers chairs for six beneath a vintage chandelier. The craftsman fireplace and cute café shutters on the windows suffuse your communal meals with nostalgic charm. You’ll love the antique buffet with leaded glass and the plush oriental carpet.



The galley-style kitchen has space enough for cooking plus a clever breakfast nook for sharing morning muffins or a midday snack. A gas range, microwave, dishwasher and built-in refrigerator provide the tools you’ll need for gourmet creations. All our Savannah vacation homes are fully stocked with dishes, glassware, cookware and flatware for your use throughout your stay.



The back door leads you to a shared brick courtyard where lush greenery frames bistro seating. A formal fountain provides a bit of water music for your intimate tete-a-tetes and “me” time.



Upstairs, the second floor is a dedicated sleeping space. The first bedroom has a queen-sized bed with an intricately carved blanket chest at the end. A pair of corner windows illuminate an easy chair and cast the antique fireplace in a lovely light. Bedside tables with reading lamps and a ceiling fan cater to your comfort level.



The second bedroom boasts a cushy queen-sized bed flanked with nightstands and lamps. The original fireplace features a gleaming peacock-style screen, and a personal-sized TV keeps you entertained while you wind down at night.



A pretty daybed furnishes the third bedroom, suitable for a single adult or child. An overstuffed chair, wall-mounted lamp and lacy window dressings add elegant touches.



Two designer-detailed full bathrooms plus a half bath ensure that there’s never a queue for tooth-brushing. All our Savannah vacation rentals include a washer-dryer for guest use plus a generous supply of towels and bed linens. As a one of our deluxe vacation rentals Savannah GA, The Parsonage at Forsyth also offers dedicated parking, air conditioning/heating, and Wi-Fi.



Things to Do In Savannah’s Historic District



The Parsonage is one of a handful of Savannah vacation homes near Forsyth Park. Established in the 1840s, Forsyth covers a full 30 acres of recreational space, so you and your crew are sure to find plenty of activities to try throughout your stay. Some of the park's most popular features include:



    The Forsyth Fountain, a frequent backdrop for small weddings and photo ops, built in 1858

    Fragrant garden for the blind

    Forsyth Farmers Market, open every Saturday year-round

    Tennis and basketball courts

    Playing fields

    Walking paths

    Children’s play areas





You’ll also find plenty of nearby restaurants and snack stations to fuel your family and friends as you enjoy your stay. Start your morning at The Sentient Bean at 13 E. Park Ave. for gourmet coffee and an all-day breakfast, soup, sandwich and salad menu.



Local 11 Ten, just a block south of the park, is a great place to get fresh seafood, wine and more. If you’re just plain tuckered out after a lively day in the park, stop by the Forsyth Café for refreshments. The menu for this onsite venue covers favorites for kids and grownups alike, plus snacks and beverages.



When you want to sample some of the city’s most popular shops, simply catch the free shuttle bus at one of several stops along the circumference of the park. You can get on or off at any of the Historic District’s 22 town squares too. Alight at City Market or River Street for local artwork, candy, clothes and souvenir shopping.



As a city that celebrates throughout the year, Savannah also offers lots of festivals and events. One may coincide with your time at vacation rentals Savannah GA, such as:



    The Savannah Film Festival – October

    Martin Luther King Jr. Parade – January

    Savannah Irish Festival and Greening of the Fountain – March

    Savannah Jazz Festival – September
