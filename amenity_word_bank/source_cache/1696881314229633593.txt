The space
Welcome to Majestic Garden Retreat, a charming and thoughtfully curated carriage house tucked away in the heart of Savannah’s Historic District. Surrounded by lush greenery and classic Southern charm, this hideaway blends cozy elegance with the timeless character that makes Savannah one of the South’s most beloved destinations.

Recently refreshed with warm, inviting interiors and eclectic touches, the home is designed to feel both elevated and comfortable — the perfect setting for a romantic getaway, weekend escape, or extended stay in the Hostess City.

Step through the shared courtyard entrance, that is covered in beautiful greenery, make your way inside to your own peaceful Savannah retreat. Once inside, you'll find a well-equipped kitchenette with that offers a conventional microwave and mini-fridge, yet still offers everything you need to enjoy a quiet meal at home before heading out to explore the Historic District.

The cozy dining nook overlooks the lush garden courtyard and creates the perfect place to sip morning coffee, enjoy takeout from a local favorite, or unwind with a movie after a day wandering Savannah’s cobblestone streets and historic squares.

Downstairs, you’ll also find a convenient powder room along with a washer and dryer, making the space ideal for both short visits and longer stays.

Upstairs, the serene bedroom features a comfortable queen bed, soft natural light, and a full bathroom with a walk-in shower. French doors open onto a spacious private deck overlooking the garden courtyard below — a peaceful outdoor escape perfect for reading, lounging beneath the moss-draped trees, or enjoying a quiet glass of wine in the evening air. If you'd like to escape to the lush garden, the courtyard is shared amongst the property with all guests, but you're welcome to utilize the space for a quiet place to relax.

Whether you’re visiting Savannah for its history, architecture, food scene, or simply to slow down and recharge, Majestic Garden Retreat offers an unforgettable stay in one of the city’s most charming settings.
