A short walk from everything in Savannah's famed Historic District, Lavender on Perry offers an amazing way to spend some time in the sweetest and prettiest Southern city of them all! The space
Lavender on Perry Offers a Window on Savannah

When arriving at Lavender on Perry, the first thing you'll notice is the home's cheerful violet exterior. There is also a small covered landing with rails leading to the door. The property offers direct sidewalk access and off-street parking for one vehicle.



A Look Inside One of the Top Savannah Vacation Homes for Location

When entering Lavender on Perry, you'll find a lovely entryway nook for keeping shoes and umbrellas. Next, enter a cozy and inviting living room featuring a full-sized couch, coffee table, mounted flat-screen television, fireplace and recliner. A set of large windows on the front wall of the living room will invite in natural light as you recline. You'll notice that the home's main living area is tastefully decorated. In addition, rustic hardwood floors pair elegantly with gray walls and white molding.



Guests of this home can enjoy a round table with dining for four positioned in front of a large window in the main living area. This dining area leads to the home's petite kitchen. Inside this kitchen is where you'll find modern white cabinets topped in granite. The kitchen is fully decked out with new fixtures and appliances in stainless steel. Guests can enjoy a refrigerator, dishwasher, oven and microwave. A drip coffee maker is also provided. The kitchen is where you'll also find a full-sized washer with dryer.



A door situated in the kitchen allows for easy access in and out of the home to the private courtyard. Relax on the private patio made of beautiful brickwork. The patio area is fully fenced with shade provided by mature trees.



The dual queen bedrooms inside Lavender on Perry offer gentle, relaxing sleeping spaces. Both rooms feature two night tables, windows, ceiling fan, wall mounted flat-screen television, and a closet for keeping all your clothes organized as you unpack and unwind on your vacation.



The full bathroom of the home is an elegant space located upstairs off the hallway, and features a large vanity with sink, large wall-mounted mirror and tiled tub with shower. There is also a smaller half bathroom off the living area downstairs featuring a pedestal sink. The half and full bathrooms in the home feature crisp, white accents throughout.



For those seeking vacation rentals Savannah GA visitors can enjoy, Lavender on Perry is a fresh home with many classic touches to enrich your Southern experience. Guests can enjoy modern conveniences like Wi-Fi and a laundry area. In addition, generous ceiling fans and windows throughout the home are ideal for creating soothing breezes.



Things to See and Do When Staying at One of the Best Savannah Vacation Rentals

Lavender on Perry is one of the top Savannah vacation homes due to its location within walking distance of nearly all of Savannah's most notable attractions. That means that occupants of the property can walk to many lush parks, historic monuments, shopping districts and cultural attractions. Of course, the home's cheery, bright exterior gives it a signature Savannah look that borrows from the famed painted porches of the Savannah Historic District.



A polished, complete Savannah itinerary should include key points like Forsyth Park, Bonaventure Cemetery, the Sorrel Weed House, the Mercer Williams House Museum and the American Prohibition Museum. Savannah's River Street is always a highlight for anyone looking for lively pubs, live music and fun crowds. In addition to guided walking tours, trolley and riverboat tours are available for giving you new perspectives on a very old city. Savannah is also famed for its haunted tours that take place after dark! If you're seeking vacation rentals Savannah GA visitors can stay at during the city's many events, your calendar is sure to be full. Lavender on Perry truly is your window on the world that is Savannah. This is an ideal property for anyone looking for a cozy home that can comfortably fit up to four guests.
