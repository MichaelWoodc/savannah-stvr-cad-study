=== FULL DESCRIPTION ===
Southern Comfort Too - Experience a true bohemian escape located on the charming, historic side of Huntingdon Street in Savannah. Up to four Lucky guests will call this bright and airy 2-bedroom "home sweet home".

The space
Southern Comfort Too - Experience a true bohemian escape located on the charming, historic side of Huntingdon Street in Savannah. Up to four Lucky guests will call this bright and airy 2-bedroom "home sweet home".

Enter into the foyer on the first level of this bohemian, chic, humble abode. The actual living space is situated on the second floor of this property, above the ground floor unit. Upstairs you will find the main living space that offers an ample amount of seating, and a large Flatscreen TV. The dining room that is open to the main living space, can sit up to four Lucky guests. The kitchen sits just off of the back of the home and has all the modern conveniences you could wish for, complete with marble countertops, dishwasher, fridge, and stovetop, ideal for extended stays. A designer's dream bathroom is situated conveniently between the main living space and kitchen with hand-laid marble tile, a generous sized walk-in shower and a high-end double vanity.

Off of the kitchen in the back of the home, you will find a large private porch and large daybed. Right off of the courtyard space, you will conveniently find your two private parking spaces. Facing Huntingdon Street, you'll find the two well-situated bedrooms both with spacious closets. The King size bedroom offers a desk, large dresser, two closets and a flatscreen TV - A true primary suite. The Queen size bedroom  has plenty of flare, with a solid wood dresser and generous wall mirror.

Sleeping Configuration:
Bedroom 1: King bed
Bedroom 2: Queen bed

What is parking like for this home? 
Guests can take advantage of the two private parking spaces located behind the home of this property.

We can welcome your dog companions at this pet friendly home, but ask that you find other arrangements for cats, birds, and other furry friends. There is a $130 pet fee (non-refundable) with a 35 lb. maximum weight limit per dog and a limit of 2 dogs per reservation. Some homes may allow larger dogs, please inquire for more information.
