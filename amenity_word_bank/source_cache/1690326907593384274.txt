Built in 1899 and thoughtfully restored in 2026, this historic Victorian retreat blends timeless Savannah charm with modern comfort.

Located just a 5 mins walk from Forsyth Park in the Historic Victorian District, the home features curated vintage décor, heart pine floors, and rich character throughout. 

Enjoy coffee on the side porch, unwind in the private hot tub under string lights, or gather for dinner in the backyard. This home was designed for memorable group in the heart of Savannah.

The space
Step inside and immediately be welcomed by dramatic wallpaper details, original architectural charm, and carefully curated interiors inspired by Savannah’s historic elegance.

Located only 2 blocks from Forsyth Park, you’ll be within walking distance of Savannah’s best cafés, restaurants, grocery stores, and historic landmarks while still enjoying a peaceful residential setting.
