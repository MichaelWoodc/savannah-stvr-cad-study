=== FULL DESCRIPTION ===
Make our Savannah vacation homes an integral part of your plans to experience the South's most hospitable city. We provide you with everything you need to feel right at home so that you can focus on immersing yourself in the sights, flavors and sounds of this vibrant city. Crawford Estate is one of our premium properties, ideally situated in the midst of Savannah's most historic neighborhoods.

The space
The Accommodations



The neat front porch and brick exterior of the Crawford Estate belies the deluxe accommodations you will find within. A four-bedroom, 4.5-bathroom property with a private courtyard, dedicated parking and a whopping 3,108 square feet of living space, you won't find any other Savannah vacation homes that can compare.



An open floor plan on the main level, built-in bookcases and a decorative fireplace in the living room, formal dining plus a light-washed breakfast nook are some of the sumptuous features that greet you as you first walk in the door. Professionally decorated to make the most of light and color, the Crawford accommodations seem to be straight from the glossy pages of House Beautiful.



The main floor also contains a fully outfitted kitchen, which includes lovely glass-doored cupboards, a gas range, microwave, roomy refrigerator and a countertop coffee maker. The drawers and cabinets are filled with all of the dishes, glassware, cookware and utensils you might need to prepare gourmet meals for your entire crew.



Just off the breakfast room, a generous, covered back porch offers a place to wind down with your favorite beverage as the air begins to cool. Just beyond, your private, brick-lined courtyard awaits with verdant landscaping and privacy.



On the second floor, two bedrooms, each with its own private bathroom, offer accommodations for four. The master suite features a fabulous king-sized sleigh bed, bedside tables with lamps, a dresser with a flat-screen TV and a desk for your computer. The attached full-sized bath boasts a romantic jetted tub for two. The second bedroom has a bath with a glass-doored shower.



Large windows keep the atmosphere light and airy. A wealth of closet space makes it easy to stow your clothing, luggage and other gear out of view yet within convenient reach.



The third floor is an adorable loft with two queen beds. This aerie includes its own spacious rooftop balcony with cushy seating plus an ensuite bath with a shower. Don't be surprised if the members in your group vie for this prized space.



Central air and heating plus ceiling fans throughout enable you to customize your home away from home to suit your comfort level. In addition, there is a laundry area for your use.



The Crawford Carriage House is also included in the rental, which is another reason this accommodation is such a rarity among vacation rentals Savannah GA. This well-organized studio apartment has eye-catching bleached wood floors, a cozy living room with a television and DVD player, and a place for you to use your laptop.



The full kitchen includes a handy breakfast bar, a range, refrigerator, French press coffee maker and a microwave. Of course, like all Savannah vacation rentals, the Carriage House kitchen is fully stocked with a full complement of culinary tools.



The boudoir area provides you with a queen bed, a nightstand and lamp, and a rocking chair. The bathroom opens off this space and features a contemporary glass-doored shower.



Sights and Activities near Vacation Rentals Savannah GA



When you stay at the Crawford Estate, you are only a few steps from one of the Savannah Historic District's town squares. Crawford Square is a great place to stroll, people watch, and take a peaceful break from your busy itinerary.



One feature that distinguishes Crawford Square from the other 21 squares in the city is the fact that it is fenced. Although all of the squares originally had fences, Crawford has the only one remaining. This east-side square was established in 1841, with the surrounding neighborhood taking shape around the mid-century. Nearby, you can tour the Colonial Cemetery and historic police barracks dating to the 1870s.



Catch the free Historic District shuttle bus at Crawford Square, and venture farther afield around the city. If your visit includes a Saturday, be sure to head to Forsyth Park's weekly farmer's market. You can find fresh produce, artisan baked goods, music and plenty of other visitors from vacation rentals Savannah GA.



While there, stroll over to the fabulous Forsyth fountain, the park's centerpiece. You'll likely happen upon a wedding ceremony taking place in front of the spectacular backdrop. If you have children in your group, they'll love the two playgrounds designed specifically for the very young and the school-aged sets.



Jump back on the shuttle bus and ride down to the Savannah River waterfront, a great place to watch the big cargo ships come and go. You will also find plenty of unique gift shops, mouth-watering restaurants and open-air seating areas. At the eastern end of River Street, stop by the famous Waving Girl Statue and Olympic Monument.



Check with our staff when reserving Savannah vacation rentals to find out about local festivals and events that coincide with your stay. As a city that celebrates the entire year round, Savannah is always hosting great events, from the SCAD Sidewalk Arts Festival in April to the Savannah Craft Brew Fest in September. You'll likely find one that appeals to you.
