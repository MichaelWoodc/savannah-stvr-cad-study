=== FULL DESCRIPTION ===
Gather in the heart of Savannah on iconic Jones Street in this historic mariner cottage duplex. Guests enjoy the entire property — two private condos offering 4 bedrooms, 2 bathrooms, 2 full kitchens, multiple living spaces, a large dining room, balcony, deck, and courtyard. Perfect for groups who want to stay together while still enjoying plenty of private space and authentic Savannah charm. Walkable to all of the incredible sites our city has to offer!

SVR 03180 and 03179

The space
This historic mariner cottage duplex offers the entire property — two private condos in one historic building perfect for families, friends, groups, or special getaways.

Together, the home includes 4 bedrooms, 2 bathrooms, 2 full kitchens, multiple living spaces (one large living room on the first floor and one smaller den on the second floor, both of which have pull-out sofas), and a large dining room on the second floor designed for gathering and spending time together while still allowing everyone to enjoy their own private space and comfort.

The first-floor condo features two bedrooms (one king & one queen), one bathroom, a spacious, central living room, full kitchen, and access to the deck and courtyard. Upstairs, the second condo spans the second and third floors and includes a bedroom with king bed, a loft-style bedroom (two twin beds) up on the third-floor, spacious dining room, full kitchen, cozy den, bathroom, and balcony (with spiral staircase to make getting to all of the outdoor spaces below a breeze)! 

Historic architectural details and old Savannah charm have been thoughtfully preserved throughout both condos, creating a warm and authentic atmosphere full of character. Guests most enjoy the courtyard with seating, picnic table, and grill — perfect for relaxing after a day exploring downtown.

Located in Savannah’s Landmark Historic District, you’ll be steps away from beautiful squares, restaurants, shops, parks, and riverfront attractions while staying on one of the city’s most famous and picturesque streets.

Guest access
The entire duplex is yours! There are no shared spaces when you book this listing, as you are reserving the entire building, including all outdoor spaces, for yourself!

Other things to note
Please note! We tried to maintain many of the original architectural features, including the four fireplace hearths. However, these are NON-OPERATIONAL (thus, not listed as a fireplace amenity), and are just for aesthetic appeal and a bit of old-world charm!
