=== FULL DESCRIPTION ===
The Georgia Peach is a chic and luxe loft located in the heart of historic downtown! It’s close to popular restaurants, shopping, and Plant Riverside. Being walking distance to everything, you will be able to enjoy all Savannah has to offer! The loft has two bedrooms and boasts fun and unique  artwork throughout. With all the personal touches, you will feel right at home. Don’t miss out on this opportunity to experience Savannah in this amazing space and location!
