=== FULL DESCRIPTION ===
This Airey-inspired Townhome brings timeless 1940s architecture that provides an inviting and comfortable stay in Savannah's historic district. Featuring two bedrooms, one and a half bathrooms, and a beautiful shared courtyard, this townhome is the perfect place to experience southern living for four guests. With original hardwood floors, high ceilings, and splashes of color, you enjoy every minute of your stay. Just 10 minutes from Forsyth Park, a short drive to the riverwalk!

The space
Enter The Tea Olive A with a simple step up from the brick-laid sidewalks on Taylor Street. Enjoy a roomy living area with oversized sofas and a smart TV to stream your favorite shows. Flowing from front to back of the primary floor, you will find a freshly updated kitchen with butcher block countertops and a significant splash of color. The kitchen has everything you need to cook, including pots, pans, dishware, and more. There is also a half bath right there in the hallway. 
Brew your morning coffee in the kitchen and step out into the shared courtyard to enjoy a large deck, patio furniture, and plenty of greenery. Whether early morning or evening, the courtyard is a wonderful place to enjoy beautiful Savannah weather.
Head upstairs to find two bedrooms and a full bathroom. Each bedroom features a queen mattress with crisp linens and plush pillows. Large windows and high ceilings fill the rooms with natural light. Closets and dressers provide ample storage for extended stays.
The bathrooms are outfitted with a combination tub/shower, vanity, toilet, and all essentials, including toilet paper, towels, and a hair dryer.
Are you working remotely during your stay? All homes have high-speed Wi-Fi and ample room to set up your home office.  
Love this townhome but need more space? You can rent the neighboring townhome and backyard cottage as a single reservation hosting up to 16 guests. Check out our separate listing for the availability of all three units together.

Neighborhood highlights
Tea Olive A is located on the east side of the Historic District, just minutes from Forsyth Park, Mother Matilda Beasley Park (with a dog park!), and many of Savannah’s best attractions.
Start your day by grabbing your morning coffee and breakfast just a couple blocks down at Carly’s Café and enjoying a park bench in Calhoun Square or Whitefield Square.
Enjoy excellent dining options by heading towards the riverwalk and stopping at The Olde Pink House for fine southern dining. Grab some laid-back bites and brews at Spanky’s Pizza Galley & Saloon. Enjoy delicious southern dining with a river view at Huey’s or Vic’s on the River. A short drive will take you to the River Walk and City Market area off Broughton Street. With endless shopping, sites, and eateries, you’ll find adventure at every corner.
Start your bar/pub tour by stopping into some staples of Savannah, including Six Pence Pub, O’Connell’s Irish Pub, or Dub’s Public House.
Explore the rich history of Savannah by stopping into one of the many museums and historical sites. Just minutes from Blue on Broad are the Web Military Museum, Telfair Museums, Owens-Thomas House & Slave Quarters, and the American Prohibition Museum.
A stop in Savannah wouldn’t be complete without a walk-through of Forsyth Park, just a 20-minute walk away. Or enjoy Washington Square, another beautiful green space just minutes away.
Want to take it all in at once while riding or biking? We are just minutes from Old Town Trolley Tours of Savannah and Savannah Bike Tours.
Getting around is easy with walkable streets, bike rentals, and Uber service. Jump on Savannah’s public transit system with the Price & Gordon SB bus stop just a 3-minute walk from the townhome.
Ready for a little on-the-water adventure? Try out the Savannah Belles Ferries to see the beautiful skyline of Savannah from the riverfront.
