A spacious Victorian four blocks east of Forsyth Park with off street parking for one car. We pay the full 15.5% Airbnb service fee on behalf of guests.

Spacious Victorian just four blocks east of Forsyth Park, on East Park Avenue. 1.3 miles to River Street with off street parking for one car.

Maximum 4 people allowed

This is a duplex, and the lower unit is also a short term vacation rental. Check out Savannah Joy at: www.airbnb. com/h/savannah-joy

Located in the south historic Victorian District just 4 blocks east of Forsyth Park and 1.3 miles from the river with huge vaulted ceilings, numerous windows allowing lots of natural light, transom windows above every door and adorable decorative fire places throughout; this southern retreat, built in the year Nineteen Thirteen for J.J. Waring, welcomes you in true historic Savannah style. Off Street parking for one car is available in the back of the house. There is also free street parking in front, and on the side of the house.
You have a front porch and small back yard, both shared with the lower unit and a small private back porch. Two bedrooms with queen size beds. There is also a small bedroom with a twin size bed. A firm MAXIMUM of 4 guests is allowed as we have only one bathroom and noise travels to the lower unit. A living room, dining room and a fully equipped kitchen. One full bath. 
Please note that the front and back stairs to this apartment are steep.
Please note: We are on the corner of a through street so you will hear typical city street traffic. If you are a light sleeper, you may not be comfortable here.

Guest access
There is a keypad door lock at the front door for easy checkin and no fussing with keys. A fully equipped kitchen, washer & dryer, dishwasher, iron, blow dryer, internet, dvd player, movies and games and beach chairs and towels are available for your convenience.
