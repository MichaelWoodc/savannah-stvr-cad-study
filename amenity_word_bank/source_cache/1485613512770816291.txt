=== FULL DESCRIPTION ===
Oglethorpe Square, 1856. Horse carriages still pass daily.

This is Bosch Huis Rosé: six suites, every key, the whole house to yourselves.

Skip the car: River Street one way, Broughton shops the other, Michelin-worthy tables between.

Out back: an infrared sauna, a cold plunge, city bikes, and the best part of your morning. All yours.

Loved like a home, because it is one.

Bosch Huis began as a dream. Guests like you make it real. Tap the heart, see my Host Profile, or book it.

The space
BOSCH HUIS ROSÉ — THE WHOLE HOUSE
Six suites, three floors, one booking. On the ground floor, the Garden Suite sleeps four across two queen bedrooms — the coolest, quietest sleep in the house — and the Rosé Suite puts a king at the front, where the square starts outside the window. On the main floor, the Zee Suite pairs a king with big windows and a hanging chair everyone claims first, while the Lila Suite hides a spa bathroom nearly the size of its cozy king bedroom. And the top floor is a world of its own: a king under Sorbet's famous pink walls, a queen in Kiwi — one of the largest rooms in the house — and a living room between them with an oversized sleeper sofa and a 75-inch screen for the last movie of the night.

Every suite keeps its own Nespresso machine, mini-fridge, microwave, and smart TV. Book the house, and the doors between worlds are all yours.

MAIN LEVEL
A long hallway leads to the kitchen, lined with art collected over the years. Local artists, European finds, antique market treasures. Free snacks along the way, and a photo wall of past guests. Add yourselves — a whole group photo, this time.

The kitchen is chef-grade: gas range, All-Clad, Vitamix, KitchenAid, a Samsung refrigerator with filtered water and ice. Usually it's shared among suites. On a whole-house stay, it's simply yours — cook the big dinner, claim the long table, make the morning loud.

OUT FRONT
All of Savannah, one minute at a time: Broughton Street's shops in one, the squares, galleries, and bars in the same, the Riverfront in five, Forsyth Park in ten.

This is the booking the house was made for. Proposals, milestone birthdays, a bachelorette with every key on one lanyard, a family finally under one roof. Write to us before booking for anything beyond a quiet stay — gatherings are approved in advance through Airbnb, with their own guidelines.

THE HOUSE
This home has stood on Oglethorpe Square since 1856, and it acts its age in the best ways: heart-pine floors that announce your footsteps, stairs that have carried 170 years of guests, walls that hold stories and, occasionally, sound. Book it whole and every creak is yours — no neighbors, no shared anything. It's not a hotel built last year, and that's the point.

THE ASSURANCES
Smoke detectors throughout. First aid stocked. Cameras outside only — never inside. The same small cleaning team, every stay. If maintenance ever touches your dates, you'll hear it from us first.

ALWAYS IMPROVING
Nearly every dollar this home generates is reinvested — into the property, our team, and the local community. We're passionate about historic houses, love Savannah wholeheartedly, and it shows in what keeps getting better.

A SAVANNAH NOTE
This is the South, in a walking city that's been alive since 1733. Church bells ring, carriages clatter, and on weekend nights downtown hums. A professional pest team treats the property regularly, but nature predates the service contract. That's the habitat, not the housekeeping.
