=== FULL DESCRIPTION ===
A luxurious historic 1897 home with 3 bedrooms and lush outdoor oasis. Perfect for SCAD parents, traveling professionals & history buffs.

The space
Welcome to Shain Manor! This Victorian Era home built in 1897 has been completely redone into a spectacular home away from home.

The Shain Manor Full House is a 3-story home with 3 bedrooms & 3.5 baths. We also rent the upper and lower floors individually (see separate listings for Garden Suite & Parlor Suite).

Upon entering, you will be welcomed by an abundance of natural light  and Guests are treated to 2 newly remodeled kitchens and 2 elegant living rooms! Offering many places for hanging out and relaxing, watching movies, playing games or just having some quiet time.

The ground level has an open floor plan, with a living room, new kitchen, dining area, and a bedroom with a queen-sized bed, with a new mattress and luxury cotton linens. Additionally, there is a full en-suite bathroom, with a tub/shower combo & laundry closet with a full-size washer/dryer.

Upstairs, on the 2nd floor, you will find a large living room, separate dining room, and a Chef’s kitchen that offers a full array of Thermador appliances including a professional 36” gas stove with griddle & oven, 30” refrigerator/freezer, Scotsman gourmet clear ice machine, wine and beverage fridge, microwave drawer, a countertop Breville toaster oven & toaster and a Cuisinart pod-coffee maker. The sink has a reverse-osmosis room temperature & boiling water filtration system. The kitchen is completely equipped for all your cooking needs. There is also a powder room, designed with intricate Carrara marble tile around the corner.

On the 3rd Floor, in the parlor landing there is a private game table, for those interested in a game of chess, checkers or backgammon. There is also a comfy chaise you can use to read, lounge in or work on your laptop. 

Additionally, there are two additional bedrooms and two bathrooms on the 3rd level. The primary suite features a king-sized Ralph Lauren-inspired bed with luxury cotton linens and pillows, chaise and a large desk. The en-suite bathroom has a marble double vanity with two sinks and a large walk-in shower with a hand-held shower head. The spacious master closet includes a full-size washer/dryer.

The 3rd floor second bedroom has 2 full size beds, also with brand new mattresses and luxury, cotton linens. There is a chair and ottoman for relaxing under the bespoke artwork made with local flora. This room has a closet and dresser for plenty of storage as well as a small desk for private moments to write or work. 

The pièce de résistance is the 2-story outdoor oasis! The European-inspired, ground floor courtyard features a painted mural wall, comfy seating and lush greenery.  Up the circular staircase is a large breezy deck located right off the kitchen! There is a gas grill & gas firepit for guests while enjoying the neighborhood sounds of church bells, horses clomping, ships’ horns and views of fireworks from the river during holidays.

Located between Columbia & Greene Squares, Shain Manor is on the corner of York & Price Streets. We are a 5-minute walk to River Street; 4 blocks to the retail mecca of Broughton Street & walkable to all the must-see sights!
