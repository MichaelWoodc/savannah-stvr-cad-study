=== FULL DESCRIPTION ===
Spend a little time wrapped in the luxury and beauty of Crawford Chic. Standing out among Savannah vacation rentals for its sprawling layout, Crawford Chic sleeps six overnight guests comfortably. The home pampers guests with a large kitchen, bright & airy atmosphere, 2.5 bathrooms, generous bedrooms, gated parking and serene porch made of the stuff of Southern-style dreams! Take a look inside one of the Savannah vacation homes perfect for making life in the city feel like a dream!

The space
Light, airy and carefree: Crawford Chic meets all your fondest vacation expectations and then some. Conveniently located near Crawford Square, one of the Historic District’s original greenspaces, this prime example of Savannah vacation homes is close to everything too. You’ll feel like royalty when the free shuttle bus stops right outside your door to whisk you away to dining, shopping and sightseeing destinations, all within minutes of your lovely home base. You can even venture farther afield to enjoy our heated swimming pool, reserved exclusively for Savannah vacation rentals guests. Whether your ideal vacation includes shooting a few hoops, strolling our unique Southern gardens or enjoying the city’s cultural events, it’s all here waiting for you in and around your Crawford Chic accommodations.



What’s Inside



This spacious walk-up condominium sleeps six guests, with two large bedrooms and three bathrooms. Charming, shuttered windows and a neutral color scheme usher you in the front door. The living room features comfortable seating, a ceiling fan and a smart TV. Feel free to sink into a state of bliss as you relax, converse or catch up on the news after an action-packed day in Savannah.



The open floor plan also includes a dining area just beyond the living room. Chandelier-style lighting, wall art and a soft area rug help delineate the space. The farmhouse table provides seating for four. You’ll also find a desk and chair when you want to sit down and use your laptop. Crawford Chic provides your entire group with Wi-Fi on demand.



Next to the dining room, the full kitchen features a granite-topped island, complete with breakfast bar seating. When it’s your turn to cook, you can still enjoy visiting with friends as you work. There is a refrigerator, range-oven, dishwasher and microwave for your use. We fill the drawers and cupboards at our vacation rentals Savannah GA with tableware, glasses and cookware to ensure that you have everything you need. You’ll even find a handy powder room just off the kitchen, complete with laundry facilities.



Step out the back door to enjoy the balcony overlooking Crawford Square. Enjoy coffee in the morning and iced beverages in the evenings at the bistro table, and watch the world go by. It’s a great way to soak up the sounds and scents of your bustling Savannah neighborhood from a bird’s eye view.



Take the gracious staircase upstairs when bedtime nears. There, you’ll discover two master suites that each come with their own private baths. The first bedroom boasts a king-sized bed that benefits from plenty of natural light. A dresser, ceiling fan and smart TV cater to your storage, comfort and entertainment needs.



The adjoining bathroom features a glassed-in shower with ample room for two, granite countertops and plenty of drawer space. We supply our Savannah vacation homes guests with lots of soft, absorbent towels for a luxurious bathing experience throughout their stay.



The second master suite has a king-sized bed, an abundance of windows and a double closet. The private bath features a tub/shower nook.



In addition, this great accommodation offers two twin-sized mattresses for two more guests. It also includes private, gated parking for up to two vehicles, heating and air conditioning, keypad entry and outdoor lighting. Don’t forget your exclusive access to our Tybee Island pool. It’s a great place to cool off and meet guests from other Savannah vacation rentals.



Day Trip to Wormsloe Plantation



If you brought your car to Savannah, take a short but scenic drive to the Isle of Hope, just 11 miles from downtown. This 2 by 4-mile peninsula is the site of the Wormsloe historic site. Built in the 18th century by one of Savannah’s founders, Noble Jones, the 822-acre estate features a long avenue shaded by live oaks. You'll also find the remains of the original plantation house and eight miles of scenic trails.



Construction of the Wormsloe manse relied on the labor of indentured servants, since at the time, slavery was banned throughout the state of Georgia. When lawmakers lifted the ban in 1749, Jones brought in slaves to work the plantation. He grew food crops and tried to establish a silk operation by planting mulberry trees for the worms, but that venture never took off.



Wormsloe became an official historic site in the 1970s, with the state of Georgia as the conservator. The grounds contain a museum, outbuildings including a wattle-and-daub hut, and an interpretive trail.



If you visit Wormsloe, make a day of it with a stop at the nearby Shops at Sandfly just a couple of minutes down the road. Depending on the time of day, you can stop by Cutter’s Point Coffee shop for a quick pick-me-up or dine on fresh seafood at the Driftaway Café. Both are within a short drive of the Wormsloe historic site.



There are plenty of great activities, venues and eateries nearby Crawford Chic too. Take a walking tour of the area’s town squares. Each one has its own attractions.



Nearby Chippewa Square boasts a theater and a museum while Lafayette Square stands in the shadow of the ornate St. John the Baptist Cathedral. This edifice is a must-see for many of our vacation rentals Savannah GA guests.
