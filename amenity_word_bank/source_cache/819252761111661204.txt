=== FULL DESCRIPTION ===
Discover the charm of Savannah's history in our recently renovated 2BR/1BA apartment, located in the heart of downtown.  Enjoy modern amenities, including updated appliances and furniture, in a beautifully decorated space with fun accents. 

Only three blocks from Forsyth Park, you'll have easy access to food, bars, parks, and events.  Large windows provide plenty of natural light and brighten up the rooms.  Book your stay now and immerse yourself in the best of Savannah's downtown culture!

The space
SVR-02968 

Don’t hesitate to reserve this beautifully decorated and furnished apartment!

This might be a new listing, but we have a solid track record of providing high-quality spaces and great experiences.  Book in confidence!

Guest access
Guests will have access to the apartment, the private upstairs porch, the assigned parking spot (B) behind the building and the street parking across the building.  Please refer to the check-in guide on the app for more details.
 
*Note:  The upstairs apartment is in a duplex with a bottom apartment, so we ask that the space is treated with dignity and respect.  Please keep noise to a minimum and respect the quiet hours of 8pm to 8am.  Parties and events are not allowed at anytime.

Other things to note
This is an upstairs apartment and you must climb 20 steps to reach the top.  You will enter the apartment using the door on the right (B).

The front door does not auto-lock.  Please take a moment to ensure the deadbolt is locked as you come and go.  From the outside, simply press the “Schlage” oval and turn the deadbolt.  Give it a tug afterward to ensure it is locked.  From the inside, simply turn the deadbolt.

The parking area is found at the back of the property. It has ONE assigned parking spot for your apartment (B).  This area is shared with the downstairs apartment (A), which is also allocated one parking spot.  Please park in the correct parking spot.  You can park any additional vehicles on the street in front of the property.

Please be aware that even though we spray for bugs on a quarterly basis, you might see palmetto bugs, wasps, and other insects during your stay.  They usually come out to die, but please do your best to keep them out of the house by keeping the windows and doors shut at all times, only bringing food items to the dining room, and keeping trash in the trash can.

After a year of being idle, construction on the property next door has resumed and is expected to continue through Winter 2023.  Current noise levels have not been an issue for guests, but we want to make sure you are aware as you book. But feel free to look at our reviews, as you will see, it has not impacted the stays and our guest’s experience.  Noise machines and earplugs are provided to help mitigate this situation.

Neighborhood highlights
You will not want to move out after staying here! Hurry and reserve your stay!

When you stay in this apartment, you will be living in some of Savannah's most desirable real estate - right in the heart of Downtown! Forsyth Park is only steps from your downtown property - simply turn right at the sidewalk, walk three blocks, and you will find yourself at the famous fountain!

Having a car is unnecessary when living here - you can walk, ride, or bike anywhere in this part of town. Convenience and grocery stores are just a few minutes' walk from the upstairs apartment - without mentioning the plethora of cafes, bars, and restaurants.

Set off East towards Collin’s Forsyth for breakfast before making your way North along Whitaker Street. Walk through each square to shop local retailers, grab a coffee or quick bite, or park yourself at a bench and just enjoy the Savannah scenery. Those in the mood for a true Southern meal will love being a stone's throw away from Mrs. Wilkes Boarding Room, with other local favorites such as 700 Drayton or Crystal Beer Parlor just a short walk from the apartment.

Popular tourist areas such as River Street and City Market are just a 20-minute walk away, while Savannah's local green space - Forsyth Park - is just three blocks from the front door.
