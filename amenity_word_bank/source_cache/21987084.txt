=== FULL DESCRIPTION ===
This updated Bungalow is in the heart of the Victorian District! Relax on the front or back porch or take a stroll just 3 blocks to Forsyth Park. Built in 1902, you will find that many original details remain. We have made every effort to modernize and make it comfortable, too! Amenities include fast WiFi, cable, and a large flatscreen TV. Our home is located a scenic 20 minute walk or a quick Uber from the busier downtown area. 

SVR-01174

The space
Situated in the Victorian District of Savannah, you would never know that this home was built in 1902 as a two unit bungalow. Two stairs lead up to the front door and then the rest of the condo is one level. Both bedrooms have queen sized beds and luxe linens. Appliances in the fully stocked kitchen are stainless steel, and the quartz counter tops are brand new! The heart pine flooring has been refinished and is truly a historic gem. The layout of this home is great for entertaining, and boasts a large and open living/dining room area.

Guest access
Guests have full access to the unit through a private entrance. Amenities include a washer/dryer, Keurig coffee maker, front AND back porch.

Other things to note
Don't be afraid to reach out if you need anything!  We live super close-by and are always happy to help out, even mid-stay.

Neighborhood highlights
Savannah neighborhoods have experienced a renaissance over the last several years. Turn of the century homes are now being restored to their original grandeur, inviting visitors to Savannah to experience a touch of history.

This home is located in the heart of the Victorian District, which is about a 20 minute scenic walk to the downtown/river area. We are two blocks from Forsyth Park, making it a very easy stroll to their Saturday morning farmer's market. The Victorian District is an inclusive neighborhood of SCAD students, vacationers, and permanent residents. Expect to find everyone enjoying the area well into the evening. Access to the downtown restaurants, nightlife, and sightseeing is also easily accessible by Uber. The local surrounding area offers many gems as well. Finish your day off on either the shared front porch or your private back porch.
