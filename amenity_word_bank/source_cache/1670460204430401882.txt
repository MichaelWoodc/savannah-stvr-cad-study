=== FULL DESCRIPTION ===
This is wonderful space for families or small groups traveling to Savannah. Enjoy a spacious 2BR/2BA garden-level apartment located in the Victorian District. Thoughtfully designed with a comfortable layout, fully equipped kitchen, and inviting living space. Walk to Forsyth Park, restaurants, and shops while enjoying a relaxed, local Savannah stay

The space
Welcome to your Savannah home away from home, this beautifully designed new construction, garden-level apartment offers the perfect blend of space, comfort, and location in the heart of the Historic Districts.

Thoughtfully laid out with two bedrooms and two full bathrooms, this home is ideal for couples, families, or friends traveling together. Each bedroom provides a comfortable, private retreat, while the open living space invites you to relax, gather, and unwind after a day exploring the city.

The fully equipped kitchen and eat-in kitchen area make it easy to enjoy meals at home, while the living room offers a welcoming space to settle in. Designed with a balance of charm and simplicity, the apartment feels both elevated and easy to live in.

Step outside and you’re just a short walk to Forsyth Park, where you can enjoy morning walks beneath the oaks, weekend markets, and one of Savannah’s most iconic landmarks. From there, continue exploring the Historic District’s restaurants, coffee shops, boutiques, and beautiful squares.

Whether you’re here for a weekend getaway or an extended stay, this garden apartment offers a comfortable and convenient home base to experience Savannah like a local.

We can’t wait to host you!

SVR-03090

Guest access
Guests will have full access to the ground level apartment.There is a small front patio that is also available for guest use.

Other things to note
Please note this is a ground level apartment situated below the main home. The main home is also used as a vacation rental and you may hear people above walking around from time to time. As with many homes in Savannah’s Historic District, you may occasionally experience normal city sounds (road noise, emergency vehicles, sirens, trains, ship horns, trash trucks, neighbors, etc). Street parking is available nearby but not guaranteed. We recommend reviewing all listing details prior to booking and reaching out with any questions you may have.
