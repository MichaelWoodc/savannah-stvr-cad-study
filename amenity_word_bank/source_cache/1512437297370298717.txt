=== FULL DESCRIPTION ===
Welcome to this spacious 2 bed, 2 bath carriage house in the heart of Savannah! Inside, you’ll find sun-drenched rooms with tall windows, two cozy king beds, fully renovated bathrooms and kitchen with an open concept living/dining room. The home is centrally located—just steps from Savannah’s squares, shops, and dining—yet tucked away on a quiet street, giving you the perfect balance of walkability and privacy. This rental is perfect for traveling with family or friends!

The space
Tucked behind one of the city’s  more storied streets, this renovated historic carriage house blends timeless charm with modern luxury. With 2 bedrooms and 2 full bathrooms, it’s perfect for families, couples, or friends traveling together. Just steps from Savannah’s famed squares, coffee shops, and boutiques, you’ll be in the heart of the Historic District, yet tucked away in your own private retreat. You will be just a short 5 minute walk to the iconic Forsyth Park.

Living Room – Light-filled with a large bay window, cozy teal sectional that pulls out to a queen sleeper bed, smart TV, and modern touches. Desk area set up for the remote workers. Perfect for relaxing after exploring the Historic District.
Dining Area – Gather around the wood table with seating for six, framed by natural light shining in through a second bay window.
Kitchen – Recently updated with white shaker cabinets, gold hardware, stainless steel appliances, and stunning countertops. Fully stocked for cooking at home, plus a coffee bar with a nespresso  for your morning pick-me-up.
Bedrooms – Two spacious bedrooms with king beds, plush mattresses, fresh linens, soft throws, and large windows that let in plenty of light. Each offers a cozy, modern retreat.
Bathrooms – Freshly updated with stylish vanities, gold fixtures, and tub/shower combos. Stocked with plush towels and essential toiletries.
Extras – High-speed WiFi, washer/dryer, and keyless entry for convenience.

SVR-03168

Guest access
Guests have full private access to the carriage house, including a driveway for easy parking. The home sits tucked behind historic walls, giving you a quiet retreat in the heart of the city. Guests will not have access to the garage.

Other things to note
Please note that guests will have access to one off street parking space. Guests will not have access to the garage. The Carriage is house is multiple levels, you will enter on the the first level and take two a set of stairs up to the second level where you will find the living room, dining room and kitchen. Another set of stairs will take you to the top/third floor where both bedrooms and bathrooms are located. 

 Traffic & noise may increase on major streets, or during festival/peak tourism times—but being on a quieter lane will mitigate that. Parking can be limited downtown, so off-street or private parking becomes a big plus.
