Pet-Friendly Town Home, Historic Charm, Great Location!

Other things to note
Welcome to Waldburg Terrace, located in Savannah's Victorian District, which combines historic elegance with contemporary style. The top two floors of this Victorian row house are home to this stylish three-bedroom townhome with two and a half bathrooms. 



From Waldburg Street, use the front stairs to reach the second-floor two-story apartment. Tall French windows, hardwood floors, and high ceilings add warmth and coziness to the expansive living room, accentuated by the brick wall detail and neutral color palette. The parlor-style living room is blended with old and new. New furnishings and original brick details of the former working fireplaces have been carefully restored to show their original beauty. The living area has a comfortable sofa and large flat-screen television. A dining table for up to six is perfect for sharing meals together. 



Dual entrances lead to the brand-new kitchen. You'll have everything you need to whip up tasty meals on the fly or create culinary masterpieces. The kitchen is fully equipped, with a 6-burner gas range and oven and all stainless steel appliances. A breakfast counter serves as the focal point for guests, and a back door leads to a private sun porch with steps down to the backyard. Below is a stone courtyard with privacy fencing and off-street parking for two cars. 



There are three bedrooms and two bathrooms on the second floor. Bedroom 1 has a king-sized bed, a Smart TV, and another beautiful original fireplace. The newly renovated bathroom features a claw-foot tub within an enclosed shower, offering perfect spa-like relaxation. Bedroom 2 has a King-size bed, a Smart TV and shares a Jack-and-Jill bathroom. Bedroom 3 has a Queen Bed and shares the Jack-and-Jill bathroom with an all-new walk-in shower. 



The first floor includes a powder room half bath and full-size washer and dryer. This townhome has onsite, private parking for two vehicles behind the backyard privacy fence. Nearby parking is on-street and non-metered, making parking a breeze. Park your car and take local transportation on the Dot Shuttle that can be accessed near Forsyth Park. Walk or bike to many nearby amenities, including Forsyth Park. Many events are held at the park throughout the year. It is an excellent choice for picnics, outdoor sports, walking, jogging, and free tennis courts. This is also where you will find the famous Forsyth fountain, which is a popular spot for weddings in Savannah. Additionally, the Forsyth Farmer's Market is a great early Saturday morning event to attend. 



Guests will also be located only a short 20-25 minute drive to Tybee Island, Savannah's beach. Come out for a day trip to enjoy the beach, fishing, visiting Ft. Pulaski, or climbing Georgia's oldest and tallest lighthouse.
