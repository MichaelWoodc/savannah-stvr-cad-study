=== FULL DESCRIPTION ===
This studio carriage house is located off of Gordon Lane, only one block away from the city's largest green space, Forsyth Park. Birds Nest Carriage provides an intimate space for two, ideal for a romantic getaway, or perfect for an extended stay.

The space
Located off of Gordon Lane, this historic brick carriage house sits in the tree-lined residential area of the downtown historic district. You won't compromise location, as this property is only one block from the Fountain at Forsyth Park. Whether booking for a couples getaway, or an extended stay, you'll enjoy your stay at Birds Nest Carriage House.

Upon entering you are greeted by a charming, spiral, wrought iron staircase that leads to the studio. Up-top you will find the unit to be packed with amenities, such as a stove top, fridge, large bathroom, and two spacious closets. You'll feel at home here with a spot to store all your luggage, and a lovely renovated bathroom with a spacious shower  A Queen size bed sits in the middle of the room, with a smart TV at the foot of the bed for you to enjoy after exploring the city all day. 

Guests are also able to enjoy the private tree-lined courtyard, shared with the garden-level tenants. Due North you'll come across many of Savannah's local shops, cafes, and eateries. 

What is parking like for this home?
Parking for this home is on-street and metered, which is enforced by the City of Savannah from Monday through Friday, 8am to 5pm. Guests may conveniently pay using the ParkSavannah app, feed the meter or parking kiosks directly, or park on a nearby non-metered street. Please take note of posted Sweep Zone signs when parking on the street.

We can welcome your dog companions at this pet friendly home, but ask that you find other arrangements for cats, birds, and other furry friends. There is a $130 pet fee (non-refundable) with a 35 lb. maximum weight limit per dog and a limit of 2 dogs per reservation. Some homes may allow larger dogs, please inquire for more information.

Stayed about a week
I recently stayed at this charming apartment and had a generally pleasant experience. The location is fantastic, with nearby free parking (for those who are resourceful) and easy access to restaurants, parks, squares, SCAD, and the historic district. The bed was comfortable, the room clean, and the bathroom spacious with a good hot shower and ample towels. 
However, the apartment's entry is in an alley lined with trashcans and a steep stairway may be challenging for some with mobility issues. Additionally, intrusive light at night affected the ambiance. The kitchen, though equipped with essentials, felt cramped and lacked basic cooking items like salt and pepper and any consumables that might actually help.  The kitchen had limited space and a small fridge.  We did have a few meals (sandwiches, hard boiled eggs, fruit) but that's all you can manage.
Overall, it's a cozy and convenient choice, but guests should consider the accessibility challenges and limited kitchen amenities.
