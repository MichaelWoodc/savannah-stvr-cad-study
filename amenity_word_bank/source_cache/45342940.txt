=== FULL DESCRIPTION ===
Experience historic Savannah from a luxurious 3-story townhome and separate Carriage House in Crawford Square. Perfect for families or groups, this property comfortably sleeps 10. Features include a private courtyard, an elevator, and two private parking spots. Enjoy an authentic local experience in a prime, walkable location just a half-mile from the bustling shops and restaurants of Broughton Street.

The space
Discover the perfect blend of historic charm and modern luxury in this exquisite Savannah property, comprising a main townhome and a separate Carriage House connected by a private courtyard.

Main Townhome (3-Story):

Main Level: A sophisticated living area with a large Smart TV. The fully equipped kitchen boasts a large island with seating for four, a wet bar, and a wine refrigerator. This level opens directly to the private courtyard.

Second Level: Accessible by stairs or a private, wood-paneled elevator, this floor is a dedicated master suite. It features a King bed, a massive walk-in closet, and a luxurious full bathroom with double sinks and a huge walk-in shower.

Third Level: Two more elegant bedrooms reside here—one with a King bed and one with a Queen bed. Each has its own private full bathroom and a full-size closet. A laundry room with a washer and dryer is also conveniently located on this floor.

Carriage House: This quaint and private space offers a complete living experience, including:

A fully equipped kitchen.
A cozy living area with a Queen pull-out sofa bed and a Smart TV.

A private bedroom with a King-size bed.

A full bathroom that also contains a washer and dryer.

Key Amenities:

High-Speed Wi-Fi: 94 Mbps for all your connectivity needs.

Dedicated Workspace: A designated area perfect for remote work.

Family-Friendly: A Pack ’n play/travel crib is available for younger guests.

Entertainment: Smart TVs with live and streaming channels are in all bedrooms.

Guest access
Guests will have exclusive and private access to the entire property, including the main townhome, the separate Carriage House, and the private courtyard that connects them.

For your convenience, you will have access to two private parking spots in the garage. Additional free parking is typically available on the street in front of the main house. Metered on-street parking is also available nearby. Please be mindful of posted street sweeping signs to avoid ticketing.

Other things to note
STVR Compliance: Our home is in full compliance with all Short-Term Vacation Rental (STVR) laws in Savannah, Georgia. Our STVR permit number is SVR-02255.

Pre-Booking Inquiries: If you have any questions before securing your booking or wish to speak with us first, please click on our host profile picture and use the “contact host” button to send us a direct message. We are happy to help!

Neighborhood highlights
The Historic District isn't just a collection of buildings; it's a living, breathing testament to Savannah's enduring charm and hospitality. Whether you're a history buff, a food enthusiast, or simply seeking the allure of Southern elegance, this neighborhood invites you to step into the past and savor the present.

Wander along the meticulously preserved streets, lined with majestic mansions, Federal-style townhouses, and charming Victorian homes. Each building stands as a testament to Savannah's rich history, showcasing the city's evolution from its colonial roots to the elegant Antebellum period.

Forsyth Park, a verdant oasis at the district's southern end, invites you to leisurely strolls amidst fragrant gardens and ornate fountains. The iconic Forsyth Fountain, framed by the regal Live Oak alleys, serves as a timeless centerpiece.

Indulge your senses in the district's culinary delights, with quaint cafes and fine dining establishments offering a taste of Southern hospitality. Don't forget to savor the renowned dishes like shrimp and grits or indulge in a refreshing glass of sweet tea.
