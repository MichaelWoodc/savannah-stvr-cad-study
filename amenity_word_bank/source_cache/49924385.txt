=== FULL DESCRIPTION ===
This tastefully decorated, and modern home is located in the heart of the Downtown Historic District, only steps away from Savannah's Famed Forsyth Park.

The space
This tastefully decorated, and modern home is located in the heart of the Downtown Historic District, only steps away from Savannah's Famed Forsyth Park. This three-story home with two private parking spaces can accommodate up to 8 Lucky Guests.

This is the perfect getaway if you are looking for a modern, newly constructed townhouse with tall ceilings, an ample amount of natural light, and upgraded furnishings and finishes. Large groups and families will love the amount of space this home has to offer, in addition to the unmatched comfort and hospitality while living like a true Savannah Local. 

The main home is technically the second level of this three-story town home and is accessed by a set of stairs. Enter into the chic living space with L-shaped sectional large enough to accommodate all guests, fitted with a smart TV. The adjacent kitchen features top-of-the line appliances, with granite countertops, while being fully stocked with everything you need to prepare home-cooked meals! Stools at both the kitchen counter and island are perfect for bonding over a bottle of wine and cheese board before heading out on the town. Just at the back of the home, you'll find a formal dining nook and half bath just behind the kitchen.

The three bedrooms can be found on the third level. The first room, also known as the "main" bedroom, features a comfortable king-size bed, smart television, balcony, and a luxurious ensuite bathroom. The additional two guest rooms each have Queen size beds and share a full bath found in the hall. One guest room has private access to a balcony!  

Guests are a short walk away from the Fountain at Forsyth Park, and a beautiful stroll from downtown's main attractions such as River Street, City Market, and Bay Street.
