=== FULL DESCRIPTION ===
A charming hideaway nestled in Savannah’s South Historic District. Perfect for a romantic escape or peaceful solo retreat, this pet-friendly one-bedroom, one-bathroom home accommodates up to two guests in comfort and style.

The space
Welcome to Secret Savannah Sanctuary B – a charming hideaway nestled in Savannah’s South Historic District. Perfect for a romantic escape or peaceful solo retreat, this pet-friendly one-bedroom, one-bathroom home accommodates up to two guests in comfort and style.

What makes this home special?

Open and bright floor plan: an open living and dining area greets you with hardwood floors and abundant natural light. Relax on the plush sofa with a Smart TV for entertainment, or enjoy meals in the fully equipped kitchen, featuring sleek black stainless-steel appliances, quartz countertops, and a cozy dining table.

Peaceful Sleeping Accommodations: The tranquil bedroom offers a Queen-size bed, Smart TV, and ample closet space, while the modern bathroom boasts quartz finishes and a walk-in shower designed for relaxation. Enjoy the convenience of a shared washer and dryer available on site during your stay.

Step outside and discover the beauty of Savannah’s South Historic District—cobblestone streets, moss-draped oaks, and landmarks like Forsyth Park and the Cathedral of St. John the Baptist. When you’re ready for a change of pace, Tybee Island’s sandy beaches and coastal charm are just a short drive away.

Whether you’re strolling through historic squares or savoring the sea breeze, Secret Savannah Sanctuary B is your perfect retreat in Savannah!

Sleeping Configuration: 
Bedroom 1: Queen Bed

Parking: Non-metered Parking
This unit has free, on-street parking right outside the front door. Availability will depend on time of day and season. Our guests have not reported trouble parking near the home, even on the busiest of weekends.

We can welcome your dog companions at this pet friendly home, but ask that you find other arrangements for cats, birds, and other furry friends. There is a $130 pet fee (non-refundable) with a 35 lb. maximum weight limit per dog and a limit of 2 dogs per reservation.

SVR-03034

Other things to note
AGE RESTRICTION: All renters must be over the age of 25, unless it is a child accompanied by a parent. If a guest is found to have falsified their reservation in any way we reserve the right to evict the guest, cancel their reservation and retain ALL DEPOSITS PAID TO THAT POINT.
