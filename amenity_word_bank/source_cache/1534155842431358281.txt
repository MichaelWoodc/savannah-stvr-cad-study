Welcome to Jewel of Jones Street! Nestled above the cobblestones and beneath majestic oak trees draped in Spanish moss, Jewel on Jones Street is a one-of-a-kind, three-bedroom parlor-level home that perfectly captures the charm and grace of Savannah.

What We Love About Jewel of Jones Street:

Unbeatable Location: Located on iconic Jones Street—voted “America’s Prettiest Street” by Southern Living Magazine—this home sits directly across from Mrs. Wilkes Dining Room and is just steps from Savannah’s most beloved landmarks, including the Cathedral of St. John the Baptist, the Mercer-Williams House, and the Sorrell-Weed House. You’ll be surrounded by the city’s rich architectural history and southern charm.

Classic Traditional Interiors: The elegant formal parlor welcomes guests with timeless French-inspired décor—refined yet relaxed, ideal for gathering with close friends and family. Just beyond, the dining room and kitchen open onto a lovely private porch that overlooks the shared courtyard, perfect for morning coffee or evening cocktails.

Each bedroom offers stunning views of historic Savannah, with natural light pouring in throughout the day. The Primary Suite is a spacious retreat with a King bed, luxurious ensuite bathroom featuring a soaking tub and walk-in shower, and a generous closet. The second bedroom features a cozy Queen bed and wall-to-wall windows overlooking charming Jones Street—complete with blinds for evening privacy. Lastly, the third bedroom is an Inviting room, with another queen bed in a bright, cheerful space. These second and third bedroom can utilize the second full bathroom with a walk-in shower, across the apartment for added privacy.

Sleeping Configuration:
Bedroom 1: King Bed
Bedroom 2: Queen Bed
Bedroom 3: Queen Bed

What is parking like for this home?
Parking for this home is on-street and metered, which is enforced by the City of Savannah from Monday through Friday, 8am to 5pm. Guests may conveniently pay using the ParkSavannah app, feed the meter or parking kiosks directly, or park on a nearby non-metered street. Please take note of posted Sweep Zone signs when parking on the street.
