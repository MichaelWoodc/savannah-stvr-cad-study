=== FULL DESCRIPTION ===
Step into Savannah’s history without sacrificing modern comfort. This bright, beautifully renovated 1-bedroom flat sits on the second floor of a 150-year-old Victorian home, just one block from iconic Forsyth Park. Here, historic character meets thoughtful upgrades: soaring ceilings, tall windows, decorative fireplaces, and repurposed original wood details blend seamlessly with a fully modern kitchen, keyless entry, and high-speed Wi-Fi.

The space
Perfect for couples, solo travelers, or anyone looking to explore Savannah’s most beloved sights on foot, our home offers both charm and convenience in one of the city’s most picturesque neighborhoods.

-650 sq ft of light-filled living space with vintage architectural touches.
-Spacious bedroom with a plush queen bed and en-suite bathroom.
-Cozy living room ideal for relaxing after a day of sightseeing.
-Fully equipped kitchen with full-size appliances, Keurig coffee maker, and a backsplash crafted from the home’s original floorboards.
-Decorative fireplaces throughout (non-working but full of charm).
-Historic touches including framed documents and photos of the home’s original owners.

Unbeatable location – 1 block to Forsyth Park, 2 blocks to the free DOT shuttle, walkable to coffee shops, restaurants, and markets.
Free, well-lit street parking right outside the door.
Easy check-in with keyless entry.
Fast Wi-Fi & Smart TV for downtime.
Thoughtful amenities – extra towels, snacks, and flexible baggage drop-off.

Whether you’re here for a weekend getaway, a special event in Forsyth Park, or to explore Savannah’s squares and waterfront, our home puts you close to it all while offering a quiet, comfortable retreat to come back to.

NOTE: Two adult guests are permitted per reservation overnight. Any additional overnight guests will be charged $50.00 per night, per guest.

STVR Certificate: SVR-02820

Other things to note
Search online for “visit Savannah” and you’ll find Savannah’s official “guide” to the city. A great place to start upon your visit so that you are sure to take advantage of Savannah’s historic beauty and rich culture.
