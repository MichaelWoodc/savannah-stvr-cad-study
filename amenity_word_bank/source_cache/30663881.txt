=== FULL DESCRIPTION ===
Beautifully renovated 1890 victorian home just four blocks from Savannah's famous Forsyth Park. Captivating historic district neighborhood is perfect for relaxing and people watching from the spacious porch. Enjoy a short, scenic walk to some of Savannah's best shops, cafes, and restaurants.

The space
Your group will have plenty of room to spread out with five bedrooms, two large common area for gatherings, and a fenced in back yard. Pottery Barn and West Elm furnishings are a stylish and comfortable match for the unique, original details in this fully restored 1890 home. When you're not out exploring Savannah, you'll love people-watching from the large, covered front porch. Worn out from the day?  Take it easy watching Netflix from the comfy couch.

This 1890 victorian home is divided into two units. One up and one down. This listing is for booking both units. You will have access to the entire home.
