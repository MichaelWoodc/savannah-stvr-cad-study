=== FULL DESCRIPTION ===
Escape to the heart of Savannah and experience the charm of local living at our Relaxing Poolside Condo. Nestled in a serene location, this cozy 1 bed, 1 bath condo is your perfect retreat. Dive into relaxation by the pool, your antidote to the Southern heat. With its inviting ambiance and convenient amenities, it's the ideal spot for those seeking a blend of comfort and adventure. Your memorable stay awaits!


Savannah License # - SVR-03085

The space
This cozy, poolside retreat is the perfect spot to slow down and savor Savannah. Enjoy a beautiful back porch overlooking the pool and evening sunsets—the ideal place to start your day with coffee or end it with a glass of wine.

Inside, you’ll find updated TVs, a comfy Queen bed, and a thoughtfully arranged living space designed for comfort and relaxation. The kitchen features full-sized appliances, making it easy to cook meals at home or warm up leftovers from your favorite local restaurants.

You’ll also have one designated off-street parking space, making it simple to come and go during your stay. Whether you’re here to explore historic Savannah, relax by the water, or enjoy a quiet getaway, this space offers a warm and welcoming home base.

Guest access
Guests have access to entire unit and all amenities including semi-private pool, gas grill, and free off-street parking.

Neighborhood highlights
Walking Score of 87/100! This condo is ideally located just 1 block from downtown Savannah’s only grocery store and only 4 blocks from Forsyth Park making it perfect for a weekend getaway or a month-long stay. Tucked away on a quiet residential lane with nearby bus routes and local conveniences, it offers the perfect balance of accessibility and tranquility. Located at the northern edge of the landmark historic District you will be within walking distance of everything. Savannah has to offer and still be able to enjoy a good night sleep away from the tourist hoopla.

It is an absolutely beautiful 20 to 30 minute walk through the historic district to the shopping district showcasing the gorgeous squares of downtown Savannah. From there, it is a quick 10 minute walk to River Street. If you’ve walked enough at that point consider hailing a petty cab to pedal you back to the condo tip them well and they will give you a personal downtown tour.🥳

Feel free to ask us for recommendations for our favorite restaurants and local downtown haunts. We can’t wait to host you!
