=== FULL DESCRIPTION ===
Discover Carriage for the Captain, a hidden gem in Savannah. Once the carriage house of Captain James Dickerson, it features a king bed and ensuite bathroom on the main floor. Upstairs, enjoy an open kitchen, cozy living area, and a half bath. Perfect for a romantic escape or a family stay, this charming retreat offers comfort and history in one unique space!

The space
Step back in time and immerse yourself in Savannah’s rich history with a stay at Carriage for the Captain, the former carriage house of Captain Henry James Dickerson. A legendary figure in Savannah’s maritime history, Captain Dickerson arrived in Savannah as a young sailor and rose to prominence as a ship owner, harbor master, and influential community leader. His legacy lives on in this beautifully restored retreat, located just steps from Oglethorpe Square in the heart of the Historic District.

This charming two-story carriage house combines historical character with modern comforts, offering the perfect escape for couples, families, or friends. The main floor features a serene king bedroom with luxurious bedding and an en-suite bathroom, creating a private sanctuary for restful nights. Upstairs, you’ll find a welcoming open-plan kitchen and living area, perfect for entertaining or relaxing. Exposed beams and historical touches add to the unique ambiance. A convenient half-bathroom is located just off the kitchen. 

No car is needed for this location. Next to Oglethorpe Square, Carriage for the Captain is an easy 3-10 minute walk to most attractions, restaurants, and bars in the Historic District. Please be aware that parking is onthe street or in a nearby parking garage and is paid.

Key Features 
- Prime Location: Situated next to Oglethorpe Square, this home is just a 3–10 minute walk to Savannah’s best attractions, restaurants, and bars. Leave the car behind and enjoy the Historic District on foot. 
- Modern Amenities: Enjoy a fully equipped kitchen, cozy living area with a flat-screen TV, and thoughtful touches throughout to ensure a comfortable stay. 
- Parking Details: Please note that parking is on the street or in a nearby garage and is paid.

Whether you're planning a romantic getaway or a family vacation, Carriage for the Captain offers a perfect blend of history, charm, and modern convenience. Book your stay today and create lasting memories in the heart of Savannah.

Other things to note
Parking is available at the State Street Parking Garage for a daily rate of $12. The garage is conveniently located just a two-minute walk across the square. Additionally, electric vehicle charging stations are available within the facility.
