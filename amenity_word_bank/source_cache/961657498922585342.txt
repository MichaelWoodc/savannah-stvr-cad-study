Neighborhood highlights
Welcome to The Broughton Street Loft! This historic 1860s loft is located on the top floor above the Savannah Bee Company on historic Broughton Street. Professionally cleaned and sanitized. The Broughton Street Loft is nestled in the center of four of Savannah’s famous squares (Ellis, Telfair, Johnson, and Wright) and puts you in the best possible spot to take advantage of downtown living in Savannah, Georgia. This fully renovated condo is as centrally located as you can get! (Walk Score 95, Bike Score 94) Steps from all of the city's most popular attractions including River Street, City Market, Forsyth Park, St. John the Baptist, the SCAD buildings, and several incredible restaurants and cafés.

Arrive stress-free with keyless entry and self-check-in. The condo is a third-floor walkup, with the entrance door located between the Savannah Bee Company and The Coffee Fox and a straight staircase leading you up to the loft!

The Broughton Street Loft comfortably sleeps 6 guests with 2 queen beds and 2 twin beds.

Fall in love with the historic charm of this loft featuring a modern-industrial style with soaring ceilings and exposed beams in the open-concept living area and multiple skylights flooding in natural light. Vintage brick walls in the living and bedrooms and original hardwood floors give the condo its old-world charm, while the kitchen and bathrooms are appointed with modern amenities.

The Master bedroom has a queen bed, a modern private bathroom with tile to the ceiling, a jetted shower panel, and a walk-in closet. The spacious guest room features a queen bed as well as a twin day bed with a trundle to accommodate 2 extra guests. See a lovely little view of downtown and the top of the bridge from the Juliette balcony.

For your convenience, there is fast WiFi, a private full-size washer/dryer, and ample closet space. Our guests enjoy complimentary coffee, creamer and sugar, tea varieties, laundry, and dish detergent for the first 30 days of their stay. Also provided are quality bed linens, bath towels, and goose-down comforters. Maps, menus, and touring information are available in our Welcome Book. For your pleasure, enjoy the local books about the city and history of Savannah.

Enjoy a comfortable night's sleep, and in the morning wake up to a cup of coffee from the Keurig in the kitchen or pop downstairs to The Coffee Fox and grab a quick pick-me-up espresso drink to give you the energy you need for a day on the town. If you're heading out to Tybee beach grab a striped towel and kids toys if you need them and enjoy the sun and the sand.

The nickname “Hostess City” fits the bill. Savannah is a warm, friendly blend of funky, scenic, romantic, and artistic vibes. Go antique shopping or thrifting in the Starland District or grab a beer at one of the many microbreweries. Take a formal walking tour, ghost tour, historical tour, or food tour through the Historic Districts. Go for a romantic carriage ride along the cobblestone streets past the historic mansions.

After a day of exploring, come back and relax on the comfortable leather sofa, stream your favorite shows and movies from Xfinity basic cable or your personal streaming services. Play a round or two of shuffleboard or have a darts competition, before heading back out to enjoy one of the top restaurants in the city a walk away or stay in and cook a nice meal in the full kitchen.

Please note that this condo gives you great access to the heart of downtown. Because of its central location, there is city noise that will filter up from the street. Though this loft is tucked away off busy Broughton Street, we've provided a white noise machine and blackout curtains in both bedrooms to help drown out the city sounds if you're a light sleeper. If you think you might be bothered by this during your stay, the downtown area may not be the spot for you. However, if what you are looking for is an exciting place to hang your hat while you see and do all you can on your weekend away, this is a great home for you during your visit to Savannah.

The Broughton Street Loft is a quick 20-minute drive or Uber from the airport. We offer complimentary use of our parking pass during your stay for one car at the Whitaker Street Parking Garage. For alternative parking options, there is on-street parking around the city and a City of Savannah Visitors Day Pass is available for purchase online for discounted meter rates. For a pass, go to the Visitors Information Center or the Parking Services office on Bryan or the 17Hundred90 Inn and Restaurant front desk at 302 E Presidents Street. We recommend you leave your car parked and enjoy the walking lifestyle that makes Savannah fabulous.

We hope that you choose to stay here. We love Savannah, and we think that staying at The Broughton Street Loft will give you the perfect slice of life in this jewel of a town.
