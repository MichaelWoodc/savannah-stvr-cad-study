=== FULL DESCRIPTION ===
Discover Savannah's charm in this historic condo, nestled in the parlor level of an incredibly restored row home in the heart of the Historic District. One bedroom, one bathroom, and an eat-in kitchen, all adorned with classic, Parisian-inspired details and architecture. A romantic getaway perfect for couples, solo-travelers, and those that want to experience a true Savannah stay! Just steps from Forsyth Park and nearby attractions, experience the city's beauty right at your doorstep!

SVR-02734

The space
This condo is located on the parlor level (first floor, up the exterior stairs) of a quintessential Savannah rowhouse in the downtown, historic district. Once inside, you will find an open living space with soaring ceilings, architectural details of a bygone era, and plenty of modern upgrades! Off the living space is a thoughtfully partitioned sleeping area, featuring king-sized bed, crisp linens, and incredible views from the floor to ceiling windows! Head beyond the living space and find an eat-in kitchen with everything you would need to whip up a quick meal before heading out to enjoy all the adventures our city has to offer! Fast wifi, spacious design, plenty of storage, laundry facilities on-site, and all the conveniences of home await!

Guest access
The entire first floor condo (parlor level, as we like to call it here in the South) is all yours! Ascend the front, exterior, wrought iron adorned staircase and enter the home's front door. Your private condo entrance is right there, off the foyer. The entire condo is all yours! If you need to access the laundry facilities, they are located in the courtyard. Just let us know and we are happy to provide details!

Other things to note
Parking can be tricky in downtown Savannah! The parking options nearby are paid street parking, which can either be paid using the kiosks/meters, the Park Savannah App, or by purchasing a day pass from the city. We will share all the details regarding navigating parking with you upon booking! There are also some free parking options a few blocks away that we will also give you more info about!
