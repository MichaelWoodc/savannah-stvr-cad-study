=== FULL DESCRIPTION ===
Off the beaten path, this charming townhome is filled with character. Centrally located and nestled away on a quiet street, Forsyth Place is your next home away from home!

The space
Nestled away on quiet street this charming town home is filled with character. An idyllic getaway for a family of four just steps away from the park, surrounded by divine historic homes. Take a stroll beneath the Spanish Moss Trees, and amongst some of the wealthiest homes on Gaston Street.

Enter and step right into the heart home! The open living space is an inviting area to plan your ventures, or catch up to share your favorite part of the day with your group. The large solid wood dining room table is great to host dinners, and enjoy your family cooked meals. The kitchen sits just on the other side, where you'll have plenty of room to prepare meals and a brew a fresh pot of local coffee! You can enjoy your meals, or morning cup of joe at the bistro set in the kitchen, or the private courtyard that sits behind the home. 

Upstairs you'll find two bedrooms with comfortable furnishings. The first bedroom sits at the front of the home, boasts with natural light, offers a queen size bed, and ample closet space. Across the hall, you can find a bathroom with tub and shower combination. Toward the back of the home, the primary bedroom awaits you - A large plush queen size bed, with walk-in closet, wardrobe and ensuite bathroom. Don't forget about your small private balcony, that can be opened to enjoy the birds whistling and views of the treelined courtyard. 

Enjoy being just a short walk to Forsyth Park, and the Farmers Market that takes place on Saturday mornings. Looking for a coffee shop to kick your day off? Head over to Sentient Bean, just at the end of Forsyth, or Collins Quarters at The Park. Feel like venturing downtown? Start at Bull Street and head South walking through each square until you reach Broughton Street!

What is parking like for this home?
Parking is non-metered, on-street. Please be sure to pay close attention to street signs when parking. Street signs state when the street cleaners come, and require you to move your vehicle to an opposite street.
