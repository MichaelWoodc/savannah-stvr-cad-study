=== FULL DESCRIPTION ===
Welcome to your downtown getaway! Toss your keys for the weekend and walk to everything. Perfect for that family trip, girls' weekend, or couples' retreat.

After a day exploring the gorgeous area, you can relax on a private patio/yard oasis, play some board games, or cook a meal using the fully stocked kitchen. Groceries/coffee are one block away; perfect to pick up supplies for a picnic at Forsyth park! 

Breathe easy: the HVAC system has a UV light to hinder airborne viruses/germs.

SVR 02861

The space
A stain glassed entryway window, glass doorknobs and beautiful modern Victorian furniture invite you in as you enter your beautiful downtown getaway ready for you to enjoy your stay!

Downstairs you will find plenty of room to relax or enjoy a meal together in our shotgun style living / dining space. A fully equipped kitchen connects behind it, perfect for a private chef experience, at your disposal! 

The back door leads you to your own private, fenced in, outdoor oasis. A rarity in downtown Savannah! 
A lawn made for cornhole tournaments or bocce games leads to a private patio with a jasmine-covered wall that blooms every spring and some string lights above creating some evening ambiance.

Upstairs there are three bedrooms and two bathrooms. Each room themed to a beautiful country. 

Primary/front bedroom: The Spain Room
A king bed, a desk, and an attached bathroom make this an ideal room to settle in for the weekend. Oh, did we mention the cute balcony overlooking historic brownstones? 

Middle bedroom: The France Room
A “Green Tea®” memory foam king bed fits comfortably in this cozy space, and the Jack and Jill bathroom allows you easy access no matter the time of day. 

Back bedroom: The Amalfi Coast Room
This quaint room hosts two single beds, and has direct access to the Jack and Jill bathroom.

Guest access
The entire house and side yard are yours to enjoy!
