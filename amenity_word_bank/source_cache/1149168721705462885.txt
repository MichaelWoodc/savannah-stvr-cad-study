Welcome to your garden-level retreat in beautiful Savannah! Tucked on a quiet block yet walkable to River Street and downtown attractions, Magnolia Garden offers the best of both worlds.

The space
Inside, you'll find a warm and inviting space with all the comforts of home. The kitchen is fully equipped for daily cooking, and both the bedroom and living room feature Roku Smart TVs.

🛏️ Queen bedroom with Roku TV
🍳 Full kitchen with cookware and appliances
🚗 Free street parking on our block
🚶‍♀️ Walkable to shops, parks, and historic sites
🌳 Private entrance below a 3-bedroom home (some noise possible)
🌼 Shared Courtyard: Enclosed outdoor space shared with guests in the main house and carriage unit
