=== FULL DESCRIPTION ===
The Main House and Garden Level at Wayne Mansion Estate offer two beautifully appointed private residences within a grand 1871 historic structure, located just one block from Forsyth Park. Guests enjoy exclusive access to the Main House and the Garden‑Level Apartment below it—two distinct living spaces that are connected by an indoor staircase, but also have their own private exterior entrances.

The space
With original architectural details, generous gathering areas, and a fully enclosed furnished courtyard, the property is ideal for families and groups seeking an elevated Savannah experience.

Two Private Residences
Your group will have exclusive use of both the two-story Main House and the Garden‑Level Apartment below it, offering a total of five bedrooms, four full bathrooms, two half baths, two living rooms and two fully equipped kitchens.

Main House
3 Bedrooms (All King Beds)  3 Full Baths + 1 Half Bath
This two‑story historic residence showcases original wood floors, a grand staircase, ornate ceiling medallions, intricate crown moldings, and marble fireplaces. The layout is designed for both comfort and elegance, featuring:
• 	Formal living room
• 	Formal dining room with seating for six
• 	Cozy TV room
• 	Furnished covered porch
• 	Fully equipped kitchen and pantry with six‑burner range, two beverage chillers, and island seating for four

Main House Bedrooms
• 	Bedroom 1 — King bed, TV, walk‑in closet, attached dressing room with vanity and oversized mirror, en‑suite bathroom with double vanity, claw‑foot tub, and oversized shower
• 	Bedroom 2 — King bed, TV, west‑facing windows for beautiful afternoon light
• 	Bedroom 3 — King bed, TV, en‑suite bathroom

Garden‑Level Apartment
2 Bedrooms (Both King Beds)  1 Full Bath + 1 Half Bath
Located directly beneath the Main House, this private ground‑floor apartment has its own entrance and furnished porch, offering a comfortable and convenient retreat with:
• 	Fully equipped kitchen
• 	One full‑size sleeper sofa in a room with a door
• 	Direct access to the courtyard

Garden‑Level Bedrooms
• 	Bedroom 4 — King bed, TV, direct courtyard access, en‑suite dual‑entry bathroom shared with Bedroom 5
• 	Bedroom 5 — King bed, TV, en‑suite dual‑entry bathroom shared with Bedroom 4

Outdoor Features
• 	Large furnished and fully enclosed courtyard ideal for gathering, relaxing, or enjoying morning coffee
• 	Side yard offering additional green space and privacy
• 	Two private off‑street parking spaces, a rare amenity in this neighborhood

Event Use

No events are permitted when booking through this listing.  Guests wishing to host an event at the property must book through the Wayne Mansion Estate listing, which also includes the two carriage houses at the back of the property as well as sign an Event Agreement and pay an Event Fee and Refundable Security deposit.
