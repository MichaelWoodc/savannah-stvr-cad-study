=== FULL DESCRIPTION ===
Ready to add some uniqueness to your Savannah trip? Welcome to the Chicken Shack, an eclectic and truly funky one-bedroom accommodation in the heart of Savannah. Featuring a mix of modern industrial, splashes of color, and an incredible backyard, the Shack is the perfect basecamp to explore the area. Just blocks from Forsyth Park and surrounded by incredible dining and attractions. 
SVR-02842

The space
Situated on a quiet corner, the Shack provides a quirky and fun space that only adds to the unique experience Savannah offers. Directly inside, you will find a small but spacious living room featuring an oversized sofa, stylish décor, and a smart TV ready to stream your favorite shows. Walk towards the kitchen and notice the industrial-style ventilation tucked along the vaulted ceiling, adding a modern flair. At the same time, unique corrugated metal cabinets make this a one-of-a-kind kitchen. Find everything you need to cook in the kitchen, including a full suite of appliances, cookware, dishware, and utensils. Directly off the kitchen is a small dining space with a steeple-style roof line wrapped in metal and large windows looking out into the large backyard.
The single bedroom features a new queen mattress wrapped in fresh linens and plush pillows and its own bathroom and private patio. The patio is fully screened in and provides comfortable furniture, making it a perfect place to enjoy morning coffee.
The bathroom features a single vanity and low-entry shower with custom tile, adding splashes of color. The bathroom is outfitted with all essentials, including towels, toilet paper, shampoo, and body wash.
Working remotely during your stay? The comfortable space and our high-speed Wi-Fi make setting up your home office a breeze.

Guest access
The entire house is setup for your use. We have a screened in patio (Mosquito-Free!) and a large yard you can let your dogs run around in! Please be sure to clean up after your pet and make sure they aren't left unattended at any point. The Yard is secure however it would be easy to jump the side fence and if you have a dog that likes to dig, it could get under the back gate.

Other things to note
We allow up to 2 dogs max. The fee for one is $100 and the fee for two is $200 and must be paid before check-in. We have a large yard you can let your dogs run around in! Please be sure to clean up after your pet and make sure they aren't left unattended at any point. The Yard is secure however it would be easy to jump the side fence and if you have a dog that likes to dig, it could get under the back gate.

Neighborhood highlights
Ideally located in the Victorian District, you will be in the heart of historic Savannah and centrally located to all the incredible eats, drinks, and sights Savannah has to offer. From your doorstep, the south side of Forsyth Park is just an 8-minute walk. Grab some baked goods and a latte at Savannah Rum Runner Bakery and Café, or sit down for breakfast at Collins Quarter at Forsyth.
Looking for a sandwich and a pint? Stroll on down to the Black Rabbit, a small yet incredible sandwich shop by day and pub by night. Need a taco fix? Try out Bull Street Tacos for scratch-made Mexican dishes.
Looking for fine dining and a great vibe? Head over to Local 11 Ten and their chic rooftop bar and lounge, The Perch. Enjoy handcrafted cocktails and delicious dishes. The Perch is open year-round and features gas heaters for cooler winter days.
Take an adventure outside of the neighborhood by visiting one of Savannah’s many museums. Mercer-William’s House Museum, Savannah African Art Museum, American Prohibition Museum, and many more are just minutes away. Tap into the artistic nature of Savannah by stopping into Savannah’s College of Art and Design Museum of Art. Want to get more hands-on and make some memories? Try blowing glass at Hostess City Hot Glass, a perfect time for the whole family.
Not enough time to walk all the sights of Savannah? Take it all in at once while riding or biking. We are just minutes from Old Town Trolley Tours of Savannah and Savannah Bike Tours. Check out the city’s skyline from the water by jumping on one of the Savannah Belle’s ferries, with multiple stops all along Savannah’s riverfront.
