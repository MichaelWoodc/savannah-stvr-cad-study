=== FULL DESCRIPTION ===
Riverview Terrace is a spacious loft-style home designed to comfortably accommodate up to four guests. This well appointed riverfront retreat stands out for its open layout, high-end finishes, and incredible location. Situated within a historic brick building overlooking the Savannah River, this sought-after property places guests in the heart of everything the Historic District has to offer. And yes, the private terrace offers beautiful views of River Street and the river beyond.

The space
Step into a brick-encrusted loft filled with natural light from a row of windows overlooking Savannah's famed River Street. This expansive open-concept living space features an oversized L-shaped couch centered around a stunning antique brick fireplace. Just steps away, the full-sized gourmet kitchen is equipped with stainless steel appliances, including a refrigerator, stove, microwave, and dishwasher. Start your morning with a fresh cup of coffee from the single-cup coffee maker before gathering around the granite-topped island with seating for four. Industrial-inspired details, soaring ceilings with exposed pipes, and elegant crystal chandeliers create a space that feels both sophisticated and inviting. In addition to a dining table for four, the kitchen island provides seating for four more, making it a wonderful place to enjoy meals or simply spend time together.



The thoughtfully designed interior includes comfortable sleeping accommodations, luxurious king beds, and four beautifully appointed full bathrooms. Tasteful finishes and spa-inspired tiled walk-in showers create a relaxing retreat after a day of exploring Savannah.



Riverview Terrace has a whimsical charm that makes it feel like a destination all its own. The home includes a full-sized washer and dryer tucked within a convenient laundry nook, as well as private parking for up to two vehicles. Additional amenities include air conditioning, heat, Wi-Fi, and cable. What truly sets this home apart is its private balconies overlooking River Street, where guests can step outside and enjoy views of the Savannah River while taking in the energy and history of one of the city's most iconic destinations. From here, you'll be within walking distance of many of Savannah's best restaurants, boutiques, museums, and attractions. It's also easy to hop aboard the Savannah Belles Ferry for a scenic ride across the river to Hutchinson Island.



While River Street beckons just outside your door, the surrounding Historic District offers even more to explore. Riverview Terrace places guests within easy reach of Savannah's renowned shopping, sightseeing, dining, and legendary haunted attractions. Wander beneath the city's famous oak canopies while discovering historic squares, beautiful architecture, and charming hidden courtyards. The Historic District is easily explored on foot, by bicycle, trolley, carriage, or guided bus tour.



Other must-see attractions include Forsyth Park, the Cathedral Basilica of St. John the Baptist, Bonaventure Cemetery, The Olde Pink House, the American Prohibition Museum, and countless other local favorites. For those looking to venture a little farther, Wormsloe Historic Site, Old Fort Jackson, and Skidaway Island State Park are all within easy driving distance. After dark, Savannah's famous ghost tours reveal another side of the city's rich history. Throughout the year, visitors can also enjoy signature events including the Savannah Irish Festival, Seafood Fest on the River, the Historic Savannah Foundation Preservation Festival, the Savannah Music Festival, the North of Gaston Street Tour of Hidden Gardens, and the Blessing of the Fleet. With its premier waterfront location, Riverview Terrace offers exceptional access to many of the city's most celebrated events and festivals.



Guests staying with Southern Belle Vacation Rentals also enjoy exclusive access to a resort-style pool on Tybee Island. Just a scenic 25-minute drive through Georgia's beautiful marshlands, Tybee offers sandy beaches and a relaxing coastal escape whenever the mood strikes. Of course, with sweeping river views and one of Savannah's most sought-after waterfront locations, you may find it hard to leave Riverview Terrace for long.
