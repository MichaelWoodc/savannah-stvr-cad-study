=== FULL DESCRIPTION ===
Welcome to Liberty Mid-Century, a stunning one-bedroom, one-and-a-half-bathroom condo located in the iconic Drayton Tower in downtown Savannah, Georgia. This thoughtfully designed urban retreat blends the timeless elegance of mid-century modern style with contemporary comfort and convenience. Whether you're planning a romantic getaway or a solo adventure full of history, charm, and culture, Liberty Mid-Century offers the ideal home base for your Savannah stay

The space
Perfectly suited for up to four guests, this condo provides an elevated vacation experience right in the center of Savannah’s most vibrant neighborhood. From its elegant interior to its unbeatable location, every detail has been curated to help you relax, unwind, and enjoy the best of what this enchanting city has to offer.



The Iconic Drayton Tower - Your Gateway to Savannah



Experience unparalleled elegance in one of Savannah’s most distinguished landmarks, Drayton Tower. Boasting seamless elevator access and a coveted central location in the vibrant heart of downtown, this residence places you mere steps from the city’s renowned squares, upscale dining, boutique shopping, and rich cultural attractions. Your stay includes a coveted parking pass for one vehicle in a nearby premium garage, ensuring effortless convenience throughout your visit. Upon arrival, indulge in the peace of mind provided by secure, keyless building access, allowing you to explore Savannah’s charms with absolute ease. The building’s sleek, minimalist architecture is perfectly complemented by the refined sophistication of your private, impeccably designed condo.



A Sophisticated Downtown Retreat



Step inside Liberty Mid-Century and discover a thoughtfully styled condo that exudes comfort, character, and luxury. The living room invites you in with plush seating, clean lines, and an abundance of natural light streaming in through large windows that offer views of the surrounding historic district. A smart TV with streaming capabilities is mounted for your convenience. It’s perfect for a cozy evening in with your favorite shows or movies after a full day of sightseeing. This space also offers a convenient sofa bed transforming the space into an additional sleeping space.



The open-concept layout creates a seamless flow between living, dining, and kitchen areas. This gives the condo an airy, spacious feel that’s perfect for four guests.     



A Full Kitchen Designed for Comfort and Style



Whether you're making a quick breakfast before heading out or preparing a romantic dinner in, you’ll find everything you need in the condo’s full kitchen. With sleek cabinetry, modern appliances, and essential cookware, this kitchen is as functional as it is stylish.



The adjacent dining table seats four, offering the ideal setting for a casual meal, a bottle of wine, or even a game night. The natural materials and warm tones of the space reflect the mid-century aesthetic while keeping the atmosphere relaxed and welcoming.



Tranquil Bedroom with King Bed



When it’s time to rest and recharge, retreat to the serene bedroom at Liberty Mid-Century. A comfortable king-size bed takes center stage, featuring a high-quality mattress that promises a restful night’s sleep. Crisp linens, soft lighting, and tasteful decor add to the relaxing ambiance, making it a true haven after a busy day in the city.



A second smart TV is mounted in the bedroom, so you can wind down with a late-night movie or morning news without ever leaving the comfort of your bed. For your convenience, you’ll also find a stackable washer and dryer discreetly located in the bedroom. This is perfect for longer stays or simply refreshing your wardrobe after a warm day exploring the city streets.



Modern Bathrooms with Designer Touches



Liberty Mid-Century features 1.5 bathrooms, offering guests both comfort and privacy. The full bathroom includes a tiled walk-in shower with modern fixtures and plenty of room to prepare for your day or unwind at night. Clean lines, soft lighting, and thoughtfully chosen finishes complete the spa-like feel.



An additional half bath is located off the main living space, providing added convenience and making it easy to host a guest or enjoy a leisurely morning routine without crowding.



Experience the Best of Downtown Savannah



What truly distinguishes Liberty Mid-Century is its premier location within the prestigious Drayton Tower. At ground level, indulge in a rejuvenating yoga and Pilates studio, savor exquisite craft cocktails and sophisticated late-night fare at Savoy Society, and explore a curated collection of boutique shops — all just steps from your door.



Beyond the building, Savannah’s most iconic treasures await. Stroll gracefully through the enchanting Forsyth Park, discover the elegance of historic squares, and immerse yourself in the city’s rich heritage with nearby tours of stately homes and museums. The city’s renowned arts scene, upscale shopping, and acclaimed dining establishments are all effortlessly accessible on foot. Begin your day with a meticulously brewed latte from a local artisan café, meander through cobblestone streets framed by majestic live oaks draped in Spanish moss, and return each evening to the refined comfort of Liberty Mid-Century. Whether unwinding in style or venturing out to experience Savannah’s vibrant nightlife and live music, every moment here exudes timeless Southern sophistication.



Perfect for a romantic escape, a cultural retreat, or an elegant sojourn, Liberty Mid-Century offers a seamless fusion of mid-century modern design, luxurious amenities, and an unrivaled address in the heart of Savannah’s captivating charm.
