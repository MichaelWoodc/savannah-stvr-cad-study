=== FULL DESCRIPTION ===
This private condo perched on the second (and third!) floors of our historic mansion, steps off Forsyth Park. Enjoy gathering with friends and family in this Victorian estate! Thoughtfully updated with all the modern amenities, while still maintaining SO many charming original details! Have a drink on one of the balconies, enjoy a meal around the dining table, or explore our beautiful city knowing that you have this awesome space to come home to!

SVR-02227

The space
Our condo might be the perfect spot for you!  With such a large space, we do want to ensure we are the right fit for your group.  We have a strict 8 guest occupancy limit, which is the maximum permitted by our license to short term rent.  Unfortunately, we can't make any exceptions to this rule.  We also rent out another condo right below this one, and we do our best to ensure both groups are able to peacefully enjoy the space.  Though we have plenty of space to stretch out, we do not permit parties or events and must enforce our neighborhood Quiet Hours after 10pm.  Before booking, please send us a message describing your group and your plans for your stay! 

The condo is expansive with a fun, old world layout.  Stretch out in one of the four king bedrooms or cozy up in the fifth bonus bedroom with a twin bed - all equipped with brand new mattresses.  Enjoy your morning coffee on either of the TWO balconies overlooking the backyard and entertain your group in the large kitchen, dining, and living areas.  If you need a break from the fun, sneak upstairs and sink into the LoveSac in the third floor den for a quick recharge!

Guest access
The entire the second and third floor is yours!  Your private entrance from the exterior leads you right upstairs to your living space.

Other things to note
Don't be afraid to reach out if you need anything!  We live super close-by and are always happy to help out, even mid-stay.
