=== FULL DESCRIPTION ===
Nestled off of quiet Jones Lane within steps to beautiful Calhoun Square, this charming carriage house is the perfect hideaway in Historic Savannah.

The space
Nestled off of quiet Jones Lane within steps to beautiful Calhoun Square, this charming carriage house is the perfect hideaway in Historic Savannah. Start your stay beneath the majestic oak trees on Calhoun Square, where your feet touch cobblestone and the cicadas make the urban soundscape. Two Lucky guests will have the opportunity to see Savannah like a local - Red Door Hideaway is truly a hidden gem!

After entering through the lane, and making your way up to your hidden oasis, you'll step into a warm, airy and inviting space. Red Door Hideaway has been thoughtfully furnished to ensure guests have everything they need, which includes historic and modern finishes that tie the space together. 

The queen size bed is centered in the middle of the room, with a large smart television mounted for your enjoyment at the foot of the bed. Just across, you'll have a quaint kitchenette, equipped with a Keurig, mini-fridge, and microwave; perfect for this cozy carriage house! After a long day of exploring our beautiful city, rinse the day away in the spa-like walk-in shower, and enjoy an evening glass of wine on your private balcony. 

Everything you need can be reached on foot. The home is equally distanced between the Historic District’s two most popular focal points, River Street and Forsyth Park, just steps away from Calhoun Square. Grab breakfast at Clary's, a well-known diner in Savannah, or eat lunch with the locals at Mrs. Wilkes Boarding House just a few blocks East. You’ll love the proximity to the exciting events at the Civic Center, shopping at City Market, and more.

1 Queen Bed

What is parking like for this home?
Parking for this home is on-street and metered, which is enforced by the City of Savannah from Monday through Friday, 8am to 5pm. Guests may conveniently pay using the ParkSavannah app, feed the meter or parking kiosks directly, or park on a nearby non-metered street. Please take note of posted Sweep Zone signs when parking on the street.

Great experience! 
This adorable little spot was exactly as pictured and described. It’s a charming carriage house in a good location. It is small, but perfect for one or two guests. It had everything I needed for a stay of several days, was comfortable and very clean, private, and perfect location for walking everywhere. Bonus: it’s located right next to a wonderful vintage bookstore called Books on Bay.
Couple of things to note:
Entry is up a wrought iron staircase. Take in consideration for luggage. I was able to carry my large suitcase and other bags, but other guests may not be able to. 
Bathroom is tiny, but very useable and was immaculately cleaned. 
Not a full kitchen, but had the necessities like coffee maker, sink, microwave, and small fridge. Who needs to cook while on vacation in Savannah?!
Now here’s the best part about this place, the host. They were extremely communicative, helpful, thoughtful, and proactive. You can’t ask for better service!
