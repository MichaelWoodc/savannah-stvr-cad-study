=== FULL DESCRIPTION ===
Built in 1854, this stunning Savannah row home blends historic charm with modern luxury. Spacious and serene, it features 2 bedrooms and 2.5 baths on two levels. As you walk into the front door at the top of the exterior stairs, you will enter the parlor level, which boasts elegant living, dining, and a chef’s kitchen opening to a balcony and courtyard. Upstairs, two bedrooms with gorgeous views, two full bathrooms, ample storage, and laundry. The perfect blend of modern and classic!

SVR-03156

The space
Experience Savannah at its finest in this extraordinary 1854 row home, where historic architecture meets modern luxury. Thoughtfully renovated with soaring windows, airy design, and high-end finishes throughout, this 2-bedroom, 2.5-bath residence is as spacious as it is stunning.

On the main, parlor level, you’ll find gracious entertaining spaces: a sunlit living room, dining room, and a chef’s kitchen with a gas range and premium appliances. French doors open to a balcony overlooking the home’s unreal courtyard — a true urban sanctuary.

The top floor features two bedrooms and two full bathrooms. The primary suite boasts a king bed, en-suite spa bathroom, and private balcony. The second bedroom offers a versatile queen Murphy bed, doubling as a quiet home office if needed. A second laundry room is also conveniently located upstairs.

Guests enjoy fast WiFi, flat screen TVs with cable and streaming services, and tranquil views throughout the home.

Nestled on Taylor Square in the heart of Savannah, this residence is steps from the iconic Clary’s Café and directly across from the historic Massie Schoolhouse. Whether sipping coffee in the courtyard, entertaining in the parlor, or wandering Savannah’s charming streets, this quintessential row home is the perfect blend of past and present.

Guest access
Guests have full access to the home, including the laundry room, the chef’s kitchen, balconies, and the courtyard. Please note -- the courtyard can also be accessed by guests staying in the carriage house, across the courtyard, so it may be shared with up to two people at times. If you need a bit more space, check our other listing, which also includes the garden level suite! Here's a link for quick reference: airbnb.com/h/modernluxuryhistoricbones

Other things to note
Don't be afraid to reach out if you need anything!  We live super close-by and are always happy to help out, even mid-stay.
