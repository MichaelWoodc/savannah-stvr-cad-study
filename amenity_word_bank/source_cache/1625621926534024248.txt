The space
Haint & Porcelain A Romantic Retreat Across from Colonial Park Cemetery

Haint & Porcelain is a romantic, ground-level retreat set directly across from Colonial Park Cemetery, where Savannah’s earliest stories rest just beyond your doorstep. Bathed in soft blue tones inspired by Southern haint traditions, the apartment is richly layered with porcelain, chinoiserie, antiques, and old-world detailing. Decorative molding, curated objects, and thoughtful styling create a space that feels intimate and storied, less like a rental and more like a hidden gem in the Historic District.



Thoughtfully Designed Living Spaces

The living room flows into a well-appointed kitchen anchored by a generous butcher-block island, ideal for slow mornings with coffee or relaxed evenings at home. The kitchen windows allow in gentle natural light and add to the cozy atmosphere of the space.



Fully stocked with modern appliances, cookware, and essentials, the kitchen makes dining in easy and comfortable. A discreet washer and dryer are conveniently tucked just beyond the kitchen area for added convenience during longer stays.



A Restful Private Sanctuary

Toward the rear of the apartment, the bedroom offers a quiet retreat away from the street. A queen canopy bed dressed in soft, hotel-grade linens sits beneath warm lighting, creating an atmosphere that feels both indulgent and calming.



The en-suite bathroom features a walk-in shower and marble vanity, offering a refined yet relaxed space to refresh after a day exploring the city.



Location & Experience

Step outside and you are immediately immersed in one of Savannah’s most atmospheric blocks. Evening ghost tours pass along the cemetery gates, Spanish moss drapes overhead, and history feels present in a way that is subtle and authentic. Just two short blocks away, the shops, dining, galleries, and energy of Broughton Street await. From morning pastries to late-night cocktails, everything you love about downtown Savannah is within easy walking distance.



This is a place for romantics, history lovers, and those drawn to beauty with depth, a space where the past lingers softly and the present is meant to be savored.



Parking Information

On-street metered parking is available directly outside the home and throughout the neighborhood, enforced by the City of Savannah Monday–Saturday, 8am–8pm. Guests may pay via the ParkSavannah app, parking kiosks, or meters. Public garages and visitor parking passes are also available nearby. Please observe posted sweep-zone signage when parking.
