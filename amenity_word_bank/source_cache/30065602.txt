The space
Step Inside One of the Top Savannah Vacation Rentals



Suite Escape is deliciously compact and cute. It fits two guests between its one bed and single bathroom. This is one of those properties with just the right amount of space for a single traveler or pair. You'll love the way this studio-style property puts everything at arm's length. The property's sleeping area features a queen-sized bed that can only be described as plush and comfortable. In addition, comfortable seating is provided. A flat-screen television is visible from the bed. The flooring inside Suite Escape is a new, fresh style of tile that keeps things cool during those hot Savannah nights.



This property's efficient kitchen area is totally fresh and updated. It features new cabinets, new countertops and new appliances. A classic drip coffee maker waits on the kitchen counter to help you get some pep in your step before taking on Savannah each morning. Of course, you won't see the feature that makes this property one of the best Savannah vacation rentals until you step outside. Suite Escape features a patio that overlooks Forsyth Park. This is a truly enviable feature that only a few Savannah visitors ever get to experience. Feel free to enjoy breakfast at the adorable bistro table on the patio. Free Wi-Fi and cable are included with this rental.

How Visitors Make the Most of Staying at Savannah Vacation Homes



The city is at your fingertips when you have one of the best vacation rentals Savannah GA visitors can choose. The obvious choice would be to begin your day by strolling just a few feet to reach Forsyth Park. Lush greenery, inviting walking paths and gorgeous fountains wait for you in this beautiful and historic park. A walk in the park is only just the beginning. You can then move on to a robust itinerary that includes visits to fascinating spots like Bonaventure Cemetery, the Cathedral of Saint John the Baptist, Pin Point Heritage Museum, the American Prohibition Museum and the Webb Military Museum. You can also head to River Street for amazing restaurants, bars, shops and entertainment options. Even just spending some time admiring the homes, parks and specialty museums in the famous Savannah Historic District can be a real treat. Don't forget to check out haunted tours after dark if you're interested in seeing the spookier side of Savannah. The vacation rentals Savannah GA visitors choose to stay at also place them in the perfect spot for making excursions to nearby Tybee Island to swim, tour lighthouses or play in the sand.



Anyone renting a vacation home for a little while in Savannah should look into the prospect of attending one of the city's very fun festivals. Savannah actually hosts many themed festivals throughout the year. These acclaimed events honor food, reading, art, Southern culture and so much more. Visitors can also look forward to fireworks in the summer and decorated mansions during the holiday season. The list of the year's biggest highlights includes the St. Patrick’s Day Parade in March, the self-guided Savannah Tour of Homes and Gardens in March, the River Street Fourth of July Celebration, the Pin Point Seafood Festival in September, Oktoberfest in October, the Savannah Film Festival in October, the Up the Cup New Year’s Eve Celebration and the Savannah Book Festival in February.
