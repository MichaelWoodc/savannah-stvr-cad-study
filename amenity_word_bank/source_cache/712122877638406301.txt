=== FULL DESCRIPTION ===
Best exotic Magnolia Inn is a beautiful Victorian townhouse that was separated into 2 apartments (duplex) years ago. This is the first floor apartment. The home features 12 foot ceilings, oversized windows, and front and back porches as well as access to a shared back yard. Charming details remain intact and we have enjoyed mixing antiques, vintage finds and modern pieces to make the home unique and inviting. Casper beds and lovely linens welcome you back from your days of touring.

SVR 01515

Guest access
The entire 3 bedroom unit is for your use with a private entrance and private back porch. The backyard is shared. You will receive a welcome email 2 days before your arrival with the code for the front door and other detailed instructions. Parking is unassigned street parking but it is easy and ample.

Other things to note
Parking is currently on the street in front and side of the property. With the exception of St. Patrick’s Day, parking is ample and easy!

Neighborhood highlights
Savannah is an incredibly fun place to visit! We do provide a list of favorite restaurants and activities but it's an amazing place to just wander as well. Best, Exotic is just a few blocks east of Forsyth Park and one block south of the Landmark Historic District so you will be walking distance from all that the Landmark and Victorian Districts have to offer. Savannah has a vibrant arts and events scene so many weekends feature special festivals, but the city is always filled with interesting activities. We have a guide book at the house as well as some suggestions for food and fun online but let us know if you have any specific questions we can answer!
