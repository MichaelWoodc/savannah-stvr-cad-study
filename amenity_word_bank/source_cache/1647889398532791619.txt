=== FULL DESCRIPTION ===
Wayne Mansion Estate is an extraordinary multi‑residence property just a block away from Forsyth Park, offering four fully private living spaces arranged around a large, beautifully furnished and fully enclosed courtyard. Built in 1871, the estate features a two‑story historic main house with a garden‑level two‑bedroom apartment beneath it and two carriage house units located across the courtyard.

The space
The property offers a perfect mix of communal spaces and privacy, ideal for large groups who want to gather comfortably while still enjoying their own retreat.

Four Private Residences

All four units are reserved exclusively for your group, offering a total of nine bedrooms, ten beds, seven full bathrooms, three half baths, and four fully-equipped kitchens.

Main House (3 Bedrooms with King Beds, 3 Full Baths + 1 Half Bath)  
A two‑story residence showcasing original wood floors, a grand historic staircase, ornate ceiling medallions, intricate crown moldings, and marble fireplaces. Includes elegant formal living room, formal dining room with seating for six, cozy TV room, furnished covered porch, and fully equipped kitchen and pantry with six burner range, two beverage chillers, and island seating for four.  

Garden‑Level Apartment (2 Bedrooms with King Beds, 1 Full Bath + 1 Half Bath) — Located directly beneath the Main House, this private ground‑floor apartment has its own entrance and furnished porch, a fully equipped full kitchen, one full size sleeper sofa in a room with doors, and direct courtyard access.  

Ground‑Floor Carriage House (2 Bedrooms with Queen Beds, 1 Full Bath + 1 Half Bath) — Situated across the courtyard from the Main House

Upstairs Carriage House (2 Bedrooms with Queen Beds, 2 Full Baths) — Located above the ground‑floor carriage house and also across the courtyard from the Main House, this two‑bedroom residence offers elevated courtyard views  

Outdoor Features
- Large furnished and fully enclosed courtyard ideal for gathering, relaxing, or enjoying morning coffee  
- Side yard offering additional green space and privacy  
- Two private off‑street parking spaces, a rare amenity in this neighborhood

Bedroom 1 (Main House) - King bed, TV, walk-in closet, attached dressing room with vanity and extra large mirror, en-suite bathroom with double vanity, claw foot tub and oversized shower
Bedroom 2 (Main House) - King bed, TV, west-facing windows for beautiful afternoon light
Bedroom 3 (Main House) - King bed, TV, en-suite bathroom
Bedroom 4 (Garden Level) - King bed, TV, direct courtyard access, en-suite dual-entry bathroom shared with Bedroom 5
Bedroom 5 (Garden Level) - King bed, TV, en-suite dual-entry bathroom shared with Bedroom 4
Bedroom 6 (Lower Carriage) - Queen bed
Bedroom 7 (Lower Carriage) - Queen bed, TV
Bedroom 8 (Upper Carriage) - Queen bed, TV
Bedroom 9 (Upper Carriage) - Queen bed

***Please note that there are additional charges and contract terms required to use the property as an event venue.  Guests interested in using the property for an event should inquire with Arise Property Management before booking.  Guests hosting events not authorized by Arise Property Management will be in breach of contract, resulting in immediate cancellation of the events, fines, and eviction from the property.
