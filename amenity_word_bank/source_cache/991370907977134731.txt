=== FULL DESCRIPTION ===
Experience Savannah's old-world charm in our new, cozy carriage house. Nestled on a quiet lane in the Victorian District, this one-bed, one-bath studio is packed with modern comforts! Unwind in a plush king bed, stay connected with fast WiFi, and relax in this perfect space for two! Enjoy the city's amazing dining options or stay in for the night and take advantage of the fully-equipped kitchen. Private parking space...included! Explore historic Savannah from this idyllic retreat.

SVR 02797

The space
Welcome to our adorable carriage house studio! This newly constructed space is designed for your utmost comfort. The fully equipped kitchen boasts everything you need to whip up a delicious meal, complemented by a cute retro refrigerator. Enjoy your morning brew with our Keurig coffee maker and provided K-cups, sugar, and creamer. The modern sitting area features a wall-mounted flat-screen smart TV with streaming capabilities. Drift off in the luxurious king-sized memory foam bed, freshly made with crisp white linens. The chic, spa-like bathroom offers a walk-in, glass-enclosed shower. Your parking space is conveniently located right outside your door, ensuring easy access throughout your stay.

Guest access
The entire carriage house is all yours! There are no shared spaces or limitations on your access.

Other things to note
Don't be afraid to reach out if you need anything!  We live super close-by and are always happy to help out, even mid-stay.
