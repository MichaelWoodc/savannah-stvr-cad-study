=== FULL DESCRIPTION ===
Nestled within another beautifully designed and historic building by Alfred S. Eichberg, The Alfred Suite is a perfectly refinished two-bedroom, one-bathroom escape in the heart of Savannah. The property features original in-lay floors and classic touches with all your desired modern comforts. Ideal for up to four guests, you will be just steps from Forsyth Park, dining, attractions, and much more. We are RIGHT IN FRONT OF THE START OF ST. PATRICK'S DAY PARADE!!
SVR-01636

The space
Welcome to your historic retreat! Nestled on the first floor of this grand Alfred S Eichberg home, you'll ascend just five steps to your private entrance, where a world of charm and comfort awaits. Step inside to find yourself among one of the beautiful historic homes of Savannah.
The journey begins in the inviting dining area,  filled with natural light streaming through large bay windows, casting a warm glow upon terracotta tile floors, rustic board, and batten walls. A dining table set for four awaits, promising memorable meals and lively conversations.
Venture further into the spacious living room through a large cased opening, where high ceilings, classical décor, and original hardwood floors call you to unwind and relax. Sink into the plush oversized sofa or cozy reading chair, indulge in entertainment on the smart TV, or simply enjoy the setting.
Adjacent lies your fully appointed galley kitchen, adorned with exposed brick, where culinary adventures await. Equipped with a full appliance package, cookware, and dishware, it invites you to unleash your inner chef and create delicious meals at your leisure.
Discover tranquility in the primary suite, where a sumptuous king bed adorned with crisp linens awaits. Bathed in sunlight from large bay windows, adorned with an original brick fireplace, and furnished with ample storage and entertainment options, it promises nights of blissful repose.
Adjacent, find solace in the unit's well-appointed bathroom, offering a shower-tub combination, single vanity, and all the essentials for your comfort and convenience.

Retreat to the second bedroom, boasting a comfortable queen mattress draped in fresh linens, illuminated by an oversized window, and adorned with simple yet stylish décor, ensuring a restful night's sleep. For those blending work with pleasure, fear not – our high-speed WiFi stands ready to support your endeavors, whether video conferencing or multitasking.

Neighborhood highlights
Just one block east of Forsyth Park, you will be in the heart of historic Savannah and centrally located to all the incredible eats, drinks, and sights Savannah has to offer. Stroll into Forsyth Park from your door to enjoy Savannah’s beautiful mornings. Grab breakfast at Collins Quarter at Forsyth, which has indoor and outdoor seating.
Looking for a sandwich and a pint? Stroll on down to the Black Rabbit, a small yet incredible sandwich shop by day, and pub by night. Need a taco fix? Try out Bull Street Tacos for scratch-made Mexican dishes.
Looking for fine dining and a great vibe? Head on over to Local 11 Ten and their chic rooftop bar and lounge known as The Perch. Enjoy handcrafted cocktails and delicious dishes. The Perch is open year-round and features gas heaters for cooler winter days.
Take an adventure outside of the neighborhood by visiting one of Savannah’s many museums. Mercer-William’s House Museum, Savannah African Art Museum, American Prohibition Museum, and many more are just minutes away. Tap into Savannah's artistic nature by stopping into its College of Art and Design Museum of Art. Want to get more hands-on and make some memories? Try blowing glass at Hostess City Hot Glass, a perfect time for the whole family.
Not enough time to walk all the sights of Savannah? Take it all in at once while riding or biking. We are just minutes from Old Town Trolley Tours of Savannah and Savannah Bike Tours. Check out the city’s skyline from the water by jumping on one of the Savannah Belle’s ferries, with multiple stops all along Savannah’s riverfront.
