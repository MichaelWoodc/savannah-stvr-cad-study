=== FULL DESCRIPTION ===
Timeless Savannah architecture, abundant natural light, refinished hardwood floors, and stylish modern updates, Park View offers an unforgettable stay for families and groups seeking space, charm, and an unbeatable location.

The space
Welcome to Park View, a beautifully restored 1875 Victorian home offering two fully renovated apartments—Park View A (first floor) and Park View B (top floor)—combined into one spacious, five-bedroom retreat directly across from the iconic south end of Forsyth Park. With timeless Savannah architecture, abundant natural light, refinished hardwood floors, and stylish modern updates, Park View offers an unforgettable stay for families and groups seeking space, charm, and an unbeatable location.

Guests enter through a shared front porch overlooking Forsyth Park—a perfect place to enjoy your morning coffee or unwind at sunset. Inside, the home provides two comfortable living areas, each with generous seating, flat-screen TVs, high ceilings, and decorative (sealed) fireplaces that enhance the home’s historic character.

Park View features two fully equipped kitchens, each newly updated with stainless-steel appliances, ample counter space, and eat-in dining areas. Whether you’re preparing a celebratory dinner or a simple breakfast before exploring Savannah, the kitchens offer the perfect blend of beauty and functionality.

A Home Designed for Larger Groups: Across its two levels, Park View offers five well-appointed bedrooms and three full bathrooms, creating the ideal setup for multi-family groups or guests needing extra privacy.

With its unbeatable location, flexible layout, and beautifully updated historic design, Park View is the perfect choice for groups seeking comfort, charm, and the trusted hospitality of Lucky Savannah. Whether you're in town for a wedding, a family trip, or a long-awaited getaway, this home offers the quintessential Savannah stay. The home’s thoughtful layout allows your group to spread out while still enjoying shared gathering spaces on both levels.
