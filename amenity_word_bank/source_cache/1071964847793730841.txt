=== FULL DESCRIPTION ===
Fully renovated ground floor apartment in an historic 1854 townhouse overlooking Chatham Square. 
This prime location is just one block from Forsyth Park and walkable to a variety of museums, restaurants and shopping.

The space
This stunning fully renovated garden/ground level suite in an historic 1854 townhome overlooking charming Chatham Square. This prime location is just one block from Forsyth Park and the neighborhood contains some of the city’s best row houses. It’s a short walk from a variety of museums and historic sites, as well as restaurants, grocery stores, and shops.

Living room is open & spacious with comfy seating & a dining table. The newly renovated kitchen features stainless steel appliances and granite countertops. It is fully equipped and ready to whip up a meal or store great take out. 

The bedroom has queen bed and en suite bathroom with large, walk-in shower. Off the bedroom is the private patio with seating for you & the shared courtyard is for all guests in the building. 

Gather for meals in the dining room and admire the Razzia Louis Vuitton posters and the original brick floors and walls. This striking 1854 townhome mixes timeless furnishings with rich patterns and accents for a robust atmosphere with hints of eclecticism.

This townhouse is part of Savannah's historic GORDON ROW. Gordon Row was built in two phases between 1852 and 1854. In the first phase, the 3 or 4 houses on each end of the block were built and in the second phase the middle portion was built. There are a total of 15 houses in the row.

The houses were built in the Federalist (US) or Regency Style (UK). Each house is four stories high and contains about 3,300 square feet. The Federalist Style emphasizes square, classical features, plain decorations and mirror image rooms.
