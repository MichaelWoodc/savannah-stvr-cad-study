Lady Gwinnett

Absolutely stunning from top to toe, Lady Gwinnett is a sterling example of Savannah vacation homes, located in the coveted Forsyth Park neighborhood. With sparkling new accommodations for groups of up to six, including three bedrooms and two and a half baths, plus whole-house Wi-Fi, a fabulous outdoor living space and even a gas grill, the Lady Gwinnett offers the kind of luxurious comfort that makes your Savannah vacation rentals stay a memorable experience.



What You Will Find Inside

A stately marble fireplace claims a place of honor along one wall while the flat-screen television, mounted just above, offers you an entertainment option to enjoy during your vacation rentals Savannah GA stay. A plush sectional and comfortable armchairs provide plenty of seating for everybody in your group.



The open main-floor layout is ideal for enjoying the company of your friends and family during mealtimes and relaxing evenings after busy vacation days. The living area transitions seamlessly to a formal dining room where a big farmhouse-style table supplies an inviting gathering place for communal meals. The adjacent eating bar adds intimate seating for breakfast and snacks, and it is the perfect place to plan your itinerary for your Savannah vacation home stay.



Glass pendant lights with candle-style bulbs add vintage charm to the eating/kitchen area, and floor-to-ceiling cabinets are stocked with all the cookware, utensils, beverage ware, and dishes you could possibly need for the duration of your stay. As with all of our vacation rentals in Savannah GA, this sparkling kitchen features a roomy fridge, range-oven, microwave, and dishwasher, plus countertop extras like a teakettle, coffee maker, and toaster. Whether your group likes to plan and create its own meals or you prefer to eat out and sample all the varied cuisine the South has to offer, you will love the convenience of this full kitchen.



The first floor of Lady Gwinnett also includes a handy half-bath and access to the private, covered back patio where you can enjoy the cushy seating in the cool of the evening. 



On the second floor, you will find three bedrooms furnished with deluxe king-sized beds, side tables with reading lamps and ceiling fans. Charming shuttered windows adjust easily to block or invite the light. The master bedroom has seating space and an enormous walk-in closet where you can store luggage and clothing. The attached bath contains a large marble-tiled shower.



A second full bathroom is central to the other two bedrooms and features a tub/shower combination and a double vanity. All of our Savannah vacation homes include bedding and towels. Just across the hall, you will find a full-capacity washer and dryer for your use throughout your Savannah getaway.  



What to See and Do During Your Visit

One of the city's most popular attractions for visitors in Savannah is Forsyth Park, which is only a few blocks from your Lady Gwinnett accommodation. You will find a variety of activities within this 30-acre greenspace, from shopping for fresh produce at the year-round Saturday Farmer's Market to people-watching at the spectacular fountain at the park's north end. Formal gardens, children's playgrounds, picnic areas, sports fields, and even a café are all part of this popular Savannah destination.



When you want to venture farther afield, you can join other Savannah vacation rental guests on the free Historic District shuttle bus. Board at Forsyth Park and tour the area's 22 historic squares, each of which has its own unique features such as monuments, adjacent museums and neighboring architecture. Originally, each square served as an anchor for the surrounding neighborhoods. Each is named after a figure of historic significance, such as founding father James Oglethorpe and the Marquis de Lafayette.



Another option for touring the district is a ride aboard the Old Town Trolley, which takes you to the far edge of town where the Savannah River ushers cargo ships into the city's busy harbor. Along the way, you can browse the shops and galleries of City Market, refresh yourself at one of the area's eclectic restaurants or follow your sense of adventure wherever it takes you.



Savannah is a city that celebrates 12 months per year, so chances are good you can find an event going on during your stay at Savannah vacation homes. 



No matter what time of year you plan to visit, Lady Gwinnett, one of Southern Belle's top Savannah vacation rentals, provides the perfect home base. Get in touch with us today to make your reservation.
