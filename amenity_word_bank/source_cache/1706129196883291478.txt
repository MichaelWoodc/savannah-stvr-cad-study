=== FULL DESCRIPTION ===
Steps from iconic squares, renowned restaurants, charming boutiques, and historic landmarks, this beautifully restored three-story estate blends classic architecture with upscale modern comfort for an unforgettable stay in the Hostess City of the South

The space
Experience timeless Southern elegance at Elsewhere on Madison Manor, a grand luxury vacation rental in the heart of Savannah’s Historic District designed for wedding parties, family gatherings, corporate retreats, and large group getaways. Perfectly positioned just steps from Savannah’s iconic squares, renowned restaurants, charming boutiques, and historic landmarks, this beautifully restored three-story estate blends classic architecture with upscale modern comfort for an unforgettable stay in the Hostess City of the South.

Ideal for groups of up to 12 guests, Elsewhere on Madison Manor offers an expansive layout with multiple living spaces, elegant entertaining areas, and private accommodations that allow everyone to spread out comfortably. By reserving the entire estate, guests enjoy exclusive access to both the main residence and the private garden-level apartment, creating the perfect setup for bridal parties, multi-family vacations, reunion weekends, or executive stays in downtown Savannah.

Inside the main house, guests are welcomed by soaring ceilings, gleaming hardwood floors, ornate fireplaces, sparkling chandeliers, and curated historic details that capture the grandeur of old Savannah. The spacious formal living room evokes the feel of a classic Southern parlor, while thoughtfully updated interiors provide the convenience and luxury today’s travelers expect.

The main residence features four full bedrooms, one additional partial bedroom, and four full bathrooms across three spacious levels. The main floor includes a beautifully appointed queen suite with a private en-suite bathroom, making it ideal for guests seeking first-floor accommodations. Upstairs, the third-floor suite functions almost like a private retreat of its own, complete with an additional living area, dining space, and a secondary kitchen — perfect for wedding prep, extended family stays, or guests wanting additional privacy.

The fully equipped chef’s kitchen on the main floor serves as the heart of the home, offering modern appliances, ample prep space, and room for gathering over meals and cocktails before a night out in Savannah’s Historic District.

The private garden apartment adds exceptional flexibility for larger groups. Sleeping up to four guests, this separate lower-level retreat includes a queen bedroom, two full bathrooms, and an additional partial bedroom with a Murphy bed — ideal for couples, grandparents, or members of the wedding party who appreciate added privacy while remaining connected to the group.

Outside, guests can unwind in the secluded brick courtyard, one of the home’s most charming features. Surrounded by classic Savannah architecture and lush greenery, this peaceful outdoor space is perfect for morning coffee, evening wine gatherings, or intimate al fresco dinners. A built-in brick grilling station and outdoor dining area make entertaining effortless after a day exploring Savannah’s historic streets. 

Whether you’re visiting for a Savannah wedding weekend, bachelorette celebration, family reunion, holiday gathering, or luxury group vacation, Elsewhere on Madison Manor offers the perfect combination of historic charm, upscale comfort, and unbeatable downtown location. From the elegant interiors to the private outdoor spaces, every detail has been designed to create an authentic and memorable Savannah experience.
