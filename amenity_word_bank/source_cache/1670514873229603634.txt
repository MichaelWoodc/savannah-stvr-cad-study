We are so excited for your Savannah getaway in this beautifully designed new construction home in the Victorian District. It is a fabulous space for groups and families traveling together. The space
Welcome to this grand new construction Savannah retreat designed for gathering, relaxing, and experiencing the city in style.

Located on West Anderson Street in the Victorian District, you are centrally located between the Historic and Starland Districts. This beautifully appointed home offers spacious living, timeless character, and thoughtfully curated interiors throughout. Perfect for families, group getaways, or longer stays, the home blends historic charm with modern comfort in a way that feels both inviting and luxurious.

Inside, you’ll find multiple living spaces designed for connection and comfort, along with a fully equipped kitchen and dining area ideal for shared meals and entertaining. Each bedroom is designed as its own retreat, offering comfort, privacy, and a place to unwind after a day exploring Savannah.

Step outside and you’re just a short stroll to Forsyth Park, one of the city’s most iconic destinations. From there, continue on to nearby coffee shops, restaurants, boutiques, and historic squares that make Savannah so special.

Whether you’re gathering with friends, traveling with family, or simply looking for a beautiful place to call home during your stay, The Anderson House offers the space, location, and experience to make it unforgettable.

We can’t wait to host you!
