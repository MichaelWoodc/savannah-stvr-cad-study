=== FULL DESCRIPTION ===
Located in one of Savannah's most desirable secured residential buildings, Editor's Cut sits on the third level, with views of Bay Street and Ellis Square. Guests are just steps away to every downtown attraction in this spacious two-bedroom apartment.

The space
Located in one of Savannah's most desirable secured residential buildings, Editor's Cut sits on the third level, with views of Bay Street and Ellis Square. Guests are just steps away to every downtown attraction in this spacious two-bedroom apartment.

After reaching the third floor via elevator access, step in to your bright Savannah escape, equipped with all of the amenities you could wish for. 

The kitchen is fitted with granite countertops, stainless steel appliances and provides plenty of space to prepare home-cooked meals. The breakfast bar is open to the main living space, ideal for hosting and entertaining your guests while keeping the conversation flowing. Guests will appreciate having this dedicated dining space with seating for 4, and a fully-stocked kitchen that fits all of your needs.

The living space consists of two plush sofas, a flatscreen television, fitted with a reading nook. Gather together at the end of the day, and catch up on your favorite shows. In the morning, open up the balcony door, and feel the gusts of the Savannah River while drinking your cup of joe.

The spacious Primary Suite sits off of the living space and offers a queen-size bed. Enjoy having a walk-in closet, smart TV, and en suite bathroom with a large dual vanity, a walk-in shower, and a jacuzzi tub. 

Your guests should not fret, as the Guest Suite features a plush queen-size bed, a walk-in closet, and en suite bathroom. Just across the hall, a full-size washer and dryer is available in the unit for convenience. 

Step across the street, and head down to River Street where you will enjoy stunning views of the Savannah Riverfront with open beverage container in hand... then walk South towards the charming Historic District with plenty of local and name-brand shops to pass the time. This apartment is ideal for those wanting to be in the heart of it all!

What is parking like for this home?
You'll have access to your own parking space in the nearby Whitaker Street Garage with 24/7 access.

I recently enjoyed my stay at Lucky Savannah and it was exceptional. This condo is perfectly located between a Five Guys and a Starbucks. The drop-off points at the corner streamline arrivals, whether from the airport or a ride service.
Check-in was seamless with the electronic keypad. Initially unsure about locking up upon leaving, I found the host incredibly responsive, resolving my issue promptly. Just tapping anything on the keypad was the solution!
Location-wise, it's a gem—one block from the scenic Riverwalk, 1.5 blocks to Market Place and a mere seven-minute walk to bustling Broughton Street, filled with shops and restaurants. If you prefer not to drive, Uber is easily available.
The condo itself was spotless and matched online descriptions perfectly. It’s well-stocked, so there’s no need to bring extras. The filtered water from the refrigerator was a thoughtful touch, eliminating the need for bottled water.
As a solo traveler, I felt completely safe!
