=== FULL DESCRIPTION ===
One of our Savannah vacation rentals to consider on your Georgia vacations is the Habersham House. This is a nearly 2,500 square foot property with three bedrooms and two and a half baths. It's a fantastic location for vacation homes Savannah between East Perry Street and East Perry Lane, which puts it near the Colonial Park Cemetery and a variety of good restaurants like the Bier Haus and Screamin' Mimis.

The space
Finding things to do near your vacation homes Savannah won't be hard when you're in walking distance of many historic sites. Right nearby the Habersham House is the Colonial Park Cemetery, which covers about six acres and is now more of a park, since it has been closed to new burials since the mid-1800s. Deep in the historic district is four renovated blocks that is now known as the City Market. This is one of the top visited places in downtown Savannah and features jazz clubs, restaurants, many open-air cafes, boutiques and gift shops. Another top attraction in Savannah is the 26 acres that constitutes Forsyth Park. Prominently featured in the well-known book and movie "Midnight in the Garden of Good and Evil," this park really shines in the spring time when all the flowers are in bloom. The famous iron fountain completes the park known as the "pride of Savannah" and is modeled after a similar fountain in Paris. Another feature from the "Midnight in the Garden of Good and Evil" book and film is Monterey Square and The Mercer House. This house is available for tours and also has a museum. A quick way to see Savannah is to take one of the many tours. You can choose from horse-drawn carriages, pedal-cabs and guided walking tours among others.



Savannah is always full of things to do and various festivals and celebrations happen all year round. The following is a short list of events that might be available:



• Annual Martin Luther King, Jr. Day Celebration in January

• Georgia Heritage Society's Colonial Faire and Muster in February

• Savannah Music Festival in March

• Fourth of July Fireworks on the Beach in July

• Seafood & Music Festival in August

• Savannah Jazz Festival in September

• Savannah Harbor Boat Parade of Lights in November

• Holiday Tour of Homes in December
