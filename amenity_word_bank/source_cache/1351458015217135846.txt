=== FULL DESCRIPTION ===
Gaston Haus is a modern yet rustic carriage house offering a peaceful retreat while keeping you just moments from Savannah’s vibrant Historic District.

The space
Tucked away beyond majestic oak trees and cobblestone streets, Gaston Haus is a modern yet rustic carriage house offering a peaceful retreat while keeping you just moments from Savannah’s vibrant Historic District. Newly constructed and thoughtfully designed with high-end furnishings and elegant fixtures, this one-bedroom hideaway blends modern comfort with timeless Southern charm.

What Makes Gaston Haus Special?

Unbeatable Location: Step outside and stroll through Savannah’s most picturesque streets, where the clip-clop of horse-drawn carriages echoes beneath canopies of centuries-old oaks. Whether sipping morning coffee in nearby Taylor Square or enjoying an evening walk past historic mansions, you’ll be perfectly positioned to experience the city’s best dining, shopping, and sightseeing.

Refined Luxury & Thoughtful Design: Though intimate in size, Gaston Haus feels spacious and bright, with natural sunlight streaming through every window. Every detail, from the sleek galley kitchen to the spa-inspired bathroom, has been carefully curated for comfort, relaxation, and style.

Tranquil Sleeping Quarters: The plush queen-sized bed is fitted with luxury linens and blackout shades, ensuring a restful night’s sleep after a day of exploring Savannah’s historic squares, galleries, and riverfront charm.

Sleeping Configuration:
Bedroom: Queen Bed

What is parking like for this home?
Parking for this home is on-street and metered, which is enforced by the City of Savannah from Monday through Friday, 8am to 5pm. Guests may conveniently pay using the ParkSavannah app, feed the meter or parking kiosks directly, or park on a nearby non-metered street. Please take note of posted Sweep Zone signs when parking on the street.
