=== FULL DESCRIPTION ===
Under new management as of fall 2025, this historic Savannah home has been fully renovated in the summer of 2025  and professionally redesigned with all-new furnishings, upgraded HVAC and insulation, refreshed millwork, lighting, paint, and décor. Featuring five bedrooms, four bathrooms, and a balcony overlooking Forsyth Park, it blends modern comfort with timeless charm. Managed by Verdant House, a local Superhost team known for design and hospitality excellence. 

SVR-01722

The space
This is a large stand-alone home! It's a 5-bedroom, 7 beds plus a queen size sofa bed pull-out, and 5-bathroom beauty with multiple sprawling porches and a balcony with views of Forsyth Park. Also, 3 out of 5 of our bedrooms have ensuite bathrooms. The house is recently fully renovated and features an open living area with a kitchen that is fully stocked. This beautiful place, which is right on the shuttle route, will be your perfect home away from home in historic downtown Savannah.

The house is large and right off of Forsyth Park. The living space has a sectional couch with an open floor plan. The kitchen is fully stocked.

Forsyth Park is right around the corner. There are amazing restaurants, coffee shops, and grocery stores very close by.

The house is right on the route of the free shuttle.
