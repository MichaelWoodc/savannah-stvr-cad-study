=== FULL DESCRIPTION ===
Discover romance and adventure at this historic, Italianate Victorian Mansion in Savannah, Georgia. Affectionately called Maisonette La Belle, this one-bedroom apartment is perfect for up to four visitors. Learn more about our Savannah vacation rentals so that you can unwind, explore and renew yourself on that next trip.

The space
Stepping Into a Modern Garden Apartment



Start your vacation off without any hassle at our private entrance. Use the keypad and check-in feature to access the property. Meeting with a manager isn't part of your first day at Savannah vacation homes. Inside, walk into the cheerful living room with two club chairs and a comfy sofa. With a flat-screen TV and storage throughout the living room, you can easily unpack as you enjoy a favorite show.



At our vacation rentals Savannah GA, preserving the home's architecture but offering modern conveniences is our priority. That's why Maisonette La Belle includes the original staircase as an accent piece in the hallway. Beneath it, enjoy the half-bath as you entertain loved ones in the common space.



Nearby, slide open the barn door to reveal the master bedroom. It has a queen-size bed, additional linens and extra blankets for your convenience. Naturally, the bedroom accommodates two people. For your other two guests, the living room sofa opens into a bed in the evening.



Continue onto the en suite bathroom. Inside, there's a single vanity and upgraded tile shower. Towels, a hair dryer, hangers and other accessories are included, too.



Throughout the property, find safety resources, such as a lockbox and outdoor lighting. Feel safe with the included fire extinguisher, carbon monoxide detector, first aid kit and smoke detector as well. For your comfort, Savannah vacation homes include air conditioning, heating, multiple ceiling fans and Wi-Fi connectivity.



Dine casually or formally in the spacious dining room. Seating for four guests under a dazzling chandelier creates a romantic mood every evening. Connected to the dining area is the modern kitchen. Indeed, this kitchen includes the essentials, such as plates, silverware, pots and pans. Also, use the major appliances for grand meals or snacks. The property includes a refrigerator, stove, oven, microwave, dishwasher and coffee maker. Bring your own groceries to start cooking.



For added convenience, the kitchen is also home to a laundry washer and dryer. Fitting snugly beneath the kitchen countertop, these appliances also complement the included iron and ironing board. Simply access these accessories in the nearby storage closet.



Relaxing With the Outdoor Amenities



Our vacation rentals Savannah GA include two outdoor areas for your convenience. First, the shared courtyard offers a huge, open-air space for spreading out on warm evenings. Enjoy the table and chairs, wood-burning fireplace and the nearby fountain. Plenty of potted plants decorate the courtyard for a charming time unwinding with loved ones.



Next, a private patio awaits just outside your dining room's side door. Here, you'll find lounge chairs and a covered patio. With a brick walkway and a small garden nearby, this private retreat is perfect for reading a book or enjoying the breezes across the property.



Remarkably, our Savannah vacation rentals include pool access. Located at 19 Silver Avenue, Tybee Island, Georgia, a resort-style pool with lounge chairs and an outdoor grill are available to all guests. Because it's exclusively reserved for our guests, there's plenty of space to spread out. Bring your personal items, and enjoy a day by the pool as the oversized umbrellas provide a shady contrast to the sunny deck.



Venturing Into the City and Beyond



Located in the Victorian District East, Maisonette La Belle is near some of the top landmarks in Downtown Savannah, such as Forsyth Park. Indeed, simply walking to the area's shops, restaurants and coffee shops is possible. Consider seeing some of the museums in the region, such as the Mercer Williams House Museum or Savannah History Museum. Fascinating walking tours are also available, too. For example, discover Savannah's architecture during a day tour or experience a ghost tour during a twilight walk.



An old-fashioned experience involves Savannah's famous horse-drawn carriages. Perfect for an early-evening ride, take a carriage around the nearby parks and historic homes. Because of the romance behind these carriages, the experience matches nicely with your Savannah vacation itinerary. If you have a special occasion, make an advanced reservation. These rides are popular and can be booked up rapidly.



Venture a bit further to the Savannah River. Referred to as River Street, this promenade is home to many shops and restaurants. Pick up a Southern souvenir here as you look out on the boats dotting the river. Depending on the month, different festivals are held along this route. From brew fests to Halloween carnivals, River Street is a hub for both tourists and locals alike.



Because you have access to our heated pool on Tybee Island, consider a day to explore this region. Referred to as Savannah's beach, Tybee Island has a laid-back style. Typically, visitors enjoy the beach, explore the pier and experience the nightlife. Tybee Island is about 20 to 30 minutes away from your vacation home, so you can drive out in the morning and return in the evening without too much time spent on the road.



Experiencing Maisonette La Belle with its modern upgrades creates a timeless adventure for your next vacation. Booking Savannah vacation homes is convenient with our online reservation process. Pick your dates, and privately check in on your chosen days. Welcome to your Southern retreat circa 1880.
