The space
Welcome to Elsewhere on Madison Square Main, a grand historic Savannah retreat overlooking iconic Madison Square in the heart of the Historic District. Designed for wedding parties, family gatherings, executive retreats, and upscale group getaways, this beautifully restored luxury home combines timeless Southern charm with spacious modern comfort — all within walking distance of Savannah’s best restaurants, shops, nightlife, museums, and historic landmarks.

Perfect for groups of up to 10 guests, this expansive three-story residence offers elegant gathering spaces, multiple living areas, and thoughtfully designed accommodations ideal for spending quality time together while still enjoying privacy and room to spread out.

Inside, guests are welcomed by soaring ceilings, gleaming hardwood floors, ornate fireplaces, elegant chandeliers, and historic architectural details that capture the essence of old Savannah. Rich textures, classic furnishings, and abundant natural light create an atmosphere that feels both sophisticated and inviting.

The home features four full bedrooms, one partial bedroom, and four full bathrooms spread across multiple levels. The main floor includes a beautifully appointed queen suite with a private en-suite bathroom, ideal for guests seeking first-floor accommodations. Upstairs, the third-floor suite offers additional flexibility with its own living area, dining space, and secondary kitchen — perfect for wedding prep, extended families, or guests wanting additional privacy during their stay.

At the heart of the home, the spacious chef’s kitchen is fully equipped with modern appliances, generous prep space, and everything needed for group meals, cocktails before dinner, or slow mornings enjoying coffee together before exploring Savannah.

Step outside into the private brick courtyard, one of the home’s most charming features. Complete with a built-in grilling station and outdoor dining area, this secluded outdoor space is ideal for evening gatherings, celebratory dinners, or simply unwinding beneath the glow of Savannah’s historic ambiance.

Whether you’re planning a Savannah wedding weekend, bachelorette celebration, holiday gathering, or luxury Historic District getaway, Elsewhere on Madison Square Main offers an unforgettable stay filled with elegance, comfort, and unbeatable location.
