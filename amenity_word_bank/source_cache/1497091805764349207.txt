=== FULL DESCRIPTION ===
Some trips are about the places you visit. The best ones are about the people you share them with. Welcome to The Henry House, a beautifully restored 1900 Victorian just a short walk from Forsyth Park. With 4 king bedrooms, 2 kitchens, 2 living rooms, 3 bathrooms, 2 parking spaces and 2 covered porches, it's thoughtfully designed for families and friends to gather, reconnect, and experience Savannah together—all while enjoying the comfort of having space to spread out.

The space
# THE SPACE

Welcome to **The Henry House**, a beautifully restored 1900 Victorian where historic charm meets modern comfort.

Your group will enjoy the **entire main home**, which is one private residence spanning **two levels**. The home is connected by an interior staircase, making it ideal for families and friends traveling together while also providing a little extra privacy between floors.

**Main Floor**

* 2 King bedrooms
* 2 Full bathrooms
* Fully equipped kitchen
* Comfortable living room
* Covered front porch
* Washer & dryer

**Upper Floor**

* 2 King bedrooms
* 1 Full bathroom
* Fully equipped kitchen
* Comfortable living room
* Covered front porch
* Washer & dryer

With **4 king bedrooms, 3 full bathrooms, 2 kitchens, 2 living rooms, and 2 covered porches**, everyone has room to gather while still enjoying their own space to relax.

One of the things guests love most about The Henry House is its thoughtful layout. Share breakfast before a day exploring Savannah, gather for conversation after dinner downtown, or enjoy a quiet cup of coffee on one of the porches while the rest of the group sleeps in.

### Historic Character, Modern Comfort

Originally built in 1900, the home has been carefully restored to preserve its beautiful Victorian architecture while offering today's modern conveniences, including:

• High-speed Wi-Fi

• Smart TVs

• Fully stocked kitchens

• Washer & dryer on both floors

• Central heating and air conditioning

• Complimentary parking for two vehicles

Please note that because this is a historic two-story home, **guests staying in the upstairs bedrooms will need to use the interior staircase**. If anyone in your group has mobility concerns, we recommend assigning them one of the two king bedrooms located on the main floor.

Whether you're reconnecting with lifelong friends, traveling with family, celebrating a special occasion, or discovering Savannah for the first time, The Henry House was thoughtfully designed to bring people together.

Guest access
You'll have exclusive access to the entire main home, including both the main floor and upper floor of this beautifully restored Victorian.

Main Floor:
• 2 King bedrooms
• 2 Full bathrooms (including one private ensuite and one hall bathroom)
• Fully equipped kitchen
• Comfortable living room
• Covered front porch
• Washer & dryer

Upper Floor:
• 2 King bedrooms
• 1 Shared full bathroom
• Fully equipped kitchen
• Comfortable living room
• Covered front porch
• Washer & dryer

The two levels share a welcoming front foyer. Each floor also features a lockable interior door, allowing you to enjoy the home as one spacious residence while providing additional privacy between floors whenever desired.

Please note that guests staying in the upstairs bedrooms will need to use the staircase.

You'll also enjoy two designated parking spaces.

For the safety and security of our guests and property, exterior security cameras monitor the front entrance, rear entrance, and parking areas. There are **no cameras or recording devices inside the home.**

The home is equipped with both front door and backyard cameras for added security.

NOTE: The separate carriage house located behind the main home may be occupied during your stay. It has its own private entrance, parking area, and outdoor space, allowing both homes to enjoy privacy.

Other things to note
THINGS TO KNOW:

We want every guest to have a wonderful stay at Kenneth Manor, so here are a few helpful things to know before booking:

**Historic Home**
Kenneth Manor was originally built in 1900 and has been thoughtfully restored to preserve its historic character while offering modern comforts. As with many historic homes, you may notice charming architectural details and unique features that reflect its history.

**Two-Story Home**
The home spans two levels connected by an interior staircase. Two king bedrooms are located on the main floor and two king bedrooms are upstairs, making it an ideal layout for families, multiple couples, and friends traveling together.

**Parking**
Two designated parking spaces are included with your reservation. Additional street parking may also be available nearby, subject to city regulations.

**Sensitive Plumbing**
Like many historic homes, Kenneth Manor has older plumbing. To help prevent backups, please flush **toilet paper only**. Baby wipes, paper towels, feminine hygiene products, "flushable" wipes, and other items should be placed in the waste bins provided.

**Quiet Hours**
To help preserve the peaceful character of our neighborhood, we kindly ask that guests observe quiet hours between **9:00 PM and 9:00 AM**.

**Smoke-Free Home**
Kenneth Manor is a **100% smoke-free property**, including cigarettes, cigars, vaping, marijuana, and all other smoking products.

**Weapons & Illegal Substances**
For the safety and comfort of all guests, **firearms, weapons, and illegal substances are not permitted anywhere on the property.**

**Pets**
Approved pets are welcome! Please let us know before booking if you plan to bring a pet so we can confirm your reservation and apply the appropriate pet fee. Undisclosed pets are not permitted.

**Packages & Mail**
Packages and mail cannot be delivered to the property. If you need to receive an item during your stay, we're happy to recommend nearby Amazon Lockers, UPS Stores, or other secure pickup locations.

**Security Cameras**
For the safety and security of our guests and property, exterior security cameras monitor the front entrance, rear entrance, and parking areas. There are **no cameras or recording devices inside the home**.

**Please Review Our House Rules**
Before booking, we kindly ask that you review our complete House Rules. They cover important information about the home and help ensure a comfortable stay for you, your fellow travelers, and our neighbors.

We look forward to welcoming you to Kenneth Manor and hope your stay is filled with wonderful memories.

Neighborhood highlights
The Kenneth Manor is located in Savannah's beautiful Victorian District, one of the city's most charming and walkable historic neighborhoods.

Just a short stroll from the iconic Forsyth Park, you'll find yourself surrounded by tree-lined streets, beautifully preserved Victorian homes, cozy cafés, local bakeries, boutiques, neighborhood restaurants, and the timeless charm that makes Savannah so unforgettable.

Start your morning with coffee from a nearby café before wandering beneath the famous live oaks of Forsyth Park. Spend the afternoon exploring Savannah's historic squares, unique shops, museums, art galleries, and River Street, then enjoy an easy walk home through one of the city's most picturesque neighborhoods.

Unlike staying in the busy downtown core, The Henry House offers the best of both worlds—a peaceful residential setting where you can truly relax, while still being close enough to experience everything Savannah has to offer.

Whether you're visiting for a weekend getaway, reconnecting with family, or exploring the city with friends, you'll quickly discover why so many visitors fall in love with this neighborhood.
