=== FULL DESCRIPTION ===
Elegant second-floor flat just off Lafayette Square in Savannah’s Historic District. Filled with large windows and beautiful natural light, this home showcases original hardwoods, classic architectural details, and a spacious living area with a statement fireplace.

Enjoy a stylish kitchen with stainless steel appliances and a large island, plus a private balcony overlooking the historic streets. With 3 bedrooms (1 king, 2 queens), 2 full baths, and in-unit laundry, it’s the perfect home base.

The space
This elegant second-floor flat blends historic Savannah charm with modern comforts. Featuring a sunlit bay window, ornate columns, and a grand fireplace, the open living space is perfect for relaxing after a day of exploring. The fully equipped kitchen boasts custom green cabinetry, stainless steel appliances, and a spacious island. With three comfortable bedrooms (1 king, 2 queens) and two classic bathrooms, the home comfortably accommodates up to six guests. Located just off Lafayette Square in the heart of the Historic District, you’ll be steps from Savannah’s most iconic landmarks.

Guest access
Guests have a private access and will receive a key code prior to arrival to enter the home. This keycode is changed after every guest for your safety.
