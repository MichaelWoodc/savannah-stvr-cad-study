=== FULL DESCRIPTION ===
Welcome to The Quaint Quarters on Jones, a charming two-bedroom, one-bathroom ground-floor apartment nestled in the heart of historic downtown Savannah. This cozy retreat blends timeless character with modern comforts, featuring historic details and a lovely, shared courtyard. Just minutes from Forsyth Park and steps away from dining, shops, and top attractions, it’s the perfect spot to experience Savannah’s charm, culture, and vibrant city life. Book now for an unforgettable stay! SVR-02637

The space
Enter Quaint Quarters on Jones through the main entrance, leading to a charming, shared brick-lined courtyard, where you'll find your private ground-floor entrance. Inside, you're immediately greeted by historic timber beams and the original fireplace casing, immersing you in the rich history of Savannah. The cozy living room features a comfortable sofa facing a smart TV for streaming your favorite shows, while a quaint dining set for two doubles as a perfect workspace.
The efficient kitchen is fully equipped with modern appliances, including a 24-inch range, and thoughtfully stocked with cookware, utensils, and dishware. The original, white-washed brick adds a lovely architectural touch, complementing the kitchen’s historic charm.
Off the kitchen, you'll find the first bedroom, boasting a brand-new queen mattress draped in crisp linens. The white-washed brick and exposed beams create a serene atmosphere for a restful night’s sleep. The adjacent bathroom features a low-entry shower, custom tilework, and a single vanity, stocked with essentials like towels, toiletries, and more.
Directly off the living room is the spacious primary suite, complete with a new king-size mattress, plush pillows, and fresh linens. The room also offers ample storage with a dresser, perfect for longer stays, and has a private entrance leading outside for added convenience.
Enjoy the perfect balance of historic charm and modern comfort in this thoughtfully designed space!

Guest access
The entire place is set up for your use! There is a shred courtyard in the rear that you may use at your leisure. The access to washers/dryers is through the courtyard as well.

Neighborhood highlights
The Quaint Quarters is located just north of Forsyth Park on the west side of the Historic District. Surrounded by some of the best dining options in town, you are just a minute's walk from many of the local and out-of-town favorites.
Grab a delicious southern bite and Mrs. Wilke's Dining Room. Pour a perfect pint at the Crystal Beer Parlor, or start your day with a short walk to Savannah Coffee Roasters.
Short strolls towards the river walk will only increase your options with places like Noble Fare, fine dining, The Olde Pink House, The Lady & Sons, Chart House, and many more.
Start your bar/pub tour by stopping into some staples of Savannah, including Six Pence Pub, O’Connell’s Irish Pub, or Dub’s Public House. Enjoy riverfront views at Myrtle & Rose Rooftop Bar down on the river walk.
Are you looking to shop while you are in town? You are just a 14-minute walk from the famed City Market, where you can enjoy endless boutique shops and many brand-name stores.
Explore the rich history of Savannah by stopping into one of the many museums and historical sites. Just minutes from Blue on Broad are the Web Military Museum, Telfair Museums, Owens-Thomas House & Slave Quarters, and the American Prohibition Museum.
A stop in Savannah wouldn’t be complete without a walk-through of Forsyth Park, just a 20-minute walk away. Or enjoy Washington Square, another beautiful green space just minutes away.
Want to take it all in at once while riding or biking? We are just minutes from Old Town Trolley Tours of Savannah and Savannah Bike Tours. Ready for a little on-the-water adventure? Try out the Savannah Belles Ferries to see the beautiful skyline of Savannah from the riverfront.
