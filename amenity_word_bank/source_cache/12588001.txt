=== FULL DESCRIPTION ===
Wake each morning to views of Savannah's graceful live oaks before stepping outside to explore one of America's most walkable historic cities. Perched inside the historic Bird Baldwin House, Bird Baldwin Forsyth Suite blends timeless architecture with modern comforts, offering a relaxing home base just steps from Savannah's renowned restaurants, museums, squares, and shopping.

The space
After a day spent wandering beneath Spanish moss, touring historic landmarks, or enjoying cocktails downtown, return to your quiet retreat overlooking the leafy canopy. With secure building access, keyless self check-in, and a dedicated parking space behind the building, every part of your stay is designed to feel simple and enjoyable.



Bird Baldwin Forsyth Suite is located on the third floor of the historic Bird Baldwin House and is accessible by stairs only. Once upstairs, you'll be rewarded with charming views through Savannah's live oak canopy and a peaceful atmosphere that feels pleasantly removed from the bustle below. The inviting living room offers a comfortable place to unwind after a day of sightseeing. Comfortable seating includes a sofa and loveseat, while large windows frame picturesque oak branches that remind you you're truly in the heart of historic Savannah. A cozy bistro table for two creates the perfect setting for morning coffee, an afternoon snack, or an evening glass of wine while planning tomorrow's adventures. Stream your favorite shows or enjoy cable television as you unwind after exploring the city.



The fully equipped kitchen provides everything needed to prepare meals throughout your stay. Featuring a full-size refrigerator, additional mini refrigerator, microwave, dishwasher, two-burner cooktop, and stacked washer and dryer, the space combines thoughtful functionality with convenience for both short getaways and extended visits. Whether you're preparing breakfast before heading out or enjoying a quiet dinner after a full day downtown, you'll have everything you need close at hand.



When it's time to rest, the spacious primary bedroom offers a welcoming retreat anchored by a comfortable king bed designed for restful nights after long walks through Savannah's historic streets. Soft surroundings and a peaceful atmosphere make it easy to unwind, while the bedroom television with cable and streaming allows you to settle in for a relaxing evening whenever you choose to stay in. The bathroom features updated finishes, a classic pedestal vanity, and a beautifully tiled walk-in shower. Bright, clean, and thoughtfully designed, it provides everything needed to refresh before another day exploring Savannah.



Near the rear entrance of the condo, guests will find outdoor seating designated for the unit. While this shared area is not considered a private outdoor space, it offers a pleasant place to enjoy the fresh air, sip your morning coffee, or relax for a few moments before heading out to experience the city.



One of the greatest advantages of Bird Baldwin Forsyth Suite is its exceptional location. Situated on Liberty Street in the center of Savannah's Historic District, many of the city's most recognizable landmarks, museums, restaurants, and shops are just a short walk away. Start each morning with a fresh cup of coffee from Savannah Coffee Roasters next door before setting off to explore Savannah on foot. Spend your days wandering picturesque squares, browsing local boutiques, touring historic homes, or lingering over long lunches before returning to your comfortable home away from home.



Forsyth Park is approximately ten minutes away on foot, offering beautiful gardens, the famous fountain, shaded walking paths, and the popular Saturday farmers market. Walk about ten minutes to Broughton Street to discover local boutiques, galleries, and shopping, or continue fifteen minutes to River Street, where historic cobblestone streets meet waterfront restaurants, entertainment, and scenic river views.



History lovers will appreciate being just five minutes from the Telfair Museum and only a short stroll from the Green-Meldrim House, the Sorrel-Weed House Museum, and many of Savannah's beautifully preserved historic landmarks. Nearby Pulaski Square provides one of the city's most picturesque settings for leisurely walks beneath towering live oaks draped with Spanish moss. Dining options surround the neighborhood. Enjoy Southern favorites at Mrs. Wilkes' Dining Room, gather for dinner at The Public Kitchen, or experience one of Savannah's oldest restaurants at Crystal Beer Parlor. As evening arrives, enjoy handcrafted cocktails at Artillery Bar, rooftop views at Peregrin, or a lively atmosphere at McDonough's before returning to your peaceful retreat.



Savannah offers unforgettable experiences throughout the year. Spring welcomes blooming gardens, the internationally known St. Patrick's Day celebration, and the Savannah Music Festival. Summer brings outdoor dining, riverfront evenings, and Fourth of July festivities. Autumn features the Savannah Jazz Festival and SCAD Film Festival, while winter transforms the city with Savannah Holly Days, the River Street Christmas Market, and the beloved Lighted Boat Parade.



Rich in history, thoughtfully furnished for comfort, and perfectly positioned for exploring Savannah on foot, Bird Baldwin Forsyth Suite captures everything travelers love about staying in one of the country's most beautiful historic cities. We look forward to welcoming you and helping you create memories that will keep drawing you back to Savannah for years to come.
