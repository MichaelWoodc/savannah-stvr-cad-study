=== FULL DESCRIPTION ===
The Loft on Taylor

The space
Magnolia trees, scampering squirrels, gas lights, and shady foliage line the brick sidewalk facing the entry of this lovely second-floor apartment situated in a quiet neighborhood within the coveted downtown historic district. Inside, the main living area, featuring high ceilings, built-in cabinetry, and modern leather furnishings, is the perfect place to curl up with a favorite mystery novel or catch up with old friends over glasses of local wine or refreshing cocktails. Enjoy a good movie on the large flatscreen cable TV with plenty of hot microwave buttered popcorn and oven-baked jalapeño poppers. Savor your first cup of coffee on the Juliet balcony while watching squirrels scurry about in the morning breeze. As you enjoy the sun spilling in through the front bedroom windows, keep an eye out for a glimpse of the occasional trolley or horse-drawn carriage! Spend an afternoon touring the nearby shops, cafes, restaurants, and museums just a short walk away from your front door then come home to research your next Savannah adventure with the free WiFi while drifting off in the comfort of a downy-soft bed.



What's nearby:

This home is nestled in Savannah's prestigious downtown historic district, so there's no shortage of amazing activities you'll find during your stay. Enjoying a truly unbeatable location, you'll be just one-and-a-half blocks from the Savannah squares, one-third of a mile to Forsyth Park, and less than a mile from the Savannah River. It's also just a short walk from restaurants, like neighborhood favorites Treylor Park and The Olde Pink House (both under one mile away). Historical landmarks Monterey Square, Whitefield Square, and Calhoun Square are all within one mile of the home as well!



Things to know:

Free WiFi and cable

Full kitchen

Snowbird friendly

Street parking only.
