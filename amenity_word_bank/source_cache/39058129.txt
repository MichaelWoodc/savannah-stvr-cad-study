=== FULL DESCRIPTION ===
Built in 1852, this 2nd-story Carriage House suite, is ever-so charming. With windows overlooking Charlton Lane and onto the beautiful courtyard, this property is very popular with guests. Originally  built to house staff and  horses, today, our carriage house has been renovated to provide a warm, cozy and sophisticated lodging, complete with modern amenities which are sure to please the most discriminating luxury traveler.

SVR-01498
For concierge services visit 120WestJones.com

The space
The interior has the original exposed beams, hardwood floors and brick walls.  Immediately upon entering the quarters, guests are delighted with the hunting and equestrian décor which pays tribute to the Carriage House’s 19th century role in the life of the household. Paintings of horses, dogs, birds, rural landscapes and hunt scenes grace the walls, as well as carved wooden deer heads, antlers and vintage duck decoys. The suite includes a kitchenette that houses all the modern appliances you will need for your stay – mini refrigerator/freezer, toaster, coffee maker, kettle and microwave oven.  The original wooden staircase leads guests to the master suite, complete with a comfortable queen size bed, furnished with Parachute linens, in-suite bath, generous closets, in-suite washer and dryer, and a private sitting area. The owners have gone to great lengths to provide furnishings that create an understated sophisticated, warm and inviting space to appeal to today’s discriminating luxury traveler.

SAFETY:
