The space
Welcome to The Squares of Savannah, a stunning collection of five unique and thoughtfully designed apartments located in a prime location. Surrounded by a beautifully manicured courtyard and garden, this property offers a charming retreat in the heart of Savannah’s historic district.

The Madison is a charming carriage apartment in this historic home—a spacious 1-bedroom, 1-bathroom unit that comfortably accommodates up to 2 guests. Just across from the picturesque Forsyth Park, this apartment is furnished with tasteful finishes, blending historic charm with modern comfort.

The bedroom features a plush queen bed, with an ample amount of storage and a window overlooking the charming courtyard. The bathroom offers a walk in shower and ample storage space, ensuring a comfortable and convenient stay for all.

What Makes The Squares of Savannah Special?

- Unbeatable Location: Perfectly situated on Forsyth Park, guests can enjoy Savannah’s largest green space just outside their door. The park is ideal for morning strolls, weekend farmers’ markets, and leisurely afternoons. Additionally, the property is just steps from local coffee shops, renowned restaurants, and the vibrant Starland District.

- Enchanting Outdoor Spaces: The lushly landscaped courtyard provides a serene space for all guests to enjoy. Whether you’re savoring a morning coffee, an evening glass of wine, or simply soaking in the beauty of Savannah, this shared garden area offers a peaceful escape.

The Madison at The Squares of Savannah promises a memorable stay filled with charm, comfort, and the perfect blend of historic elegance and modern luxury.
