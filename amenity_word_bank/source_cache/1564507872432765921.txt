The space
The Artist Retreat: A Stylish, Comfortable Stay in Downtown Savannah

Welcome to The Artist Retreat, a bright and beautifully designed two-bedroom, one-bathroom apartment located in a historic Victorian-style duplex in the heart of downtown Savannah. This thoughtfully curated space provides a comfortable setting for a romantic trip, a family getaway, or a weekend with friends.



The open layout creates a natural flow between the living, dining, and kitchen areas. Colorful accents and curated artwork give the home personality and charm, while comfortable furnishings make it easy to unwind after a day exploring the city. The living room centers around a smart TV and cozy seating, creating an inviting place to gather, relax, or enjoy a movie night.



The kitchen is fully equipped for both casual and more elaborate meals. Modern appliances, sleek countertops, and ample storage make cooking easy and enjoyable. The dining table seats six, and the island seats three, offering flexible space for meals, morning coffee, or conversation.



Comfortable Bedrooms and Flexible Layout

Both main bedrooms feature queen canopy beds with soft linens and calming décor, creating peaceful retreats at the end of the day. A third room with a twin daybed offers additional sleeping space for a child or guest, making the apartment ideal for families or groups. Down the hall, a sunlit library provides a quiet corner to read, sip tea, or enjoy a moment of solitude.



The full bathroom has been thoughtfully designed with a modern, spa-like atmosphere. A sleek, tiled walk-in shower, contemporary fixtures, and clean, elegant finishes create a serene space for relaxing and refreshing after a day of exploring Savannah. Plush towels and thoughtful touches enhance the luxurious feel, making your daily routine a true retreat.



A washer and dryer are available within the apartment, making longer stays convenient and stress-free.



Savor Savannah Outdoors

Step outside to a charming outdoor courtyard, a peaceful retreat tucked away from the energy of downtown. This inviting space, shared with the other unit in the duplex, features comfortable seating, a dining table with an umbrella, and lush greenery. It’s the perfect spot for enjoying morning coffee, al fresco meals, evening cocktails, or simply relaxing outdoors with a good book while soaking in the quiet ambiance.



Convenient Downtown Location

The Artist Retreat is located in the East Historic District, a walkable area of downtown, just steps from local squares, cafés, and parks. Forsyth Park is about a ten-minute stroll away, perfect for picnics, people-watching, or capturing picture-perfect moments. From Forsyth, you can hop on the free DOT trolley, which takes you throughout downtown and makes navigating the city effortless.



Free street parking is available directly in front of the home. You can park your car and explore Savannah at your own pace, enjoying the tree-lined sidewalks, beautiful architecture, and vibrant local culture.



Local boutiques, coffee shops, restaurants, and charming squares are all within a short walk from the apartment. Whitfield Square and Mother Matilda Beasley Park are nearby as well, offering additional green spaces to unwind.



Modern Amenities for a Smooth Stay

Keyless entry makes check-in simple and secure. The apartment includes a smart TV, a fully equipped kitchen, a washer and dryer, and a peaceful courtyard to enhance your stay. With plenty of space to relax and spread out, it’s perfectly designed for both quick visits and longer stays.



From the comfortable living spaces to the inviting outdoor area and the exceptional walkable location, The Artist Retreat offers a stylish and convenient base for your Savannah getaway. Book your stay and enjoy a beautifully designed home that puts all the best of the Historic District right at your doorstep.
