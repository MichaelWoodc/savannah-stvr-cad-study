Crawford Chic + Coastal lives up to its name in every way possible! This brilliant three-bedroom home envelopes up to 8 overnight guests in luxury. While spacious and airy, this gorgeous property never loses its "antique coastal" appeal. With a list of modern amenities that includes 3.5 bathrooms, it stands out as one of the most practical Savannah vacation rentals. The fact that this lovely townhome home consists of a spacious one-bedroom bedroom Garden Apartment and a two-bedroom Main House means that guests can enjoy the right mix of "togetherness" and privacy when exploring the city with friends or family. There's also a sunny porch right off the Main House kitchen that's perfect for making sweet Southern memories with the group. Of course, what makes this home stand out among all other Savannah vacation rentals is its enviable location right next to Crawford Square.



Starting with the Main House nestled within Crawford Chic + Coastal, guests can step into a large living space that's been updated from top to bottom. This portion of the property has a more formal personality compared to its accompanying Garden Apartment. It's easy to feel right at home on a plush sofa facing a television. After taking in this perfect gathering space, turn your eyes to the state-of-the-art kitchen that merges with the living room to create a wonderfully social layout. This all-white kitchen with new appliances in stainless steel really takes one's breath away with its crisp, polished craftsmanship. Beautifully designed cabinets topped with stone are positioned perfectly among a full-sized stove, refrigerator, microwave, dishwasher, and coffee maker. There's a dark-wood center island with additional seating to accompany the home's formal dining table with seating for four. The Main House also offers a sunny porch with seating located just off of the kitchen. This portion of the property can accommodate up to six guests.



The Main House offers two beautiful bedrooms for guests to choose from when planning their enchanted slumbers in Savannah. The first is a king bedroom with its own en-suite full bathroom featuring double vanities and a glass-enclosed shower. This room also has its own smart TV. The second bedroom is a king bedroom that also features its own en-suite full bathroom with a tub/shower combination. Finally, additional sleeping space can be found on two twin air mattresses tucked away in the home. A half bathroom is conveniently located near the main living area. The list of amenities included with the Main House includes a washer/dryer and gated off-street parking for one to two vehicles.



The Garden Apartment portion of this property can fit up to four guests. Like the Main House, it greets visitors with a spacious, open living area with a large television. There's also a dining table with room for four. Enjoy a large, fully equipped kitchen with a refrigerator, oven, dishwasher, and microwave in stainless steel. Guests can easily whip up everything from full meals to coffee in this welcoming kitchen.



The king bedroom in the Garden Apartment pampers guests with a smart TV. Guests can also experience luxury in a full bathroom with vanities and a glass-enclosed shower. For additional space, two twin air mattresses are tucked away. This portion of the home has its own washer and dryer. Guests can utilize street parking.

  

Both the Main House and Garden Apartment offer tons of natural light that's carried into the property using large windows and glass doors. Both also provide an elevated living experience among Savannah vacation homes. From high-end wood flooring to dazzling light fixtures, every inch of Crawford Chic + Coastal has been updated to create a magazine-quality final finish.



Guests who choose two Savannah vacation homes in one by booking Crawford Chic + Coastal will enjoy the extravagant luck of being located next to many of Savannah's best attractions! This home is nestled right next to famous Crawford Square. One of Savannah's 22 squares, Crawford Square was named in honor of Secretary of the Treasury William Harris Crawford. Its manicured layout includes a playground, basketball court, and gazebo. As you walk past Crawford Square, the rest of Savannah gently unfolds to invite guests on historic adventures through leafy streets lined with homes that seem to whisper their secrets with the light breeze. Top items to include in a Savannah itinerary include the complete Savannah Historic District, Forsyth Park, the Cathedral of Saint John the Baptist, Bonaventure Cemetery, River Street, Pin Point Heritage Museum, Wormsloe Historic Site, and much more! Of course, anyone looking for vacation rentals Savannah GA visitors can use to experience the city's great festivals and events is probably already considering the Savannah Music Festival, Savannah Jazz Fest, the River Street Food Festival, the Annual North of Gaston Street (NOGS) Tour of Hidden Gardens, and more.



Guests booked at Crawford Chic + Coastal enjoy access to a private pool located just 30 minutes away in beautiful Tybee Island. This resort-style heated pool offers a great place to relax while touring the coastal wonders of Tybee during a day trip. It's just one of many reasons why Crawford Chic + Coastal is one of the best vacation rentals Savannah GA visitors looking to experience everything the area has to offer can book! Don't miss this property when looking for Savannah vacation rentals with room for up to 8 guests!
