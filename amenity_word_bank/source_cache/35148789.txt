In a city with such a vivid history, many Savannah vacation homes attempt to capture this unique essence. If you’re searching for an accommodation that’s both convenient and filled with true Savannah charm, then 515 Liberty Carriage may fit the bill.

The space
Designed to evoke a sense of stylish serenity, this luxurious rental home transports guests into a world rarely offered by Savannah vacation homes. Located on East Liberty Street, the very best of Savannah is only a few steps away. Whether you wish to take a stroll and enjoy the historic architecture of a bygone era or dive into one of the many bars and restaurants, 515 Liberty Carriage holds the key to a vacation that’s uniquely you.



As you navigate the masterfully planned streets in the nation’s largest historic downtown area, you’ll feel the air around you change. With every passing block, the worries and stress of the daily grind fade into the background. After parking the car and stepping out into the vibrant Historic District East neighborhood, you’re greeted by a newly constructed building that promises an unforgettable adventure. The next step is the greatest. It’s this step into your temporary home that you’re officially on vacation.



While the majority of vacation rentals in Savannah GA are equipped with the standard comforts home, few elevate these familiar amenities like the 515 Liberty Carriage. Immediately upon entering this refined carriage house, you’ll feel like you’ve entered the pages of a luxury magazine.



If you’re tired of stale and predictable interior designs offered by most Savannah vacation rentals, the sleek styling and unique furnishings placed throughout the 515 Liberty Carriage is a welcomed change. The well-appointed living room delivers a hefty dose of style and comfort. Enjoy an intimate conversation on the plush sofa or watch your favorite movie on the mounted HDTV.



Constructed as an open floor plan, the living room and kitchen flow into each other. This celebrated design style ensures even if you’re stuck preparing a meal, you can socialize with your loved ones in the living room. The dark hardwood floors highlighted by brightly colored walls create an airy environment, while the numerous windows flood the interior spaces with natural light.



Should you choose to make a homemade meal instead of walking to one of the many acclaimed restaurants in the neighborhood, you’ll enjoy a chef-inspired kitchen. Like many vacation rentals in Savannah GA, 515 Liberty Carriage comes with everything you need to make a light breakfast or a decadent dinner.



Located off the kitchen is the stackable washer and dryer, which is an excellent amenity if you have kids, or a clumsy friend. While the living spaces are brilliantly designed when the time comes to rest from your adventures, get ready to experience yet another level of sophistication.



With one bedroom and bathroom, 515 Liberty Carriage comfortably sleeps four guests. The bedroom features a luxurious queen bed, which comes with everything needed for a heavenly night of rest and relaxation. As you’d expect from quality Savannah vacation homes, the bedroom offers ample storage for luggage and clothing in its closet and dresser.



While the two guests lucky enough to snag the bedroom get to enjoy the dreamscape of luxury linens, other guests aren’t unlucky. Located in the living room is a surprisingly comfortable air mattress. In just a few minutes, the public space transforms into another bedroom. As with the master bedroom, the air mattress comes with linens and pillows chosen to rejuvenate exhausted travelers.



What constitutes a perfect vacation? Is it the attractions and entertainment options? Perhaps it’s the cultural history and overall atmosphere of a destination that transforms a bland visit into an epic getaway. No matter what you demand in a vacation, all of us require one essential element: bliss.



Whether you’re looking for a quick weekend excursion with your loved one or an extended adventure with friends and family, there’s a historic coastal city guaranteed to delight. Savannah, GA., may sound familiar, but until you experience the eclectic atmosphere, delicious cuisines and historic charm of this well-preserved port city, it’s impossible to grasp the unique qualities of Savannah.



Savannah isn’t your standard historic city. While its popularity may seem new, this vibrant coastal area has played an important role in human development for over 400 years. Beloved by Native American tribes, specifically Guale, Choctaw and Creeks tribes, these lands served as vital hunting grounds and community centers. However, the city as we know it today began in 1733 by British philanthropist and Parliament member, General James Edward Oglethorpe.



As the oldest city in Georgia, Savannah has a rich history of going against the grain. In 1862, the nation’s first emancipation proclamation was given by Union General David Hunter. While President Lincoln revoked this act, historians believe this proclamation pressured Lincoln to give his infamous Presidential Emancipation Proclamation one year later. This is just one example how this distinguished coastal city marches to the beat of its own drum.



Nowadays, we can enjoy the fruits of this labor. With over 100 unique neighborhoods, beautiful public spaces and an eclectic vibe, it’s no wonder over 13 million visitors flock to this otherworldly place. While booking a trip to Savannah is an almost guaranteed good time, it’s not just coming to the city that makes a perfect vacation. Like any other major vacation spot, where you stay is what makes or breaks a trip.



As you search for vacation homes Savannah GA, few offer the unparalleled convenience of 515 Liberty Carriage. Perfectly positioned in one of the most desirable neighborhoods, you’re only several blocks in any direction from the very best attractions this city offers. Whether you want to explore the haunted past lurking behind every corner or treat yourself to an exciting night on the town along the famed River Street, 515 Liberty Carriage gives you immediate access.
