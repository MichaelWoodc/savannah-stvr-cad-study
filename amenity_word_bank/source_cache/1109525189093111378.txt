=== FULL DESCRIPTION ===
Welcome to our downtown Savannah retreat, the perfect homebase for your stay! This condo is located on the second floor of a classic townhome, steps from Forsyth Park, the Mercer Williams House and all of the downtown sites our city has to offer! Spacious bedroom with king sized bed and walk-in closet, comfy living room with pull-out sofa for an extra guest, tastefully renovated bathroom, and eat-in, updated kitchen awaits you! Historic charm, unbeatable location, and oh-so-Savannah!

SVR-02735

The space
Ascend the exterior staircase and enter the foyer of this timeless, Savannah townhome. Climb up the interior stairs, where you will find the private entrance to your condo and let the vacation begin! Once inside, you will be greeted with an large and open living room space, completed with flat-screen TV, pull-out sofa, and tons of class! Towards the front of the home, step through the original double doors (a staple in Savannah period architecture!) and into the spacious bedroom with king sized bed, floor to ceiling windows (with incredible views of the live oak lined street and square below!) and huge walk-in closet with vanity! Hungry? We've got you covered! Full, eat-in kitchen with lots of thoughtful touches (not to mention fully stocked!) awaits! Dining for four at the adorable table, enjoy a cup of coffee from the Keurig (kcups, creamer, and sugar provided, of course!), or a nice dinner in. Our thoughtfully renovated bathroom features a spa-like glass enclosed shower and so much style! Staying with us for a while? No worries, laundry-facilities in the condo are there if you need them! But the best part is our location! We love offering a cozy, comfortable space for guests, but the real fun begins when you discover how walkable and centrally located this condo is from all the fun, history, and dining downtown Savannah has to offer! Cheers y'all, we can't WAIT to host you!

Guest access
The entire second floor condo is all yours and completely private.

Other things to note
Parking can be tricky in downtown Savannah! The parking options nearby are paid street parking, which can either be paid using the kiosks/meters, the Park Savannah App, or by purchasing a day pass from the city. We will share all the details regarding navigating parking with you upon booking! There are also some free parking options a few blocks away that we will also give you more info about!
