Welcome to our charming carriage house at on Bolton lane, nestled in the heart of historic Savannah, GA. This two-story retreat blends modern comfort with classic southern charm, perfect for your stay.
Short-Term Vacation Rental # SVR-00390

The space
Step into a welcoming living area complete with contemporary couches, a stylish coffee table atop a cozy rug, and a flat screen TV for your entertainment. The kitchen boasts stainless steel appliances, granite countertops, and a convenient bar stool seating area, perfect for casual dining. A washer and dryer are discreetly located just off the kitchen for your convenience.

The second floor offers two serene bedrooms. The first bedroom on the left features a comfortable queen-sized bed, while the second bedroom boasts a luxurious king-sized bed and another flat screen TV. A shared full bathroom and a cozy desk nook complete this level. The carriage house accommodates up to 4 guests with its 2 bedrooms and 1 bathroom.

Guest access
The carriage house private entrance is located on Bolton Lane. There is 1 parking spot available on Bolton Lane in front of the garage door.

Neighborhood highlights
Situated just steps from downtown Savannah’s finest attractions, you’ll be surrounded by top dining spots such as Local Ten Eleven, Leoci’s, and The Mansion on Forsyth. Enjoy the convenience of trolley tours, eclectic shops, and the vibrant events held at Forsyth Park, including the popular jazz festivals and free concerts. A pleasant stroll takes you to River Street, while a short 20-minute drive brings you to the sandy shores of Tybee Island for a day at the beach. For coffee enthusiasts and grocery runs, The Sentient Bean, Brighter Day, and Kroger are all just a minute’s drive away.

Experience the best of Savannah’s historic charm and modern convenience at our inviting carriage house. Book your stay today!
