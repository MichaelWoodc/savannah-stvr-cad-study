SVR-01971 

Located in the very heart of Historic Downtown Savannah, this stylish 2BR apartment puts you within walking distance of the most popular and trendy area shops, restaurants, coffee shops, galleries, and entertainment venues. Comfortably sleep four guests.  The space is best suited for travelers who want to experience Savannah like a local - right in the heart of the nightlife and entertainment Savannah is best known for.

The space
This gorgeous two-bedroom, one-bathroom apartment is on the 2nd-floor of an 1860 historic building on Broughton Street. The spacious 1,272-square-foot unit features original hardwood flooring throughout and original exposed brick in the bedrooms. 

Conveniently located above the Savannah Bee Company and Coffee Fox, you’ll enjoy close proximity to everything downtown Savannah has to offer. Walk to popular area shops, restaurants, museums, art galleries, and entertainment venues - including Jepson Center for the Arts, Lafayette Square, and City Market.

Parking is typically found on the street at the rate of $2 an hour. Street parking is free between the hours of 8pm and 8am (Mon-Sat and all day Sunday) North of Liberty St.  South of liberty St., parking is free between 8pm and 8am (Mon-Fri) and all day on Sundays.  You can also find parking at Whitaker Street Parking Garage, which is only two blocks away.  The garage's daily rate is $16.  Lastly, you can also purchase a visitor parking pass, which is good for 24 ($15) or 48 hours ($24).  For more information, please visit this link: savannah. com/parking-in-savannah/

Guest access
Guests will have access to the apartment and all of its amenities using the provided codes.  The building and apartment codes are sent at 3pm on your arrival day.  Guests are not allowed to enter the premises prior to the approved check-in time.

Internal doors with electronic locks lead to storage closets; please do not attempt to open.

Other things to note
The Home was built in 1860 and is located in a very vibrant part of downtown.  Nearby cafes, restaurants, and bars are at your fingertips, but this does mean there is some city noise.  To make your stay as comfortable as possible, we have installed brand new, custom-built windows with additional CitiQuiet panels to dampen the noise.  We also include noise-dampening curtains, sound machines, and earplugs for our light-sleeping guests.  The best of city living!

This is a 2nd-floor apartment, so please be mindful of the 25 steps to the apartment entrance.

Parties, pets, and smoking are absolutely prohibited.
