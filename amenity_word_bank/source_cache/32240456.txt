=== FULL DESCRIPTION ===
Feel right at home in this hidden gem behind historic Jones Street. Guests will enjoy this light-filled, one-bedroom apartment with full kitchen and living area.

The space
Some of Savannah's most coveted charm can be found off the main path, like this beautifully newly renovated one-bedroom carriage house located on historic East Jones Lane. Guests will enjoy private lane access to this light-filled home, just a short walk from the city's most popular restaurants, squares, and attractions.

Enter into the home and find a fully equipped kitchen featuring all the essentials, adjacent to a spacious living area with a wall-mounted flat-screen TV. The sofa pulls out to a Queen sleeper, offering additional space for guests.

Up a set of stairs, the full bath and bedroom await. The bathroom features a spacious walk-in shower, and a washer and dryer are conveniently located in a closet just off the bedroom. The bedroom includes a comfortable King-size bed, creating a relaxing retreat after a day of exploring.

Start your day with a complimentary cup of Savannah Coffee Roasters coffee before heading north toward the city center. A stroll along Bull Street boasts some of the city's most famous squares and green spaces, passing through iconic landmarks such as The Cathedral of St. John the Baptist and the Sorrel-Weed House. Grab breakfast at Clary’s Cafe, a beloved Savannah staple just a short stroll away - the entire Savannah experience is all within reach.

Parking:
Parking for this home is on-street and non-metered on nearby Taylor Street.

Sleeping Configuration:
Bedroom : King Size Bed
Living Room: Queen Sleeper Sofa

Please note: This home does not include courtyard access.
