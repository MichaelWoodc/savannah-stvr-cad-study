Welcome to your perfect Savannah getaway! This beautifully curated one-bedroom condo is nestled inside the iconic Graham Building, built in 1924 and overlooking one of Savannah's most beloved squares, Oglethorpe Square. 

The moment you step inside, you'll feel the warmth of nearly a century of Southern history blended seamlessly with thoughtful modern updates designed for today's traveler.

This mid-century modern retreat offers the ideal balance of vintage character and contemporary comfort. Original architectural details have been lovingly preserved throughout, while fresh updates ensure you enjoy every modern convenience during your stay. Large windows flood the space with natural light and frame stunning views of the tree-lined square below, where you can watch the world go by from the comfort of your own living room.

The spacious living area features a comfortable sofa, a smart TV with streaming apps, and a curated selection of reading materials - perfect for a cozy night in. 

The separate bedroom is anchored by a plush king bed with premium linens, ample closet space and a large dresser so you can wind down in total comfort after a day exploring Savannah's cobblestone streets.

The fully equipped kitchen has been thoughtfully updated with everything you need to prepare meals during your stay. An electric stove, oven, full-size refrigerator, microwave, Keurig coffee maker, toaster, blender, tea kettle, and a generous supply of cookware, dishes, and utensils make cooking a pleasure. 

Enjoy your morning coffee at the kitchen dining bar while planning the day's adventures, or prepare a full dinner with fresh ingredients from nearby markets.

The updated bathroom features an original cast-iron shower and tub combo with modern fixtures, stocked with complimentary shampoo, conditioner, body soap, and fresh towels. A hair dryer and iron are provided so you're always ready for dinner reservations or a night out on the town.

Located directly on Oglethorpe Square, you are steps away from Savannah's finest dining, shopping, and nightlife. The famous Owens-Thomas House & Slave Quarters museum is right across the square. Walk two blocks to the Olde Pink House, stroll to Leopold's Ice Cream in under a minute, or explore Broughton Street's boutiques and galleries. River Street, Forsyth Park, City Market, and dozens of historic squares are all within an easy walk from your front door.

Whether you're planning a romantic escape, a solo adventure, a girls' trip, or a relaxing business stay, this historic condo delivers the location, character, and comfort to make your Savannah visit truly unforgettable.

Neighborhood highlights
Oglethorpe Square is one of Savannah's six original colonial squares, established in 1742, and remains one of the most coveted addresses in the Historic District.

The condo sits inside the Graham Building, directly facing the square and adjacent to the famous Owens-Thomas House & Slave Quarters, one of the finest examples of English Regency architecture in America.

You are genuinely in the heart of it all: Leopold's Ice Cream is half a block away, the Olde Pink House is two blocks, Broughton Street's boutiques and restaurants are a three-minute walk, City Market is about seven minutes on foot, and River Street is a 12-minute stroll.

Forsyth Park and its iconic fountain are a pleasant 15-minute walk south through the squares. Nearby dining favorites include The Grey, The Olde Pink House, Mrs. Wilkes Dining Room, Husk Savannah, Zunzi's, and Treylor Park.

Coffee lovers will enjoy The Coffee Fox, Gallery Espresso, and Blends. Colonial Park Cemetery, the Telfair Museums, the Jepson Center for the Arts, and the SCAD Museum of Art are all within a few blocks.
