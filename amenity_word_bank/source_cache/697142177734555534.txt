If beautiful surroundings make you feel right at home, you’re sure to love Duckie's Cottage. An artfully decorated Savannah vacation rental, Duckie's Cottage offers accommodations for up to six, a full kitchen, and visual delights wherever you look. From the welcoming front porch to the fabulous location in Historic Savannah, this wonderful option as a vacation rental in Savannah GA is set to exceed your expectations.

The space
Duckie's Cottage is a true artist's retreat located in the midst of great shopping, dining, museums, and more on East Broad Street. It's a wonderful choice for guests seeking Savannah vacation rentals with room for up to four people. This two-bedroom, one-bathroom showstopper boasts a vibrant interior full of artsy, eclectic décor. Once settled in, guests enjoy a shared courtyard with seating on the deck in the back of the home. The home also boasts a classic Savannah covered front porch with a pink front door. Duckie's Cottage also has the distinction of being situated next door to Mother Mathilda Beasley Park and Dog Park. There simply isn't a more charming place to stay when seeking Savannah vacation homes with room for four!



This home has a semi-open concept that creates an airy, spacious feel for up to four guests. Step into an adorable living room that prickles the senses with bold artwork on the walls, blue couches, and dual-sided stone fireplace. A mounted smart TV with streaming access ensures that nobody will miss their favorite shows! The living room opens to a sunny dining area featuring a table with seating for four set beside the opposite side of the grand fireplace. Next, a full kitchen with forest-green cabinets provides ample space for preparing meals while on vacation. Enjoy a full-sized oven, dishwasher, refrigerator, and microwave. The kitchen also comes equipped with cooking tools.



It's now time to discover the comfort waiting in the home's first bedroom. Featuring a queen bed, this room offers a window, generous storage, and a cozy vibe. The second bedroom features a king bed. Additional sleeping space can be found on the full-size sofa bed located in the living room. This bed folds away with ease when not in use. Finally, the home offers a full bathroom with a walk-in shower.

The home also offers air conditioning, heat, Wi-Fi, private parking, keyless entry, a grill, and many more extras! Of course, a shared courtyard that offers privacy in the back of the home is an ideal place for relaxing. Many people fall in love with this home because it offers a taste of cottage living right in the heart of a major city. Guests truly feel like Savannah locals once they check in.



Duckie's Cottage serves as the perfect base for seeing all of the historic delights of Savannah! Living with a famed park right next door creates the perfect excuse to enjoy a walk whenever the mood strikes. In addition, the rest of Savannah's top attractions are also very accessible from this home. A key point of interest for every Savannah visitor is the Savannah Historic District. Housing many of the most celebrated estates in the country, this stretch of the city is known for its colorful porches decked out with ornate flower pots and statues. A satisfying Savannah itinerary should also include visits to Bonaventure Cemetery, Forsyth Park, the Cathedral of Saint John the Baptist, the Sorrel Weed House and Museum, the American Prohibition Museum, the Mercer Williams House, and Olde Pink House. A spot called City Market is a pedestrian-only zone offering terrific opportunities for shopping and dining in Savannah. Of course, you want to be by the river when it's time to find a great place to hear some live music. Head to River Street to enjoy the exceptional restaurants, bars, and jazz clubs that make up the city's waterfront area. This is also a great place to hop on a Savannah River ferry to take a trip from downtown's riverwalk to Hutchinson Island. For those who enjoy guided tours, Savannah is a top city. Guests can book trolley tours through the Savannah Historic District, after-dark cemetery tours, pub crawls, and more.



While Savannah is fun every day of the year, the city becomes especially vibrant when festivals and events roll into town. The city constantly hosts food, music, and cultural festivals. The highlights include the Savannah Voice Festival, the Savannah Jazz Festival, the Savannah Greek Festival, the Savannah Film Festival, Christmas on the River, and more. Don't forget that Savannah is located just a short drive from beautiful Tybee Island! A premier beach destination, Tybee Island offers a number of exceptional beaches showing off the beauty of coastal Georgia.
