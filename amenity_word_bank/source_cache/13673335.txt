=== FULL DESCRIPTION ===
This Downtown Savannah Queen Anne's Victorian garden apartment on Oglethorpe Avenue has one private off street parking spot in the heart of downtown!! That alone makes it an ideal vacation rental but it also has ZERO stairs, and gorgeous designer furnishings to make your stay comfortable and memorable. Two separate Queen size bedrooms. One bathroom is off the hallway convenient for all.   City of Savannah SVR 00472 (Savannah Luxury Rentals)

The space
This apartment has a large living room with a dining table, a spacious galley kitchen, two small bedrooms that are totally separate from one another and a full bathroom with a combination tub/shower, equally accessible to each bedroom off the hallway. There is a washwer and dryer in the hallway for your convience (please bring detergent if you plan to do laundry). There is a shared small back patio which the upstairs guests walk through to enter. There is also one dedicated parking space you may add to your booking or use plentiful public paid parking around the house.

I rarely post critical reviews but I think that other customers should understand that this was not a high-quality experience.  On the positive side, this is in the Historic District which is already a massive plus, it’s across from a famous and fascinating cemetery, and it is within walking distance of charming squares, restaurants, and other amenities.  On the negative side, the bedrooms are incredibly cramped, the dining room table was unstable and creaky, the dishwasher was very loud and leaky, and the pantry was stuffed with spare parts.  There were other issues that I will not list, but the overall picture was that of inattentive preparation and organization.  For the price we paid, I expected a minimal level of comfort and quality and we did not get it.  I am giving this three-stars solely because of the location; the property itself deserves a lower score.
