=== FULL DESCRIPTION ===
City Market Central is your access to the heart of downtown Savannah, right in the center of the shopping, the nightlife, the restaurants, the history and the hustle.  Throw your car keys away, you're not going to need them.  City Market Central is ready to be your landing pad in the First City.

**We provide a parking pass for a parking deck one block from the condo to make your stay a little easier.  This is good for one vehicle.**

SVR-00317

The space
We have everything you need to make yourself at home.  Full kitchen with appointments, wine fridge, spacious bedrooms with storage, and  beautiful afternoon light from the west facing windows to relax in while kicking your feet up before the evening begins.

Downtown Savannah is a vibrant, exciting town, and there is action all the time.  Our place gives you direct and immediate access to all that the downtown has to offer. 

The apartment is on the second floor, accessible by staircase.  You can wander, pop in for a break, and head back out without ever thinking about getting in a car.

And we know that parking in downtown Savannah can be challenging, and that is why we provide a parking pass for you for a secure parking deck very close to the apartment.  We want to make things as easy as possible for you during your stay. 

 **The pass gives you 24 hour access to the Robinson Parking Garage, but there is not an assigned or guaranteed spot.  All parking is based on availability. 

**You will need the pass to enter and to exit the deck.**

Guest access
The apartment is private and at your complete disposal.  There is room in the closets, drawers, and bathroom shelves for you to unpack and spread out.

Other things to note
The apartment is in the heart of downtown.  If you are not used to city sounds during the night, it can be noticeable.  We provide white noise machines for those who would like them, but please understand, there can be street noise at times.  We hope that you will enjoy the immediate and untrammeled access to all that the downtown has to offer.
