=== FULL DESCRIPTION ===
This charming studio 2 blocks to Forsyth offers everything you need for a weekend getaway or short work from home-away-from-home trip! We have a queen bed and pull-out twin couch, perfect for couples with a child or 3 comfortable friends :) We offer a cozy faux-fireplace/heater with a large Smart TV above - perfect for a cozy night in, or playing music and pre-gaming a night out :) We have strong WiFi for those who just HAVE to get that email out tonight ;).

SVR-02403

The space
The studio has a fully functional kitchenette with a stovetop, mini-fridge, toaster, microwave and Keurig coffee maker. You have everything you need to cook a small meal or reheat those much deserved leftovers after a long day out in the town! We have a full spacious bath with a shower/tub combo. Aside from the queen bed, there is a pull-out twin in the love seat. The space is super comfy and of course, an easy walk to Forsyth Park and all that the town has to offer!

Guest access
The whole studio is yours! The only shared space that you may access, is the laundry facility across the hall. It is free of course, so use at your own will! Just be cognizant of others around you :) There is a bike rental shop down the street, and you may use the garage to store bikes if you'd like!

Other things to note
Just to reiterate - the space where the laundry room is across from your unit, and it is shared with other guests from the upstairs. This is the only shared space in the house.

Neighborhood highlights
We are in the heart of the Historic District and only 2 blocks from Forsyth...being nice and quiet at night and an easy walk to EVERY thing, you cannot beat this location! Though mostly residential, there are plenty of restaurants and shops only a couple of blocks away! If you're interested in renting a bike while you're here, Savannah on Wheels is just a block away! We are a short walk away to some of the best (and LOCAL!) places in town including Local 11ten, Betty Bombers, Sentient Bean, Brighter Day (Natural Foods), and of course Collins Quarter at Forsyth! Please ask us about all of our hand-picked local favorites and we are more than happy to recommend!!
