=== FULL DESCRIPTION ===
Welcome to “The Stella”, our Victorian townhome in beautiful Downtown Savannah's Victorian District. Vintage, kitschy, & fun décor to fit the festive mood when on vacation. 

Located just 2.5 blocks south of enchanting Forsyth Park, the largest park in all of historic downtown Savannah. 

Two free, designated off-street parking spaces included with ample on street (non-metered) parking also available.

SVR-00701 | Proud City of Savannah Short-Term Vacation Rental Certificate license holder.

The space
Spacious for a large group to enjoy each other's company while visiting and exploring the beautiful city of Savannah. 

Private, fenced courtyard with patio seating, grill, plenty of dining space between the breakfast room and farm house table in dining room graced by crystal chandelier. 

Bright and colorful living room that includes a click-clack sofa with flat screen TV. 

One full vintage bath with clawfoot tub with shower surround located downstairs with a 2nd near identical bath located on the upper level. 

All bedrooms located upstairs which includes:
- a large bedroom with 2 full-size stylish & modern beds +TV  
- large middle bedroom with comfy king bed + TV  
- a queen bedroom with TV
- small 'trunk room' with vintage full-size bed. New, tempurpedic-type mattresses of course in all bedrooms!
- Note: The blue velvet sofa in the living room is a 'click-clack' sofa that converts to a flat bed of sorts...more like a very firm futon than a sofa bed. Linens provided. 

Fully stocked kitchen with stainless appliances, electric stove/oven, microwave, refrigerator with ice/filtered water dispenser, dishwasher. Coffee maker with coffee stocked, blender, toaster, crockpot provided as well. Ample cooking & kitchen wares, dishes and utensils.

Full size washer/dryer upstairs in separate sitting/dressing room.

Two designated off-street parking spaces provided with our rental home locate just behing the fenced courtyard on the alley-way.

Guest access
Pull-down attic space, under the home, or along the side of the home and fence line beyond the confines of the fenced courtyard is OFF-ACCESS!! For privacy and safety concerns, there is no reason you need to access these particular spaces.

Neighborhood highlights
The Victorian District borders the Landmark Historic District. Enjoy the leisurely 5 minute walk to Savannah’s iconic Forsyth Park or spend the day strolling the 1.2 miles from our home under Spanish moss draped trees to River Street, the most northern border of historic downtown, exploring all our beautiful Savannah Squares, quaint shops, and eateries along the way!


Kroger, downtown's only main grocery store is only 5 blocks away with Brighter Day, a local health and organic market within 3 blocks of the house as is the weekly Saturday morning Forsyth Park Farmer's Market.


Locally owned coffee shops, bbq and slider joints, a Taqueria and Tequila bar, a Mediterranean restaurant with full bar and expresso bar, pizza parlor and sports bar, Cuban inspired takeout, All-American diner, fine dining, rooftop lounge, craft-brew store, and more all within a few block stroll of our home as well.
