=== FULL DESCRIPTION ===
The Gaston Hideaway sleeps four with a living room, kitchen, a bedroom, and a bathroom. Southern Belle Vacation Rentals offers basic amenities in all their Savannah vacation homes allowing you to travel light. Amenities that you might not expect are linens, blankets, extra pillows, towels, a hairdryer, shampoo, hangers, an iron, and an ironing board. In their vacation rentals Savannah GA visitors can expect to find laptop-friendly housing with WiFi.

The space
A few blocks from Gaston Hideaway is Forsyth Park. The large park features a beautiful fountain from 1858. It is a copy of the Place de la Concorde fountains in Paris. The park also has two playgrounds, basketball courts, tennis courts, open fields, a garden for the blind, and a concert and special events venue. Among the events held at the park is the Farmers Market on Saturdays.



The Savannah Center of Art and Design (SCAD) is next to Forsyth Park. This art college allows visitors to explore the artwork displayed on the grounds and in the art museum. You can also purchase some of the artwork created by the students.



Learn about historic Savannah on a walking tour. Check out what is available and select the ones that interest you. Make sure you also take a walking tour of haunted Savannah. The city has been declared the most haunted city in the US, so explore this side of Savannah. The haunted tours include locations such as the Mercer Williams house and the Colonial Park cemetery.



Savannah offers a variety of beautiful beaches. Some of the beaches provide the opportunity to birdwatch or watch the cargo ships. You can also take a riverboat cruise or trolley car tour, explore the waterfront, or visit the birthplace of Girl Scout founder Juliet Gordon Low.



Tybee Island offers a lighthouse, the Pier and Pavilion, the Tybee Post Theatre, the Fort Pulaski National Monument, and the Marine Science Center. Kayaking, jet-skiing, boat rentals, and chartered fishing expeditions allow you to get out on the water.



Enjoy Southern hospitality as you explore the dining, shopping, and nightlife available in Savannah. Try Low Country Boil or other seafood dishes. Ask local residents for tips on finding the best Southern cuisine.



Don't miss some of the annual events in Savannah. Among the activities to consider are the Tybee Island polar plunge, the Georgia Days Celebration, the Tybee Island Mardi Gras Festival, the Savannah Irish Festival, the Seafood Festival, the Fourth of July Celebration on Tybee Island, the Labor Day Weekend Beach Bash on Tybee Island, the Savannah Jazz Festival, the Pirate Fest on Tybee Island, the Boat Parade of Lights in Savannah Harbor, tours of Savannah homes decorated for Christmas, and New Year's Eve fireworks on Tybee Island.



The private entrance leads you upstairs to the kitchen in the Gaston Hideaway. The living room is part of an open area that includes the kitchen. It has two large windows for natural light. The living room has a sofabed, two straight chairs, a coffee table, a large-screen TV, a TV stand, another stand, and a table with two chairs.



Between the kitchen and the living room, there is another table that seats four. In the kitchen, we provide a refrigerator-freezer, stove, microwave, dishwasher, coffeemaker, toaster, dishes, utensils, and cooking basics. This is everything you need except your food to prepare meals and snacks.



The bedroom is off the living room. It is furnished with a queen-size bed, two nightstands, a large-screen TV, a stand, and a free-standing, full-length mirror. There are two large windows for natural light.



There are doors to the bathroom from the bedroom and the kitchen. Therefore, the bathroom can be accessed without going through the bedroom. The bathroom includes a vanity with storage, a toilet, a mirror, and a walk-in shower.
