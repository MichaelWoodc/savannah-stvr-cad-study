=== FULL DESCRIPTION ===
This newly remodeled apartment in a 1870's building is located on Monterey Square, one of the most beautiful areas of historic Savannah. The high ceilings, plaster walls, heart-of-pine floors, and ornate crown molding are just some the beautiful details to adorn the property from days past. This is a stunning example of type and will transport you to the slower, kinder ways of the South.  
  
City of Savannah SVR-01653

The space
The apartment is located on the top floor of a historically beautiful 4-story building. It has two spacious bedrooms; one king-sized and one queen-sized bed. A brand new kitchen for all your cooking needs and a comfortable living room with large windows and plenty of lighting. Wifi, cable TV, central heat and air are among the amenities.  

Whether you are accompanied by family or just traveling with a friend, this beautiful penthouse apartment is the perfect space for your weekend getaway. Serving to be both private and comfortable, our apartment has everything you need to feel at home. Located within walking distance of everything Savannah has to offer, you will find yourself falling in love with the charming culture this Southern city is known for.   

We are in the heart of historic Savannah, located on Monterey Square. One block away is the Forsyth Park and numerous restaurants and shops surround the area. The location is quiet and peaceful as it is a solid distance away from the bars and partygoers that line the waterfront area. It is ideal for those who want to dig into the fascinating history, sublime architecture, wonderful boutiques and shops, and the delicious restaurants that Savannah has to offer!

Guest access
The large foyer and stairwell are shared by guests however this unit has a private door and takes up the entire top floor of this building. We use coded door locks for access that we will provide upon booking.

Other things to note
Being located in the center of town, you are able to walk to most places. Uber also proves to quite handy as well.  

Parking is on-street and spots are easy to find at this location. 
Parking in enforced Monday through Friday, 8AM - 5PM. There is no charge for parking over the course of the weekend. 

Meters are best handled with a "Visitors Parking Day Pass" available to cover all day/night parking for travelers. Our guests who come with a vehicle may want to purchase a City of Savannah Day Pass which is $24 for 48 hours or $15 for 24 hours. 

You can get the pass from the Parking Services office at 100 East Bryan Street. (Monday - Friday, 8AM - 4:30PM) 

The nearest non-metered free public street parking starts 2 blocks south from this location. 

This is the easiest way to leave your car parked and enjoy the walking lifestyle that makes Savannah famous!
