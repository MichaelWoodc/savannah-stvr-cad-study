=== FULL DESCRIPTION ===
Discover timeless elegance in our 950 sq/ft designer-curated 1871 parlor apartment in Savannah's Historic District. This sunlit gem comfortably features a king bed, spacious living room, dedicated workspace, and a full kitchen. Enjoy historic charm with modern comforts, just steps from Forsyth Park. Perfect for a romantic getaway and to explore the city on foot.

The space
Experience timeless elegance in this fully redesigned 1871 gem on Taylor Street. Spanning 950 sq/ft, this stunning parlor-level apartment has been artfully curated by our interior designer to blend historic charm with modern comfort. Sunlight streams through tall windows, illuminating original hardwood floors, high ceilings, and charming decorative fireplaces.

Key Features:
*   **Living Room:** Spacious area with a crystal chandelier, comfortable sleeper sofa, and a dedicated workspace with a desk and complimentary WiFi.

*   **Kitchen:** A fully equipped kitchen with modern, full-sized appliances, a large island for meal prep, and a cozy breakfast nook.

*   **Master Suite:** A serene retreat featuring a luxurious king-sized bed and access to a small private balcony.

*   **Historic Details:** Original hardwood floors, high ceilings, and decorative fireplaces throughout.

Guest access
Guests will have exclusive and private access to the entire apartment to relax and unwind. This includes the living room, full kitchen, master suite, and the private balcony. Enjoy your own space in the heart of the city.

There is ample street parking all around our home, both paid-metered as well as free.

Other things to note
*   **Accessibility:** Please note that this is a parlor-level apartment, accessible via one flight of stairs from the building's entryway.

*   **License:** City of Savannah STVR# SVR-02743
