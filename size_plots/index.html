Listing size and calls for service

Citizen-initiated recorded-address calls, not verified 911-origin records. Each parcel counts once, including zero-call parcels. Size is the largest known listing size on that parcel, not the sum of units or observed occupancy. Bedroom and guest maxima can come from different listings. Calls belong to the parcel and cannot be assigned to an individual rental unit.

Each point is the mean annual call count for parcels of exactly that size. Neighbor calls are totals across immediate residential neighbors sharing more than 0.1 m of boundary; across-street and corner-only properties are excluded. Combined totals are deduplicated within each focal neighborhood. Shared neighbors can appear in several neighborhoods. These rates are per focal parcel, not per neighbor parcel, and have no booking-occupancy adjustment. Groups with few parcels are unstable; connecting lines are descriptive, not fitted trends.
