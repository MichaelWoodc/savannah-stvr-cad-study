Perfect "live like a local" spot. It's the perfect blend of history and modern style and convenience (granite counter tops and a 2 person jacuzzi tub in the bathroom). Savannah License # - SVR-00569

The space
This fantastic condo is in the perfect location in the Landmark Historic District of beautiful downtown Savannah, GA. The unit features a large bedroom with a king size bed, large living area, fully equipped kitchen and luxury bathroom with a 2 person Jacuzzi hot tub garden bath and a tankless water heater. Ohhh-la la. This historic cottage, originally constructed in the late 1800's, has been eloquently renovated and furnished with modern conveniences and a contemporary feel. Walking distance to grocery, bars, fine dining, coffee shops,and world famous Forsyth Park make this an unbeatable location. A 20 minute walk to the shopping district through the heart of the historic city provides a complete Savannah experience. Also, a short 20 minute drive to Tybee Island for a beach day. 

Did we mention there is a pool?  The pool is shared by 8 condos, so it is not 100% private.  But , rarely is there more than 1 group of guests down there at a time.  And EVERYBODY loves to come home from a beautiful  day out in Savannah to relax in or by the pool!

Nightly rates range from about $110 to $165. Rates vary based on seasons, special events, availability, holidays, and how interesting I find your inquiry;) Please message for exact quote!
