=== FULL DESCRIPTION ===
Stay in a comfortable two-bedroom condo in a Victorian home built in 1900, located in the Victorian district just minutes to Forsyth Park. This private downstairs unit features one queen bed, one full bed, a full kitchen, washer and dryer, large TV, high-speed Wi-Fi, a sectional, and an outdoor Blackstone griddle. Enjoy a relaxed residential setting a convenient location to Savannah’s historic neighborhoods, downtown, and the riverfront.

City of Savannah STVR Certificate #SVR-03080

The space
This comfortable first-floor condo has an open living and dining area, a fully equipped kitchen, two private bedrooms, and one full bathroom with a shower and tub combination. One bedroom has a queen bed, and the second has a full-size bed. A queen air mattress and additional linens is available for an additional guest.

The condo also includes a washer and dryer, Wi-Fi, and a large TV. Guests may also enjoy the shared backyard, patio and Blackstone griddle, along with a private side deck.

Guest access
Guests have a private entrance to the condo, a private side deck, and access to the shared backyard and patio. The fenced backyard is convenient for guests traveling with pets.
Free street parking is available in front of the property.

Other things to note
This is a private downstairs condo in a historic Victorian home. Guests have their own private entrance. The backyard is shared but is typically quiet and rarely used by multiple guests at the same time. Free street parking is available in front of the property. Streets are swept Monday and Thursday nights, so please pay close attention to posted street signs.
