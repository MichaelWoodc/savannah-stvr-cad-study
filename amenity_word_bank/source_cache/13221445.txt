=== FULL DESCRIPTION ===
1790s Washington Square Mansion, steps to River Street and Broughton Street! Sleeps 8 in the heart of Savannah's Historic District!

The space
No expense was spared on the modernization of this property. You'll find a fully outfitted gourmet kitchen with island seating for 3, a formal dining room, & 4 bedrooms all with spacious, en suite bathrooms!
3 bedrooms have queen beds & the 4th has 2 vintage twin beds. 

PARKING: 
This reservation includes ONE private, garage parking space. 
Parking for additional vehicles: Downtown Savannah has metered, on-street parking Monday through Friday. SOME parts of the Historic District are free on Saturday/Sunday. The City of Savannah uses Meter Pay Stations or coin meters. You can download the APP "Park Savannah" for easy payments from your phone 
OR
Visitor Parking Passes are available for 24hours/$15.00 and 48hours/$24.00. Passes can be purchased at the City of Savannah Parking Services Office at 100 East Bryan Street. Please call them with any questions about parking in the city.

Luxury Living Properties is not just a premier luxury vacation rental company. We are passionate about saving Savannah's resplendent historic homes from ruin. We meticulously restore them back to the shining jewels they were in the 19th century. We have curated special homes for the enjoyment of vacation and business travelers of all generations. Our goal is simple - to create a unique, historic atmosphere for our guests to experience the "True Savannah" and "Live like a local". On nearby Tybee Island we are focused on providing ocean views & fun amenities with a variety of beach houses, town homes and condos, all with amazing views of the Atlantic Ocean. Our stylish and comfortable properties provide a perfect home away from home for families, romantic getaways or solo travelers.
