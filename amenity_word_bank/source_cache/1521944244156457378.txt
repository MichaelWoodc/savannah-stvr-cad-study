=== FULL DESCRIPTION ===
Stay at Hideaway on Jones, a 2BR/1BA condo on Savannah’s iconic Jones Street. Perfect for 4 guests, this stylish retreat features a king bed, double bed, full kitchen, smart TVs, washer/dryer, and secure keyless entry. Relax in the shared courtyard or walk to Forsyth Park, shops, dining, and River Street. The ideal blend of comfort, charm, and location in Savannah’s Historic District.

The space
Hideaway on Jones

Welcome to Hideaway on Jones, a charming two-bedroom, one-bathroom condo tucked along one of Savannah’s most beloved streets. Perfectly suited for up to four guests, this inviting retreat offers an authentic Savannah experience where historic beauty meets modern comfort. Whether you’re planning a romantic getaway, a weekend escape with friends, or a small family trip, Hideaway on Jones provides the ideal home base for your adventures in the Hostess City.



A Historic Street with Timeless Charm

Jones Street is often celebrated as the most beautiful street in Savannah, and for good reason. Lined with towering oaks draped in Spanish moss, cobblestone sidewalks, and rows of meticulously restored townhomes, this picturesque avenue captures the essence of Savannah’s old-world charm. Staying at Hideaway on Jones means you’ll wake up in the heart of this enchanting setting each morning, surrounded by the city’s most photographed scenery.



Beyond the beauty right outside your door, the condo’s central location places you just a short walk from Savannah’s famous squares, Forsyth Park, and the city’s bustling dining and shopping districts. Whether you’re here to immerse yourself in history, savor local cuisine, or simply stroll the shaded streets, you’ll find it all within easy reach.



Welcoming Interiors

Step inside Hideaway on Jones, and you’ll be greeted by a warm, inviting space that feels both stylish and comfortable. The living room sets the tone with plush seating, tasteful décor, and a smart TV for evenings spent relaxing after a day of sightseeing. Whether you’re catching up on your favorite shows or gathering with friends for a glass of wine, this cozy space makes it easy to unwind.



The condo has been thoughtfully designed to balance modern amenities with timeless character. Hardwood floors nod to the property’s historic roots, while updated furnishings and conveniences ensure a seamless stay for today’s travelers.



A Kitchen Made for Gatherings

The full kitchen at Hideaway on Jones makes it simple to enjoy meals together during your stay. Outfitted with modern appliances, ample counter space, and all the essentials you’ll need for cooking, it’s perfect whether you’re whipping up a quick breakfast before heading out or preparing a full dinner at home.



Adjacent to the kitchen is a dining table with seating for four, providing the perfect setting to share meals, plan the day ahead, or linger over coffee in the morning. For guests who prefer the ease of dining out, you’ll find some of Savannah’s most beloved restaurants just blocks away, giving you the best of both worlds.



Comfortable Bedrooms

When it’s time to rest, Hideaway on Jones offers two inviting bedrooms designed with relaxation in mind. The first bedroom features a king-sized bed, crisp linens, and a smart TV for winding down at night. With plenty of space to settle in, this room is a peaceful retreat after busy days exploring the city. The second bedroom is equally welcoming with a double bed that provides comfort for additional guests. Thoughtful touches and cozy décor make it feel like a home away from home.



Together, the bedrooms can accommodate up to four guests, making this condo ideal for couples, friends, or small families traveling together.



  

A Bright and Functional Bathroom

Hideaway on Jones includes a full bathroom with a shower and tub combination. Whether you prefer a quick rinse or a long soak, you’ll find the space clean, functional, and well-equipped for your stay. Fresh towels and essential amenities are provided to ensure your comfort.



Courtyard Escape

One of the standout features of this property is its shared courtyard, a delightful spot where you can enjoy a bit of fresh air without leaving home. With seating and a table set up for outdoor dining, it’s perfect for sipping your morning coffee, sharing a meal outside, or enjoying a glass of wine in the evening. Surrounded by brickwork and lush greenery, the courtyard feels like a hidden oasis in the middle of downtown Savannah.



Everyday Convenience

For longer stays, or simply for added convenience, Hideaway on Jones is equipped with a washer and dryer available for guest use. Whether you’re refreshing beach towels after a day trip to Tybee or making sure you’re packed with clean clothes for the journey home, this feature helps take the stress out of travel.

Secure keyless entry also makes check-in and coming and going a breeze. With this modern system, there’s no need to keep track of keys during your trip. Just arrive, settle in, and start enjoying your stay.



Explore Savannah from Your Doorstep

One of the biggest perks of staying at Hideaway on Jones is its unbeatable location. Step outside and find yourself immersed in the history, charm, and energy of Savannah’s historic district. You’ll be within walking distance of Forsyth Park, the crown jewel of Savannah with its iconic fountain, gardens, and shaded walking paths. The city’s famed squares are also nearby, each offering its own unique beauty and history, from lively Chippewa Square to serene Monterey Square.



If shopping and dining are on your itinerary, you’ll discover boutiques, art galleries, coffee shops, and restaurants just minutes away. Savannah’s food scene is second to none, with options ranging from casual bites to fine dining. And when you’re ready for a change of pace, River Street awaits with its bustling shops, nightlife, and stunning waterfront views along the Savannah River.



Whether you’re here for sightseeing, culture, or relaxation, Hideaway on Jones puts it all at your fingertips.



Why Guests Love Hideaway on Jones

Guests are drawn to this property for its combination of location, comfort, and style. The condo provides all the essentials for a stress-free stay, yet feels special thanks to its setting on one of the most beautiful streets in the country. With two bedrooms, a cozy living space, a full kitchen, and a courtyard, it’s the perfect place to settle in and create lasting memories in Savannah.



Plan Your Stay

Savannah is a city best explored on foot, and staying at Hideaway on Jones means you’ll be in the middle of it all. Spend your days wandering historic streets, visiting museums, and indulging in Southern cuisine, then return to the comfort of your condo each evening. With its convenient amenities and timeless charm, Hideaway on Jones offers an authentic Savannah experience you’ll want to return to again and again.
