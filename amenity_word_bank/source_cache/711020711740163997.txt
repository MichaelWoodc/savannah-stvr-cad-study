Come stay at this spacious 3 bedroom loft in the heart of downtown! Close to all the best activities, restaurants, and shopping that Savannah has to offer. You will be steps away from Plant Riverside, City Market, and so much more. Perfect for larger groups - bachelorette trips, and families.
