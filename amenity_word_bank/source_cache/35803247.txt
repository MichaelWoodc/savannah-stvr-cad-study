=== FULL DESCRIPTION ===
Welcome to “The Clara”, our Victorian townhome in beautiful Downtown Savannah's Victorian District. Located within a few short blocks of iconic Forsyth Park, this 4-bedroom/2-bath, vacation home offers all the comfortable and cozy luxuries you’re looking for in a historic setting AND provides 2 free, private/designated off-street parking spaces with ample on street (non-metered) parking also available.

SVR-00090 | Proud City of Savannah Short-Term Vacation Rental Certificate license holder.

The space
Entering “The Clara” vacation home you’ll find a large living area with comfortable seating including a sofa, loveseat, flat screen LCD TV, DVD player, drop-leaf table with two chairs, and a secretary desk. (A comfy twin roll-away bed with foam mattress is stored with extra linens in the closet under the stairs.)

The formal dining room with semi-open floorplan to the living room offers a 6-seat dining room table with a buffet server. An additional 6-seat dining table is located adjacent in the breakfast room to accommodate a total of 12 dining guests.

The kitchen is modern and well equipped with all cooking and kitchenware to prepare a fine meal. Fridge with icemaker and filtered water dispenser. Dishwasher and disposal. Electric smooth top range and oven. Microwave, blender, toaster, and coffee maker. 

Step out the french doors from the kitchen onto the classic back porch with charming wicker furniture overlooking our private courtyard…a perfect place to sip morning coffee and bird watch for red cardinals, our resident mourning doves, or the occasional squirrel.
 
A full bath is located downstairs off the kitchen with a vintage claw foot tub with shower combo. The second, near identical full bath, is located upstairs adjacent to the large laundry/dressing room conveniently autonomous of the bedrooms (so as not to disturb any sleeping beauties when the bath is in use late night or early morning).

The grand staircase takes you to the 2nd floor where the  aforementioned full bath and dressing/laundry room is located in addition to 3 large bedrooms, and a tiny 4th bedroom known as the ‘trunk room’. 

The ‘master’ bedroom is bright and sunny with the beautiful bay window, two full/double beds that sleep four, a large dresser, matching antique ‘washstand’ with seating area, closet, and a LCD TV. 

The second bedroom boasts the comfy King bed, dresser, closet, a recliner, and LCD TV.

The third sunny bedroom is furnished with the queen bed, large wicker dresser, and TV with DVD player.

The fourth bedroom or Trunk room, a customary Savannah term used in our historic homes, comfortably accommodates a full size bed and dresser.

Park up to two of your vehicles in the free designated off-street parking spaces provide in rear of the home. Enjoy the leisurely 5 minute walk to Savannah’s iconic Forsyth Park or spend the day strolling the 1.2 miles from the rental under Spanish moss draped trees to River Street exploring all our beautiful Savannah Squares, quaint shops, and eateries along the way!

Guest access
Guest have use of the entire home including courtyard and designated parking on the premises for two vehicles. Guest are not to access attic space, crawl space, or areas behind the fenceline between the neighboring homes.

Other things to note
We welcome children and families, although our home has not been childproofed. Please inquire with any questions or concerns.

Must be able to safely climb stairs, carry luggage to bedrooms upstairs as needed,  and step into clawfoot tubs.

Detailed, written check-in/access instructions will be emailed within one-week of the stay. Guest must confirm total guest count and a list of guest names staying on the premises, confirm cell number of the person booking the home and a secondary contact number of an alternate guest prior to check-in. A signed Rental Agreement may be required with written acknowledgment of House Rules.
