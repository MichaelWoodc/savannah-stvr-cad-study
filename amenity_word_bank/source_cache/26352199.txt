Bedrooms in this property are expansive and offer much more than just a bed. One of the bedrooms here has a writing desk, flatscreen TV, decorative fireplace and tons of space. The other bedroom also offers a TV and fireplace, plus a cozy reading nook next to a window. The bathroom is very spacious and includes a tub, standing shower and laundry machines.

Top Events Near Savannah Vacation Homes 



Attending special events in Savannah is always a fun activity when staying in vacation rentals Savannah GA. There are a variety of special events all year long, but there's definitely some great things to do in the summer. In June, you can head over to River Street and enjoy the Seafood Festival, which also features live entertainment. Another big June event is the Savannah Food Truck Festival. Along with around 50 trucks serving a range of food, you can also find a craft beer garden, craft vendors, live entertainment and much more. 



If you're staying in our Savannah vacation rentals around early July, you won't want to miss the River Street Fourth of July Celebration. The long promenade of River Street is perfect for viewing fireworks over the Savannah River. While you wait for the evening festivities, you can browse the shops and restaurants. 



In August, you'll find the Savannah Gourmet Seafood & Spirits Festival. This event is a big draw as it features many celebrity chefs and famous mixologists. Some other events to consider during the year include: 



• Martin Luther King Parade in January 

• Saint Patrick's Day in Savannah in March 

• Savannah Garden Expo in April 

• Oktoberfest on the River in October 

• Savannah Harbor Boat Parade of Lights in November 

• City Market New Year's Eve Celebration in December

Savannah Vacation Homes Put You Within Reach of the Beach

There are beaches on the east, north and west sides of the island. The interior has quaint neighborhoods, shops, restaurants and spots to rent gear for island activities. The can't-miss attraction on Tybee is undoubtedly the Tybee Island Lighthouse on the north end. Climb to the top and enjoy the most amazing view of Savannah and Tybee.
