=== FULL DESCRIPTION ===
This distinguished retreat is designed for the sophisticated traveler, offering a bright, vibrant atmosphere that radiates warmth and natural light throughout, with private parking, and a large courtyard!

The space
Welcome to Lazy Bones (previously known as "Grand Luther) – Your Perfect Home Away from Home! This distinguished retreat is designed for the sophisticated traveler, offering a bright, vibrant atmosphere that radiates warmth and natural light throughout. Every corner of Lazy Bones invites you to relax, unwind, and enjoy the charm of Savannah.

What Makes Lazy Bones Unique?

- Bright and Inviting Space: This open and airy home is ideal for both entertaining and cozy nights in with loved ones. The spacious layout, filled with natural light, is perfect for families, friends, and even young children. Every room has been thoughtfully designed to create a welcoming environment that feels just like home.

- Meaningful Touches: From a gallery wall celebrating world-renowned musicians to a record player and carefully curated local artwork from SCAD galleries, Lazy Bones is filled with hand-selected pieces that make your stay truly memorable.

- Prime Location: Located just a block from a fully enclosed dog park, two blocks from the market, and three blocks from Forsyth Park, this home is perfectly positioned off Price and East Gaston Street. Enjoy leisurely strolls around the historic district, ensuring you never miss a beat of what Savannah has to offer!

- Private Courtyard Oasis: The private courtyard at the back of the home is your downtown sanctuary. Meticulously crafted by the owner, this space invites you to relax, sip a drink, and reflect on the day’s adventures.

All three bedrooms and two full baths are located on the second floor, providing a serene escape for a restful night. The first bedroom features a Queen bed, antique writing desk, and full-sized closet. The middle bedroom is perfect for kids or friends, with a Full-size bed and a Twin bed with trundle. The primary suite, facing Price Street, offers a King-size bed and large Smart TV. The upstairs bathrooms bookend the hall, one with a tub/shower combo and the other with a standing shower.

What is parking like for this home? This home includes 2 compact parking spaces located directly behind the courtyard. For additional parking, we recommend finding free parking on Gaston Street, which can be easily found directly next to your home. 

Sleeping Configuration:
Bedroom 1: Queen Bed
Bedroom 2: Full Bed + Twin with Trundle
Bedroom 3: King Bed

We can welcome your dog companions at this pet friendly home, but ask that you find other arrangements for cats, birds, and other furry friends. There is a $130 pet fee (non-refundable) with a 35 lb. maximum weight limit per dog and a limit of 2 dogs per reservation. Some homes may allow larger dogs, please inquire for more information.
