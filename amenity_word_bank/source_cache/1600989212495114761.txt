=== FULL DESCRIPTION ===
Welcome to the Historic Downtown Victorian Home Near Forsyth, a beautifully restored two-story residence from 1900. Located in Savannah’s charming Victorian District, just a short walk from Forsyth Park and minutes from River Street, this elegant home offers private gated parking, a fenced yard, modern amenities, and timeless Southern character. Clean, spacious, and pet-friendly! SVR-03245

The space
Upstairs, the Historic Downtown Victorian Home Near Forsyth features four spacious bedrooms with: 1 King bed 2 Queen beds 2 Twin beds Downstairs, the parlor room serves as a fifth bedroom, offering a queen-size sofa bed with an ultra-comfortable mattress pad — perfect for additional guests or anyone who prefers to avoid stairs. Enjoy a fenced yard, private gated parking, and all the conveniences of home: an alarm system, smart TV with Netflix and Amazon Channels, high-speed Wi-Fi, a fully equipped kitchen, and a charcoal grill. Each bed is plush, and the house is spotlessly clean and inviting.

Other things to note
Dogs are welcome upon pre-approval. No cats, due to allergy sensitivities.
