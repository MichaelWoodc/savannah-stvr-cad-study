=== FULL DESCRIPTION ===
"Gilded Iron & Oak" on Broughton St. is your 5-star Gilded Age haven and Savannah flex. This spectacular 3BR, 2-story row house built in 1883 is an architectural masterpiece, blending original exposed brick & hardwood floors with stunning modern design. Enjoy private off street parking and a chic, enclosed courtyard. Live where the action is: we’re steps from River Street, Forsyth Park, the city's best bars, and directly on the iconic St. Patrick’s Day parade route. Book the ultimate experience.

The space
Step right into the opulence of a Gilded Age inspired retreat at the "Gilded Iron & Oak" on historic Broughton Street. This spectacular two-story row house, originally commissioned in 1883 by Alderman Daniel O'Connor, is an architectural masterpiece that perfectly blends Savannah's history with the conveniences of modern, high-end living.

The main level of this luxury 3 bedroom residence is designed for seamless entertaining. The open flow showcases the home's historic pedigree with hardwood floors and authentic exposed brickwork that serve as the backdrop for stunning, insta-worthy modern design. The living and dining areas are complemented by a convenient guest bath downstairs. The gourmet kitchen perfectly merges historic exposed brick walls with rich wood cabinetry, granite countertops, and modern stainless steel appliances, offering a high-end, functional space that flows seamlessly into the main living areas and provides direct access to an enclosed garden courtyard perfect for morning coffee or evening cocktails.

Upstairs, three private bedrooms are curated as tranquil escapes. Each room is appointed for ultimate comfort, featuring designer linens and sophisticated decor.  The primary bedroom features original dark hardwood floors, a stately fireplace with a white brick chimney, and a sunny bay window seating area overlooking Broughton St, all beneath a sophisticated tray ceiling.  The second bedroom delivers dramatic historic character with its preserved exposed brick wall, a decorative fireplace, and a skylight that provides both natural light by day and celestial sights at night. The charming third bedroom overlooks the courtyard through its large picture windows, providing a quiet retreat with views of the surrounding trees and excellent natural light.   The upper floor also includes two full bathrooms, ensuring comfort and privacy for all guests.

Forget the stress of parking—you have two guaranteed private off-street parking spaces, a priceless convenience in the Historic District. 

The property’s location is your Savannah flex. You are steps from River Street, Forsyth Park, and the city’s best bars and dining. Better yet, the home sits directly on the iconic St. Patrick’s Day parade route. 

Book the ultimate luxury city experience.
