=== ABOUT THIS SPACE ===
Live in the lap of luxury in this historically preserved 1869 mansion just steps from Forsyth Park. With 5 bedrooms and 5.5 baths, The Wedding Cake Main House sleeps 10 guests. Located on Monterey Square it is perfect for 5 couples, families or group retreats.

The space
One of Savannah’s most photographed and famous houses. It is truly one of the finest homes in the South East. It is now available as a luxury vacation rental. 

This 1869 2nd Empire Baroque Home takes your breath the minute you see it. The Award Winning Mansion shows off an elaborate white bay window is so ornately decorated with stained glass windows and ornamental iron work that the long time residents of Savannah have always said it resembles an elegantly decorated 'wedding cake'.  

The prized 'Wedding Cake Mansion' is the jewel of Monterey Square. The historical square was made famous from being featured in the movie 'Midnight in the Garden of Good and Evil'. Monterey Square is unquestionably the most beautiful square in Savannah. The square is graced by mansions on all corners, draped in Spanish moss and anchored with historic monuments.

The common spaces, Living Room, Dining Room, Kitchen and Keeping Room are elegantly decorated to meet the historic refinement of the historic home. Five regal bedrooms each with luxurious en suite bathrooms offer private sanctuary away from your group.

The gourmet kitchen has custom designed cabinetry, top of the line stainless steel appliances and granite counter tops will make the everyday chef or the culinary master feel at home.
