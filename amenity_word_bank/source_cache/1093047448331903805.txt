Neighborhood highlights
The condo is nestled in the heart of the enchanting Historic District of Savannah, Georgia, where cobblestone streets weave through a tapestry of timeless beauty and Southern charm. Steeped in history and adorned with beautiful architecture, this district is a living museum inviting you to step back in time and immerse yourself in the rich heritage of the American South.

Be sure to explore Forsyth Park, a verdant oasis in the heart of the district, where fragrant magnolia trees stand sentinel over lush gardens and a majestic fountain takes center stage.

No visit to Savannah would be complete without a stroll down River Street, where historic warehouses have been transformed into charming boutiques, galleries, and restaurants. Listen to the echoes of the past as you walk along the cobblestone waterfront, where ships once unloaded their cargo, and the spirit of maritime adventure still lingers in the air.

For those with a penchant for the paranormal, Savannah's Historic District offers a tempting array of ghost tours and haunted attractions. From the notorious Mercer-Williams House to the shadowy alleyways of Factor's Walk, plenty of spine-tingling tales are waiting to be uncovered in the moonlit streets of this haunted city.

As the sun sets over the Savannah River, casting a golden glow upon the city's historic skyline, you'll understand why Savannah has captured the hearts of so many.

Welcome to Savannah, where history comes to life at every turn.
