=== FULL DESCRIPTION ===
Experience the charm of Savannah at Broughton Street Main House, a beautifully restored Historic District retreat just a short walk from River Street, Broughton Street, shopping, dining, and Savannah's iconic squares. Accommodating up to 6 guests, this inviting home features a private courtyard, garage parking, a fully equipped kitchen, and complimentary access to Southern Belle's heated pool on Tybee Island.

The space
Immerse Yourself in Southern Hospitality

With its 22 tree-lined public squares, huge array of well-preserved antebellum mansions and vibrant arts, dining and entertainment scenes, Savannah has a lot to offer. It’s easier to make the most of your visit when you stay in the heart of the city, and you’ll get that and more by booking a stay at the Broughton Street Main House. This lovely vacation home includes a courtyard where you can enjoy a cup of coffee during the morning or sip a cocktail during the evening. The property is professionally cleaned after each stay, ensuring the safety and health of guests.



Savannah is known for being a great place for pedestrians, and you can take advantage of its prime walkability with a stay at Broughton Street Main House. Located on Broughton Street just east of Price Street, the property is within easy walking distance of many of the city’s best attractions, including the popular River Street dining and shopping district. You can easily walk to the river from this property and to many of the city’s most popular public squares. With a garage parking space included onsite, this property is a great jumping-off point for all kinds of Savannah-area adventures.



Amenities

Broughton Street Main House has everything you need to enjoy an unforgettable visit to the Hostess City of the South. Whether you’re coming to Savannah to enjoy an event like the Savannah Jazz Festival or to explore sites made famous by the novel and subsequent movie, Midnight in the Garden of Good and Evil, this luxury vacation home has you covered.

   

Guests of this property also enjoy access to our heated, resort-style swimming pool on Tybee Island. From March 1st through October 31st, the swimming pool is open from 10 a.m. until 5 p.m. seven days per week. From November 1st through February 28th, the pool is open on Fridays and Saturdays online from 10 a.m. until 5 p.m.



When to Visit

The Broughton Street Main House is available throughout the year, and there’s never a bad time to come to Savannah. As you will see below, the city hosts events throughout the year. If you’re coming to enjoy one of them, book a stay at this property to make the most of your visit.



This vacation home includes air conditioning and a heater, so you can stay comfortable no matter when you visit.



Book Broughton Street Main House for Your Savannah Getaway

Visiting Savannah is like being whisked away to a simpler, bygone era, and the best way to immerse yourself in the city’s unique history and culture is by booking a stay at Broughton Street Main House. With accommodations in the Historic District, you’ll be close to the best attractions and events, allowing you to make the most of this pedestrian-friendly city. Book your stay today to lay the groundwork for a special visit to the Hostess City of the South.

Heated Pool Access

Our guests have access to our beautiful, heated pool that is located on the South End of Tybee Island. For our Savannah properties, it is a scenic 25-minute drive through Wilmington and Whitemarsh Island, taking you over the Savannah River Delta onto Tybee Island. The Pool is open during the below dates and times.



March 1 - Oct 31: Open 7 days a week from 10am-5pm.

Nov 1 - Feb 28/29: Open Friday and Saturday from 10am-5pm



Additional Pool Hours available during the off-season are listed below. If these dates fall in the middle of the week, the pool will be open as listed below and every Friday and Saturday.



Day before Thanksgiving, Thanksgiving Day, and the day after Thanksgiving

Day before Christmas Eve, Christmas Eve & Day, and the day after Christmas Day

Guests with Pets 

This property happily welcomes your furry travel companions! Guests may bring up to 2 dogs, with a combined weight limit of 50 lbs.​​​​​​​ Pets must be added to your reservation at the time of booking, and a non refundable $130 pet fee will apply.



Long-Term Stays

For our long-term stays, 30 days or more, we require a deposit and our office will reach out to you for details. For these reservations, we require an additional cleaning at a cost every 30 days. The cleaning cost varies by property. Our office will be in touch to schedule.



Occupancy 

City of Savannah Code of Ordinances, Division II, Part 8, Chapter 3 (Zoning), Article 8.0, Section 8.4.37(b) – Short-Term Vacation Rental Use Standards: Occupants shall not exceed four adults for dwelling units with no more than two bedrooms. For dwelling units with three or more bedrooms, the number shall not exceed two adults per bedroom. 



Southern Belle Occupancy Policy: This home accommodates up to 6 adults and up to 2 children, with the total number of guests not exceeding the home’s maximum approved occupancy of 8 guests.
