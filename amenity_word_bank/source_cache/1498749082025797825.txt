=== FULL DESCRIPTION ===
Circa 1854, this stunning Savannah row home blends historic charm with modern luxury. Spacious and serene, it features 3 bedrooms and 3.5 baths on three levels. A private ground floor suite offers its own living room, kitchenette, and bath. The parlor level boasts elegant living, dining, and a chef’s kitchen opening to a balcony and courtyard. Upstairs, 2 BRs with gorgeous views, 2 full bathrooms, ample storage, and laundry room. Historic beauty truly meets refined comfort.

SVR-03156 and 01793

The space
Experience Savannah at its finest in this extraordinary 1854 row home, where historic architecture meets modern luxury. Thoughtfully renovated with soaring windows, airy design, and high-end finishes throughout, this 3-bedroom, 3.5-bath residence is as spacious as it is stunning.

The garden level suite offers a private retreat with its own living room, kitchenette, king bedroom, full bathroom, and laundry, plus direct courtyard access. On the parlor level, you’ll find gracious entertaining spaces: a sunlit living room, dining room, and a chef’s kitchen with a gas range and premium appliances. French doors open to a balcony overlooking the home’s unreal courtyard — a true urban sanctuary.

The top floor features two bedrooms and two full bathrooms. The primary suite boasts a king bed, en-suite spa bathroom, and private balcony with breathtaking views of the cathedral. The second bedroom offers a versatile queen Murphy bed, doubling as a quiet home office if needed. A second laundry room is also conveniently located upstairs.

Guests enjoy fast WiFi, flat screen TVs with cable and streaming services, and tranquil views throughout the home.

Nestled on Taylor Square in the heart of Savannah, this residence is steps from the iconic Clary’s Café and directly across from the historic Massie Schoolhouse. Whether sipping coffee in the courtyard, entertaining in the parlor, or wandering Savannah’s charming streets, this quintessential row home is the perfect blend of past and present.

Guest access
Guests have full access to the entire home, including both laundry areas, the chef’s kitchen, balconies, and the courtyard. Please note -- the courtyard can also be accessed by guests staying in the carriage house, across the courtyard, so it may be shared with up to two people at times. The garden-level suite is fully integrated into the property, making it ideal for multi-family or group stays.

Other things to note
Don't be afraid to reach out if you need anything!  We live super close-by and are always happy to help out, even mid-stay.
