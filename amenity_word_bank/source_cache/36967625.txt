=== FULL DESCRIPTION ===
Discover this home, a newly renovated historic retreat in Savannah's Victorian District. This edgy and artsy triplex, built around 1830, blends rustic brick, light pine floors, and modern accents. Enjoy the open-concept living space and relax on the outdoor deck/patio. Perfect for those seeking a unique and comfortable stay near local attractions.

The space
Step into a stylish space where modern design meets historic charm in Savannah’s Victorian District. Just a 7-minute walk to Forsyth Park, 1.5 miles from River Street’s shops and dining, and boasting an 89 Walk Score, this quaint condo offers easy access to cafes, trolleys, and local attractions. Enjoy an outdoor deck, fast WiFi, and an EV charger during your stay.

The living area blends eclectic styles with steampunk hardware, black-and-white Cuban tile floors, and modern seating in neutral tones with unique prints. It’s an open, cozy space ideal for intimate stays, perfect for light meals at the bar (no full dining table). Natural light pours in through multiple windows, enhanced by ornate mirrors, and blackout shades are available for a more intimate atmosphere.

The bedroom is a peaceful retreat with a queen-size bed, vintage bedside tables, high-thread-count cotton sheets, and a black ceiling fan for comfort. A dedicated workstation provides a convenient space for work or leisure. The kitchen is equipped for light meals, featuring a stainless steel high table that doubles as a dining or bar area. Step out to the private deck off the bedroom to enjoy Savannah’s beautiful weather.

Conveniently located near shops, restaurants, and nightlife, it’s easy to explore Savannah. Walk to Forsyth Park and the popular Saturday Farmers Market, or take a short drive to River Street, I-16, Tybee Island (about 25 minutes), and historic Fort Pulaski. Whether you’re here for history, art, or a relaxing getaway, this townhome condo offers the perfect blend of comfort, style, and convenience.

Guest access
This is a private triplex and you have access to the property and your unit. Please note that there are exterior cameras for security.

Other things to note
Please note that parking is available on a first-come, first-served basis, with one space per home. On-site parking is located immediately to the left of the building. Free street parking is also available on W. Park Ave. and Montgomery St. Guests are responsible for adhering to all posted parking signage and street-cleaning restrictions.

Neighborhood highlights
You don’t have to go far to experience Savannah’s charm, history, and local flavor. Forsyth Park - the city’s iconic 22-acre green space with its famous fountain, café, arboretum, and frequent events - is just four blocks east.

Walk down Park Avenue to the south end of the park to visit local favorites like Sentient Bean Coffeehouse, Brighter Day Natural Foods, Betty Bombers, and the American Legion Bar. Nearby, you’ll also find Local 11Ten, one of Savannah’s most highly regarded restaurants, along with the Mansion on Forsyth Park Hotel, home to piano lounges, 700 Drayton Restaurant, and an art gallery.

Within walking distance of Josephine’s Carriage House, Rumrunner’s Bakery (two blocks north on Montgomery Street) offers breakfast and lunch for dine-in or takeout. Sandfly Barbecue, about 3.5 blocks away near the SCAD Fashion Building, is a local favorite for classic Southern barbecue and sides.

At the north end of Forsyth Park, the Savannah Design District offers unique shopping, including home furnishings, clothing, jewelry, and specialty boutiques. Downtown Savannah, River Street, and the Starland Art District are all nearby. While some guests enjoy walking, pedicabs, rental bikes, Uber, the CAT bus system, or a car are convenient options. Tybee Island Beach is about a 20–25 minute drive, with Fort Pulaski National Monument making a great stop along the way.
