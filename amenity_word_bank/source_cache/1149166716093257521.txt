Experience the charm and convenience of historic Savannah in this charming carriage house. Located within walking distance to all the main attractions of Downtown Savannah, this federal style row-house is the perfect home base for your trip. And with a small garage space included and free street parking, you won't have to worry about finding a spot in the bustling city.
