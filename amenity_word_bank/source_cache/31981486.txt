=== FULL DESCRIPTION ===
Located on Savannah's most famed street, enjoy all the modern amenities this home has to offer while staying true to the build date of 1860. This historic compound can host your family, and friends comfortably.

The space
Enjoy a grand Savannah getaway at The Broughton Estate, a historic luxury vacation rental offering all the modern amenities you’ve come to expect while traveling. Accommodating up to thirteen Lucky guests, this spacious home is located directly on Broughton Street — Savannah’s iconic “Main Street” — and within short walking distance of the Historic District’s best shopping, award-winning restaurants, galleries, nightlife, and top attractions.

An architectural gem dating back to 1860, Broughton Estate showcases original historic details including intricate ironwork, soaring ceilings, grand parlors, and meticulously maintained interiors that blend timeless elegance with modern comfort. Enter the home on the ground level, where you’ll find a fully stocked kitchen designed for extended stays, a cozy breakfast nook, and a large, inviting bedroom featuring a King bed.

On the second floor, impeccable crown moldings frame the bright and grand parlors, creating an ideal space for gathering and entertaining. A formal dining room with seating for six opens seamlessly to the tastefully decorated living room, offering both comfort and flow for group stays.

Located on the third floor are two spacious bedrooms — one with a Queen bed and Twin sleeper chair, and a primary suite with a King bed. Guests will enjoy luxurious beds dressed in hotel-grade linens, professionally prepared for each stay, along with generous closet space for unpacking and settling in. The primary suite includes an en suite bath with double vanity, standing shower, and a large soaking tub for relaxing after a day exploring Savannah’s Historic District.

Walk past the main home and through the expansive private courtyard to access the Carriage House. The upper-level studio and ground-level studio apartments each feature a full bathroom and fully stocked kitchen, offering additional privacy and flexibility for larger groups, multi-family travel, or wedding guest accommodations. Decorated with classic Southern touches while prioritizing guest comfort, each Queen-size bed is dressed in hotel-grade linens, professionally pressed between visits. Two large closets and a standing chest of drawers encourage guests to unpack and feel at home. A washer and dryer can be found in each Carriage House apartment for added convenience.

What's parking like for this home?
The private garage, located across the courtyard, accommodates two full-size sedans or two mid-size SUVs — a rare and valuable amenity in downtown Savannah. This secure off-street parking provides peace of mind and eliminates the stress of searching for Historic District parking.
