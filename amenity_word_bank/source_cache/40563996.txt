=== FULL DESCRIPTION ===
Century old cottage located steps from the historic district, River Street is .9 miles! Half of a Two-Unit Townhouse; both sides are completely fenced with separate backyards and private dipping pools.  Inside you’ll find Two Stacked Bedrooms, enjoy two Comfy King-sized Beds. Follow the Hallway to a Modest Living Room, and Antique Folding Table for meals. The Petite Kitchen includes all appliances and Washer/Dryer. Almost all the space is in the bedrooms. The backyard shines here!

The space
The duplex is a winner when you want outdoor space with a pool, walking distance to the historic district and large comfy beds. It has a Great Backyard with an Unheated Private Pool, to cool down from the Savannah heat!  Enjoy sipping a beverage in the backyard or a dip in the pool. Each side has a completely private, separate entrance and backyard. 
The apartment is a historic cottage originally built in 1871! There are 2 bedrooms, each with a King Size Bed, 1-bath and it is 1/2 of a duplex. 
 A small living room is suitable for planning your next day's adventures. Grab a bite to eat in the functional kitchen that's great for ready-to-eat or on-the-go meals, not for creating complex signature dishes although it is well stocked with kitchenware. An antique folding table serves as your sit-down dining space.
There is a full-size Washer and Dryer for your convenience. 
Our place is a Clean, Down-To-Earth Cottage and is especially practical for business travelers, or traveling companions desiring multiple rooms or small families.

If you need more than two bedrooms, both sides of the duplex are available, see link below.
 airbnb.com/h/downtownhistoricduplex

Guest access
We use a keyless lock system for a no contact entry for guests.  Just enter your password number provided to you in the Welcome Message through the Platform.

Other things to note
You may either park your vehicle right on the street out front or on the nearby side streets if you prefer (E Harris or E Charlton). There are typically plenty of spaces available and it is free on the one side of E. Harris and the one side of E. Charlton.  The metered parking spaces are free after 5 PM until 8 AM and Sunday all day.
