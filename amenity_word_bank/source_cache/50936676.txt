=== FULL DESCRIPTION ===
Your Savannah escape awaits! This charming apartment is just off of Greene Square, tucked away on State Street. Perfect for those looking to be within walking distance to Savannah's best restaurants, shops, and museums.

The space
Looking for the perfect location, but don't desire to be in the hustle and bustle of Savannah's nightlife? State Street Escape is tucked away off of quiet Greene Square, only one block from Broughton Street and just minutes from Savannah's famed River Street!

The décor of this charming home creates an inviting and comfortable atmosphere for your entire party. Stepping into the living space, guests have an ample amount of plush seating, where they can gather together at the end of the day and catch up on their favorite shows. The open floor plan of this property flows from the combined living space with breakfast bar, into the fully equipped kitchen. The elevated, and spacious kitchen features all of the modern conveniences one could wish for. Everything has been tastefully selected to make this an exceptionally convenient spot to call home… even just for a few days!

Tucked away, and boasting with natural light, the Queen-sized bedroom features a soothing, neutral palette and with a ensuite bathroom. The beautiful ensuite bath has been recently remodeled, and features an elevated vanity, plus a spacious shower/tub combination. 

Greene Square acts as a serene oasis just nearby the hub of bustling downtown. Guests are only a short walking distance away from the Riverfront, City Market, and most of downtown's shopping and dining options. 

Sleeping Configuration:
Bedroom 1: Queen Size Bed
Living Room: Queen Size Sleeper Sofa

Parking: Metered Parking
Parking for this home is on-street and metered, with enforcement by the City of Savannah Monday through Saturday, 8am to 8pm. Guests may conveniently pay using the ParkSavannah app, feed the meter or parking kiosks directly, or utilize a nearby public parking garage. Pre-paid visitor parking passes are available for purchase at the Parking and Mobility Services office. Please take note of posted Sweep Zone signs when parking on the street.

We can welcome your dog companions at this pet friendly home, but ask that you find other arrangements for cats, birds, and other furry friends. There is a $130 pet fee (non-refundable) with a 35 lb. maximum weight limit per dog and a limit of 2 dogs per reservation. Some homes may allow larger dogs, please inquire for more information.
