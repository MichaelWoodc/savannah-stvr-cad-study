=== FULL DESCRIPTION ===
Enjoy a stunning rooftop balcony directly on the St. Patrick's Day parade route in this historic 4th-floor apartment. Once home to a sea captain, it features a luxurious king bed, a movie room, and an airy living space with exposed brick and wood beams. With two full bathrooms, a queen pullout couch for extra guests, and a 100% walkable location in Savannah's Historic District, this spacious one-bedroom retreat offers comfort and charm for an unforgettable stay.

The space
Perched atop the historic home of Captain Henry James Dickerson, a renowned 19th-century sea captain and influential entrepreneur, this luxurious apartment offers an unparalleled stay in the heart of Savannah’s Historic District. Captain Dickerson’s legacy as a sailor, businessman, and civic leader is woven into the very fabric of this stunning residence, nestled on picturesque Oglethorpe Square.

This spacious one-bedroom, two-bathroom retreat combines historical charm with modern elegance. Architectural details like exposed brick walls, original wood beams, and large windows bathe the space in natural light. The open-plan living area includes a fully equipped kitchen, a dining space, and a cozy lounge perfect for relaxing after exploring the city. A queen-sized pullout sofa accommodates extra guests comfortably.

Unmatched Features 
- Rooftop Balcony: Step onto the private rooftop oasis, where comfortable outdoor seating and dining spaces invite you to unwind while taking in breathtaking views of Savannah’s skyline. It’s the perfect spot to sip your morning coffee or enjoy a sunset toast. 
- Prime St. Patrick’s Day Parade Location: Experience the excitement of Savannah’s legendary St. Patrick’s Day Parade, with your front-row seat right from the balcony. 
- Luxurious Amenities: The king bedroom offers plush bedding for a restful night, and the apartment features a movie room for cozy evenings. Two full bathrooms provide convenience and privacy for all guests.

Important Details 
- Accessibility: Please note that guests will need to climb stairs to access this fourth-floor apartment. 
- Parking: Street parking and nearby garage parking are available for a fee. 
- Walkability: Situated in a 100% walkable location, you’re steps away from Savannah’s finest restaurants, shops, and historical landmarks.

Immerse yourself in Savannah’s charm, history, and vibrant culture at Savannah City View. Whether you’re here for a romantic getaway, a family escape, or a festive holiday celebration, this exceptional apartment promises an unforgettable experience.

Other things to note
Parking is available at the State Street Parking Garage for a daily rate of $12. The garage is conveniently located just a two-minute walk across the square. Additionally, electric vehicle charging stations are available within the facility.
