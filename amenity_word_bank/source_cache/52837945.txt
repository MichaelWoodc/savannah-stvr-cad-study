The space
✨ Romantic Canopy Studio on Forsyth Park (1888 Victorian) ✨

Step into a romantic 1888 Victorian studio filled with charm, soul, and a touch of Savannah’s artistic heritage. For over a century, this home has welcomed women artists and creatives who lived, painted, and studied within these very walls. Their presence left behind a warm, expressive energy that still gives the studio its uniquely cozy, inspiring feel today.

This intimate retreat blends classic architecture with curated SCAD-inspired art and features:

Highlights
   •   12-foot ceilings and original heart pine floors that are over 100 years old
      •   These floors are wonderfully rustic and authentic, with a naturally aged patina and a few faint speckles of historic white paint — lovingly preserved as part of the home’s character (not newly refinished).
   •   Luxurious king canopy bed with a highly-rated Tuft & Needle mattress
   •   Decorative original coal-burning fireplace (non-working)
   •   Blackout curtains for deep rest
   •   Bright full bathroom with tub and shower

Kitchen & Amenities
   •   Studio-size kitchen with a charming vintage-style dishwasher
   •   Keurig with dark roast pods, cream & sugar
   •   Two closets + full dresser for rare, generous storage in a studio
   •   Eclectic artwork celebrating Savannah’s creative spirit and artistic legacy
