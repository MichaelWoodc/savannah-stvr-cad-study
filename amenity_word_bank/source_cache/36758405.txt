=== FULL DESCRIPTION ===
Located in the heart of Savannah along Broughton St. this beautiful loft provides a modern and chic accommodation for 3 guests. Featuring 1 queen bed and an oversized sofa/futon, this freshly updated studio puts you right in the middle of all the shopping, restaurants, and attractions that Savannah has to offer.  Walk just 8 mins to the River Walk, 15 minutes to Forsyth park and enjoy a neighborhood full of quaint stores and restaurants. 18 min to SAV International.

SVR-02001

The space
Located on the second floor of a beautiful mixed-use brick building enjoy a side street entrance and fantastic views out onto bustling Broughton St. below. Step into the studio and be greeted by beautiful original features like hard plank floors, exposed brick, and oversized windows providing an abundance of natural light. 
Directly inside the front door you will find the studio’s bathroom and an in-unit stackable washer/dryer. Experience the low entry shower with custom slate tile and an oversized shower head while noticing all the bathroom essentials including toilet paper, towels, shampoo, body wash, and a hair dryer
Following the bathroom and laundry closet is the studio’s wall-separated bedroom featuring a brand-new queen mattress with fresh linens. Tasteful décor, high ceilings, and deep blues provide the perfect space for a restful night sleep. Staying for longer? A dresser and mirrored closet provide ample storage space. 
Step into the studio further and find an open concept between the kitchen, dining room, and living space. A fully appointed kitchen featuring custom cabinets, stainless steel appliances, and kitchen essentials gives you the ability to cook during your stay. From kitchen utensils and dishware to small appliances such as a coffee maker, toaster, and blender, this kitchen has everything you need. 
A small dining set sits directly off the kitchen and is set for four. The living room boasts an oversized comfortable sofa and smart TV set up for your favorite streaming services. Accented by a beautiful, exposed brick wall, industrial ductwork, and sleek décor you will be living the urban loft lifestyle as you sit and relax at the end of the day. 
Working remotely during your stay? The studio provides table space and high-speed WIFI capable of supporting video conferencing and multi-tasking.

Neighborhood highlights
Situated on Broughton street you will be in the heart of all the southern charm Savannah has to offer. Surrounded by brick storefronts you can stroll down Broughton and simply take in all the small businesses and restaurants right in your neighborhood.

Enjoy one of the many coffee and breakfast spots such as The Coffee Fox, Kayak Kafe, The Funky Brunch Café, or Maple Street Biscuity Company.
Looking to excite your tastebuds? Check out the trendy Chive Sea Bar and Lounge serving seafood with Asian and southern touches surrounded by a 1920’s art deco feel. Grab classic southern seafood at the Savannah Seafood Shack, or dive into an incredible Italian dish at Bella Napoli Italian Bistro. Craving a wood fire pizza? A stop into 45 Bistro is an absolute must.
Ready to pour a few pints? Walk on over to the Savannah Taphouse for an upscale sports bar. Get your retro on by heading to Totally Awesome bar, featuring pool tables and a nostalgic atmosphere. Ready to be creeped out by an inordinate amount of Abe Lincoln drawings while you sip your beer? Check out Abe’s on Lincoln. It’s an experience.
A trip to Savannah is not complete without exploring a little of its historic colonial past. Fill your schedule by checking out the Telfair Academy, a beautiful art museum, or stroll through the Webb Military Museum. Relive the prohibition area by heading to the American Prohibition Museum. Don’t forget to grab a drink at their cocktail bar to celebrate freedom. Or fancy exploring Savannah’s ocean ties? Check out the Ships of the Sea Maritime Museum.
Not enough time to walk all the sights of Savannah? Take it all in at once while riding or biking. We are just minutes away from Old Town Trolley Tours of Savannah and Savannah Bike Tours. Check out the city’s skyline from the water by jumping on one of the Savannah Belle’s ferries, with multiple stops all along Savannah’s riverfront.
Getting around is easy with walkable streets, bike rentals, and uber service. Jump on Savannah’s public transit system with the Broughton & Jefferson WB stop just a 2-minute walk from the loft.
