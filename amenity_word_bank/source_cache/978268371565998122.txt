=== FULL DESCRIPTION ===
Welcome to Historic Heights on Broughton, your top-floor luxury retreat in the heart of Savannah's Historic District. Located in a beautifully restored 1891 building, this spacious condo features exposed brick, original hardwood floors, elevator access, and stunning city skyline views. Walk to Broughton Street shopping, City Market, River Street, Plant Riverside, award-winning restaurants, rooftop bars, and Savannah's best attractions, all just steps from your door.

The space
Experience Savannah from the heart of it all! Perched on the top floor of a beautifully restored 1891 historic building, Historic Heights on Broughton blends timeless Savannah character with modern comfort. Exposed brick walls, original hardwood floors, soaring ceilings, and beautiful city skyline views create a warm and inviting space you'll love coming home to after a day of exploring.

Located on iconic Broughton Street, you'll be steps from Savannah's best boutiques, coffee shops, award-winning restaurants, rooftop bars, and vibrant nightlife. River Street, the Plant Riverside District, City Market, and many of Savannah's beautiful historic squares are all just an easy walk away, making this the perfect home base for experiencing the city without ever needing your car.

Thoughtfully designed for couples, families, friends, and business travelers alike, this spacious two-bedroom, two-bath condo comfortably accommodates up to 4 guests comfortably.

Both bedrooms feature plush queen beds with soft linens for a restful night's sleep, while the queen sleeper sofa in the living room provides additional sleeping space when needed. Two beautifully updated bathrooms are stocked with fresh towels, complimentary toiletries, and everything you need to settle in comfortably.

The bright, open living space combines historic architectural details with modern amenities, creating the perfect place to gather after a day in the city. Curl up by the electric fireplace with your favorite movie, enjoy game night with family and friends, or simply relax while taking in the charm of this unique historic building.

The fully equipped kitchen includes stainless steel appliances, everything you'll need to prepare meals at home, and a spacious island that's perfect for morning coffee before heading out to explore Savannah. An in-unit washer and dryer are also available for your convenience, making the condo ideal for both weekend escapes and extended stays.

We can't wait to host you!

SVR-02829

Guest access
Guests will enjoy exclusive access to the entire condo throughout their stay, with the exception of two owner's storage closets. The condo is located on the top floor of a secure historic building and offers elevator access and keyless entry, making arrivals and departures easy.

Other things to note
Because you're staying in the center of Savannah's Historic District, you'll experience the vibrant energy that makes downtown so special. Restaurants, bars, and nightlife are just outside your door, particularly on Friday and Saturday evenings, so some city noise is to be expected. We've provided a sound machine to help ensure a restful night's sleep.
