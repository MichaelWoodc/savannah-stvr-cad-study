=== FULL DESCRIPTION ===
This cute 2 bedroom/2 bath cottage has all the historic charm you want with all the modern amenities you need! On Tattnall Street in the Historic District, this Cottage is the perfect choice for everything from a romantic getaway to a girl's weekend.

The space
Built circa 1876, this charming historic home home has been recently refurbished to showcase the home’s exposed ceiling beams and wide plank heart pine floors. Warm and inviting, you’ll feel right at home during your stay in Savannah! 

The living room has a wall mounted television and a new leather sofa bed. Twin decorative fireplaces flank the dining area, which seats 4 to 6 people comfortably. The remodeled kitchen has granite counter tops and stainless steel appliances. A door from the kitchen leads to a private paved courtyard perfect to enjoy coffee, refreshments, or grill your evening meal.

On the main floor, the bright and sunny primary bedroom has a queen bed, a TV, dresser and a spacious closet. Adjacent is a full bathroom with a pedestal sink and a tub/shower combination. The upstairs loft bedroom has a king-sized bed, TV, a full bath, and full sized washer and dryer. 

In the residential area of the Historic District, you’ll have a quiet and relaxing retreat to return to each night while still being within walking distance to several Savannah landmarks, including Mrs. Wilkes Dining Room and the infamous Mercer-Williams House of Midnight in the Garden of Good and Evil. 

As a historic cottage, this home has original features uncommon to many of today’s homes. Specifically, the stairs to upstairs bedroom are steeper than newly built homes and should be taken into consideration for those with mobility issues.
 
Sleeping Configuration:
Bedroom 1: Queen Bed
Bedroom 2: King Bed
Living Room: Queen Sleeper Sofa

We can welcome your dog companions at this pet friendly home, but ask that you find other arrangements for cats, birds, and other furry friends. There is a $130 pet fee (non-refundable) with a 35 lb. maximum weight limit per dog and a limit of 2 dogs per reservation. Some homes may allow larger dogs, please inquire for more information.

What is parking like for this home?
There are two private parking spaces located to the right of the front door to the home.
