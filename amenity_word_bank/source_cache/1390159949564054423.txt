=== FULL DESCRIPTION ===
Tucked away in Savannah’s Historic District, this modern apartment is a beautifully designed retreat just steps from iconic squares, shops, and restaurants. With a king bed, shared garden courtyard, and walk-everywhere location, it’s the perfect home base for couples, friends, or solo travelers looking to experience Savannah like a local.

The space
Welcome to this spacious ground-level garden apartment nestled in the heart of Savannah’s Historic District. Designed with modern comfort and Southern charm, this private retreat comfortably sleeps up to four guests with a large king bedroom and queen sleeper sofa bed. 

The space features a full modern bathroom, a fully equipped kitchen for coffee mornings or cozy dinners in, smart TVs in both the living room and bedroom, and an in-unit washer and dryer for added convenience. Guests also enjoy access to a shared courtyard—an unexpected garden oasis right in the city—along with easy self check-in for a seamless stay.

Located just blocks from Savannah’s most iconic squares, scenic parks, boutique shopping, and beloved restaurants, everything is easily walkable. Whether you’re strolling oak-lined streets, exploring the Riverfront, or unwinding in the courtyard after a day out, this peaceful hideaway puts you right in the heart of Southern charm. With thoughtful touches, fast Wi-Fi, free nearby street parking, and attentive Superhost hospitality. 

This home is ideal for couples, solo travelers, and small groups seeking a relaxed, locally curated Savannah stay.

All renters must be over the age of 25, unless it is a child accompanied by a parent.

License Permit Number: SVR-03119

Guest access
Guests will have exclusive access to the entire private apartment, along with shared access to the peaceful courtyard located on the property. The courtyard is a lovely spot to enjoy morning coffee or unwind in the evening. Entry is via self check-in with a secure keypad, allowing for a smooth and flexible arrival. Street parking is available nearby and is free, though guests should be mindful of posted street-sweeping signage and metered streets on neighboring streets.

Other things to note
We are a smoke free property. This includes No vaping. Smoking and vaping is not allowed in the Courtyard. As this home is located in the heart of Savannah’s Historic District, guests may hear typical city sounds, including occasional street noise, pedestrians, nearby restaurants, or horse-drawn carriages during the day and early evening. While the apartment is designed to be a peaceful retreat we want guest to be aware there may be city noises they may not be accustomed to. Many guests appreciate the lively atmosphere—it’s all part of the charm of staying in the city center.
