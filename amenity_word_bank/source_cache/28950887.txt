=== FULL DESCRIPTION ===
This updated, modern 1BR/1BA loft is located in the most beautiful area of Savannah, Georgia. The space is conveniently located steps away from Savannah's most popular shops and is about a 5 minute walk to River Street and Plant Riverside. With hardwood floors, granite counter tops, exposed brick and new renovations, it is a luxurious home away from home.

*We also have two other units next to this one to accommodate larger groups!*

STVR Certificate # SVR-02888

The space
Highlights include an ensuite master bedroom, an open floor plan, stainless steel appliances, granite countertops, exposed brick walls, ample storage and much more. We also have an en-suite washer and dryer. 

The loft is located across from Kate Spade, Fab'rik, Lululemon, Kendra Scott, Spartina and many more!

Guest access
Once inside of the building, you will have access to the floor this unit is located on via stairs or elevator. You will then have complete access to the unit as there will be no one else inside.

Other things to note
Smoking is not allowed inside the unit. We ask that you politely find a smoking area outside!

Pets are allowed in the unit however you will be charged a pet fee of $50 per pet.

We are only 2 blocks away from Plant Riverside District!

***Please note we require all guests to submit a valid form of ID through our check in form prior to check in. If you do not submit the ID we will not be able to send you check in instructions.***
