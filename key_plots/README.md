# Key capacity and amenity plots

These are exploratory recorded-address associations, not proof that a property, guests, size, or amenities caused an incident. The selection deliberately finds rising series in these same data and is not independent validation. Overlapping minimum-threshold groups are not independent; small end groups can be unstable. Zeros are included. STVR rates use a 59% occupancy scenario, whereas residential reference lines use calendar years; see both rates before comparing. A same-category baseline is the meaningful like-for-like call outcome; the all-calls line is context only. The parking period is shorter. Existing plots and evidence remain untouched.

100 rising minimum-threshold charts; selection audit includes all 110 scanned candidates. For each plot, open same-category for legibility or all-calls-context for the requested full residential total. Curves retain all original points, even those with fewer than 10 parcels. Selection requires at least four n>=10 groups with positive weighted slope and last supported mean above first; it does not require a smooth or monotonic rise.

## Amenity combinations

The ten copied review candidates come from an adjusted search across many combinations. None passed the 5% false-discovery screen in the supplied results; these are not established causal effects or evidence of interaction. Conditions apply within one listing. The original analysis, data, and uncertainty details are in ../party_combination_analysis/. All 10 plots include same-category and all-call residential references.
