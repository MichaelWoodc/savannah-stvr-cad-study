=== FULL DESCRIPTION ===
This brand new townhouse with both parlor and garden level condos will take your breath away as soon as you step inside.  Boasting over 2,800 square feet, it was tastefully decorated with an eye for design and fully equipped with modern amenities and appliances in two kitchens. Perfect for a family getaway, a girls' weekend, a gathering with friends, or even a business destination! The house is located on beautiful Liberty St in the Landmark Historic District of Savannah.

SVR 02098 and 02099

The space
This lovely townhouse features two open concept living rooms and kitchens and dedicated dining space for six.  On the third floor, two primary bedrooms await with spa-like en-suite, private baths.  Guests who prefer not to climb stairs for bed-time can tuck into the ground level king bedroom.  Both living room couches pulls out into a KING sized, super luxe, amazingly comfortable bed...perfect for an extra couple or kids!  All told, there are three full bathrooms and another convenient half bath. This entire three-story main house with private porch and courtyard is all yours.

Guest access
The entire townhome with both parlor level and garden level townhome is all yours!  They are connected via an indoor staircase as well as both the front and back stairs outside.

Other things to note
Don't be afraid to reach out if you need anything!  We live super close-by and are always happy to help out, even mid-stay.

The BEST part of our home? The location! We are in the heart of the Downtown Historic District, making the best places to dine, drink, and explore super accessible! We are with a 15 minute walk from the Savannah Riverfront, City Market, The Olde Pink House, Clary's, The Fountain at Forsyth Park, Mrs. Wilkes, and the Colonial Park Cemetery! Better yet, all of Savannah's beautiful squares are right outside of your door...perfect for exploring both the beautiful live oaks with their hanging moss as well as the historic architecture that frames each one! Want to check out SCAD? We are within walking distance to almost all of their downtown campus buildings!

If you choose to stay with us and need lots of recommendations...don't worry! We are happy to provide personalized recommendations based on your interests and preferences...just ask us! We are passionate about our little Southern city and are happy to provide lots of awesome spots to check out! We can share a digital list of full of our personal favorites, from quiet local spots to must-sees attractions! And we live right around the corner, so we are ALWAYS here if you need us!
