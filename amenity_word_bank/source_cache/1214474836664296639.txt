The space
Located in the heart of downtown Savannah, just steps from Monterey Square, Southern Belle Carriage combines historic charm with modern amenities. Enjoy easy access to Forsyth Park, local restaurants, and shopping.

Located on Jones Lane, this 1-bedroom, 1-bathroom, two-story carriage house is rich with historic details. Accessed via lane, guests will enjoy the beautiful features it offers, such as refinished hardwood floors and an ample amount of space for your guests to spread out!

What Do We Love About Southern Belle Carriage? 

- Inviting Spaces: The charming living room has a smart TV, exposed brick, and a dining table for four. The kitchen is fully equipped with everything needed to prepare home cooked meals, including a microwave, stove, oven, and full-size refrigerator!

- Spacious Bedroom: The bedroom boasts a king-size bed and a twin bed, perfect for children, large windows overlooking the tree-lined street, and a smart TV. 

- Location: Southern Belle Carriage is located on East Jones Lane, near the famous Mercer-Williams House and Congregation Mickve Israel, both of which offer tours of their historic buildings. Enjoy your stay in this beautifully preserved townhome in Savannah's historic district.

Sleeping Configuration:
Bedroom: King Size Bed + 1 Twin Bed

What is parking like for this home?
Parking for this home is on-street and metered, which is enforced by the City of Savannah from Monday through Friday, 8am to 5pm. Guests may conveniently pay using the ParkSavannah app, feed the meter or parking kiosks directly, or park on a nearby non-metered street. Please take note of posted Sweep Zone signs when parking on the street.
