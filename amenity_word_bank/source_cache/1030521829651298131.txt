=== FULL DESCRIPTION ===
Welcome to this beautifully renovated 5-bedroom, 3-bathroom carriage house in Savannah's historic Victorian District, designed for groups of up to 11 guests. Located a 2-minute walk from Forsyth Park, this 2-story home features 12-foot ceilings, heart pine floors, and access to a shared heated outdoor pool and spa, making it an ideal retreat for groups exploring the best of Savannah.

The space
This carriage house is located on a compound with 3 units in total. This listing is for the carriage house unit. Please inquire if you are interested in renting these units together.

A few steps are required to access the front door. This is a 2-story home with bedrooms on both floors, accessible via an interior staircase. The unit features 12-foot ceilings, plaster moldings, and heart pine floors. The shared outdoor pool and spa are located directly in front of the carriage house entrance. Neighborhood highlights
Located a quick 2 min walk away from Forsyth Park, our home places you at the doorstep of Savannah’s acclaimed food and art scenes. Check out the SCAD Museum of Art, or spend the day shopping and dining along the riverfront. There are a few homes in the neighborhood that need to be renovated, so while this could still be considered a transitional neighborhood, we have never had any safety issues. As far as safety is concerned, common sense is the general rule of thumb, just like in any major city. All guests after booking will receive a personalized list of recommendations including locals favorite spots, restaurants, and activities.
