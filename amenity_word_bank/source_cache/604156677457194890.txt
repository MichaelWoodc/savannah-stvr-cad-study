=== FULL DESCRIPTION ===
The "Silk-n-art" refers to my collection of silk wall hangings and fine art. This large 2 story apartment is in the best part of the historic district, quieter, softer, more gentile like the nearby mansions.  Besides 2 king bedrooms each with private skylit baths, there is a private flex room with a gel foam sofa pull-out bed downstairs that can be used as an office or a bedroom.  There’s also a couch in the LR.   Read more in "The Space".
SVR-02556

The space
The rooms are  graciously proportioned, the ceilings tall, and the wood floors elegant.  The large Roku tv's are on stands in every room to allow more tasteful art on the walls.  The mattresses are gel foam and are extremely comfortable with hi thread count luxury bedding.  
the towels are exotic, silver-infused, cotton meant to be hung and reused. The large kitchen includes a large dining area with 6 thickly-upholstered chairs and an inlaid mid century modern formal dining table.  The kitchen and dining area are bright and well stocked.  It overlooks a large private heavily planted patio deck with a small fountain.  

Walk out your front door and there are gorgeous mansions in all directions. Forsyth Park is just two blocks west with some of the best architected mansions along the way!  A unique coffee shop is one block to the east, and a Kroger's is one block south.  Walk north from Forsyth Park along Bull St. with its amazing shops and restaurant and you are led to River St, the oldest tourist hot spot.

 walk to River street

Guest access
You have access to everything.

Other things to note
One dog but zero cats are allowed and only if their nails are ground recently to protect the walnut and oak floors.  $95 dollar pet fee. It is courteous to the comfort and feelings of others if you have your dog recently groomed before your trip.  Shedding dogs not allowed. Sorry!  If your dog sheds a lot but you decide to bring him, you agree to pay $125 extra fee. People hate renting a place with dog hair and so much extra cleaning time is required, sometimes causing the carpets to be cleaned every inch with masking tape because some breeds have hair that resist vacuuming.

Security cameras by the front and back door.  Very private unit with no shared common spaces or entryways.
