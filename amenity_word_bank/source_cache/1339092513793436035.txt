The space
Welcome to The Captain’s House, a historic gem built in 1853 by Captain Henry James Dickerson, a prominent figure in Savannah’s maritime history.

Eleven bedrooms and eleven bathrooms, perfect for large groups. 7 king beds and 4 queen beds plus two queen pullout couches so that you and your crew can have privacy while enjoying Savannah as a group. Five kitchens await your every need. You won't find a better location in Savannah. Add to that a rooftop balcony, luxury bedding and incredibly well maintained historic home and you've got a vacation to remember.

This home is 5 separate apartments, rented all together for large groups. All king bedrooms are ensuite.

This beautifully restored property offers 11 bedrooms and 9 full and 2 half bathrooms, making it the perfect destination for large groups, family reunions, or small retreats. 

With accommodations for up to 26 guests, this home provides a unique and unforgettable setting for your gathering.

Located in the heart of Savannah’s Historic District, no car is needed to explore the city’s charm. Simply Uber from the airport and enjoy walking to iconic landmarks, squares, shops, and restaurants. Situated directly on the St. Patrick’s Day parade route, The Captain’s House offers unparalleled access to one of Savannah’s most celebrated events.

Inside, you’ll find exposed brick, spacious interiors, and breathtaking views of Savannah. Whether you’re planning a festive celebration or a peaceful retreat, The Captain’s House is the ultimate blend of history, luxury, and convenience.

Paid street parking is available. There is a parking garage available at the far side of Oglethorpe Square, a short walk away. You will not need a car while staying at the Captain's House. Broughton Street and all shopping and restaurants are an easy 2-10 minute walk. River Street is a 7 minute walk.

Highlights included morning coffee on the porches, late-night chats on the rooftop deck, and relaxing in the theater room with massage chairs. A clean place and plush beds made for a great stay.
We’ve officially found our annual Savannah getaway spot—thank you, Sid!!!
