What should be included when planning an itinerary in beautiful, highly hospitable Savannah? The Savannah Historic District can be a great spot for dipping one's toes into the splendor that is Savannah's famed architectural traditions. Look for the boundaries of the Savannah River, East Broad Street, Forsyth Park, and Martin Luther King, Jr. Boulevard to know that you're exploring within one of the nation's largest historical landmarks! Admiring the gorgeous estates with "painted" porches is one of the best activities to do during a dreamy Georgia afternoon. Be sure to add Bonaventure Cemetery, the Cathedral of Saint John the Baptist, Sorrel Weed House, the American Prohibition Museum, Olde Pink House, the Wormsloe Historic Site, and the William Mercer Home to your list. When it's time to eat and shop, consider heading to City Market to check out Savannah's premier pedestrian-only zone. This little treasure is home to many one-of-a-kind shops that offer terrific gift items. Savannah's famed River Street is another great area to focus on when it's time to dine, shop, dance, and more! This is considered the place to "party" in Savannah on the weekends. River Street is home to many great pubs, bars, and clubs where you can enjoy live music while river ferries float by. You can even consider taking a ferry from downtown's riverwalk to Hutchinson Island. When seeking more excursions, add pub crawls, fun trolley tours, and haunted cemetery tours to the list of adventures that show off all of the thrills and delights of Savannah. Finally, Savannah is a fantastic place to catch a festival. The list of festivals that attract visitors from around the country each year includes the Savannah Jazz Festival, the Savannah Craft Brew Fest, the Savannah Film Festival, and the Pin Point Seafood Festival.
