=== FULL DESCRIPTION ===
Stunning 1 bedroom, 1 bathroom Jones Street apartment. This chic living space on ever popular Jones Street, offers true Savannah elegance and unbeatable Southern charm. *Dog Friendly*

The space
Boasting prestigious city addresses, the wide, tree-lined Jones Street is a local favorite with original cobblestone streets and some of Savannah’s most exquisite homes. This stunning 1 bedroom, 1 bathroom apartment features hardwood floors, decorative fireplaces, and a lush courtyard. Gorgeous hardwood floors, grand decorative fireplaces, and a lush courtyard. 

Located within walking distance of a variety of locally-owned shops and restaurants, as well as beautiful Forsyth Park, Broughton Street and City Market. The location of this elegant little place makes it easy to stay in and relax, or venture out and take in that all Savannah has to offer. 

This property is literally steps from downtown!  Located on famous Historic Jones Street, you can walk anywhere in the city with ease.  Don't feel like walking?  You can enjoy a free Downtowner or call a pedi-cab for a stroll around the city.
