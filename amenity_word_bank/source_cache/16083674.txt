=== FULL DESCRIPTION ===
Garden Suite-Your Family Oasis at Forsyth Park. 
Park Off-street, Walk Everywhere.
One of The Suites at Forsyth

Barely a stone's throw from Forsyth Park, The Suites at Forsyth will set the bar for your vacation rental experience in Savannah. Lovingly restored in the classic Savannah style, you will fall in love with this 1870's townhouse. One of three units in the building, Garden Suite is located on the ground floor.

City of Savannah Short-Term Vacation Rental Certificate SVR-00406

The space
Old World Grace and Modern Convenience with Assigned Parking for One Car--A Rarity in Savannah!

Your breath will catch as you enter.  This gorgeous space feels like you’ve stepped into the pages of a design magazine. With exceptional attention to detail, the beautiful restoration blends old world grace and modern convenience. The original heart pine floors gleam, the modern kitchen and bathrooms glow, and the 12-foot ceilings frame it all. To create this perfect blend of old Savannah charm and a modern designer's touch, all of the furnishings and art were sourced locally and thoughtfully in Savannah.

In the foyer is a roll-top desk where you can check email. The gracious and welcoming living room boasts a 12-foot ceiling and an antique Italian chandelier. Every corner of this room is a feast for the eyes. There is a comfortable sofa and chairs, reading lamps, and a 50-inch high definition TV. The living room flows easily into the huge eat-in kitchen with a round farm table at its center. It is a perfect place to gather with friends and family to cook a great meal. We supply coffee (Free Trade Organic), teas, spices and oils to save you time and money. 

The library, with its cozy day bed, is tucked off the kitchen and is a great place to slip away to curl up with a book or take a nap. Here you will find another desk as well as our lending library. The laundry room features a full-sized washer and dryer and an iron and board.

The sleeping quarters are down the hallway from the main living area. Each bedroom has its own full bath directly across the hall from it. One bedroom has a comfortable king bed, nightstands, large dresser and a wardrobe. The second has a comfortable queen bed, nightstands, a dresser, and a small wardrobe. Both bathrooms are gorgeously tiled and filled with an assortment of personal care products including a hair dryer. One bath has a full-sized shower and the second has an original cast iron tub and shower. Quality bed linens and towels are provided, along with soaps, shampoo and conditioner. You'll also find a white-noise sound machine in each bedroom. The just-installed heating and cooling system will keep you comfortable no matter what the weather brings. 

Reach your private deck through the back of the suite. With a table for six and direct access to the incredible courtyard, this is a perfect place to dine al fresco, enjoy your morning coffee, or sip sweet tea or wine in the afternoon. If you're feeling social, simply step off your deck into the lush tropical oasis that is the shared courtyard.

For your entertainment, enjoy WIFI, cable TV with 70 channels, as well as streaming video--bring your own codes. For music, you can choose Pandora on the Amazon Fire TV Stick or the Amazon Echo for effortless music streaming. Simply say "Echo, play jazz" (or your favorite selection) and the music will play. 

We use Seventh Generation cleaning products and choose fragrance-free whenever possible. 

Two Outdoor Living Rooms
Gather with friends on the graceful front porch under the glow of the gas lanterns. Each walk-up porch offers seating for six and recessed lighting adds to a warm glow to the gas lamps. 

Delight in the rear courtyard, our crown jewel. This tropical oasis is ideal for morning coffee, afternoon cocktails, dining al fresco, and enjoying the warm Savannah nights in a gently-lit dream garden. 

The fenced and secure courtyard is shared by all of the guests of The Suites at Forsyth* and was designed to be experienced both communally and privately. The center is a large patio where you can gather with family, friends and meet other guests. If you desire a private experience, then slip into one of the four sheltered spaces that nestle at the periphery of the courtyard. These four ‘rooms’ are tucked into the foliage; each with vintage outdoor chairs and tables. The lovely lighting, lush landscaping, and splashing water from the fountain will make the courtyard your favorite place in Savannah--your ‘outdoor living room’.

*The Suites at Forsyth consist of six private residences within two historic, attached homes. We’re confident you’ll fall in love with our 1870's townhouses just like we did! We are two sets of friends who fulfilled a long-held desire to renovate a beautiful structure in one of Savannah's historic districts.

Guest access
This 1150 sq. ft. private apartment is at your complete disposal. There is room in the closets, drawers and bathroom shelves for you to unpack and feel at home. Garden Suite is one of three apartments sharing the front door and the front porch on this side of the house. There are six units total that share the courtyard.

Other things to note
