=== FULL DESCRIPTION ===
The Miss Rose Historic Town Home is a warm, private home perfect for couples, solo adventurers, small families, and the family pup. 

The Townhome includes a living & dining area downstairs, a master bedroom suite with a queen-sized bed, and a bathroom upstairs: TV,  cable & free WiFi. A full kitchen with a coffee station and microwave.

This listing is awarded ‘Super Host’ status in the Historic District of Savannah.

GA SVR -01477

The space
The historic Chelsea is in the beautiful Victorian neighborhood of Savannah’s historic district. Perfect for a family of two adults and up to two children who are old enough to be comfortable in a house with stairs. 

Forsyth Park is located just three blocks from your Town Home. It is a beautiful 30-acre park that is home to many of Savannah’s outdoor festivals and offers something for the whole family. The city’s most famous fountain crowns the Park's northern edge and serves as an inspiring backdrop for contemplation, selfies, marriage proposals, and weddings. Sometimes the stroll to the fountain is crowded with our seasonal farmer’s market or dotted with local artisans working to the sounds of a lively trumpet. On quieter days, you may see locals out with their dogs, groups playing frisbee or having a picnic, avid readers in hammocks, and art students sketching the surrounding beauty. There are tennis and basketball courts and paved paths for walking, jogging, or biking. 

Forsyth Park also has an all-accessible playground with slides, swings, and climbers that can accommodate up to 300 children. The ground is covered with rubber matting and mulch, there are even interactive educational stations. Between the playground and the gated fragrant garden for the blind, there is a second playground with swings, climbers, and a slide. A building near the center of the park houses a café, restrooms, and a canopied bandshell where kids can dance to their heart's content. There is a spray pool in front of the bandshell for kids to enjoy on warmer days. There are several restaurants near the south end of the park too. Being so close to your Town Home we highly recommend checking out Forsyth Park!

Here in Savannah, you will discover amazing American history around every corner. Within walking distance of your Town Home, you can explore any of the 22 squares amid thriving businesses and historic buildings while reading about Savannah’s history on beautiful plaques. You may choose to stroll back in time, shop along our famous River Street, or listen to live music in Market Square. Savannah offers a diverse selection of museums, many of which provide special areas for children. 

Feel like having a beach day? Head to one of the East Coast’s most pristine beaches found on Tybee Island, just a short drive away. Go on a sunset dolphin cruise or have some fun in the sun paddle-boarding, kayaking, fishing, swimming, or just lazing around. The Savannah area is such a wonderful place, and we welcome you to come to enjoy a good dose of our southern hospitality!

Neighborhood highlights
This is an 1869 editorial piece in the Savannah Morning News, “This part of the city offers a fine opportunity to give Savannah an appearance of beauty which it has never known to date. By building cottage and townhomes which will not be too large for a small family nor too small for a moderate sized one: houses that will suit the pecuniary resources of a class of people who are not by any means wealthy , but who are in point of respectability , intelligence , etc ranked with the best. At present such people have to either adopt the tenement system, or live two or three families in one house, or else take refuge in a boarding house”.

The Historic Victorian neighborhood has always been and is still today delightfully overgrown with an abundant of Wisteria, Jasmine, Azaleas, Mulberry Trees, Magnolia Trees, Fig Trees, Walnut Trees, and Great Oaks, with their gentle swaying Spanish moss. Misteltoe is everywhere, not just in the trees of the streets and beautiful gardens, but in Forsyth Park which is the centerpieces of the community and host summer concerts, book fairs, art festival and a host of community activities. The Victorian neighborhood is designated by the Secretary of the Interior as a United States National Historic Landmark, which means it is classified as a nationally significant historic places because it possess exceptional value or quality in illustrating or interpreting the heritage of the United States. The Victorian neighborhood boast of 969 original Victorian structures, many are now Inns, Bed and Breakfasts, and Executive Rentals that allow people from all over the world to actually step into and live our history, and still enjoy great restaurants and world class cultural event as illustrated by The London Times publishing that The Savannah Music Festival is the best in the world.
