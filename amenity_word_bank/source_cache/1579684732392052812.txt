The space
Tucked just below the main residence, the garden-level apartment at Abecorn Manor offers a thoughtfully designed two-bedroom retreat in the heart of Savannah’s Historic District. Located on the ground level, this home is an excellent option for guests seeking easier access, making it especially well-suited for those with mobility considerations or anyone who prefers minimal stairs.

One of our favorite features is how calm and comfortable the space feels while still being just steps from the action. The apartment enjoys great natural light for a garden level and offers a cozy, functional layout that works beautifully for couples, friends, or extended stays. With two well-appointed bedrooms and a full bathroom, the home feels both private and practical.

The apartment includes a full kitchen, perfect for preparing light meals, enjoying morning coffee, or unwinding after a day exploring Savannah. While the city’s renowned dining scene is just outside your door, having a comfortable space to dine in adds flexibility and ease—especially for longer stays.

Step outside and you’re moments from Forsyth Park, iconic tree-lined streets, neighborhood cafés, and some of Savannah’s best restaurants. The walkability of this location is hard to beat, allowing guests to experience the city at a relaxed, local pace.

The garden apartment at Abercorn Manor is ideal for guests looking for a central location, easy access, and a quieter place to recharge, all while staying immersed in the charm and beauty of historic Savannah.

The location was excellent, but we experienced several issues during our stay. We initially had trouble getting in and needed assistance. The faucet would not shut off and required repair; although we were told someone would come the next day, there was no heads-up call or text before the technician arrived. We were also provided only one roll of toilet paper for the week.
There were cleanliness concerns, including ants in the microwave, dirty vents, stained blinds, a dirty white couch, and a dusty porch with leaves and dusty furniture. The kitchen light cover was broken and taped with peeling masking tape. Morning noise was also disruptive.
We understand Lucky Savannah recently acquired this property and may be in the process of updating units, and they did honor the quoted price. However, given the current condition and recent price increase, we feel the value did not match the cost. On a positive note, the beds were clean and comfortable.
