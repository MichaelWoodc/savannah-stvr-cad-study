=== FULL DESCRIPTION ===
Savannah vacation homes remain the best way to fully immerse yourself in this Southern city that drips with as much nostalgic charm as Spanish Moss. By staying in vacation rentals Savannah GA, you get far more privacy and space than you would in a hotel, but without forgoing the amenities. We provide such a large selection of Savannah vacation rentals that you won't have any difficulty finding the one perfect for your trip.

The space
Beautiful Apartment on Monterey Square 



All of our Savannah vacation homes are in delightful locations with a number of attractions within walking distance. But there aren't many vacation rentals Savannah GA like our Under the Red Door on Monterey property. This gorgeous apartment faces the famous Monterey Square, which fans of the Midnight in the Garden of Good and Evil book and movie will immediately recognize. It offers two bedrooms, one bathroom and just over 800 square feet of space. It can accommodate up to four guests and features a number of great amenities. Our entire selection of Savannah vacation rentals offer standard amenities like wireless internet, TVs throughout, stocked necessities and the like. This one features new appliances, a courtyard, two king beds and was recently remodeled. 



The courtyard is possibly the most stunning feature of this property. It offers comfy wicker seating, lush greenery, a charming old fountain, and cobblestone and brick accents. What a beautiful place to enjoy Savannah mornings, or evenings! The main living room has a classically elegant appearance with a color scheme of black and white. Plush seating abounds, and track lighting lends ample brightness. Adjacent is the full kitchen with everything you need to make great meals. A stacked washer and dryer gives added convenience for longer stays. The bedrooms continue the black and white decor and offer exceptional space, plus extras like seating and a huge flatscreen TV. One of the bedrooms even offers covered patio access. The full bathroom is very roomy and bright and includes a spacious standing shower. 



Walk To Forsyth Park 



One of the best things about our Savannah vacation homes is the great locations that put you right in the middle of historic neighborhoods. With this property, you'll be facing Monterey Square, and you'll also only be a short distance from the Mercer-Williams House. The location gets even better when you realize that it's only one major street up from Forsyth Park. Some of the most desirable vacation rentals Savannah GA have locations near Forsyth Park. You can easily spend half the day exploring this storied part of historic Savannah. Also nearby are Calhoun Square, Chatham Square, the Massie Heritage Center and the Georgia Historical Society. You can simply step outside the door of our Savannah vacation rentals and find yourself in the middle of the Historic District and everything it has to offer. 



Getting around Savannah is easiest to do on foot with a good map. The north end of the city holds River Street, the Colonial Park Cemetery and Broughton Street, which is famous for its shopping opportunities. The south section is mostly occupied by Forsyth Park, various historic squares, and a number of museums and art galleries. If you're looking for the famous Bird Girl statue on the cover of the Midnight in the Garden of Good and Evil, you'll find it in the Jepsen Center for the Arts. 



Just Outside Savannah 



When you want even more interesting things to do near our Savannah vacation homes, venturing just outside Savannah can meet that need. Our vacation rentals Savannah GA in the Historic District are only 30 minutes from Tybee Island. The island is often known as Savannah's Beach, and it is just close enough to make an easy day trip, but also far enough away to feel like you've taken a vacation within a vacation. Water activities are a popular pastime here, but so is simply lounging on the beach. You can rent most water equipment right on the island, or you can take lessons. Charters for both fishing and dolphin watching are also available. 



More adventurous Savannah vacation rentals guests might consider taking a kayak out to Little Tybee, which is only accessible via water routes. It's uninhabited and perfect for a wilderness camping trip. For avid cyclists and hikers, the McQueen’s Island Trail is just 10 miles east of the city. It used to be part of an excursion rail line that took guests between Savannah and Tybee. The line was abandoned in 1933.
