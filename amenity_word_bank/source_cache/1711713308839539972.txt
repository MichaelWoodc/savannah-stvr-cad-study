=== FULL DESCRIPTION ===
Welcome to Salon d'Art, an art-filled historic Savannah retreat just blocks from Forsyth Park. Thoughtfully curated with bold original artwork, designer furnishings, soaring ceilings, and timeless architectural details, this elegant home blends historic charm with modern comfort. Walk to Savannah's best restaurants, coffee shops, squares, and attractions, then return to your private courtyard oasis for an evening under the moss-draped oaks.
SVR-00711

The space
Salon d'Art was designed for travelers who appreciate beautiful spaces and unforgettable experiences.
Located on historic Price Street in Savannah's Landmark Historic District, this thoughtfully restored residence combines original architectural character with a carefully curated collection of artwork and furnishings that create a true gallery-like atmosphere.
Inside you'll find:
• Professionally curated artwork throughout the home
• Spacious living areas designed for gathering and conversation
• Fully equipped kitchen for extended stays
• Beautiful historic details and high ceilings
• Fast WiFi and Smart TVs
• Premium bedding and linens
• Designer lighting and furnishings
• Private outdoor courtyard for morning coffee or evening cocktails
Whether you're visiting Savannah for a romantic getaway, a family vacation, a wedding weekend, or a long-awaited friends trip, Salon d'Art offers a memorable setting unlike a typical vacation rental.
After a day exploring the city, unwind with a glass of wine in the courtyard, admire the artwork throughout the home, or stroll through the surrounding historic neighborhood as the gas lanterns begin to glow.

Other things to note
• Self check-in for a seamless arrival experience
• Professionally managed by South Key Management Co.
• Starter supplies provided
• Fast WiFi throughout the property
• No pets permitted
• No parties or events
• Quiet hours observed in accordance with city regulations

Please be very careful not to touch the artwork. If there are any accidents, please let us know right away so we can take care of it.

Our family (four adults) had a great stay at Salon D'Art. The decor is interesting. The ground floor has a living room, dining room, kitchen, bathroom and covered patio. The second floor has two comfortable bedrooms and another bathroom. This is a late nineteenth century building with tall ceilings and 15 or 16  steps to upstairs. No problemo for 78 year old me, but not ideal for the mobility challenged. AC worked well. Parking was easy at this location and I did not have to feed the meter. The home is on the eastern perimeter of the North Historical District, walkable to Mate' Factor (a great bakery)  Veratina (an excellent Italian restaurant) and not far from Kroger grocery.
