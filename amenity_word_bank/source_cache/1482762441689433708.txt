=== FULL DESCRIPTION ===
Oglethorpe Square, 1856. Carriages still pass daily.

The Zomer Suite is the whole top floor — two bedrooms, two baths, and a living room between. Call it the penthouse; we do. 

Skip the car: River Street one way, Broughton shops the other, Michelin-worthy tables between.

Out back: an infrared sauna, a cold plunge, and the best part of your morning.

Loved like a home, because it is one.

Bosch Huis began as a dream. Guests like you make it real. Tap the heart, see my Host Profile, or book it.

The space
THE ZOMER SUITE
The whole top floor, and no one on it but you. Two bedrooms bookend a living room of your own: Sorbet on the front, a king bed under its famous pink walls with morning light over the square; Kiwi on the quiet back, a queen in one of the largest rooms in the house. Between them, an oversized queen sleeper sofa imported from Australia, a 75-inch smart TV that turns evenings into cinema, and every streaming service running.

Two baths, one per side — Sorbet's is brand-new, with a rain shower and bidet. Each bedroom keeps its own Nespresso machine, mini-fridge, microwave, and 55-inch TV, so both ends of the floor run on their own schedule. Fresh towels everywhere, already waiting.

MAIN LEVEL
One floor down, a long hallway leads to the kitchen, lined with art collected over the years. Local artists, European finds, antique market treasures. Free snacks along the way, and a photo wall of past guests. Add yourselves.

The kitchen is chef-grade: gas range, All-Clad, Vitamix, KitchenAid, a Samsung refrigerator with filtered water and ice. It's shared, but every suite has its own essentials, so barely one in a hundred guests use it. Private slots on request. Guests call it "beautiful" and "private."

OUT FRONT
All of Savannah, one minute at a time: Broughton Street's shops in one, the squares, galleries, and bars in the same, the Riverfront in five, Forsyth Park in ten.

WAYS TO BOOK

PRICING
One rate covers four. Each additional guest adds $50 per night, covering the extra cleaning and wear, plainly. That's the whole story. Pets stay for one flat fee per booking; a whole pack costs a little more. Weekdays run gentler, longer stays earn a discount. Military and teachers booking ahead: say so, and the rate says thank you.

CELEBRATIONS
Proposals, anniversaries, birthdays with a zero in them, a bachelorette claiming the whole house. Write to us before booking for anything beyond a quiet stay — larger gatherings are approved in advance through Airbnb, with their own guidelines.

THE HOUSE
This home has stood on Oglethorpe Square since 1856, and it acts its age in the best ways: heart-pine floors that announce your footsteps, stairs that have carried 170 years of guests, walls that hold stories and, occasionally, sound. On the top floor, the sound is mostly yours — you're above all of it.

THE ASSURANCES
Smoke detectors throughout. First aid stocked. Cameras outside only — never inside. The same small cleaning team, every stay. If maintenance ever touches your dates, you'll hear it from us first.

ALWAYS IMPROVING
Nearly every dollar this home generates is reinvested — into the property, our team, and the local community. We're passionate about historic houses, love Savannah wholeheartedly, and it shows in what keeps getting better.

A SAVANNAH NOTE
This is the South, in a walking city that's been alive since 1733. Church bells ring, carriages clatter, and on weekend nights downtown hums. A professional pest team treats the property regularly, but nature predates the service contract. That's the habitat, not the housekeeping.
