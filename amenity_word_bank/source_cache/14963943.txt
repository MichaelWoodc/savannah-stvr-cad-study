=== FULL DESCRIPTION ===
The convenience of a downtown hotel, this condo has the comfort and charm of a bed and breakfast.

The space
Built in 1917 and ideally located just a few minutes walk from River Street, our Lucky Lincoln Condo comfortably accommodates four guests. You’ll have the convenience of a downtown hotel with double the space! 

The home is uncluttered and modern, while retaining tons of historic charm including the original heart pine floors, crown molding, and ornamental fireplaces. The open floor plan is bright, cheerful and tastefully appointed with cozy furnishings and photos from the surrounding Historic District. The spacious living room has plenty of seating and a flat-screen TV, perfect for finally watching that movie you’ve been meaning to see. The living room opens to the dining room, which has seating for four. The kitchen has plenty of counter space, including a breakfast bar, and is fully equipped so you can enjoy a home cooked meal. 

The bedrooms are down the hall, where you’ll also find the shared renovated full bathroom with walk-in shower. The first bedroom has a queen sized bed with a writing desk and closet. The second bedroom has a King bed with a rattan woven headboard. 

This home is ideally located within a short walk to many of Savannah’s historic points-of-interest and incredible selection of dining options. Around the corner from the cutest gift shops - Salacia Salts' gift shop with Savannah-artist made gifts owned by Savannah's entrepreneur of the year (2019), Cup2Cup Coffee for a cup of joe, and Savannah's best thai restaurant (PJ's Thai). You’ll be well situated to explore Savannah’s rich history with the Owens Thomas House and the Davenport House Museum both just a block away. 

Why Lucky Savannah Loves this Property:
- Enjoy an evening stroll to Leopold’s Ice Cream, a local’s favorite and the perfect way to spend any Savannah evening.
- You’ll be just minutes from the excitement of River Street, with Savannah’s best shopping, dining and historic attractions all within walking distance. 

What is parking like for this home?
Parking for this home is on-street and metered, with enforcement by the City of Savannah Monday through Saturday, 8am to 8pm. Guests may conveniently pay using the ParkSavannah app, feed the meter or parking kiosks directly, or utilize a nearby public parking garage. Pre-paid visitor parking passes are available for purchase at the Parking and Mobility Services office. Please take note of posted Sweep Zone signs when parking on the street.
