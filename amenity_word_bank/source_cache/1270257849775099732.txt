=== FULL DESCRIPTION ===
Located on iconic Broughton St. in the heart of Savannah, The Broughton Noir blends modern elegance with historic charm. Perfect for up to four guests, it features a plush queen bed and a comfortable pull-out sofa. Recently updated, the loft boasts high-end amenities in a sleek, moody design. Immerse yourself in Savannah’s vibrant downtown, with dining, shopping, and top attractions just steps away. Experience the perfect mix of style, comfort, and convenience in this exceptional space.

The space
SVR-03048
Step into this beautifully renovated loft, where modern sophistication meets historic charm. The sleek, moody ambiance invites you to relax in style. An open-concept layout seamlessly blends the kitchen, dining, and living areas, all anchored by striking dark-washed brick walls. The kitchen boasts floor-to-ceiling cabinets, a full suite of premium appliances, and everything you need to prepare gourmet meals, brew a perfect cup of coffee, or mix a craft cocktail.
The living space offers an oversized pull-out sofa and a luxurious high-end massage chair—ideal for unwinding after exploring the vibrant streets of Savannah. Between modern slatted walls, you'll find a large smart TV, perfect for streaming your favorite shows.
In the cozy ensuite bedroom, a plush queen mattress with fresh linens and soft pillows ensures restful nights, while the dark-washed brick and tasteful décor add a calming, refined touch. The bathroom features a low-entry shower, chic vanity, and all essentials, including towels, shampoo, body wash, and a hair dryer—everything you need for a truly indulgent stay.

Neighborhood highlights
Situated on Broughton street you will be in the heart of all the southern charm Savannah has to offer. Surrounded by brick store fronts you can stroll down Broughton and simply take in all the small businesses and restaurants right in your neighborhood.
Enjoy one of the many coffee and breakfast spots such as The Coffee Fox, Kayak Kafe, The Funky Brunch Café, or Maple Street Biscuit Company.
Looking to excite your tastebuds? Check out the trendy Chive Sea Bar and Lounge serving seafood with Asian and southern touches surrounded by a 1920’s art deco feel. Grab classic southern seafood at the Savannah Seafood Shack, or dive into an incredible Italian dish at Bella Napoli Italian Bistro. Craving a wood fire pizza? A stop into 45 Bistro is an absolute must.
Ready to pour a few pints? Walk on over to the Savannah Taphouse for an upscale sports bar. Get your retro on by heading to Totally Awesome bar, featuring pool tables and a nostalgic atmosphere. Ready to be creeped out by an inordinate amount of Abe Lincoln drawings while you sip your beer? Check out Abe’s on Lincoln. It’s an experience.
A trip to Savannah is not complete without exploring a little of its historic colonial past. Fill your schedule by checking out the Telfair Academy, a beautiful art museum, or stroll through the Webb Military Museum. Relive the prohibition area by heading to the American Prohibition Museum. Don’t forget to grab a drink at their cocktail bar to celebrate freedom. Or fancy exploring Savannah’s ocean ties? Checkout the Ships of the Sea Maritime Museum.
Not enough time to walk all the sights of Savannah? Take it all in at once while riding or biking. We are just minutes away from Old Town Trolley Tours of Savannah and Savannah Bike Tours. Check out the city’s skyline from the water by jumping on one of the Savannah Belle’s ferries, with multiple stops all along Savannah’s river front.
