The space
Welcome to The Suite Peach on Broughton Street, a boho styled vacation rental waiting for you to enjoy a memorable and cozy home base during your trip! 

This condo is located on the 2nd floor of a restored 1891 building in the heart of Savannah's downtown Historic District. You'll find original features such as exposed brick walls, beautifully restored hardwood floors, and an unbeatable location that is steps away from high end shopping, Savannah's food scene and lively nightlife! 

Our fully equipped kitchen is a chef's dream, boasting stainless steel appliances and all the essentials you need to prepare delicious meals. Enjoy your morning coffee at the kitchen island, or gather around the dining table for a leisurely meal. There is a Keurig coffee pot and K-Cups provided. We stock a drip coffee pot as well, if you prefer to brew your own coffee grounds. 

The bedroom offers a queen size bed, fresh linens and lots of closet storage. The bathroom is appointed with modern fixtures and stocked with plenty of towels and starter toiletries. All bedding is provided for your use. A washer and dryer are available for your use inside of the condo.

The living area is a comfortable oasis where you can relax after a day of exploring. Sink into the sofa, watch your favorite shows on the flat-screen TV, or simply unwind with a good book. Board games and a record player are provided for extra fun! 

Don't miss the opportunity to experience Savannah in style and comfort. We can't wait for you to stroll around the Historic District in our stylish bike cruisers. Bike locks are provided, but not helmets. 

Book your stay in our historic luxury condo today and create lasting memories in this enchanting city and discover the magic of Savannah from the comfort of our luxurious condo. We look forward to hosting you and ensuring your stay is nothing short of extraordinary!

Guest access
Guests will enjoy exclusive access to the entire private condo for the duration of their stay.

Upon arrival, you'll enter through the secure main entrance of the condominium building before taking the elevator to your floor. From there, it's just a short walk down the interior hallway to your private condo entrance.

Inside, you'll have everything you need to feel at home, including a fully equipped kitchen, spacious living and dining areas, comfortable bedrooms, and a full-size washer and dryer located inside the condo for your convenience. The entire condo is yours to enjoy—there are no shared interior spaces.

Other things to note
Because the condo is located in the center of downtown, you'll experience the energy that makes Savannah so special. Restaurants, bars, and nightlife are just outside your door, particularly on Friday and Saturday evenings, so some city noise should be expected. For your comfort, we've provided a sound machine to help ensure a restful night's sleep.
