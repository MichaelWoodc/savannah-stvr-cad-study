How to read these patterns

These are the top ten supported upward associations from the existing exploratory trend ranking, not ten proven causal factors. They compare exact-size group means, weighted by parcel count. Selection required at least 30 parcels, four size levels, ten category calls and group-mean R² of 0.20. Ranking uses fitted rise per one standard deviation of size relative to the mean rate. R² describes group means, not variation explained among individual properties. Multiple comparisons are not corrected.

All ten selected series currently involve non-owner-occupied parcels. Some compare overlapping cohorts, so they are not independent findings. Approved capacity and licensed bedrooms are summed across certificates sharing a parcel. Advertised size is the maximum known listing value on that parcel. Calls belong to parcels and cannot be attributed to individual rental units or guests.

The 59% booking-occupancy adjustment is a scenario. Amenity labels are captured evidence, not proof of behavior. High recorded calls are review priorities, not established offenders or violations. Plot files contain trend lines, cohort averages and a compatible residential reference. Following your preference, plots are stored as separate files rather than embedded here.
