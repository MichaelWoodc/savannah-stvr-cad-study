=== FULL DESCRIPTION ===
This newly remodeled apartment in a 1870's building is located on Monterey Square, one of the most beautiful areas of historic Savannah. Serving to be both private and comfortable, our beautiful garden apartment has everything you need to feel at home. Located within walking distance of everything Savannah has to offer, you will find yourself falling in love with the charming culture this Southern city is known for.  (City of Savannah SVR-01649)

The space
The apartment is located on the first floor of a historically beautiful 5-story building and is lucky enough to have its own private entrance. It also has two spacious bedrooms with a queen-sized bed in one a twin bed in the other, a functional kitchen for all your cooking needs, and a comfortable living room with large windows and plenty of lighting. Wifi, cable TV, central heat and air are among the amenities.

Guest access
We use coded door locks for access that we will provide upon booking.

Other things to note
Parking is on-street and spots are easy to find at this location. 
Parking in enforced Monday through Friday, 8AM - 5PM. There is no charge for parking over the course of the weekend. 

Meters are best handled with a "Visitors Parking Day Pass" available to cover all day/night parking for travelers. Our guests who come with a vehicle may want to purchase a City of Savannah Day Pass which is $24 for 48 hours or $15 for 24 hours. 


You can get the pass from the Parking Services office at 100 East Bryan Street. (Monday - Friday, 8AM - 4:30PM) 


The nearest non-metered free public street parking starts 2 blocks south from this location. 


This is the easiest way to leave your car parked and enjoy the walking lifestyle that makes Savannah famous!

The location is quiet and peaceful as it is a solid distance away from the bars and partygoers that line the waterfront area. It is ideal for those who want to dig into the fascinating history, sublime architecture, wonderful boutiques and shops, and the delicious restaurants that Savannah has to offer.
