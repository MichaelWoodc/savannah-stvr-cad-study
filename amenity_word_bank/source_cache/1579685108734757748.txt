=== FULL DESCRIPTION ===
Perfectly positioned in Savannah’s Historic District, Abercorn Manor Main is a light-filled four-bedroom residence just steps from Forsyth Park and surrounded by some of the city’s most beloved cafés, bakeries, and restaurants.

The space
Perfectly positioned just one block from Forsyth Park in Savannah’s Historic District, Abercorn Manor Main is a thoughtfully designed, light-filled four-bedroom residence that blends comfort, style, and an unbeatable location. Surrounded by some of the city’s most beloved cafés, bakeries, and restaurants, this home offers an ideal setting for friend getaways, extended stays, or relaxed retreats.

Professionally designed by a talented local interior designer, the home is outfitted with tasteful finishes and comfortable, high-quality furnishings that create a warm, inviting atmosphere throughout. Natural light pours in through large windows, highlighting the home’s high ceilings and curated details that feel both refined and effortlessly livable.

Designed with connection and flexibility in mind, the layout offers multiple spaces to gather and unwind. The main level features a full kitchen, perfect for everything from morning coffee to shared meals, along with a spacious living and dining area that serves as the heart of the home. Upstairs, a secondary gathering room provides an additional lounge space—ideal for groups who want room to spread out, relax, or enjoy separate conversations.

With four well-appointed bedrooms and 2 newly renovated bathrooms (one walk-in shower and one shower/tub combination), the home comfortably accommodates families, couples, or small groups traveling together. Every space has been intentionally arranged to balance comfort and functionality.

Step outside and you’re instantly immersed in the charm of Savannah. Being just a block from Forsyth Park means easy morning walks, afternoon lounging under the oaks, and effortless access to everything that makes this neighborhood so special.

Abercorn Manor Main is a standout choice for guests who appreciate thoughtful design, generous living spaces, and a prime walkable location—perfect for gathering, reconnecting, and experiencing Savannah at its best.
