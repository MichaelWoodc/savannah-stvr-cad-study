=== FULL DESCRIPTION ===
Experience Savannah’s charm in this historic 6-bedroom, 4.5-bath home just 2 blocks from Forsyth Park. Perfect for large groups, it blends classic Southern elegance with modern comfort across two beautifully restored units. Enjoy spacious kitchens, peaceful koi pond views, expansive porches, and premium linens throughout. Walk to top dining, River Street, and landmarks—your ideal group getaway in the heart of the Historic District. SVR-02904 + SVR-02905

The space
This full-house rental combines two beautifully appointed units:
Lower Unit (Koi Retreat)
Enjoy bright, single-level living with three sunlit bedrooms and 2.5 modern baths. The chef’s kitchen boasts stainless steel appliances, a breakfast bar, and Keurig plus drip coffee makers. Relax in the open living/dining area with smart TV and fast Wi-Fi, or step onto the expansive porch overlooking the koi pond and lush gardens. Central A/C, heating, and an in-unit washer/dryer make longer stays effortless.
Upper Unit (Koi Escape)
Perched on the second floor, this chic 3-bedroom, 2-bath sanctuary blends historic charm with elegant modern décor. Original hardwood floors, high ceilings, and classic molding provide an inviting atmosphere. Cook in the fully equipped kitchen, unwind on the shaded porch with treetop views, and enjoy peaceful privacy with no shared walls. Premium linens, beautiful bathrooms, and full laundry amenities complete the experience.

Neighborhood highlights
The Koi Estate is perfectly located just two blocks west of Forsyth Park on Park Avenue West—placing you in one of Savannah’s most beloved and walkable neighborhoods. From charming cafés to vibrant nightlife and centuries-old landmarks, everything you want to experience is just minutes away.
Steps from Forsyth Park & Local Favorites
Enjoy a morning stroll through iconic Forsyth Park, just two blocks away.
Grab coffee at The Sentient Bean, a beloved neighborhood staple.
Savor brunch at Collins Quarter at Forsyth, known for its Australian-inspired menu and shaded patio.
Pick up incredible BBQ to-go at Saqib’s BBQ Emporium or unwind with a craft cocktail at The Black Rabbit, all just around the corner.
Venture into Savannah’s Vibrant Downtown
Sip a pint at Molly MacPherson’s Scottish Pub, a cozy local favorite.
Enjoy live music, games, and nightlife at Congress Street Social Club.
Explore City Market, Savannah’s historic open-air market filled with restaurants, shops, art, and local music.
Stroll down to the Savannah Riverwalk, lined with charming boutiques, riverfront dining, and historic cobblestone paths.
Immerse Yourself in Savannah’s Rich History
Visit the Telfair Academy, Ships of the Sea Maritime Museum, and the American Prohibition Museum—all within walking distance.
Discover the city's story with Old Town Trolley Tours or explore on two wheels with Savannah Bike Tours.
Easy Access & Transportation
Enjoy walkable streets, bike rentals, and convenient rideshare options.
Catch the Savannah Belles Ferry for a scenic riverfront cruise.
Hop on public transit just steps from the loft at the Barnard and Park stop.
Whether you're in town to relax, explore, or experience the best of Savannah's food and culture, The Koi Estate places it all at your doorstep.
