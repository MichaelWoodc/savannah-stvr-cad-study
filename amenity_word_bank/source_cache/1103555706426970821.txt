=== FULL DESCRIPTION ===
Welcome to Beauty on Barnard, a lavish corner-lot townhome mere steps from Forsyth Park in Savannah's vibrant heart. Enjoy a stunning fusion of classical elegance and modern comfort with three bedrooms, two and a half bathrooms, and luxurious amenities for up to six guests. Beautiful décor and fresh finishes elevate your stay while downtown's culinary delights, libations, and attractions await beyond your doorstep. Unwind in style at The Barnard, where luxury meets convenience.
SVR-02825

The space
Nestled within a historical gem dating back to 1880, that was once owned by one of the area's most renowned distillers this two-story townhome epitomizes timeless elegance, seamlessly blending modern luxuries with its rich heritage. As you enter the gracious foyer, the inviting living room welcomes you with brand-new sofas and reading chairs bathed in natural light pouring through oversized bay windows. Every detail exudes sophistication, from the elegant crown molding to the original hardwood flooring, creating a warm and inviting atmosphere.
Flowing effortlessly through the large, cased opening, you'll discover the gourmet chef's kitchen, adorned with a granite waterfall island, gold accents, and a commercial-grade gas stove. Fully equipped with all the essentials for culinary adventures, this kitchen becomes a hub for socializing and meal preparation, with ample prep space and barstools for four.
Continuing through another cased opening, the formal dining room awaits, exuding an air of grandeur with its moody green walls, gallery art, and a stunning chandelier. Step outside from the dining room onto the spacious screened-in patio or the private secluded patio with a grill, offering serene spaces for relaxation and alfresco dining.
Ascending the long staircase to the second floor, three luxurious suites await, each designed for ultimate comfort and tranquility. The first suite boasts a sumptuous king bed adorned with crisp linens, while antique dressers provide ample storage. Natural light floods the room, highlighting the peaceful natural tones throughout.
Discover the first full bathroom and second bedroom, where a classic cast iron standalone tub and a queen-sized bed await, accompanied by modern amenities such as a smart TV and a dedicated workspace.
Finally, the primary suite awaits, offering a truly indulgent retreat with its incredible king bed, original brick fireplace, and large bay windows overlooking the picturesque surroundings. The ensuite bathroom, adorned with impeccable gold accents and an exposed brick fireplace, promises a spa-like experience.
Every detail has been thoughtfully curated to ensure a luxurious stay, with fully equipped bathrooms with essentials for your convenience. Indulge in the epitome of luxury and charm at this historic townhome, where every moment is infused with elegance and grace.

Neighborhood highlights
Located in the historic district just steps west of Forsyth Park, the Beauty on Barnard puts you in the heart of Savannah. Surrounded by everything the hostess city of the south has to offer, you will enjoy easy access to everything that makes for a memorable stay in Savannah.
Start your mornings with a walk over to Forsyth Park, just steps away. Stroll down to the Sentient Bean for espresso, coffee, and small breakfast bites, or grab breakfast at Collins Quarter, Forsyth. Are you craving some excellent diner-style food for breakfast? Walk down to Fork & Dagger Diner to enjoy that retro diner we love.
Enjoy a variety of food types throughout the city. Just minutes from your door, stop in the Peking House for incredible Chinese food. Grab a Cubano from the Cuban Window Café, or get classy with seasonal American dishes with a southern touch at Local 11ten Food |Wine.
Ready to let loose and have a little fun? Savannah is home to some of the best pubs and nightlife around. Stop into the White Whale Craft Ales tasting room to taste some incredible local brews. Stop by The Black Rabbit, an old-school sandwich shop by day and pub by night. Are you heading north out of the neighborhood? Check out Savannah’s staple pubs like Six Pence Pub (British Inspired), O’Connell’s Irish Pub, or McDonough’s Restaurant & Lounge.
A trip to Savannah is only complete with exploring its rich colonial past. Stop through one of the many unique museums throughout the city, including the Ralph Mark Gilbert Civil Rights Museum, Beach Institute African American Cultural Center, or the fantastic Telfair Museums. Take Savannah’s vibrant art scene by stopping in the SCAD Museum of Art.
Remember to dedicate some time to exploring Savannah’s River Front! A little bit of a walk from the home but easily accessible by car and public transport, the riverfront provides an incredible Savannah experience full of small shops, cobble street roads, and endless dining options.
Not enough time to walk all the sights of Savannah? Take it all in at once while riding or biking. We are just minutes from Old Town Trolley Tours and Savannah Bike Tours. Check out the city skyline from the water by jumping on a Savannah Belle’s Ferries, with multiple stops along the Riverfront.
