The elegant home presents a beautiful front entrance, lined with large windows, spacious patios and outdoor balconies.

Each apartment is beautifully decorated and thoughtfully designed. The large windows fill the home with light. Art adorns the walls, and each apartment features a large, flat-screen smart TV. You can access all of your streaming subscriptions with a strong WiFi connection throughout the estate. The rooms are full of artfully placed lamps, tables, rugs and storage furniture for an elegant appearance and room to store all of your items. All of the apartments at the Drayton Place Main House are air-conditioned and heated, and ceiling fans allow you to adjust the temperature to your liking. The ornamental fireplaces present an elegant appearance and classic look.



Several units offer full kitchens, while others offer kitchenettes. The kitchenettes are equipped with a refrigerator/freezer, dishwasher, induction burner, microwave and toaster oven. A coffee maker is found in each unit to prepare your morning coffee. Each unit also has a stacked washer and dryer for all of your laundry needs, and you'll have an ironing board, iron and all the hangers you need. Each unit has its own full bathroom, fully stocked with linens and towels, featuring either a tiled walk-in shower or a tub/shower combo.



Step into the curtained outdoor porch with elegant red curtains and comfortable patio furniture, enjoy morning coffee on the upstairs patio overlooking Thomas Square, or bring all of your guests together around the gas grill in the back courtyard for a barbecue meal.
