=== FULL DESCRIPTION ===
Expansive 5-bedroom historic estate directly across from iconic Forsyth Park. This beautifully restored residence offers the perfect blend of Southern charm, modern comfort, and an unbeatable location in the heart of Savannah's Historic District.

The space
Experience the very best of Savannah from this expansive 5-bedroom historic estate directly across from iconic Forsyth Park. This beautifully restored residence offers the perfect blend of Southern charm, modern comfort, and an unbeatable location in the heart of Savannah's Historic District.

Ideal for family vacations, wedding groups, reunions, corporate retreats, and extended stays, this spacious home comfortably accommodates up to 10 guests with five well-appointed bedrooms, three full bathrooms, and two additional half bathrooms spread across multiple levels. Rich in historic character yet thoughtfully updated with modern conveniences, this one-of-a-kind property delivers an authentic Savannah experience just steps from the city's most beloved attractions.

What do we love about this home? 

Historic Elegance Meets Modern Comfort: Inside, guests will find bright, inviting living spaces filled with natural light, tasteful furnishings, and timeless architectural details. Whether gathering for morning coffee before exploring the city or unwinding after a day of sightseeing, the home's spacious layout provides plenty of room for everyone to relax and reconnect.

Unmatched Forsyth Park Location: Situated directly across from Forsyth Park, Savannah's most recognizable landmark, guests can enjoy immediate access to 30 acres of beautiful green space, walking paths, fountains, tennis courts, playgrounds, and the renowned Forsyth Farmers' Market. Step outside and immerse yourself in the beauty of moss-draped oak trees, historic architecture, and the vibrant energy that makes Savannah one of the South's most beloved destinations.

Perfect for Groups Visiting Savannah: Whether you're planning a wedding weekend, family celebration, girls' getaway, golf trip, or corporate retreat, this spacious historic home offers the rare combination of privacy, comfort, and walkability. The dual-residence layout allows guests to enjoy ample space while still gathering together under one roof.

After a day exploring Savannah's cobblestone streets and historic squares, return home to relax, dine, and enjoy the charm of one of the city's most desirable neighborhoods. Create lasting memories in a property that embodies everything travelers love about Savannah: historic beauty, modern amenities, and an unforgettable location overlooking Forsyth Park.
