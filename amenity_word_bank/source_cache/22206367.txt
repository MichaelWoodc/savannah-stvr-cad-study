=== ABOUT THIS SPACE ===
Classic Savannah grey brick row house in the residential mixed use portion of the Historic District. One set of exterior steps to the main front door and living area. Another set of interior steps to the two bedroom suites (each has a King bed). The master bedroom has a luxurious walk in double shower and a soaking tub. The front bedroom has its own bathroom with a walk in single stall tiled shower (no tub).

The space
The 2 bedrooms each have their own private attached bathroom, making this the ideal house for 2 couples traveling together. There is also an expansive main floor with living room, dining room, kitchen and half bath. A fantastic back porch completes the package. 

A beautiful authentic Savannah house located in the most sought after section of downtown, the true 'Historic District'. Sleeping arrangements on the upper floor consist of a master bedroom with King bed and huge luxury bathroom attached, a 2nd bedroom with King size bed and attached private bathroom. Each bedroom has its own flatscreen cable television. 

High end leather sofa is a pullout queen sofa bed in living room allows the house to sleep 6 (4 adults and two extra guests under 18 years of age). 

Modern luxury kitchen is fully equipped with plates, glasses, silverware, pans and cooking utensils. Floorplan of the downstairs is ideal for socializing, with the dining room, living room and kitchen all open to one another including a bar in the living room looking into the kitchen. 

Ideal location on beautiful Jones Street in the Historic District of Savannah. Walk to anything downtown!! Close to Forsyth Park, Kroger (grocery store), a huge selection of restaurants, boutiques, bars and all that the Historic District has to offer. 

Private back porch with fans and luxurious outdoor seating. 

Parking is free and easy on the street right outside the house.

Guest access
This is a typical downtown Savannah setup where the "main house" is above a separate "garden level apartment". You get the private living room, dining room, kitchen, two bedrooms and 2.5 bathrooms all to yourself plus your back deck. Other renters will be below you in the garden apartment but no interior space is shared. The main house entrance is totally separate from the garden apartment. You will only encounter the other guests when you look down from your back porch if they are using the patio below. You should respect that others are below you and remember not to be too loud in the living room at night. Nobody can hear whatever you do in the bedrooms though, only the living room and dining room areas have some noise transference to the ground level unit.

Other things to note
I wanted to mention that Savannah has certain bugs which we can control to some extent with poison (which every hotel and vacation rental will use on perimeters) but guests still can see them, even if they’re slower and dying from walking across poison barriers. Specifically palmetto bugs are prolific here. If guests see one we do not issue any refunds due to seeing these.

This is a traditional vacation rental home, not a “bed and breakfast”. We do not provide food or drinks but we do provide basic cooking and serving utensils, a coffee maker, and dish soap. Downtown Savannah has a major supermarket (Kroger) and a 24 hour market (Parkers on Drayton) so there is no need to pack everything from home if shopping when you get here makes more sense. 

What to bring with you:

A typical grocery run prior to arriving at your vacation home would include coffee, beer/wine, milk, eggs, butter, bread and any other basic groceries you would need for the meals you eat in the home. 

When it comes to coffee, bring your own ground coffee. The home has a 12-cup drip coffee maker.  We do not typically provide coffee but if you find coffee in the home please feel free to use it.

You should bring or buy your own napkins, laundry detergent, your own beach towels and beach gear, and your own cooler if you need one.  

What we provide:

We provide toilet paper (2 extras per bathroom), liquid hand washing dish soap, bathroom soap, sheets on each bed, and at least one bath towel for each guest. We do not permit our towels to be taken out of the house which is why you must bring your own towels if you visit the beach. 

If you find we have left seasonings, oil, dishwasher pellets, or any unopened food or drinks then of course feel free to use what is there. We occasionally leave items but just cannot promise them all the time. 

Please also note that in Savannah, sugar ants can smell cookies, spilled sugar or syrup, or an open sugar container from a mile away and will find a way in through a windowsill or door frame. It can be somewhat like a cartoon picnic where they march in a line to find the food. To avoid this try to keep your snacks contained in air tight baggies, the refrigerator, or buy single serve packets and put the trash in the outdoor trash cans as soon as possible. 

The following is standard Savannah Luxury Rentals information: We provide sheets, toilet paper and soap. If you prefer thick cushioned toilet paper bring it along but use it sparingly in our older plumbing and never flush "flushable" wipes or other products that are likely to cause a clog.
