=== FULL DESCRIPTION ===
Bell Mansion Main on Pulaski Square was originally built in 1854, and features a lovingly restored interior that blends historic charm with modern luxury appointments.

The space
Bell Mansion Main on Pulaski Square is your own private slice of heaven In Savannah’s National Historic Landmark District. Built in 1854, this impeccably restored mansion is nestled on quiet Pulaski Square. Featuring floor to ceiling windows that boast with natural light, impressive 14-foot ceilings, original moldings and wood floors, you will appreciate the lovingly restored interiors that blend historic charm with modern luxury appointments. 

The grand exterior staircase leads up to the second level entrance, which is deemed the parlor level here in the south. You'll find that the open floor plan on this level is an entertainers dream! The open gathering room and dining room create an inviting and intimate space, perfect for entertaining while enjoying mesmerizing views of Pulaski Square where you'll be transported through time. The kitchen is situated just at the back of the home, with high-end finished and appliances, such as marble countertops, a gas-range, and everything you need to prepare meals.

Six of the bedrooms are spread out amongst the top ravishing levels of the main home. The third floor has three bedrooms, with access to luxurious bathrooms, an ample amount of closet space, and tasteful furnishings. The primary suite faces charming Pulaski Square, with a plush king size bed, and a state of the art, luxurious ensuite bathroom. Also on this floor, you'll find a small sitting room with a flat screen television and a wet bar with a wine cooler - a great space to hangout! Heading up to the fourth floor four additional bedrooms can be found, where guests will enjoying soaring views of the treelined streets, each with their own ensuite bathroom.

Whether you're here for a formal event, or family reunion, Bell Mansion Main on Pulaski Square is the perfect home for your party. Start your day of exploration through the squares as you venture down Barnard Street, that leads all the way to the river front. You’ll be a short walk from the Cathedral of St. John the Baptist, Forsyth Park, and so many other well known monuments and venues in Savannah.
