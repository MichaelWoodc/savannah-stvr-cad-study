=== FULL DESCRIPTION ===
Welcome to the Grand Hall, located on Hall St. This beautiful Victorian features five bedrooms, three and a half baths, and elegant accents throughout. Set up as a split unit, the home has two kitchens and two living spaces, providing ample room to spread out. Step out back to enjoy an upper and lower deck, and your own private hot tub and pool!
SVR-02729 + SVR-02730

The space
True to the colonial past of Savannah, the grand hall incorporates Victorian architecture while providing you with all of the modern amenities you want today.
Configured as a duplex, renting the whole home accommodations provides you ample room for the larger group.
Enter the first level through the distinct yellow front doors and enjoy a flowing design from front to back. You will enter a long primary hallway on the first floor with beautiful original hardwood flooring. Directly inside, the primary bedroom features a king mattress with fresh linens and comfortable pillows. Large bay windows and a bedroom sofa provide the perfect seating area to relax, whether starting your day or unwinding at the end of the day.
Following the hall towards the back, you will pass the formal dining space with an original fireplace and dining table set for four. A cased opening with a bar top and stools provides additional seating that looks directly into the kitchen.
At the end of the hall is the first-floor galley kitchen featuring custom cabinets, granite countertops, and new appliances. The kitchen is fully appointed with everything you need to cook, including pots, pans, dishware, kitchen utensils, and small appliances.
Off the dining room, you can enjoy a lovely living space with new comfortable furniture and a smart TV ready to stream your favorite shows. A washer and dryer are hidden in cabinetry below the TV for your use.
At the end of the living room are an ensuite and a bunkroom. In the ensuite, find a new queen bed with crisp linens. Stylish décor, contemporary end tables, and a dresser provide a comfortable environment for a great night’s rest. The bunk room features a full and twin mattress, perfect for the kids or extra friends.
Both bathrooms have been updated with modern touches and custom tile work. Both bathrooms are fully equipped with all the essentials, including towels, toilet paper, shampoo, body wash and
The second floor features a fully equipped second unit with two bedrooms and one bathroom. An internal staircase and a private rear entrance provide access to the second floor. 
Enjoy a small but comfortable living space featuring an original fireplace, oversized furniture, and a smart TV, providing the perfect place to relax at the end of the day.
The living room opens directly to the kitchen featuring a unique design and is fully appointed, providing everything needed to cook, including pots, pans, and additional dishware. Large windows and a French door pour natural light into the space and open to the private veranda—a perfect spot for enjoying the Savannah breeze.
Head up another flight of stairs to find both bedrooms and the unit's bathroom. Directly at the top of the stairs is the primary bedroom featuring a new king mattress, comfortable linens, and large bay windows that fill the room with light.
To the right of the stairs, find the second bedroom featuring an angled roofline with a skylight and a queen mattress. This cozy room provides a unique and comfortable space for a great night's rest.
The bathroom has been fully updated, featuring a pedestal sink, a stand-up shower with custom green tile, and outfitted with all essentials, including towels, toilet paper, shampoo, body wash, and a hair dryer.

Neighborhood highlights
Located on Hall St, you are just minutes from everything in Savannah.
Grab excellent Cuban food down the road at café Rancho Alegre Cuban just down the road. Swing around the corner to Crystal Beer Parlor for a drink or sit down for a delicious southern dish at Mrs. Wilkes Dining Room, an authentic mom-and-pop restaurant.
Are you looking for a great outdoor vibe with beautiful old-growth trees? Stop into Collins Quarter at Forsyth for brunch and enjoy the Savannah breeze through the trees.
Head to the City Market and River Walk and enjoy additional southern dining at The Lady and Sons. Grab a traditional dinner breakfast at Treylor Park. Get a little fancier with a delicious meal at St. Neo’s Brasserie, and don’t forget to check out their basement speakeasy-inspired bar.
A trip to Savannah is only complete with exploring a little of its historical colonial past. Fill your schedule by checking out the Telfair Academy, a beautiful art museum, or stroll through the Webb Military Museum. Relive the prohibition area by heading to the American Prohibition Museum. Don’t forget to grab a drink at their cocktail bar to celebrate freedom. Or fancy exploring Savannah’s ocean ties? Check out the Ships of the Sea Maritime Museum.
Not enough time to walk all the sights of Savannah? Take it all in at once while riding or biking. We are just minutes from Old Town Trolley Tours of Savannah and Savannah Bike Tours. Check out the city’s skyline from the water by jumping on one of the Savannah Belle’s ferries, with multiple stops all along Savannah’s riverfront.
Getting around is easy with walkable streets, bike rentals, and Uber service. Jump on Savannah’s public transit system with the MLK & Gaston NB stop just a 1-minute walk from the house.
