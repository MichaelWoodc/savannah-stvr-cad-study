=== FULL DESCRIPTION ===
This sophisticated 2 bedroom condo has a style all its own! Nestled in a row home in the Victorian District, you're just steps away from famous Forsyth Park! And can't miss the unbelievable mural from Savannah artist Kipper Millsap! Makes for the perfect backdrop for those picture perfect moments! Combined with the comfy, king beds, private courtyard with grill, and well equipped kitchen...you'll adore exploring the city from & winding down in this home base after all your adventures!

SVR-03148

The space
Our beautifully decorated two bedroom, one bathroom condo is located on the first floor of our Victorian duplex. It's just steps away from Forsyth Park, and on Saturdays, the farmer's market! Each bedroom has a comfy king sized bed to lay your head and a tv to help relax at the end of a long day of sightseeing. The kitchen is fully stocked, so whether you're grabbing a quick cup of coffee to get your day started or staying in and cooking you'll have everything you need!

Guest access
The entire first floor condo is yours! Entry to both the condo and the building are keyless. You'll be provided with a code the day of your arrival so you can come and go as you please.

Other things to note
Street parking is free and easy and right outside your door, just be aware of the one night a week sweep zone!
