=== FULL DESCRIPTION ===
Located in a historic building on Broughton Street, one of the most iconic streets in Savannah, this condo offers the perfect blend of modern amenities and Southern charm. With two bedrooms, it's the perfect size for a family or group of friends traveling together. The living space is spacious and inviting, with plenty of room to relax and unwind after a day of exploring the city. Book your stay now and get ready to fall in love with the magic of this vibrant city.

The space
The fully equipped kitchen is perfect for preparing meals at home, and the dining area is the perfect spot to enjoy them. But with so many renowned restaurants and cafes just steps away, you may want to indulge in the local cuisine instead.

Step outside and immerse yourself in the charm of Savannah. Enjoy the stunning architecture, unique boutiques, and local art galleries that line the streets. From historic landmarks to trendy hotspots, there's something for everyone here.

Make this 2 bedroom condo your home base during your time in Savannah and experience the perfect blend of modern comfort and Southern charm.
