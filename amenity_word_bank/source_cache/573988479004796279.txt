=== FULL DESCRIPTION ===
With its stunning architecture, interesting past, and rich culture, Savannah is a great spot for a vacation. If you plan on staying in town for a bit, check out Thrive on Perry. This beautiful vacation home is the perfect space to stay in while you explore the city.

The space
Enjoy a Cozy Getaway at Thrive on Perry



Thrive on Perry is one of our most charming Savannah vacation homes for couples and singles. The roomy apartment is situated inside a lovely brick building. When you walk in, you immediately step into a welcoming living space. Wood flooring, extra-high ceilings, and two sets of French doors make the area look big and spacious. Most of this home is an open floor plan, but a few doors lead to the full-sized kitchen and bathroom.



For all our vacation rentals Savannah GA, we thoughtfully decorate with designer-inspired pieces. The color palette for Thrive on Perry is a warm gray with crisp white and cheery yellow accents. Traditional-style furniture and a few tasteful pieces of art make the space feel elegant and stylish. Natural wood and woven textures add some softness and warmth, so the entire apartment feels as relaxing and welcoming as possible.



When you want a private retreat, Thrive on Perry is a great option. This home is just the right size for two people. You can explore Savannah on your own or bring a friend or loved one with you. The sleeping space has a queen size bed with two nightstands and reading lamps. When you aren't sleeping, you can enjoy a meal together at the high-top dining table or relax on the extra-wide loveseat in the living room.



Get All the Comforts of Home on Your Vacation



One of the great things about our vacation rentals Savannah GA is that they provide just about everything you would have at home. Instead of sitting in a bland, sterile hotel, you can enjoy the town while living in an actual house. For example, the full-sized bathroom makes it easy to enjoy your daily grooming rituals. You can relax in the cast-iron bathtub or take a quick shower.



The fully-equipped kitchen makes it easy to enjoy meals at home instead of having to eat out every time you want food. Our kitchen has a stove, oven, refrigerator, and microwave, so you can make an easy breakfast or create an elaborate dinner. To make it easier to cook, we provide essentials like pans, utensils, and dishes. The kitchen even has a toaster, Keurig, and other smaller appliances.



To help you enjoy your stay, we offer plenty of technological amenities as well. Thrive on Perry has free Wifi along with cable and satellite television. You can get some work done on a laptop or catch up on your favorite show. 



Relax With All Sorts of Luxurious Amenities



Our Savannah vacation rentals are stocked with all sorts of fantastic amenities to make your experience extra special. We start by offering you the option of self check-in with a keypad lock, so you can start your vacation at whatever time is convenient. The entire apartment has central heating and air, so you can stay comfortable regardless of outdoor weather.



To help you stay comfortable, we offer plenty of fresh linens, towels, and pillows. In the bathroom, you can find soap and shampoo. Thrive on Perry also has a closet for all your clothes along with hangers. Other helpful amenities we provide include a hairdryer, iron, and ironing board.



If you're considering this vacation home, don't forget about our outdoor amenities. Thrive on Perry comes with two adorable balconies where you can view the charms of city life. All our guests also have free access to a resort-style swimming pool. Situated on lovely Tybee Island, this pool is heated in the winter and surrounded by cozy lounge chairs and pavilions.



Explore Savannah's Attractions From a Conveniently Central Location



Thrive on Perry is situated in a quiet neighborhood within the charming Northern Historic District of Savannah. Like many other historic Savannah homes, it is next to a peaceful town square. In Crawford Square, you can explore a romantic setting with a pergola and stunning live oak trees. In addition to providing you with access to nature, the neighborhood around Thrive on Perry also contains a few charming shops and restaurants. You can grab a bite at Ta Ca Asian Fusion or get a souvenir from the Melted Grape candle store.



The location of our Savannah vacation homes makes it easy to see all the other wonders of Savannah. It is close to the waterfront, so you can visit popular spots like The Olde Pink House, Little Duck Diner, or Leopold's Ice Cream. This part of town also includes a lot of fascinating history, like the Maritime Museum, and interesting culture like the SCAD Museum of Art. You can also hop on a free trolley to visit the Victorian District and see more incredible architecture. Depending on the time of year you stay at our Savannah vacation rentals, you can catch a concert, shop at the farmer's market, or participate in a holiday parade.
