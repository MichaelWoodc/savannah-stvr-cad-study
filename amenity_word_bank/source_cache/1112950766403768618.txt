=== FULL DESCRIPTION ===
Spacious new-construction 2BR/2BA duplex in Savannah's walkable Victorian District — minutes to Forsyth Park, SCAD, and local dining. Enjoy soaring ceilings, a private balcony with outdoor seating, in-unit W/D, and a bright upper-level layout with all utilities and WiFi included. Free street parking available. Short walk to Strangebird, The Black Rabbit & White Whale Craft Ales. Available for short or mid-term stays.  Your Savannah home base awaits.

The space
The upper level of this new-construction duplex feels like your own private floor — soaring ceilings, luxury vinyl floors, and a bright open layout throughout.
Both bedrooms feature king beds with memory foam mattresses that guests consistently rave about, and generous closet space for longer stays.

The kitchen is fully stocked with cookware, appliances, and both drip and K-cup coffee — everything you need to settle in and cook like you're home.

A full-size dedicated desk with high-speed WiFi makes it easy to work remotely or extend your stay into a true mid-term retreat.

Smart TV in the living room and primary bedroom, fresh linens provided, in-unit washer/dryer, and all utilities included.

Step outside to your private balcony with outdoor seating — the perfect spot to unwind with a drink after a day exploring the city.

Guest access
Guests will have full access to the unit, with exception of one locked owners closest which is used for storage.

Other things to note
Units are set up with wifi electronic locks for easy self check-in.  There is one street facing camara on the exterior for guest and host safety.
