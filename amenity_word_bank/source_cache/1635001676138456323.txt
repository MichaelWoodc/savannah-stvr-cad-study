=== FULL DESCRIPTION ===
Stay in a beautifully preserved Victorian-style home in Savannah’s walkable East Historic District, offering four bedrooms, three full baths, two living rooms, and two full kitchens when both residences are reserved together. With four queen beds, two twin beds, and a charming shared courtyard, it’s ideal for reunions, wedding weekends, and group getaways near Forsyth Park.

The space
The Artist Retreat Estate: A Historic Home Designed for Gathering

Welcome to The Artist Retreat Estate, a beautifully preserved 1800s Victorian home located in Savannah’s charming East Historic District. When both residences within the home are reserved together, the estate offers a rare opportunity to experience an entire historic property designed for connection, comfort, and inspiration for up to 8 guests.



Savannah has long been a city shaped by artistry. From its iconic squares to the lasting influence of Savannah College of Art and Design, creativity is woven into daily life here. This historic home reflects that spirit, blending original architectural character with refined artistic design and thoughtful modern updates.



Spacious Living & Gathering Areas

Across both residences, guests will enjoy two living rooms designed for comfort and conversation. Tall windows allow natural light to fill the spaces, highlighting classic details and curated furnishings that feel both timeless and welcoming.



Spacious dining areas provide generous seating for group meals, celebrations, or casual evenings in. Whether you are preparing brunch before a day of exploring or hosting a shared dinner at home, the fully equipped kitchens make it easy to cook and gather together.



Four Bedrooms & Flexible Layout

With four bedrooms and two additional sleeping spaces (each featuring a twin bed) across both residences, the estate is ideal for family reunions, wedding weekends, or even milestone celebrations.



Charming Courtyard Setting

Tucked behind the home, a private courtyard offers a peaceful outdoor escape in the heart of downtown. Surrounded by greenery and furnished with comfortable seating and dining space, it is perfect for morning coffee, afternoon relaxation, or evening conversations beneath the Savannah sky.



Walkable East Historic District Location

Located in Savannah’s desirable East Historic District, the estate places you within easy walking distance of the city’s most beloved attractions. Forsyth Park is just about a ten-minute stroll away, offering expansive green space and the city’s iconic fountain. Nearby squares such as Whitfield Square provide additional quiet charm, while restaurants, coffee shops, boutiques, and historic landmarks are just steps from your door. Free street parking is available, allowing you to park and explore the city at your own pace.



Modern Amenities for a Seamless Stay

Each residence includes keyless entry for secure self check-in, smart TVs, fully equipped kitchens, and in-unit washer and dryer access. When reserved together, The Artist Retreat Estate offers the rare combination of historic character, generous space, and modern convenience in one cohesive downtown property.



From its preserved Victorian architecture to its inviting interiors and unbeatable location, The Artist Retreat Estate provides a distinctive and memorable setting for your Savannah getaway.
