=== FULL DESCRIPTION ===
Welcome to Park Place, a beautifully restored 1800s one-bedroom, one-bathroom  pet-friendly condo in the heart of downtown Savannah. This second-floor condo is perfect for four guests ready to explore, featuring beautiful original hardwood floors, a comfortable king mattress, a pull-out sofa, and a private balcony. Just a five-minute walk to Forsyth Park and surrounded by incredible dining, attractions, and adventures.

SVR-02424

The space
Originally built in 1880, Park Place has been freshly remodeled to include all the modern amenities you desire while preserving the historic colonial feel that attracts many to downtown Savannah.
Enter the home through the second-floor entrance and enter a spacious open concept connecting the living room, kitchen, and dining spaces. In the living room, find a comfortable pull-out sofa, sitting chairs, and a smart TV ready to stream your favorite shows.
Directly off the living room, find the fully appointed kitchen with a full appliances package, pots, pans, cooking utensils, small appliances, and dishware. A peninsula island provides two bar-height stools, and a small dining table offers two bar stools.
Open the back door to your private balcony with comfortable patio furniture—an excellent place to enjoy your morning coffee or watch the sunset on beautiful Savannah.
Original pocket doors separate the condo’s primary bedroom and includes the single bathroom. The bedroom features a king bed wrapped in crisp linens. The room lives large and comfortable, with multiple large windows filling the room with abundant natural light. A second smart TV lets you enjoy shows from the comfort of your bed, and a dedicated workspace makes working remotely on your trip a breeze.
The bathroom features a shower/tub combination and a single vanity. The bathroom is outfitted with all essentials, including towels, toilet paper, shampoo, and body wash.

Guest access
Access to a private deck can be found just off the kitchen area. The front deck looks over Lincoln Street, allowing visitors to take in some fresh air in the morning. The deck also has a small area that seats two and is conveniently situated next to a large tree, which provides plenty of shade on those warmer days. 
 
The King bedroom can be separated from the rest of the apartment with original pocket doors, and hosts a tiled bathroom with a tub/shower combo. The room is also perfect for those working from home. It comes with a full corner desk as well as solid 100mbs WiFi-connection.

Other things to note
Only one (1) dog permitted with a one-time $100 fee. Must be less than 50 pounds and be able to provide proof of flea/tick treatment. Fee must be paid before check-in. 
This is part of a 4 unit condo, the main building entry is shared with the other 3 units and has long-term residents, so please be courteous and cognizant of your noise-levels!

Neighborhood highlights
Located in the Victorian District East on the corner of East Park Ave and Habersham St, the Park Place puts you in the heart of Savannah. Surrounded by everything the hostess city of the south has to offer, you will enjoy easy access to everything that makes for a memorable stay in Savannah.
Start your mornings with a walk over to Forsyth Park, just five minutes, and stop through The Sentient Bean for espresso, coffee, and small breakfast bites. Are you craving some excellent diner food for breakfast? Walk down to Fork & Dagger Diner to enjoy that retro diner we love.
Enjoy a variety of food types throughout the city. Just minutes from your door, stop in the Peking House for incredible Chinese food. Grab a Cubano from the Cuban Window Café, or get classy with seasonal American dishes with a southern touch at Local 11ten Food |Wine.
Ready to let loose and have a little fun? Savannah is home to some of the best pubs and nightlife around. Stop into the White Whale Craft Ales tasting room to taste some incredible local brews. Stop by The Black Rabbit, an old-school sandwich shop by day and pub by night. Are you heading north out of the neighborhood? Check out Savannah’s staple pubs like Six Pence Pub (British Inspired), O’Connell’s Irish Pub, or McDonough’s Restaurant & Lounge.
A trip to Savannah is only complete with exploring its rich colonial past. Stop through one of the many unique museums throughout the city, including the Ralph Mark Gilbert Civil Rights Museum, Beach Institute African American Cultural Center, or the fantastic Telfair Museums. Take Savannah’s vibrant art scene by stopping in the SCAD Museum of Art.
Remember to dedicate some time to exploring Savannah’s River Front! A little bit of a walk from the home but easily accessible by car and public transport, the riverfront provides an incredible Savannah experience full of small shops, cobble street roads, and endless dining options.
Not enough time to walk all the sights of Savannah? Take it all in at once while riding or biking. We are just minutes from Old Town Trolley Tours and Savannah Bike Tours. Check out the city skyline from the water by jumping on a Savannah Belle’s Ferries, with multiple stops along the Riverfront.
