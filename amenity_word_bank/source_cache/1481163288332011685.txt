=== FULL DESCRIPTION ===
This beautiful Victorian home, built in 1910, is located in the heart of Savannah’s historic Thomas Square District. It showcases timeless charm with original hardwood floors, high ceilings, and classic architectural details. The home features one cozy bedroom with a queen bed and a full bathroom with a tub and shower. The living area includes a queen-size sofa sleeper and a streaming TV for your entertainment. One dedicated parking space is provided, making your stay even more convenient.

The space
This one-bedroom, one-bathroom unit features a spacious living area with a queen sleeper sofa, a fully equipped kitchen, a comfortable dining area, and a private back deck—perfect for relaxing after a day exploring the city. A dedicated driveway parking space is included for your convenience.

Thomas Square Affair is ideally located near restaurants, the city center, and scenic spots. Guests love this space for its high ceilings, welcoming ambiance, and unbeatable location—just a short walk to Savannah’s free DOT shuttle system.

Guest access
During your stay, you’ll have access to all the amenities our home offers, including WiFi, laundry facilities, a TV with online streaming, complimentary towels, and bathroom essentials such as soap, shampoo, and makeup wipes. To get you started, we provide three rolls of toilet paper per bathroom and one roll of paper towels. For longer stays or frequent cooking, you may wish to bring or purchase additional supplies.

This unit is located on the lower left side of the property. The upstairs unit—a 2-bedroom, 1-bath rental—is also available and listed as Thomas Square Affair Unit B. Please park in the driveway on the left side of the house (1 parking space provided).

Other things to note
Please be considerate of the neighborhood and our neighbors. City of Savannah Noise Ordinance for vacation rentals is between the hours of 9pm- 8am. Please remember that sound carries very well throughout Savannah, most especially in courtyards, porches, patios, and verandas.

If you have questions you need answered before securing your booking, or you simply want to talk with us first, please click my profile picture at the top of this listing and then click the "contact host" button to send an email directly to us without submitting a reservation request.

Our home is in compliance with all Short-Term Vacation Rental laws in Savannah, Georgia. Our STVR permit number is SVR-02799
