=== FULL DESCRIPTION ===
Bring everyone together at Broughton Street House Estate, a beautifully restored Historic District retreat featuring both a spacious main house and private carriage house. Sleeping up to 10 guests, this unique property offers a private courtyard, garage parking, fully equipped kitchens, and complimentary access to Southern Belle's heated pool on Tybee Island. Walk to River Street, local dining, shopping, and Savannah's iconic historic squares.

The space
Welcome to Broughton Street House Estate

Experience the timeless charm of Savannah from Broughton Street House Estate, a beautifully restored Historic District retreat that brings together a spacious main residence and a private carriage house to create one unforgettable getaway. Designed for families, friends, wedding parties, and group vacations, this unique property comfortably accommodates up to 10 guests while offering the privacy and flexibility of two separate living spaces. Located on the eastern end of Broughton Street just a few blocks from River Street, you'll be perfectly positioned to experience everything that makes Savannah one of the South's most beloved destinations.



Spend your days strolling beneath moss-draped oaks, exploring historic squares, shopping local boutiques, dining at award-winning restaurants, or discovering museums and galleries—all within easy walking distance of your front door. Whether you're planning a long weekend, celebrating a milestone, or gathering loved ones for a memorable vacation, Broughton Street House Estate offers the perfect blend of historic character, modern comfort, and an unbeatable location. As an added bonus, guests also enjoy complimentary access to Southern Belle's heated resort-style swimming pool at 19 Silver Avenue on Tybee Island, giving you the opportunity to experience both downtown Savannah and the coast during your stay.



A Historic Home with Room for Everyone

One of the things that makes Broughton Street House Estate so special is the space it provides for larger groups. Your reservation includes exclusive access to both the beautifully restored main house and the private carriage house, allowing everyone to stay together while still enjoying plenty of room to spread out. The main residence welcomes you with inviting gathering spaces filled with natural light and timeless architectural details that reflect the beauty of historic Savannah. Comfortable living areas provide the perfect place to relax after a day of sightseeing, while elegant touches throughout the home create a warm and welcoming atmosphere.



The fully equipped kitchen includes full-size appliances, cookware, dishes, and everything needed to prepare meals throughout your stay. Whether you're making a quick breakfast before exploring downtown or sitting down together for dinner after a busy day, you'll find everything you need. The adjoining dining room creates a wonderful setting for shared meals, celebrations, board games, or simply spending quality time together.

Sleeping accommodations have been thoughtfully arranged for comfort and convenience. The main house features two king bedrooms, one queen bedroom, two full bathrooms, one half bath, and a sleeper sofa for additional sleeping space.



Just across the private courtyard, the carriage house offers another complete living space with a king bedroom, leather sleeper sofa, full bathroom, fully equipped kitchen, and dining area for four. This separate residence is ideal for grandparents, another family, or anyone who appreciates a little extra privacy while remaining just steps away from the rest of the group. Historic charm can be found throughout both homes, from beautiful hardwood floors and decorative fireplaces to elegant lighting, classic architectural details, and thoughtfully selected furnishings. At the same time, modern conveniences ensure a comfortable stay, including complimentary Wi-Fi, multiple televisions, central heating and air conditioning, and an in-home washer and dryer.



Relax, Gather & Enjoy Your Stay

Between adventures around Savannah, Broughton Street House Estate offers plenty of places to slow down and simply enjoy being together. The private courtyard serves as the heart of the property, creating a peaceful outdoor space where guests can sip morning coffee, enjoy lunch in the fresh air, or unwind with evening drinks after exploring the city. Surrounded by the main residence and carriage house, the courtyard offers a quiet retreat while remaining just steps from Savannah's vibrant Historic District. Parking is another convenience that sets this property apart. Two private garage parking spaces are included with your stay, allowing you to leave your vehicles behind and explore much of Savannah's Historic District on foot. The garage is accessed from the rear alleyway, and because the entrance requires a fairly tight turn, mid-size cars and smaller SUVs are recommended for the easiest access.



Discover the Best of Savannah

One of the greatest advantages of staying at Broughton Street House Estate is its exceptional location. Situated in Savannah's Historic District, the property places you within walking distance of many of the city's most iconic attractions. Start your day with coffee from a neighborhood café before browsing the locally owned boutiques and specialty shops along Broughton Street. Just a few blocks away, River Street offers historic cobblestone walkways, waterfront restaurants, candy shops, live entertainment, and scenic views of the Savannah River.



Take time to explore Savannah's twenty-two historic squares, visit the iconic Forsyth Park, or discover nearby museums, galleries, and beautifully preserved historic landmarks. From Southern favorites and fresh seafood to rooftop dining, bakeries, coffee shops, and craft cocktail bars, you'll find no shortage of incredible local dining options nearby. Please note that because Broughton Street House Estate is located on historic Broughton Street, guests may occasionally experience the sounds and activity that naturally come with staying in the heart of a lively downtown neighborhood, a location many visitors consider part of the authentic Savannah experience.



Your Historic Savannah Getaway Awaits

Whether you're gathering with family for the holidays, celebrating a birthday or anniversary, attending a wedding, or planning a weekend with friends, Broughton Street House Estate provides the perfect setting for making lasting memories. With accommodations for up to 10 guests, a beautifully restored main home, a private carriage house, a peaceful courtyard, two garage parking spaces, heated pool access, and an exceptional location in Savannah's Historic District, this one-of-a-kind property offers everything you need for a comfortable and unforgettable stay.
