Experience modern comfort and contemporary charm in this bright 2BR, 2BA condo in Savannah, perfectly located in the vibrant Thomas Square neighborhood. Perfect for weekend escapes, business trips, or extended stays, this stylish home offers a true local feel with exceptional walkability.
