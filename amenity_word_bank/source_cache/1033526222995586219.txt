Stay in the popular neighborhood of Starland while exploring all of historic Savannah. This location is the best of both tourism & localism. 

Our house is a 20-min (2 mile) walk or FREE trolley 🚃 ride down to Forsyth Park, Downtown, the riverfront, and other tourist sites. And it is a quick walk to coffee, food, shopping like:

• 1 Bathroom
• Bedroom 1 with queen bed

• Bedroom 3/sunroom with 2 full size beds
• 1 Balcony

Guest access
Guests have access to the entire first floor in this historic colonial duplex. 

The only shared space is the landing when entering from the main entrance, the driveway (long driveway, tandem parking, first come first serve unless otherwise requested).

There is also ample free street parking in front of the house.

✖︎ No shoes indoors

✖︎ No pets

Please do not book if you plan on doing any of these.
