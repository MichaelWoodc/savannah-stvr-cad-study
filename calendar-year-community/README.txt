Local exploratory copy of the Savannah study. Open index.html. Calendar-year rates throughout; no booking-occupancy adjustment. All call categories are included initially. An optional exploratory category selection was chosen after inspecting the same data: retain a category when either the 6+ or 8+ advertised-guest non-owner STVR group (up to three permits per parcel) had a higher citizen-initiated residential-neighbor call rate than nonlicensed residential parcel neighborhoods. This selection is exploratory and does not establish causation; groups can overlap and small counts can be unstable. See category_selection.csv for all rates. The map permits choosing all categories. Owner/non-owner status comes from permit types; mixed permits may match both. Up to three permits is an imperfect size proxy. Source: Savannah STVR study, commit 5a4f940. Data period 2024-09-21 to 2026-10-16.
