=== FULL DESCRIPTION ===
Stay in the heart of the Historic District but with the modern amenities you enjoy on vacation! Up to nine Lucky guests will call this modern second-level loft "home".

The space
Located in the heart of historic downtown Savannah, Southern Market is your updated, spacious alternative to the typical historic home. Guests will enjoy walking just a few steps to Savannah's best attractions, dining, and shopping!

Enter off Montgomery and up the full flight of stairs to your 2nd floor modern loft amongst the oak trees. There's plenty of comfortably seating for everyone to catch up and unwind after a long day of exploring. Just off of the living room, you'll find a spacious kitchen with every appliance needed for cooking a full meal. Choose to dine at the breakfast nook, or enjoy a meal al fresco on the private balcony overlooking downtown.

All three bedrooms are also on this level, with the first being the primary with King size bed. Enjoy large closets, flat screen TVs, and en suite baths in every room. The primary bedroom is especially spacious and boasts beautiful views of downtown. The full bath is en suite with Jack and Jill doors to the hall. A daybed is also available in the main hall to allow guests more space.

The second bedroom has a Queen size bed. The en suite bath has a tub/shower combination. Down the hall you'll find the final bedroom with a twin and trundle bed. This room is loft-like as a short stairwell leads guests to the laundry room. The twin and trundle bed is connected to a full bath with a tub/shower combination. 

Southern Market is right on Montgomery Street, which leads North to city attractions such as Ellis Square at City Market, Broughton Street, and of course River Street. Some of the city's most sought-after restaurants and bars are only a short walking distance away.

Parking: Metered Parking
Parking for this home is on-street and metered, with enforcement by the City of Savannah Monday through Saturday, 8am to 5pm. Guests may conveniently pay using the ParkSavannah app, feed the meter or parking kiosks directly, or utilize a nearby public parking garage. Pre-paid visitor parking passes are available for purchase at the Parking and Mobility Services office. Please take note of posted Sweep Zone signs when parking on the street.
