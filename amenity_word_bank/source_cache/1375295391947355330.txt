=== FULL DESCRIPTION ===
Modern + cozy ground-floor condo located in the heart of Savannah’s Historic District. Perfect for guests with mobility concerns, this charming unit provides the ultimate blend of comfort, convenience, and Southern charm!

The space
Discover The Heidiway Hip Pad, a modern and cozy ground-floor apartment ideally located in the heart of Savannah’s Historic District. Perfect for guests with mobility concerns or anyone who prefers to avoid stairs, this charming unit provides the ultimate blend of comfort, convenience, and Southern charm. Whether you're in town for a quick getaway or an extended stay, you’ll love the unbeatable access to Savannah’s most famous attractions, dining, and entertainment.

What Makes The Heidiway Hip Pad Special?

- Unbeatable Walkable Location: Located just steps away from trolley tour stops, the DOT free shuttle bus, and some of the best dining and historic landmarks in Savannah, The Heidiway Hip Pad is your perfect home base. With everything from art galleries to scenic squares just minutes away, you'll be able to explore the city on foot, making it an ideal choice for tourists visiting the Savannah Historic District.

- Comfortable and Modern Furnishings: Thoughtfully designed for travelers, this unit is equipped with everything you need for a comfortable stay. Relax in stylish, comfortable furnishings, make yourself at home with plenty of storage for your belongings, and enjoy the fully stocked kitchen—complete with all the tools and equipment needed for a home-cooked meal. Whether you’re here for a weekend or a week, this space offers everything you need for a memorable stay in Savannah.

- Charming Private Patio: Start or end your day on the peaceful patio at The Heidiway Hip Pad. Enjoy the refreshing Savannah breeze while sipping coffee in the morning or a cocktail in the evening. The private outdoor space is perfect for relaxing with family, friends, or even your furry companion, making it an ideal spot to unwind after a day of exploring the Historic District.

This property features two spacious bedrooms, each with a luxurious king-size bed—no need to fight over who gets the bigger bed! Perfect for couples, small families, or solo travelers, both rooms offer plenty of space to relax after a day of sightseeing. The updated bathroom is located at the back of the apartment, ensuring privacy, and includes a modern vanity and a spacious tub-shower combination to help you unwind.

Sleeping configuration:
Bedroom 1: King Bed
Bedroom 2: King Bed

What is parking like for this home?
Guests have two designated parking spaces directly behind the home. Guests will be able to fit two full-size (sedans, mid-size SUV's, and small trucks) vehicles in tandem.

We can welcome your dog companions at this pet friendly home, but ask that you find other arrangements for cats, birds, and other furry friends. There is a $130 pet fee (non-refundable) with a 35 lb. maximum weight limit per dog and a limit of 2 dogs per reservation. Some homes may allow larger dogs, please inquire for more information.
