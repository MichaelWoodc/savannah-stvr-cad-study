=== FULL DESCRIPTION ===
Host the entire family in this gorgeous three-story town home with towering ceilings and off-street parking just a short walk to Forsyth Park.

The space
A welcome addition to Savannah’s sought-after Victorian District, Howard Grande Main + Garden is a multilevel, three-bedroom townhome just a stone’s throw from the iconic Forsyth Park Fountain—one of the most recognizable landmarks in historic downtown Savannah. This walkable Savannah vacation rental places you steps from the park and minutes from the heart of the Historic District.

Park your car in the private garage and step up into the contemporary living quarters of your dreams. The first level hosts a private garden apartment for two—complete with a full bath and separate street-level entry, ideal for added privacy or multi-generational travel.

On the second level, you enter the two-story main home. With soaring ceilings and an open floor plan, this Savannah vacation rental is designed for effortless entertaining, family gatherings, and weekend getaways with friends. Take advantage of the granite countertops and stainless steel appliances to prepare a full meal, or step outside onto the cobblestone streets and enjoy a short, walkable distance to some of Savannah’s most renowned restaurants, cafés, and local favorites.

The upstairs hosts two bedrooms, each with en suite baths for comfort and convenience. The primary bedroom features a King size bed, while the guest room offers a Queen. A dedicated laundry room with a stacking washer and dryer in the main home makes extended stays easy and comfortable.

The garden level makes for the perfect in-law suite or private guest space, complete with a Queen size bed, kitchenette, and en suite bath. The main level and garden level do not connect internally, making this layout ideal for guests who appreciate additional privacy while traveling together.

Step outside and walk directly to Forsyth Park, where oak-lined paths, open green space, tennis courts, and the popular Saturday farmers market create a true Savannah experience right at your doorstep. This prime Victorian District location offers easy, walkable access to Savannah’s historic squares, boutique shopping, and top dining destinations. Continue north through the Historic District to River Street, known for its waterfront views, restaurants, and lively atmosphere.

Whether you're a family looking for a quiet retreat near Forsyth Park, or a pair of couples wanting to experience Savannah’s walkable charm, local culture, and dining scene, Howard Grande is the perfect home base for your stay.

What is parking like for this home? Guests have access to one parking space in the attached private garage. Additional parking in the area can be found on-street and non-metered (free!)

There is a $130 pet fee (non-refundable) with a 35 lb. maximum weight limit per dog and a limit of 2 dogs per reservation. Some homes may allow larger dogs, please inquire for more information.
