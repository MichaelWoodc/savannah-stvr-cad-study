=== FULL DESCRIPTION ===
This 3-bedroom, 2.5-bathroom home is perfect for a family vacation or a couples' getaway. Step inside and be welcomed by the beautiful hardwood floors, high ceilings, and elegant decor that will make you feel right at home. The living area is spacious and features comfortable seating, perfect for relaxing after a day of exploring the city.

The space
The kitchen is fully equipped with everything you need to whip up a delicious meal, including stainless steel appliances and granite countertops. Enjoy your meals at the dining table or step outside and dine al fresco on the charming patio.

Upstairs, you'll find three cozy bedrooms, each with its own unique charm. The master bedroom boasts a luxurious king-size bed, an en-suite bathroom, and a private balcony. The two other bedrooms feature queen-size beds and share a spacious bathroom.

Take advantage of the home's prime location and walk to some of Savannah's best restaurants, attractions, and nightlife. In the evenings, relax on the front porch and take in the sights and sounds of this vibrant city. Book now for an unforgettable stay in the heart of Savannah!

STVR Certificate: SVR-01906

Other things to note
This home is part of an estate which includes a garden suite below and carriage house behind. All potential guests must be aware of this and be willing to respect other guests on the property. Adhering to the house rules includes taking off shoes, respecting the city noise ordinance, etc.
