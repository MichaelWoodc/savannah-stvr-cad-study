=== FULL DESCRIPTION ===
Posh and memorable, Drayton Escape represents metropolitan living in the heart of Savannah. This is a sophisticated urban residence that pulls out all the stops for glamorous Southern living. It can comfortably accommodate up to four overnight guests within its 1,044 square feet of space. Guests enjoy a large bedroom and 1.5 bathrooms. This home is also perfect for a single occupant or couple. The unit can be reached by elevator.

The space
Drayton Escape



A Polished Savannah Vacation Rental With a Great Location



Step into a roomy, open-concept layout with a boho-chic living room made for relaxation. A leather couch, accent chairs and flat-screen television invite you to brush the city off for a minute in complete luxury. A stunning chef's kitchen awaits just steps away from the living room to pamper you with seating for three at a wood-topped, tiered center island set under twin crystalline chandeliers. The island parallels a long counter accented with floating, open-shelf storage. Featuring gorgeous white cabinets, this kitchen is stocked with a slide-in stove, dishwasher, full-sized refrigerator, coffee maker and oversized sink. All appliances are stainless steel. A glass-topped dining table with seating for four provides the perfect dining space just beyond the kitchen.



The entire back wall of Drayton Escape's main living area is lined with oversized windows looking out over the neighborhood around you. You can enjoy sunshine or moonlit views as you prepare meals, dine or relax on the couch. Guests will enjoy a clean, fresh atmosphere in this airy residence that features modern wood floors, eye-catching wall art, high-end lighting fixtures and recessed lighting.



Continuing the clean, modern theme of the house, Drayton Escape's bedroom features an attractive platform bed. Additional modern touches in the bedroom include a flat-screen television & twin night tables. The bedroom shares the same theme of wall-to-wall oversized windows as the main portion of the home. Situated off of the bedroom, Drayton Escape's large bathroom features an extra-long vanity with double sinks. There is also a large, fully tiled walk-in shower. The half-bath features simple and modern fixtures. A laundry nook featuring a washer with a dryer is located adjacent to the bathroom off of the living room. This is also where you'll find a full roster of cleaning supplies stored with other household essentials.



This is a modern, luxury-oriented home offering all the amenities needed for business or pleasure. It comes equipped with Wi-Fi, air conditioning, laundry and satellite/cable television. Guests booked at Drayton Escape enjoy access to our resort-style pool on Tybee Island.



Beyond the Walls of Drayton Escape: Exploring Savannah



This home's central location within Savannah opens up endless possibilities for exploration. Savannah is a city known for its walking tours. For visitors seeking adventure, that covers everything from after-dark pub crawls to haunted cemetery tours. Trolley tours that will take you to the best spots for shopping and dining also offer pleasant options for touring Savannah. A riverboat cruise is another option that opens up a new side of Savannah.



First on any itinerary should be the Savannah Historic District. Peppered with historic homes and buildings, this is the best area for admiring the architecture. A complete tour of Savannah is never finished without stops at Bonaventure Cemetery, Forsyth Park, the Cathedral of St. John the Baptist, Chippewa Square, Colonial Park Cemetery, the American Prohibition Museum, the Ships of the Sea Maritime Museum and the Mercer Williams House. When looking for the best pedestrian spot in Savannah, find your way to City Market to shop and dine among cobbled walkways full of artsy, urban energy. For live music and dining with river views, River Street is the place! Guests planning longer stays at a Savannah vacation home rental can plan for excursions to Skidaway Island State Park, Old Fort Jackson and the Wormsloe Historic Site. Savannah is famed for its roadways lined in Southern oaks, ornate porches, stone fountains and charming trolleys. Weaving in and out of carefully manicured parks that abut Savannah neighborhoods to get from place to place is one of the best ways to enjoy the city.



The Excitement Never Ends: Events to Attend While Staying at a Savannah Vacation Home



Many visitors are drawn to Savannah throughout the year for its epic festivals and cultural events. Topping the list of big draws are the Savannah Food and Wine Festival, the Savannah Jazz Festival, the Savannah Tour of Homes and Gardens, the Sidewalk Arts Festival, the River Street Fourth of July Celebration and the Savannah Film Festival.

This is a beautiful choice for anyone looking for a one-bedroom Savannah vacation rental with a contemporary, clean-slate aesthetic. With a full list of amenities, this home provides everything needed for a stay of any length.
