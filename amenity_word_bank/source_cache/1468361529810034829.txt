=== FULL DESCRIPTION ===
Oglethorpe Square, 1856. Carriages still pass daily.

The Zee Suite is on the main floor. It's colorful, it's cozy and the light pours in. "A must-stay," some guests call it.

Skip the car: River Street one way, Broughton shops the other, Michelin-worthy tables between.

Out back: an infrared sauna, a cold plunge, and the best part of your morning.

Loved like a home, because it is one.

Bosch Huis began as a dream. Guests like you make it real. Tap the heart, see my Host Profile, or book it.

The space
THE ZEE SUITE
The main floor, where the light pours in. There's a king bed with a plush mattress and premium linens, big windows, a walk-in shower with fresh towels waiting, and a hanging chair from the ceiling that guests always claim first. A Nespresso machine, mini-fridge, microwave, and 55-inch smart TV with every streaming service, so the suite runs on its own schedule.

MAIN LEVEL
A long hallway leads to the kitchen, lined with art collected over the years. Local artists, European finds, antique market treasures. Free snacks along the way, and a photo wall of past guests. Add yourselves.

The kitchen is chef-grade: gas range, All-Clad, Vitamix, KitchenAid, a Samsung refrigerator with filtered water and ice. It's shared, but every suite has its own essentials, so barely one in a hundred guests use it. Private slots on request. Guests call it "beautiful" and "private."

OUT FRONT
All of Savannah, one minute at a time: Broughton Street's shops in one, the squares, galleries, and bars in the same, the Riverfront in five, Forsyth Park in ten.

THE ESSENTIALS

Other things to note
WAYS TO BOOK
This listing is the Zee Suite — the main floor, the morning light, the best sleep in the house. The rest of the collection, suite by suite or the whole house at once, lives on my Host Profile. Traveling as a group? Take every key.

PRICING
One rate covers two. Each additional guest adds $50 per night, covering the extra cleaning and wear, plainly. That's the whole story. Pets stay for one flat fee per booking; a whole pack costs a little more. Weekdays run gentler, longer stays earn a discount. Military and teachers booking ahead: say so, and the rate says thank you.

CELEBRATIONS
Proposals, anniversaries, birthdays with a zero in them, a bachelorette claiming the whole house. We arrange what the moment needs: flowers waiting, a table no one can get, a photographer who knows the light on the square. Write to us before booking for anything beyond a quiet stay — larger gatherings are approved in advance through Airbnb, with their own guidelines.

THE HOUSE
This home has stood on Oglethorpe Square since 1856, and it acts its age in the best ways: heart-pine floors that announce your footsteps, stairs that have carried 170 years of guests, walls that hold stories and, occasionally, sound. It's not a hotel built last year, and that's the point.

THE ASSURANCES
Smoke detectors throughout. First aid stocked. Cameras outside only — never inside. The same small cleaning team, every stay. If maintenance ever touches your dates, you'll hear it from us first.

ALWAYS IMPROVING
Nearly every dollar this home generates is reinvested — into the property, our team, and the local community. We're passionate about historic houses, love Savannah wholeheartedly, and it shows in what keeps getting better. 

A SAVANNAH NOTE
This is the South, in a walking city that's been alive since 1733. Church bells ring, carriages clatter, and on weekend nights downtown hums. A professional pest team treats the property regularly, but nature predates the service contract. That's the habitat, not the housekeeping.

OUR STANDARD
If it's reasonable and we have notice, we'll find a way.
