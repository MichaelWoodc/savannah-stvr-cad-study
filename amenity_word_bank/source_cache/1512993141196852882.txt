=== FULL DESCRIPTION ===
Stay in a beautifully restored upper-floor Victorian in the heart of Savannah’s Historic District, accessed by a private entrance via a shared interior corridor and stairway. With two ensuite bedrooms and a sleeper sofa accommodating up to 6 guests, this light-filled retreat offers historic charm, modern comforts, and a prime walkable location just steps from Broughton Street, City Market, and River Street.

The space
Nestled in the heart of Savannah’s Historic District, this beautifully restored Victorian home showcases original details, large windows, and high ceilings that fill the space with natural light. Thoughtful design elements—like a decorative fireplace and curated furnishings—honor the home’s history while offering all the modern conveniences needed for a comfortable stay.



The home includes two large bedrooms, each with generous closet space. The primary bedroom features an ensuite bath, while the second bedroom also offers a private bathroom, making this layout ideal for couples, families, or friends traveling together. A cozy living room, formal dining area, and fully equipped kitchen with a gas stove make relaxing or dining in effortless. An in-unit washer and dryer add extra convenience for longer stays.



Southern charm continues outdoors with a wide front porch overlooking Broughton Street, perfect for morning coffee or evening people-watching as the city buzzes below. Located directly on Savannah’s most vibrant street, you’re steps from shopping, dining, galleries, and nightlife, with City Market and River Street just a short stroll away. Whether you’re here to explore historic Savannah or simply soak in the atmosphere, this classic Victorian retreat delivers an unforgettable stay.

Guest access
Guests have a private access code and will receive a key code prior to arrival to enter the home. This keycode is changed after every guest for your safety.

Other things to note
The home’s prime location is a major highlight for guests. Situated directly on lively Broughton Street, you’ll be steps from Savannah’s best shopping, dining, and nightlife, with City Market and River Street just a short stroll away. The surrounding Historic District is rich with cultural landmarks, art galleries, and iconic attractions, making it the perfect spot to immerse yourself in the city’s charm. After a day of exploring, guests can relax on the wide front porch and watch the world go by, enjoy a meal in the formal dining room or fully equipped kitchen, or simply unwind in the cozy living space. With its inviting porch, modern amenities, and unbeatable location, this Victorian gem offers a truly memorable Savannah experience.
