The chic historic townhouse sits on charming Chatham Square, one block from Forsyth Park. The area contains some of the city’s best row houses. This prestigious area is a short walk to the numerous museums, historic sites, restaurants, and shops.

The space
This historic townhouse sits on charming Chatham Square, one block from Forsyth Park. The area contains some of the city’s best row houses. This prestigious area is a short walk to the numerous museums, historic sites, restaurants, and shops.

Recently renovated with a designers eye toward style, the parlor level suite of this 1854 townhouse overlooks Chatham Square. 
Living room features a smart TV, Wi-Fi and comfy seating. 
The dining room seats four and the kitchen features stainless steel appliances, granite counter tops & is outfitted with anything you need to whip up a meal or heat up take-out. 

The sunny master bedroom has a queen bed, smart TV. The en suite bathroom is also newly renovated with a tiled, walk-in shower. The suite has a private back porch and the below courtyard (shared with other guests in building).  

HISTORIC GORDON ROW: Gordon Row was built in two phases between 1852 and 1854. In the first phase, the 3 or 4 houses on each end of the block were built and in the second phase the middle portion was built. There are a total of 15 houses in the row. The houses were built in the Federalist (US) or Regency Style (UK). Each house is four stories high and contains about 3,300 square feet. The Federalist Style emphasizes square, classical features, plain decorations and mirror image rooms.
