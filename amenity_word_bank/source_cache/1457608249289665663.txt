=== FULL DESCRIPTION ===
Welcome to Savannah! We are thrilled to host you in this updated studio apartment nestled inside the iconic 1920s Graham Building, right on picturesque Oglethorpe Square. With original exterior brick details, hardwood floors, a queen-size bed, and well stocked kitchenette, the space blends historic charm with modern comfort. Just steps from shops, cafés, museums, and the iconic riverfront, this studio is perfect for solo travelers or couples looking to stay in the heart of the Historic District.

The space
Stay in the heart of Savannah’s Historic District in this beautifully updated first-floor studio. Tucked into one of downtown’s most picturesque neighborhoods, this charming retreat blends historic character with thoughtful modern touches.

Unwind on the plush queen memory foam mattress and enjoy fresh bed linens and soft towels provided for your stay. A Pack ’n Play is available upon request for guests traveling with little ones. Best suited for solo travelers or couples, this cozy space offers a peaceful place to recharge.

The recently renovated bathroom features a spacious walk-in tiled shower and is filled with natural light.

The retro-style kitchenette is well-stocked with essentials including a mini fridge, microwave, blender, coffee maker, and dishes—perfect for preparing light meals or your morning brew.

You’ll also enjoy complimentary Wi-Fi and a 40” smart TV for relaxing nights in.

We can’t wait to welcome you!

SVR-03134

Other things to note
Please note that the studio does not have a full size kitchen, just a kitchenette. There is not a dishwasher, but a second pedestal sink between the kitchenette and bathroom that can be used to hand wash dishes. There is not laundry in the studio, however there is coin laundry available in the building basement if needed. Parking is not included, but there is a city parking garage across the street, as well as city metered street parking.
