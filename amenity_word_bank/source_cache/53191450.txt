=== FULL DESCRIPTION ===
This historic cottage is located within walking distance of nearly everything within the Savannah historic district! Carefully furnished in a purposeful colonial style, our space will sweep you away to simpler times. The cottage has a quaint, almost suburban, feel but is very much right in the heart of the action. With relevant, sometimes humorous, decor, you will feel right at home in this comfy condo!

Short-Term Vacation Rental Certificate: SVR-02453
Business Tax Certificate: GBU20210515

The space
CHARLTON COTTAGES:
• Short walk to numerous shops, restaurants, bars, museums, and parks 
• Located in the heart of the Savannah historic district, in the renowned Beach Institute neighborhood
• Enjoy the open container laws outside the premises in one of America's few municipalities that allows it!
• Two on-site private parking spaces!
• Charlton cottages preserve original 19th century feel, including original wood flooring (recently restored) and 4 way fireplaces
• Private washing machine and dryer in unit
• Front porch and private back patio for ample seating and ways to soak up the rays

THE UNIT:
• Self check-in with Schlage Smart Lock System (no key required)
• Bountiful amenities including soaps, shampoos, and conditioners included
• Full Kitchen, with Oven, Fridge, Dishwasher, Microwave, Coffee maker, and Pots & Pans
• Newly installed hot water tank
• Linens and Bath towels provided

PRIVATE PATIO:
• Private backyard and patio area, with outdoor seating
• Seats can either be used with or without cushions
• Backyard area is perfect for some afternoon libations or sunbathing
• Completely fenced area provides total privacy for those wanting a relaxing break from the action

BEDROOM:
• Brand new hybrid king bed (Purchased in 2021)
• Plentiful closet space (and hangers) for everything you bring
• Mounted flat screen TV with cable and streaming apps 
• Tasteful wall art to help lull you away from the modern world into a whimsical escape
• Ceiling fan to provide additional cooling to the room for those warm Savannah summer nights

LIVING ROOM:
• Beautifully restored original hardwood flooring
• Recently purchased 55” Smart TV with access to all your favorite Smart TV apps

• Numerous side tables for drinks, games, snacking, etc.
