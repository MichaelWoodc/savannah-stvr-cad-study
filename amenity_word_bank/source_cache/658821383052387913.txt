=== FULL DESCRIPTION ===
Drayton Place Carriage House Unit 2 is a premier property for anyone searching for Savannah vacation rentals offering room for up to two guests. This one-bedroom, one-bathroom charmer dazzles with a fully updated interior, quaint outdoor courtyard, and entertainment features. The home's enviable location in Savannah's famed Thomas Square neighborhood places guests next door the vibrant Starland District.

The space
Claim your rightful place in Savannah’s most popular new destination when you make your reservation for Drayton Place Carriage Unit 2. Located near everything and right next door to the Starland district, Drayton Place is a stunning estate in the quiet neighborhood of Thomas Square. Not far from Forsyth Park, Savannah’s biggest playground, your Drayton Place stay will be filled with opportunities for adventure. It comes with unlimited access to our Tybee Island swimming pool, which is reserved exclusively for Tybee and Savannah vacation rentals guests.



The Carriage House Accommodation for Two



This beautifully designed suite for two features lovely updates that enhance the historic charm. Premium hardwood floors set the stage for a cozy living room. Comfortable seating and a large smart television with streaming provide entertainment on that rare evening in. Colorful upholstery and art add vibrance to this airy, neutral-toned space to create an energized calm. A bistro table, tucked into a corner, provides seating for mealtime.



Gracious French doors open off the living room to reveal the master bedroom. A roomy king-sized bed promises a luxurious night’s sleep. You'll find nightstands with lamps if you want to read or go online at bedtime, and a trio of windows welcome the morning light.



The ensuite bathroom features a tub/shower combination and a supply of soft, fluffy towels to see you through your Savannah vacation rentals stay. A pedestal sink and tiled floor fit right in with the clean, modern lines you’ll enjoy throughout the Carriage House Unit 2 apartment.



The nearby kitchen boasts an induction burner, refrigerator, toaster-oven, dishwasher and plenty of cupboard space. Inside, you’ll find all the plates and glasses you’ll need to enjoy a meal. Like all our vacation rentals Savannah GA, this one includes a washer-dryer set, too, so you can refresh your wardrobe as needed.



Wi-fi, ceiling fans, central AC and heating keep you comfortable and engaged throughout your Savannah vacation homes stay. A shared, private courtyard offers café lights, bistro seating and a shared gas grill.



Fun, Food and Sun in Savannah



The Thomas Square Streetcar District, which surrounds Drayton Place, received the distinction of a National Historic District in 1997. Once a suburb of the city, Thomas Square sits just outside the greater Savannah Historic District. It includes more than 1,000 original buildings, among them the Sacred Heart Church, the St. Francis Orphanage for African-American Children and a charitable branch of the Georgia Infirmary. They all were built during the 19th century. You’ll find a wealth of history and culture practically at your front door.



Neighborhood homes display a stunning variety of architectural styles, including Beaux Arts, Queen Anne, Victorian, Neoclassical Revival and Craftsman. Take in the styles and their characteristic details on an evening stroll as the air begins to cool and the Starland District begins hopping. Since 2005, the area has been undergoing some rebuilding and sprucing up, so it is a prime place to enjoy yourself.



Just a few blocks to the north, Forsyth Park awaits with 30 acres of outdoor activities. Walking paths, gardens, playing fields, playgrounds, tennis courts and basketball courts surround the crown jewel of the park, a French-style fountain. It’s a gathering place for local residents and vacation rentals Savannah GA guests alike. Weddings, reunions, music and celebrations converge around the picturesque fountain. Be sure to bring your camera because you never know what you’re going to find.



If possible, stop by the park on Saturdays during your visit. A year-round farmer’s market takes place, offering everything from freshly baked pastries to locally grown produce. It’s a great opportunity to immerse yourself in the culture of the South.



Named after the 33rd governor of Georgia, Forsyth Park dates to the mid-1800s. View the park’s historic monuments, enjoy a picnic or throw a frisbee on the expansive playfields.



There are also plenty of places to eat in and near the park. Collins Quarter at Forsyth Park is a casual café with gourmet options for breakfast, brunch and lunch. Nearby, you’ll find 700 Drayton Restaurant a fancier option that serves breakfast, brunch, lunch and dinner too. Drayton serves coastal Georgia cuisine in an elegant mansion setting.



The Sentient Bean at 13 E. Park Ave. is another Forsyth icon catering to your coffee and dessert cravings. It also serves vegetarian fare. Also in the Forsyth vicinity, The Haunt on Abercorn St. serves forest-to-table dishes and esoteric beverages. They source ingredients locally and add their own artful twists.



If you’re in the mood for a cultural experience, the Savannah African Art Museum is a short walk away on 37th Ave. It features art collections from Central and West Africa nations. Storytelling, dance, craft days and more offer opportunities for you to get involved and socialize.



Beat the heat whenever you like at our exclusive heated swimming pool. Located on nearby Tybee Island, it’s a perfect way to see some of the native landscapes and fauna along the way. It’s a less than 30-minute drive, and it’s hard to visit Savannah without seeing some of the fabulous Atlantic coastline. At the pool, you can lounge, shelter beneath sun umbrellas, get some exercise and meet other Savannah Vacation Homes guests.



Get in touch with us today to book your excellent Savannah adventure. Don’t forget to pack your swimming gear!
