=== FULL DESCRIPTION ===
Set in the heart of Savannah's Historic & Shopping District, Villa on Broughton Main + Carriage pairs historic charm with modern comfort. Sleeping up to 10, it's ideal for bridal parties, reunions, or big group getaways right in the center of it all.

The space
Located in the heart of Savannah's Historic & Shopping District, Villa on Broughton Main + Carriage combines historic charm with modern comfort and style. This unique home includes both a main house and a private carriage house, accommodating up to 10 guests — making it an excellent choice for bridal party retreats, family reunions, or large group getaways in downtown Savannah.

The main floor welcomes you into a spacious living room, the perfect gathering space for guests. Beyond it, an open-concept dining area flows into a fully equipped kitchen featuring stunning countertops and new stainless steel appliances, ideal for preparing meals for a large group. Step outside to the private courtyard, furnished for relaxing evenings.

The main house sleeps up to 8 guests across two bedrooms and two sleeper sofas. One sleeper sofa sits in a lofted room above the primary bedroom, a great option for parents wanting to keep kids close by. The primary bedroom opens through sliding doors onto a small private balcony overlooking the courtyard. Both bedrooms include ensuite bathrooms and generous closet space. The separate carriage house studio accommodates 2 additional guests and features its own full-size kitchen and bathroom — ideal for multigenerational travel or added privacy within the group.

After a day exploring Savannah, unwind with a cocktail in the private courtyard or take in the views from the rooftop patio.
This Savannah vacation rental sits in one of the city's most sought-after historic neighborhoods, just minutes from the lively end of Broughton Street and Savannah's renowned shopping, dining, and sightseeing. City Market, Bay Street, and River Street are all within a short two-block walk.

Whats parking like for this home?
Guests will have access to one small private garage space (small to midsize vehicles only). All other parking can be found on-street, metered, Monday-Saturday 8AM-8PM. We encourage guests to pay close attention to street signs, as they state when the street cleaner comes.

We can welcome your dog companions at this pet friendly home, but ask that you find other arrangements for cats, birds, and other furry friends. There is a $130 pet fee (non-refundable) with a 35 lb. maximum weight limit per dog and a limit of 2 dogs per reservation. Some homes may allow larger dogs, please inquire for more information.

We stayed at this Airbnb for my friend’s bachelorette party, and it worked perfectly for our group. We’re all in our 30s and 40s and there was plenty of room for the 6 of us to sleep and hang out comfortably. The house is in a quiet neighborhood and walking distance to a ton of shops and restaurants.
One thing to note is while the main house has 3 bathrooms, it only has 1 shower, which is off the master bedroom. There is a huge tub in the upstairs hallway bathroom and a 2nd shower in the carriage house, so there are certainly options. But, worth noting because you could easily sleep 6-8 people in the main house alone and you’d have to walk outside to get to the second shower in the carriage house. 
The shower setup had very little impact on our group, and is the only thing any of us considered even a tiny complaint. The house was one of the cleanest and well kept Airbnbs I’ve ever stayed at, and I’d definitely stay again next time I’m in Savannah!
