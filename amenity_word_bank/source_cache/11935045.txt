=== FULL DESCRIPTION ===
This parlor level (2nd floor) apartment is in the center of Savannah's Landmark Historic District, on Abercorn just north of Liberty. This is right next to Colonial Park Cemetery which is a famous downtown landmark. One private parking spot in back!

City of Savannah SVR-02455

The space
This apartment is centrally located in the heart of the busy downtown Savannah historic district. The King bedroom windows face Colonial Park Cemetery. The living room has a pullout sofa. The bathroom is off the living room. There is a small equipped kitchen and a stacker washer and dryer.

Guest access
You get the entire apartment to yourself (kitchen, bathroom, living room, bedroom). There is a main exterior entry to the building, a shared foyer and then your apartment door.

$15 flat fee for use of one Private Off Street Parking Space Behind the Home! If you do not wish to use our off street parking space or have more than 1 vehicle you will have to park on street where parking is metered around the home. We suggest downloading the ParkSavannah app to pay for your numbered spot with a credit card on your phone.

Other things to note
I wanted to mention that Savannah has certain bugs which we can control to some extent with poison (which every hotel and vacation rental will use on perimeters) but guests still can see them, even if they’re slower and dying from walking across poison barriers. Specifically palmetto bugs are prolific here. If guests see one we do not issue any refunds due to seeing these.

There is one designated off street space for your vehicle. If you have a 2nd vehicle it will need to park on-street where meters around the home run Monday - Saturday from 8am-8pm and are free nights and on Sunday. Meters are best handled by downloading the ParkSavannah App to pay for numbered spaces by credit card. 

This is a truly historic building. Expect notable differences between modern properties and this apartment.  This is about half of the hotel price within a block of the home. 

Events, holidays and stays during March & April may require 3 night minimums and/or have higher rates than the standard ones published.

This is a traditional vacation rental home, not a “bed and breakfast”. We do not provide food or drinks but we do provide basic cooking and serving utensils, a coffee maker, and dish soap. Downtown Savannah has a major supermarket (Kroger) and a 24 hour market (Parkers on Drayton) so there is no need to pack everything from home if shopping when you get here makes more sense. 

What to bring with you:

A typical grocery run prior to arriving at your vacation home would include coffee, beer/wine, milk, eggs, butter, bread and any other basic groceries you would need for the meals you eat in the home. 

When it comes to coffee, bring your own ground coffee. The apartment has a 12-cup drip coffee maker.  We do not typically provide coffee but if you find coffee in the apartment please feel free to use it.

You should bring or buy your own napkins, laundry detergent, your own beach towels and beach gear, and your own cooler if you need one.  

What we provide:

We provide toilet paper (2 extras per bathroom), liquid hand washing dish soap, bathroom soap, sheets on each bed, and at least one bath towel for each guest. We do not permit our towels to be taken out of the house which is why you must bring your own towels if you visit the beach. 

If you find we have left seasonings, oil, dishwasher pellets, or any unopened food or drinks then of course feel free to use what is there. We occasionally leave items but just cannot promise them all the time. 

Please also note that in Savannah, sugar ants can smell cookies, spilled sugar or syrup, or an open sugar container from a mile away and will find a way in through a windowsill or door frame. It can be somewhat like a cartoon picnic where they march in a line to find the food. To avoid this try to keep your snacks contained in air tight baggies, the refrigerator, or buy single serve packets and put the trash in the outdoor trash cans as soon as possible. 

The following is standard Savannah Luxury Rentals information: We provide sheets, toilet paper and soap. If you prefer thick cushioned toilet paper bring it along but use it sparingly in our older plumbing and never flush "flushable" wipes or other products that are likely to cause a clog.
