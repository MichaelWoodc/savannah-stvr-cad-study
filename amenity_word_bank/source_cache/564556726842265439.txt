=== FULL DESCRIPTION ===
Enjoy our peaceful and spacious condo, perched on the second floor of our historic Savannah estate, just steps away from Forsyth Park! This 1-bedroom, 1-bathroom condo (with a convenient pull-out sofa, great for your extra guest!) is the perfect home base for your Savannah stay! Fully-equipped kitchen with all the amenities, cozy and comfortable living space with flat screen SmartTV, fast WiFi, and the cherry on top...a HUGE private balcony! 

SVR 02704

The space
This second floor condo has one large bedroom with king sized bed, wall-mounted TV, closet, and cozy reading nook. Step down the hall and enter the open kitchen and living space. The kitchen is perfect for a quick breakfast in or a full 5-course dinner...it is truly chef inspired! The bathroom has a spa-like feeling with walk-in shower. And best of all...the expansive balcony, perched amongst these Savannah live oaks! The perfect spot for a hot cup of coffee, a happy hour glass of wine, or just a relaxing nap while taking in our amazing Southern weather!

Guest access
The entire condo is all yours!

Other things to note
Don't be afraid to reach out if you need anything!  We live super close-by and are always happy to help out, even mid-stay.

Collin's in Forsyth, Crystal Beer Parlor, 700 Drayton, Local 11Ten and other incredible bars and restaurants are right outside your door.
