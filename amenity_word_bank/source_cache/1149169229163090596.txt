=== FULL DESCRIPTION ===
Welcome to Magnolia Carriage—your serene retreat nestled in vibrant downtown Savannah. Just steps from iconic River Street and the city’s famed cobblestone lanes, this beautifully appointed guesthouse offers the perfect blend of charm, comfort, and convenience.

The space
🛏️ Relax in Comfort
Unwind in style with plush memory foam mattresses, cozy linens, and thoughtfully curated decor. Whether you're here for a romantic getaway or a solo escape, restful nights await.

🍳 Fully Equipped Kitchen
Cook up your favorite meals in the well-stocked kitchen, complete with cookware, utensils, seasonings, and cooking oil—everything you need for a night in.

🚗 Easy Parking & Walkability
Enjoy the convenience of a private garage spot suitable for a mid-size or smaller vehicle, or take advantage of free street parking on the block. With Savannah’s top attractions, restaurants, and shops just a short stroll away, you may not even want a car.

🌳 Historic & Peaceful Location
Tucked away on a quiet block of a tree-lined street in the historic district, Magnolia Carriage offers a peaceful haven after a day of exploring. Guests also have access to a shared courtyard—perfect for sipping sweet tea and soaking in the Southern charm. Every corner of Savannah tells a story—immerse yourself in its rich history, vibrant arts scene, and unforgettable hospitality.

✨ Your Savannah Story Starts Here
Whether you're relaxing in the courtyard or discovering hidden gems around town, Magnolia Carriage is your perfect home base. We can’t wait to host you!
