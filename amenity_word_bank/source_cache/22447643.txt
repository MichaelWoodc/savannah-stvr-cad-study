=== FULL DESCRIPTION ===
This smartly renovated cottage is the perfect retreat for a couple or family looking to stay in style in the historic district. One King bed, two Twins, and a huge living space, private parking and backyard make the Gordon Cottage a perfect retreat!

The space
Located on East Gordon Street, this smartly renovated cottage is the perfect retreat for a couple or family looking to stay in style in the historic district. A private parking space is located directly behind the property, and there is plenty of non-metered parking in the front. 

Gordon Cottage features a spacious living room with large, inviting sofas perfect for lounging. The adjoining dining room can comfortably sit four, and is conveniently connected both to the bathroom and kitchen. The kitchen has all the conveniences you could need, including plenty of counter space, a breakfast nook, and a washer and dryer tucked away in the back corner. 

Up the stairs you’ll discover two large bedrooms. The primary bedroom features a king size bed and flat screen television. The second bedroom offers two, comfy twins, perfect for friends, family or kids! Making your way to the back of the home, you will locate the beautiful, serene, private courtyard.

Why Lucky Savannah Loves this Property:

- Pick a direction and walk! In just ten minutes you’ll find yourself at River Street, Forsyth Park, and several other Savannah attractions!

- The private courtyard in the back of the property has enough shade to be enjoyed in the summer. 

- The industrial modern decor is offset with lots of natural light and glass paneled doors. 

Parking: There is one designated parking space behind the home. Additional parking in this area of Savannah is on-street and non-metered. Please be aware of posted street sweeping schedules when parking.
