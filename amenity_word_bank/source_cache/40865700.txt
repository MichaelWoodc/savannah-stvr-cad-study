=== FULL DESCRIPTION ===
Newly built three-story home that offers true refined Southern Luxury, with private parking, a heated plunge pool, and elevator.

The space
Just off of Liberty Street, you will feel like a local in the Downtown Historic District. Just a few short steps up to the main level, guests will enter into their new home away from home. The main level offers an inviting living space with chefs kitchen and a formal dining area. Conveniently placed, your guests can use the half bath on the main floor. Off the dining room, step out onto your back porch for a morning cup of joe with views of your private plunge pool, and the cathedral in the distance. After sending your luggage up via elevator, the third floor consists of two primary suites, each fitted with an en suite bathroom, and sophisticated decor. 

Downstairs, ground level living is a great option for guests or family members that have mobility issues. The garden apartment offers a full kitchen equipped with high-end stainless steel appliances, dedicated dining space and large kitchen island. At the back of the home, you'll find a roomy bathroom with a tiled shower and tub combo and Queen bedroom.

Your fourth bedroom and bathroom can be found in the carriage, a standalone apartment behind the home located on the second level. The rest of your party simply crosses the courtyard and walks up a secure flight of stairs. The carriage features a full kitchen with granite countertops, featuring stainless appliances. Guests can catch up on their favorite shows on the flat screen TV, and the sofa also converts to a Queen bed.  The bedroom has a Queen bed with en suite bath, fitted with a shower and tub combination. 

Private Parking: The private garage accommodates 1 small/mid-size sedan. Nearby parking is on-street and non-metered, although guests will need to be aware of the posted sweep zone signs.
