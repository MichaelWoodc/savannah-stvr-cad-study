The space
Welcome to Elsewhere on Madison Square Garden, a charming and sophisticated retreat overlooking one of Savannah’s most beloved historic squares. Perfect for couples, small families, wedding guests, business travelers, or extended stays, this beautifully appointed garden-level residence offers the ideal blend of historic character, modern comfort, and an unbeatable Historic District location.

Nestled beneath the grand main residence, this private Savannah hideaway places you steps from Madison Square, Forsyth Park, renowned restaurants, boutique shopping, museums, and the city's most iconic attractions. Whether you're visiting for a romantic getaway, wedding weekend, or an immersive Savannah experience, you'll find yourself in the center of it all.

Inside, guests are welcomed by warm interiors, elegant furnishings, and thoughtful touches that create a relaxed yet refined atmosphere. Historic charm blends seamlessly with modern conveniences, offering a comfortable space to unwind after a day exploring Savannah's cobblestone streets and picturesque squares.

The residence features a spacious queen bedroom, two full bathrooms, and an additional sleeping area with a Murphy bed, making it ideal for couples, small groups, or families seeking extra flexibility. The open layout provides plenty of room to gather while still maintaining privacy and comfort throughout your stay.

The fully equipped kitchen offers everything needed to prepare meals at home, from morning coffee before a stroll through the Historic District to intimate dinners after a day of sightseeing. Comfortable living spaces invite guests to relax, reconnect, and enjoy the slower pace that makes Savannah so special.

Step outside and enjoy the beauty of one of Savannah's most desirable locations. Madison Square sits just beyond your doorstep, offering scenic green spaces, historic monuments, and easy access to the city's most celebrated landmarks.

Whether you're attending a wedding, celebrating a special occasion, working remotely, or simply escaping for a few days in Savannah, Elsewhere on Madison Square Garden offers the perfect combination of location, comfort, and historic Southern charm.

Sleeping Configuration:
Bedroom 1: Queen Bed
Flex Room: Queen Murphy Bed

What is parking like for this home? 
Parking for this home is on-street and metered, which is enforced by the City of Savannah from Monday through Friday, 8am to 5pm. Guests may conveniently pay using the ParkSavannah app, feed the meter or parking kiosks directly, or park on a nearby non-metered street. Please take note of posted Sweep Zone signs when parking on the street.
