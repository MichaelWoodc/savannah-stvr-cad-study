=== FULL DESCRIPTION ===
Welcome to your home away from home in the heart of Savannah. This one-bedroom, one-bath apartment is tucked on a quiet street in the Historic District. Just steps from squares, cafés, and shops. You will be in a super walkable location, yet peaceful enough area to truly unwind and relax. Recently updated, the space offers modern comforts, lots of natural light, a fully stocked kitchen, and a stunning bathroom all wrapped into a cozy retreat that honors its historic roots.

The space
If you’re looking for a Savannah getaway where you can walk out the door and immediately feel like you’re in the middle of everything you came here to experience, this is it!

Tucked away on a quieter street in the heart of Savannah’s Historic District, this beautifully renovated one-bedroom apartment is just a short walk from River Street, Broughton Street, Savannah’s historic squares, coffee shops, restaurants, shopping + so much more.

It really gives you the best of both worlds. You can spend your days exploring Savannah on foot, then come home to a quiet, comfortable space to slow down and relax at the end of the day.

This cozy garden-level apartment has been thoughtfully renovated to feel comfortable and elevated while keeping the historic details that make Savannah homes so special.

The brick floors are one of our favorite details and add so much character to the space.

Inside, you’ll find a comfortable living room with a Smart TV, fully stocked kitchen + coffee bar, dedicated workspace, beautifully updated bathroom and private queen bedroom.

It’s the perfect home base for a couple’s Savannah getaway, girls’ weekend, solo trip or even a longer stay when you want to settle in and experience the city a little more like a local.

The bright, updated kitchen is fully stocked with stainless steel appliances, cookware, dishes, glassware + the everyday essentials you’ll need during your stay. We also stock na small coffee bar with a Keurig and coffee essentials for your mornings.

Whether you’re actually planning to cook or just need somewhere to keep the leftovers from all the Savannah restaurants you’re going to try, you’ll have everything you need.

Enjoy a comfortable queen bed with fresh linens, extra pillows and plenty of space to unpack.

You’ll also find hangers, an iron, steamer and extra bedding tucked away in the closet.

Traveling with a little one? We also provide a Pack ’n Play.


The newly renovated bathroom is bright, fresh and thoughtfully updated with a spacious vanity, beautiful lighting and a stunning tiled walk-in shower.

We also provide fluffy towels and basic toiletries so you can pack a little lighter.

You’re right in the heart of Savannah’s Historic District, surrounded by the beautiful architecture, moss-draped live oaks, historic squares + cobblestone streets that probably made you want to visit Savannah in the first place.

River Street is about a five-minute walk away, and you can easily walk to Broughton Street, restaurants, coffee shops, boutiques, museums, bars and so many of Savannah’s most-loved spots.

And when you are ready to call it a night, you get to leave some of the busier tourist areas behind and come home to a quieter little corner of the Historic District.
