=== FULL DESCRIPTION ===
This beautifully renovated garden level apartment in Historic Downtown Savannah was built in 1872. Feel like a local while you stay in this meticulously renovated 2 bedroom, 2 bath apartment. This unit features a queen bed, a double bed, fold out sofa, fully equipped kitchen, high speed wifi, and flat screen tv. Easy meter-free on-street parking. Well-behaved pets welcome. No barkers please.

Just a short walk away from Forsyth Park, River St, downtown shopping, restaurants, and entertainment.

The space
Just a short walk away from Forsyth Park, City Market, River Street, and Broughton Street, you won't need your car to get to shopping, restaurants, and entertainment. Wander out the front door to Taylor Square, or around the corner where famous Forsyth Park is just two blocks away. Close enough to walk to everything, but in a quiet residential area.

A walk to:

* Forsyth Park
* City Market
* River Street
* Broughton Street shopping
* Downtown Design District
* countless restaurants, coffeeshops, and bars
* all 22 downtown Squares
* The Mercer House
* The Pink House
* The Owens Thomas House
* The Juliet Gordon Lowe birthplace and Girl Scout original Headquarters
* The Telfair and Jepson Art Museums
* Savannah Civic Center
* Savannah College of Art and Design (SCAD)


A short drive to:

* Tybee Island beach
* Bonaventure Cemetery
* Savannah Hilton Head International Airport

Guest access
Travelers will have access to a spacious living room, kitchen, two bedrooms and two bathrooms, as well as a private courtyard, shared with the owner, and the upper floor unit guests.

SVR Cert. No. SVR-00092
