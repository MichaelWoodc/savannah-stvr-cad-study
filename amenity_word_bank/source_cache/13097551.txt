=== FULL DESCRIPTION ===
One of our quality Savannah vacation rentals is the Jay-Ann House. Located right near Washington Square, this rental can sleep 6 guests in three bedrooms with four bathrooms. As with all of our vacation homes Savannah, we provide a variety of great amenities, including all kitchen appliances and utensils, linens, Wi-Fi, flat-screen TVs and more. Everything you need to feel at home on your Georgia vacations is here.

The space
The master bedroom features en suite accommodations, one of the nicest bathrooms out there featuring a multi-head stand-up shower and double sinks, and a luxurious king-sized bed. The second bedroom also features en suite accommodations and a queen-sized bed. Upstairs you will find a loft style room with a king-size and twin-size bed perfect for the little ones in your party. Finally a queen bed rounds out the properties sleeping quarters with it's easy access to the outstanding upstairs bathroom.



You won't have to head too far south of your vacation homes Savannah to find the huge Colonial Park/Cemetery. This cemetery is about six acres in size and is the oldest municipal cemetery in the city. It has been closed since 1853 to interments, but was the main cemetery between 1750 and the time it closed. Savannah is well-known for its parks, and probably the most famous of these is Forsyth Park. Prominently featured in the "Midnight in the Garden of Good and Evil" book and movie, this park is a popular place for both tourists and locals. You can wander the trails or have a picnic on the grounds. River Street is another big draw, with its rows of old cotton warehouses transformed into artist lofts, boutiques and gourmet restaurants. Of course, no trip to Savannah would be complete without taking one of the famous tours. You can choose from a spooky ghost tour, a horse drawn carriage tour or even a pub crawl.



One of the best things about Savannah is all the celebrations that take place throughout the year. Check with local information to see if any of these festivals might be during your stay.



• Annual Martin Luther King, Jr. Day Celebration in January

• Georgia Heritage Society's Colonial Faire and Muster in February

• Tybee Island Beach Bum Parade in May

• Savannah Asian Festival of June

• Fourth of July Fireworks on the Beach in July

• Oktoberfest in October

• Savannah Harbor Boat Parade of Lights in November

• Holiday Tour of Homes in December
