=== FULL DESCRIPTION ===
Tucked in a quiet neighborhood of Savannah’s Historic District, Courtyard Cottage is a thoughtfully renovated, peaceful retreat. Enjoy a fully equipped kitchen and a private courtyard—perfect for morning coffee or afternoon wine. 

City Market is just a 10-minute walk, so a car isn’t needed. If you bring one, free street parking is available on the cottage side of the street, with paid parking across the way. 

Savannah STVR Certificate #SVR-00629.

The space
Courtyard Cottage is your relaxing getaway in beautiful Historic Savannah located a block and a half from Troup Square.  This thoughtfully renovated space features a private courtyard that feels like an outdoor room--one where you can dine al fresco, enjoy your morning coffee, check your email and sip sweet tea or wine in the afternoon.  

With a nod toward mid-century modern design, Courtyard Cottage is eclectically furnished and decorated with modern and local art. Open the door and step into the kitchen with original heart pine floors and a table with seating for four. You’ll find it is well-appointed with the tools you need to cook a great meal. From there, head to the living area with comfortable sofa and chairs, reading lamps and a 40-inch TV, as well as a lending library. The tiled bathroom is adjacent to the living room and is easily accessible from both bedrooms. A door from the living room takes you out to the charmingly landscaped courtyard.  With landscape lighting, you can even enjoy the courtyard at night. 

Both bedrooms offer a comfortable queen bed, two nightstands, reading lamps, and a closet. The full-size stacked washer and dryer is tucked in neatly. Quality bed linens and towels are provided, along with soap, shampoo, conditioner. You will also find a hair dryer, iron and ironing board. 

For your entertainment, enjoy wifi and cable TV. Bring your codes for Netflix, etc., through the smart TV.

Savannah is best experienced on foot. Park your car in front of the cottage and explore the moss-draped streets, hidden gardens and beautiful architecture of our charming city. Everything you need is in walking distance—historic sites, restaurants, pubs and groceries.

We designed our charming Courtyard Cottage with your comfort in mind. Come enjoy Savannah like a local.

Guest access
This 525 sq. ft. private cottage is at your complete disposal. There is room in the closets, drawers and bathroom shelves for you to unpack and feel at home.
