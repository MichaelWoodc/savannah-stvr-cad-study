=== FULL DESCRIPTION ===
Welcome to the Koi Escape. a serene, stylish 3-bedroom, 2-bath retreat perched on the second floor of a beautifully restored historic home. Located just 2 blocks from Forsyth Park, this upper-level unit offers peaceful views, a private upper porch, and modern amenities - all within walking distance of Savannah’s top restaurants, nightlife, and landmarks! Relax, recharge, and soak in the charm of the Historic District from your elevated Savannah sanctuary. SVR-02905

The space
Private second-floor unit with no shared interior space
Three comfortable bedrooms with premium linens and blackout curtains
2 updated bathrooms with quality finishes and fresh towels
Spacious, shaded second-story porch overlooking garden and koi pond
Full kitchen with stainless steel appliances, Keurig & drip coffee, and all essentials
Bright open-concept living and dining space with smart TV and fast Wi-Fi
Central A/C and heat for year-round comfort
In-unit washer/dryer—perfect for longer stays
Professionally cleaned and sanitized before each guest

Bedroom breakdown:
Bedroom 1: King bed + ensuite bathroom
Bedroom 2: Queen bed
Bedroom 3: Queen bed
Living Room: Pullout Couch (Queen)

Guest access
Guests have full private access to the home, including the second-story porch, koi pond, and garden area. The home includes free off-street parking in the rear driveway (up to 2 vehicles). Enjoy self-check-in with a smart lock for added flexibility and security. There is a camera on the porch for security purposes only.

Other things to note
This is the lower unit of a duplex, so please be courteous to your neighbors and keep noise levels to a minimum after 10pm and before 8am, especially when outside :)

Neighborhood highlights
The Koi Retreat is ideally situated just two blocks west of Forsyth Park on Park Ave W. Enjoy easy walks to fantastic dining like Collins Quarter at Forsyth. Grab a coffee right in the neighborhood at the Sentient Bean. Pick up some tremendous BBQ to go at Saqib’s BBQ Emporium, or grab a drink at The Black Rabbit—all just minutes from the apartment. Ready to explore outside of just the neighborhood? Spice up your nightlife by heading to Molly MacPherson’s Scottish pub for that perfect pint, or check out Congress Street Social Club, where you can enjoy live music, sports, and billiards. Take in the true essence of Savannah by stopping by the City Market, an 18th-century open-air market that has existed in Savannah since the 1700s. Enjoy four blocks of restaurants, shops, and more! Once there, head just a few blocks to enjoy the famous Savannah Riverwalk. Experience the incredible history of Savannah by stopping by the Telfair Academy, an elegant, long-standing art museum, The American Prohibition Museum, Ships of the Sea Maritime Museum, and many more, all just a short walk from the apartment. Want to take it all in at once while riding or biking? We are just minutes from Old Town Trolley Tours of Savannah and Savannah Bike Tours. Getting around is easy with walkable streets, bike rentals, and Uber service. Jump on Savannah’s public transit system with the Barnard and Park stop just a 1-minute walk from the loft. Try out the Savannah Belles Ferries to see the beautiful skyline of Savannah from the riverfront, with a stop right at the riverfront.
