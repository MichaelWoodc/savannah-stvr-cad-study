=== FULL DESCRIPTION ===
Pet friendly condo located in historic area

Other things to note
Welcome to your Historic Savannah vacation at Waldburg Garden apartment. This beautifully restored historic townhome was originally built in 1875 and fully renovated to include all modern amenities while maintaining its original charm. Located just two blocks from beautiful Forsyth Park and its iconic fountain and walking distance to the heart of historic Savannah, this is a perfect location to immerse yourself in Savannah life, where you'll be spoiled with many good dining options, strolls through live oak-draped squares, galleries, and museums. 



The quaint ground-level garden apartment is perfect for a couple or a small family visiting Historic Savannah. The spacious apartment offers a private entrance with its covered patio and a shared courtyard and garden. The interior has been renovated and includes one bedroom with a Queen size bed, a full bathroom with a beautiful all-new walk-in shower, and a parlor-style living area with a sleeper sofa and a flat-screen TV. Plan to prepare excellent meals in the all-new kitchen, fully equipped with stainless appliances, new cabinetry, quartz countertops, and a four-burner gas range and oven.



Enjoy quiet mornings or evening cocktails on the backyard-covered patio. The outdoor space includes a fenced yard for your furry family members. The backyard space is shared with the upstairs apartment. Wifi is available. Street parking is available nearby on Waldburg Street or surrounding streets. No reserved off-street parking is available.



Walk or bike to many nearby amenities, including Forsyth Park: Many events are held at the park throughout the year. It is an excellent choice for picnics, outdoor sports, walking, and jogging.



Guests will also be located only a short 20-25 minute drive to Tybee Island, Savannah's beach. Come out for a day trip to enjoy the beach, fishing, visit Ft. Pulaski, or climb Georgia's oldest and tallest lighthouse. 



PETS: This home is pet-friendly for up to two (2) adult dogs with approval. Cats are not allowed in any TVR property. Please Note: Tybee City ordinance does not allow dogs on any Tybee Island beaches.
