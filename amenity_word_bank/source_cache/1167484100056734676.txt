=== FULL DESCRIPTION ===
Experience historic charm and fresh design in our downtown, riverfront condo! This beautifully designed 3BR/2BA gem features original heart pine floors, a spacious living and dining area, a fully stocked kitchen (with cute bar!), and a cozy reading nook!  Enjoy amenities like fast WiFi, in-unit laundry, and TWO PARKING PASSES for a nearby garage. Perfectly located in the heart of everything, this condo offers a blend of history and contemporary comfort for an unforgettable stay.

SVR-02993

The space
Welcome to our stunning condo, perfectly tucked on Factor's Walk in the heart of historic downtown Savannah. This bright and light-filled space inside a gorgeously revitalized 1857 building, boasts sweeping views of the Savannah River through floor-to-ceiling windows, offering an incredible backdrop for your stay.

The modern kitchen is fully stocked with all the conveniences and amenities you could need, including a Keurig coffee maker with complimentary K-cups, creamers, and sugar. Gather around the large dining table that seats eight comfortably, ideal for family meals or entertaining friends.

Relax in the spacious living room, featuring comfortable seating and plenty of space for everyone to unwind. Enjoy your favorite shows on the flat-screen TV with streaming capabilities, and stay connected with fast WiFi throughout the condo.

Each of the three bedrooms is a serene retreat, two with a king-sized bed and one with a queen, all featuring large closets for ample storage. Two of the three bedrooms additionally have wall-mounted, flat screen smart TVs. The two full bathrooms offer a spa-like experience with double sink vanities and large, walk-in showers. Adorable original features, such as a charming reading nook, add unique character and charm to the space.

For your convenience, there is an in-unit washer and dryer. Two parking passes for the nearby Congress Street iParkit garage, just a few blocks away, are included with your stay.

Located in the perfect spot, you'll be in the heart of everything that historic Savannah has to offer. Whether you're here to explore the city's rich history, enjoy its vibrant dining scene, or simply relax by the river, our condo is the ideal home base for your Savannah adventure.

Guest access
The entire condo is all yours from check-in at 4pm until check-out at 10am! There are no shared spaces and it is entirely private.

Other things to note
- The best thing about our condo, in our opinion, is the location! We are in the heart of downtown Savannah, overlooking the river, and central to all the amazing places that folks come to the city to visit! HOWEVER, with our incredible location comes noise! Guests should expect noise from the river side tourists, adjoining and nearby businesses, and nearby traffic. Noise should be expected at all hours. If you don't feel as though you would be comfortable with urban, city noise throughout your stay, we do not recommend this space for your Savannah travels. 

- We include two parking passes for nearby Congress Street iParkit garage. The garage is located three blocks away. Unfortunately, Airbnb does not provide the option to check off "off-site, free parking" in the amenities list, so we like to clarify that we do provide these two passes for this nearby garage, however it is three blocks away from the condo and not "on-site." You can, however, pull up to the condo to unload your luggage while idling your vehicle, then proceed to the parking garage, to avoid having to tote your belongings.
