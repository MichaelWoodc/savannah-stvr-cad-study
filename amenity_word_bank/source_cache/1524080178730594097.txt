The space
Step into this inviting studio apartment nestled inside the historic Graham Building, located in the heart of Savannah’s Historic District. Built in the 1920s, this iconic building has witnessed nearly a century of Savannah’s story and now it’s ready to be part of yours. The space has been thoughtfully updated to blend vintage character with modern comfort featuring a spacious kitchen, updated bathroom and balcony,  that make it feel like a true retreat.

Why Guests Love It:
-Iconic location in a historic downtown building
-Walkable to River Street, Broughton shops, and Forsyth Park
-Clean, quiet, and thoughtfully decorated with curated touches
-Ideal for solo travelers or couples

Inside, you’ll find a comfortable queen bed with fresh, plush linens, a fully equipped kitchen, perfect for light cooking and morning coffee. 

Enjoy a renovated bathroom stocked with essentials. 

One of the studio’s standout features is the private balcony, offering a perfect spot to sip coffee or a glass of wine while soaking in the Savannah breeze and the sounds of the city.

Whether you're traveling solo or as a couple, the layout is ideal for relaxing after a day of wandering the city’s cobblestone streets.

Located just off Oglethorpe Square, this studio places you within walking distance of Savannah’s most iconic landmarks—River Street, Broughton Street, Colonial Park Cemetery, and dozens of cafés, shops, and squares are just steps from your door. The neighborhood is quiet yet central, offering the best of both worlds for travelers looking to explore with ease.

This is the perfect home base for a Savannah visit! We can't wait to host you!
