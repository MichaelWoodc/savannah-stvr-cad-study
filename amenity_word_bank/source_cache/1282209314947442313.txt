=== FULL DESCRIPTION ===
This completely renovated studio apartment overlooks Oglethorpe Square. Have your morning coffee or afternoon cocktail on the balcony overlooking picturesque Oglethorpe Square. This unit is located right in the heart of Downtown Savannah, walking distance to everything! 

Message for discounted rate options. 

Have 4 guests? Book our sister property right next door, https://www.airbnb.com/rooms/2105926.

The space
Located beside the infamous Owens-Thomas House and Oglethorpe Square, the historic yet modern one bedroom studio comfortably accommodates two guests. The studio is located in The Graham Building. The studio features a comfortable king bed and 1 full bath. Parking options include on street parking and the State Street Parking Garage located conveniently next door. All parking options will be discussed in detail in the check-in email. 

 City of Savannah Short Term Vacation Rental (STVR) Certificate - SVR - 02969.

Guest access

Other things to note
You can book the neighboring unit for 2 more guests and the 4 of you will be neighbors with adjacent balconies!
