=== FULL DESCRIPTION ===
Make The Lemon Drop your Savannah home away from home! 🍋 We’ve squeezed as much color, character & Southern charm as possible into this bright first-floor 2BR/1BA condo that sleeps 6! Once a neighborhood storefront dating to 1910, it now offers tons of natural light, a spacious living + dining area, full kitchen & washer/dryer. Tucked in the quiet Victorian District just a few blocks from Forsyth Park 🌳, it’s a sweet home base for exploring Savannah! 💛

SVR 02491

The space
Welcome to The Lemon Drop! 🍋 We've squeezed as much color, character, and Southern charm as possible into this bright and cheerful first-floor Savannah condo. With two bedrooms, room for up to six guests, a full kitchen, in-unit laundry, and a fantastic location in the Victorian District, it's the perfect home base for your Savannah getaway!

✨ A LITTLE HISTORY + A LOT OF CHARACTER

One of our favorite things about this unit is its history! The front of the space was originally used as a neighborhood storefront around 1910. Today, the oversized bay windows that once welcomed customers fill the living space with tons of beautiful natural light and give you the perfect spot to sit back, relax, and watch Savannah go by.

We've filled the space with color, whimsical details, and plenty of personality while making sure you still have all the comforts of home.

🛋️ LIVING + DINING

Walk through the front door into a spacious, light-filled, open-concept living, dining, and kitchen area. There's plenty of room for everyone to gather for meals, plan the day's adventures, play a game, or kick back after a day exploring Savannah.

High-speed WiFi and a Smart TV make it easy to stream your favorite shows and movies—just sign in to your preferred streaming services and make yourself at home!

🍳 FULL KITCHEN

Whether you're making a quick breakfast before heading out or cooking dinner at home, the kitchen is stocked with the essentials you'll need during your stay.

You'll find a large basin sink, dishwasher, range, refrigerator, cookware, dishes, and other kitchen basics.

And because Savannah exploring requires caffeine, there's a Keurig with a few starter K-Cups provided. ☕️🍋

🌈 BEDROOM ONE – KING BED

Bright, playful, and full of personality, the first bedroom features a fun rainbow mural spanning one wall and a whimsical velvet king bed with crisp, comfortable bedding.

There's also a Smart TV, so you can curl up and stream your favorite shows at the end of the day.

💚 BEDROOM TWO – KING + TWO TWINS

The second bedroom brings even more color with its cheerful green design and a flexible sleeping setup that's especially great for families and groups.

1 King Bed
2 Twin Trundle Beds
Smart TV

👶 TRAVELING WITH LITTLE ONES?

We've got you covered! A Pack 'n Play is provided and available for guest use, so that's one less bulky thing you have to bring along.

🚿 BATHROOM

The freshly renovated bathroom is bright, modern, and clean, with a beautiful walk-in shower for a little spa-like feel during your stay.

🧺 WASHER + DRYER

Packing light? Staying a little longer? We've got your back! A private washer and dryer are located right inside the condo and are available for guest use.  Detergent provided.

🌳 WALKABLE + CLOSE TO FORSYTH PARK

The Lemon Drop is tucked into Savannah's beautiful Victorian District, a quieter, more residential part of the city that still puts you close to the action.

You'll be just a few blocks from iconic Forsyth Park, making it easy to start your morning with a walk beneath the live oaks, visit the famous fountain, grab a coffee, or enjoy one of the many events happening in the park.

You'll also have neighborhood restaurants within walking distance! Grab a slice at Pizza Party 🍕 or head to Tacos + Tequila for tacos and margaritas. 🌮🍹

Ready to explore downtown? Savannah's downtown nightlife, restaurants, historic squares, and River Street are about a 20-minute walk away, or just a very quick Uber ride. You can also hop aboard Savannah's FREE DOT shuttle, which picks up at Forsyth Park and makes stops throughout the Historic District—an easy way to explore without worrying about driving or parking! 🚎

We love that this location gives you the best of both worlds: a peaceful neighborhood to come home to with so much of Savannah easily accessible on foot, by free shuttle, or with a quick ride. 🌳✨

🚗 PARKING

This condo does not have a dedicated off-street parking space, but there is free and easy street parking around The Lemon Drop and throughout the surrounding neighborhood. No meters, parking garages, or extra daily parking fees—find a spot and start enjoying Savannah!

🐾 PET FRIENDLY

Don't want to leave your four-legged family member behind? Bring them along! The Lemon Drop is pet friendly with an additional pet fee. 🐶💛

Please include your pet when making your reservation so the appropriate pet fee can be added to your stay.

💛 LOCALLY OWNED + MANAGED

We're Megan & Justin, Savannah locals, and we love getting to share our city with our guests! The Lemon Drop isn't managed by a big, far-away vacation rental company—you'll have local hosts who know Savannah and are nearby if you need us.

We're also always happy to share our favorite restaurants, bars, coffee shops, sightseeing spots, hidden gems, and things to do. Looking for the perfect brunch? A great cocktail? Kid-friendly activities? Something fun to do on a rainy day? Just ask! We love helping our guests experience Savannah like a local.

🍋 TRAVELING WITH A BIGGER GROUP?

The Lemon Drop is actually home to multiple completely separate vacation rentals, each with its own private entrance and no shared indoor spaces.

If you're traveling with a larger group, consider booking one or more of our other units - you can find those under our profile. It's a great way for families and friends to stay just steps from each other while still having their own bedrooms, bathrooms, kitchens, living spaces, and a little privacy!

Whether you're visiting for a weekend getaway, family vacation, girls' trip, wedding, or just because, The Lemon Drop gives you a colorful, comfortable place to call home while you experience one of our favorite cities. 🍋💛

Guest access
The entire first floor condo is all yours! You'll enter using your own coded front door and there is no shared space whatsoever.

Other things to note
Parking is free and easy on the street right out front! Just be careful for the once per week posted sweep zone times! We provide a handy little sign right on the inside of the condo door to help you with parking which reminds you where you can and cannot park each night!

Neighborhood highlights
We are located in the Victorian District of Savannah, which is the area immediately bordering the Landmark Historic District to the South. We are four blocks off of Forsyth Park. While the Victorian District has its far share of awesome new spots to check out and explore, we are also pretty close to the downtown, better known tourist locations as well! We are 1.5 miles to the riverfront area and City Market. If you want to get downtown quickly and don't want to drive, walk through the gorgeous scenic squares, or take an Uber, you can just hop on the FREE dot shuttle express trolley, with a nearby stop right on Forsyth Park!
