=== FULL DESCRIPTION ===
Our authentically Savannah, historic carriage house offers a private retreat in the heart of downtown! Perfect for a romantic getaway or solo adventure. Explore the city's rich history, museums, or take in all the scenic squares Savannah is famous for! After enjoying all our city has to offer, relax in the cozy living room, prepare a full meal in the well-equipped kitchen, or step outside into the intimate courtyard! We are so excited to host you here in the Hostess City, y’all!

SVR 02737

The space
This charming, historic carriage house is nestled on one of Savannah’s historic cobblestone lanes, steps from gorgeous Jones Street and all that downtown has to offer! Walk through the private gate and into our idyllic, sweet courtyard garden space. You’ll notice seating out there, great for a morning cup of coffee, lush greenery, and historic brick all around! Once you step inside the house, you’ll find a comfortable living room space, perfect to relax and unwind. The sofa even pulls out into a sleeper, ideal for your third guest (or just a quiet movie night in)! In the kitchen, you’ll find everything you’d need to prepare your own meals, including an awesome gas range, full sized refrigerator, and adorable table for two!

Upstairs, you’ll find the master bedroom with huge king-sized bed made up with crisp white linens, ensuring a peaceful night’s rest. The spa-like bathroom, located just off the bedroom, is freshly renovated and features a tub and shower…perfect for every preference! And check out the fixtures…a designer’s dream! Right outside the carriage house, just steps away, you will find laundry facilities, ideal for those staying with us a bit longer (or if you just prefer to pack a nice clean suitcase after your stay)!

We hope our thoughtful décor, attention to details, and excellent downtown Savannah location provide the perfect backdrop for your stay here!

Guest access
The entire carriage house is all yours and is private. The courtyard space is shared as are the laundry facilities. Access details, including navigation instructions, parking guidance, and door codes will be sent on the afternoon of your arrival, a few hours prior to your 4pm check-in time.

Other things to note
If you are bringing a pet - please add them to your guest list.  The pet fee will then be included automatically.

Parking can be tricky in downtown Savannah! The parking options nearby are paid street parking, which can either be paid using the kiosks/meters, the Park Savannah App, or by purchasing a day pass from the city. We will share all the details regarding navigating parking with you upon booking! There are also some free parking options a few blocks away that we will also give you more info about!
