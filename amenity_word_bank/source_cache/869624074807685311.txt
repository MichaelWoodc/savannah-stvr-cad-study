The LaRoche Estate on Pulaski Square is one of our most renowned Savannah vacation rentals. Recently featured in Gray Magazine, this home is a stand-out townhome on Pulaski Square. Designed centuries ago by Savannah architect Isaac LaRoche, the house is filled with hallmark Savannah charm that makes the ideal vacation backdrop.



The front of the home features the geometrically placed windows and intricate detailing that were features of LaRoche's design. Inside, exposed brickwork, soaring ceilings, graceful fireplaces, and sweeping staircases add to the home's historic feel. Stepping into this townhome truly feels like stepping back in time.



However, even though The LaRoche Estate on Pulaski Square has a time-honored design, it's still filled with modern comforts. We want our guests to be as comfortable as possible, so this home is equipped with spacious bathrooms, cutting-edge appliances, and stylish decor. The combination of vintage beauty and present-day luxury is sure to get you in the mood for exploring the rest of Savannah.
