=== FULL DESCRIPTION ===
Welcome to Dickerson House, the home of a former sea captain. This gorgeous two-bedroom, two-bathroom apartment is located directly on the parade route for Savannah's St. Patrick's Day Parade. No need for a car, you can walk to all of the best restaurants and bars from this location.

The space
Step into Savannah's rich maritime history at the Dickerson House, the former residence of Captain Henry James Dickerson, a prominent 19th-century sea captain and entrepreneur. Born in Maryland and drawn to Savannah's bustling harbor at the age of 19, Captain Dickerson rose from sailor to ship owner, building a fleet of vessels that played a vital role in the city's commerce and wartime efforts. His legacy lives on in this beautifully preserved home, situated at the vibrant corner of York and Abercorn Streets.

This stunning two-bedroom, two-bathroom apartment offers the perfect blend of historical charm and modern luxury. Located directly on Savannah’s renowned St. Patrick’s Day Parade route, the Dickerson House provides front-row seats to one of the city's most cherished traditions. From here, you’re just steps away from Savannah’s finest dining, nightlife, and cultural landmarks—no car required.

Inside, you’ll find two spacious king bedrooms, each with a private en-suite bathroom, ensuring comfort and privacy. The open-concept living, dining, and kitchen area is perfect for relaxing or entertaining, with a convenient half bath nearby. Luxury bedding, thoughtfully curated furnishings, and a private outdoor patio overlooking historic Abercorn Street enhance your stay, offering a tranquil retreat in the heart of the city.

Please note: Guests will need to be able to climb stairs to access the property. 

Stay where history meets hospitality and make unforgettable memories at the Dickerson House. Whether you’re here to explore Savannah’s storied past or to enjoy its vibrant present, this extraordinary home is ready to welcome you.

Other things to note
Parking is available at the State Street Parking Garage for a daily rate of $12. The garage is conveniently located just a two-minute walk across the square. Additionally, electric vehicle charging stations are available within the facility.
