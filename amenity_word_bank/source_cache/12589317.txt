=== FULL DESCRIPTION ===
Tucked inside the historic Bird Baldwin House in the heart of Savannah's Historic District, Bird Baldwin Magnolia offers the rare combination of authentic historic character, modern comfort, dedicated parking, and an exceptionally walkable location. Leave your car parked and spend your days wandering beneath the city's iconic oak trees, discovering hidden squares, lingering over cocktails, and returning home to a spacious, welcoming retreat designed for relaxation and connection.

The space
Whether you're planning a romantic Savannah getaway, a long weekend with a friend, or a history-filled escape, Bird Baldwin Magnolia places you in the center of everything that makes Savannah unforgettable, all while providing the comfort and convenience that transform a good trip into a truly memorable one.



Located on the ground floor of the historic Bird Baldwin House, this spacious one-bedroom condominium offers step-free access, keyless self check-in, gated entry, and a dedicated parking space directly behind the building. For travelers who appreciate both convenience and character, it is difficult to find a more desirable combination in Savannah's Historic District. From the moment you arrive, you'll feel the unique rhythm of Savannah. Step through the gated entrance, settle into your private retreat, and begin exploring one of America's most beautiful and walkable cities. Savannah Coffee Roasters sits directly next door, making it easy to start each morning with freshly roasted coffee before heading out to discover the city's museums, architecture, gardens, shops, and celebrated dining scene.



Inside, Bird Baldwin Magnolia embraces the warmth and charm that make historic Savannah accommodations so special. The spacious living area features charming architectural details, comfortable furnishings, and plenty of room to unwind after a day spent exploring. Sink into the oversized leather sectional with ottoman and enjoy a quiet evening with your favorite movie, stream a show, or simply relax and plan tomorrow's adventures. For guests who enjoy slower moments, a cozy game table invites friendly rounds of chess or checkers over a glass of wine. Whether you're returning from a ghost tour, an afternoon of shopping, or dinner at one of Savannah's renowned restaurants, the living space creates the perfect setting to reconnect, relax, and enjoy each other's company.



The fully equipped galley kitchen provides everything needed to prepare breakfast before a day of sightseeing or enjoy a relaxed dinner at home. Amenities include a full-size refrigerator, microwave, dishwasher, and two-burner cooktop. While there is no traditional full-size oven, the kitchen is thoughtfully equipped for preparing many of your favorite meals during your stay. The adjacent dining area comfortably seats four guests, creating a welcoming space to enjoy morning coffee, share meals, play cards, or map out the next day's itinerary. Many guests find that some of their favorite vacation memories happen during these simple, unhurried moments spent together.



At the end of the day, retreat to the comfortable primary bedroom, designed to provide a restful and peaceful escape. The cozy queen bed invites you to sink in after long walks through Savannah's historic streets, while the in-room television offers streaming and cable options for a quiet evening indoors. The atmosphere feels warm, comfortable, and intentionally relaxed, making it easy to settle into vacation mode. The en suite bathroom features updated finishes, a single vanity, and a beautifully tiled tub and shower combination with a glass enclosure. A stacked washer and dryer tucked conveniently into the closet allows guests to pack lighter and enjoy the practical comforts of home throughout their stay.



Just outside the rear entrance, guests will find a pair of rocking chairs and a side table near the gated parking area. While this space is not fully private, it offers a pleasant spot to enjoy a quiet cup of coffee in the morning, pause after an afternoon of sightseeing, or unwind with a drink as the evening settles over the city.



One of Bird Baldwin Magnolia's greatest advantages is undoubtedly its location. Situated on Liberty Street in the center of Savannah's Historic District, many of the city's most beloved attractions are accessible on foot, allowing you to experience Savannah the way it was meant to be experienced: slowly, intentionally, and one beautiful square at a time. Take a leisurely ten-minute walk to Forsyth Park, where you can enjoy the famous fountain, weekend farmers market, and shaded walking paths. Head ten minutes in the opposite direction to reach Broughton Street, Savannah's premier shopping district, filled with boutiques, galleries, restaurants, and local businesses. Continue toward River Street, approximately fifteen minutes away, where cobblestone streets, riverfront dining, and historic warehouses create one of Savannah's most iconic experiences.



History enthusiasts will especially appreciate the property's location. The Telfair Museum is just a five-minute walk away, while the historic Olde Pink House area, the Sorrel-Weed House Museum, and the Green-Meldrim House are all within easy walking distance. Scenic strolls through nearby Pulaski Square offer the perfect opportunity to admire Savannah's famous architecture, lush landscaping, and timeless beauty.



When it's time to dine, you'll find some of Savannah's most beloved restaurants just steps from your door. Enjoy elevated Southern cuisine at The Public Kitchen, experience a true Savannah institution at Mrs. Wilkes' Dining Room, or grab a casual meal and local beer at Crystal Beer Parlor. For evening cocktails, nearby favorites include Artillery Bar, Peregrin Rooftop, and McDonough's, each offering its own distinct Savannah atmosphere.



No matter the season, Savannah offers something special for visitors. Spring brings blooming azaleas, the world-famous St. Patrick's Day celebration, and the Savannah Music Festival. Summer invites guests to enjoy evening walks beneath moss-covered oaks, waterfront dining, and Fourth of July festivities. Fall brings cooler temperatures and beloved annual events such as the Savannah Jazz Festival, SCAD Film Festival, and Savannah Book Festival. During the winter months, visitors can experience the charm of Savannah Holly Days, the Lighted Boat Parade, and the festive River Street Christmas Market.



Whether your ideal Savannah getaway includes historic tours, ghost walks, museum visits, shopping excursions, architecture tours, fine dining, or simply slowing down and enjoying the beauty of the city, Bird Baldwin Magnolia provides the perfect home base. Comfortable, spacious, and exceptionally well located, this historic Savannah vacation rental offers something increasingly rare: the ability to experience the city's rich history and vibrant culture while enjoying the practical conveniences that make travel effortless. Park your car, settle into the rhythm of Savannah, and discover why guests return to this remarkable city again and again.



We invite you to make Bird Baldwin Magnolia part of your own Savannah story.
