=== FULL DESCRIPTION ===
A breezy Savannah hideout located in the heart of the city’s Starland neighborhood. This jewel is surrounded by other local gems, offering a walking-distance experience that is both rich in flavor and steeped in Savannah Style.

The space
Casa Topo’s southern-classic space allows guests to spread out with room enough to sleep 6, a full kitchen, and a quiet backyard.And, because it’s Savannah, Casa Topo naturally comes with a porch perfect for cool cocktails and cooler friends to share in a starry evening. Casa Topo is a space designed for those who want to experience the city as a local. We have on street parking and we hope you’ll be able to park right in front of the casa but it’s first come first serve on our street! Welcome to the neighborhood, babe.

Other things to note
We love our neighborhood but it is an urban area. You’ll see all walks of life around you. We have on street parking, please be aware of parking signs for street sweeping nights. (We have them pointed out to you on the fridge)If the concert venue is having a show, parking will get tight on the weekends. So keep your parking spot and take the DOT free trolley!
