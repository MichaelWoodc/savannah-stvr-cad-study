=== FULL DESCRIPTION ===
Slip away to your private rooftop retreat on Savannah’s iconic Jones Street~ often called “The Prettiest Street in America.”

Featured in Condé Nast, Savannah Peach Penthouse is a romantic hideaway tucked inside a historic 1853 home, just a beautiful stroll from Forsyth Park.

Wake up in a KING bed, sip morning coffee from Serena & Lily swing chairs on your PRIVATE ROOFTOP TERRACE, and take in sweeping views over Savannah.

Plus, enjoy a complimentary GOLF CART for your Tybee Island adventures.

The space
✭ WELCOME TO SAVANNAH PEACH PENTHOUSE 🍑

Perfectly perched on iconic Jones Street in the heart of Savannah’s Historic District, the Peach Penthouse is your little hideaway above the city.

Park once and wander.

Stroll beneath the oaks to Forsyth Park and Savannah’s beautiful squares. Browse the boutiques and galleries of the Downtown Design District. Mrs. Wilkes Dining Room is just across the street, the Mercer-Williams House is only steps away, and the cobblestones of River Street are waiting beyond.

This is the Savannah people come here dreaming about.

⸻

✭ YOUR PRIVATE ROOFTOP

This may just become your favorite spot in Savannah.

Begin your morning with coffee or rooftop yoga as the city wakes below. Settle into the Serena & Lily swing chairs while church bells drift through the air. Pour something lovely as the skyline turns golden at sunset, then switch on the Edison lights and linger beneath the stars.

Prefer to stay in? Fire up the electric grill and enjoy dinner with a view.

Your rooftop includes:

• Serena & Lily swing chairs
• Adirondack seating
• Sweeping Savannah skyline views
• Edison lights
• Yoga mats
• Electric grill 

And best of all ~ the rooftop is PRIVATE and exclusively yours during your stay.

🖤 “Jones St. is truly beautiful, the views and the rooftop deck are fantastic! The location is perfect for walking everywhere.” ~ Stephen, ’25

⸻

✭ SOUTHERN CHARM + THOUGHTFUL LUXURIES

Inside, historic Savannah meets a little indulgence.

Antiques and original Southern artwork give the penthouse a collected-over-time, lived-in gallery feel, while luxurious linens, plush towels and thoughtful details make it feel like a true getaway.

You’ll enjoy:

• Oversized KING bed
• Custom blackout Roman shades + drapes
• Plush towels + terry-cloth bathrobes
• Oversized walk-in shower + toiletries
• Fully equipped kitchen
• Wine cooler + Waterford wine glasses
• Keurig + drip coffee maker + French press
• Bar cart for cocktail crafting
• High-speed Wi-Fi
• 2 Smart TVs
• Dedicated workspace
• Washer + dryer
• Record player
• Games + books
• Charging stations
• Sound machine + ceiling fan

🖤 “The decor was on point ~ a total vibe! The towels were next level—better than you find in a luxury hotel. The terrace was perfect ~ the views, the breeze, and the hanging chairs.” ~ Friedhelm, ’25

⸻

✭ YOUR KING BEDROOM

After a day wandering Savannah, retreat to your oversized KING bedroom, where beautiful historic windows fill the room with morning light.

When you’re ready to sleep in, custom Roman shades + blackout drapes, a ceiling fan and sound machine make that wonderfully easy.

There’s also a Smart TV, dedicated workspace, charging stations, lighted makeup mirror, closet space and thoughtful storage.

🖤 “The bed was heavenly, the Roman shades & blackout drapes made for very restful sleep.” ~ Allie, ’24

⸻

✭ A KITCHEN MADE FOR STAYING IN

Savannah has incredible restaurants ~ but some evenings you may not want to leave the rooftop.

The kitchen is fully appointed with a full-size refrigerator, wine cooler, oven + stove, dishwasher, Calphalon cookware and Cuisinart bakeware.

And because the little things matter: Waterford wine glasses.

Because even takeout tastes better in Waterford. 🥂

⸻

✭ YOUR TYBEE ISLAND ADVENTURE

Savannah getaway + beach day? Yes, please.

Tybee Island is about a breezy 25-minute drive away, where you’ll have access to my electric Club Car golf cart to cruise the island in true vacation style.

Your complimentary Tybee perk includes:

• Electric Club Car golf cart + parking decal
• Bicycles, if you prefer two wheels
• Beach chairs + umbrella
• Cooler + beach towels
• Complimentary parking for your car

Pack your swimsuit ~ I’ve got the rest. ☀️

🖤 “We really enjoyed Tybee Island for an afternoon ~ beautiful beaches, fun to drive around the golf cart, and great, chill restaurants.” ~ Greg, ’24

⸻

✭ THE VIEW IS WORTH THE CLIMB 🍑

Savannah wasn’t built with elevators ~ and that’s part of her charm.

Your stay begins with the classic Jones Street stoop, continues up the interior staircase, and finishes with one last climb to your private penthouse sanctuary.

At the top? Your own rooftop retreat, Savannah skyline views, church steeples, sunshine and a little bit of magic.

PLEASE NOTE: This historic home is a walk-up with multiple flights of stairs and NO elevator.

I want you to know before booking so there are no surprises ~ except the good kind.

⸻

✭ A LITTLE HISTORY

Savannah Peach Penthouse is tucked inside the historic William Remshart Row House, built circa 1853 and part of Savannah’s celebrated Historic District.

William Remshart lived here with his family ~ including an astonishing 17 children.

I sometimes wonder about all the lives these walls have witnessed over the past 170+ years.

For a few days, you get to become a little part of the story.

⸻

Savannah Peach Penthouse is made for anniversaries, honeymoons, romantic escapes, birthday trips, girls’ getaways, solo adventures ~ and anyone who wants to experience Savannah from somewhere that actually feels like Savannah.

I would be honored to host your Savannah escape.
