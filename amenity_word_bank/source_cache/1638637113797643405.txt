The space
Midnight Magnolia

Welcome to Midnight Magnolia, a beautifully restored 1900 Victorian retreat in Savannah’s Historic District, just a short walk from Forsyth Park.

Designed for travelers who want to experience Savannah rather than simply visit it, this spacious 3-bedroom, 2-bath home combines original historic character with moody, modern design and the comforts of a private home.

Start your morning with coffee on the private balcony, spend the day exploring Savannah’s iconic squares, restaurants, boutiques and bars on foot, then come home to your own private backyard.

Inside, you’ll find a comfortable king suite, a queen bedroom with its own sitting area, a third bedroom, two full bathrooms, a fully equipped kitchen, in-unit washer and dryer, dedicated parking and thoughtful touches throughout.

Why guests love Midnight Magnolia:

* About an 8-minute walk to Forsyth Park
* Located in Savannah’s Historic District
* Private balcony
* Private backyard with fire pit and outdoor seating
* Dedicated off-street parking and free street parking available 
* 3 bedrooms and 2 full bathrooms
* Premium Tempur-Pedic mattresses in the primary and second bedrooms
* Fully equipped kitchen with gas range
* In-unit washer and dryer
* Dog-friendly
* Historic 1900 Victorian architecture
* Professional cleaning between every stay

Whether you’re visiting Savannah for a weekend getaway, a family trip, a celebration, a wedding, or simply a few days of exploring, Midnight Magnolia gives you the best of both worlds: the character of historic Savannah and the privacy of your own home.

The Space

Primary Suite

A comfortable private retreat featuring a premium Tempur-Pedic king mattress and a private bathroom with a beautifully tiled walk-in shower.

Second Bedroom

Third Bedroom

Living & Dining

Laundry

Guest Access

You’ll have private access to the entire second-floor home, including the living spaces, bedrooms, bathrooms, kitchen, balcony and private outdoor areas associated with Midnight Magnolia.

A smart lock provides convenient self check-in.

One dedicated off-street parking space is available near the rear entrance on the Anderson Street side of the property. Additional street parking is generally available nearby; please observe posted signs and street-sweeping restrictions.

Location

One of Midnight Magnolia’s biggest advantages is simple: you can leave the car behind.

Forsyth Park is approximately an 8-minute walk away, putting you close to Savannah’s famous historic streets, architecture, restaurants, coffee shops, bars and green spaces.

Savannah is best experienced on foot, and this location makes it easy to explore the city at your own pace before returning to your private Victorian retreat.

A Note About the Historic Home

Midnight Magnolia was built in 1900, and we’ve intentionally preserved its historic character while updating the home for modern guests.

As with many authentic historic Savannah homes, you may notice a few signs of age and character, including a slight floor variation in the hallway and occasional urban street noise.

There is also no machine dishwasher.

These details are part of what makes the home feel genuinely historic rather than newly constructed, and we want guests to know exactly what to expect before booking.

Cleaning & Comfort

Midnight Magnolia is professionally cleaned between every stay.

Fresh linens and towels are provided, and the home is thoroughly cleaned and prepared before each arrival.

Dogs

Well-behaved dogs are welcome. Please review the house rules for the specific pet requirements and fees.
