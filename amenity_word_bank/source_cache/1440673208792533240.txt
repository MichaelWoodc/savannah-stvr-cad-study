=== FULL DESCRIPTION ===
Oglethorpe Square, Savannah, 1856. Horse carriages still pass daily.

The Rosé Suite, ground floor: first to hear the hooves, first out into Savannah, front row on the square.

Skip the car: River Street one way, Broughton Street shops the other, Michelin-worthy tables between. 

Out back: a sauna, a cold plunge, and the best part of your morning.

Loved like a home, because it is one.

Bosch Huis began as a dream. Guests like you make it real. Tap the heart, see my Host Profile, or book it.

The space
THE ROSÉ SUITE
The ground floor, front of the house: one bedroom with a king bed, plush mattress, and premium linens, cozy the way an 1856 home does best. A bathroom with a walk-in shower and single vanity, fresh towels already waiting.

The square starts at your window. Carriages in the morning, gas-lit evenings a block in any direction, and the front door puts you into Savannah before other guests have found their shoes. Out back, the sauna and cold plunge wait for your return. A Nespresso machine, mini-fridge, microwave, and 55-inch smart TV with every streaming service mean the suite runs on its own schedule.

MAIN LEVEL
One flight up, two ways to get there: the front staircase, or the back one by the parking lot. A long hallway leads to the kitchen, lined with art collected over the years. Local artists, European finds, antique market treasures. Free snacks along the way, and a photo wall of past guests. Add yourselves.

The kitchen is chef-grade: gas range, All-Clad, Vitamix, KitchenAid, a Samsung refrigerator with filtered water and ice. It's shared, but every suite has its own essentials, so barely one in a hundred guests use it. Private slots on request. Guests call it "beautiful" and "private."

OUT FRONT
All of Savannah, one minute at a time: Broughton Street's shops in one, the squares, galleries, and bars in the same, the Riverfront in five, Forsyth Park in ten.

WAYS TO BOOK

PRICING
One rate covers two. Each additional guest, up to four, adds $50 per night, covering the extra cleaning and wear, plainly. That's the whole story. Pets stay for one flat fee per booking; a whole pack costs a little more. Weekdays run gentler, longer stays earn a discount. Military and teachers booking ahead: say so, and the rate says thank you.

CELEBRATIONS
Proposals, anniversaries, birthdays with a zero in them, a bachelorette claiming the whole house. We arrange what the moment needs: flowers waiting, a table no one can get, a photographer who knows the light on the square. Write to us before booking for anything beyond a quiet stay — larger gatherings are approved in advance through Airbnb, with their own guidelines.

THE HOUSE
This home has stood on Oglethorpe Square since 1856, and it acts its age in the best ways: heart-pine floors that announce your footsteps, stairs that have carried 170 years of guests, walls that hold stories and, occasionally, sound. It's not a hotel built last year, and that's the point.

THE ASSURANCES
Smoke detectors throughout. First aid stocked. Cameras outside only — never inside. The same small cleaning team, every stay. If maintenance ever touches your dates, you'll hear it from us first.

ALWAYS IMPROVING
Nearly every dollar this home generates is reinvested — into the property, our team, and the local community. We're passionate about historic houses, love Savannah wholeheartedly, and it shows in what keeps getting better. 

A SAVANNAH NOTE
