=== FULL DESCRIPTION ===
Refined Southern charm meets modern comfort in this two-bedroom, two-bathroom retreat in Savannah’s East Historic District. Sleeping up to four guests, the home features two queen beds, one twin bed, a private en suite with stand up shower, full kitchen, in-unit laundry, and a shared courtyard — all within walking distance of Forsyth Park and downtown attractions.

The space
The Artist Retreat Unit B - A Refined Historic Escape

Welcome to this beautifully appointed two-bedroom, two-bathroom apartment located in a historic Victorian-style duplex in Savannah’s charming Historic District. Designed with refined southern décor and timeless details, this spacious retreat comfortably accommodates up to four guests and offers an inviting setting for a family getaway or a weekend with friends.



The open layout creates a seamless flow between the living, dining, and kitchen areas, allowing everyone to gather comfortably while still enjoying room to spread out. Classic furnishings, soft neutral tones, and elegant accents give the home a warm, welcoming feel that reflects Savannah’s historic charm. The living room features comfortable seating centered around a large smart TV, creating the perfect place to relax after a day spent exploring the city’s cobblestone streets and picturesque squares.



The kitchen is fully equipped with full-size appliances, generous counter space, and ample storage, making meal preparation both simple and enjoyable. Whether you’re brewing coffee before a morning stroll or preparing dinner at home, you’ll have everything you need. 



Comfortable Bedrooms and Thoughtful Bathroom Layout

This apartment features two thoughtfully designed bedrooms, each offering its own sense of comfort and privacy. Two of the bedrooms include queen beds dressed in soft, high-quality linens, creating peaceful retreats at the end of the day. There is also an additional sleeping space that features a twin bed, making it a wonderful option for a child or an additional guest. The traditional yet elegant décor carries throughout each space, blending warmth with understated elegance.



One of the queen bedrooms includes a private en suite bathroom with a stand-up shower, offering added convenience. The second full bathroom is located off the hallway and features a shower and tub combination, ideal for guests who appreciate the option of a relaxing soak or for families traveling with children. Both bathrooms are finished with clean, classic details and plush towels to ensure comfort throughout your stay.



A washer and dryer are conveniently located within the apartment, making longer stays easy and stress-free.



A Peaceful Outdoor Courtyard

Step outside to enjoy the charming courtyard tucked behind the building, shared with the other unit in this historic duplex. This inviting outdoor space features comfortable seating, a dining table with an umbrella, and lush greenery that creates a quiet escape from the energy of downtown. It’s an ideal setting for sipping morning coffee, enjoying an al fresco meal, or unwinding in the evening beneath the Savannah sky.



Modern Amenities for a Seamless Stay

Keyless entry ensures a smooth and secure check-in process. The apartment includes a smart TV, a fully equipped kitchen, an in-unit washer and dryer, and access to the peaceful courtyard. With two bedrooms and two full bathrooms, the home offers both comfort and practicality for families and small groups alike.



Walkable Location

Situated in Savannah’s East Historic District, this apartment places you within easy walking distance of the city’s most beloved attractions. Forsyth Park is just about a ten-minute stroll away, offering open green space, scenic walking paths, and the iconic fountain that has become a symbol of the city. From there, you can hop on the free DOT trolley to explore even more of downtown with ease.



Nearby, Whitfield Square and Mother Matilda Beasley Park provide additional neighborhood green spaces perfect for relaxing outdoors. Local cafés, boutiques, restaurants, and historic squares are all just steps away, inviting you to experience Savannah at your own pace. Free street parking is available directly in front of the building, making arrival convenient and stress-free. Once you’re settled in, you may find you rarely need your car at all.



From its refined decor, to its exceptional walkable location, this downtown retreat offers a timeless and comfortable home base for your Savannah getaway. Whether you’re here for a long weekend or an extended stay, you’ll find everything you need for a memorable and relaxing experience.
