This is a lovely brick home with modern updates and only few steps from Forsyth Park in Historic downtown Savannah. It is 10 minutes from downtown & 20 minutes from Tybee. The home features 4 big bedrooms, 2 comfortable living areas with pullout couch and fully stocked kitchen. There are 2 Bedrooms and 1.5 BR downstairs and 2 bedroom and 1BR upstairs.
