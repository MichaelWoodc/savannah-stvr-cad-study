The space
Located in the heart of downtown Savannah, just steps from Monterey Square, Southern Belle Parlor combines historic charm with modern amenities. Enjoy easy access to Forsyth Park, local restaurants, and shopping.

Located on the second level of this four story home, this 1-bedroom, 1-bathroom apartment is rich with historic details. Accessed via an exterior staircase, guests will enjoy the beautiful features it offers, such as refinished hardwood floors and stunning views of Savannah's skyline, including the Cathedral of St. John the Baptist.

What Do We Love About Southern Belle Parlor? 

- Inviting Spaces: The charming living room has a smart TV above a decorative fireplace and a dining table for two. The kitchen is fully equipped with everything needed to prepare home cooked meals, including a microwave, stove, oven, and full-size refrigerator!

- Spacious Bedroom: The bedroom boasts a king-size bed, a smart TV, and an ensuite bathroom that offers a walk-in shower.

- Location: Southern Belle Parlor is located on East Taylor Street, near the famous Mercer-Williams House and Congregation Mickve Israel, both of which offer tours of their historic buildings. Enjoy your stay in this beautifully preserved townhome in Savannah's historic district.

Sleeping Configuration:
Bedroom 1: King Size Bed
Living Room: Queen Size Sleeper Sofa

What is parking like for this home?
Parking for this home is on-street and metered, which is enforced by the City of Savannah from Monday through Friday, 8am to 5pm. Guests may conveniently pay using the ParkSavannah app, feed the meter or parking kiosks directly, or park on a nearby non-metered street. Please take note of posted Sweep Zone signs when parking on the street.
