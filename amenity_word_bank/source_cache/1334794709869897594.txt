=== FULL DESCRIPTION ===
Escape to this charming one-bedroom apartment in the heart of Savannah's Historic District. Perfect for two, this top-floor retreat blends modern comfort with Southern charm, featuring a king bed and two cozy brick fireplaces. Located just steps from Jones Street and Forsyth Park, it's the ideal walkable base for exploring the city's history and culture. Enjoy a fully equipped kitchen and high-speed Wi-Fi for a perfect stay.

The space
Welcome to your perfect escape in the heart of Savannah’s Historic District. This beautifully designed one-bedroom apartment blends timeless Southern charm with modern comfort, offering a serene base for you to explore the city's vibrant history and culture.

Step inside and relax in the cozy living room, featuring a comfortable leather sofa, swivel chairs, and a rustic brick fireplace. The adjoining kitchen is fully equipped for your culinary adventures with updated cabinetry, stone countertops, and stainless steel appliances.

Your peaceful retreat awaits in the king suite, complete with a second brick fireplace and built-in storage. The private en-suite bathroom adds a touch of luxury with its beautifully tiled tub and shower combination, perfect for unwinding after a day of sightseeing.

Key Amenities:

Plush king-size bed
En-suite bathroom
Fully equipped kitchen with stainless steel appliances
Two cozy brick fireplaces
High-speed Wi-Fi & large television
Air conditioning & ceiling fans
In-unit washer and dryer

There is ample street parking all around our home, both paid-metered as well as free! We are happy to provide more detailed parking instructions to help guide you when you book with us. 

Whether you’re here to wander Forsyth Park, explore the historic squares, or enjoy Savannah’s culinary delights, Charming Historic District Retreat places you at the center of it all. Come experience Southern living at its finest!

Guest access
Guests will have private and exclusive access to the entire one-bedroom apartment, Unit 4. Self-check-in with a smart lock is available for your convenience, allowing you to arrive and settle in with ease at any time after check-in.
