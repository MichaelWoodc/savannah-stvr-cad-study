=== FULL DESCRIPTION ===
This beautiful first floor townhome has everything you'll need to feel like a true Savannah local. This home features a remodeled kitchen, historic architectural details, and front door access to our city's most beautiful park.

The space
Welcome to the Delia Row, a charming Savannah vacation rental located directly across from Forsyth Park in the heart of the Historic District. This beautifully furnished first-floor apartment blends timeless Southern character with modern comfort and can easily accommodate up to four Lucky guests.

Step inside and relax in the inviting living area, featuring a plush queen-size sleeper sofa, a 37" flat-screen TV, a DVD player, and a curated library of classic movies—perfect for cozy evenings in. The spacious bedroom offers a luxurious king-size bed, a comfortable seating area ideal for reading or people-watching, and a 50" television for winding down after a day of exploring Savannah’s historic streets.

Wake up refreshed and start your day with a peaceful walk across Forsyth Park to Collins Quarter at Forsyth for coffee or brunch. Later, enjoy cocktails or fine dining at Hotel Bardo and Saint Bibiana, both just minutes away. Whether you’re planning a romantic getaway, a business trip, or a longer stay in Savannah, this Delia Row residence offers an unbeatable location and all the comforts of home.

Traveling with family or friends? Ask about our additional one-bedroom homes located in the same building—ideal for group stays near Forsyth Park.

Why Lucky Savannah Loves This Home:

Prime Location: Situated directly across from Forsyth Park, Savannah’s most famous landmark and gathering place for festivals, markets, and weddings.
Free Parking: Ample non-metered street parking surrounding the home—no garages, meters, or parking headaches.

Modern Comforts: Complimentary Wi-Fi, multiple TVs, and a thoughtfully designed interior for both comfort and convenience.

Stay steps from the park and minutes from Savannah’s best restaurants, boutiques, and attractions—all while enjoying the comfort, charm, and hospitality you can only find with Lucky Savannah.

What is parking like for this home?
 This unit has free, on-street parking right outside the front door. Availability will depend on time of day and season. Our guests have not reported trouble parking near the home, even on the busiest of weekends.
 
Sleeping Configuration:

We can welcome your dog companions at this pet friendly home, but ask that you find other arrangements for cats, birds, and other furry friends.
