Welcome to Chatham Carriage

Nestled just off Chatham Square and only two blocks from Forsyth Park, this beautifully remodeled retreat offers the perfect blend of charm and convenience. You're within easy walking distance to all that Downtown Savannah has to offer.

The space
🛏️ Sleep in Comfort
The space features one Tuft & Needle memory foam bed for a restful night’s sleep, plus a comfortable full size sofa bed—ideal for couples, friends, or small families.

🍳 Cook Like a Pro
The kitchen is fully stocked with everything you need to prepare meals at home.

📺 Stream in Style
Log into your favorite apps on the smart TV and enjoy your shows just like at home.

🧼 Pamper Yourself
We provide ultra-plush towels and a complete suite of Public Goods care products—free from harmful chemicals and naturally scented with essential oils.

📲 Effortless Check-In
Our check-in and check-out process is fully digital for your convenience. Need anything? Just message us through the app and we’ll respond promptly.

🚲 Explore on Two Wheels
Two cruiser style bicycles are available for your riding pleasure. Simply message us for the lock code and enjoy Savannah on the go.

STVR Certificate: SVR-03189

Other things to note
Bikes are available for your riding pleasure. Please message us to get the lock code for use.
