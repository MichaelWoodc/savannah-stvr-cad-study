=== FULL DESCRIPTION ===
Located on Gaston Street in the Historic District, this home is just steps away from beautiful Forsyth Park.  Guests love the private courtyard with original brickwork and plenty of Savannah charm.

The space
This spacious first floor apartment is located on historic Gaston Street, and features detailed millwork and hardwood floors. With two bedrooms and two full baths, up to 6 Lucky guests will be comfortably accommodated during their stay. 

Guests will enter through the private courtyard into the sunroom, where you’ll find comfortable seating, a TV, and window seat. The sunroom doubles as a third bedroom, and when closed off from the rest of the home it has an en suite bath. The recently renovated kitchen has stainless steel appliances and every gadget you’d need to create a gourmet meal to enjoy in the adjacent dining room. 

Both bedrooms have Queen sized beds, plenty of room to unpack, and large windows that filter in the beautiful Savannah sunlight. A formal living room provides space to get some work done, or just curl up on the couch with a good book. 

For breakfast, we suggest taking a short walk to Clary’s Cafe, a local favorite and the setting for several scenes in Midnight in the Garden of Good and Evil. Just blocks from Forsyth Park, Savannah’s largest park, you’ll be well situated to attend several yearly music and food festivals as well as our weekly Saturday farmer's market. 

Why Lucky Savannah Loves this Property:
- A short walk from the Savannah Civic Center, this property is ideally located for events like SCAD graduation and concerts. 
- Plenty of non-metered parking can be found on adjacent Tattnall Street. 
 
Sleeping Configuration:
Bedroom 1: Queen Bed
Bedroom 2: Queen Bed
Sunroom: Queen Sized Murphy Bed

PARKING: Parking for this home is on-street and metered, which is enforced by the City of Savannah from Monday through Friday, 8am to 5pm. Guests may conveniently pay using the ParkSavannah app, feed the meter or parking kiosks directly, or park on a nearby non-metered street. Please take note of posted Sweep Zone signs when parking on the street.
