The space
Welcome to The Hidden Garden Retreat, a charming one-story garden apartment nestled in the heart of Savannah's Historic District. Offering convenient step-free access directly from E. Huntingdon Street, this peaceful retreat is an excellent choice for guests seeking the ease of a single-level stay, including those with mobility considerations.

Blending timeless Savannah character with modern comfort, The Hidden Garden Retreat provides a welcoming space to unwind after a day of exploring the city's historic streets, beautiful parks, and celebrated dining scene. Inside, you'll find thoughtfully appointed living spaces, a fully equipped kitchen, and inviting accommodations designed to make you feel at home, whether you're visiting for a few nights or settling in for a longer stay.

Just beyond your door, a lush shared courtyard offers a quiet escape beneath the shade of mature trees and classic Southern landscaping. It's the perfect place to enjoy your morning coffee, relax with a good book, or end the day surrounded by the peaceful charm that makes Savannah so unforgettable.

Located just a short stroll from Forsyth Park and many of the Historic District's most beloved restaurants, boutiques, galleries, and landmarks, The Hidden Garden Retreat offers the best of both worlds: a tranquil setting with effortless access to everything the city has to offer. Whether you're planning a romantic getaway, an extended visit, or simply appreciate the convenience of a one-story home with direct street-level access, you'll find The Hidden Garden Retreat to be a comfortable and memorable place to call home during your stay.
