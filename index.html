Neighbor = a shared boundary longer than 0.1 m, with no overlapping interiors. Corner contacts, across-road proximity, and neighbors of neighbors are excluded. Each unique Call Number is counted once within each focal neighborhood.

Annual mean = sum of each focal parcel’s neighborhood calls ÷ focal parcel count ÷ exposure years. Both cohorts use residential neighbors only, and the cohorts overlap. Overlapping neighborhoods can reuse the same call; a cohort sum is not a regional unique-call count.

Only definitive exact normalized parcel matches are used. Unresolved candidates are excluded. The local license inventory is a snapshot; license status during each call is not established. Past expiration dates are flagged in the license audit.

The neighbor-only numerator excludes the focal parcel entirely. A caller’s address or reporting location does not establish where an event originated, or that it was caused by an STVR. The comparison is descriptive; random controls are not matched on neighborhood or building size.
