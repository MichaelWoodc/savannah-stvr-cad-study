Beautiful 2B/2B newly constructed dublex in the walkable Thomas Square district - minutes to Starland Yard, local cafes and restaurants, and the popular Forsyth Park.
This first floor apartment boasts soaring ceilings, artwork by local artists, a bright, modern layout with all utilities and wifi included, outdoor patio with seating, and in-unit W/D. Free street parking. Short walk to Foxy Loxy Cafe, Nom Nom Poke Shop, and Strangebird. Your perfect Savannah Stay!

The space
Enjoy a comfortable, modern stay minutes from downtown Savannah. 

This spacious 2B / 2B home features a bright and clean apartment with fully equipped kitchen, WiFi,  comfortable beds, dedicated work areas, and a relaxing living room perfect for unwinding after exploring the city. 

Enjoy original art within this home by local artists —watercolors, oil paint, pencil and acrylic! All curated to specifically enhance your stay in Savannah!

We offer a 50” Smart TV in the living room, fresh linens, in-unit washer/dryer, and all utilities included. 

Guests love the convenient location with easy access to Savannah’s historic district, restaurants, shopping, hospitals, SCAD, and nearby attractions.
Whether you’re visiting for work, vacation, or an extended stay, this home is designed to feel comfortable, clean, and welcoming.
