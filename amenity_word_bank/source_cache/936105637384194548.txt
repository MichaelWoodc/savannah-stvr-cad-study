=== FULL DESCRIPTION ===
Located near the City Market and the Riverwalk and directly above the renowned Flock to the Wok and Peacock Lounge, this 2-bed, 2-bath apartment is a foodie and explorer’s dream.  Freshly updated, the space provides a comfortable stay in the heart of Savannah’s vibrant downtown. Just steps from Ellis and Johnson Square and surrounded by dining, pubs, shopping, and historical attractions, your stay will be filled with adventures. See the neighborhood section for more info! 
SVR-02792

The space
Situated on the corner of Whitaker St. and Congress Ln and above Flock to the Wok restaurant, this second-floor apartment boasts beautiful touches like original hardwood floors, an updated kitchen, chic and comfortable furniture, and space for up to four guests.
Enter the apartment through the private entrance to the right of Flock the Wok. Through the apartment doors, you will enter the main space that features an open concept from the kitchen to the living room. High ceilings with oversized windows fill the room with natural light.
Enjoy a fully appointed kitchen with all-new appliances, granite countertops, and everything you need to cook during your stay. We have you covered with pots, pans, kitchen utensils, and dishware. Brew your morning coffee with the coffee provided.
In the living room, unwind and relax on the oversize sofa and two reading chairs. A new smart TV and high-speed WIFI are ready to stream your favorite shows after a day of exploring.
 Finally, on the primary floor, you will find a stackable washer and dryer in the closet for your use and a powder room with a toilet and pedestal sink.
Follow the long staircase to the third floor, where two ensuites boast private bathrooms. Both ensuites are outfitted with new comfortable queen mattresses wrapped in crisp linens and plush pillows, providing a perfect place to rest. Closets and dressers provide ample storage for extended stays.
The bathrooms feature a shower/tub combination in one and the other with a low-entry shower. Both bathrooms are outfitted with essentials, including towels, toilet paper, shampoo, body wash, and a hair dryer. 
Are you working remotely during your stay? Our WIFI supports video conferencing and multi-tasking to get the job done.

Guest access
*We have a parking pass for the Whitaker Street Garage, located just a block from the building :)* 
The entire condo unit is for your use! Please be cognizant of your neighbors as there are 3 condo units in this building and an amazing restaurant and bar downstairs.

Neighborhood highlights
Being upstairs neighbors to Flock the Wok and Peacock Lounge, you won't run out of delicious and convenient cocktails! Looking for that easy and incredibly delicious dining option? Your downstairs neighbor, Flock the Wok, provides a stylish environment with incredible Asian food ranging from seafood wok, dumplings, noodles, and more.
Continue to explore all of the flavors Savannah has to offer; all just steps from the Loft. Enjoy French-inspired dishes at Circa 1875, grab a traditional southern dish at The Lady & Sons, or crack a few oysters at Sorry Charlie’s Oyster Bar. Breakfast is easy and delicious by checking out Treylor Park Diner or the Little Duck Diner.
Enjoy Savannah's incredible nightlife with a stop in the Peacock Lounge, Totally Awesome Bar, Jens’s and Friends, Moon River Brewing, and many more. Transport to London by stopping into Six Pence Pub, a British-inspired pub, or bring out your inner Irish with a stop into O’Connell’s Irish Pub.
A trip to Savannah is complete with exploring a little of its historic colonial past. Fill your schedule by checking out the Telfair Academy, a beautiful art museum, or stroll through the Webb Military Museum. Relive the prohibition area by heading to the American Prohibition Museum. Don’t forget to grab a drink at their cocktail bar to celebrate freedom. Or fancy exploring Savannah’s ocean ties? Check out the Ships of the Sea Maritime Museum.
Not enough time to walk all the sights of Savannah? Take it all in at once while riding or biking. We are just minutes from Old Town Trolley Tours of Savannah and Savannah Bike Tours. Check out the city’s skyline from the water by jumping on one of the Savannah Belle’s ferries, with multiple stops all along Savannah’s riverfront.
Getting around is easy with walkable streets, bike rentals, and Uber service. Jump on Savannah’s public transit system with the Whitaker and Broughton WB stop just a 1-minute walk from the loft.
