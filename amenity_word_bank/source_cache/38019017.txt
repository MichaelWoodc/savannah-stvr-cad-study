=== FULL DESCRIPTION ===
If you're looking for one of our cozy Savannah Georgia Vacation Rentals, then you may want to linger on the Crawford Carriage House property. This charming and cheerful carriage house includes one bedroom and one bathroom with sleeping room for two guests. This is one of our Vacation Rentals Savannah GA that would be perfect for a couple. The bedroom features one queen bed, and the bathroom offers a walk-in shower. There is also a full kitchen in the unit with a dishwasher for your convenience.

The space
You won't find boring houses with our Savannah vacation homes. We always employ creative and beautiful design techniques to make you feel right at home. This carriage house is light and bright with lots of windows, hardwood floors and a largely white decor scheme. The living room features a beautiful loveseat, which is perfect for two people. The bed and dresser are immediately adjacent in a studio arrangement. Nearby, you'll also find the kitchen, which makes the most of the cozy space with a three-seat breakfast bar and full-size appliances. Like all of our Savannah Georgia Vacation Rentals, we provide a number of extra niceties to make your stay easier, including all linens, towels, utensils, dishes, paper towels and more.



Start exploring Savannah from your Vacation Rentals Savannah GA just by walking out the door. This property's location between East Liberty and East Oglethorpe puts it right near many interesting places. Probably the top one is Colonial Park Cemetery, which is just a short walk to the west down East Perry Lane. This historic cemetery opened in 1750 and became the final home to many of the city's earliest residents. In 1820, many victims of yellow fever ended up in this cemetery. The cemetery was closed to new burials before the beginning of the Civil War. It now has the appearance of a stately park with Spanish moss dripping from the old oaks. Not surprisingly, it's also a hot spot on many of the ghost tours in Savannah. If you're really brave, at least one tour will take you through at night!



If you head to the north of this property, you'll find more historic squares in the form of Oglethorpe Square, Columbia Square and Greene Square. The Owens-Thomas House and the Gutstein Gallery by the Savannah College of Art and Design are also in this area. To the south of this property, you'll find the amazing gothic architecture of the Cathedral of Saint John the Baptist, as well as the Andrew Low House Museum and a few more historic squares. Although you can definitely walk to places further away, you may wish to enlist the Old Town Trolley Tour or Chatham Area Transit to get to River Street on the north end of the Historic District and Forsyth Park at the south end.



If you have some extra time staying in our Savannah vacation homes, then you might want to take the 30-minute trip out to Tybee Island for some fun in the sand and surf. Tybee Island is an amazing and idyllic spot that is small on land but big on adventure. Relax on the Atlantic beaches, then take in some cute beach shops and then explore a few of the waterfront restaurants. Tybee is an easy day trip if you just want to soak in rays and saltwater. However, you can also make it just as adventurous as your Savannah excursions. Tybee offers a bevy of things to do, from ecology marsh walks, watercraft rentals, fishing and much more. Whatever you do, you won't want to miss the two main attractions in the Marine Science Center and Tybee Lighthouse. You can find the latter on the north end of the island and the former on the southeast side. Tybee is easy to walk around, or you can rent a bike and see the island that way. The options are endless.
