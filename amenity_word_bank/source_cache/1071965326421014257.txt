=== FULL DESCRIPTION ===
Two-story apartment features a gorgeous living room, large windows over looking Chatham Square, a dining room and spacious kitchen, 2 1/2 half baths & laundry.  On the 2nd floor are 2 bedrooms with en-suite bathrooms.

The space
Wake up to views of Chatham Square from the master bedroom. Enjoy the en-suite bathroom with double shower heads, twin vanities, and adjoining walk-in closet. The kitchen is outfitted for cooking meals at home or plating a nice take out meal. 
The private, rear balcony looks over the walled courtyard and fountain. 

This historic townhouse was painstakingly restored and decorated with comfort & charm in mind and the best part is the central location - walking distance to everything in the Historic District!

NOTE: This is is a 3-story suite with one flight of stairs going up to the entrance, then one flight up to the living room/kitchen floor,  then one flight of stairs up to the bedrooms. 

HISTORIC GORDON ROW: Gordon Row was built in two phases between 1852 and 1854. In the first phase, the 3 or 4 houses on each end of the block were built and in the second phase the middle portion was built. There are a total of 15 houses in the row.

The houses were built in the Federalist (US) or Regency Style (UK). Each house is four stories high and contains about 3,300 square feet. The Federalist Style emphasizes square, classical features, plain decorations and mirror image rooms.

The Row was speculatively built; meaning that the homes were built as a lot then sold rather than individually designed for the owners. As a result, each of the 15 houses was nearly identical.

When built originally, the cost per house was between $3,500 and $4,000. The market was country planters, or farmers, who wanted a house in town for their visits to Savannah.

When originally built, the kitchens were on the ground level. The house was only two rooms deep with matching, or mirroring, windows facing the front and the back porch.
