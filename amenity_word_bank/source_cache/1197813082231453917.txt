Perfect One-Bedroom Getaway in Historic Savannah

Discover the charm and elegance of Savannah with a stay at the Monterey Square Carriage House, a delightful one-bedroom, one-bathroom vacation rental between the iconic Armstrong Kessler Mansion and the Mercer Williams House. Situated on one of Savannah’s most beautiful squares, this luxurious carriage house offers a truly unforgettable experience for those seeking comfort and convenience in the heart of the city.

SVR-03162

The space
The bedroom is located on the ground level and features a cozy queen bed and high-end furnishings. Upstairs, you’ll find the sophisticated living room thoughtfully designed to capture the historic essence of the home. The fully equipped kitchen has everything you need to prepare your favorite meals. The unit also includes a washer and dryer for your convenience.

Step outside onto your private balcony overlooking a tranquil garden, where you can enjoy a peaceful morning coffee or unwind with a glass of wine in the evening. The serene setting, surrounded by Spanish moss-draped mansions and historic monuments, creates the perfect backdrop for relaxation.

Nearby, explore Savannah’s rich history with visits to The Sorrel-Weed House, Green-Meldrim House, and the Mercer Williams House Museum. The famous Fountain at Forsyth Park is just steps away. Broughton Street is just a short walk from shopping and dining, and City Market and River Street are a 15-minute stroll from the home.

Property Highlights:

- One-bedroom carriage house with queen bed
- Full bath with tub/shower combo and vanity
- Fully equipped kitchen and in-unit washer/dryer
- Private balcony overlooking a serene garden
- Steps from Monterey Square, Forsyth Park, and other historic sites
- Convenient access to shopping, dining, and Savannah’s famous River Street

Important Information:

- Maximum occupancy: 2 guests (strictly enforced, no exceptions, including infants/children)
- Minimum age to reserve: 25 years old (no exceptions)
- NO PARTIES, NO BACHELORETTE/BACHELOR GROUPS
- No smoking or vaping allowed
- Pets Welcome

Other things to note
Parking is conveniently available on the street and metered at this location. Finding parking spots on Bull St or Monterey Square is easily achievable. These areas are within a short walking distance from the property
Just so you know, parking enforcement by the City of Savannah is effective from Monday to Friday, 8 AM to 5 PM, while parking is free on the weekends from Friday at 5 PM to Monday at 8 AM.
To handle meters, purchasing a "Visitors Parking Day Pass" for all day/night parking is recommended. Travelers coming with a vehicle can buy a City of Savannah Day Pass for $24 for 48 hours or $15 for 24 hours. These passes are available at the Visitors Information Center or the Parking Services office at 100 East Bryan Street (Monday - Friday, 8 AM - 4:30 PM). Additionally, the Park Savannah app simplifies the payment process for weekday parking.
The nearest non-metered free public street parking can be found 2 blocks south of this location. Guests are advised to keep an eye out for posted Sweep Zone signs.
