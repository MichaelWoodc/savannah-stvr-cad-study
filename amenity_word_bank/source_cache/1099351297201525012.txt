=== FULL DESCRIPTION ===
Experience Savannah's rich history in this 3-bed, 3-bath gem by architect Alfred S. Eichberg. The fully renovated and beautifully decorated unit offers a custom kitchen, shared porch, spacious bedrooms, and historic charm. Featuring a spacious design perfect for six guests, The Alfred is ideal for unwinding after a day of adventure. Just 2 mins to Forsyth Park, 20 from SAV International.
SVR-01316

The space
Constructed in 1891 and believed to be one of the four remaining residential structures designed by the renowned architect Alfred S. Eichberg, this unit is truly a piece of Savannah's history. Originally born in New York, Eichberg moved to Georgia and was commissioned on notable structures, including the Telfair Hospital, New Hanover County Courthouse (NC), and Eichberg Hall of the Savannah College of Art and Design.  Known for his Romanesque revival style of design, you’ll find incredible detail and architectural aspects throughout the Alfred Inn.
Located on the mid-level of the entire residential home that is now a quad-plex, the unit provides a spacious design, high ceilings, and historic touches with fresh modern updates.
Enjoy pure comfort at The Alfred. Sink into the oversized sectional in the living room, perfect for unwinding as you stream your favorite shows on the new TV. Transition seamlessly into the fully custom kitchen, with new appliances, granite countertops, and all the culinary essentials for crafting meals. Gather around the dining table set for six.
This elegant space features two ensuite bedrooms and a third bedroom with an adjacent full bathroom. Each bedroom features new furnishings, including comfortable beds and TVs. The main suite is accented by stunning bay windows framing a new queen mattress with fresh linens. At the same time, the second and third bedrooms offer queen mattresses and antique touches, adding to the historical ambiance.
Vaulted ceilings and oversized windows flood the rooms with natural light, creating an open and airy atmosphere. With three bathrooms, ample space is provided for your convenience. Each bathroom has been tastefully updated with new vanities, fixtures, and custom tile work, offering a blend of modern luxury and historic charm. Enjoy a rejuvenating step-in shower or a relaxing shower/tub combination, with the powder room conveniently located in the front hall. All bathrooms have towels, body wash, and shampoo, ensuring a seamless stay.

Guest access
The entire space is set up for your use! Please feel free to enjoy the rear deck/balcony just be sure to keep noise levels to a minimum from 10PM - 8AM. You may park one vehicle in the rear parking area behind the building (kindly stay on the left side).

Other things to note
This is part of a quadplex, so please be courteous to your neighbors and keep noise levels to a minimum from 10PM - 8AM.

Neighborhood highlights
Ideally located in the Victorian District East, you will be in the heart of historic Savannah and centrally located to all the incredible eats, drinks, and sights Savannah has to offer. From your door, the southern portion of Forsyth Park is just a 2-minute walk. On your way, grab a delicious cup of coffee at the Sentient Bean or Alexander’s Bistro, featuring fair trade coffee and lattes. Need a breakfast bite to start your day? Pick up some great American-style breakfast with a retro feel at Betty Bombers Diner.
Looking for a sandwich and a pint? Stroll on down to the Black Rabbit, a small yet incredible sandwich shop by day pub by night. Need a taco fix? Try out Bull Street Tacos for scratch-made Mexican dishes.
Looking for fine dining and a great vibe? Head on over to Local 11 Ten and their chic rooftop bar and lounge known as The Perch. Enjoy handcrafted cocktails and delicious dishes. The Perch is open year-round and features gas heaters for cooler winter days.

Take an adventure outside of just the neighborhood by visiting one of Savannah’s many museums. Mercer-William’s House Museum, Savannah African Art Museum, American Prohibition Museum, and many more are just minutes away. Tap into Savannah's artistic nature by stopping into its College of Art and Design Museum of Art. Want to get more hands-on and make some memories? Try blowing glass at Hostess City Hot Glass; it's a perfect time for the whole family.
Not enough time to walk all the sights of Savannah? Take it all in at once while riding or biking. We are just minutes away from Old Town Trolley Tours of Savannah and Savannah Bike Tours. Check out the city’s skyline from the water by jumping on one of the Savannah Belle’s ferries, with multiple stops all along Savannah’s riverfront.
