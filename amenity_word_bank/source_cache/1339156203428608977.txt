=== FULL DESCRIPTION ===
Incredible location and views from this unique property, located on the St. Patty's Day Parade route and a short 2 minute walk from Broughton St and all shopping/dining. Six ensuite king beds and 4 queen beds plus one pullout couch await your group in this large, historic home. Soak in Savannah's rich history and enjoy this unbelievably well preserved historic mansion.

The space
Welcome to The Southern Sailor, a historic retreat built in 1853 by Captain Henry James Dickerson, a key figure in Savannah’s maritime history.

This unique property seamlessly combines four charming apartments into one expansive home, offering 10 bedrooms and 10 bathrooms. Perfect for large groups, family reunions, or small retreats, The Southern Sailor comfortably accommodates up to 24 guests, providing plenty of space to gather, relax, and enjoy Savannah’s timeless beauty.

Located in the heart of Savannah’s Historic District, this home offers an incredibly walkable location where no car is needed. Simply Uber from the airport and explore the city on foot, from historic squares and landmarks to world-class dining and shopping. Positioned on the St. Patrick’s Day parade route, The Southern Sailor puts you at the center of one of Savannah’s most celebrated traditions.

Inside, you’ll find a stunning blend of historic charm and modern amenities, with exposed brick interiors and panoramic views of Savannah’s skyline. Whether you’re planning a special celebration, a peaceful retreat, or a memorable group getaway, The Southern Sailor provides a truly unique and inviting space to create unforgettable memories.

All king bedrooms (total of 6) are ensuite for incredible privacy and comfort. Enjoy luxury linens on each bed. All four apartments of the main home are combined to form this unique listing, perfect for groups of any size. Guests will enjoy the extremely walkable location as well as 4 kitchens, 3 outdoor balconies (one rooftop) and even a movie room. Let the Souther Sailor create an amazing backdrop to your unforgettable vacation in Savannah.
