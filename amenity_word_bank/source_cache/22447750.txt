=== FULL DESCRIPTION ===
Historic charm abounds in this 2 bedroom, 2.5 bath home. With a living room with sleeper sofa, separate dining room, and huge kitchen with tons of light you'll feel right at home for your stay in Savannah.

The space
This beautiful home is only minutes away from historic River Street, bustling Broughton Street, and within steps to Mother Mathilda Beasly Dog Park. With a living room with sleeper sofa, separate dining room, and spacious light-filled kitchen, you'll feel right at home for your stay in Savannah.

The parlor, which opens to the dining room, will be to your left when you enter the foyer. A door in the dining room leads to a private deck, the perfect quiet spot to enjoy a cup of coffee as you start you day. Inside, just off of the dining room is the elevated kitchen, which features an ample amount of counter space and everything you need to enjoy an evening in. The home's half bath is neatly tucked away under the staircase. 

At the top of the stairs you’ll find the laundry room and a subway tile shower/tub combination, with a large vanity, that boasts with light. Each bedroom features a king size bed, large smart television, and plenty of storage for your belongings. The main bedroom, facing the front of the home overlooking the tree-lined streets, additionally features a bright and airy ensuite bathroom with walk-in shower.

In the morning, stroll down the cobblestones of Jones Street, dubbed “America’s prettiest street”  by Southern Living Magazine, for breakfast at Clary’s Cafe, the setting for several breakfast scenes in Midnight in the Garden of Good and Evil. From there, you’ll be steps away from the Cathedral of St. John the Baptist, which underwent a major restoration to preserve their beautiful stained glass windows and architectural integrity. 
 
What is parking like for this home?
Non-metered Parking
This unit has free, on-street parking right outside the front door. Availability will depend on time of day and season. Our guests have not reported trouble parking near the home, even on the busiest of weekends.

Sleeping Configuration:

Bedroom 2: King Bed
Living Room: Queen Sleeper Sofa

We can welcome your dog companions at this pet friendly home, but ask that you find other arrangements for cats, birds, and other furry friends. There is a $130 pet fee (non-refundable) with a 35 lb. maximum weight limit per dog and a limit of 2 dogs per reservation. Some homes may allow larger dogs, please inquire for more information.
