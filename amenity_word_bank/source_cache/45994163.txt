=== FULL DESCRIPTION ===
Welcome to your top-floor luxury retreat in the heart of Savannah's Historic District! This beautifully restored loft combines historic charm with modern comfort, featuring exposed brick walls, soaring ceilings, and abundant natural light. Located on iconic Broughton Street, you'll be just steps from boutique shopping, award-winning restaurants, City Market, River Street, the Plant Riverside District, and the very best of Savannah, all within easy walking distance!

The space
From the moment you walk inside, you'll appreciate the character that makes this loft so special. Original exposed brick walls, beautifully restored hardwood floors, soaring ceilings, exposed ductwork, and abundant natural light create a bright, inviting space that feels both historic and modern.

Thoughtfully designed for couples, solo travelers, or a weekend getaway with friends, the open-concept studio offers everything you need for a comfortable stay while making the most of its unique loft layout.

Highlights You'll Love

• Top-floor historic loft with elevator access
• Right on iconic Broughton Street
• Walk to City Market, River Street & Plant Riverside
• Exposed brick, hardwood floors & industrial details
• Queen sleeper sofa
• Fully equipped kitchen
• High-speed Wi-Fi & Smart TV
• In-unit washer/dryer combo
• Ceiling fans in both the living area and bedroom
• Complimentary sound machine for added comfort
• Curated Hosting Savannah digital guidebook

After a day exploring Savannah's cobblestone streets and beautiful squares, settle into the cozy living area with your favorite movie, relax with a glass of wine, or simply watch the city come alive outside your windows.

The fully equipped kitchen includes everything you need to prepare breakfast before heading out or enjoy a quiet dinner at home. A Keurig coffee maker with complimentary coffee is ready to help start your mornings.

When it's time to unwind, sink into the plush queen memory foam mattress with fresh linens for a restful night's sleep. The queen sleeper sofa also features a premium thickness Koala sleeper sofa, providing comfortable accommodations for additional guests.

The bathroom is stocked with fresh towels, complimentary toiletries, and everything you need to settle in comfortably. For longer stays, you'll also appreciate the convenience of the in-unit washer and dryer.

Please note this is an open-concept studio. The sleeping area does not have a separate bedroom door. A Pack 'n Play is available

We cannot wait to host you!

SVR 02190

Guest access
Guests will enjoy exclusive access to the entire condo throughout their stay.

Upon arrival, you'll enter through the secure entrance of the historic building before taking the elevator to the top floor. From there, it's just a short walk down the hallway to your private condo entrance. Please note there are two small set of seven to ten stairs in the hallway.

Parking is not included with the reservation.

Other things to note
Because you're staying in the heart of downtown Savannah, you'll experience the vibrant energy that makes this neighborhood so special. Restaurants, bars, and nightlife are just outside your door, particularly on Friday and Saturday evenings, so some city noise should be expected.

To help ensure a restful night's sleep, we've provided a sound machine for your comfort.
