=== FULL DESCRIPTION ===
Stay in a beautifully preserved 1892 Victorian landmark just 3 blocks from Forsyth Park. This home blends historic Savannah charm with modern comfort, featuring two king bedrooms, a queen sleeper sofa, spacious living areas, original architectural details, and a fully equipped kitchen. Relax on the front porch swing, unwind in the shared courtyard, and enjoy the convenience of private off-street parking, high-speed Wi-Fi, and a walkable location near Savannah’s best dining and attractions.

The space
The Hall House | Walk to Forsyth Park

Welcome to The Hall House, a beautifully preserved historic Savannah residence just steps from Forsyth Park.

This spacious first-floor residence blends timeless architectural character with modern comfort, offering soaring ceilings, original heart pine floors, decorative fireplaces, elegant chandeliers, and gracious rooms designed for gathering and relaxation. Whether you’re visiting Savannah for a weekend getaway, wedding celebration, family vacation, or extended stay, The Hall House offers an authentic experience in one of the city’s most beloved historic neighborhoods.

Located in the heart of Savannah’s Historic District, you’ll be within easy walking distance of Forsyth Park, acclaimed restaurants, coffee shops, boutiques, galleries, and many of the city’s most iconic squares and landmarks.

The Space

Originally built in 1892, The Hall House showcases the charm and craftsmanship that make Savannah’s historic homes so special. Tall ceilings, oversized rooms, original heart pine floors, decorative fireplaces, and beautiful architectural details create a sense of space and character rarely found in modern accommodations.

The elegant living and dining room serves as the heart of the home, featuring comfortable seating, a decorative fireplace, a large Smart TV, and a formal dining area perfect for gathering after a day exploring the city.

The fully equipped kitchen includes everything needed to prepare meals during your stay, whether you’re enjoying a quiet breakfast before heading to Forsyth Park or gathering with family and friends for dinner.
