=== FULL DESCRIPTION ===
Stay within short walking distance to Savannah's most notable historic downtown attractions! This two-story clapboard house built in 1862 can host up to six Lucky guests and has ample parking for three cars!

The space
Guests looking for a truly local experience will fall in love with Blue on Broughton's historic charm. Nestled in the up-and-coming, East end of Broughton Street, this two-bedroom house built in 1862 is adorned with cast iron details and blue-painted clapboard siding. Inside, a beautifully appointed vacation rental with all the modern amenities expected to make your stay comfortable.

The first floor boasts a converted parlor room with Queen bed, and an adjacent living room. These guests can use the nearby half bath, making getting ready a breeze. A full kitchen, equipped with every essential you'd need for a home-cooked meal, is just around the corner and has ample storage space for extended stays. A washer/dryer are also inside the home - great for young families!

Upstairs, find two bedrooms with two full baths. The first bedroom features a King size bed, with the second bedroom furnished with a Queen. Both bedrooms have their own dressers, flat screen TVs, and en suite baths.

Park your car in the private lot behind the home and explore Savannah by foot for the remainder of your stay. Blue on Broughton is located on the East end of Broughton Street: Savannah's "main" street scattered with some of the best shopping and dining in town. Most notably, guests are encouraged to stop by local favorites: Capital Bee Co., Hygge Savannah, East End Provisions, and Leopold's Ice Cream.

What is parking like for this home?
Guests receive private parking for 3 vehicles located behind the home, off-street and secure. Additional vehicles can find nearby metered parking on Broughton Street, which is enforced by the City of Savannah Monday through Saturday from 8am to 8pm. Guests can purchase a pre-paid parking pass from the Savannah Visitor's Center to avoid handling the meters.
