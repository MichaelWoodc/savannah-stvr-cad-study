=== FULL DESCRIPTION ===
Tastefully modern garden-level apartment right off of the beautiful, historic Jones Street. The owner of this unit is a famous Emmy winning Director, who just recently had the whole place remodeled. It is very clean, modern and tastefully decorated. We are right in the heart of the Historic District with some of the most iconic restaurants and shops just a stone's throw away. We offer free WiFi, a Smart TV, washer/dryer and plenty of lounge space for a perfect, relaxing getaway!

SVR-02083

The space
We offer a very spacious living/dining area with plenty of seating. We have a generously sized kitchen that is fully equipped with all standard-sized appliances for those who like to cook. Includes a drip coffee maker for your use and we stock coffee and fixings to go with it. The queen bedroom has a dresser and a walk-in closet for those who like to pack a bit on the heavier side! The bathroom has a walk-in glass shower with modern tile. If you need to do laundry while you are here, there is a washer/dryer in the closet for your use :)

Guest access
You may use anything in the apartment that suits your fancy :) as we stated above, we have a free washer/dryer for your use in the closet by the bathroom.
**NEW PATIO ACCESS OFF THE BEDROOM**

Other things to note
There are residents that live above, next door, in front, etc. (many of whom live permanently) so we just ask that you are respectful and courteous to your neighbors.
