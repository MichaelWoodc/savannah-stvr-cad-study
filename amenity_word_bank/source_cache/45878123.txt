=== FULL DESCRIPTION ===
Built in 1900, this large Victorian home has been completely renovated top to bottom. Your party will enjoy being just a short walk to Forsyth park, or a few blocks to be a part of the action offered in historic downtown Savannah.

The space
This conveniently located four bedroom, two bath, Victorian Home offers a historic feel and an ample amount of space for your entire party. Only located three blocks from Forsyth Park, walking distance to downtown attractions, and easy access to I-16. Parade routes and race routes are as close as 1-4 blocks depending on the event. This home has been divided up into two apartments, perfect for those with family members that have difficulty with stairs. 

Each floor features a spacious living room, a fully-stocked kitchen, dedicated dining space, two bedrooms, and one full bath. Downstairs offers a poker table, ideal for a game night in with friends or family and is open to the kitchen, perfect for entertaining! High ceilings, hardwood floors and original fireplaces adorn the space with all the character and charm you'd expect to see in a Victorian mansion. Yet modern amenities abound with everything from central heat and air condition, tv and wifi so you can stay up to date on all your favorite streaming shows. 

The property is only three blocks from Forsyth Park which is one of Savannah's largest and most active. Forsyth is over 30 acres of green space, walking paths and the beautiful moss draped live oak trees Savannah is known for. Around the park you'll find boutiques, coffee shops and even an organic grocery. If you happen to be visiting on the weekend, Forsyth hosts the local farmer's market every Saturday morning and sometimes there's even a concert in the bandshell.

What is parking like for this home?
The Montgomery House has plenty of free street parking and the city offers a free shuttle from Forsyth to downtown Savannah every 10 minutes. It's easy to leave your car at the house and explore all that Savannah has to offer!
