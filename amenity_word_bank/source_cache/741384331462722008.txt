=== FULL DESCRIPTION ===
Featured on HGTV Magazine's "A Local's Guide to Savannah"! Heidi's Hideaway is a wonderful retreat located in Historic Savannah within walking distance to great shopping, dining, museums, and more! It's a wonderful choice for guests seeking Savannah vacation rentals with room for up to six people. This two-bedroom, one-bathroom charmer boasts a vibrant interior full of cozy décor. Once settled in, guests enjoy a shared courtyard with seating on the deck in the back of the home.

The space
Inside the Cottage



The main living area features an airy, open floor plan that maximizes the space. Gleaming hardwood flooring and a unique, two-sided brick fireplace suggest the charm of yesteryear while the sofa and matching armchairs add a contemporary vibe. The patterned area rug picks up the bold contrasts of throw pillows, and an oval coffee table adds a warm wood centerpiece. Artful lighting and mirrors create a sense of expanse. A wall-mounted television is great for checking on the weather or watching your favorite shows.



Just beyond the living room, the dining area features a table and chairs, the flip side of the fireplace and a tapestry above. In the adjacent kitchen, you will find everything you need to create meals for up to four. Like we do in all our Savannah vacation homes, we’ve filled the drawers and cupboards with flatware, glasses, dishes and cookware for your use. There’s a refrigerator/freezer, range and oven, dishwasher and microwave too. You’ll even find convenient coking basics, like salt and pepper, near to hand.



A bedroom with a queen-sized bed opens off the living room. Glass-topped bedside tables and reading lamps anticipate your nighttime comfort. The second bedroom boasts a king-sized bed. A chest of drawers offers clothes storage. The sofa sleeper in the living room accommodates two more guests.



The bathroom has a single vanity and a tub with a shower. A handy washer and dryer for your laundry are tucked neatly in the corner. We stock a good supply of soft, fluffy towels at all our vacation rentals Savannah GA, and Heidi's Hideaway is no exception. We also provide bed linens, pillows and blankets.



A cute, shared sundeck in back features generous seating and a charcoal grill. A privacy fence ensures seclusion as you enjoy the fresh air and Savannah sunshine. Additional amenities at Heidi's Hideaway include central air and heating, a selection of toiletries and Wi-Fi. As Savannah vacation rentals guests, you get unlimited access to our private swimming pool, just a scenic drive away on Tybee Island.



Experience Savannah



Heidi's Hideaway is just a stone’s throw from Mother Mathilda Beasley Park and Dog Park, so if children or pets are part of your group, they won’t lack for activities. Named for a 19th-century Catholic nun who was born into slavery, the park is a lasting tribute to her legacy. After becoming free and moving to Savannah, Beasley worked as a dressmaker to earn a living. She also ran a school for black children from 1850 to 1860, which was at odds with the law. The park features a playground for the kids, a sports field and a fenced-off dog park for your favorite pooch.



You also have the option to visit Forsyth Park, an easy walk from the cottage through tree-lined avenues. Experience the year-round Saturday Farmers’ Market where you’ll find fresh produce, baked goods and local crafts. Forsyth features two playgrounds, a wide expanse of sports fields, the Fragrant Garden for the Blind and a fabulous fountain. This fountain is the centerpiece of the park. People gather there for weddings, group pictures and to cool down in the refreshing spray.



You can grab a quick bite at the park’s café before embarking on the free shuttle bus, which travels throughout Savannah’s Historic District. The bus stops at each of 22 squares in the district and at shopping hubs like City Market. You’ll find plenty to do at the market, from shopping to eating. You can see local artists at work or listen to live music. You’re sure to meet other guests from vacation rentals Savannah GA along the way.



Trade the bus for a pedicab or horse-drawn carriage as you continue your exploration of Savannah. Make your way to the riverfront and watch the big freighters come and go. Savannah is one of the busiest ports in the country.



You will also find more shopping ops in the converted cotton warehouses along Riverwalk. Stop for a refreshing beverage or enjoy a fresh seafood meal at popular restaurants like Vic’s on the River or The Shrimp Factory. Experience Savannah’s seagoing flavor at The Pirate House.
