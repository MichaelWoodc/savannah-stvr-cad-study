This property welcomes you in with a cozy living room. A plush sofa, area rug, wood floor and a large flatscreen are all in this room. One of the best things about our Savannah vacation homes is how you can simply walk outside your door and immediately be a part of the downtown action. Walking the streets of Savannah is typically the best way to get around, but you can also enlist the services of the Chatham Area Transit in the downtown area, or you can hop on a tour. You can walk the entire concourse, watch ships come into the port, or hop on a charter, or riverboat tour out onto the river.



It's hard to run out of things to do near our Savannah vacation homes. If you get tired of exploring the downtown area, it's only a quick half hour drive to Tybee Island and the warm Atlantic beaches. Tybee Island is a popular destination for our Savannah guests, and it's easy to see why. Spend a day lounging on the beach, or visit the lighthouse at the north end of the island, or book a charter to explore the waters, watch dolphins, or catch fish. The activities on Tybee Island are almost as endless as Savannah itself. Atlantic beaches are located on the east side of the island, and the river beaches are located on the west side.
