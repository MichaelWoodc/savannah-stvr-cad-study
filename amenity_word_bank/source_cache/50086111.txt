=== FULL DESCRIPTION ===
Tall ceilings and windows flooded with natural light make Forsyth Artist Retreat a distinctively enjoyable vacation home. This Savannah townhome has an open living plan. A large dining, living room, and kitchen area stretches across the ground floor while a loft overlooks the living space. This main area provides a gathering space for your whole vacation group while separate bedrooms branch off to give you a little privacy.

The space
This sleekly updated home is the perfect backdrop to a vacation. Warm natural wood and soft cream hues combine to provide a restful ambiance in the main living space. This theme continues in the bedroom, where light shades of grey, white, and tan are the main color palette. Bright pops of green and red hues provide a cheerful splash of color in a few key spaces.



One of the standout spaces within Forsyth Artist Retreat is the kitchen. Granite countertops and stainless steel environments provide an enjoyable environment for whipping up meals when you don't want to go out. The kitchen is fully equipped with all the appliances and cooking essentials you'll need. Use the toaster and coffee maker to create a quick breakfast, or cook a full dinner party with the stove and oven.



Plan Big Group Trips in This Spacious Rental



When you choose Forsyth Artist Retreat for your Savannah vacation homes, you don't have to exclude your loved ones. This roomy home accommodates six guests, so you can bring along your friends and family. Our spacious three bedroom, three bathroom home is great for bachelorette trips, family getaways, and group vacations.



Sleeping spots for you and your guests are evenly distributed throughout the home. In the master, you can rest on the king sized bed or take a shower in the en suite bathroom. The two other bedrooms are equally spacious with each one holding a queen sized bed and a seating area. If your party needs a little more room, check out the loft upstairs. This spacious second floor has couches that fold out to sleep additional guests.



The bedrooms aren't the only roomy part of our accommodations. The living room and dining area is also sized to suit the needs of larger parties. With an open floor plan, some members of your group can relax in the living room while others work on cooking breakfast. Everyone can easily gather in the living room to watch a movie, or you can meet up around the large dining table to discuss your plans for the day.



Access a Wide Range of Luxurious Amenities



Our vacation rentals Savannah GA stand out because the amenities thoughtfully provide all the home-like comforts you need. Cooking essentials, dishes, soaps, shampoos, and more are all available in case you forgot something. A washing machine, dryer, and iron make it easy for you to get ready each day, and you get fresh linens, towels, extra pillows, and extra blankets to add to your comfort. When you want to get some work done or relax with a favorite show or game, our free Wifi, cable, and satellite TV are always available.



Forsyth Artist Retreat provides the perfect spot for outdoor relaxation. Gather on the spacious balcony to have cool drinks and catch a breeze on sunny afternoons. The cozy patio furniture easily fits your whole group, making it a great spot for evening dinners. Just like all our other Savannah vacation rentals, Forsyth Artist Retreat also provides access to our heated pool complex on Tybee Island. Take a dip in the water, relax in the sunshine, or grill a nice lunch at our resort style swimming pool.



Explore All of Savannah's Attractions



Just a block away from Forsyth Park, Forsyth Artist Retreat is perfectly situated to show you all that Savannah has to offer. You can easily walk to local favorites like Analog Public House, The Sentient Bean, and Cuban Window Cafe. Our Savannah vacation rentals are also right by one of the stops on the free Savannah trolley. Ride the trolley downtown to experience all sorts of delicious foods, tasty beverages, and fun shopping opportunities.



While you're staying at our Savannah vacation homes, be sure to check out all the wonderful history and tradition of Savannah. This town has a rich and fascinating history. Take a walking tour of the Historic District to view antebellum architecture or visit the Savannah History Museum to learn more about the town. Savannah might be historic, but it's also one of the leading regions for modern art enthusiasts. Visit the SCAD Museum of Art to see cutting edge exhibits, or go to the City Market to see items produced by local artisans. Whatever your preferences, there's always plenty of fun things to do while you are staying at Forsyth Artist Retreat.
