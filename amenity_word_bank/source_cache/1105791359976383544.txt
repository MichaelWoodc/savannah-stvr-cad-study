=== FULL DESCRIPTION ===
Tucked right along the edge of Forsyth Park, The Drayton puts you in the heart of Savannah. Located on the third floor, enjoy the big front porch overlooking Forsyth Park and Drayton Street, three large bedrooms, two bedrooms, and ample space for up to six guests. Fresh updates, stylish décor, and original touches create
a relaxing environment to unwind in after exploring. Minutes from everything Savannah offers, including dining, attractions, pubs, and more.
SVR-01744

The space
Welcome to your charming Savannah sanctuary nestled on the third floor, promising an unforgettable stay filled with comfort and convenience. Ascend the stairs and step onto the balcony, where seating for six invites you to savor the sights and sounds of this historic city.
Step through the doors into the inviting living room with hardwood floors, a new oversized sofa, and sitting chairs for lounging and streaming your favorite entertainment on the smart TV. Sunlight streams through the windows, infusing the space with warmth and airiness, while a cozy two-seat table offers a relaxing spot for a meal or catching up on correspondence.
Enjoy effortless cooking in the fully appointed galley kitchen, with all the essential accessories, including a fridge, range, dishwasher, and elegant granite countertops. Pour a glass of wine before gathering around the four-seat dining table, which can extend to accommodate up to eight guests, for a hearty meal and lively conversation.
Retreat one of the three elegantly furnished bedrooms, each offering a unique charm. The first bedroom boasts a queen bed adorned with crisp linens, antique furniture, and gleaming hardwood floors. The second bedroom features a queen bed in a modern wood frame, complemented by matching furniture and vibrant artwork. In contrast, the third bedroom offers a queen bed nestled within a timeless brass frame, set against hardwood floors and a striking orange rug.
Refresh in the two full baths, one with a tub and the other with a standalone shower, ensuring everyone's comfort and convenience. The primary bathroom has been fully refinished with custom tile work and modern touches. Both bathrooms are outfitted with all the essentials, including toilet paper, towels, shampoo, body wash, and a hair dryer.
Enjoy access to high-speed WiFi, a washer and dryer for added convenience, central AC for year-round comfort, and a gas grill on the back deck, complete with two seats, perfect for al fresco dining and relaxation amidst the cityscape.
Welcome to your Savannah retreat, where comfort, style, and modern amenities converge to create an unforgettable getaway experience. For those blending work with pleasure, fear not – our high-speed WiFi stands ready to support your endeavors, whether video conferencing or multitasking.

Neighborhood highlights
Located on the east edge of Forsyth
Park, you will be in the heart of historic Savannah and centrally located to
all the incredible eats, drinks, and sights Savannah has to offer. Stroll into
Forsyth Park from your door to enjoy Savannah’s beautiful mornings. Grab
breakfast at Collins Quarter at Forsyth, which has indoor and outdoor seating.
Looking for a sandwich and a pint?
Stroll on down to the Black Rabbit, a small yet incredible sandwich shop by
day, and pub by night. Need a taco fix? Try out Bull Street Tacos for
scratch-made Mexican dishes.
Looking for fine dining and a great
vibe? Head on over to Local 11 Ten and their chic rooftop bar and lounge known
as The Perch. Enjoy handcrafted cocktails and delicious dishes. The Perch is
open year-round and features gas heaters for cooler winter days.
Take an adventure outside of the neighborhood by visiting one of Savannah’s many museums. Mercer-William’s House Museum, Savannah African Art Museum, American Prohibition Museum, and many more are just minutes away. Tap into Savannah's artistic nature by stopping into its College of Art and Design Museum of Art. Want to get more hands-on and
make some memories? Try blowing glass at Hostess City Hot Glass, a perfect time for the whole family.

Not enough time to walk all the sights of Savannah? Take it all in at once while riding or biking. We are just minutes from Old Town Trolley Tours of Savannah and Savannah Bike Tours. Check
out the city’s skyline from the water by jumping on one of the Savannah Belle’s ferries, with multiple stops all along Savannah’s riverfront.
