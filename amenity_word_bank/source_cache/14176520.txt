=== FULL DESCRIPTION ===
Completed in August 2026, this two-bedroom apartment occupies the third floor of new building, just one block from Forsyth Park. Settle into a fresh, light-filled home base in Savannah’s Victorian District. An entirely new guest experience in the same walkable Savannah location we've hosted in for years.

Inside, you'll find two queen bedrooms, a sleek marble bathroom, a well-equipped kitchen, private laundry and an inviting living area with oversized windows. 

SVR-02346

The space
Welcome to Apartment B, our new third-floor retreat in Savannah’s Victorian District.

The apartment was designed for travelers who want the convenience of a modern space while being able to experience much of Savannah on foot.

TWO PRIVATE BEDROOMS

Each bedroom is furnished with:

Queen bed
Ceiling Fan
45" TV
Blackout window treatments
Private closet

A PLACE TO UNWIND

The sunny living room features a comfortable sofa, 55" television and additional seating for relaxing after a day around Savannah. There is also dining space for four.

Oversized windows bring in wonderful natural light, while contemporary artwork and soft green accents give this unit its own personality.

KITCHEN

The compact but fully equipped kitchen has everything needed for an easy stay, including:

Full-size refrigerator
Dishwasher
Microwave
Two-burner electric cooktop
Keurig coffee maker
Cookware, dishes and basic kitchen essentials

It's ideal for breakfast before heading out, reheating leftovers from one of Savannah's restaurants, or putting together a casual meal at home.

BATHROOM

The newly constructed bathroom has a clean, modern feel with marble flooring, a generous walk-in shower and open built-in storage stocked with fresh towels and bath essentials.

PRIVATE LAUNDRY

Apartment B has its own combination washer/dryer tucked inside the unit, as well as an iron and ironing board.

LOCATION

One of our favorite things about this apartment is where it puts you.

Forsyth Park is just one block away, while Savannah's Historic District stretches out to the north and the restaurants, bars, coffee shops and independent businesses of Starland are to the south.

That means you can spend the morning walking beneath the oaks around Forsyth, head downtown for sightseeing, and wander toward Starland for dinner without necessarily getting in a car.

The free DOT shuttle also stops nearby and provides an easy way to explore more of downtown Savannah.

Guest access
Apartment B is entirely yours during your stay.

Check-in is contactless and simple. We'll send your personal entry code on the morning of arrival, and you may let yourself in anytime after 3:00 pm.

Please keep in mind that Apartment B is on the third floor and the building does not have an elevator. Guests must be comfortable using stairs.

Prefer fewer stairs? Check out my other listing- Apartment A is located on the second floor, giving you the elevated, private feel of an upper-floor apartment without the extra flight required for our top-floor apartment.

Other things to note
Street parking surrounding the building is free and unmetered, but spaces are not assigned or reserved.

Please check posted street-sweeping signs when you park, as vehicles occasionally need to be moved for scheduled cleaning.
