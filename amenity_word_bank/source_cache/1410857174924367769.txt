=== FULL DESCRIPTION ===
Step into the heart of Savannah’s Historic District and experience the charm, romance, and allure of a bygone era—without sacrificing modern comforts—at Above the Tea Room Off Madison Square. This one-bedroom, one-bathroom vacation rental sits tucked above a quaint local tea room, welcoming guests with an enchanting courtyard entrance and a warm, inviting interior that perfectly reflects the spirit of this iconic Southern city.

The space
Above the Tea Room Off Madison Square – A Timeless Gem Among Savannah Vacation Rentals



With accommodations for up to four guests, this delightful condo offers the ideal home base for a romantic retreat, a small family getaway, or a solo escape steeped in history and elegance. Located just steps from the famed Madison Square, you’ll be surrounded by cobblestone streets, lush oak-lined parks, and centuries of rich heritage. Whether you’re here for the architecture, the cuisine, or the unforgettable atmosphere, Above the Tea Room is one of the Savannah vacation rentals that puts you right in the center of it all. Please note that the property is located upstairs and requires climbing one flight of stairs to access the rental.



A Charming Southern Welcome



From the moment you arrive, you’ll know you’ve found something special. The entrance to the condo is tucked behind a whimsical courtyard that feels like a secret garden—lush, serene, and photogenic. It’s the perfect spot to enjoy a quiet morning coffee before heading out to explore or to unwind in the evening with a glass of wine under the stars.



This enchanting entryway enhances the condo’s unique appeal, making you feel like you’ve uncovered one of Savannah’s best-kept secrets. Located above a lovely neighborhood tea room that fills the air with subtle floral aromas, your stay here will feel like a fairy tale come to life.



Southern Comfort Meets Classic Style



Step inside Above the Tea Room Off Madison Square and be instantly charmed by its graceful décor and welcoming ambiance. The living room is outfitted with cozy furnishings and a large window overlooking a beautiful street. Whether you’re curling up with a book, streaming your favorite shows on the flat-screen TV, or using the high-speed WiFi to share your day’s adventures, you’ll find everything you need to relax and recharge.



With warm tones, tasteful artwork, and natural light spilling in through the windows, the space offers a perfect balance of historic character and modern convenience—making it a standout choice among Savannah vacation homes.



A Cozy Kitchen with all the Essentials



Savannah’s food scene is a must-experience, but when you’re in the mood for a quiet meal in, you’ll appreciate the condo’s compact yet well-equipped kitchen. While the space is cozy, it includes everything you need for the basics—a stove, oven, microwave, fridge, coffee maker, and all the essential cookware and utensils. It’s perfect for preparing a light breakfast before heading out for the day, or heating up leftovers from one of the city’s incredible restaurants. The nearby dining table for two creates a charming little nook for casual meals, coffee breaks, or late-night snacks.



Restful Nights in the Queen Bedroom



After a day exploring the city’s many treasures, retreat to the peaceful queen bedroom where plush linens, soft lighting, and a welcoming bed invite you to fully unwind. Ample storage ensures you can settle in comfortably, and the clean, calm ambiance makes it easy to drift off to sleep and wake up refreshed. For additional sleeping arrangements, the living room features a queen-size convertible sofa, with cushions that unfold to create a floor-level sleeping surface for up to two guests, making the space ideal for couples, friends, or small families seeking cozy Savannah vacation homes.



Stylish Bathroom with Walk-In Shower



The condo’s modern bathroom features a sleek walk-in shower, contemporary fixtures, and plush towels to enhance your comfort. Whether you're preparing for a night out or rinsing off the day's adventures, this spa-like space is designed to meet your needs with both function and style.



Location, Location, Location – Right by Madison Square



What makes Above the Tea Room Off Madison Square one of the most desirable vacation rentals Savannah GA offers is its unbeatable location. Nestled in the very heart of the Historic District, the condo is just steps from the iconic Madison Square—one of the city's most beloved and picturesque parks.



You’ll be within walking distance of historic landmarks like the Green-Meldrim House, the Mercer-Williams House, and the Savannah College of Art and Design. Forsyth Park and River Street are just a short stroll away, along with countless shops, museums, and renowned dining options.



Enjoy the luxury of walking Savannah’s storied streets at your leisure, knowing that your charming home-away-from-home is always close by. There’s no need to worry about transportation or logistics—this location puts everything at your fingertips.



Perfect for a Romantic Escape or Small Group Getaway



Whether you’re celebrating a special occasion, enjoying a weekend away with your partner, or exploring the city with a close friend or two, Above the Tea Room Off Madison Square offers everything you need for a memorable stay. As one of the most charming and unique Savannah vacation rentals, it’s a place where guests can relax, connect, and soak up the city's historic charm in style.



Discover Why Savannah Vacation Homes Are So Special



There’s something undeniably magical about staying in a historic home in Savannah. From the cobblestone streets and moss-draped oaks to the rich architectural heritage, this city has a way of wrapping you in its charm. Above the Tea Room captures that essence perfectly.



Unlike standard hotels, Savannah vacation homes like this one provide a more intimate, personal experience. Here, you're not just visiting—you’re living like a local, immersed in the beauty, culture, and rhythm of daily life in one of the South’s most beloved cities.

From the moment you arrive until your last stroll through Madison Square, this cozy condo invites you to relax, explore, and fall in love with Savannah all over again.
