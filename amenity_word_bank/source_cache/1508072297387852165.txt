The space
Welcome to Secret Savannah Sanctuary C – a charming hideaway nestled in Savannah’s South Historic District. Perfect for a romantic escape or peaceful solo retreat, this carriage house is a pet-friendly one-bedroom, one-bathroom second story walk-up, that accommodates up to two guests in comfort and style.

What makes this home special?

Open and bright floor plan: This second story walk-up has an open living and dining area that greets you with hardwood floors and abundant natural light. Relax on the plush sofa with a Smart TV for entertainment, or enjoy meals in the fully equipped kitchen, featuring sleek black stainless-steel appliances, quartz countertops, and a cozy dining table.

Peaceful Sleeping Accommodations: The tranquil bedroom offers a queen-size bed, Smart TV, while the modern bathroom (that connects) boasts a vanity, a walk-in shower designed for relaxation, and a full-size stackable washer + dryer.

Step outside and discover the beauty of Savannah’s South Historic District—cobblestone streets, moss-draped oaks, and landmarks like Forsyth Park and the Cathedral of St. John the Baptist. When you’re ready for a change of pace, Tybee Island’s sandy beaches and coastal charm are just a short drive away.

Whether you’re strolling through historic squares or savoring the sea breeze, Secret Savannah Sanctuary C is your perfect retreat in Savannah!

Sleeping Configuration: 
Bedroom 1: Queen Size Bed

Parking: Non-metered Parking
This unit has free, on-street parking right outside the front door. Availability will depend on time of day and season. Our guests have not reported trouble parking near the home, even on the busiest of weekends.

We can welcome your dog companions at this pet friendly home, but ask that you find other arrangements for cats, birds, and other furry friends. There is a $130 pet fee (non-refundable) with a 35 lb. maximum weight limit per dog and a limit of 2 dogs per reservation.
