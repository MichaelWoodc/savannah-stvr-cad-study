**Gorgeous, New Build, Short or Mid-Term Stays Available**
This gorgeous new construction offers complete furnishings, inclusive utilities/internet, and a range of desirable amenities for an exceptional stay.  Located in the Victorian District West, only minutes away from SCAD Eckburg Hall and Number Nine, local dining and Forsyth Park.The whole group will enjoy easy access to everything from this centrally located place.

The space
This duplex located in the Victorian District in Savannah is perfect for weekend trips or mid term stays. This listing is for the full duplex, both upper and lower units that are identical in size and setup, each with its own private entry.  The four bedrooms (2 downstairs and 2 upstairs) are spacious and comfortable, with king-sized beds that guarantee a good night’s sleep. The fully stocked kitchens have everything you need to prepare meals, including cookware, utensils, and appliances. The living rooms are cozy and inviting, with a smart TV for your entertainment. The bathrooms are clean and modern, with all the necessary amenities. High-speed internet is available throughout the property, so you can stay connected with ease and work comfortably at the dedicated workstation. All linens are provided, so you can travel light and enjoy a hassle-free stay.  Utilities included as well.

Guest access
Guests will have full access to both units, with exception of one locked owners closest in each unit which is used for storage.

Other things to note
Units are set up with wifi electronic locks for easy self check-in.  There is one street facing camara on the exterior for guest and host safety.
