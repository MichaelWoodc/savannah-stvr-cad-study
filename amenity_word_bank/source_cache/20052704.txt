From River Street, you can also enjoy a river boat cruise or take a dolphin watching tour out onto the river.



If you have extra time to spend in Savannah or just really need to see the beach, then a trip over to Tybee Island might be the perfect getaway. It's only a half-hour drive from downtown and you'll find several beaches, opportunities for fishing, plenty of dining options and the old Tybee Lighthouse and Museum awaiting your arrival.
