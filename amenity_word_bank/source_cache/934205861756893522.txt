=== FULL DESCRIPTION ===
Welcome to our charming 2 bedroom, 2 bathroom garden-level condo located on the ground floor of a magnificent historic federalist style mansion in a prime downtown location! This stylish space offers a unique combination of modern comfort and historic ambiance. As you step into this space, you'll find an inviting and cozy living room, fully equipped kitchen, and adjacent dining area. We also provide one free off-street parking space, ensuring that you have a hassle-free stay!

SVR 02755

The space
This enchanting garden-level condo, nestled within the historic walls of a grand federalist style mansion in downtown Savannah. With two bedrooms and two full bathrooms, as well as a plethora of charming features, this is the perfect retreat for couples, friends, or families seeking a delightful blend of historic charm and modern comfort.

As you step into the condo, you'll discover a spacious and comfortable living room. Unwind on the plush sofa  after a long day of exploring all that Savannah has to offer and indulge in your favorite shows on the cable TV with streaming capabilities. The fast WiFi ensures seamless connectivity throughout your stay!

Our fully equipped kitchen invites you to showcase your culinary prowess. Modern appliances, ample counter space, and a charming dining area create the perfect setting for memorable meals.

The two bedrooms (one with a king bed and the other with a queen) offer a peaceful retreat. Both bedrooms are set with fresh, white linens, memory foam mattresses, and thoughtful touches! The two bathrooms provide convenience and privacy for all guests.

Throughout the condo, you'll find a harmonious balance of historic allure and contemporary upgrades. Immerse yourself in the unique character and architectural details that whisper tales of the past. Yet, rest assured that modern amenities and upgrades ensure your stay is comfortable and convenient.

And the best part? Our location! With downtown Savannah at your doorstep, you'll have easy access to an array of attractions, shops, and dining options. Explore the city's rich history, take leisurely strolls along cobblestone streets, and soak up the vibrant atmosphere that surrounds you.

For added convenience, we provide one free off-street parking space, ensuring a stress-free experience for those traveling by car.

Whether you're seeking a romantic getaway, a memorable trip with friends, or a family adventure, our space promises a welcoming haven in the heart of Savannah!

Guest access
The entire first floor condo (what we here in Savannah call the garden-level) is all yours! While this condo is entirely separate from the main, upstairs house (there are no shared spaces) we do always like to make guests aware that other guests will likely be staying in the upstairs unit as well.

Other things to note
Don't be afraid to reach out if you need anything!  We live super close-by and are always happy to help out, even mid-stay.
