Neighborhood highlights
This is probably our favorite neighborhood in the city! The house is off a quiet street split by a treeway full of Spanish-moss covered oaks. Stroll just two blocks and enter the middle of gorgeous Forsyth Park, the city's crown jewel! Take in the beautiful architecture and shaded streets surrounding this condo that straddles Savannah's Victorian and Historic Districts. Collin's in Forsyth, Crystal Beer Parlor, Black Rabbit, 700 Drayton, Local 11Ten and other incredible bars and restaurants are right outside your door.

If you choose to stay with us and need lots of recommendations...don't worry! We are happy to provide personalized recommendations based on your interests and preferences...just ask us!
