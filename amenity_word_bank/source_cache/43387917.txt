=== FULL DESCRIPTION ===
Stay steps from Forsyth Park in this charming 2BR/1BA downstairs Savannah retreat. The primary bedroom has a king bed, and the second bedroom has a twin daybed that converts to a king, perfect for two couples or a family. Enjoy one dedicated hot tub in the shared courtyard, one off-street parking space, and an easy walk to bars, restaurants, shopping, farmers markets, River Street, and Savannah’s top attractions.

The space
The home is a top/down, and this listing covers the rooms in the lower-level apartment. The larger bedroom features a king-sized bed, while the other bedroom has two twin-sized beds. 

There is one full bathroom. 

The small kitchen has all the necessary appliances for your stay. There is a large living room with a smart TV and seating for eight. Through the windows, you’ll see the streets of Savannah’s historic district beckoning.

Enjoy the convenience of free off-street parking, then unwind in the double courtyard adorned with string lights—the perfect setting for morning coffee or evening cocktails. Whether you’re visiting for a weekend getaway or an extended stay, you’ll feel right at home.

Guest access
Keyless Entry for access.

Parking area with an outdoor security camera.

Other things to note
The house was recently painted and decorated. There are so many cool paintings and creative touches.

Hot Tub – Please note that the patio area is shared between both units. Each unit has its own private hot tub for guest use; however, the patio itself is a shared outdoor space.

Parking area with an outdoor security camera. Parking is on side of the house (Huntingdon St.) There is enough space for 2 cars, take only 1 space.

There is a laundry room next to the stairs outside the living room.

Neighborhood highlights
The Historic District in Savannah is very unique. Savanah was Georgia's first city and was built around 26 squares, the only city like it in the world. Guest who love history and a great time will thoroughly enjoy Savannah at any time of year.

The South Historic District of Savannah, GA is one of the oldest neighborhoods in the city. This traditional neighborhood is located in the heart of downtown Savannah and is home to a variety of architecture, some of which dates back to the early 1800s. Many of the original Georgian and Federal-style homes are still standing, and they are a great representation of the city’s historic past. Additionally, the district is known for its grand, cobblestone streets, which are perfect for walking or biking, as well as its many parks and squares. With its unique character and charm, the South Historic District is a wonderful area to experience and explore Savannah’s amazing history.
