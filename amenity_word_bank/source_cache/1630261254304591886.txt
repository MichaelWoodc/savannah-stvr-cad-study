=== FULL DESCRIPTION ===
Welcome to Unit C at Park Place, a bright and inviting second‑floor retreat tucked into the quiet back corner of the property’s walled courtyard. Just a three‑minute walk to both Forsyth Park and Chatham Square in Savannah’s South Historic District, this thoughtfully designed home sits in an area with free street parking and short walks to both the nearest north–south and east–west fare‑free shuttle stops, making it easy to explore the city like a local.

The space
The Space

This charming upstairs unit offers a peaceful home base with everything you need for a comfortable stay.

Inside you’ll find:  

- A comfortable living and dining area with plentiful seating and a smart TV  
- A fully equipped kitchen with everything you need for cooking at home  
- 2 spacious bedrooms, each with a queen bed and ample storage  
- 1 full bathroom, stocked with Public Goods bath products  
- A quiet corner location in the back of the courtyard—ideal for guests who value calm and privacy  

Step outside to enjoy the shared, walled courtyard, a lovely spot for morning coffee or an evening wind‑down after exploring the city.

Walkable Location
You’ll be staying in one of Savannah’s most desirable neighborhoods, surrounded by leafy squares, beautiful architecture, and local favorites.

Next door, the Savannah Yoga Center offers convenient drop‑in classes, and Savannah on Wheels—just a 6‑minute walk away—rents both standard and electric bikes for easy city cruising.

Perfect for…
- Couples traveling together  
- Small groups or families seeking a quiet, private home base  
- Business travelers who want comfort and walkability  
- Guests who want to live like locals in a primarily residential pocket of the Historic District  
- Visitors attending events at Enmarket Arena — enjoy an easy 11‑minute walk through two peaceful squares to reach the arena shuttle pick‑up, or opt for a quick 6‑minute drive
