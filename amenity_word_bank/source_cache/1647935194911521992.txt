=== FULL DESCRIPTION ===
The Lower Carriage House is a beautifully restored brick carriage house built in 1871, located just one block from Forsyth Park within the historic Wayne Mansion Estate. This private ground‑floor residence blends classic charm with modern comfort.

The space
About the Space  
Enter directly into your private ground‑floor residence. Inside, warm natural light and original hardwood floors create a welcoming, peaceful atmosphere. The layout is ideal for couples, friends, or small families who appreciate privacy and thoughtful design.

Bedrooms & Bathrooms  
- Bedroom 1 – Queen bed  
- Bedroom 2 – Queen bed 
- One full bathroom (shower only, no tub), conveniently located between the bedrooms  
- One additional half bath for extra convenience

Kitchen, Living & Laundry  
- Fully equipped full kitchen with range, refrigerator, dishwasher, microwave, and essentials  
- Comfortable living area with large smart TV  
- Dining area suitable for meals or remote work  
- In‑unit washer and dryer for convenient laundry access

Location  
Located one block from Forsyth Park, the Lower Carriage House offers effortless walkability to Savannah’s best cafés, restaurants, boutiques, and historic sites. Guests enjoy a short walk to the largest grocery store in downtown Savannah, making it easy to stock up during longer stays. The home is also near a stop for the north–south fare‑free DOT shuttle, providing quick, car‑free access to the rest of the Historic District. In addition, you’re within easy walking distance of several of Savannah’s beautiful historic squares, perfect for morning strolls or exploring the city’s charm at your own pace.

Please note that there is no on-site parking included with this listing.  Free street parking is available in the immediate area.
