2 King Beds • 2 Hot Tubs • 2 Parking Spaces • Sleeps 8

Stay steps from Forsyth Park in Savannah’s Historic District. This listing includes both apartments in the same home, giving your group extra privacy and space to spread out. Enjoy a gorgeous private courtyard with two hot tubs, perfect for couples, friends, or groups. Walk to the Forsyth Fountain in 1 minute and River Street in under 15 minutes. Free off-street parking included.

The space
Enjoy the privacy and flexibility of two separate apartments with one shared entrance, making this a perfect setup for families, friends, bridal parties, or groups traveling together.

The lower apartment features two bedrooms and one bathroom. The primary bedroom has a king-size bed, while the second bedroom includes two twin beds. The bathroom has a walk-in shower, toilet, and sink. This unit also includes a washer and dryer, conveniently located near the entry door.

The upper apartment offers two additional bedrooms and one bathroom. The primary bedroom has a king-size bed, and the second bedroom has a queen-size bed. There is also a pullout sofa in the upstairs living room for extra sleeping space.

Step outside to the private back courtyard, a peaceful retreat to enjoy year-round. The courtyard features two hot tubs, comfortable patio furniture, and plenty of privacy for you and your guests to relax after a day of exploring Savannah.

Guest access
The guest will have access to the entire home.

Other things to note
Hot Tub – Please note that the patio area is shared between both units. Each unit has its own private hot tub for guest use; however, the patio itself is a shared outdoor space.

Parking is on side of house (Huntingdon St.) There is enough space for 2 cars.

There is an outdoor security camera in the parking area.

There is a laundry room at the bottom of the entry stairs.
