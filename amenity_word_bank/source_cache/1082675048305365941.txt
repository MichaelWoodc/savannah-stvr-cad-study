=== FULL DESCRIPTION ===
Stay in a beautifully renovated historic home just a short walk from Savannah’s iconic Forsyth Park. 

Park your car, unpack your bags, and explore Savannah on foot.

You’ll be minutes from Forsyth Park, some of Savannah’s best restaurants and cafés, and the charming streets of the Historic District.

Start your morning with a walk through Forsyth Park. Grab coffee and breakfast nearby. Spend the afternoon exploring the Historic District. Come home, relax, and head back out for dinner and drinks

The space
One block from the breathtaking Forsyth Park and steps from Savannah's legendary Historic District, this stunning 126-year-old apartment has been completely and lovingly renovated — without sacrificing an ounce of its original soul. Soaring ceilings, glowing hardwood floors, and cobblestone streets outside your door set the scene. Starbucks and a full-service Kroger are right across the street for everything else. Whether you're here as a couple, a family, or a crew of friends ready to fall in love with Savannah, this is your perfect home base.

Guest access
Guests have sole access to the whole apartment and shared front porch.

Other things to note
The parking area is shared with the downstairs. So please do not block in the other car(s). Dogs are permitted with a one time $75 pet fee.
