Beautifully renovated apartment 2 blocks from Forsyth Park. Completely equipped with all essentials to make your stay feel just like home. Enjoy a nice evening out on the back patio or just relax and enjoy the spacious living room equipped with WiFi/TV.

The space
This is a late 1890's Victorian home - we have kept some of the original features, such as the doors/trim, hardwood floors and some fixtures. It has been updated/renovated to adjust to the times, giving you everything you need to call this space home!
