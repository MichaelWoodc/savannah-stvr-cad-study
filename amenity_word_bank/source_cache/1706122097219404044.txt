=== FULL DESCRIPTION ===
Elsewhere on Madison Square Carriage House is a private and beautifully restored Savannah retreat overlooking historic Madison Square in the heart of the Historic District.

The space
Welcome to Elsewhere on Madison Square Carriage House, a private and beautifully restored Savannah retreat overlooking historic Madison Square in the heart of the Historic District. Perfect for couples, small families, wedding guests, business travelers, or extended stays, this charming carriage house offers the ideal blend of historic character, modern comfort, and one of Savannah’s most sought-after locations.

Just steps from Forsyth Park, award-winning restaurants, boutique shopping, museums, and Savannah’s iconic squares, you'll enjoy the convenience of being able to explore much of the city on foot while returning home to your own peaceful retreat.

Inside, a relaxed and welcoming atmosphere unfolds across two thoughtfully designed levels. Warm lighting, comfortable furnishings, and timeless details create a space that feels both inviting and serene. Historic charm pairs beautifully with modern amenities, offering guests the perfect place to unwind after a day spent discovering Savannah.

A vintage-style interior staircase leads to the upper level, where the main living area serves as the heart of the home. Please note that the staircase is steep and narrow and may not be suitable for all guests. Upstairs, modern luxury touches blend seamlessly with classic Savannah character. A comfortable living room, thoughtfully selected furnishings, and abundant natural light create an ideal space for relaxing, reconnecting, or planning the next day's adventures.

The carriage house also features a fully equipped kitchen, making it easy to prepare everything from a quick breakfast before exploring the Historic District to a quiet dinner at home after an evening out in Savannah.

Whether you're visiting for a romantic getaway, wedding weekend, anniversary celebration, remote work stay, or simply to experience Savannah's historic charm, Elsewhere on Madison Square Carriage House offers an unforgettable stay in one of the city's most desirable locations.

From its historic setting overlooking Madison Square to its inviting interiors and walkable location, this private carriage house provides the perfect home base for experiencing the very best of Savannah.

What is parking like for this home? 
Parking for this home is on-street and metered, which is enforced by the City of Savannah from Monday through Friday, 8am to 5pm. Guests may conveniently pay using the ParkSavannah app, feed the meter or parking kiosks directly, or park on a nearby non-metered street. Please take note of posted Sweep Zone signs when parking on the street.
