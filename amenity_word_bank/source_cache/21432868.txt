=== ABOUT THIS SPACE ===
Steps from restaurants, all the best shopping, museums and tourist landmarks. Truly a prime downtown location. King bedroom. Pullout sofa. Updated kitchen with television! Original beautiful historic hardwood floors. Parking is on the street or in public garages nearby.

City of Savannah SVR-00903

The space
This is a one bedroom unit in a historic multi-unit condominium building. The condo has a remodeled kitchen with granite counter tops. The kitchen has a large TV and a dining table. There is an office area with a desk chair and a spacious bedroom with King size bed. This condo has a cozy living room at the back of the unit with a pullout sofa bed. The bathroom has a combination tub/shower and it is conveniently located off the hallway. The floors in the living space are original heart of pine historic wood. The ceilings are tall like most historic residences in our city, which gives the feeling of spaciousness. Note that the Airbnb settings reflect the pullout sofa as a 2nd bed and this is not our design. There is one King size "real bed" and one Queen size pullout sofa bed. There are no other sleeping spots.

Guest access
This is a condominium (apartment) building in the heart of the city. You will have a code for a secure main building door, and then your unit #2 is on the main floor and it has its own private entry door with a separate lock. Once inside your unit there is no shared space and the full bathroom, full kitchen, bedroom, living room and office space are all yours to enjoy during your stay. There is a shared laundry room in the building, around the corner from this condo so let us know if you need to use it and we will give you the code and the guidelines.

Other things to note
I wanted to mention that Savannah has certain bugs which we can control to some extent with poison (which every hotel and vacation rental will use on perimeters) but guests still can see them, even if they’re slower and dying from walking across poison barriers. Specifically palmetto bugs are prolific here. If guests see one we do not issue any refunds due to seeing these.

Parking is all on street and metered around the building Monday - Saturday from 8am-8pm and free nights and on Sunday. We suggest downloading the ParkSavannah app to pay for your numbered spot with a credit card on your phone. You may also choose to park in the State Street parking garage on the same block as the condo if there is availability.

Events, holidays and stays during March & April may require 3 night minimums and/or have higher rates than the standard ones published.

Downtown Savannah has a major supermarket (Kroger) and a 24 hour market (Parkers on Drayton) so there is no need to pack everything from home if shopping when you get here makes more sense. 

What to bring with you:

A typical grocery run prior to arriving at your vacation home would include coffee, beer/wine, milk, eggs, butter, bread and any other basic groceries you would need for the meals you eat in the home. 

You should bring or buy your own napkins, laundry detergent, your own beach towels and beach gear, and your own cooler if you need one.  

What we provide:

This condo comes with starter coffee and breakfast bars and/or other snacks! We do not provide refrigerated groceries/perishables but the owners of this unit do provide packaged snacks and coffee. This is unique and is not the case for all of the rentals hosted by Mary/Savannah Luxury Rentals so please note coffee and breakfast bars is a special feature of this particular listing. 

We also provide toilet paper (2 extras per bathroom), liquid hand washing dish soap, bathroom soap, sheets on each bed, and at least one bath towel for each guest. We do not permit our towels to be taken out of the house which is why you must bring your own towels if you visit the beach. 

If you find we have left seasonings, oil, dishwasher pellets, or any unopened food or drinks then of course feel free to use what is there. We occasionally leave those extra items but just cannot promise them all the time. 

Please also note that in Savannah, sugar ants can smell cookies, spilled sugar or syrup, or an open sugar container from a mile away and will find a way in through a windowsill or door frame. It can be somewhat like a cartoon picnic where they march in a line to find the food. To avoid this try to keep your snacks contained in air tight baggies, the refrigerator, or buy single serve packets and put the trash in the outdoor trash cans as soon as possible. 

The following is standard Savannah Luxury Rentals information: We provide sheets, toilet paper and soap. If you prefer thick cushioned toilet paper bring it along but use it sparingly in our older plumbing and never flush "flushable" wipes or other products that are likely to cause a clog.
