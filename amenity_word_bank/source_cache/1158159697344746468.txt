=== FULL DESCRIPTION ===
Nestled along the prestigious Gaston Street, Hartridge House Main stands as a testament to unrivaled Southern luxury living. Just a stone's throw from Forsyth Park and Calhoun Square, this exquisite mansion embodies the essence of refined elegance.

The space
Nestled along the prestigious Gaston Street, Hartridge House Main stands as a testament to unrivaled Southern luxury living. Just a stone's throw from Forsyth Park and Calhoun Square, this exquisite mansion embodies the essence of refined elegance and prime location.

Key Features:

- Prime Location: Situated amidst the verdant beauty of tree-lined streets and historic squares, Hartridge House Main offers unparalleled proximity to Savannah's cultural treasures. With Forsyth Park just steps away and the vibrant historic district merely a 10-minute stroll, guests are immersed in the heart of Savannah's charm.

- Refined Luxury: Every detail of The Hartridge House speaks of sophistication and curated elegance. Impeccable finishes and hand-selected furnishings create an ambiance that transcends mere accommodation, promising a truly memorable guest experience.

- Chef's Kitchen and Formal Dining: Whether indulging in intimate gatherings or hosting grand dinner parties, the chef's kitchen stands ready to cater to every culinary need. Equipped for both professional catering and home-cooked delights, it sets the stage for epicurean adventures and shared moments of culinary delight.

- Rooftop Retreat: Ascend to new heights of relaxation and enchantment on the rooftop deck. Adorned with string lights and crowned with panoramic views of the historic district, this sanctuary offers an outdoor haven complete with a sectional lounge, wet bar, and a hand-painted mural by local artist Julia McGahee.

- Intimate Courtyard: Escape to the tranquility of the intimate courtyard, where verdant greenery invites moments of serenity and conviviality. Perfect for leisurely evenings with loved ones, it provides a picturesque backdrop for cherished memories.

Ensuring utmost privacy and comfort, The Hartridge House boasts three bedrooms distributed throughout the estate. The main house features three luxurious bedrooms, each accompanied by its own ensuite bathroom.

Guests will have access to a private garage space that can accommodate a mid-size SUV or full-size sedan. *Lucky Savannah nor the property owner will be liable for any damage incurred to guests vehicle as restrictions are clearly advertised*.
