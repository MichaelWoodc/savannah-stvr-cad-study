=== FULL DESCRIPTION ===
This charming, FIRST-FLOOR apartment boasts the finest location in the downtown historic district within easy walking distance to everything Savannah has to offer and is just steps from the famous shopping street, Broughton Street, and an easy walk to River Street/Plant Riverside.   If shabby chic yet elegant and classy are your style than pack your bags and come on.  We have everything you'll need for your Savannah Adventure.  Park with ease with two parking decks right down the street.

The space
Back to hosting our wonderful home again for you as our guest after a two-year hiatus on another adventure and are thrilled to be back and look forward to having you in our home.  You won't be disappointed!

NOISE DISCLAIMER:  Since this is a first floor apartment you get the advantage of being on the first floor; however, with that occasionally comes some noise from the apartment above of which we have no control over, AND, it's in the heart of the city so there are some street noises from time-to-time (like the clip-clop of the horse’s hooves and carriages and the occasional church bells), BUT we have noise machines in both bedrooms for those who may be light sleepers.

Neighborhood highlights
This FIRST-FLOOR apartment is located within walking distances to many of the historic homes, buildings, parks, squares and many places of historical significance and interest. Broughton street is just steps away, literally, from the apartment, and it's an easy walk to River Street and the newly acclaimed, Plant Riverside District Entertainment Center, with Forsythe Park being a little further of a walk. Situated between Oglethorpe Square and Columbia Square with Lincoln Street Parking Garage and State Street Parking Garage right down the street. Over 100 restaurants and eateries to choose from and walk to most any of them. Park your car when you arrive and never drive anywhere until you're ready to head home.
