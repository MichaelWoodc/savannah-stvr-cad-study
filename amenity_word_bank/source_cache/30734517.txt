=== FULL DESCRIPTION ===
Historic carriage house from 1850 renovated by its owner, a local interior designer. Original hardwood floors and decorative fireplaces, new marble bathroom and chic decor items. Beautiful natural light. You cannot beat this location in Savannah's historic downtown district. Walk everywhere, close to everytquiet and peaceful. Enjoy the charming courtyard garden for a glass of wine or your morning coffee. Washer/ dryer available. No private parking. See parking  for options. No pets. SVR-02206

The space
Cozy and very quiet, stylish space with separate bedroom and living area. 2nd floor of a carriage house in a Historic Home built in 1850. One flight of stairs up. Enjoy a beautiful a peaceful outdoor courtyard. Original wood floors, brand new carpeting in bedroom, all marble bathroom, all new fixtures.
Kitchen is fully equipped, clean bedding, plushy towels. 
Entry and exit through the back lane of the historic home.

Guest access
Guests can access the entire apartment and shared back courtyard.

Other things to note
Parking info:
Parking is on street. Usually pretty easy to find a spot around the airbnb on the same block. Meters are in effect Mon-Fri 8am to 5pm. It's free anyday after 5pm, and free to park overnight. You can feed the meter remotely from a mobile phone using the Park Savannah App. You can refill it remotely, you don't have to move the car each time. 

If you don't want to deal with feeding meters, or are staying for a few days you can get a visitor's parking pass at the Bryan Street Parking office (100 E Bryan Street)they cost $15 per day, you put it on your dashboard and you don't need to feed meters anytime. 

Another option is  to  leave your car in an inexpensive City parking garage nearby. There are two: The Liberty Street Parking garage is just a 3 minute walk away and is fairly inexpensive.
