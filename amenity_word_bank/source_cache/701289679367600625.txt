=== FULL DESCRIPTION ===
Pet-friendly, beautifully restored historic home with plenty of space.

Other things to note
Welcome home to the Waldburg Manor. This beautifully restored historic home was originally built in 1875 and has been completely renovated to include all modern amenities, whilst maintaining its original character. Located just 2 blocks from beautiful Forsyth Park and its iconic fountain, and walking distance to the heart of historic Savannah, this is a perfect location to immerse yourself in Savannah life, where you'll be spoiled with many dining options, leisurely strolls through live oak-draped squares, galleries, and museums. 



The home consists of two separate apartments, each with its own separate living areas, kitchens, and bathrooms. The ground level, Waldburg Garden apartment, offers a private entrance, with its own courtyard and garden. Upstairs, find the entrance to Waldburg Terrace, a more classic, historic brownstone apartment, complete with hardwood floors and high ceilings, and a two-story layout. 



Beginning on the ground level, the downstairs Garden apartment includes 1 bedroom with a Queen size bed, a full bathroom with a beautiful all-new walk-in shower, and a full parlor-style living area. The fully equipped kitchen has a 4 burner gas range and oven. The outdoor area offers a bit of privacy with a shaded porch and a fenced backyard, shared with the Terrace apartment. 



Upstairs, the second-floor entry opens up to Waldburg Terrace, a second-floor two-story apartment. Tall French windows, hardwood floors, and high ceilings add warmth and coziness to the expansive living room, accentuated by the brick wall detail and neutral color palette. The parlor-style living room is blended with old and new. New furnishings and original brick details of the former working fireplaces have been carefully restored to show their original beauty. The living area has a comfortable sofa and large flat-screen television. A dining table for up to six is perfect for sharing meals together. You'll have everything you need to whip up tasty meals on the fly or create culinary masterpieces in the fully equipped kitchen with a 6-burner gas range and oven and all stainless steel appliances. 



There are three bedrooms and two bathrooms on the second floor. Bedroom 1 has a king-sized bed, a Smart TV, and another beautiful original fireplace. The newly renovated bathroom features a claw-foot tub within an enclosed shower, offering perfect spa-like relaxation. Bedroom 2 has a King-size bed, a Smart TV and shares a Jack-and-Jill bathroom. Bedroom 3 has a Queen Bed and shares the Jack-and-Jill bathroom with an all-new walk-in shower. 



This townhome has onsite, private parking for two vehicles behind the backyard privacy fence. Nearby parking is on-street and non-metered, making parking a breeze. Park your car and take local transportation on the Dot Shuttle that can be accessed near Forsyth Park. Walk or bike to many nearby amenities, including Forsyth Park. Many events are held at the park throughout the year. It is an excellent choice for picnics, outdoor sports, walking, jogging, and free tennis courts. This is also where you will find the famous Forsyth fountain, which is a popular spot for weddings in Savannah. Additionally, the Forsyth Farmer's Market is a great early Saturday morning event to attend. 



Guests will also be located only a short 20-25 minute drive to Tybee Island, Savannah's beach. Come out for a day trip to enjoy the beach, fishing, visit Ft. Pulaski, or climb Georgia's oldest and tallest lighthouse.
