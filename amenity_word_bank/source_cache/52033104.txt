As one of our larger vacation rentals Savannah GA, Savannah Charm is a two-bedroom, two-and-a-half-bathroom townhome can comfortably house four people.



There's Nothing Like a Soft, Cozy Bed to Return Home to

We are sure you're visiting Savannah for its well-preserved historic landmarks, remarkable original architecture, relaxing riverside views, and locally-caught seafood cuisine — not to lay in bed all day. You could do that at home, after all!



Even though we know you'll be spending much of your stay across Savannah's many worthwhile attractions, we deck our Savannah vacation homes out with industry-leading, therapeutic-quality beds to make sure you're recharged for every fun-filled day you plan on having here in Savannah, Georgia.



Tucked away on the second floor of this Savannah townhome, this master bedroom features a queen-size bed centered on beautiful hardwood flooring; the room features a cozy blue, white, and gray color scheme that's set off by bright sky blue accents.



Immediately across from the foot of the bed is a flatscreen TV on a spacious four-drawer dresser, centered in between a pair of white double-doors lined top to bottom with windows. Both doors lead to the master bedroom's second-story balcony, which overlooks the rear of the townhome.



Bedroom number two features a closet and a remarkably spacious bathroom. A wide white marble sink top acts as the bathroom's centerpiece, wide enough to reasonably, comfortably accommodate at least three people getting ready in the mirror simultaneously.



Heading further into the bathroom is a modern dual-flush toilet that's followed by a roomy shower with dual sliding glass doors, each of which features a sturdy grab bar for added in-shower stability. Guests looking for maximum relaxation can opt out of a shower by drawing a bath in the shower's bathtub.



Although it features a different layout and floor plan, bedroom number one is similarly home to more-than-ample amenities than the average bedroom. Firstly obvious is the bedroom's super-spacious king bed, which is separated from the wall via an in-theme dark blue upholstered headboard; wider than it is long, the king-size bed is surrounded by two all-white, dainty yet sleek end-table-and-lamp combos.



Also based on the second story, bedroom number one sports an en suite bathroom complete with a sink, shower, and dual-flush toilet. If you've never used a rainfall shower before, now's your chance to see what all the fuss is about! This master bedroom's shower also has a detachable second showerhead, a grab bar, and a single sliding glass door.



Across the room from the bed is a mid-size closet and a three-deep drawer, on top of which is a flatscreen TV of the same size and specifications as bedroom number two's HDTV.



Lastly, on the left of the bed are three square, nine-pane windows spaced roughly four feet apart with nautical-themed artwork centered in between them. You're also sure to enjoy the white faux bear rug on the floor — talk about a super soft, fuzzy home for your feet!



Unlike the previous two bedrooms, this last lodging area isn't a master bedroom. It also doesn't have a full bathroom — however, its small half-bath will be plenty to keep your kids or other younger family members out of your master bedroom!



Aside from its aforementioned half-bathroom, this space's most defining characteristic is its pair of deceptively comfy twin beds. They're placed on the ends of a lengthy, roughly 25-foot-long and foot-and-a-half-high bookshelf-slash-bed-support. In between the two beds is a tall, bed-length wardrobe with plenty of space for this bedroom's residents.



Unlike the other two bedrooms at Savannah Charm, the additional sleeping area has a fully-fledged living room equipped with a full-size couch, a single-occupancy love seat, and a round ottoman that doubles as a coffee table. On the other side of the room — opposite the living room furniture — is a large flatscreen TV atop a sleek black entertainment system.



Savannah Charm's Backyard Paradise

The ground floor is also home to a very large walled-in backyard area. Upon its gray brick floor are element-resistant outdoor chairs — a love seat and two single-seaters in a half-circle around a raised fire pit — a table-and-umbrella combination surrounded by four chairs, and a sprawling gas grill and lineup of stainless steel ovens.
