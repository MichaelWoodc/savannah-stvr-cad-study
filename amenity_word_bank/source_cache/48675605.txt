=== FULL DESCRIPTION ===
This place is simply amazing. Incredibly posh condo inside of a sweeping, historic Victorian estate just STEPS off of Forsyth Park! Vibrant, eclectic, and well-appointed furnishings with a large living space, light-filled, well-equipped kitchen, and three bedrooms (master boasts a clawfoot bath en suite!) with 3 bedrooms and two full picturesque bathrooms. Even has a beautiful office, overlooking our lovely patio and large backyard.

SVR-2226

The space
Perfect for your Savannah getaway, this home is perfectly located just steps off famous Forsyth Park, and truly provides guests with the quintessential Savannah experience. Nestled among Spanish moss adorned live oaks, and oh-so-rare private large outdoor space, this home boasts PLENTY of space for your entire group of family and friends! Master bedroom features a gorgeous, canopy, king-sized bed with en suite bathroom (and clawfoot tub!), while the two additional bedrooms have a super comfy queen and a twin (with second twin trundled below)...providing the perfect sleeping arrangement for your whole group! Living room has large flat screen TV, super fast Wifi throughout, and tons of room for lounging and relaxing!

Guest access
The entire condo is all yours!

Other things to note
Don't be afraid to reach out if you need anything!  We live super close-by and are always happy to help out, even mid-stay.
