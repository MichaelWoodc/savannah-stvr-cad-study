=== FULL DESCRIPTION ===
Charming and bright newly built Carriage House off of Liberty Lane is located in the Downtown Historic District. Book with ease knowing you are in one of Savannah's residential areas, still within walking distance to River Street and much more.

The space
Located off of East Liberty Lane, this charming and bright carriage house welcomes four Lucky guests. The location of this property is one you cannot beat - Within walking distance to the River front, Savannah's Shopping District on Broughton Street, and many more venues!

Atop of the stairs you'll find an electronic lock, that will work with your unique door code for ensured safety. Step on in to your new digs, and get settled! 

The open and inviting space boasts with natural light, and provides plenty of space to move about without feeling crowded. The living room is open to the galley style kitchen, and offers a smart flatscreen television, dedicated dining space for two, and a queen size sleeper sofa. The kitchen is equipped with modern conveniences such as stainless steel appliances, a full-size oven, fridge and dishwasher. An ideal set-up for short-term or extended stays!

Moving to the bedroom, guests will find a Queen size bed, and an ample amount of storage for their belongings. The en suite bathroom boasts with natural light and wallpaper, tying this quaint space together. Guests will appreciate having plenty of counter space to get ready for a day out on the town!

Looking to start your day off with brunch? Head South West toward Oglethorpe Avenue to Collin's Quarters. While on your way, take a short detour and stroll through Colonial Park Cemetery. Pop over to some of Savannah's most famed shops just over on Broughton Street, such as The Paris Market, a boutique offering an eclectic range of antiques, housewares, gifts & jewelry. Heading into the evening, step over to The Public and enjoy dinner on their rooftop terrace. Looking for a fun way to end the night? Enjoy hand crafted cocktails with your party at The Lost Square Rooftop Bar, over looking River Street.

What is Parking Like For This Home?
This unit has non-metered on-street parking. Parking is not available in the lane. Availability will depend on time of day and season.
