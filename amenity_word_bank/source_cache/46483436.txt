=== FULL DESCRIPTION ===
Welcome to The Harper Abode—a cheerful, historic Victorian home on Warren Square with room for eight, two kitchens, beautiful original details, and a cozy courtyard. Walk to River Street, shopping, dining, and Savannah’s best sights.

The space
Welcome to The Harper Abode, a cheerful four-bedroom Queen Anne–style Victorian overlooking historic Warren Square in downtown Savannah. With room for up to eight Lucky guests, this bright and beautifully preserved home blends Victorian charm with modern comfort—perfect for families, couples, and groups looking to stay steps from everything.

This sun-filled home features soaring ceilings, original decorative hearths, and tasteful furnishings throughout. Both living spaces offer comfortable seating for your entire group, each featuring a flat-screen TV and an original (now decorative) fireplace. The kitchens—yes, two—have been thoughtfully updated with stainless steel appliances and plenty of counter space to cook meals together. Just off each kitchen, you’ll find cozy dining areas that seat up to four.

On the first floor, original pocket doors separate the main living area from the primary bedroom, an iconic detail of Victorian homes. The hardwood floors continue into the bedroom, where you’ll find a decorative hearth, mirrored armoire, carved wooden headboard, and a plush king-size bed. A full bath with a tub/shower combination sits just before the kitchen. The first-floor guest bedroom features a comfortable queen bed and a fully renovated en-suite bathroom with a tub/shower combination and a spacious vanity.

Upstairs, two additional bedrooms provide privacy for families or groups. The large upstairs bathroom offers a relaxing atmosphere with marble countertops and a subway-tile soaking tub/shower combination. Down the hall, a charming guest room includes a bunk bed—perfect for kids or solo sleepers. At the front of the home, the second primary bedroom boasts beautiful natural light, another original fireplace with exposed brickwork, and a second king-size bed.

 What is the parking situation at The Harper Abode?
Guests enjoy two designated parking spaces located directly behind the home—easy, convenient, and rare in downtown Savannah!

Q:  How close is the home to Savannah’s top attractions?
A: You’re a short stroll to River Street, the Historic District, local restaurants, boutiques, and some of Savannah’s most beautiful squares.

Q: Is this home good for families or groups?
A: Yes—The Harper Abode sleeps up to eight guests, with two king bedrooms, a queen room, and a bunk room perfect for kids or singles.

Q: Is there outdoor space?
A: Absolutely! Enjoy a private courtyard with seating and a gas grill.

Q: Are there two kitchens?
A: Yes—both kitchens are fully updated with stainless steel appliances and plenty of prep space.

Q: Does the home have historic features?
A: Tons! Pocket doors, original fireplaces, soaring ceilings, hardwood floors, and classic Victorian details throughout.
