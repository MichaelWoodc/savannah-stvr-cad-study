Step into this beautifully restored 4-bedroom, 2-bath apartment with a full kitchen and dining area just waiting for you to enjoy. Two king bedrooms and two queen bedrooms await your stay. A street-level balcony is a great place to people-watch. This home is located next to Oglethorpe Square and is directly on the parade route.

The space
Step Into History and Modern Comfort in this Fully Walkable Location. Every space has been thoughtfully curated to ensure selfies can be taken. 

Welcome to this beautifully restored 4-bedroom, 2-bath apartment located in the heart of Savannah’s Historic District. 

Highlights about this property:
-Located on Oglethorpe Square, one of the most popular square's in Downtown Savannah.
-2 minute walk to Broughton Street. The most popular shopping street in Savannah. 
-A get bar/ get ready vanity station.
-Every room has its own theme. 
-5 minute walk to River Street. This street has all the restaurant and bars. 

Inside, you’ll find two king bedrooms and two queen bedrooms, offering a perfect retreat after a day of exploring Savannah. The apartment features a full kitchen and a spacious dining area, perfect for preparing and enjoying meals together. Step out onto the street-level balcony to take in the vibrant surroundings and enjoy a prime spot for people-watching or parade viewing – this home sits directly on the parade route and next to beautiful Oglethorpe Square.

WARNING: Expect to argue over which room you're going to be sleeping in. 

Stairs: This is a ground floor apartment. One step down to access
Parking: Paid street parking or parking garage close by
Noise: Please be aware there is some street noise.
