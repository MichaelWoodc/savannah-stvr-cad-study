=== FULL DESCRIPTION ===
Step back in time at this beautifully restored historic carriage house just steps from Forsyth Park in Savannah’s Landmark Historic District. Thoughtfully designed with meticulous renovations, this private luxury retreat blends historic charm with modern comfort. The clean, elegant space features 2 bedrooms, 1 bath, and a peaceful outdoor courtyard with seating—perfect for relaxing after exploring the city.

SVR-03239

The space
Tucked away on a quiet street just steps from Forsyth Park, this private historic carriage house offers a beautiful blend of old-world charm and modern luxury in the heart of Savannah’s Landmark Historic District. Meticulously designed and thoughtfully renovated, the space feels like stepping back in time while enjoying the comfort and convenience of a carefully curated, pristine home.

Inside, you’ll find a cozy and elegant living area centered around a historic hearth with a Frame TV mounted above, creating a warm and inviting space to relax after a day exploring the city. The living room is both stylish and comfortable—perfect for unwinding with a glass of wine or a movie.

The home features two beautifully appointed bedrooms, one with a spacious king bed (and TV) and one with a comfortable queen. 

The bathroom has been pristinely renovated with marble finishes and a walk-in shower, offering a spa-like experience that complements the historic character of the home.

The full kitchen is equipped with all the amenities you need to make preparing meals during your stay a breeze. Gather around the large dining table for dinner, morning coffee, or planning your next Savannah adventure.

Step outside to enjoy the private outdoor courtyard with seating, a peaceful spot to relax in the fresh air after a stroll through the nearby squares.

For added convenience, the home also includes a laundry area with a full-sized washer and dryer.

Located just one block off Forsyth Park and one block from Chatham Square, you’ll be surrounded by Savannah’s iconic architecture, historic squares, charming cafes, restaurants, and boutiques. Despite being so close to everything, the carriage house is nestled away on a quiet street, offering a peaceful retreat in the middle of the city.

Parking: Free street parking is available one block away, while paid parking is located directly out front. We provide full guidance and details upon booking!

Whether you're visiting Savannah for a romantic getaway, a relaxing weekend, or a historic city escape, this carriage house offers a luxurious and memorable stay in one of the city’s most sought-after locations.

Guest access
The entire carriage house and courtyard are all yours and completely private!

Other things to note
Don't be afraid to reach out if you need anything!  We live super close-by and are always happy to help out, even mid-stay.
