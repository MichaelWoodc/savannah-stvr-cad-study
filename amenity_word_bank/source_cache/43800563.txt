=== FULL DESCRIPTION ===
Charming carriage house in a historic home built in 1850 with a beautiful courtyard garden. Located in the design district with the most stylish and chic boutiques just around the corner. Walk to the park, stores and restaurants. Peaceful and quiet. Renovated and furnished by the owner, a local interior designer. Renovated bathroom and kitchen. Parking is on street. Read details in the parking section before asking. No pets. ANY 3rd occupant must be disclosed at inquiry. SVR-02205

The space
Ground level of a Charming Carriage House. Original hardwood floors and exposed brick give it the Savannah Historic feel.  Separate bedroom, with queen bed, living room has a queen sleeper sofa. 
Entry is through the lane. No stairs involved. Very quiet property. Washer and dryer available for use in separate laundry space.

Guest access
Self check in by checking in through the app where you’ll find codes and detailed instructions. 
You have access the shared back courtyard and use any of the tables and chairs. 
The entire apartment is privately yours.

Other things to note
***PARKING INFO***
There is no private parking. Parking is on the street, finding a spot is not hard at all around the apartment. The street you want to park in is Barnard St. just steps away from the carriage house but you can park on any street surrounding the carriage house. Don't park in the Lane, it's loading and unloading only. Free parking all weekend from Friday at 5pm to Monday morning 8am. 

Mon - Friday 8am to 5pm there are meters in effect. It is free everyday after 5pm and free to park overnight any night.  You can either feed the meter at the meters on the corners with a credit card or cash or even from your phone with the Park Savannah App. 

If you don't want to worry about meters get a visitor's parking pass at the visitor's center just 5 min walk from the apartment  **CLOSED DUE TO COVID**. It costs $15 per day, they will give it to you for as many days as you want. Put it on your dashboard and don't have to feed any meter. DURING COVID: Get passes at the Parking and Mobility Office on Bryan Street also downtown just a few minutes away. You have to call ahead, airbnb won't let me disclose website or number here but just search "Savannah Parking and Mobility" and got to "Visitor" tab, it's all clear there. 

If you wish to park in a covered parking garage, there is one 3 minute walking distance away, called the Liberty Street Parking Garage, it is very inexpensive and costs about $17 per day. Covered and safe. 

FREE option: there are streets about 5 minute walking distance that don't have meters, if you want to know where just ask me and I'll send you a little map indicating those streets. They are on E Jones St. or South of Gaston St. Not far at all. Just be mindful of the street sweeper signs.

Neighborhood highlights
This charming Carriage house is located in the most coveted area of Downtown Savannah's Historic District. It is within walking distance of everything downtown, but in a very quiet and peaceful area. Coffee shop just around the corner, all stores and restaurants within walking distance. Free DOT shuttle at the corner (not running due to COVID), free "Downtowner" rides downtown, get the app. Ubers are very inexpensive and readilyt available as all distances are just a few minutes ride.
