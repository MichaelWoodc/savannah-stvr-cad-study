=== FULL DESCRIPTION ===
Spread out in this impressive four-bedroom loft right in the heart of downtown! The city's best shopping and dining are only steps away, with City Market and River Street within walking distance.

The space
Experience the city like a local in your very own downtown loft. Up to ten Lucky guests will call this spacious condo home, only steps away from Savannah's prime shopping and dining attractions. 

Newly renovated with lofty ceilings and exposed brick, Telfair Loft is the ideal downtown headquarters. Catch up with old friends in the open living area before going out on the town for dinner and drinks. Or, make use of the brand new kitchen and formal dining room for a cozy night in. 

A primary bedroom with King size bed, writing desk, and en suite bath is located just off the main living space. The second bedroom is two steps up from the living area and boasts a Queen size bed and the perfect nook to enjoy your morning coffee. Up to four guests can spaciously accommodate the middle bedroom with two Queen beds and a walk-in closet. Another two guests can occupy the back bedroom with Full bed. 

Between the newly renovated kitchen, stacking washer/dryer, and flat screen smartTVs in every room, Telfair Loft truly leaves no amenity aside for your most comfortable vacation yet.

Start your day with a local brew from The Coffee Fox and make your way down to The Paris Market Brocante & Cafe for some retail therapy, recently voted "The South's Best Shop" by Southern Living. Head towards City Market and grab lunch in Little Duck Diner before starting a walking tour with Genteel and Bard. After learning of Savannah's rich history, cap the night with dinner at Chive Sea Bar & Lounge and drinks on the roof at Rocks on the Roof / Bohemian Hotel.

Sleeping Configuration:
Bedroom 1: King Size Bed
Bedroom 2: Queen Size Bed
Bedroom 3: Two Queen Size Beds
Bedroom 4: Full Size Bed

What is parking like for this home?
Parking for this home is on-street and metered, with enforcement by the City of Savannah Monday through Saturday, 8am to 8pm. Guests may conveniently pay using the ParkSavannah app, feed the meter or parking kiosks directly, or utilize a nearby public parking garage. Pre-paid visitor parking passes are available for purchase at the Parking and Mobility Services office. Please take note of posted Sweep Zone signs when parking on the street.
