7 bedrooms, 5 bathrooms in a completely walkable location in Savannah's Historic District. 3 king beds, all ensuite and 4 queen beds await you here! Spacious living and dining areas and luxury linens. This unit is made up of the combination of two lower apartments in this ginormous, historic home. Book today!

The space
A Historic Retreat in Savannah's Historic District

Step back in time and experience the charm of Savannah’s Historic District in this spacious 7-bedroom, 5-bathroom home, once owned by a 19th-century sea captain. With a prime, walkable location near Oglethorpe Square, this historic gem offers the perfect blend of modern luxury and vintage character. 

Inside, you'll find 3 king beds with ensuite bathrooms and 4 queen beds, all outfitted with premium linens for a comfortable stay. The home features expansive living and dining spaces, perfect for gatherings, and is made up of two beautifully combined lower-level apartments in this stately residence. 

Leave your car behind! Stroll to Broughton Street for Savannah’s best shopping or venture a bit further north to explore the bustling Bay and River streets. This home is also in a prime spot for the city’s iconic events, sitting directly on the St. Patrick’s Day parade route and other festival paths. 

Parking is available at the State Street Parking Garage for a daily rate of $12. The garage is conveniently located just a two-minute walk across the square. Additionally, electric vehicle charging stations are available within the facility.
