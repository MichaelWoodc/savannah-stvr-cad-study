Above Broughton 3002
Located in a wonderful vintage neighborhood where night life, restaurants, boutiques and Savannah's top visitor destinations are just footsteps from your front door, Above Broughton 3002 is one of our best-addressed Savannah vacation rentals. Whether you are planning a weekend getaway or an extended stay, vacation rentals Savannah GA on Broughton Street put you at the center of the action.

The space
The Above Broughton Flat



This part of town is famous for its eclectic shops, which range from fabulous antique and whatnot stores such as The Paris Market to unique vendors of locally sourced products like Savannah Bee. Above Broughton 3002 reflects that unique character with vintage brick accents and streamlined designer comfort that will make your Savannah vacation homes stay a memorable one.



This one-bedroom, one bath loft offers you all the comforts of home wrapped up in a simply elegant package. It features a dedicated parking space and an optional elevator for easy accessibility. Like all of our Savannah vacation homes, the flat boasts a full kitchen stocked with dishes and cookware, bed linens and towels plus a washer-dryer, so you can pack light.



Although designed for convenience, Above Broughton 3002 is one of those premium vacation rentals Savannah GA that lets you know as soon as you walk in the front door that comfort and style are also priorities. Like many of our more popular Savannah vacation homes, it has an open floor plan that encourages togetherness. The plush living room sofa doubles as a fold-out bed, and the large flat-screen invites you to kick back with your favorite TV programs.



Nearby, a handy breakfast bar provides a perch for coffee time or a quick snack. The roomy, U-shaped kitchen contains contemporary stainless-steel appliances and generous cupboard space that keeps your culinary tools accessible and organized.



The compact dining area seats four while large windows and lovely hardwood floors serve to open up and expand the lofty space. You will have a panoramic view of Broughton Street from your third-floor flat while being insulated from the noise of this thriving downtown district.



The bathroom makes great use of space with a stylish pedestal sink and a roomy shower while the wonderfully luxurious king-sized bed promises a good night's rest at the close of each busy day during your Savannah vacation rentals visit. You will wake up refreshed and eager to go out and explore the city. Check the weather report on the wall-mounted TV as you get ready.

Easy Access to the Best of Savannah's Historic District



Broughton Street accommodates pedestrian traffic, and several of the places you most want to visit during your Savannah vacation rentals stay are just a short walk away. A typical mix of the old and the new, the Broughton district is home to the Savannah College of Art and Design. Galleries, art supply houses, coffee shops and studios infuse the vibe of the street with a creative, cultural dynamic that many visitors to vacation rentals Savannah GA find irresistible.



Curated collections and designer brands combine to make Broughton Street shopping ultimately satisfying. Kate Spade, Lululemon and Kendra Scott rub shoulders with locally-owned shops like Zia, which offers upscale imported goods and designer jewelry, and 24E Design Company, which sells a bit of everything.



If you have the kids along, be sure to look in at Planet Fun. Comic books, vintage action figures and nostalgic games like Donkey Kong make browsing just as enjoyable as buying. Afterwards, earn extra points with a stop at Leopold's Ice Cream for homemade goodies.



The free Historic District shuttle stops at all the city's town squares as well as other points of interest, so you can see more of Savannah's most frequented sites along with local residents and other Savannah vacation homes guests. These include City Market and River Walk where you can make the most of your visit while tuning into the Historic District's distinctly intriguing atmosphere.

City Market, nestled between Ellis and Franklin squares, covers four city blocks. Located in the same spot since the 18th century, City Market underwent renovations in 1985 to become a mecca for Savannah vacation rentals guests seeking a complete Southern experience. With its wealth of restaurants covering the gourmet gamut, live music, and plenty of unique shops, this large marketplace is one of the city's central hubs.



Take a trolley car from City Market to the Savannah River Street, a must-see section of the city for Savannah vacation rentals guests. Stately brick warehouses converted to stores and galleries, cobble-paved streets and other remnants of bygone days make you feel as if you are stepping back in time while the river views and bustle keep you firmly rooted in the present.

However, no matter what time of year you spend in Savannah vacation homes, there is so much to see and do in the hospitality center of the South that you will stay entertained from start to finish. Contact Southern Belle to reserve premium vacation rentals Savannah GA today.
