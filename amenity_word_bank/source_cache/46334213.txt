=== FULL DESCRIPTION ===
Second floor charming apartment, perched upon Warren Square, overlooking the Spanish Moss tree lined streets. Located off of the beaten path, only one block from the hustle and bustle, this property is fit for your next getaway!

The space
The bright exterior of our two-bedroom, Queen Anne Style Victorian Home, cheerfully welcomes you to this unique charming property. Perched upon Warren Square on the second story, Harper House Too can comfortably accommodate up to Four Lucky guests for a true “live like a local” Savannah experience.

The space gleams with natural light, and boast with high ceilings, original, decorative hearth and tasteful furnishings. The living space comfortably seats your entire party, also fitted with A flat-screen TV, oriental rug, original fire place that is now decorative. Stepping into to the spacious kitchen, you'll find original wood work and flooring throughout the home, a classic feature in Victorian homes. The kitchen has been tastefully upgraded with stainless steel appliances, open shelving to modernize the space, and a large butcher block island perfect for preparing home meals. Tucked away, you'll find the solid wood dining table that can seat up-to Four Lucky Guests.

Just off the kitchen, the large bathroom features a serene setting with marble countertops, and a large subway tile soaking tub/shower combination. Down the hall, you'll find the guest bedroom which is fitted with a bunk bed perfect for children, or singles of the group. At the front of the apartment, the primary bedroom awaits, with an ample amount of windows, another original fireplace with brickwork, and a comfortable King size bed.

You’ll be just minutes from the excitement of River Street and Broughton Street, with Savannah’s best shopping, dining and historic attractions all within walking distance. 

What is parking like for this home?
Guests have access to one designated/reserved parking space in the lot behind the home. 

Sleeping Configuration:
Bedroom 1: King Size Bed
Bedroom 2: Twin Size Bunk Bed

We can welcome your dog companions at this pet friendly home, but ask that you find other arrangements for cats, birds, and other furry friends. There is a $130 pet fee (non-refundable) with a 35 lb. maximum weight limit per dog and a limit of 2 dogs per reservation. Some homes may allow larger dogs, please inquire for more information.
