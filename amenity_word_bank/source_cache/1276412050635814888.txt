Welcome to our 2 bedroom, 1 bath Victorian townhome in Savannah’s historic district, where classic character meets easy comfort.

• Private porch with table seating for coffee or an evening drink
• Smart TVs, WiFi, books, and board games for laid-back time in
• Full kitchen with stainless steel appliances and a coffee bar
• About 3 blocks to Forsyth Park; walkable to Savannah sights and dining

Settle in and enjoy the porch, then make this historic stay your Savannah home base.

☆☆ BEDROOMS ☆☆

This gorgeous apartment accommodates 5 guests in 2 bedrooms and a sleeping nook.

The Streetview Suite – The street view bedroom at The Victorian Manor exudes elegance and comfort, featuring a luxurious king-size bed adorned with extra sleeping pillows, accent pillows and a cozy blanket. The room also features a Roku smart TV, fast Wi-Fi, bedside tables with reading lamps and a cozy seating area, perfect for relaxation.

The Hearth Room – The middle bedroom offers a luxurious king bed adorned with extra pillows and a cozy blanket for ultimate comfort. This inviting space includes a Roku smart TV, high-speed Wi-Fi, a spacious dresser, and bedside tables with stylish lamps to enhance your stay.

The Sleeping Nook - The nook is a cozy converted space featuring a twin bed and smart TV, perfect for a restful retreat. Curtains provide a touch of privacy, creating a snug hideaway with plenty of charm and character.



☆☆ BATHROOM☆☆

The bathroom exudes vintage charm with a classic vanity, toilet, and tub/shower combo. Essentials like a hair dryer, shampoo, conditioner, and body wash are provided, creating a cozy, well-appointed space for your relaxation.



☆☆ KITCHEN☆☆

The bright and airy kitchen is fully equipped with all the appliances, utensils and cookware you need to feel at home.



☆☆ OUTSIDE SPACE ☆☆

The exterior of this home boasts a charming upstairs private porch, complete with a dining area featuring a table and chairs—perfect for savoring morning coffee or evening sweet tea and wine under beautiful lighting. On the lower level, the welcoming front porch offers rocking chairs and additional seating for guests to enjoy.
