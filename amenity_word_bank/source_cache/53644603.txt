Neighborhood highlights
The immediate neighborhood is diverse mix of residential housing. Some life-long locals, some SCAD students, some recent transplants, etc.

Our easy walk bars are Wormhole, Starland Yard, and Water Witch. They are all less than a 15 minute walk when heading south on Bull st from 36th St. Our favorite area bars (Lone Wolf Lounge & Moodrights) are more of a 20 minute walk or a 5 minute Uber (our preference).

During the day, Forsyth park is a 15-20 minute walk when heading north on Bull St from 36th st. This walk is dotted with many coffee and food options, as well as some small local vendors.

River st and all the usual Savannah traffic is more like a 40 minute walk that can be done by the adventurous during the day, but we definitely recommend a ride from downtown at night.
