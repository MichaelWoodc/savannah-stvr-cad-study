=== FULL DESCRIPTION ===
The Salon is a large and gracious residence overlooking famous Jones Street in the heart of the historic district. At an impressive 1,400 square feet, this beautiful offering is the largest of all 4 lodgings at 120 West Jones Street, encompassing the entire 3rd floor of the house. The Salon has retained its historic character, including original heart-pine floors and elegant molding, while being tastefully updated with modern conveniences.


SVR-01495
For concierge service visit 120WestJones.com

The space
The Space - Please note, this is a 170+ year old home. Please be respectful of guests above and below you. 
The owners offer the Salon as a 2-bedroom suite to provide 4 guests an exceptional experience in spacious and gracious accommodations. The Master bedrooms’ long Italianate windows overlook “America’s prettiest street” (Food and Wine Magazine and Southern Living) paved with red bricks and lined with ancient live oak trees. Both bedrooms have comfortable beds, furnished with Parachute linens, as well as their own private sitting areas. The living room contains chic bespoke sofas for lounging, lighting by Ralph Lauren, and a 50” SmartTV. The kitchen has been completely renovated and includes state-of-the-art appliances such as Bosch convection wall oven, Bosch built-in microwave, Bosch dishwasher, Bosch built-in refrigerator and Jenn Air cooktop with downdraft. Coffee/tea and condiments for such are provided. There is a charming dining area for those perfect dine-in occasions adorned with antique French copper. The enormous bath has also been completely renovated and boasts a large glassed-in shower with multi-jets. Elegant mirrors, a chandelier and sconces provide old-world charm in a modern atmosphere. The owners have left nothing unconsidered as there is an in-suite washer/dryer set by ASCO to ensure longer-staying guests have all the comforts of home. French doors open off the bath onto a private outside balcony where guests can share an evening’s glass of wine or a bright morning’s coffee or tea.
