Welcome to our Charming Historic Downtown Home - The Mint Julep

Experience the allure of historic Savannah in our charming Mint Julep home. Nestled in the heart of downtown, this classic residence exudes comfort and simplicity, allowing you to immerse yourself in the city's timeless charm.

A short stroll will lead you to Forsyth Park's iconic fountain, opening the door to all the treasures that historic downtown Savannah has to offer."s peaceful and centrally-located place.
SVR-02752

Other things to note
Situated in the Beach Institute District, this area is known for its well-preserved 19th-century homes, cobblestone streets, and lush oak-lined avenues draped with Spanish moss.

Prime Location & Nearby Attractions
Nestled on a quiet yet well-connected street, our home provides easy access to some of the city's most iconic spots:
•	Forsyth Park (5-minute walk | 0.3 miles): Perfect for a morning stroll, picnic, or simply enjoying the famous Spanish moss-covered oak trees.
•	River Street (10-minute drive | 1.5 miles): Explore Savannah’s vibrant waterfront, lined with historic buildings, boutiques, and great restaurants.
•	Broughton Street (8-minute drive | 1.2 miles): The city’s main shopping district, featuring a mix of local stores, designer boutiques, and trendy cafés.
•	SCAD Arnold Hall (5-minute drive | 1 mile): One of the main academic buildings of the Savannah College of Art and Design, housing classrooms, studios, and lecture halls. Ideal for students, parents, or visitors exploring SCAD.
•	Trolley Stop Nearby: 
The Old Town Trolley Stops #5 and #6 are both just an 8-minute walk from the property:
Stop #5: 207 E Gordon St, Savannah, GA 31401
Stop #6: 622 Drayton St, Savannah, GA 31401

Local Markets & Shops Nearby
•	Forsyth Farmers' Market (10-minute walk | 0.5 miles): Held every Saturday at the south end of Forsyth Park, offering fresh, locally sourced produce and goods.
•	Parker’s Market Urban Gourmet (6-minute drive | 1.1 miles): A 24/7 gourmet market with fresh food, wine, and artisanal products.
•	City Market Savannah (8-minute drive | 1.5 miles): A historic open-air market with local vendors, shops, and dining options.
•	Whole Foods Market (12-minute drive | 3.2 miles): Great for organic groceries, fresh produce, and prepared meals.
•	Kroger Supermarket (6-minute drive | 1.5 miles): A convenient option for groceries and essentials.
A Peaceful Yet Convenient Location
A peaceful residential street, well-connected to the city’s attractions. The area sees moderate traffic, making it easy to get around by car, bike, or even on foot. Street parking available, and the neighborhood is pedestrian-friendly.
