=== FULL DESCRIPTION ===
Underscoring Savannah’s rich history, Liberty Garden is your traditional Savannah garden apartment. Featuring 1 bedroom with a king bed, and 1 bathroom, this first-floor apartment provides all the elegant touches that will transport you into Savannah’s colonial past. Boasting a shared garden patio with seating and a firepit you will experience Savannah like a long-time local, taking in all the sights, sounds, and smells of the vibrant downtown filled with stories of the past.

The space
Tucked in the lower level of a historic townhome built in 1868 for John Hernandez, the garden apartment features its own private entrance and all the amenities you need for a comfortable and tasteful stay in Savannah. 
Enter below the main townhome staircase and step into the small, yet spacious apartment. Walk through the foyer to a comfortable living space featuring an original masonry fireplace, custom built-ins, and original hardwood floors. We have a new pull-out couch for 2 as well. Just off the living space, find a small den with a designated workspace, along with high-speed Wi-Fi this apartment is perfect for the remote worker. 
Continuing off the back of the living area, enter the private bedroom through original French doors where you will find a brand-new king mattress with comfortable linens and pillows. Historic colonial dressers and end tables provide ample storage, perfect for longer stays. 
At the back of the apartment enjoy a modernized kitchen with a full 30-inch range, along with granite countertops and a stainless-steel refrigerator. The kitchen is well appointed with everything you need to cook during your stay, including cooking utensils, pots, pans, and dishware. The kitchen comes with small appliances such as a microwave and coffee maker. 
Directly off the kitchen, you have access to the fully enclosed garden patio. Featuring beautiful natural foliage, washed brick planters, and high walls, this private patio space is perfect for enjoying Savannah nights. Although a shared space with the unit above, this large patio provides ample room to spread out and enjoy the fresh air. From the patio, a rear exit door provides access to off-street parking and a Tesla charging station.

Neighborhood highlights
Looking to be right in the heart of everything downtown Savannah has to offer? Near the historic Colonial Park Cemetery, Liberty Garden is located in the North Historic District and boasts incredible access to all the best attractions, eats, and pubs.
Nestled on Liberty Street, a split city road featuring coble stone curbs, old-growth trees, and historic architecture, you will be surrounded by Savannah’s rich past. Steps from your door you can find yourself in one of the many small squares including Lafayette Square, Chippewa Square, Crawford Square, and Madison Square. History abounds with sites like Harper Fowlkes House, Savannah Theatre, Telfair Museums, Beach Institute of African American Culture, and many more.
Excite your taste buds with the many amazing restaurants all a stone’s throw from the apartment. Grab incredible southern dishes at the highly rated Mrs. Wilkes Dining Room. Craving a diner? Check out Clary’s Café. Want your favorite vegan dish? Fox and Fig Café is minutes away. Jumpstart your hunger by taking a slightly longer 16-minute to the Savannah River Street for more incredible dining like The Lady & Sons, Vic’s on the River, The Olde Pink House, and Treylor Park.
Fancy a pint? Stop into Six Pence Pub, a British-inspired pub, just minutes down liberty street. Looking for that small dive bar? Check out The Original Pinkie Masters for a cash-only bar with great deals and incredible staff. Need the perfect cocktail? Slide over to the Artillery Bar, a sophisticated speakeasy in a restored armory with imaginative cocktails and an extensive bourbon list. Want to enjoy your drinks in a funky art-inspired bar/restaurant? Check out Savoy Society. A homey bar/restaurant where pups are even welcomed!
Looking to do some local shopping or curb that sweet tooth? Take the short walk over to the historic City Market and enjoy four blocks of storefronts and restaurants including Savannah’s Candy Kitchen, The Little Crown by Pie Society, and Tree House Savannah, an incredible spot with a rooftop balcony, live music, and more. All right in the City Market!
Want to take it all in at once while riding or biking? We are just minutes away from Old Town Trolley Tours of Savannah and Savannah Bike Tours.
Getting around is easy with walkable streets, bike rentals, and uber service. Jump on Savannah’s public transit system with the Abercorn & Liberty SB stop just a 1-minute walk from Liberty Garden. Looking to travel via the Savannah River? Try out the Savannah Belles Ferries to see the beautiful skyline of Savannah from the riverfront.
