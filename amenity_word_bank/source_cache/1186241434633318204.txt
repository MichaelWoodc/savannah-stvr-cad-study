=== FULL DESCRIPTION ===
Updated 2-bedroom/2.5-bath townhome located in the heart of the Historic District. Steps away from iconic landmarks like River Street, Forsyth Park, and the bustling City Market.​

*  Walk to top attractions, restaurants, and shops.
*10 minute walk to River Street
* Two comfortable bedrooms each with an en-suite bathroom.  The  second bedroom is a stylish loft space that overlooks the living room below. 
* Fully equipped kitchen, high-speed Wi-Fi, smart TV.
* Free On-Site Parking  
​

The space
Open living space with floor-to-ceiling windows looking out into the courtyard. 
The dining room has seating for six, and is just off of the galley-style kitchen with all the amenities of home. 
The back bedroom has sliding doors to a small patio, while the front loft-style bedroom overlooks the living room. 
Both bedrooms have plenty of space to unpack, so you can settle into your home-away-from-home.

Other things to note
Savannah is a walking city and the architecture and squares are best enjoyed on foot. 
Bicycles are a great mode of transportation in Savannah; however, bicycles are prohibited in squares, parks, and sidewalks throughout Savannah.  

 Other parking in this area is paid on-street parking.  The City of Savannah uses Meter Pay Stations on each block. You can download the APP "ParkSavannah" to pay meters from your phone 
OR
Visitor Parking Passes are available for 24hours/$15.00 and 48hours/$24.00. Passes can be purchased at the City of Savannah Parking Services Office at 100 East Bryan Street. (Each parking spot is numbered.) Paid parking runs Monday-Saturday 8 AM - 8 PM.

Neighborhood highlights
Welcome to the Historic District of Savannah, one of the most walkable and charming neighborhoods in the South! This home is perfectly situated just steps from River Street, Broughton Street, City Market, and the city’s iconic oak-lined squares.

Guests can easily explore top attractions like Forsyth Park, SCAD Museum of Art, and the Savannah Riverfront—all without needing a car. Whether you’re here for history, shopping, food, or the famous ghost tours, you’ll love how close everything is.

Stroll to:

Forsyth Park – 10 min walk
River Street – 15 min walk
SCAD – 5 min walk
Local cafés, boutiques, and award-winning restaurants – all nearby
Trolley tours and historic landmarks right outside your door
This unbeatable downtown Savannah location is perfect for couples, families, business travelers, and anyone looking to experience the city’s rich history and vibrant culture up close.

Bonus: Free, dedicated on-site parking included—an absolute rarity in the Historic District!
