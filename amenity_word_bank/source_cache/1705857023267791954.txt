Twilight Garden Escape is a stylishly remodeled cottage in Savannah's historic district, offering a perfect blend of modern comfort and historic charm. The two-bedroom retreat features a cozy living area, a fully equipped kitchen, and a charming patio with a gas firepit for relaxing evenings. Located just a mile from Forsyth Park and within walking distance of top restaurants, it's an ideal spot for exploring the vibrant culture of Savannah.

The space
KEY FEATURES: 

☀ 2 bedroom / 1 bath / Sleeps 4

☀ Board games and books for enjoyment 

☀ Convenient location that is walkable to attractions in Historic Savannah

☆☆ BATHROOM☆☆ The bathroom at Twilight Garden Escape is elegantly designed, featuring modern fixtures and amenities that ensure a comfortable and refreshing experience. The bathroom is stocked with towels, shampoo, conditioner and body wash needed for the duration of your stay.
