Analysis settings

Party and amenity evidence

Permit types come from the workbook: Type 1 is owner-occupied and Type 2 is non-owner-occupied. Exported all-type plots show separate owner/non-owner lines. Parcels carrying both types form a separate mixed group; their shared calls cannot be assigned to one certificate type, and they are excluded from either pure-type selection. The permit-type selection stays in the table and grouped-data download.

How approved and advertised values are used

Approved guest capacity and licensed bedrooms come directly from the license workbook's Max. occupancy and Bedrooms columns. The recorded limit is used whether it equals two per bedroom, two per bedroom plus two, or another value. No general bedroom formula substitutes for the recorded approved limit.

Each graph counts a parcel once. Where several licenses share a parcel, approved capacity and licensed bedrooms are totals across its unique licenses. Advertised sizes are the largest known value among listings that pass the selected listing filters. The table shows each licensed address and its own approved figures; shared parcel calls repeat in the table and must not be summed across license rows.

The over-limit filter compares a listing with the total recorded allowance for the certificates actually matched to that listing. Additional same-address certificates are not silently added. Over-limit and amenity tests must pass in the same listing. Selecting these filters requires matched listing information; an address without an Airbnb match has unknown amenity/advertised data and is excluded by those filters.
