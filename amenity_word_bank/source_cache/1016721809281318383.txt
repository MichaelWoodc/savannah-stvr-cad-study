Welcome to our charming 2-bedroom, 1.5-bathroom townhouse in the heart of historic Crawford District! Immerse yourself in Southern hospitality with modern comforts. This elegantly furnished home boasts a fully equipped kitchen, cozy living spaces. Situated just steps away from local attractions, restaurants and the river and right on Crawford Square.

The space
Step into a thoughtfully designed space where classic Southern elegance meets contemporary comfort. The decor is tastefully chosen to create a warm and inviting ambiance.

Two bedrooms provide restful nights for all guests. Cozy linens and ample storage ensure you have everything you need for a relaxing stay.

The fully equipped kitchen is a chef's dream. Whip up your favorite meals with modern appliances and ample counter space.

The bathroom is stylishly updated with modern fixtures.

You're just steps away from Savannah's historic squares, renowned landmarks, excellent dining options, and cultural attractions. This central location lets you experience Savannah's charm like a local.

Immerse yourself in the spellbinding allure of Savannah. Stroll under the moss-draped oaks, savor local cuisine, and create unforgettable memories in this quintessential Southern city. Our condo is the perfect retreat for those who appreciate the beauty and history of this unique city.

This is the perfect retreat for your Savannah experience. Whether you're seeking a romantic getaway, a family adventure, or a friends' retreat, this townhouse is your perfect home base.
