=== FULL DESCRIPTION ===
Azalea Apartment at Jasmine Quarters overlooks Washington Square and is spacious and beautifully curated in a genteel Savannah style with all modern amenities making it a generous and comfortable apartment for up to four guests.
There are two bedrooms each with a king bed and there are 2.5 bathrooms.
A shareable Level II EV charger is available on the property.

 (SVR-00099)

The space
The Downtown Historic District location makes this apartment accessible to all of Savannah's sites, restaurants, and shopping.  It is 3 blocks from the waterfront, a 10-minute walk from The City Market, and overlooks Washington Square.  There is WiFi, washer & dryer, dishwasher, stove, microwave, refrigerator,  Keurig coffee machine with a starter set of K-Cups, and shared courtyard.  2 HD TVs are available with a Chromcast.  There is no cable TV.

Guest access
The entire apartment is a dedicated vacation rental with a keyless lock system allowing for self check-in.  We generate a new code for each guest.
Guests do share a vestibule at the front entrance with Begonia Apartment.  One parking space is provided in a garage behind Azalea on East Congress Lane.

Other things to note
Jasmine Quarters is only 20 minutes from both the Savannah/Hilton Head Airport and Tybee Island Beach.  Savannah is known for music, film  and book festivals as well as home and garden tours, various museums and beautiful architecture.  Definitely make your restaurant reservations before you come to town!
