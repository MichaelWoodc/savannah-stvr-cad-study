=== FULL DESCRIPTION ===
If you're looking for a true Historic Savannah lifestyle, this is where you want to be, just minutes from City Market, Johnson and Ellis Squares, and a short walk to River Street. This spacious and fun apartment is the perfect getaway for all!

The space
If you're looking for a true Historic Savannah lifestyle, this is where you want to be, located in the lively and urban downtown district. Steps from Nightlife, City Market, Johnson and Ellis Squares, and a short walk to River Street. Bee's Knees is conveniently located above Savannah Bee Company, a local favorite to tourists and Savannah Natives. 

Enter from Broughton Street through the secure building entrance, and head upstairs to your new home away from home! Upon entering, you'll be greeted by high ceilings, original wood flooring throughout and charming decor. The open floor plan makes entertaining a breeze, allowing the conversation to flow effortlessly from space to space. The upgraded kitchen provides all of the modern conveniences and necessities you need to prepare home cooked meals, including a quaint dining space for four! Moving over the living room, guests will find an inviting sectional opposite a flat screen TV, mounted above the modern fireplace. 

No need to argue over bedrooms! Both bedrooms feature amazing views of Broughton Street, both offering a King Size Bed, in addition to an ample amount of storage. Conveniently located across from the rooms, the full size bathroom offers a large vanity, and a upgraded tiled tub/shower combination. Also adjacent to the rooms,  a washer and dryer is yours to use as you need - perfect for short, or extended stays!

For those looking to stay in the heart of it all, this is the spot for you! Start your day off at The Coffee Fox, just outside of your doorstep, and roam all of Broughton Street for day shopping, brunch, or drinks! Head North to City Market for live music, or evening strolls, where you'll additionally find museums, restaurants and so much more. 

Parking:
Parking for this home is on-street and metered, with enforcement by the City of Savannah Monday through Saturday, 8am to 8pm. Guests may conveniently pay using the ParkSavannah app, feed the meter or parking kiosks directly, or utilize a nearby public parking garage. Pre-paid visitor parking passes are available for purchase at the Parking and Mobility Services office. Please take note of posted Sweep Zone signs when parking on the street.
