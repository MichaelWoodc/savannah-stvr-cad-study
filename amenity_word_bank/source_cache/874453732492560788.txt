Step into a time capsule as you enter our historic apartment, where every corner tells a story. Cathedral ceilings adorned with original repurposed wood, period windows, doors, and a siding facade reminiscent of a bygone era make this space a craftsman's dream. Immerse yourself in the architectural wonder of pre-US Civil War Savannah.

While history surrounds you, we've ensured your stay is nothing short of luxurious, comfortable, and modern.

Stay Here! You will not want to leave!

The space
Welcome to our exquisite one-bedroom, one-bathroom historic apartment in the heart of downtown Savannah, Georgia! Dating back to 1790, this charming abode is a true testament to the rich history of this city, offering a unique blend of old-world charm and modern conveniences.

Whether you're seeking a romantic getaway, a historic exploration, or a memorable weekend in Savannah, our apartment is the ideal home base. Experience the magic of the past with all the comforts of today. Book your stay with us now and prepare to create unforgettable memories in the heart of Savannah, Georgia. 

**Historic Grandeur**
Step into a time capsule as you enter our historic apartment, where every corner tells a story. Cathedral ceilings adorned with original repurposed wood, period windows, doors, and a siding facade reminiscent of a bygone era make this space a craftsman's dream. Immerse yourself in the architectural wonder of pre-US Civil War Savannah.

**Modern Comforts**
While history surrounds you, we've ensured your stay is nothing short of luxurious. Revel in the convenience of keyless entry, smart TVs for your entertainment, and a washer and dryer combo for your convenience. The fully equipped kitchen, complete with a dishwasher, invites you to channel your inner chef and create delicious meals.

**Prime Location**
Our apartment's central location is a mere 7-minute walk to the iconic River Street. Discover an array of culinary delights, explore Savannah's renowned architecture, and indulge in the vibrant nightlife that has earned Savannah the title of the Hostess City.

**Safety and Security**
Your safety is paramount. Custom key codes provide you with peace of mind, while our security camera system diligently watches over public spaces. Additionally, our apartment is conveniently situated across from the original Savannah police department, which continues to serve as its headquarters.

**A Hidden Oasis**
As an added amenity, we invite you to unwind in our meticulously designed courtyard. This oasis was crafted using the original "Savannah gray" bricks from the building's historic footprint, creating a tranquil retreat where you can relax and soak in the ambiance of this beautiful city.

We look forward to welcoming you to our historic haven!

Guest access
Guests will have access to the right apartment (A) on the blue side-by-side duplex, the shared courtyard, and the metered street parking found on either Lincoln St., Habersham St., or Oglethorpe Ave.  Please refer to the check-in guide on the app for more details.

*Note:  The apartment is in a property that houses other guests much like an Inn, so we ask that the space is treated with dignity and respect at all times.  Please keep noise to a minimum between 8pm and 8am as per city ordinance.  Parties and events are strictly prohibited.

Neighborhood highlights
When you stay in this apartment, you will be living in some of Savannah's most desirable real estate - right in the heart of Downtown! The best restaurants and sights are only steps from your rich and luxurious property.

Having a car is unnecessary when living here - you can walk, ride, or bike anywhere in this part of town. Once you have booked, we will send you plenty of recommendations on what to do, see, eat, and drink while in Savannah - this list should kickstart your planning.

Convenience and grocery stores are just a few minutes walk from the historic apartment - without mentioning the plethora of cafes, bars, and restaurants. Walk through each square to shop local retailers, grab a coffee or quick bite, or park yourself on a bench and just enjoy the Savannah scenery.

Those in the mood for a true Southern meal will love being close to Mrs. Wilke's Boarding Room, with other local favorites such as 700 Drayton or Crystal Beer Parlor just a short walk or Uber ride from the property.

Popular tourist areas such as River Street and City Market are just a 5-minute walk away, while Savannah's local green space - Forsyth Park - is about 20 minutes away on foot or five minutes by car.

Once you're here, you will not want to move out!
