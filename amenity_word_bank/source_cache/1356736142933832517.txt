The space
Located in an historic high-rise in the heart of Savannah, The Lollygag is a newly renovated, stunning 1,250 sq. ft. corner loft designed for modern comfort and convenience. With oversized windows offering panoramic city views, this bright and airy retreat accommodates 2-4 guests and includes secure Wi-Fi, heat/AC, a washer/dryer, and cable TV.

What Makes The Lollygag Special?

- Prime Location – Step outside to Savannah’s top restaurants, cocktail bars, and nightlife. The FREE dot express shuttle and Savannah Trolley Tours stop just around the corner!

- Exclusive Parking – A rare find! Guests receive a reserved underground parking space with 24-hour access, just steps from the building.

- Modern Luxury & Security – Enjoy elevator access and secure entry via key fob for a stress-free stay.

The chef’s galley kitchen is fully equipped with an oven, microwave, refrigerator, dishwasher, Cuisinart cookware, and a Brita water filter. Guests can enjoy meals at the marble-topped dining table for four, perfectly positioned to take in Savannah’s stunning sunsets. For cocktail enthusiasts, the dining area includes a drinks cupboard with barware and a Vita-Mix blender to craft the perfect drink. Coffee lovers will appreciate the Cuisinart coffee maker with local Savannah Roasters coffee provided.

The bedroom offers a Queen adjustable bed, blackout shades, smart TV, bedside charging stations, oversized walk-in closet with a hotel safe, luggage racks, iron, steamer & drying rack. Professionally cleaned linens provided. The ensuite bathroom offers a luxurious experience with a large shower room featuring a Victorian clawfoot tub, a teak shower seat, and a double vanity with LED-lit mirrors. Natural light filters in through high-set windows, creating a relaxing atmosphere. Guests can indulge in exclusive CAVA bath products for an added touch of luxury.

For those looking to stay active, guests have exclusive access to a private, state-of-the-art gym on the ground floor, available only to residents and guests.

You truly won’t need a car at The Lollygag, thanks to its unbeatable walkability and vibrant surroundings. Nestled in the heart of Savannah’s historic district, you’ll be just a short stroll from the River Walk and the city’s 22 charming squares. Dotted with park benches, majestic oak trees draped in Spanish moss, and statues of prominent Savannahians, these squares offer a perfect place to relax and take in the city's beauty.

Immerse yourself in Savannah’s rich history by joining a walking, pub, food, ghost, or historical tour—each offering a unique perspective on the city’s past and culture. We highly recommend starting with a historic district tour to gain a deeper appreciation of how Savannah became one of the South’s most beautiful and unforgettable cities.

*This vibrant downtown location offers an authentic urban experience, where guests may hear typical city sounds such as early morning trash collection or the buzz of nightlife. To ensure a restful stay, earplugs and eye masks are thoughtfully provided in the bedroom.*
