=== FULL DESCRIPTION ===
If you are looking for a quiet getaway to Savannah, the Wright Suite is the right suite for you! This pretty apartment oozes Savannah charm and is perfect for couples, solo guests, business travelers and anyone who wants to experience all that Savannah has to offer. Food, entertainment, Forsyth Park, River Street...walk anywhere you desire from this apartment on Jones street. This apartment is on the third floor of an historic house smack dab in the heart of the historic district.

The space
If you are looking for a quiet getaway to Savannah, the Wright Suite is the right suite for you! One of the Jones Street Suites, this pretty apartment in an historic home oozes Savannah charm and is perfect for couples, solo guests, business travelers and anyone who wants to experience all that the historic district has to offer. Food, entertainment, Forsyth Park, River Street...walk anywhere you desire from this apartment on Jones St.

Guest access
Please note this suite is on the 3rd floor.  There is a flight of stairs outside to enter the property, and another flight of stairs once you enter the property to get to the suite.

Other things to note
There isn't a designated parking space for this suite, but there are typically plenty of parking spaces available on the street.  You may either park your vehicle right on the street out front or on the nearby side streets if you prefer. Parking on Jones Street is metered Monday – Friday 8 AM-5PM and they have a 2 hour time limit.  Parking is free after 5PM during the weekdays and all day on Saturday and Sunday, with no time limit.
