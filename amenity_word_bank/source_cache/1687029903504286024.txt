=== FULL DESCRIPTION ===
Designed for guests seeking elevated accommodations, exceptional entertaining spaces, and resort-style amenities, this extraordinary home blends sophisticated design with the relaxed atmosphere of a private Savannah oasis.

The space
Maison Barnard by Lucky Savannah is a one-of-a-kind luxury retreat located in the heart of Savannah’s vibrant Starland District, offering an experience unlike anything else in the city. Designed for guests seeking elevated accommodations, exceptional entertaining spaces, and resort-style amenities, this extraordinary home blends sophisticated design with the relaxed atmosphere of a private Savannah oasis.

From the moment you arrive, Maison Barnard sets itself apart. The outdoor experience is truly unmatched, featuring a private saltwater pool, sauna, conversational fire pit, and an incredible pool house complete with a custom bar designed for gathering, celebrating, and unwinding in style. Whether you’re spending afternoons poolside, hosting an intimate evening under the stars, or enjoying cocktails before a night out, every detail of this property was curated to create an unforgettable stay.

Inside, the home showcases impeccable craftsmanship, luxurious finishes, and thoughtfully selected furnishings throughout. The chef’s kitchen is outfitted with top-of-the-line appliances and premium finishes, making it equally suited for preparing meals at home or hosting a private catered dinner experience. Expansive living spaces, soaring ceilings, and abundant natural light create a warm yet refined atmosphere throughout the home.

What we love about Maison Barnard:

Complete Savannah Oasis: Unlike any other home in Savannah, Maison Barnard offers a truly resort-style experience with a private pool, sauna, pool house, custom bar, and multiple outdoor gathering spaces designed for entertaining and relaxation.

Incredible Finishes & Furnishings: Every inch of the home reflects exceptional quality, from the designer furnishings to the luxury appliances and custom details throughout.

Direct Access to Victory North: Maison Barnard shares a unique connection with Victory North, one of Savannah’s premier live music venues. Enjoy a concert just steps from your door, then return home to your own quiet luxury retreat. The Historic District is also just a quick Uber ride away, giving you the perfect balance of energy and privacy.

The home offers three graciously sized bedrooms, each filled with natural light, tall ceilings, and plush king beds. The stunning primary suite features a spa-inspired ensuite bathroom, an impressive walk-in closet, and private access to a lush outdoor porch. Two additional guest bedrooms share a beautifully appointed hall bathroom with a spacious walk-in shower, creating comfort and privacy for families, couples, or groups traveling together.

Perfectly positioned in Savannah’s creative and eclectic Starland District, Maison Barnard places guests near celebrated restaurants, cocktail bars, coffee shops, galleries, and local favorites while offering a level of luxury and amenities rarely found in the city.

Maison Barnard is more than a vacation rental, it is a destination experience all its own.

What is parking like for this home? 
Guests will have access to two private, gated, off-street parking spaces. 

A Note on Events: 

Maison Barnard sits directly adjacent to Victory North, Savannah's premier event venue, making it the natural choice for wedding parties, rehearsal dinners, elopement weekends, milestone celebrations, and corporate retreats that require a home base as memorable as the occasion itself.

The home is fully equipped to host private events on-site, with in-house catering available, a pool house bar, and outdoor spaces that accommodate both intimate gatherings and larger soirées. For guests booking in connection with a Victory North event, Maison Barnard is the obvious answer to where the real celebration happens.

Please contact us to discuss event bookings, buyout rates, and catering arrangements.
