=== FULL DESCRIPTION ===
Our fun, bright, and pristinely renovated 2BR, 1BA condo is in the heart of Savannah's Victorian District, just a few blocks from Forsyth Park! When it comes to impeccable design and attention to detail, this is the ideal place to call home while you enjoy Savannah!  Gorgeous upgraded kitchen, adorable dining nook, modern, spa-like bathroom, and even a private parking space just for you! We hope the love we have poured into our brand new condo shines through, welcoming every guest!

SVR 02492

The space
Welcome to Lemon Lane at The Lemon Drop! 🍋🌿 Tucked away at the rear of our colorful circa-1900 Savannah home, this bright and beautifully renovated first-floor condo feels like your own little hideaway in the Victorian District.

With two bedrooms, one bathroom, a full kitchen, private in-unit laundry, dedicated off-street parking, and your very own fenced courtyard, Lemon Lane has everything you need to settle in and feel at home while exploring Savannah!

🌿 YOUR OWN PRIVATE COURTYARD

One of our favorite things about Lemon Lane is the outdoor space! Enter through the gate and you'll find your own small private, fenced courtyard—a little oasis that's all yours during your stay.

Enjoy your morning coffee outside, gather around the outdoor dining area, or fire up the gas grill for dinner at home. When the sun goes down, sparkling string lights add a little extra ambiance for a relaxing Savannah evening. ✨

🍳 FULL KITCHEN + DINING

Step inside to a thoughtfully renovated full kitchen with modern finishes and everything you need for your stay. Whether you're throwing together breakfast before heading out or enjoying a quiet dinner at home, you'll have the essentials on hand.

The kitchen includes a range, dishwasher, refrigerator with ice maker, cookware, dishes, and other kitchen basics.

There's also a Keurig with a few starter K-Cups provided, because every good day of Savannah exploring should start with coffee! ☕️🍋

🛋️ COMFORTABLE LIVING SPACE

The living room gives everyone a comfortable place to kick back after a day of sightseeing. Stream your favorite shows on the Smart TV using our high-speed WiFi, curl up on the sofa, or just enjoy a little downtime before heading back out for the evening.

The living room also features a queen pull-out sofa, giving larger groups some additional sleeping space when needed.

🛏️ BEDROOM ONE – KING BED

The primary bedroom features a comfortable king-sized bed with a comfortable mattress, crisp white linens, and its own Smart TV.

🛏️ BEDROOM TWO – QUEEN BED

The second bedroom features a queen-sized bed, also with a comfortable mattress, fresh white linens, and its own Smart TV.

Both bedrooms are designed to give you a peaceful, comfortable place to recharge before another day in Savannah. 😴

🚿 SPA-LIKE BATHROOM

The bathroom is one of the spaces where the love we poured into Lemon Lane's renovation really shines!

You'll find a beautiful tiled walk-in shower with a sliding glass door, a statement wood-plank ceiling, modern finishes, and plenty of fresh, fluffy towels. It's bright, clean, and just a little bit spa-like. ✨

🧺 WASHER + DRYER

Packing light or staying awhile? No problem! Lemon Lane has its own private washer and dryer available for guest use, making longer stays and traveling with kids that much easier.  Detergent provided.

🚗 PRIVATE PARKING

Parking in Savannah doesn't have to be complicated! Lemon Lane includes one dedicated off-street parking space right outside the unit, so you'll always know where you're parking when you come home.

Additional free street parking is also available if your group is traveling with more than one vehicle.

🌳 WALKABLE + CLOSE TO FORSYTH PARK

Lemon Lane is located in Savannah's beautiful Victorian District, a quieter, more residential part of the city that still keeps you close to all the fun.

You'll be just a few blocks from iconic Forsyth Park, making it easy to start your morning with a walk beneath the live oaks, visit the famous fountain, grab a coffee, or enjoy one of the many events happening in the park.

You'll also have neighborhood restaurants within close walking distance! Grab a slice at Pizza Party 🍕 or head to Tacos + Tequila for tacos and margaritas. 🌮🍹

Ready to explore downtown? Savannah's downtown nightlife, restaurants, historic squares, and River Street are about a 20-minute walk away, or just a very quick Uber ride. You can also hop aboard Savannah's FREE DOT shuttle, which picks up at Forsyth Park and makes stops throughout the Historic District—an easy way to explore without worrying about driving or parking! 🚎

We love that this location gives you the best of both worlds: a peaceful neighborhood to come home to with so much of Savannah easily accessible on foot, by free shuttle, or with a quick ride.

🐾 PET FRIENDLY

Your four-legged family members are welcome at Lemon Lane! 🐶💛 We are pet friendly with an additional pet fee.

Please include your pet when making your reservation so the appropriate pet fee can be added to your stay.

💛 LOCALLY OWNED + MANAGED

We're Megan & Justin, Savannah locals, and we love getting to share our city with our guests! The Lemon Drop isn't managed by a big, far-away vacation rental company—you'll have local hosts who know Savannah and are nearby if you need us.

We're always happy to share our favorite restaurants, bars, coffee shops, sightseeing spots, hidden gems, and things to do. Looking for the perfect brunch? A great cocktail? Kid-friendly activities? Something fun to do on a rainy day? Just ask! We love helping our guests experience Savannah like a local.

🍋 TRAVELING WITH A BIGGER GROUP?

Lemon Lane is one of multiple completely separate vacation rentals at The Lemon Drop, each with its own private entrance and no shared indoor spaces.

If you're traveling with a larger group, consider booking one or more of our other units - look at our profile to see these listings. It's a great setup for families and friends who want to stay just steps from each other while still having their own bedrooms, bathrooms, kitchens, living spaces, and privacy.

We poured a lot of love into renovating and designing Lemon Lane, and we're so excited to share it with guests coming to experience Savannah's rich history, fun culture, incredible food, and Southern charm. Come settle in, make yourself at home, and enjoy your own little slice of The Lemon Drop! 🍋🌿💛
