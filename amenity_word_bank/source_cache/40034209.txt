=== FULL DESCRIPTION ===
When you step outside our vacation rentals Savannah GA, you can generally just start walking to begin exploring the city. Going south from this property on Habersham will bring you to Troup Square. If you continue east and then south, you will come across the Beach Institute African-American Cultural Center, which is a great place to explore African-American history in Savannah. If you head west, you can find a few more squares like Lafayette and Madison, as well as the Andrew Low House.

The space
Nothing in Savannah is too far away when you stay in our Savannah vacation rentals. Even though many guests enjoy walking, you can also take the Chatham Area Transit in the historic downtown, or the Old Town Trolley. Three of the most popular places to visit in Savannah include Forsyth Park, River Street and the City Market.



You'll find Forsyth Park on the south side of the historic distract and it covers 26 acres of beautiful trails and blooming plants. As you might expect, spring is the best time to see the park in full bloom. The park is overwhelmingly popular with guests and locals alike. It also plays host to various special events during the year.



At the north end of the historic district you'll find the River Street promenade along the Savannah River. Back in the late 1800s it was a major trade hub with cotton warehouses lining the street. In the present day, those warehouses have been replaced with a number of restaurants, shops and attractions. You'll find Emmet Park here, as well as the starting points for dolphin watching charters and riverboat tours.



Go to the very heart of the historic district and you'll find the four blocks of City Market waiting for you with jazz clubs and open-air restaurants. This market has a nostalgic air that captures the feel of Savannah's old open markets. City Market is also known as one of the best spots in the historic district for nightlife.



Another option when you stay in Savannah vacation homes is to take a short drive east to visit Tybee Island. Everything slows down to island time when you're on this charming and picturesque island. You'll have the opportunity to lounge on Atlantic beaches, try some local seafood, or get active by biking around the island or renting watercraft equipment. The must-visit spot on Tybee Island, aside from the beautiful beaches, is the Tybee Lighthouse on the very north end. The lighthouse overlooks the port from the ocean to the Savannah River.



If you're looking for extra things to do during your stay, consider coming around a holiday to take advantage of the many special events occurring in and around Savannah. Some examples are:



• Martin Luther King, Jr. Day Celebration in January

• Georgia Heritage Society's Colonial Faire and Muster in February

• Savannah Black Heritage Festival in February

• Saint Patrick's Day in Savannah in March

• First Saturday on the River festival from March to December

• Fine Arts on the River Festival in May

• Fourth of July Fireworks on the River in July

• Oktoberfest on the River in October

• Tybee Pirate Fest in October

• Savannah Harbor Boat Parade of Lights in November

• Holiday Tour of Homes in December
