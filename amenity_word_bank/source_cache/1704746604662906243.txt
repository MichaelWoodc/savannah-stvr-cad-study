=== FULL DESCRIPTION ===
Tucked away on a quiet lane just off Broughton Street, this charming carriage house apartment offers a peaceful retreat in the heart of Savannah's Historic District. Walk to River Street, City Market, shops, restaurants, and historic squares while enjoying the privacy of your own hidden getaway. The perfect home base for couples, friends, and travelers looking to experience Savannah's best attractions on foot.

The space
This charming carriage house offers the perfect combination of privacy, comfort, and an unbeatable Savannah location. Nestled behind the bustle of the city's premier shopping and dining district, you'll enjoy a peaceful retreat while being just steps from everything that makes Savannah so special.

Thoughtfully designed for couples, friends, solo travelers, or extended stays, the carriage house features an open concept studio apartment feel with comfortable living spaces. You will get to enjoy a fully equipped kitchen, and all the modern conveniences needed to feel right at home. Whether you're spending your days exploring historic squares, shopping local boutiques, or dining at Savannah's best restaurants, you'll love returning to your own private hideaway at the end of the day.


After a day of exploring, relax with a glass of wine, enjoy a quiet evening tucked away from the crowds, or simply unwind in a space designed for comfort and convenience. Whether you're visiting for a romantic getaway, a girls' trip, a business stay, or your first visit to Savannah, this hidden carriage house offers an authentic local experience in one of the city's most desirable locations.

Why Guests Love This Home

• Private carriage house tucked away on a quiet historic lane
• Steps from Broughton Street shopping, dining, and nightlife
• 5-minute walk to River Street
• Less than 10-minute walk to City Market
• Approximately 20-minute walk to Forsyth Park
• Fully equipped kitchen
• Fast Wi-Fi and Smart TV
• Ideal for couples, solo travelers, business travelers, and extended stays
• Located in Savannah's highly sought-after North Historic District

Experience Savannah like a local while enjoying the comfort, privacy, and charm of your own hidden retreat in the heart of the city.

Guest access
Guest will have full access to the carriage house apartment. There is a private entrance with smart lock access.

Other things to note
The rental is located in the heart of downtown, yet on a more residential street. There are some city noises such as trash trucks, ship horns, sirens, trains and vehicle traffic that you may not be accustomed to.

Guests should also know that the main house is also a vacation rental.
