Welcome to the Historic Downtown Victorian Home Near Forsyth, a beautifully restored two-story residence from 1900. Located in Savannah’s charming Victorian District, just a short walk from Forsyth Park and minutes from River Street, this elegant home offers private gated parking, a fenced yard, modern amenities, and timeless Southern character. Enjoy a fenced yard, private gated parking, and all the conveniences of home: an alarm system, smart TV with Netflix and Amazon Channels, high-speed Wi-Fi, a fully equipped kitchen, and a charcoal grill.
