The space
Welcome to The Squares of Savannah, a stunning collection of five unique and thoughtfully designed apartments located in a prime location. Surrounded by a beautifully manicured courtyard and garden, this property offers a charming retreat in the heart of Savannah’s historic district.

The Chippewa Square Apartment is a charming garden-level unit located on the ground floor of this historic home. With 3 bedrooms and 1 bathroom, it comfortably accommodates up to 5 guests, offering a warm and inviting retreat overlooking the iconic Forsyth Park. Thoughtfully designed with tasteful finishes, this apartment seamlessly blends historic charm with modern comfort.

The sleeping arrangements are perfect for families or groups, featuring a luxurious king bed in the primary bedroom, a full bed in the second and a twin bed in the third bedroom. Each space has been carefully curated to provide a relaxing and restful stay

What Makes The Squares of Savannah Special?
Unbeatable Location:
Perfectly situated on Forsyth Park, guests can enjoy Savannah’s largest green space just outside their door. The park is ideal for morning strolls, weekend farmers’ markets, and leisurely afternoons. Additionally, the property is just steps from local coffee shops, renowned restaurants, and the vibrant Starland District.

Enchanting Outdoor Spaces:
The lushly landscaped courtyard provides a serene space for all guests to enjoy. Whether you’re savoring a morning coffee, an evening glass of wine, or simply soaking in the beauty of Savannah, this shared garden area offers a peaceful escape.

The Chippewa at The Squares of Savannah promises a memorable stay filled with charm, comfort, and the perfect blend of historic elegance and modern luxuries.

Guests will have access to 1 private parking space directly behind the home off of the lane. Parking can accommodate 1 mid-size SUV or full-size sedan - trucks, large SUVs, etc., are encourage to park on-street.
