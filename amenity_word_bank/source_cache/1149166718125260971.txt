=== FULL DESCRIPTION ===
This luxurious estate offers plenty of space for families or groups with its 3 separate units and a total of 5 bedrooms. Guests can enjoy the convenience of off-street parking, a rarity in downtown Savannah, and can easily explore the city on foot. The property exudes southern charm with its classic federal-style architecture and its location on an idyllic tree-lined street draped with Spanish moss.

The space
Discover the perfect blend of contemporary and vintage design at this stunning estate featuring three uniquely designed units: the spacious 3-bedroom main house, the charming garden apartment, and the cozy carriage house. Each unit is fully equipped with its own kitchen, allowing for a comfortable and convenient stay.

Get lost in your favorite shows and movies with the six smart TVs scattered throughout the estate. Whether you're looking to relax in the courtyard around the fire pit, or enjoy a stroll around the neighborhood, this estate offers the ultimate sanctuary for all guests. Book your stay now and experience luxury living at its finest.
