=== FULL DESCRIPTION ===
Experience the mystery of Savannah as you follow directions to your secret floor in the heart of downtown. Savor the industrial feel of the original mechanical room of an 1800s department store. Watch the top of freight ships pass under the iconic Talmadge Bridge from your elevated view.  

SVR-00843

The space
We are located within steps of the St. Patrick's Day festivities in City Market and two blocks from the St. Patrick's Day Parade Route! 

Two king bedrooms with exposed brick, one bathroom with a luxurious shower featuring two shower heads, full kitchen with modern appliances, comfortable living room, and transom windows to bring natural light into every room. Enjoy a rarely available view of the Talmadge Bridge and downtown Savannah. Exposed brick, beautiful light fixtures, quiet ceiling fans, and washer/dryer. 

This was our home for over 4 wonderful years until early 2018; it is where we lived when we got married, and where we brought home our first baby and watched him take his first steps. You'll benefit from our extensive local tips and our pride in making our former home a unique and wonderful place to stay. This isn't your typical vacation rental experience - it's better!

Guest access
The condo is completely set up for guests. Make yourself at home! 

Children and infants are very welcome but please note that the condo is not child proofed. We trust every parent to understand their own children and limitations as we are parents ourselves. Feel free to ask questions about any potential concerns!

Other things to note
The penthouse is the only unit on the top floor of a historic building. The elevator stops on the 4th floor and you must take one flight of stairs from the 4th to the 5th. The small effort provides you with a tremendous amount of privacy and quiet.

Because the condo is high above bustling Broughton below and shares no walls with neighbors, we are proud to offer one of the quietest and most private escapes in the heart of downtown. The condo shares a floor with the elevator room and you will be able to hear the low mechanical hum of an old fashioned elevator when it is in use. Many days and evenings no street noise reaches us at all, but you should not be surprised to hear some of the excitement, particularly on weekends. While we have never received a complaint about noise, we provide noise machines for maximum comfort while you sleep.

This is an urban condo with no private parking spaces. You may pay for parking on the street or in the nearby Robinson or Whitaker Garage (both about one block away). We are happy to answer your questions. Just let us know!
