=== FULL DESCRIPTION ===
Experience comfort and charm in this riverfront condo in a historic, Savannah 1857 architectural jewel! This bright, airy 1BR/1BA retreat mixes original details with modern convenience! Enjoy a fully stocked kitchen with eat-in counter seating for four, spacious living room (with pull-out!) fast WiFi, and in-unit laundry. One parking pass for a nearby garage is included. Meticulously designed with modern amenities, this gem offers an exceptional stay in the heart of historic downtown!

SVR-02994

The space
Welcome to our exquisite condo in the heart of historic downtown Savannah! Located in a beautifully preserved 1857 building, this bright and light-filled retreat offers sweeping views of the Savannah River through floor-to-ceiling windows, providing a picturesque setting for your stay.

Step into the fully stocked kitchen, equipped with all modern conveniences and amenities to make your culinary adventures a breeze. Enjoy a fresh cup of coffee from the Keurig, complete with complimentary K-cups, creamers, and sugar. The eat-in counter with seating for four makes dining in convenient and enjoyable.

The living room is designed for comfort and relaxation, featuring chic décor and a soothing atmosphere. The sofa pulls out into a queen-sized bed, perfect for accommodating an extra guest. Both the living room and the bedroom are equipped with flat-screen TVs with streaming capabilities, ensuring you have plenty of entertainment options. Stay connected with fast WiFi throughout the condo.

The bedroom is a tranquil oasis with a king-sized bed and a spacious closet for all your storage needs. The full bathroom boasts a spacious sink vanity and a large, walk-in shower, creating a spa-like experience that invites you to unwind and rejuvenate.

Stunning original features, such as the heart pine floors and the incredible architecture of the 1857 building, add charm and character to this exceptional space. For your convenience, an in-unit washer and dryer are available, making it easy to take care of laundry during your stay.

One parking pass for the nearby Congress Street iParkit garage, just a few blocks away, is included with your stay, ensuring you have a hassle-free parking experience.

With its perfect location in the heart of everything, our condo offers easy access to all the best attractions, dining, and entertainment that historic Savannah has to offer. Whether you're here to explore the rich history, enjoy the vibrant city life, or simply relax by the river, this condo provides an exceptional home base for your Savannah adventure.

Guest access
The entire condo is all yours from check-in at 3pm until check-out at 10am! The condo is completely private. To access the condo, you will enter the building, passing through a small common foyer and hallway. At the end of the hallway there is your coded entry to your condo! Please note, the foyer/hallways area is common, with two coded entry doors (one for you, at the end of the hallway and the other off the foyer for the neighboring condo).

Other things to note
- The best thing about our condo, in our opinion, is the location! We are in the heart of downtown Savannah, overlooking the river, and central to all the amazing places that folks come to the city to visit! HOWEVER, with our incredible location comes noise! Guests should expect noise from the river side tourists, adjoining and nearby businesses, and nearby traffic. Noise should be expected at all hours. If you don't feel as though you would be comfortable with urban, city noise throughout your stay, we do not recommend this space for your Savannah travels. 

- We include one parking pass for the nearby Congress Street iParkit garage. The garage is located three blocks away. Unfortunately, Airbnb does not provide the option to check off "off-site, free parking" in the amenities list, so we like to clarify that we do provide one pass for this nearby garage, however it is three blocks away from the condo and not "on-site." You can, however, pull up to the condo to unload your luggage while idling your vehicle, then proceed to the parking garage, to avoid having to tote your belongings.
