=== FULL DESCRIPTION ===
The Victorian Duo is a charming and vibrant 4-bedroom, 2-bathroom; two story apartment located in the heart of downtown Savannah, offering the perfect blend of historic charm and modern luxury. Recently renovated, the apartment features colorful decor, large windows that flood the space with natural light, and tall ceilings that highlight its historic character. Just three blocks from Forsyth Park, guests can enjoy easy access to the city's best cafes, bars, and restaurants.

The space
KEY FEATURES:

☀ Victorian home built in 1910

☀ 4 bedrooms / 2 baths / Sleeps 8

☀ Two modern, well-equipped kitchens with stainless steel appliances + coffee bars (Keurig & drip makers)

☀ Dog friendly (fee applies)

☀ WiFi and Roku entertainment systems throughout

☀ Board games and books for enjoyment

☀ Convenient location, walkable to Forsyth Park & Historic Savannah attractions

☀ Private outdoor covered porch for relaxing or dining

☀ Free street parking

☀ Washer & dryer in each unit for guest use



☆☆ BEDROOMS ☆☆

The Victorian Duo features four beautifully decorated bedrooms across two levels, each designed for comfort and relaxation.



Primary Bedrooms (2) – Each unit includes a spacious primary bedroom with a luxurious king-size bed, extra pillows, cozy blankets, Roku smart TV, fast Wi-Fi, and bedside tables with reading lamps. One also includes a small seating area, perfect for curling up with a book.



Secondary Bedrooms (2) – Each second bedroom features a queen-size bed with extra pillows and a soft blanket for added comfort. Both rooms are equipped with Roku smart TVs, fast Wi-Fi, and bedside tables with lamps.



☆☆ BATHROOMS ☆☆

The home includes two full bathrooms, one on each level. Both feature modern walk-in showers with fresh fixtures while maintaining touches of historic charm. Stocked with shampoo, conditioner, and body wash, these bathrooms are ready for your stay.



☆☆ KITCHENS ☆☆

Each unit has its own bright, fully equipped kitchen with stainless steel appliances, cookware, utensils, and dishware. Coffee lovers will appreciate the coffee bars with both Keurig and drip coffee makers. Whether you’re cooking a family meal or grabbing a quick bite, you’ll have everything you need.



☆☆ LIVING & DINING ☆☆

Each apartment offers a comfortable living space with Roku smart TVs, colorful accents, and plenty of natural light from tall Victorian windows. Dining tables in each unit provide seating for meals, board games, or planning your Savannah adventures.



☆☆ OUTDOOR SPACE ☆☆

The exterior of this home features a charming private covered porch with a dining table and chairs, perfect for morning coffee, evening wine, or simply relaxing after a day of exploring.



☆☆ LOCATION ☆☆

Situated just 2.5 blocks from Forsyth Park and 1 mile from River Street, The Victorian Duo places you in the heart of Savannah’s Historic District. You’ll be within walking distance of the city’s best cafes, bars, and restaurants, with easy access to the free DOT trolley and Uber.
