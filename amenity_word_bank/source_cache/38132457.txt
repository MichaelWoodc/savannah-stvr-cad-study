=== FULL DESCRIPTION ===
Incredible location on historic and famous Jones Street!  This spacious studio on the "most beautiful street in the USA" is in the heart of the Historic District of Savannah!  Enjoy being close enough to walk to many downtown restaurants, sites, and the river, while still being able to take advantage of a quiet, tucked away retreat in the heart of the city!

SVR-00825

The space
Our adorable residence has everything you might need for a vacation away from it all!  The studio apartment features a queen bed and full bath.  Our kitchen has all the essentials including silverware, basic cookware, coffee, cream and sugar.  In the living room, the couch pulls out to a sofa bed for an extra guest or two kiddos.

Guest access
The entire bottom level of the carriage house is yours!  The courtyard is a shared space with guests who may be staying in the unit above.

Other things to note
This carriage house has two units - ask about booking both for larger parties! Check our profile for pictures of the other listing.

I stayed 1  night at this studio-style carriage house on Jones Street. While the location in the South Historic District is absolutely prime & the property has all the standard amenities, the cleanliness & comfort fell short. The layout is essentially one open room (only the bathroom has a door), making it ideal for a couple, but not for families. The sofa is extremely worn, & the pull-out mattress was completely shot, uncomfortable, & full of chip crumbs. The queen bed was pushed into a far corner, & I noticed stains on the bedskirt & hairs on the fitted sheet. Additionally, the pillows had a noticeable body odor smell. The cleanliness of the rest of the unit was also disappointing. The tile floors turned our socks brown & felt sticky in areas. The bathroom shower was cramped, had soap scum buildup, & the bottom of the liner was moldy. Compared to other updated, pristine properties in the historic district, this unit felt significantly overpriced for the condition it is in.
