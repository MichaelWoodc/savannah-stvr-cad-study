Park View-A Private Retreat at Forysth Park. 
Park Off-Street, Walk Everywhere.
One of The Suites at Forsyth

Barely a stone's throw from Forsyth Park, Park View at The Suites at Forsyth will set the bar for your vacation rental experience in Savannah. Lovingly restored in the classic Savannah style, you will fall in love with this 1870's townhouse. One of three units in the building, Park View is located upstairs.

City of Savannah Short-Term Vacation Rental Certificate SVR-00408

The space
A Fresh and Modern Space with Assigned Parking for One Car--A Rarity in Savannah!

Beautiful views of famous Forsyth Park are seen from both the living room and kitchen of this thoughtfully renovated space. With gleaming, new hardwood floors, 11-foot ceilings, fully remodeled kitchen and bath, and furnishings and art that were sourced locally from Savannah's antique and consignment shops, this gorgeous urban retreat is the perfect place to experience the old-world charm of Savannah in a fresh and modern space. 

From the front door climb the stairs to the welcoming second floor foyer. Enter a code to enter Park View and you step inside to find an original postal service desk where you can sit to write letters or catch up on email. The living room comfortably seats four and has an oversized, original Milo Baughman sofa for lounging, along with reading lamps and a 40-inch TV. Adjacent to the living room is the airy and functional kitchen providing everything you need to both get the day started and cook a great meal. We supply coffee (Free Trade Organic), teas, spices and oils to save you time and money. The exquisitely tiled bath features a custom tub and shower and an assortment of personal care products. 

The bedroom offers a view of the gorgeous courtyard behind the house. With a super-comfortable queen bed, two night stands, two dressers and a large closet, you will unpack and feel at home. Hidden in a closet you will also find a full-sized washer and dryer. Quality bed linens and towels are provided, along with soaps, shampoo, and conditioner. You will also find a hair dryer, iron and ironing board, as well as a white-noise sound machine. The brand-new heating and cooling system will keep you comfortable year-round.

For your entertainment, enjoy WIFI, cable TV with 70 channels, as well as an streaming video--bring you own codes. For music, you can choose Pandora on the Amazon Fire TV Stick or the Amazon Dot for effortless music streaming. Simply say "Echo, play jazz" (or your favorite selection) and the music will play. 

We use Seventh Generation cleaning products and choose fragrance-free whenever possible. 

Two Outdoor Living Rooms
Gather with friends on the graceful front porch under the glow of the gas lanterns. Each walk-up porch offers seating for six and recessed lighting adds to a warm glow to the gas lamps. 

Delight in the rear courtyard, our crown jewel. This tropical oasis is ideal for morning coffee, afternoon cocktails, dining al fresco, and enjoying the warm Savannah nights in a gently-lit dream garden. 

The fenced and secure courtyard is shared by all of the guests of The Suites at Forsyth* and was designed to be experienced both communally and privately. The center is a large patio where you can gather with family, friends and meet other guests. If you desire a private experience, then slip into one of the four sheltered spaces that nestle at the periphery of the courtyard. These four ‘rooms’ are tucked into the foliage; each with vintage outdoor chairs and tables. The lovely lighting, lush landscaping, and splashing water from the fountain will make the courtyard your favorite place in Savannah--your ‘outdoor living room’.

*The Suites at Forsyth consist of six private residences within two historic, attached homes. We’re confident you’ll fall in love with our 1870's townhouses just like we did! We are two sets of friends who fulfilled a long-held desire to renovate a beautiful structure in one of Savannah's historic districts.

Guest access
This 525 sq. ft. private apartment is at your complete disposal. There is room in the closets, drawers and bathroom shelves for you to unpack and feel at home. Park View is one of three apartments sharing the front door and the front porch on this side of the house. There are six units total that share the courtyard.

Other things to note
Park View is located at the top of a set of stairs. Please consider your ability to carry your luggage.

Neighborhood highlights
All of historic Savannah is literally at your doorstep. Park your car in its private lot and explore the tree-lined streets and gardens of this gorgeous city. Within a few blocks you will find a major grocery store along with restaurants, bars, coffee shops, and more. Around the corner is the stop for the free Dot Shuttle.

Make the most of being so close to Forsyth Park. Shop for veggies each Saturday morning at the Farmer’s Market and also enjoy one or more of the annual events: staging area for the St. Patrick’s Day Parade, Picnic in the Park, Shalom Y’all Jewish Food Festival, Children’s Book Festival, Greening of the Fountain, Sidewalk Arts Festival, Doggie Carnival, Savannah Jazz Festival, Buddy Walk, Mattress Dash, Savannah Pride Festival and Earth Day as well as the many running events: Rock ‘n’ Roll Marathon, Heart of Savannah 5K, Savannah Mile, the Color Run, Publix Savannah Women’s Half and 5K and the Race for Preservation.
