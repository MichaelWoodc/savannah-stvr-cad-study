=== FULL DESCRIPTION ===
Another great aspect of booking Savannah vacation rentals with Southern Belle is the great locations. Our Sky High Savannah property is in the Broughton area. This puts it right in the heart of one of the trendiest places in Savannah. As one of our midsize vacation homes Savannah GA, Sky High Savannah  can sleep a total of four guests in two bedrooms with two bathrooms.

The space
Take a step into this modern apartment and enjoy an integrated, open living and dining area with kitchen. A brick facade sets off the living room along with windows and comfortable sofa seating. The kitchen is immediately adjacent and has lots of room to cook great meals during your stay. The bedrooms are well-appointed and furnished with plenty of windows and a ceiling fan. Despite the overall modern appearance of this apartment, the occasional brick wall and wood floors add a nostalgic touch. Windows offer great views of the surrounding neighborhood.



The next step after checking into your Savannah vacation rentals is to decide where to visit first. This home happens to be near two historic squares in Ellis and Telfair. Ellis is right next to the exciting City Market district and it also features a water fountain, lots of shaded tables and a unique life-size chess set. Telfair is the backyard paradise of the Telfair Academy and Jepson Center, which are both great places to enjoy art. You can wander through the square and then go visit the nearby museums.



Forsyth Park is always the number one must-see spot for guests staying in our vacation homes Savannah GA. The 30 acre park is a fun place to relax at after long hours of sightseeing, but you'll still want to have your camera ready for the sights inside the park. The Forsyth Fountain, war memorials, Fragrant Garden for the Blind and the three centuries old Candler Oak are all top sights. Also available inside the park are lots of space for picnics, a pool, playground and cafe.



Another fun thing to do in Savannah is embark upon a history tour. Savannah's history goes back some three centuries to 1733 and the available tours reflect all the different facets of this long history. You can take Civil War tours, architecture tours, ghost tours and much more. The most popular tours are the walking tours through the Historic District and Bonaventure Cemetery. If a walking tour sounds too tiring, then you can also opt for a trolley ride in the Old Savannah Tour or Old Town Trolley Tour.



Any extra time on your stay and you might consider taking the short drive out to Tybee Island. This oceanfront paradise is only 30 minutes outside downtown Savannah. There you'll have your choice of sandy beaches, secluded beaches and riverfront beaches. You can simply relax or stroll through the neighborhood shops, or check out some water activities with available charters and watercraft rentals.
