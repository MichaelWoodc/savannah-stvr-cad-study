=== FULL DESCRIPTION ===
Experience Savannah charm on famous Jones Street in this historic mariner cottage. Our cozy, first-floor condo features 2 bedrooms (king + queen), 1 bath, full kitchen, laundry, fast WiFi, TVs in both bedrooms, and a pullout sofa for that extra guest. Relax in the lovely, shared courtyard with seating, picnic table, and grill or unwind in the comfortable living room with plenty of board games and a little library after exploring downtown Savannah from our perfect, walkable location!

SVR 03179

The space
Welcome to your authentic Savannah getaway on iconic Jones Street, in Savannah's Landmark Historic District — often considered one of the most beautiful streets in America! Nestled inside an authentic, mariner cottage duplex, this private first-floor condo blends timeless Savannah charm with modern comfort for the perfect downtown stay.

This thoughtfully designed space has a separate entrance and features two comfortable bedrooms — one with a king bed and one with a queen bed — plus a pullout sofa in the living room for that additional guest. Both bedrooms include TVs, and the home is equipped with fast WiFi, making it ideal for relaxing after a day exploring the Historic District.

The full kitchen includes everything you need for cooking at home, while the bathroom features a large, double-head shower and in-unit laundry for added convenience. In the living room, you’ll find a cozy little library, board games, and inviting spaces to unwind and enjoy the slower pace of Savannah life.

Step outside to the lovely, shared courtyard, complete with seating areas, a picnic table, and a grill — perfect for morning coffee, afternoon cocktails, or winding down after a full day exploring the city!

Located in the heart of historic downtown Savannah, you’ll be within walking distance of beautiful squares, restaurants, shops, parks, and riverfront attractions while enjoying the authentic character and old-world charm that make Savannah so unforgettable.

Guest access
The entire first floor condo of this duplex is yours! Once you enter the front door, guests will find their private entrance for condo A. Up the stairs is the separate, private entrance for the other condo in the duplex. The courtyard is a shared space with guests who may be staying in the unit above.

Other things to note
This mariner cottage row home has two units - ask about booking both for larger parties! Check our profile for pictures of the other listing.

Please note! We tried to maintain many of the original architectural features, including the two fireplace hearths. However, these are NON-OPERATIONAL (thus, not listed as a fireplace amenity), and are just for aesthetic appeal and a bit of old-world charm!
