=== FULL DESCRIPTION ===
Discover Savannah from the Corner Store Retreat, a stylish and peaceful 1-bedroom apartment in the heart of the historic district. Comfortably sleeps up to 4. Features a private brick patio, a fully equipped kitchen, and luxury touches. Perfectly located for exploring the city's best landmarks, dining, and shops on foot. Your ideal urban home base for an unforgettable trip.

The space
Welcome to the Corner Store Retreat, an elegant and peaceful residence in the heart of Savannah's vibrant urban landscape. This stylish apartment is your perfect home base for exploring the city.

Step into the warm and inviting open-plan living area, where a comfortable green sofa invites you to relax after a day of sightseeing. The living space flows seamlessly into a modern kitchen, complete with a breakfast bar for casual meals. The serene bedroom features a unique botanical accent wall and offers direct access through a glass door to your own private brick patio—a perfect spot for morning coffee or an evening drink.

To enhance your stay, we provide a luxury experience with thoughtful touches:
* A well-stocked coffee bar to start your day right
* A full-size luxury air mattress can be provided for parties of up to four guests.

Guest access
You will have private and exclusive access to the entire one-bedroom apartment, including the charming brick patio. For your convenience and security, entry is managed with a smart lock. We will provide your unique access code prior to your arrival for a seamless self-check-in experience.

Other things to note
* This is a one-bedroom, one-bathroom apartment. For parties of up to four guests, a full-size luxury air mattress can be provided.
