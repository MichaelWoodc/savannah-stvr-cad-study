Historic 3BR Downtown Savannah Apartment with Modern Comfort

Step into the perfect blend of historic charm and modern luxury in this beautifully renovated 3-bedroom, 2.5-bathroom apartment in the heart of downtown Savannah. 

With spacious interiors, premium amenities, and an unbeatable location, this home is ideal for families, business travelers, or anyone looking to experience Savannah in style.  

🏡 Highlights of Your Stay:
✨ Historic Charm, Modern Comfort – Newly renovated while preserving stunning architectural details.  
🛏️ Spacious & Cozy Bedrooms – All beds feature 14-inch memory foam mattresses with soft and firm pillows for personalized comfort.  
🛁 Luxurious Master Suite – Located on the third floor, this private retreat boasts an ensuite bathroom, a private balcony, and a projector for movie nights.
🚗 2 Off-Street Parking Spots + EV Charger – A rare find in downtown Savannah! Bring your electric vehicle and park with ease.  
🛒 Walkable to Everything – Stroll or Uber to Savannah’s best dining, shopping, and nightlife. Kroger grocery store is just two blocks away. 

📍 Unbeatable Location:
🏙️ Walk or Uber anywhere downtown – Enjoy easy access to historic landmarks, River Street, Forsyth Park, and more.  

🔹Additional Details:
- There are two other Airbnb listings and a long-term tenant on-site.  Those other spaces are not included in this listing.  Feel free to inquire about booking for larger groups.
- 🚫 No pets or parties allowed to maintain a peaceful environment for all guests.  

Come experience the charm, comfort, and convenience of downtown Savannah.  Book your stay today! ✨

Guest access
Guests will have full access to apartment "A" in the duplex, shared access to the backyard and patio, and a full driveway for two cars, equipped with EV charger.

*Note:  The apartment is in a property that houses other guests, so we ask that the space and neighboring property are treated with dignity and respect at all times.  Please keep noise to a minimum between 8pm and 8am as per city ordinance.  Pets, parties, and events are strictly prohibited.

Other things to note
We ask that the space, other guests, and our neighbors are treated with dignity and respect.  Please respect the home and its rules.  This will help us maintain peace and ensure that all of our guests have a wonderful Savannah Stay!

This is a three-level apartment and you must climb 17-steps to reach the second story where two guest rooms are located.  The master suite is located on the third floor.  There are no bedrooms on the first floor.

You can park up to two compact cars on the driveway located to the right of the brick building.  Please ensure to not block the sidewalk to avoid fines. 
 You can also park for free on the street in front of the property, on East Waldburg Street. This area is shared with other residents and it is on a first-come, first-served basis with no assigned spots.

Please be aware that even though we spray for bugs on a quarterly basis, you might see palmetto bugs, wasps, and other insects during your stay.  They usually come out to die, but please do your best to keep them out of the house by keeping the windows and doors shut at all times, only bringing food items to the dining room, and keeping trash in the trash can.
