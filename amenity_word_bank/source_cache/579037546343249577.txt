=== FULL DESCRIPTION ===
Treat yourself to a calm and peaceful stay in this charming yet incredibly spacious townhome! Located just two blocks from Forsyth Park and on a quiet, tree-lined street, this rental allows you to savor the best of Savannah’s historic district with class and tranquility. This is a prime location that's walking distance to all that Savannah has to offer...you won't want to miss out!

SVR-02402

The space
On the top floor of this palatial residence, you’ll find an open floor layout and large bay windows that allow in plenty of sunlight throughout the day. The living room’s neutral green walls reflect back natural light, allowing for its guests to enjoy a warm and welcoming coffee date early morning or mid-afternoon. Come nightfall, you can take in the spacious living room for a cocktail with friends or share some laughs over a board game! A large, Victorian-style desk sits off to the right of the east side of the room, by its expansive windows, and offers a glass top for easy writing. It's the best place in the home to get some work done while also getting in your daily dose of Vitamin D. Sturdy, wooden floors uphold the townhome’s charming furniture which include a detailed, full body mirror, a 6-person dining table, and even a small chess area! A stunning chandelier located right above the dining table adds an extra touch of elegance to the space. 

On the first level, you’ll find a roomy kitchen with plenty of cabinetry and stainless steel appliances for effortless baking and cooking. Its beige/brown granite countertops make for easy cleaning, cutting and weekly meal prepping. The kitchen also sports two lateral, double-pane windows, allowing in plenty of fresh air whenever necessary. A round table big enough for four sits off to the corner and leads into the hallway. There you’ll find a beautiful red velvet couch overlooking the stairway.

Guest access
The whole parlor level of the home is yours! We do offer access to the laundry room downstairs, where you'll find the washer/dryer side by side and is free for you to use! PLEASE NOTE - this is the only shared space in the home, where the unit downstairs (ground level) may access the laundry space also. Please be cognizant of noise levels when coming down to do laundry, so you do not disturb our other guests.

Other things to note
Just to reiterate - the space where the laundry room is downstairs, is shared with other guests in the downstairs unit. This is the only shared space in the house, and is separated be a secure door.
