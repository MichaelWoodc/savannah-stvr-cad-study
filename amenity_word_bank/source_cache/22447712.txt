=== FULL DESCRIPTION ===
Stay beneath the towering oak trees along Forsyth Park in this brand new two-bedroom town home with private parking.

The space
A welcome addition to the Victorian District, Howard Grande is a multilevel, two-bedroom town home just a stone's throw away from the famous Fountain at Forsyth Park. 

Park your car in the private garage and step up into the contemporary living quarters of your dreams. The first floor boasts high ceilings and an open floor plan, ideal for family gatherings and weekend getaways with close friends. Take advantage of the granite countertops and stainless steel appliances and cook a full meal for you and your guests, or, step onto the cobblestone pavement and walk a short distance to some of Savannah's most renowned restaurants and cafes.

The upstairs hosts two bedrooms, each with en suite baths. The primary bedroom boasts a King size bed, and the guest room has a Queen size bed. A laundry room with stacking washer and dryer makes extended stays ideal. Expecting additional guests? Add on the downstairs garden apartment by renting the full home, Howard Grand Main + Garden, for an extra Queen bedroom and full bathroom!

Head South a few blocks and find yourself immersed in Savannah's hipster culture - with a wine bar, vegan cafe, and whole foods market adjacent to the Forsyth Park Farmer's Market. A few minutes' walk in the other direction gets you closer to the bustling city center. Take the scenic route through downtown's beautiful squares and end at the well-known River Walk - a great place to grab a beer and people watch.

Whether you're a family looking for a quiet respite close to the city center, or a pair of couples wanting to tap into the local scene, Howard Grande is the perfect home for you!

Sleeping Configuration:
Bedroom 1: King Size Bed
Bedroom 2: Queen Size Bed

We can welcome your dog companions at this pet friendly home, but ask that you find other arrangements for cats, birds, and other furry friends. There is a $130 pet fee (non-refundable) with a 35 lb. maximum weight limit per dog and a limit of 2 dogs per reservation. Some homes may allow larger dogs, please inquire for more information.
