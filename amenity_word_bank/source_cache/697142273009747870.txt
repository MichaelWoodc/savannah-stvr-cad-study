If beautiful surroundings make you feel right at home, you’re sure to love Houston Street Retreat. A cozy Savannah vacation rental by Crawford Square Savannah, Houston Street Retreat offers the perfect accommodations for a couple's getaway. From the welcoming entryway to the convenient location in Historic Savannah, this wonderful option as a vacation rental in Savannah GA is set to exceed your expectations.

The space
Houston Street Retreat proves that a small vacation home can be truly grand. The home peppers in many antique touches that give it a rare feel of authenticity. Once inside the home, a petite foyer leads into a parlor-like living room with oversized windows, soft couches, and a flat-panel television. Next, a crisp, pristine kitchen located just off of the living room shines with a full-sized oven, refrigerator, and microwave in stainless steel. The kitchen also shows off lovely white cabinets topped with butcher-board countertops. A dining table with seating for two provides a cozy spot for dining at home.



Next, the home's stunning bedroom dazzles with an inn-like atmosphere. Enjoy a plush queen bed facing an antique brick fireplace. The bedroom also features twin windows, a ceiling fan, a dresser, and a flat-panel television. Lastly, a full bathroom provides the ultimate oasis for enjoying a fully tiled "floating" shower with glass doors.



Houston Street Retreat offers an incredible location that makes it one of the top Savannah vacation rentals for guests looking to see, do, and experience everything that beautiful Savannah has to offer. When planning a Savannah itinerary, travelers can focus on museums, historic landmarks, famous parks, amazing restaurants, fun festivals, guided tours, and much more. There's a little bit of something for everyone in Savannah because this is a city that prides itself on hospitality.



A first stop for many people who visit Savannah for sightseeing is the Savannah Historic District. Peppered with many of the most beautiful estates in Savannah, the Savannah Historic district is an urban zone known for its bright-colored mansions, lush parks, old cemeteries, and much more. When chasing history and beauty in the city, an itinerary simply has to include Bonaventure Cemetery, Forsyth Park, Cathedral of Saint John the Baptist, the American Prohibition Museum, Olde Pink House, the American Prohibition Museum, Chippewa Square, and the Davenport House Museum. For those in the mood to get off the beaten path for a while, opportunities to explore Skidaway Island State Park, Old Fort Jackson, and Wormsloe Historic Site should not be missed.



There are many ways to create a totally personalized experience in Savannah. For those looking for an incredible day of shopping and dining, City Market offers a pedestrian-only zone full of shops and restaurants. Savannah visitors should also find time to play by the water on Savannah's famed River Street. Full of bars and restaurants, River Street is an incredible place for a night out in Savannah for those who enjoy live music, bustling crowds, and river views. The River Street area is also the place to pick up a ferry to get to Hutchinson Island. Another fun thing to do in Savannah is to book a spot on a guided tour. Savannah offers a wide range of different tours to satisfy different interests. Guests can choose from trolley tours, carriage rides, guided walking tours, after-dark cemetery tours, and guided pub crawls. Finally, Savannah's amazing festivals and cultural events attract countless visitors each year. While new events are constantly being added to the city's roster, the list of ongoing favorites includes the Savannah Jazz Festival, the Savannah Greek Festival, the Great Ogeechee Seafood Festival, the Savannah Film Festival, Christmas on the River, and Savannah’s Waterfront New Year’s Celebration.



Houston Street Retreat is undoubtedly one of the vacation rentals Savannah GA visitors will adore when the goal is to find a comfortable, quaint property to enjoy during a holiday. It is one of the rare Savannah vacation homes to pack so much into such a petite property. Guests will truly feel as though they're staying at a quaint estate that has been in the family for generations when they get comfortable at this authentic and charming property. The home's location on Houston Street makes it one of the best vacation rentals Savannah GA visitors looking to be near all of the city's top attractions can pick. Don't miss this one-bedroom, one-bathroom Savannah vacation home with room for up to two guests.
