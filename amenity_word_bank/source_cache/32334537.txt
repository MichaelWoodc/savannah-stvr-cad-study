=== FULL DESCRIPTION ===
Located on Savannah's most famed street, enjoy all the modern amenities this home has to offer while staying true to the build date of 1860.

The space
Enjoy a grand visit at Broughton Estate with all the modern amenities you learn to love while on vacation. This three-bedroom home on Broughton Street - Savannah's "Main Street" - is within short walking distance to most of downtown's best shopping, dining, and attractions. 

An architectural gem, this home offers the original historic details dating back to 1860, including intricate iron work and meticulously maintained interiors. The home's large parlors are perfect for your guests, for short or extended stays. With ample room for up to six Lucky guests, you truly feel like a local. Share a home-cooked (or catered) meal with friends and family at the formal dining room. Or, sip a few glasses of wine in the string light-lit courtyard before heading out for a night on the town.

Enter this home on the ground level, where you will locate the fully stocked kitchen, a cozy breakfast nook, and a large inviting bedroom with King bed. On the second floor, you will be amazed by the impeccable crown-moldings throughout the bright and grand parlors. A formal dining room with seating for 6 open to the tastefully decorated living room gives ease while entertaining your guests. Located on the third floor are two large bedrooms, one with Queen bed and Twin sleeper chair, and then a primary suite with King bed. Guests will enjoy the luxurious beds with hotel-grade linens and plenty of closet space to feel at home. An en suite bath with double vanity, standing shower and large soaking tub can be found just off the primary bedroom.

Whats parking like for this home?
The garage that accommodates two full size sedans, or two mid-size SUV's, is conveniently located across the courtyard to ease guests peace of mind and eliminate the stress of finding parking on-street.
