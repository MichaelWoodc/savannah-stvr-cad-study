=== FULL DESCRIPTION ===
The Sunlit Victorian is a cheerful 1898 home in Savannah’s Historic Landmark District, just three blocks from Forsyth Park. This bright two-story retreat features four upstairs bedrooms, a fun blue kitchen, a dining room with a playable piano, and a fenced backyard with a fire pit—perfect for relaxing after a day exploring the city. Ideal for families and groups, it comfortably sleeps up to 10 guests with driveway parking and dog-friendly accommodations.

The space
Nestled in the heart of the Savannah Historic Landmark District, this Victorian home offers a delightful blend of historic charm and modern amenities. Built in 1898 and meticulously renovated in 2025, the house boasts four elegantly appointed bedrooms, each designed for ultimate comfort with high-end bedding, cozy throws, and coordinating accent pillows. The open floor plan seamlessly connects the family room, dining room, and kitchen, creating a spacious and inviting environment perfect for socializing and entertaining. The kitchen, equipped with stainless steel appliances and a gas range, is perfect for cooking, while the adjacent dining room accommodates six, making it ideal for family dinners and gatherings.



The home's outdoor spaces are equally charming. The front porch, with its swing invites you to relax and enjoy a glass of wine as you watch the world go by. At the back, the rear covered porch is perfect for privacy and enjoying the fenced yard with your dog.  Additional conveniences include two designated parking spots in the driveway and free street parking in the front. Located less than three blocks from Forsyth Park, this home places you within easy reach of Savannah's most iconic attractions, making it an ideal home base for exploring the city's rich history and vibrant culture.

SVR-01363

Guest access
Guests have a private access and will receive a key code prior to arrival to enter the home. This keycode is changed after every guest for your safety.

Other things to note
The homes' prime location is a significant draw for guests. Being less than three blocks from Forsyth Park, visitors have easy access to one of Savannah's most iconic and picturesque spots. The surrounding Historic Landmark District is a treasure trove of cultural and historical attractions, charming boutiques, art galleries, and over 100 restaurants. After a day of exploring the city's vibrant scene, guests can unwind on the inviting front porch, enjoy alfresco dining on the rear deck, or let their pets play in the fenced-in area. With its ideal mix of historic charm, modern amenities, and convenient location, the home offers a truly unique and memorable Savannah experience.
