Anderson Place

The space
STVR Permit: SVR-01107



Conveniently located in the Historic District, this beautifully designed two-story home is just a mile from Forsyth Park and close to an array of fantastic attractions. 



Perfect for entertaining, the professionally decorated interior features an expansive open living area replete with stylish furnishings, sleek hardwood floors, and ample natural light. Unwind with a movie on the flat-screen TV, or spread out and relax in multiple sitting areas. Aspiring home chefs will love the modern kitchen, outfitted with marble counter space and a full suite of upscale appliances. 



 Added perks include free WiFi, a washer/dryer, and an upgraded HVAC system. 



WHAT’S NEARBY

Situated on the edge of the Savannah Historic District, your home is near the Georgia State Railroad Museum, the Savannah Children’s Museum, the American Prohibition Museum, and Chippewa Square. Travel 1 mile to the Cathedral Basilica of St. John the Baptist, or 4.5 miles to Old Fort Jackson.



THINGS TO KNOW

The fireplaces are not operable.

A long-term tenant lives in the upstairs carriage apartment located behind this rental. The apartment and the home have separate entrances.
