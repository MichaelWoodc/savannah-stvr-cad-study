=== FULL DESCRIPTION ===
Located only two blocks away from Forsyth Park, this garage apartment/ mother-in-law suite offers comfort and ease for getting around the city. There are restaurants, bars, coffeeshops, and grocery stores in very close proximity. The Savannah River is a little over 1 mile to the north. While there is both a queen size bed and pull-out couch, the space is ideal for solo adventurers and couples.

The space
This space is a newer construction with modern amenities (finished in 2015) but was designed to complement the main house (circa 1916). Reclaimed materials from the previous structure that stood at this location were used in the construction.
You will have access to the entire second floor of the garage (550 sq ft) and the back patio. The patio is shared with the main house.
This is a fully licensed Short Term Vacation Rental. SVR-00700

Other things to note
Please enjoy yourself and have a good time, but also respect the space. Thank you!

Neighborhood highlights
My home is located in the Eastern Victorian district of Savannah. It is a residential neighborhood but commerce still abounds. If you're a foodie, some top notch restaurants nearby are Local 11 Ten and Cotton & Rye. I also enjoy Tequila Town for margaritas and their interesting BBQ/ Tex Mex plates. Sentient Bean is a popular coffeeshops. The Kroger is four blocks away and Brighter Day, the local organic grocery store, is at the south end of Forsyth Park.
