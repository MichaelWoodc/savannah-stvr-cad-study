=== FULL DESCRIPTION ===
Historic Savannah Multi-Family townhome, Walk to River Street, Free Parking

Other things to note
Stay right in the heart of downtown Savannah at Habersham 2, a two-story spacious apartment in the historic Paul Hamilton Williams House, built in 1791. This home offers classic charm in an urban setting. This historic property has been professionally decorated by Tybee Vacation Rentals with premium guest comfort in mind. 



Habersham 2 features 2 bedrooms and 1 and a half baths. This historic, 2 Story apartment has a unique spiral staircase that connects the interior spaces. On the upper floor, there is a spacious living room with plenty of comfortable seating and a 55" Smart HDTV.  The kitchen is equipped with an apartment-sized refrigerator and freezer (no ice maker in the freezer) a stove and oven, a microwave oven, and a dishwasher. There is a combination coffee maker, so coffee lovers will enjoy having the choice of regular drip coffee or Keurig single cup coffee. There is an eat-in kitchen bar top table that has seating for 4 with additional seating for 2 at a cafe table on the attached porch. There is also a half bath powder room on this level.



The lower level of the property, which is on the ground level, has direct access to the parking area from the Queen room which is extra convenient for when for loading and unloading luggage. This level hosts both of the bedrooms. The first bedroom has a king-size bed and TV; the second bedroom has a Queen bed with a TV. The bathroom, located just off the hall, has a tub and shower combination. Guests will feel the rich history on this level which features original brick walls which also helps to keep the rooms cool in the summer and cozy in the winter. A utility closet with stacked washer and dryer is also located on this level off of the King bedroom. 



The main entrance to the apartment is located on the second floor, and there is a second entrance on the ground level. Wireless Internet/WIFI is available for guest use. There are raised inflatable queen and twin beds available at the home for extra sleeping. 1 car off-street parking only. This rental is not pet-friendly. Note: Fireplaces are sealed and non-working, but they add to the ambiance! Also, the interior spiral staircase is very narrow and also not recommended for people with mobility issues.



About the Paul Hamilton Williams House: The Paul Hamilton Williams House, built in 1791, is conveniently located just off the corner of Habersham and Bay Street. It is just a short walk from Historic River Street, Emmet Park, Warren Square, Factor's Walk, and many other points of interest in the historic district. The home is located right beside the popular bistro B. Matthews Eatery at the corner of Bay Street and Habersham on the Eastern end of the historic district. The house is situated in a great central location from which to explore the world-famous Historic District of Savannah, and many local attractions, pubs, and restaurants are just a short walk away. Every First Friday of the month stroll down to River Street for Fireworks and enjoy live music, the arts, and other events. There is always plenty of food, fun, and culture in Savannah!



You will be located a short 20-minute drive from Tybee Island, Savannah's beach. Come out for a day trip to enjoy the beach, fishing, visiting Ft. Pulaski, or climbing Georgia's oldest tallest lighthouse. Want to experience the best of both worlds? Savannah-Tybee Vacation Rental packages and discounts are available. Combine your vacation experiences by visiting both Historic Savannah and Tybee Island and Save 5% off.
