=== FULL DESCRIPTION ===
There's no denying that this is one of the most classic Savannah vacation rentals available. It is also a highly practical choice for those looking for room for up to six guests. Luxury on Lincoln wows from the street with help from its stately brick façade and classic shutters. Of course, the Southern stateliness continues once you step through the front door of one of the loveliest vacation rentals Savannah GA guests can choose.

The space
Luxury on Lincoln boasts 1,932 square feet of space. Six guests will find ample space for living and relaxation within the beautifully decorated walls of this estate. The home has two bedrooms, two full bathrooms and one partial bathroom. Guests enter through a fresh foyer featuring a polished staircase and stunning hardwood floors. A lovely living room with comfortable couches, a fireplace and a flat-screen television invites guests to feel at home once they enter this property. The living room has been beautifully furnished to create the feel of a contemporary Savannah parlor room. Windows in the living room face the street to provide glimpses of Savannah life.



Luxury on Lincoln's kitchen offers a purely luxurious experience for guests. This fully updated kitchen has a state-of-the-art layout featuring new cabinets, countertops and appliances. Touches like appliances in stainless steel and subway-tile backsplash create a truly high-end look. The kitchen area also contains a wet bar and wine cooler. A lovely breakfast nook with seating for four enjoys streams of sunshine within the kitchen. Luxury on Lincoln is one of the rare Savannah vacation rentals to contain a full formal dining room. This large, impeccably decorated room features a fireplace, a long buffet, a crystal chandelier and accent seating. The long, elegant dining table in the room offers seating for six.



The first floor of this home provides access to an adjoining outdoor courtyard. This private space ensures that any night spent in Savannah can turn into something magical. Guests can drag mint juleps from the home's wet bar out to the courtyard to sip in a fully private and fenced-in area. The home's brick courtyard features seating, an umbrella, a gas grill, a decorative fountain and other garden-like touches that create a very relaxing space for both morning coffee and pre-bed cocktails.



The sleeping situation inside Luxury on Lincoln boasts one king bedroom, one queen bedroom and one sleeper sofa. The home's king bedroom is truly a space of prestige. A king-sized bed, a fireplace, a crystal-accented chandelier, a large dresser, elegant linens and large windows define this room. The king bedroom is attached to an oversized bathroom featuring new fixtures, hardwood floors and a full tub with shower. The home's queen bedroom dazzles with touches like a poster bed, a petite chandelier, a fireplace, a large armoire and large windows. The second floor of the home features a fully updated bathroom with pristine fixtures, a tub with shower and a floor-to-ceiling window.



Extra Features

Luxury on Lincoln's classic charm masks a list of fully modern amenities for guests to enjoy. Flat-screen televisions peppered throughout the home, free Wi-Fi and a complete laundry setup featuring a washer and dryer ensure that no modern comforts are lacking. The location of Luxury on Lincoln is one of the property's greatest assets. The home's location allows guest to walk just a few oak-shaded streets to get everywhere within the Savannah Historic District. Of course, the elegant interior and exterior of Luxury on Lincoln make it as worthy as any attraction you'll find as you explore Savannah's historic neighborhoods. The Savannah experience is simply enriched when you stay at one of the most beautiful Savannah vacation homes available.



Staying at One of the Savannah Vacation Homes That Will Put the City at Your Fingertips

Guests enjoy a fully immersive, authentic experience when staying at a property like Luxury on Lincoln due to the home's atmosphere and location. This home serves as the perfect starting point for each day's itinerary. Guests of the property will find that it is so easy to get to attractions like Forsyth Park, Bonaventure Cemetery, the Cathedral of Saint John the Baptist, the Sorrell Weed House, the American Prohibition Museum, the Historic Savannah Theatre and the Mercer Williams House Museum. Days in Savannah can also be spent enjoying the lively vibes at spots like City Market, River Street and Riverfront Plaza. Fun excursions to spots like Old Fort Jackson and Skidway Island State Park are also in the mix. Of course, hours can be spent absorbing the beautiful architecture and painstaking upkeep behind the properties of the famed Savannah Historic District.



Savannah is known for its very unique tours. In fact, few visit the city without partaking in a guided "ghost" tour, cemetery tour, distillery tour or after-dark carriage tour. These thrill-filled activities allow visitors from out of town to really get a deep sense of the history of a city that's known for its green gardens and elaborate estates. Events that take place in the city throughout the year accompany Savannah's year-round attractions quite nicely. The list of the city's top festivals and events includes the Savannah Music Festival, the Savannah Craft Brew Fest, the Savannah Food Day Festival, the Telfair Art Fair, the Fine Arts on the River Festival, the River Street Fourth of July Celebration, the Annual Savannah Tour of Homes and Gardens, the Savannah Seafood Festival, Oktoberfest on the River, the Holiday Tour of Homes and Christmas on the River.
