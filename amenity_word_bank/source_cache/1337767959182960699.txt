=== FULL DESCRIPTION ===
This absolutely stunning 1869 townhouse will take you back in time with its quintessential Southern charm and grandeur! BOTH the parlor and garden units are tastefully restored, mixing preserved original details with all the modern amenities one might need. Comes with TWO dedicated parking spots! Perfect for a family getaway or a gathering with friends! Steps from magnificent Forsyth Park and within walking distance to all that downtown has to offer! Hard to beat this gem!

SVR-02276 & 02277

The space
The entire townhouse - two fully equipped condos, are all yours! 

Parlor (upper) Condo - Two of the three bedrooms boast king sized beds and en suite bathrooms. The third bedroom has a cozy double sized bed and can access one of the full bathrooms from the main hallway. Downstairs you will find a spacious living room with a pull-out sofa, large formal dining room with impressive light fixtures, half bathroom, and bright and sunny kitchen with modern appliances including an electric range, farmhouse sink, and all the cookware you might need for a quick breakfast or a 5 course dinner! Off the kitchen, travel down the exterior staircase to the charming courtyard garden...the perfect spot to enjoy a glass of wine or sip some sweet tea! 

Garden (lower) Condo - Spacious one-bedroom (garden-level) condo. King sized bed in bedroom with TV, little library, ample storage, and fun décor! Living room boasts a second television, another pull-out sofa, fantastic bar, picnic basket for those afternoons in the park, games, record player, and plenty of room to relax after a long day of exploring Savannah! Fully equipped and stocked eat-in kitchen is perfect for preparing a quick breakfast or a full dinner for a night in! Enjoy that southern weather and take a cup of coffee (or wine!) out to the shared patio area and enjoy the slowed down, Southern lifestyle! 

Driving into town? You will have TWO parking passes for the small private lot around the corner plus free street parking right out front!

Guest access
Both condos and the courtyard are all yours!

Other things to note
Don't be afraid to reach out if you need anything!  We live just two blocks away.

Neighborhood highlights
This is probably our favorite neighborhood in the city! The house is off a quiet street split by a treeway full of Spanish-moss covered oaks. Stroll just a block and a half and enter the middle of gorgeous Forsyth Park, the city's crown jewel! Take in the beautiful architecture and shaded streets surrounding this condo that straddles Savannah's Victorian and Historic Districts. Collin's in Forsyth, Crystal Beer Parlor, Black Rabbit, 700 Drayton, Local 11Ten and other incredible bars and restaurants are right outside your door.

If you choose to stay with us and need lots of recommendations...don't worry! We are happy to provide personalized recommendations based on your interests and preferences...just ask us!
