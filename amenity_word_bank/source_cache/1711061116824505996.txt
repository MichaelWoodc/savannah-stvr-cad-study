=== FULL DESCRIPTION ===
Make memories in this spacious historic mansion, built in 1888 - just one block from Forsyth Park! Perfect for large groups, this unique property sleeps 12 with 8 bedrooms and 4.5 baths. Guests will enjoy exclusive access to both the upper and lower condos, featuring 2 full kitchens, 2 living rooms, and plenty of room to relax and gather! Outdoor escapes await with two inviting balconies, large back porch, and backyard! Experience true Southern charm in this timeless estate!

SVR 02226 and 02227

The space
Step into the timeless beauty of historic Savannah and make yourself at home in this expansive retreat just one block from Forsyth Park. Blending preserved architectural character with modern comfort, this one-of-a-kind property offers abundant space for large groups to gather, unwind, and experience the best of Savannah together.

The first floor space features a comfortable and private living space with a large primary bedroom with a king bed, TV, and an en suite bathroom complete with a beautiful clawfoot tub. There is also a cozy queen bedroom with TV, a smaller twin bedroom, an additional full bathroom, and a laundry room. The eat-in kitchen is fully equipped for gathering and meals, while the living room provides a welcoming space to relax. A small office off the kitchen opens directly onto the back deck, where you’ll find outdoor dining furniture overlooking the lush backyard.

The upper levels (second and third floors) offer even more space to gather and unwind. On the second floor, guests will find a second full kitchen, an additional laundry space, a large entertaining area with a spacious dining table, and a living space, perfect for relaxing or socializing. This level also includes three bedrooms: two king bedrooms, each with private walk-out balconies, and one twin bedroom with a desk workspace.

The third floor features two additional king bedrooms, connected by a cozy lofted lounge space with a TV—perfect for movie nights or a quiet retreat—along with a convenient half bathroom.

With two fully stocked kitchens, multiple living areas, outdoor spaces including a deck, backyard, and balconies, and room to comfortably sleep up to 12 guests, this thoughtfully appointed home is ideal for family gatherings, celebrations, and group getaways. Free street parking is available all around the home, and the location makes it easy to walk to Savannah’s best parks, restaurants, shops, and historic landmarks.

Guest access
The entire space is yours! You have full, private access to both condos in this home, along with the back yard and deck.

Other things to note
Don't be afraid to reach out if you need anything!  We live super close-by and are always happy to help out, even mid-stay.
