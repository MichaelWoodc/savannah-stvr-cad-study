=== FULL DESCRIPTION ===
Nestled on Savannah’s famed Jones Street, this charming 1-bed, 1-bath retreat blends history with modern comfort. Featuring a cozy galley kitchen, elegant historic touches, and a peaceful sitting room, it’s perfect for relaxation. An inviting entertainment room offers space to unwind. Steps from downtown’s top attractions, dining, and the city's signature charm, this quiet escape is ideal for a romantic getaway or solo retreat. Experience Savannah’s beauty from the heart of it all! 
SVR-02637

The space
Welcome to your charming Savannah retreat on historic Jones Street! This cozy second-floor apartment blends timeless character with modern convenience, creating an inviting escape for travelers seeking comfort and style.
Step inside to find original hardwood floors and whitewashed brick walls that set the tone for a warm and welcoming stay. Thoughtfully decorated with elegant furnishings, soft lighting, and tasteful accents, the space exudes both sophistication and comfort—perfect for relaxing after a day of exploring Savannah’s cobblestone streets.
The galley kitchen is fully stocked with everything you need to prepare meals, including pots, pans, utensils, and small appliances like a coffee maker, toaster, and microwave. Whether you're cooking a romantic dinner or enjoying a morning cup of coffee, the cozy dining table for two offers the perfect setting.
This thoughtfully designed apartment features two distinct spaces for unwinding. The formal sitting room provides a peaceful nook for morning coffee or a quiet read, while the cozy entertainment area invites you to curl up on the plush sofa and stream your favorite shows on the smart TV. Whether you're seeking solitude or a laid-back movie night, this space adapts to your mood.
Retreat to the bedroom, where a brand-new queen mattress with fresh linens ensures a restful night’s sleep. The modernized bathroom is stocked with all the essentials—soft towels, toilet paper, shampoo, and body wash—so you can refresh with ease after a day of sightseeing.
Located on the second floor, the apartment is accessed through a charming common parlor lined with classic red bricks and lush greenery, offering a beautiful introduction to your stay. Just steps from Savannah’s top attractions, restaurants, and historic squares, this cozy yet elegant retreat is the perfect home base for your Southern getaway.

Neighborhood highlights
The Timekeeper’s Nook is located just north of Forsyth Park on the west side of the Historic District. Surrounded by some of the best dining options in town, you are just a minute's walk from many of the local and out-of-town favorites.
Grab a delicious southern bite and Mrs. Wilke's Dining Room. Pour a perfect pint at the Crystal Beer Parlor, or start your day with a short walk to Savannah Coffee Roasters.
Short strolls towards the river walk will only increase your options with places like Noble Fare, fine dining, The Olde Pink House, The Lady & Sons, Chart House, and many more.
Start your bar/pub tour by stopping into some staples of Savannah, including Six Pence Pub, O’Connell’s Irish Pub, or Dub’s Public House. Enjoy riverfront views at Myrtle & Rose Rooftop Bar down on the river walk.
Are you looking to shop while you are in town? You are just a 14-minute walk from the famed City Market, where you can enjoy endless boutique shops and many brand-name stores.
Explore the rich history of Savannah by stopping into one of the many museums and historical sites. Just minutes from Blue on Broad are the Web Military Museum, Telfair Museums, Owens-Thomas House & Slave Quarters, and the American Prohibition Museum.
A stop in Savannah wouldn’t be complete without a walk-through of Forsyth Park, just a 20-minute walk away. Or enjoy Washington Square, another beautiful green space just minutes away.
Want to take it all in at once while riding or biking? We are just minutes from Old Town Trolley Tours of Savannah and Savannah Bike Tours. Ready for a little on-the-water adventure? Try out the Savannah Belles Ferries to see the beautiful skyline of Savannah from the riverfront.
