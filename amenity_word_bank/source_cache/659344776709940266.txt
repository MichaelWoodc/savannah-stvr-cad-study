=== FULL DESCRIPTION ===
Elevate your trip to Savannah by staying in a piece of history. Designed by the renowned architect Alfred S. Eichberg this 3-bedroom, 3.5 bath has been fully renovated, beautifully decorated, and is ready to help you unwind after a day of adventure. Enjoy a custom kitchen, shared front porch, side/wraparound deck, gorgeous architectural touches, and preserved history throughout. 2 minutes to Forsyth Park, 20 min to SAV International.
SVR-01315

The space
Constructed in 1891 and believed to be one of the four remaining residential structures designed by the renowned architect Alfred S. Eichberg this unit is truly a piece of Savannah history. Originally born in New York, Eichberg moved to Georgia and was commissioned on notable structures including the Telfair Hospital, New Hanover County Courthouse (NC), and Eichberg Hall of the Savannah College of Art and Design.  Known for his Romanesque revival style of design, you’ll find incredible detail and architectural aspects throughout the Eichberg Manor. 
Located on the lower level of the entire residential home the manor boasts vaulted ceilings, original hardwood floors, and an open concept that makes the residence feel spacious and refreshing.
In the living room relax and unwind on an oversized sectional while you stream your favorite shows on the brand-new TV. Step right from the living space into a fully custom kitchen featuring brand new appliances, granite countertops, and small appliances like a coffee maker and toaster. The kitchen is fully appointed with all the pots, pans, chef knife set, and cooking utensils you need to cook full course meals during your stay. With a dining table set for 6 just off the kitchen, this is the perfect place to gather at the end of the day. 
The manor features two ensuites with their own bathrooms and a third bedroom with the third full bathroom adjacent. All bedrooms feature brand new everything including beds, TVs, and bedroom furniture. In the main suite find gorgeous bay windows centered on a brand-new king mattress with fresh linens and posh pillows. In the second and third bedrooms enjoy queen mattresses, new linens, and antique touches that bring out the historical feel of the home. Vaulted ceilings with oversized windows pour in natural light that makes every bedroom feel open and airy. 
With three and half bathrooms the home provides all the space you need to spread out while preparing for a day on the town. All bathrooms have been completely updated with new vanities, fixtures, and custom tile work. Enjoy a step-in shower in one bathroom and a shower/tub combination in the second bathroom. The powder room is located in the front hall. All bathrooms are outfitted with the essentials including towels, body wash, and shampoo.

Guest access
There is free parking in the rear driveway area (grass). The entire home is for your use. You have access to the side balcony (private) and the shared front porch. To reiterate, you have one king bed and 2 queens. Each bedroom has a Smart TV.

Other things to note
This is part of a quadplex, so please be courteous to your neighbors and keep noise levels to a minimum from 10PM - 8AM. 
Absolutely no parties. 
Only the number of guests listed on the reservation is permitted at the property. 
No illicit activities, peddling, or soliciting are allowed on the property. 
Do not smoke on the property or near the home. Clean up all smoke butts. 
Must be 21 years of age to drink alcohol. 
Report all damaged property to the property owner immediately. 
Treat the apartment with respect, as if it was your own property.

Neighborhood highlights
Perfectly located in the Victorian District East, you will be in the heart of historic Savannah and centrally located to all the incredible eats, drinks, and sights Savannah has to offer. From your door step the southern portion of Forsyth Park is just 2-minute walk. On your way grab a delicious cup of coffee at the Sentient Bean or Alexander’s Bistro featuring fair trade coffee and lattes. Need a breakfast bite to start your day? Pick up some great American style breakfast with a retro feel at Betty Bombers Diner.
Looking for a sandwich and a pint? Stroll on down to the Black Rabbit, a small, yet incredible sandwich shop by day, pub by night. Need a taco fix? Try out Bull Street Tacos for scratch made Mexican dishes.
Looking for fine dining and a great vibe? Head on over Local 11 Ten and their chic rooftop bar and lounge known as The Perch. Enjoy handcrafted cocktails, and delicious dishes. The Perch is open year around and features gas heaters for cooler winter days.

Take an adventure outside of just the neighborhood by visiting one of Savannah’s many museums. Mercer-William’s House Museum, Savannah African Art Museum, American Prohibition Museum, and many more are just minutes away. Tap into the artistic nature of Savannah by stopping into Savannah’s College of Art and Design Museum of Art. Want to get more hands on and make some memories? Try blowing glass at Hostess City Hot Glass, a perfect time for the whole family.
Not enough time to walk all the sights of Savannah? Take it all in at once while riding or biking. We are just minutes away from Old Town Trolley Tours of Savannah and Savannah Bike Tours. Check out the city’s skyline from the water by jumping on one of the Savannah Belle’s ferries, with multiple stops all along Savannah’s river front.
Getting around is easy with walkable streets, bike rentals, and uber service. Jump on Savannah’s public transit system with the Abercorn and Park SB stop just a 2-minute walk from the manor.
