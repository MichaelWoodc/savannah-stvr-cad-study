IMPORTANT BOOKING & HOUSE INFORMATION

We’re so glad you’re considering staying at The Queen! Because this is a historic 1888 home in a residential neighborhood, we ask all guests to review the following information carefully before booking. These guidelines help us protect the home, our neighbors, and everyone staying on the property.

Pets & Animals

We’re unable to accommodate pets or emotional support animals (ESAs). We regularly host guests with significant pet allergies, so this is a firm house policy.

If you are traveling with a service animal, please contact us before booking so we can make sure we understand your needs and any applicable requirements.

Families & Children 🧸

Families and children are very welcome! 😊 Please keep in mind, however, that The Queen is a historic home and has not been child-proofed.

The property includes several features that require close supervision of young children:

• Three separate staircases. All have railings, but they are not gated.
• Furniture, shelving, and décor are not secured or anchored for child safety.
• Original architectural features may differ from those found in newer homes.

Guests traveling with children are responsible for supervising them at all times. By booking, guests acknowledge that the home is family-friendly but not child-proof.

Guest Count & Maximum Occupancy

Please make sure your guest count is accurate when booking.

The base rate includes up to 11 guests. Guests above 11 are $30 per person, per day and must be added to the reservation before arrival.

Our maximum occupancy is 14 people total on the property at any time, including visitors. If someone will be stopping by during your stay, they must be included in your approved guest count in advance.

This requirement is important for our City of Savannah short-term rental regulations, insurance, and Airbnb coverage.

Exterior security cameras are located around the property and are fully disclosed in the listing’s safety information.

Bachelorette Gatherings, Birthdays & Group Stays

We’ve welcomed many wonderful bachelorette groups, birthdays, family reunions, and other celebrations, and respectful groups are absolutely welcome.

For celebration or group stays, we simply ask that you contact us before booking so we can make sure the home is a good fit for your plans.

For festive gatherings, we generally look for a primary guest with positive Airbnb hosting history, ideally including at least one 5-star review.

New to Airbnb? That’s okay! Please introduce yourself, tell us a little about your group and your plans, and confirm that everyone has reviewed and agrees to the house rules.

We love a fun weekend, but The Queen is not a late-night party venue. The home is located in a quiet, upscale residential neighborhood, so gatherings need to remain respectful of our neighbors.

Visitors & Outside Guests

Only registered and approved guests may enter the property.

For example, if your reservation lists eight guests, then eight people are approved to use the property. If you would like friends or family to visit during your stay, please contact us beforehand so they can be added to the approved guest count.

We can never exceed 14 people on the property at one time.

This policy helps us comply with City regulations and maintain appropriate insurance and Airbnb coverage for both guests and the property.

Events & Gatherings

The maximum occupancy is 14 people total at all times.

We are not licensed as a commercial event venue, so we cannot host weddings, receptions, large parties, or gatherings exceeding 14 people.

These limitations help us comply with City of Savannah regulations and protect our short-term rental license.

Planning something smaller, such as professional photography, filming, or another creative use involving 14 people or fewer? Please reach out before booking. We’re happy to discuss whether the property would be a good fit.

We also have several separate carriage-house accommodations toward the rear of the property that may be available if your group needs additional overnight lodging.

Decorations & Noise

We want you to enjoy your celebration while helping us preserve this historic home.

Please do not use:

• Confetti
• Glitter
• Feathers
• Tape
• Adhesives on walls, ceilings, doors, or furnishings

Even removable tape and adhesive products have repeatedly damaged the paint and plaster, so we unfortunately cannot allow them.

Please also avoid amplified music or loud gatherings. Respectful, low-key celebrations are very welcome.

Third-Party Service Providers

We’re happy to consider outside professionals such as massage therapists, hairstylists, makeup artists, photographers, and similar service providers with prior written approval.

Before arrival, please provide:

• The provider’s full name
• Business name and license, when applicable
• Contact phone number

Please do not bring unapproved vendors or service providers onto the property.

The primary guest remains responsible for any damage or issues caused by members of their group or approved outside providers.

Complimentary Luggage Storage

We offer a complimentary shared outdoor luggage-storage area beneath Unit C’s stairs. Look for the door marked with an “L” (this is separate from the Amazon delivery door).

A few things to know:

• The space is approximately 7 ft. x 5 ft.
• It is shared with guests from several units.
• It is available during daytime hours.
• Because it is an outdoor storage area, you may occasionally see leaves or light outdoor debris.
• Please do not leave valuables or irreplaceable items inside.

This is a complimentary convenience rather than a hotel-style luggage room, and belongings are stored at the guest’s discretion.

For guests who prefer a dedicated commercial luggage service, Stow My Bags on Montgomery Street is approximately one mile away and offers paid luggage services.

Bugs, Flies & Savannah Nature

Savannah’s warm climate and beautiful trees unfortunately also mean that occasional insects are a normal part of life here.

We maintain regular professional pest-control service throughout the property. Even with routine treatment, an occasional live or dead insect may sometimes appear, particularly when exterior doors have been left open.

Keeping doors closed as much as possible is one of the best ways to minimize insects inside the home.

An occasional insect sighting is considered part of the local environment rather than an indication that the property has not been professionally maintained.

Artwork

You’ll find original and tasteful artwork throughout the home, including a few classical-style artistic depictions of the human form.

We mention this simply so guests traveling with children or those who may prefer otherwise are aware before booking.

Neighborhood & City Activity

The Queen is located in a historic urban neighborhood. As with most city properties, guests may occasionally encounter normal daytime activity such as landscaping, construction, street work, deliveries, or other neighborhood maintenance.

We’re happy to consider outside professionals such as massage therapists, hairstylists, makeup artists, photographers, and similar service providers with prior written approval.

Before arrival, please provide:

• The provider’s full name
• Business name and license, when applicable
• Contact phone number

Please do not bring unapproved vendors or service providers onto the property.

The primary guest remains responsible for any damage or issues caused by members of their group or approved outside providers.

Complimentary Luggage Storage

We offer a complimentary shared outdoor luggage-storage area beneath Unit C’s stairs. Look for the door marked with an “L” (this is separate from the Amazon delivery door).

A few things to know:

• The space is approximately 7 ft. x 5 ft.
• It is shared with guests from several units.
• It is available during daytime hours.
• Because it is an outdoor storage area, you may occasionally see leaves or light outdoor debris.
• Please do not leave valuables or irreplaceable items inside.

This is a complimentary convenience rather than a hotel-style luggage room, and belongings are stored at the guest’s discretion.

For guests who prefer a dedicated commercial luggage service, Stow My Bags on Montgomery Street is approximately one mile away and offers paid luggage services.

Bugs, Flies & Savannah Nature

Savannah’s warm climate and beautiful trees unfortunately also mean that occasional insects are a normal part of life here.

We maintain regular professional pest-control service throughout the property. Even with routine treatment, an occasional live or dead insect may sometimes appear, particularly when exterior doors have been left open.

Keeping doors closed as much as possible is one of the best ways to minimize insects inside the home.

An occasional insect sighting is considered part of the local environment rather than an indication that the property has not been professionally maintained.

Artwork

You’ll find original and tasteful artwork throughout the home, including a few classical-style artistic depictions of the human form.
