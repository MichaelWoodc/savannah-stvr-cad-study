Stay steps from Savannah’s iconic Forsyth Park fountain in your own private historic carriage house. Tucked behind a classic Savannah home, this separate retreat blends Southern charm with modern comfort in a prime Historic District location.

Start your morning with coffee in the courtyard, stroll the Saturday Farmers Market, and walk to top restaurants, wedding venues, and River Street — no car needed.

The space
Step into classic Savannah charm—right across the street from the iconic Forsyth Park. This thoughtfully preserved unit features original architectural details, including wood floors, windows, trim, and doors, offering an authentic taste of the city’s historic character.

Enjoy peaceful walks through moss-covered oaks, explore local cafés, or simply relax in your cozy home base in one of Savannah’s most picturesque neighborhoods.
