=== FULL DESCRIPTION ===
This absolutely stunning 1869 townhouse will transport you back in time with its quintessential Southern charm and grandeur. This Savannah beauty has been tastefully restored, mixing thoughtfully preserved original details with all the modern amenities one might need, including a dedicated parking spot! Perfect for a family getaway or a gathering with friend! Just steps from magnificent Forsyth Park and within walking distance to all that downtown has to offer! Hard to beat this gem!

SVR-02276

The space
The entire townhouse is all yours! Two of the three bedrooms boast king sized beds and en suite bathrooms. The third bedroom has a cozy double sized bed and can access one of the full bathrooms from the main hallway. Downstairs you will find a spacious living room, large formal dining room with impressive light fixtures, half bathroom, and bright and sunny kitchen with modern appliances including an electric range, farmhouse sink, and all the cookware you might need for a quick breakfast or a 5 course dinner! Off the kitchen, travel down the exterior staircase to the charming courtyard garden...the perfect spot to enjoy a glass of wine or sip some sweet tea! Wifi and full cable programming? Of course, y'all! This impressive historic 1869 townhome is the epitome of Southern charm, grace, and class with all the modern conveniences a traveler could ask for!

SVR - 2276

Guest access
The entire 3-bedroom, 2.5 bathroom townhome is yours! The back courtyard is the only shared space.

Other things to note

Neighborhood highlights
This is probably our favorite neighborhood in the city! The house is off a quiet street split by a treeway full of Spanish-moss covered oaks. Stroll just a block and a half and enter the middle of gorgeous Forsyth Park, the city's crown jewel! Take in the beautiful architecture and shaded streets surrounding this condo that straddles Savannah's Victorian and Historic Districts. Collin's in Forsyth, Crystal Beer Parlor, Black Rabbit, 700 Drayton, Local 11Ten and other incredible bars and restaurants are right outside your door.

If you choose to stay with us and need lots of recommendations...don't worry! We are happy to provide personalized recommendations based on your interests and preferences...just ask us!
