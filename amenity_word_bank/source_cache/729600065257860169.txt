=== FULL DESCRIPTION ===

The space
This hidden gem in the beautiful Historic District, rests beneath majestic oak trees, in a prime location within walking distance to River Street, City Market, beautiful Squares, and so much more. Beytagh 1872 is your ultimate Savannah retreat!

Stepping into your beautiful digs, you'll immediately be greeted by the essence of this charming Historic home. The open floor plan boasts with tall ceilings, and large windows that fills each room with natural light. Truly an entertainers dream, as the open floor plan allows guests to never feel separated. The gathering room sits just off of the entrance, where everyone in your party can gather together for a movie or game night, which also features original stone hearth fireplaces dating back to 1872. Just beyond, you'll be able to host intimate dinners with friends and family in the formal dining room with seating for 8 guests. As the host of the party you'll find that serving your guests will be seamless, as the transition from the kitchen to the dining room is easily accessible. The renovated kitchen offers everything needed to prepare your famous home cooked meals, or craft your favorite cocktails and lattes. 

At the back of the home, the first guest suite is perfect for traveling singles, seniors or young children, as this room features two plush twin size beds and an ensuite bathroom with walk-in shower. Upstairs, the two additional guest suites can be found, which are equipped with comfortable, plush beds, to ensure a sound nights sleep. The second suite is fitted with a King, while the third offers a Queen. Both rooms have smart televisions, and a generous amount of storage space - ideal for extended stays. The full bath with tub/shower combo and large vanity is off of the hall, making it convenient for all!

Just out back your private oasis awaits! The charming courtyard is the best place to relax and unwind, or start your day. Whether your enjoying a glass of wine, or a morning cup of coffee, you'll never want to leave. The heavily shaded courtyard is a rarity here in the Historic District, and you will have ample amount of space for your activity of choice. 

What Is Parking Like For This Home?
Parking for this home is on-street and metered, with enforcement by the City of Savannah Monday through Saturday, 8am to 5pm. Guests may conveniently pay using the ParkSavannah app, feed the meter or parking kiosks directly, or utilize a nearby public parking garage. Pre-paid visitor parking passes are available for purchase at the Parking and Mobility Services office. Please take note of posted Sweep Zone signs when parking on the street.

Sleeping Configuration:
Bedroom 1: King Size Bed
Bedroom 2: Queen Size Bed
Bedroom 3: Two Twin Beds

We can welcome your dog companions at this pet friendly home, but ask that you find other arrangements for cats, birds, and other furry friends. There is a $130 pet fee (non-refundable) with a 35 lb. maximum weight limit per dog and a limit of 2 dogs per reservation. Some homes may allow larger dogs, please inquire for more information.
