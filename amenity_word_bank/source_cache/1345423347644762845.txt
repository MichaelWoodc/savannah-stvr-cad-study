The property itself was lovely, but unfortunately our experience with the hosts was very disappointing. The listing also does not clearly explain that the two units are at completely separate addresses, requiring guests to go outside and walk down the street to access the second unit.

We understand that maintenance issues happen, but the way this was handled was extremely unprofessional and significantly impacted our trip. Unfortunately, I would not recommend staying at a property managed by these hosts.
