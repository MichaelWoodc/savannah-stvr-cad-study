=== FULL DESCRIPTION ===
You absolutely cannot find any Savannah vacation homes closer to the crown jewel of the Historic District -- Forsyth Park. The aptly named A Walk in the Park boasts one of the most-wanted addresses among Savannah vacation rentals and for good reason. The 30-acre greenspace has something for everyone, from historic monuments to fresh fare every Saturday at the year-round Farmers' Market.

The space
The Accommodations 



You will succumb to the spell of A Walk in the Park as soon as you realize the full visual impact of the lovely triple-decker Victorian building. Your first-floor flat that can accommodate up to six guests and features two bedrooms with king-sized beds as well as a keen queen Murphy bed in the den. 



Just a short flight up from street-level, a welcoming entry hall ushers you in from your own private entrance, and the first door on your left is a sparkling bathroom. As you enter the living room, you will see fabulous touches like a period fireplace and vintage lighting fixtures that take you back to gracious, bygone days. 



Rich tufted leather sofas, warm hardwood flooring, generous windows and a large wall-mounted flat screen invite you to come in and make yourself at home. Just beyond, you will see the dining table with seats for six and an eye-catching decorative wine rack. 



Through an archway, the kitchen awaits. Here, you will find all of the tools, dishes and utensils you would need to cook up a family feast with the fresh seafood, vegetables and fruit you can find locally. The breakfast nook is a sunny space beneath the window, and the well-designed food prep area features a range-oven, microwave, refrigerator, dishwasher and ample cupboard space. Around the corner near the back door, a washer and dryer stack is tucked away for your use during your Savannah vacation rentals stay. 



Just across the hall from the living area is the second bathroom, which features a shower and plenty of fluffy towels. Next door, you will find one of the comfy king-sized beds, complete with plenty of pillows and plush bedding. A fireplace, a ceiling fan, a chest of drawers and tasteful artwork add to the luxurious yet completely comforting vibe. The second bedroom, just steps away, offers a double closet where you can stow your bags, bedside lamps, a ceiling fan and generous drawer space. The bleached wood motif contributes to the calm serenity of the space. 



A Walk in the Park caters to your comfort with deluxe amenities such as Wi-Fi, central heating and air conditioning, and the latest in TV technology. You will find a second flat-screen television in the den as well as a desk for computer time. Exquisite decorating makes this a fabulous place to put your feet up and relax after a busy day enjoying all Savannah has to offer. French doors from the hallway supply vintage charm while the Murphy bed makes conversion to a sleeping space at night a matter of minutes. 



A Walk in the Park and Beyond 



This accommodation overlooking Forsyth Park may well be the most conveniently located flat of all Savannah vacation homes. Not only are you a scant block away from the park's visitor center where you can find out all about the greenspace, but you are also steps away from a free shuttle bus stop when you want to go sightseeing, shopping and dining throughout this gracious Southern city. 



You are sure to meet other Savannah vacation rentals guests as you stroll through the fragrant gardens in Forsyth Park, grab lunch at a nearby café or cool off near the picturesque fountain, the centerpiece of the park. Pack a picnic lunch and spread your blanket near Forsyth's playground where the kids can entertain themselves for hours. Bring your tennis gear, basketball, bicycles and/or walking shoes to immerse yourself in the full Forsyth experience. 



Also, be sure to check online during your vacation rentals Savannah GA stay for any fairs and festivals that coincide with your visit because you are in a city that celebrates all year long. Some of the events happening in and around Savannah vacation homes near Forsyth Park include: 



Greening of the Fountain, March

Saturday Farmers' Market, all year long

Each one has something different to offer visitors and locals alike, such as the sundial in Johnson Square or the dog drinking fountain in Troup Square. City Market and the River Walk are two additional shopping and dining venues popular with vacation rentals Savannah GA guests.
