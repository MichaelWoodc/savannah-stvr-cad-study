=== FULL DESCRIPTION ===
Welcome to this luxury retreat in the heart of Savannah's historic district! This stunning one-bedroom loft is nestled on Wright Square, it offers a perfect blend of charm and modern touches with a central and walkable location for your visit! The loft sleeps up to four guests and boasts a spacious king bedroom suite. You will be on the St. Patrick's Day parade route and close to all the shops, restaurants and historical destinations of Savannah! We cannot wait to host you!

The space
Step into a world of refined elegance as you enter this beautifully appointed space. Tall ceilings, skylights and designer furnishings create an ambiance of timeless luxury. The loft boasts expansive windows that flood the bedroom with natural light. 

Indulge in the comfort of a plush king-sized bed, adorned with premium linens to ensure a restful night's sleep. The loft sleeps up to four guests. The spacious living area invites you to unwind in style with a leather sofa that pulls out to a memory foam queen size bed. Enjoy a 55in television in the living room AND bedroom. 

You will be able to prepare gourmet meals in the fully equipped kitchen, featuring top-of-the-line appliances and stunning refurbished maghogany butcher block countertops. Dine in at the sleek dining table or enjoy the ease of dining out at all the amazing local restaurants in walking distance that Savannah has to offer.

After a day of exploring Savannah's rich history and vibrant culture, pamper yourself with a rejuvenating refresh in the spacious walk-in shower. Enjoy a double bathroom vanity and spacious bathroom that has been updated to perfection! Plush towels, a hair dryer, clothing steamer and hotel size toiletries are all provided. Extra pillows and sleeper sofa bedding is provided. A pac n play travel crib is available upon request. 

As a guest you can enjoy access to high-speed internet and family games. 
 
Enjoy epitome of luxury living in Savannah's historic district – book your stay at this exquisite one-bedroom loft today and elevate your travel experience to new heights.  We can't wait for you to create lasting memories in one of the most sought-after locations in Savannah!

We can't wait to host you!

Guest access
To access the building guests will receive two codes. One to the shared building entrance and one to the condo door. Please note there is not an elevator in the building. There is a shared entry with a steep stair case to the second floor lofts.

Other things to note
Please note that this condo is in the heart of downtown living. While the location is fabulous it does come with some city noises that you may not be accustomed to from trains, sirens, trash vehicles, delivery trucks, and church bells. We do offer a sound machine and room darkening/sound dampening  shades in the bedroom.
