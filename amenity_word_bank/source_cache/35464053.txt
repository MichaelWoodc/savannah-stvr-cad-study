=== FULL DESCRIPTION ===
Located in the heart of Savannah's Downtown Historic District, you are an even distance from Forsyth Park and Broughton Street shopping. Take advantage of private parking, great location, and tons of Southern Charm at Liberty Blue!

The space
Located up the stairs on the fourth floor of this multiple unit town-home, Liberty Blue boasts with natural light, tree-lined views, and crisp furnishings. Up-to four Lucky guests can stay here without having to compromise location. 

Enter into your private foyer - a perfect place to stow your shoes, and umbrellas before heading upstairs. Just a top of the stairs you will find that the space is filled with plenty of natural light, and offers a split living floor plan. 

The dining room table seats up to four guests, open to the living space and kitchen, which is great for entertaining. The kitchen has received a recent revamp, and boasts with crisp white cabinets. All of the modern conveniences one could wish for are included, which is ideal for short-term or extended stays! Also just off of the kitchen, you will find the charming bathroom with a walk-in shower. 

Across from the dining room, you will find the first bedroom that offers a queen size bed and plenty of privacy. Moving into the living space, you will be pleased with an ample amount of comfortable seating for your entire group. Unwind and catch up on your favorite shows on the large flat screen television located in the center of the living room. 

Toward the back of the home, enter into the "primary" bedroom that offers a king-size bed and tons of space to store your belongings! When a quiet nights rest is due, you will want to unwind in this room. 

You’ll be staying right next to many popular brunch spots! After breakfast, spend some time wandering around Colonial Park Cemetery, the final resting place of many of Savannah’s earliest residents. City Market and River Street, hubs of Savannah’s nightlife and entertainment, are both within an easy walking distance from this property. 

What is parking like for this home?
Liberty Blue includes two tandem private parking spaces just behind the property.
