=== FULL DESCRIPTION ===
Stay in the heart of Savannah at Honey Bee Cottage, a charming circa-1900 two-bedroom cottage just a 5-minute walk from Forsyth Park. With a king bedroom, queen bedroom, queen sofa bed, private fenced patio, and comfortable living spaces, it’s an ideal home base for couples, friends, or a Savannah getaway with your pup. Walk to beautiful squares, restaurants, cafés, and historic sights, then come home and relax in your own private cottage.

The space
🐝 Welcome to Honey Bee Cottage!

After a day exploring Savannah’s oak-lined streets, beautiful squares, restaurants, and historic homes, come back to your own private cottage and unwind. Only two small steps to your front porch and then into your single-story home away from home.

• 2 bedrooms — 1 King + 1 Queen
• 1 bathroom | ~600 sq ft
• Private, fenced patio
• Fully equipped kitchen
• In-home washer & dryer
• Fast WiFi/Internet (1GB + dedicated workspace)
• Central A/C + ceiling fans
• Pet-friendly
• Free street parking (plentiful parking)

🛏️ Sleep Comfortably

Both bedrooms feature comfortable beds, blackout shades, and fresh, inviting spaces designed for a restful night.

• Primary bedroom: King bed, ceiling fan + armoire for clothing and storage
• Second bedroom: Queen bed
• Living room: Queen pull-out sofa
• Blackout shades in both bedrooms
• Additional mirrors make getting ready easier when traveling together

📍 Walk to Forsyth & Explore Savannah

One of the best things about Honey Bee Cottage is its location. Forsyth Park is about a 5-minute walk away, with Savannah’s famous squares, historic architecture, cafés, restaurants, and attractions waiting beyond.

With a Walk Score of 80 and Bike Score of 96, you can leave the car behind and experience much of Savannah on foot.

🚗 Getting Around

• Free street parking near the cottage
• Free DOT downtown shuttle nearby
• Complimentary electric transportation options within the Historic District

Having a car is convenient for trips outside downtown, but you won't need one for much of your Savannah sightseeing.

🌿 Your Private Patio

Step outside to your private, fully fenced patio—a quiet little spot for morning coffee, an afternoon break, or a glass of wine after exploring the city.

🐾 Bringing Your Pup?

Dogs are welcome at Honey Bee Cottage! The private fenced patio makes traveling with your pup a little easier, and Mother Mathilda Beasley Park—with its dedicated dog park—is just a short walk away. Forsyth Park is also about a 5-minute walk from the cottage, giving you plenty of options for getting outside and exploring Savannah together.

☕ Everything You Need

The cottage includes the comforts that make a longer stay easy:

• Fully equipped kitchen with dishwasher, microwave, coffee maker, cookware and essentials
• Fast WiFi (up to 1 Gbps) + Smart TVs + workspace
• Central A/C and heat
• Washer & dryer
• Beach towels and chairs for Tybee Island day trips
• Pack ’n Play available upon request
• Self check-in with a unique Smart Lock PIN provided before arrival

A motion-activated exterior security camera is located on the front porch and faces the front entrance.

STVR Permit: SVR-03253

Guest access
You have the whole cottage to yourselves ! Single-story (2 steps up onto the porch) with private entrance. Smart Lock on front door with your own, personalized code for easy self check-in.

Other things to note
📝 Good to Know

• Pet fee: $75 (applied after booking)
• Early check-in/late check-out available through the guest portal (fees apply)
• No smoking ($100 fee for violations)
• Street cleaning: Tuesdays 12:01am–4:00am—simply move your car to the other side of the street ahead of time

Neighborhood highlights
Honey Bee Cottage is tucked away on a quiet side street right in Savannah’s South Historic District, so you’ll be in the heart of everything while still enjoying some peace and quiet. Your walk to Forsyth Park takes you down a few lovely blocks of E. Hall Street, lined with beautiful historic homes. Along the way, you’ll pass a local favorite coffee shop and bakery called Mate Factor, and just a bit farther is Saint Bibbiana, a fantastic Italian spot. As you enter Forsyth Park, you’ll see The Collins Quarter, known for its amazing brunch and great atmosphere. Heading the other way brings you to Mother Mathilda Beasley Park and several of the city’s charming historic squares. It’s really an ideal location to experience the best of Savannah. Incredibly walkable with all the conveniences you may need (groceries, coffee, restaurants and a local liquor store - Johnny Ganem’s).
