=== FULL DESCRIPTION ===
Our quaint little eclectic garden unit is the most welcoming and relaxing getaway you'll find on Jones Street. Take a soothing stroll down the most beautiful street in America to all of the bars, restaurants and shops that Savannah has to offer. One block from Savannah's famous Mrs. Wilkes and two blocks from Crystal Beer Parlor. If you're looking for a quiet place to retreat after a long day out, look no further.

SVR-01129

The space
The tastefully decorated front garden unit is exceptionally spacious. In addition to the queen bedroom, we offer a sizable living room with a pull-out couch, smart TV and dining bar for two. We have a large island/bar in the kitchen to make cooking or drink prepping a breeze! We have a coffee maker, microwave, stove/oven, refrigerator, toaster and all of the utensils you need for basic cooking.

Guest access
You have the entire apartment to yourself! You will have your own private entrance, we will send the access information 48 hours before your check in date.
