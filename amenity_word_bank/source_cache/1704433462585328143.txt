=== FULL DESCRIPTION ===
Stay in the heart of Savannah's Historic District on iconic Broughton Street, home to some of the city's best shopping, dining, and attractions. This spacious townhouse features two private bedroom suites, comfortable living spaces, and a stunning private courtyard and private parking, perfect for morning coffee or evening dinners & cocktails. Walk to River Street, historic squares, Forsyth Park, restaurants, and boutiques while enjoying the comfort and privacy of your own Savannah retreat.

The space
Tucked along the residential eastern end of Broughton Street, this spacious two-bedroom, two and a half bath townhouse places you in the heart of Savannah's Historic District while offering a peaceful retreat to return to after a day of exploring.

Step outside your front door for a short stroll to River Street, City Market, Savannah's iconic squares, local coffee shops, boutiques, and some of the city's best restaurants. The location truly puts Savannah at your fingertips.

Inside, you'll find generous living spaces designed for gathering and relaxing. The open-concept layout offers plenty of room to spread out, whether you're visiting with family, friends, or enjoying an extended stay. The living room offers lots of seating for lounging or movie nights, as we ll as a fold out sofa bed. Large windows fill the home with natural light, creating a warm and welcoming atmosphere throughout. Enjoy a spacious dining room to eat in or play games at. A fully equipped kitchen is stocked with basic essentials for dining in. A convenient main floor half bathroom adds ease for guests while spending time in the living spaces.

The true highlight of this property is the stunning private courtyard.  A rare find in downtown Savannah, surrounded by lush landscaping, this outdoor oasis is perfect for morning coffee, afternoon cocktails, or unwinding under the glow of string lights after a day spent exploring the city. Enjoy an extra fun tiki outdoor tiki bar.

On the second  level of the home you will find two bedroom suites with to full size bathrooms. The master suite offers a king bed and double sink on suite bathroom with walk in shower. The second bedroom has a queen size bed and sleeper sofa with a a full size hall bathroom with shower tub/combo. 

Whether you're planning a girls' getaway, family vacation, anniversary trip, or a long weekend with friends, this beautifully located townhouse offers the perfect blend of historic charm, modern comfort, and unforgettable outdoor living.

SVR-02294

Guest access
Guests have full private access to the entire house and relaxing courtyard as well as parking in the garage (accessed from the lane). Please note there is a separate carriage house apartment above the garage. You can also book that for additional space at this link: https://www.airbnb.com/rooms/1704746604662906243

Other things to note
The rental is located in the heart of downtown, yet on a more residential street. There are some city noises such as trash trucks, ship horns, sirens, trains and vehicle traffic that you may not be accustomed to.

Guests should also know that there is a garage apartment in the rear that is also a vacation rental.

Located on the eastern end of Broughton Street in Savannah's North Historic District, this neighborhood offers the perfect blend of energy and charm. From here, you're just a 5-minute walk to River Street, a 10-minute walk to City Market, and about a 20-minute walk to Forsyth Park. With Savannah's iconic squares, museums, dining, and shopping all nearby, it's easy to explore the city entirely on foot.
