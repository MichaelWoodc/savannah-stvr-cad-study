=== FULL DESCRIPTION ===
Enjoy a stylish, romantic experience at this centrally-located 1 bedroom carriage house. Private entrance with easy street parking nearby. Walk to all the best Savannah restaurants and stroll through Forsyth Park located 2 blocks away. You'll fall asleep to the lulling sounds of the courtyard fountain, relax in the interior designed by 1chicretreat.com, and will want to move to Savannah soon enough!
This is a carriage house so there are fifteen (15) steps to enter.
SVR-02520

The space
This is an open concept studio carriage house with the possibility of closing off the bedroom space with large sliding barn doors. The bedroom has a king size bed and black-out curtains so guests may sleep in. The kitchen is a full working kitchen with oven, stovetop, microwave, chef knives, top-of-the-line coffee maker,  and all the cooking tools one needs. Bathroom provides a walk-in shower. Dining nook is ample enough for two guests plus 2 visitors.
The outdoor courtyard has comfy furniture and an awesome water fountain for maximum relaxation. This is a shared and quiet area.
