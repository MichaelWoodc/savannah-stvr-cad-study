=== FULL DESCRIPTION ===
Located on one of Savannah's oldest streets, lined up amongst expansive, rich, historic mansions this bright and stately townhouse is able to accommodate up to six Lucky guests.

The space
Located on one of Savannah's oldest streets, lined up amongst expansive, rich, historic mansions this bright and stately townhouse is able to accommodate up to six Lucky guests. 

The second level entrance brings you into the parlor level, which has a multitude of rich wood architectural details, including original hardwood floors, floor to ceiling windows, and ornate decorative fireplaces. On this floor you’ll find the main gathering room, with a large flatscreen television, which is open to the formal dining room, ideal for entertaining. At the back of the home, you'll find the fully renovated kitchen with granite countertops, stainless steel appliances, and fully equipped with everything needed to prepare home-cooked meals. 

Upstairs you’ll find the two bedrooms and a private sitting deck that overlooks the treelined courtyard. The primary suite is furnished with a tasteful mix of antique furniture, a four post king-size bed, and luxurious ensuite bath with a marble tiled shower and large soaking tub. The second bedroom also features a king size bed, with an ample amount of space to store all of your belongings. Just off of the hall, the full bathroom has a tub/shower combo, and plenty of counter space to get ready for an evening out. 

This home is ideally located within a short walk to many of Savannah’s historic points-of-interest and incredible selection of dining options. You’ll be well situated to explore Savannah’s rich history with the Owens Thomas House and the Davenport House Museum both just a block away. 

Sleeping Configuration:
Bedroom 1: King Size Bed
Bedroom 2: King Size Bed
Living Room: 1 Queen Sleeper Sofa

What is parking like for this home?
This unit has one private parking space behind the home. All additional parking can be found on-street, and is metered which is enforced by the City of Savannah Monday through Saturday from 8am to 8pm. You have the option of feeding the meter, parking in a nearby parking garage, or buying a parking pass from the Savannah Visitor Center. All of that information is included in our confirmation email sent upon booking.
