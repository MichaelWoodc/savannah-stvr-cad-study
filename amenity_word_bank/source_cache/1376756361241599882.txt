The space
Welcome to High Cotton, a one-of-a-kind luxury loft in the heart of Savannah’s Historic District. This premier vacation rental offers panoramic views of River Street, a rare rooftop deck, and an abundance of natural light. Nestled in a historic 1859 building, this beautifully restored space seamlessly blends Southern charm with modern elegance. Park your car and explore Savannah on foot—you’re just steps away from world-class dining, shopping, and historic landmarks!

What Makes High Cotton Unique?

- Unrivaled Historic Elegance & Modern Comfort:  Housed in a pre-Civil War building constructed with authentic Savannah gray bricks, High Cotton sits alongside the famed Factors’ Walk—once the heart of the city’s cotton trade. With its rich history and carefully preserved architecture, this loft offers an unforgettable glimpse into Savannah’s storied past while providing every modern luxury.

- Bright & Airy Open Floor Plan: From the moment you ascend the stairs, you’ll step into an architectural masterpiece designed for entertaining. This sun-drenched loft features a chef’s kitchen with high-end appliances, custom cabinetry, and a formal dining area. The spacious gathering room is perfect for unwinding after a day of exploring Savannah’s historic squares, museums, and waterfront attractions.

- Breathtaking River Views & Private Rooftop Deck: A true rarity in Savannah, High Cotton boasts a sprawling rooftop deck, offering unobstructed sunrise and sunset views over the Savannah River. Enjoy your morning coffee while watching cargo ships glide by or sip cocktails under the stars. On the opposite side, you’ll catch glimpses of Bay Street and Savannah’s famous St. Patrick’s Day Parade route!

Luxury Accommodations for a Relaxing Stay:
Located on the main level, the serene primary suite features a king-size bed, smart TV, generous storage, and a spa-inspired ensuite bathroom with a walk-in shower and indulgent soaking tub. The lofted second bedroom is accessed via a charming spiral staircase, this cozy space offers a queen-size bed, a dedicated workspace, and another ensuite bathroom with a walk-in shower—perfect for remote work or a peaceful getaway.

Experience Savannah Like a Local - With its unbeatable location, High Cotton places you steps away from Savannah’s most beloved attractions, including:
- River Street’s vibrant shops, restaurants, and rooftop bars
- Bay Street's picturesque Spanish moss-draped oaks
- The Savannah Historic District’s charming cobblestone streets and squares
- Ghost tours, carriage rides, and historic house museums

Whether you’re visiting for a romantic getaway, a family vacation, or a girls’ weekend, High Cotton offers an unforgettable stay in the heart of Savannah’s history. Experience Southern charm, timeless history, and unmatched luxury at High Cotton!

*Please note: This home is a second-story walk-up, requiring guests to use a staircase to reach the main level. Additionally, the second bedroom and rooftop deck are accessible only via a spiral staircase.*
