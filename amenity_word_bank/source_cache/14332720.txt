=== FULL DESCRIPTION ===
This beautiful row house in Historic Downtown Savannah was built in 1872. Meticulously restored and renovated, this home offers the historic beauty of original hardwood floors, high ceilings, pocket doors, and four fireplaces (in-op), as well as the comfort of soft beds, remodeled kitchen, 52' TV,  and Wi-Fi. In a quiet residential area on Taylor Square,  just a short walk away from Forsyth Park, River St, shopping, restaurants, and entertainment. Well-behaved pets welcome. No barkers please.

The space
Easy meter-free parking on the street just out front.

This listing is for the upper two stories of the townhome, which sleeps 6. All bedrooms are on the top floor. You have access to a private courtyard, shared with the owner, and the garden level guests (#488360).

A walk to:

    * Forsyth Park
    * City Market
    * River Street
    * Broughton Street shopping
    * Downtown Design District
    * countless restaurants, coffeshops, and bars
    * all 22 downtown Squares
    * The Mercer House
    * The Pink House
    * The Owens Thomas House
    * The Juliet Gordon Lowe birthplace and Girl Scout original Headquarters
    * The Telfair and Jepson Art Museums
    * Savannah Civic Center
    * Savannah College of Art and Design (SCAD)


A short drive to:

    * Tybee Island beach
    * Bonaventure Cemetary
    * Savannah Hilton Head International Airport

SVR Cert. No. SVR-00092

Guest access
Travelers will have access to two spacious living rooms, kitchen, three bedrooms and two and a half bathrooms, as well as a private shared courtyard.

Guests will be given a door code prior to arrival.

Other things to note
