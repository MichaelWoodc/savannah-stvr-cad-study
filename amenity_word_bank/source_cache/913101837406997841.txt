The space
Welcome to Modern Bliss nestled in the heart of Savannah's Historic District. Immerse yourself in the charm of this completely renovated abode that effortlessly combines modern luxury with timeless elegance.

 As you approach the property, its unassuming facade belies the extraordinary interior that awaits. Step inside and be greeted by soaring ceilings, exquisite stained concrete floors, and captivating solid mahogany doors that create an immediate sense of grandeur.

The first level of this remarkable home features two generously sized bedrooms, offering versatility for your needs. One of these rooms can easily transform into a multipurpose space, whether you desire a home office, yoga studio, or even a private home gym, with the added convenience of a hidden murphy bed. Additionally, you'll discover the primary suite on this floor, boasting a custom-designed, spa-like bathroom that exudes luxury. Sink into the plush king bed, draw the blackout curtains for a restful night's sleep, and enjoy your favorite shows on the smart-television, creating the perfect ambiance for relaxation and rejuvenation.

Ascending to the second floor, you'll find the heart of the home—the main living spaces. The open floor plan is a dream come true for entertainers, seamlessly connecting the custom kitchen with the inviting gathering room. Prepare culinary delights in the well-appointed kitchen, complete with top-of-the-line appliances and thoughtful details. The adjacent gathering room sets the stage for memorable evenings with loved ones, whether it's hosting a lively game night or indulging in a wine tasting session. The custom finishes and furnishings throughout this space will leave you in awe, creating an atmosphere of refined elegance and sophistication.

For moments of outdoor bliss, step onto the rooftop terrace, a true gem of this home. Adorned with a table for four, it offers an intimate setting to savor homecooked meals or sip cocktails as you soak in the charming ambiance of Savannah before venturing out for an exciting night on the town.

In summary, this vacation rental is a captivating retreat that harmoniously blends contemporary comforts with the rich history and allure of Savannah's Historic District. With its meticulously curated design, exceptional amenities, and prime location, this extraordinary property promises an unforgettable experience for discerning travelers seeking the pinnacle of luxury living.

What is parking like for this home?
Parking for this home is on-street and metered, with enforcement by the City of Savannah Monday through Saturday, 8am to 8pm. Guests may conveniently pay using the ParkSavannah app, feed the meter or parking kiosks directly, or utilize a nearby public parking garage. Pre-paid visitor parking passes are available for purchase at the Parking and Mobility Services office. Please take note of posted Sweep Zone signs when parking on the street.

We can welcome your dog companions at this pet friendly home, but ask that you find other arrangements for cats, birds, and other furry friends. There is a $130 pet fee (non-refundable) with a 35 lb. maximum weight limit per dog and a limit of 2 dogs per reservation. Some homes may allow larger dogs, please inquire for more information.
