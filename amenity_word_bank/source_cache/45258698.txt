=== FULL DESCRIPTION ===
This gorgeous Carriage House and courtyard in historic Savannah is located in the beautiful Crawford Square area. You will be surrounded by locals and fellow visitors. It is within walking distance of all that this quaint town has to offer. It is a ½ mile to busy Broughton Street, home to numerous shops and restaurants. In addition, there are plenty of charming restaurants and unique shops throughout the square mile of historic Savannah.

The space
As you walk up the stairs to your home away from home you will be greeted by pure joy. The quaint space is home to a fully equipped kitchen and living space with a Smart TV that has YouTube TV for live programming and also has Roku channels. The bedroom has a King size bed and full bathroom with washer and dryer. There is also a pull-out Queen sofa bed in the living space.

Guest access
Guests have access to 1 private parking spot located in the garage. Additional free parking is available in front of the Main house and nearby parking is on-street and metered. Guests will need to be aware of the posted sweep zone signs.

Other things to note

We've lived in Savannah for over 20 years each. We still live downtown but decided to rent our homes as vacation rentals because we want you to enjoy Downtown Savannah as much as we do. All of our homes are located in the Historic / Victorian district and in walking distance to Forsyth Park where we love to go on Saturday mornings for the Farmers Market or have a picnic on Sunday afternoon. Our kids love the playground and the grand yard. Our properties were decorated to be comfortable for you and your family/friends. We have a large sectional sofa in the living rooms. We want you to relax when you're at our homes and if that means lying on the couch and watching a movie then this is the right place for you! We are both Realtors for Salt Marsh Realty.
