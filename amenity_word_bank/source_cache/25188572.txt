=== FULL DESCRIPTION ===
Live like a local in this fully furnished carriage house located on the elegant and sought-after Jones Street, nearby tourist attractions, parks, shopping and dining.

The space
This fully-furnished carriage house is situated in the heart of Savannah's Historic District, on the elegant and sought-after Jones Street. Located within walking distance of a variety of shops and dining options, such as Public, SoHo South and Six Pence Pub. Lovely Forsyth Park is also within walking distance, as are several event venues. 

If you don't feel like walking, or need to go just little further to River Street, you can easily flag down a Pedicab for a quick and pleasant ride to your destination. Also available is the free Downtowner (its a local Uber paid for by the City)!

This carriage house has been a favorite lodging location of Joe Odom, a Savannah celebrity who played Paul Hipp in the film "Midnight in the Garden of Good and Evil." This quaint location has a lush, shared courtyard that is often visited and occupied by the the owner's two dogs, a basset hound named Leon and a poodle named Pheobe. They love company, but if you're in the mood for privacy, feel free to ask that they stay home. 

This is truly one of the best locations in the city, and after staying here you will feel like a true Savannahian. 

This Property is perfect for Corporate & Monthly rentals.  Ask about special monthly rates and discounts!

We are following all CDC Guidelines for cleaning, and doing regular deep cleans for every one of our properties.  You will be able to see a specific cleaning checklist that we have followed upon arrival or upon request.

This property features a double bed, a twin bed, and a sofa bed.
