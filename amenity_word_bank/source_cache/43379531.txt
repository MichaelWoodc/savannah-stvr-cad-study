=== FULL DESCRIPTION ===
Chic Savannah Hideaway is a bright and charming, newly remodeled top-floor corner apartment in the heart of the elegant, beautiful Historic District.

The space
Welcome to Chic Savannah Hideaway, a two bedroom, two bathroom, coastal chic condo located directly in the heart of the Downtown Historic District. Featuring two king beds, spacious bathrooms, incredible natural light, elevated decor and an unbeatable location - you'll never want to leave!

Savannah is a walker's paradise, a perfect geometric grid. Enchanting coffee shops, seriously amazing restaurants, bars, architecture, art, culture, and shopping are all located within 10 to 15 minutes. It is easy to escape into the Spanish Moss trees that line any of 22 historic squares. After exploring the city, slip back to your hideaway, kick off your shoes, and curl up on the comfortable sofa with a glass of wine. Foodies can whip up a gourmet meal in the fully stocked kitchen with all the gear. A washer and dryer are also available within the condo.

Soaring ceilings and bright southern exposure allows the natural light to flood in through the tall windows. There are plenty of options to choose your favorite entertainment programing with three big-screen smart TV's, one in each bedroom plus another in the living room. Sink into a comfortable, eco friendly new mattress with quality boutique bedding. This space is comfortable, functional, beautifully colorful, and rave worthy. If you need to attend to some work, there is a desk, large dining room table, fast WIFI, and a printer for your convenience. FedEx is one block away. Whether you are on a road trip down the east coast, meeting family for a special occasion, or connecting with the girls for a getaway, enjoy the charm of Chic Savannah Hideaway. You will be swept away and transported to a slower, kinder time. Fall in love with the culture and intrigue of the South, this is what Savannah is known for.

Located on the corner of Broughton and Whitaker, it is a quick 20-minute drive or Uber from the airport. The nickname “Hostess City” fits the bill. Savannah is a warm, friendly blend of funky, scenic, romantic, and artistic vibes. Take a formal walking tour, historical tour, or food tour through this fascinating city. Go for a carriage ride down the cobblestone streets past the historic mansions.

What is parking like for this home?
Parking for this home is on-street and metered, with enforcement by the City of Savannah Monday through Saturday, 8am to 8pm. Guests may conveniently pay using the ParkSavannah app, feed the meter or parking kiosks directly, or utilize a nearby public parking garage. Pre-paid visitor parking passes are available for purchase at the Parking and Mobility Services office. Please take note of posted Sweep Zone signs when parking on the street.

Sleeping Configuration:
Bedroom 1: King Size Bed
Bedroom 2: King Size Bed
Living Room: Queen Size Sleeper Sofa
For guests that do not wish to share a bed, they can find two queen size air mattress in the closet.
