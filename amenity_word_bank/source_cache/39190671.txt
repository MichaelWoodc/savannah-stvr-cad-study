=== FULL DESCRIPTION ===
If you're looking for a midsize property with room for four guests, then the Broughton Retreat house might fit the bill. This is a two-bedroom, one-bathroom loft-style property that includes an elevator in the building. Its convenient location overlooking Broughton Street means that you can walk everywhere.

The space
Like all of our Savannah vacation rentals, this property offers a ton of amenities. The interior features huge windows with a street view, plus shiny hardwood floors, brick accented walls and soaring ceilings. The decor is an eclectic mix of old and new with an emphasis on gold and wood tones. The main room of this property is a great place to relax with two sofas and a flatscreen TV. Nearby is the dining room as well as a gourmet kitchen with a bar that looks out into the main room. The kitchen features stainless steel appliances, including a microwave, refrigerator and stove. Utensils and cookware are provided so that you can cook your favorite meals while on vacation. The rest of the kitchen is finished in dark, cherry-tone wood to provide an appealing contrast with the granite countertops. The two bedrooms feature comfortable queen beds along with a flatscreen TV, a full-length mirror and closet space. There is also a washer and dryer in this unit for your convenience. Many of our vacation rentals Savannah GA come with laundry machines in the unit to make longer stays easier. Other amenities in this property include free Wi-Fi, a dishwasher, central heat and AC, a tiled walk-in shower and an extra air mattress for additional sleeping space.



One of the great benefits of staying in Savannah vacation homes versus a hotel is the privacy and space to move around. It allows you to feel more at home while on vacation. It also puts the charming Savannah neighborhoods right at your fingertips. Simply walk outside your door to be immediately immersed in Savannah culture. Savannah vacation rentals located near or on Broughton are popular choices with visitors who love to shop. Right nearby this property is a variety of popular stores, including The Gap, Banana Republic, Paris Market, James Gunn and more. Nearby eateries include the SubZero Bar, World of Beer, Alleycat Lounge and Barrelhouse South. Broughton is an intriguing mix of national retailers and local boutiques. This property is also located almost precisely between four squares. These squares are Telfair Square, Ellis Square, Wright Square and Johnson Square. Savannah's historic squares are some of the most storied attractions in the city.



In terms of what else to visit while you're in the city, some of the popular neighborhoods include River Street, City Market and Forsyth Park. River Street, in particular, can be a day's worth of adventure. The former cotton warehouses that used to line the river have been converted to a bevy of shops, galleries and eateries. Not only that, but you can book a riverboat tour here and spend a few hours out on the water. City Market is known for its variety of nightlife and is a great place to find jazz bars and open-air cafes. Forsyth Park might be the perfect trip in the morning if you want to walk through the trails and enjoy all the plants.



Savannah is also full of museums and old houses that display architecture and furniture from earlier time periods. One of the most famous is the Mercer-Williams House Museum, which was a featured place in the Midnight in the Garden of Good and Evil movie. The Andrew Low House is another popular one, which is where the Girl Scouts founder lived. It is rumored to be haunted as well.



If you want to venture outside of Savannah, then you might consider taking the 30-minute drive to Tybee Island. This fun little barrier island has its own complement of shops, waterfront restaurants and possible activities. The Tybee Lighthouse on the north end is probably its biggest claim to fame. Another engaging spot is the Tybee Marine Science Center, which features a number of ecological tours and displays. It's a great place to take children or anyone who loves learning about ecological diversity.



If you're looking for special events to attend while staying in vacation rentals Savannah GA, then you may have a few options depending on when you come. The following is a sampling of events that take place during the year:



• Savannah Irish Festival in February

• Saint Patrick's Day in Savannah in March

• Savannah Music Festival in March

• First Saturday on the River festival from March to December

• Fine Arts on the River Festival in May

• Fourth of July Fireworks on the River in July

• Savannah Film Festival in September

• Savannah Harbor Boat Parade of Lights in November

• Holiday Tour of Homes in December
