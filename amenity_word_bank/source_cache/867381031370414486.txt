=== FULL DESCRIPTION ===
Enjoy a stylish mid-century modern experience in this revived 100 year old condo, centrally located in the Historic District of Savannah, GA.  The condo is overflowing with original character while also providing you with updated modern amenities. Located on Oglethorpe Square, you will be surrounded by the Southern beauty and emerged in downtown living. Just steps away from Savannah's restaurant and shopping scene, you will can walk to just about everywhere downtown. We can't wait to host you!

The space
Welcome to this stunning historic one bedroom, one bathroom condo located in the Historic District of Savannah, Georgia! 

This charming condo is situated in the iconic Graham Building, built in 1924, and boasts incredible views overlooking Oglethorpe square, adjacent to the famous Thomas Owens house.

This condo has been carefully curated with a mid-century modern vibe that perfectly complements the building's historic charm. Inside, you'll find a spacious and inviting living room featuring a comfortable queen sleeper sofa and plenty of natural light pouring in through the large windows.

The bedroom is furnished with a comfy king bed and offers plenty of closet space for your belongings. The updated bathroom features modern fixtures and a spacious shower and tub combo, and the fully equipped kitchen has been recently updated with all the amenities you'll need to prepare meals during your stay. There is a smart TV in the living and a second smart TV in the bedroom for your enjoyment. Wifi is included for your stay and some fun family games. 

A pac n play is available upon request.

Enjoy these modern luxuries all while taking in the original character that has been carefully preserved to enjoy a true Savannah visit!

Located in the heart of Savannah's historic district, our condo is just steps away from some of the city's best restaurants, shops, and attractions. Take a leisurely stroll through the nearby squares and soak up the rich history and culture of this beautiful city. 

A guidebook is provided full of all our favorite suggestions of restaurants, shops, tours and more! 

Whether you're here for a romantic getaway, a fun-filled weekend with friends, or a solo adventure, we can't wait to welcome you to this cozy home and help you enjoy your stay in Savannah! We can't wait to host you!

SVR-02725

Guest access
As guests you will enter the condo building from a shared entrance. The condo is on the first floor of the building. You will have full access to the entire condo during your stay.

Other things to note
The condo does not have central air or heat. There are two window AC/heat units. They keep the condo very cool during the summer months and the units have a heat setting as well to keep the condo warm in the cooler months. Each room also has a remote operated ceiling fan. A portable floor fan is also provided.

Parking is not provided with this listing. However there is a city parking garage right next door at 100 E State Street, Savannah GA 31041.  There is also metered city street parking close by. You can download the Park Savannah app prior to arrival to pay via an app.

There is not a dishwasher, but all other kitchen necessities are provided and available for guest use.

Please note the smart TVs offer apps to streaming services. We do not offer cable or local channels.

Please note that the condo is in the heart of downtown, with that comes some city noises that you may not be accustomed to. We do provide a sound machine to help drown out any noises. We do try to make guests aware that  Fridays and Saturdays can be loud due to our close proximity to Savannah's night life.
