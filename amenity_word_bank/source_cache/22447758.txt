=== FULL DESCRIPTION ===
Be within short walking distance to the National Landmark Historic District in this contemporary garden apartment.

The space
A welcome addition to the Victorian District, Howard Grande Garden is a street-level, one-bedroom apartment within short walking distance to the city center.

Despite being a garden apartment, this home boasts tall ceilings and great natural light, ideal for travelers who wish to unwind to the sounds of charming Forsyth Park. With a Queen size bed dressed in hotel-grade linens, a flat screen TV, ample closet space, and a kitchenette, you'll be hard-pressed to leave your cozy quarters and explore Savannah's Victorian District. 

When you do wish to venture out, head South a few blocks and find yourself immersed in Savannah's hipster culture - with a wine bar, vegan cafe, and whole foods market adjacent to the Forsyth Park Farmer's Market. A few minutes' walk in the other direction gets you closer to the bustling city center. Take the scenic route through downtown's beautiful squares and end at the well-known River Walk - a great place to grab a beer and people watch.

Whether you're a traveling professional looking for a quiet respite close to the city center, or a couple wanting to tap into the local scene, Howard Grande Garden is the perfect home for you!

Parking: Non-metered Parking
This unit has free, on-street parking right outside the front door. Availability will depend on time of day and season. Our guests have not reported trouble parking near the home, even on the busiest of weekends.
