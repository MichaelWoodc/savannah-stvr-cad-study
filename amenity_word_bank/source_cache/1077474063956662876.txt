=== FULL DESCRIPTION ===
Welcome to our chic urban retreat! Experience luxury in this brand-new, custom-designed carriage house featuring unique art (some by yours truly) and stylish furnishings. The off-street parking and lane location offer some hard-to-find privacy in the Victorian District. The high ceilings give an airy feel while you unwind on plush furniture and indulge in the modern amenities. Ideal for a romantic getaway and starting point to explore Savannah's charm!

SVR 02919

The space
This one bedroom, one bathroom condo in the heart of the Victorian District truly captures the quintessential Savannah experience, where Southern charm meets modern luxury! 

The bedroom features a king-sized bed made up with fresh, crisp white linens. The living room is the perfect space to sit back and relax! Stay connected with fast WiFi and enjoy entertainment on the flat-screen Smart TV.

The bathroom features some fun tile work in a spa-like arrangement.

The kitchen is fully stocked with all the amenities and is meticulously built to ensure you have all the modern conveniences of home. Brew up a fresh cup of coffee (or pour yourself a glass of wine after a long day of exploring) and even prepare a meal if you need a break from the awesome food scene!

The art in the kitchen was a fun project for me, and I hope you love the result as much as I do!

An off-street parking space right by your door is included, making your stay hassle-free.

Guest access
The entire carriage house is all yours!

Other things to note
If you are bringing a pet - please add them to your guest list.  The pet fee will then be included automatically.

Neighborhood highlights
This brand new carriage house is located less just three blocks from Forsyth Park, where this is always something fun going on! From festivals to the Saturday Farmer's Market, to just walking the perimeter and enjoying the picturesque tree lined streets, we feel that we are situated in the perfect area for exploration and fun! Pizza Party, Das Box, Shuk, and Sentient Bean are steps away! We are also a scenic fifteen to twenty minute walk (or five minute Uber ride!) to the downtown area, including Broughton Street, City Market, River Street, and all 22 of Savannah's charmingly historic squares!
