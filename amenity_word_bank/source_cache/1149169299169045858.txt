=== FULL DESCRIPTION ===
This spacious estate includes three distinct living quarters, each with its own private entrance: a two-story main house, a garden-level unit tucked below, and a charming carriage unit above the garage. Whether you're traveling with family, friends, or a multi-generational group, everyone will enjoy both privacy and connection in this thoughtfully designed retreat.

The space
With rich hardwood floors, soaring ceilings, and abundant natural light, the interiors offer a warm and inviting atmosphere from the moment you arrive.

🛋️ Gather & Unwind
Three beautifully appointed living rooms feature cozy seating and Smart TVs, perfect for relaxing after a day of exploring Savannah.

🍽️ Cook, Dine, Enjoy
Each of the three kitchens is fully equipped with everything you need to prepare meals with ease. The elegant main house dining room provides a comfortable space to share food and conversation.

🛏️ Restful Retreats for All
Every bedroom is styled with calming decor and outfitted with plush memory foam mattresses to ensure a peaceful night’s sleep. The layout offers flexibility and privacy for groups of all sizes.

🚶‍♀️ Walkable & Convenient
Located in the heart of Savannah’s historic district, the estate is just a short stroll from River Street, Forsyth Park, and the city’s best restaurants. Two garage parking spaces for mid-size or smaller vehicles and free street parking make it easy to explore beyond downtown.

Stayed with kids
We had a wonderful stay! The location was excellent and surprisingly quiet at night. It was about a 15-20 minute walk to River St. All of the units were clean, modern, and spacious. Our family of 16 had plenty of room to gather in the main house while still enjoying the privacy of the separate suites. The kitchen was well stocked with lots of pantry space.
The standout was the communication from the owners—they responded incredibly quickly, even after hours when we had trouble getting into one of our units. 
A few minor things to note: towels and extra sheets were a little limited, and we had to use blankets for the pull-out bed. The garage is best suited for compact cars, and the units are completely separate rather than connected internally. Street sweeping is on Thursdays, so you’ll need to move your car, but we had no trouble finding nearby parking.
Overall, we’d absolutely stay here again!
