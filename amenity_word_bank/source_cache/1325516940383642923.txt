=== FULL DESCRIPTION ===
Swanky and fun 1-bedroom condo nestled on a quiet street just two blocks from Forsyth Park! You will feel right at home in this perfectly curated, garden level condo. Fast wi-fi, private parking space, pull-out sofa for that one extra guest, tons of storage, sweet shared courtyard space (with grill)! You can spin vinyl on the record player or mix a cocktail at the bar! Beautiful neighborhood, comfy bed, laundry, and so much more - the perfect home base for your Savannah explorations!

SVR-2277

The space
Spacious one-bedroom (garden-level) condo in gorgeous historic Savannah row home just a couple blocks off Forsyth Park. King sized bed in bedroom with TV, little library, ample storage, and fun décor! Living room boasts a second television, fantastic bar, picnic basket for those afternoons in the park, games, record player, pull out sofa, and plenty of room to relax after a long day of exploring Savannah! Fully equipped and stocked eat-in kitchen is perfect for preparing a quick breakfast or a full dinner for a night in! Enjoy that southern weather and take a cup of coffee (or wine!) out to the shared patio area and enjoy the slowed down, Southern lifestyle! Bring work with you? Get a few to-do list things checked off at the gorgeous roll top desk! Staying with us a bit longer? In-unit washer/dryer for your laundry needs and AMPLE storage space! Driving into town? No worries, we provide a parking pass for the small private parking lot right around the corner!

Guest access
The entire condo is all yours! This is a garden level condo, located at street level below a larger home. The outdoor courtyard is shared with that larger home.

Other things to note
Please note -- this is a garden-level apartment, below a larger condo above, in a VERY historic building. While we LOVE offering such a fun, space in a great area of this bright and vibrant city, with our fantastic location and building comes typical urban noise, from both outside as well as from neighboring homes. Please anticipate noise day and night, at all hours. If you are sensitive to noise, if you are a light sleeper, or if you just want the reassurance that your stay will be quiet throughout, we do not recommend this property (and we aren't sure that downtown Savannah in general will be a good fit).

Don't be afraid to reach out with any questions or issues! We always try to help our guests with anything that comes up, big or small.

Neighborhood highlights
This is probably our favorite neighborhood in the city! The house is off a quiet street split by a treeway full of Spanish-moss covered oaks. Stroll just a block and a half and enter the middle of gorgeous Forsyth Park, the city's crown jewel! Take in the beautiful architecture and shaded streets surrounding this condo that straddles Savannah's Victorian and Historic Districts. Collin's in Forsyth, Crystal Beer Parlor, Black Rabbit, 700 Drayton, Local 11Ten and other incredible bars and restaurants are right outside your door.

If you choose to stay with us and need lots of recommendations...don't worry! We are happy to provide personalized recommendations based on your interests and preferences...just ask us!
