=== FULL DESCRIPTION ===
Bell Mansion Garden on Pulaski Square is your own private slice of heaven in Savannah’s National Historic Landmark District. Built in 1854, this charming garden apartment is nestled on quiet Pulaski Square.

The space
Bell Mansion Garden on Pulaski Square is your own private slice of heaven in Savannah’s National Historic Landmark District. Built in 1854, this charming garden apartment is nestled on quiet Pulaski Square. You will appreciate the lovingly restored interiors that blend historic charm with modern luxury appointments.

This charming garden level apartment, is the perfect retreat packed with history and charm. The original historic beams and decorative brick fireplace, bring so much character to this quaint space. The thoughtfully chosen furnishings create a comfortable place to relax, and catch-up after a long day exploring the beautiful city. No detail was sparred while remodeling the kitchen - you'll have all of the modern conveniences one could ask for! 

The quaint bedroom is at the front of the apartment, featuring a queen size bed, and spacious closet. Just a cross the hall, a reading room can be found, perfect for those who need to catch up on work, or relax away from their party.

Start your day of exploration through the squares as you venture down Barnard Street, that leads all the way to the river front. You’ll be a short walk from the Cathedral of St. John the Baptist, Forsyth Park, and so many other well known monuments and venues in Savannah.

Sleeping Configuration:
Bedroom 1: Queen Bedroom

What is parking like for this home?
Parking for this home is on-street and metered, which is enforced by the City of Savannah from Monday through Friday, 8am to 5pm. Guests may conveniently pay using the ParkSavannah app, feed the meter or parking kiosks directly, or park on a nearby non-metered street. Please take note of posted Sweep Zone signs when parking on the street.
