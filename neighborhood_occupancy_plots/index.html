Rental and directly touching neighbors

For each rental: add unique selected calls on the rental parcel and its directly touching residential neighbors, then divide by the total number of parcels in that group. Plotted points are the arithmetic mean of those individual neighborhood ratios at each licensed occupancy. Zero-call parcels remain included. Rentals without eligible neighbors use a denominator of one. Shared neighbors can belong to multiple neighborhoods, so points are not independent.

Focal rentals require positive assessor bedrooms and explicit evidence for single-family, duplex, or duplex with carriage house. A single assessor Type 1 building section supports single-family; a Realtor multi-family record with two units or explicit duplex subtype supports duplex; three units plus explicit duplex and carriage-house text supports the third category. Building-card count and rental-license count are not used as dwelling counts. Unknown or conflicting configurations stay out of the focal cohort. Neighbors can be any positive-bedroom residential parcel; they need not be rentals or the same building type. All saved commercial/hotel exclusions apply to focal parcels and neighbors.

A neighbor shares more than 0.1 m of parcel boundary, measured in UTM zone 17N. Corners, across-road parcels and overlapping interiors do not qualify. Parcels, not buildings or rental units, are the denominator. The all-licensed view sums license occupancy limits per focal parcel; single-license view uses the individual license limit.

Selected calls: parking complaints, all noise complaints including past/phone/online variants, loud party, and non-weapon nuisance. Private parking tows and weapon nuisance are excluded. Overall includes all initiation types plus a citizen-only series. Subplots use all initiation types. Exact call types are in the linked definition file. Calls need a unique normalized recorded-address parcel; coordinate agreement corroborates, coordinate conflicts exclude, and missing coordinate matches permit address-only attribution. Intersections and ranged addresses are not assigned. Calls are deduplicated by call number in each neighborhood.

CAD period: January 1-September 20, 2024. Current licenses and later assessor/property snapshots do not establish historical 2024 configuration or license activity. These are recorded calls for service, not verified 911-origin records or proof of guest involvement. Types and missing bedroom data can limit coverage.

Included focal property types: single family: 249, duplex: 4. No duplex-with-carriage-house record met the stated evidence rules in this export.
