=== FULL DESCRIPTION ===
Welcome to the Silas Fulton House, an elegant 1860 home set on one of Savannah's most beloved cobblestone streets in the heart of the Historic District. Originally built for Silas Fulton — one of Savannah's early freedmen and entrepreneurs — this beautifully preserved property carries a story you can feel in every room. Today, it's been thoughtfully restored to offer guests a rare experience: an authentic piece of Savannah history paired with the comforts and luxuries of a modern boutique stay.

The space
The home is actually two side-by-side townhouses — Luxe Retreat (3 bedrooms, 2 baths, sleeps 6) and Haint Blue Haven (3 bedrooms, 1.5 baths, sleeps 6) — that can be booked together for a full 6-bedroom, 3.5-bath estate that sleeps 12, or rented individually for smaller getaways. It's an ideal setup for family reunions, bachelorette weekends, weddings, milestone birthdays, and multigenerational trips, where guests want togetherness and privacy in equal measure.

Inside, original details — exposed beam ceilings, post-and-beam architecture, beautiful stained glass, and traditional haint blue accents — come together with refined Southern decor and modern comforts. Sink into plush beds (frequently called out by guests as some of the most comfortable they've ever slept in), cook in a fully equipped chef's kitchen with modern appliances, stream from a smart TV, work or browse on high-speed Wi-Fi, and unwind in elegantly designed living spaces filled with light and character.

Step outside into the private courtyard, where a gas fire pit invites long evenings of laughter and storytelling under the Savannah sky. And in a city famously known for its parking challenges, the home offers a rare and welcome perk: two designated off-street parking spots for guests

Location-wise, you couldn't ask for better. The Silas Fulton House sits steps from Forsyth Park, Jones Street (often called one of the most beautiful streets in America), River Street, and the boutique shops and acclaimed restaurants of the Historic District. Walk to Leopold's Ice Cream, stroll the moss-draped squares, sip cocktails on lantern-lit patios, or set off on a ghost tour through one of the country's most atmospheric cities — it's all just outside your door.

Whether you're traveling with extended family, gathering friends for a special occasion, or simply seeking a romantic Southern escape, the Silas Fulton Historic House offers a stay that's as unforgettable as Savannah itself.
