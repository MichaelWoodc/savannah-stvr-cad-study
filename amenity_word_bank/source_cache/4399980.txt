=== FULL DESCRIPTION ===
Radiantly restored historic house built in 1866 on the "Prettiest street in the South."
My 3 Bedroom (4 bed including the bonus room) 3 Bath home will afford you the comfort and luxury of experiencing Savannah like a local in the center of the historic district steps from all the sights.

**PLEASE SEND AN INQUIRY BEFORE REQUESTING TO BOOK**

**I generally have a 3-night weekend minimum**
**I don't generally start or stop reservations on Saturdays**

Short Term Vacation Rental Permit - SVR-00347

The space
A two-story, 3 Bedroom (4 bedroom including the bonus room/den with a Murphy bed) / 3 Bath home with a remodeled kitchen, courtyard, and two parking spaces.

Original heart pine floors, exposed brick walls, ten-foot ceilings, four non-working original fireplaces, authentic Victorian furniture and decor, gas lamps, a peaceful courtyard, and all the modern amenities which allow you to relax in the charm of the 'Hostess City."

The open double parlor (living room and dining room) is a perfect place to relax in the comfortable leather couches or to eat your home-cooked meal.

Off of the remodeled kitchen downstairs, there is a cozy den/bonus room that functions as a 4th bedroom using the Murphy bed.

The tranquil courtyard is recently rebuilt and landscaped, giving shade in the summer, and full of sun during the winter, where you can enjoy your morning coffee, eat a meal, or just take things slow like a local, sipping on your favorite drinks.

The back bedroom has a balcony on the second floor overlooking the courtyard (and the St. Paddy's Day Parade route), which is great for sunbathing.

Two of the full bathrooms are upstairs: The master bath has a shower, and the bathroom shared by the 2nd and 3rd bedrooms has a tub/shower.

The third full bathroom is downstairs connected to the den/bonus room with the Murphy Bed. It has a tub with a handheld shower-head.

Guest access
You will have Full access to the entire house, courtyard, and private driveway with parking for two vehicles.

Instructions for entry to the house will be unlocked once you book and are available in the House Manual section.

Other things to note
**8 guests are my capacity by law and I cannot make exceptions. Now one else is allowed in the house, unless in the rare case I grant an exception. I do not allow bachelor or bachelorette parties, or similar type get-togethers, or any type of party weekend, parties of any nature, or large groups of friends. I mostly only allow small groups of friends, couples, or a families.**

I will allow pets on a case-by-case basis, but there will be an extra fee (usually $100 but depends on the situation/number and size of pets/etc.  All pets must be exceptionally well-trained, stay off furniture and beds, be picked up after in the courtyard and around town, and crated if left alone.

I ask if you are staying for longer than a weekend, that you do me a favor and make sure the plants inside and on the porch don't get too thirsty. They should be watered lightly twice a week please.

I have a landscaper named Belvin, middle-aged and missing a few teeth and walks with a limp because he has a prosthetic foot, a very nice guy. You may see him working out front, or possibly watering plants outside in the back which he does a few times during the week in the mornings.

**I have a strict cancellation policy, so it might be desirable for you to purchase trip cancellation insurance in case you have an emergency arise. Allianz and CSA are some of the big ones, but there are many options**

Neighborhood highlights
As stated above the house is located on historic Jones St putting it almost perfectly in the center of the historic district, within minutes on foot to top attractions. The house is located next door to the famous Clary's Cafe, a superb century-old diner that was one of the central locations in the novel and movie, Midnight in the Garden of Good and Evil; and across the street from the Tish Mansion, the second most expensive house to ever sell in downtown Savannah.

In less than five minutes you can walk to many great attractions including idyllic Forsyth Park (the prettiest city park I have seen), the Mansion of Forsyth (one of the nicest hotel bars in town), Mrs. Wilkes House (the best friend chicken I have ever had), Crystal Beer Parlor (another century old establishment that used to be a speak easy, serving the best burgers in town), Six Pence (the best pub food in town), Griffin Tea Room (an old apothecary turned into a English tea room with one of the best brunches in town, Piny Master's (considered one of the best bars in the south with a very interesting history; a very laid back casual spot), Mcdonough's Irish Pub (my favorite bar with Karaoke every night), St. John the Baptist Cathedral and Congregation Mickve Israel Synagogue (two must see architectural beauties), The Mercer-Williams house (the main setting of Midnight in the Garden of Good and Evil), and many more coffee shops, bars, restaurants and attractions. Most of the other downtown/historic district attractions and restaurant/bars are no more than a 10-15 minute walk, with the farthest few only being 20 minutes. For example, the famous River Street is only a 15 minute walk or less from where you will be staying.
