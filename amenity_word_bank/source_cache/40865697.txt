=== FULL DESCRIPTION ===
Located in the Downtown Historic District, this well-appointed, three-story home overlooks tree lined Liberty Street - No detail has been spared! Up to ten guests are welcomed at Lady Liberty Main.

The space
This newly built three-story home offers high end amenities, and upscale furnishings. Just off of Liberty Street, you will feel like a local in the Downtown Historic District. Just a few short steps up to the main level, guests will enter into their new home away from home. The main level offers an inviting living space where guests are welcomed to join together, chefs kitchen, and a formal dining area. An additional guest can be accommodated with the twin memory foam sleeper ottoman in the living room. Conveniently placed, your guests can use the half bath on the main floor. Off the dining room, step out onto your back porch for a morning cup of joe with views of your private plunge pool, and the cathedral in the distance.

After sending your luggage up via elevator, the third floor consists of two primary suites, each fitted with an en suite bathroom, and sophisticated decor. Both bedrooms are fitted with a television, and large walk-in closets perfect for storing belongings. Just between both rooms, guests can take advantage of the full-size washer and dryer. 

Making your way downstairs, ground level living is a great option for guests or family members that have mobility issues. The garden apartment offers modern amenities, such as a full kitchen equipped with high-end stainless steel appliances, dedicated dining space and large kitchen island perfect for serving. The living room can accommodate two additional guests on the sleeper ottoman, and sleeper sofa. At the back of the home, a roomy bathroom with a tiled shower and tub combo. Just down the hall, the queen size bedroom with views of the private courtyard.

Private Parking: Guests can take advantage of the private garage, that accommodates one small/mid-size sedan. Nearby parking is on-street and non-metered, although guests will need to be aware of the posted sweep zone signs.
