Welcome to your Savannah sanctuary—an elegant home with high-end finishes, plush furnishings, and all the modern comforts. Cook with ease in the fully equipped kitchen featuring stainless steel appliances and granite countertops.

The space
Enjoy meals in the beautiful dining area or grab a quick bite at the kitchen bar.
