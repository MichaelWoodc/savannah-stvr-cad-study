=== FULL DESCRIPTION ===
This beautifully renovated modern condo on the 2nd floor of our gorgeous Victorian home was completed in September 2016!  The kitchen, bathroom, bedroom, appliances, and furniture are all brand new! The original heart pine floors have been refinished and are a perfect reflection of the history of this Savannah estate.  Enjoy the Southern weather on a private balcony, or take a walk to the famous Forsyth Park less than half of a block away!

SVR-00563

The space
Natural light flows in from the large, original windows that wrap around the condo.  High ceilings and restored floors capture the history of the 130 year old home, and our updates add some modern comforts!  We think it's just the right blend of old and new!

Guest access
The whole second floor condo is yours! Entry to both the condo and the building front door are keyless. You will be provided with your code upon booking confirmation so you may come and go as you please.

Other things to note
Don't be afraid to reach out if you need anything!  We live super close-by and are always happy to help out, even mid-stay.
