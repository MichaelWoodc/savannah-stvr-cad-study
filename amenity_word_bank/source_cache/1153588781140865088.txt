=== FULL DESCRIPTION ===
Shain Manor Parlor Suite is the top 2 floors of a 3-story historic townhouse. 
This luxurious & historic 1897 home has 2 bedrooms & lush outdoor oasis that sleeps 4. Perfect for SCAD parents, traveling professionals & history buffs.

The space
Built in 1897, this Victorian Era home has been completely redone into a spectacular home away from home. When entering on the ground floor, guests will be enchanted by the period gas lanterns and lush landscaping that flank the front of the home.

Upstairs, in the Parlor/2nd floor, you will be welcomed by an abundance of natural light from all windows surrounding the home. The historic hardwood floors, original molding and architectural details have been preserved beautifully. The décor has been artfully curated by the owner, accented by natural aesthetics. Sisal rugs, an abundance of plants, antique and modern touches add elegance and sophistication.

The chef’s kitchen offers a full array of Thermador appliances which include a professional 36” gas stove with griddle & oven, 30” refrigerator/freezer, Scotsman gourmet clear ice machine, wine fridge, beverage fridge, microwave drawer, a countertop Breville toaster oven & toaster, and a Cuisinart pod-coffee maker. The sink has a reverse-osmosis room temperature and boiling water filtration system. The kitchen is completely equipped for all your dining needs.

Off the kitchen is a large outdoor terrace, equipped with a 32” professional Turbo gas grill and a gas fire pit. There is plenty of comfy seating to relax and enjoy the continual Southern breeze, neighborhood sounds of church bells, horses clomping, ships’ horns and views of fireworks from the bay during the holiday seasons!

The elegant dining room seats six comfortably and is connected to a spacious and comfortable living room with books, games and a Roku TV. There is also a powder bath next to the kitchen, designed with intricate Carrara marble tile.

Up on the 3rd Floor, in the parlor landing there is a private game table, for those interested in a game of chess, checkers, or backgammon. There is also a sofa chair you can use to read, lounge or work on your laptop.

The primary suite features a Ralph Lauren inspired king size bed with a new mattress, luxury cotton linens, chaise and a large desk. The en-suite bathroom has a marble vanity with two sinks and a large walk-in shower. The spacious primary closet includes a full-size washer/dryer.
The 2nd bedroom faces the front of the home with 2 full size beds, also with new mattresses and luxury cotton linens. There is a chair and ottoman for relaxing under the bespoke artwork made with local flora. This room has a closet and dresser for plenty of storage as well as a small desk for private moments to write or work. 

Located between Columbia & Greene Squares, Shain Manor is on the corner of York & Price Street. We are about 3 blocks from Colonial Park Cemetery, and an easy 5-minute walk to River Street. The retail mecca of Broughton Street is only 4 blocks; we are centrally located and walkable to all the hot spots!
