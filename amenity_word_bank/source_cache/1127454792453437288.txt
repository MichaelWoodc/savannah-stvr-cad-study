Nestled within Savannah's historic district, Greene Square Bliss offers a haven of tranquility and charm. Situated beside the captivating Greene Square, where ancient oak trees draped in Spanish Moss sway gently in the breeze.

The space
Nestled within Savannah's historic district, Greene Square Bliss offers a haven of tranquility and charm. Situated beside the captivating Greene Square, where ancient oak trees draped in Spanish Moss sway gently in the breeze, this two-bedroom, two-bathroom retreat embodies the essence of Savannah living.

Why we love Greene Square Bliss:

- Bright and Spacious: The open floor plan floods the space with natural light, creating an inviting atmosphere perfect for entertaining.
- Prime Location: Just a block from Broughton Street and minutes from River Street, allowing you to explore Savannah's treasures on foot, leaving your car behind.
- Tasteful Finishes: The combination of historic features and modern updates creates a luxurious yet cozy retreat, with no detail overlooked.
- Private Courtyard: Adorned with twinkling cafe lights, the private courtyard offers a serene spot to unwind with a glass of wine or morning coffee.
- Pet-Friendly: We welcome furry friends with open arms, providing all the comforts they need for a delightful stay, including dog beds and bowls.

Step inside Greene Square Bliss to discover an oasis of comfort and style. The first floor features a luxurious bathroom with a walk-in shower and a full-size washer and dryer for added convenience.

Ascend to the second floor to find two serene guest rooms awaiting you. The primary suite boasts an ensuite bathroom identical to the one downstairs, offering a luxurious feel and a spacious walk-in shower. Across the hall, the second bedroom beckons with a king bed and picturesque views overlooking the captivating Greene Square.

What is parking like for this home?
Parking for this home is on-street and metered, with enforcement by the City of Savannah Monday through Saturday, 8am to 8pm. Guests may conveniently pay using the ParkSavannah app, feed the meter or parking kiosks directly, or utilize a nearby public parking garage. Pre-paid visitor parking passes are available for purchase at the Parking and Mobility Services office. Please take note of posted Sweep Zone signs when parking on the street.

Sleeping configuration:
Bedroom 1: King Bed W/ Ensuite
Bedroom 2: King Bed 
Living room: Queen Sleeper Sofa

We can welcome your dog companions at this pet friendly home, but ask that you find other arrangements for cats, birds, and other furry friends. There is a $130 pet fee (non-refundable)and a limit of 2 dogs per reservation.
