Welcome to your Starland District getaway! This unique property features a charming 3-bedroom home and a  cozy 1-bedroom carriage house, offering space and comfort for up to 8 guests. The space
This listing consists of the main house which has 3 bedrooms 1.5 baths and the carriage house which is 1 bedroom 1 bath. In the main house, the master bedroom has a private bathroom and the other 2 bedrooms have a separate bathroom upstairs to share.  There is another half bathroom located downstairs. The main house and carriage house are separated by a shared courtyard. The carriage house has a private back entrance that can be used. The courtyard has a table for dining as well as a propane grill that can be used.  The electronic igniter is fussy and does not work sometimes and you will have to use matches or a grill lighter.
