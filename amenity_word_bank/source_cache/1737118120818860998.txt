=== FULL DESCRIPTION ===
Located in Savannah's Landmark Historic District, Price Street Place is a newly built, professionally decorated townhome just 4 blocks from Forsyth Park. Featuring 3 bedrooms, 2.5 bathrooms, and an open floor plan, this custom home boasts high-end finishes, hardwood flooring, a new kitchen with stainless steel appliances, off-street parking for 1 vehicle, and a private courtyard perfect for morning coffee

You will never want to leave this beautiful welcoming home!

The space
Treat your family to an unforgettable escapade to the heart of Savannah's Historic District at this newly built and professionally decorated townhouse! Featuring gorgeous interior decor, an open floor plan for entertaining, high-end finishes throughout, all new kitchen with stainless steel appliances, and your very own private courtyard on the back of the home, Price Street Place will delight everyone in your group. The main living space is on the first floor of the home, where you'll find the living room, dining area, kitchen, laundry room, and a half bathroom. The living room offers a welcoming atmosphere that invites you all to sit down, relax, and enjoy an entertaining time with good company. Here you'll find a sleeper sofa with a queen-size pull out mattress, a large, wall-mounted flatscreen TV with cable, and two armchairs.



Right next, the dining area is open and offers a gorgeous dining table that seats up to six lucky guests. The open concept, fully-equipped kitchen boasts beautiful granite countertops, custom cabinets, tile backsplash, stainless steel appliances, and a convenient breakfast bar with counter seating for three.



All three bedrooms are located upstairs, along with two full bathrooms. The main bedroom offers a wall-mounted flatscreen TV and an ensuite full bathroom with elegant fixtures, dual sinks, and a walk-in shower. One of the guest bedrooms has a wall-mounted flatscreen TV–each of the TVs in the home comes equipped with a Roku streaming device.



What's nearby:

This delight of a home is conveniently located in Savannah's Landmark Historic District and just four blocks away from beautiful Forsyth Park. This park is simply ideal for outdoor activities including picnics, outdoor sports, walking, jogging, playing tennis at the free tennis courts, and so much more! You can stock up on fresh groceries and goodies at the Forsyth Farmer’s Market (one mile away). Located just two blocks away are a Kroger grocery store and Mate Factor, a great coffee shop, and bakery. Beachgoers can make the 18-mile trip to Tybee Island, Savannah's beach. This is a great place for fishing, visiting Ft. Pulaski, or climbing Georgia's oldest and tallest lighthouse.



Things to know:

High-speed internet and free WiFi, perfect for working from home, distance learning, and streaming.
