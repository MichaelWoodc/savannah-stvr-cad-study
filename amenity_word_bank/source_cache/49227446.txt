The space
Your new home away from home awaits! This tastefully decorated, and modern home is located in the heart of the Downtown Historic District, only steps away from Savannah's Famed Forsyth Park. This three-story home with two private parking spaces can accommodate up to 10 Lucky Guests.

This is the perfect getaway if you are looking for a modern, newly constructed townhouse with tall ceilings, an ample amount of natural light, and upgraded furnishings and finishes. Large groups and families will love the amount of space this home has to offer, in addition to the unmatched comfort and hospitality while living like a true Savannah Local. 

The main home enters on the second level of this three-story town home accessible by a flight of stairs from street level. The main home is technically the second level of this three-story town home and is accessed by a set of stairs. Enter into the chic living space with L-shaped sectional large enough to accommodate all guests, fitted with a smart TV. The adjacent kitchen features top-of-the line appliances, with granite countertops, while being fully stocked with everything you need to prepare home-cooked meals! Stools at both the kitchen counter and island are perfect for bonding over a bottle of wine and cheese board before heading out on the town. Just at the back of the home, you'll find a formal dining nook and half bath just behind the kitchen.

The first three bedrooms can be found on the third level (one floor up from the main floor and front door). The first room, also known as the "main" bedroom, features a comfortable King bed, smart television, balcony, and a luxurious ensuite bathroom. The additional two guest rooms each have Queen size beds and share a full bath found in the hall. One guest room has private access to a balcony!  

In the charming lower level garden apartment, each square inch is carefully curated with the modern conveniences and finishes that are offered upstairs. A single bedroom with Queen bed is tucked away towards the back of the apartment and additional sleeping accommodations can be found with the Queen sleeper sofa in the living room. A full bathroom with tub/shower combo is located in the hall - easily shared by all guests.

Stay a short walk away from the Fountain at Forsyth Park, and a beautiful stroll from downtown's main attractions such as River Street, City Market, and Bay Street.

The stay was a great opportunity for my family of nine to stay and enjoy Savannah. The hosts were hospitable and very responsive. We were able to check in early and also keep our luggage at the main office on our day of departure so we could sightsee without our bags. A couple of things to improve upon: the ice machine in the fridge needs some attention (esp. when it is so hot in the summer season). With such a large group of nine, a linen service or extra towels would be appreciated. There was also no extra bedding for anyone who slept on the couch or extra blankets for when everyone was hanging out in the family room. There isn't quite enough comfy furniture for nine of us to watch a movie, so some had to lay on the ground or sit in bar stools to watch TV. A welcome basket with some coffee, snacks, and bug spray would be awesome, too. Overall, we would stay here, again. Lots of fun and beautiful. Thank you!
