=== FULL DESCRIPTION ===
From the soaring high ceilings, floor to ceiling windows, rich textiles and fixtures, you'll never want to leave! Just a short stroll to Forsyth Park, and the bustling downtown city district, this impressive two-bedroom apartment has everything you need.

The space
If you're looking for the perfect getaway, Luxe on Liberty is an enchanting retreat for your party. Located on the second floor of a Historic townhome built in 1870, this bright and charmingly appointed apartment can accommodate up to four Lucky guests in Savannah’s Historic District. From the soaring high ceilings, floor to ceiling windows, rich textiles and fixtures, you'll never want to leave! Just a short stroll to Forsyth Park, and the bustling downtown city district, this impressive two-bedroom apartment has everything you need for your Savannah vacation.

Upon entering you'll immediately be greeted by original hardwood flooring, warm accents and tones, fitted with comfortable, rich furnishings. Seize the day away while relaxing on the plush white sofa, while you catch up on your favorite shows in the sun-filled living room, with beautiful views of the treelined street. The open floor plan creates an inviting environment, and makes entertaining a breeze. While you are preparing a home cooked meal in the kitchen, you'll never feel separated from the living room, as guests can converse through the home seamlessly. The luxurious kitchen has been tastefully renovated and appointed with quartz countertops and high-end stainless steel appliances.

The first bedroom that is off of the living space is fitted with a queen size bed, two spacious closets, and flat screen television mounted above the bed. Across the hall, a spa-like bathroom with a walk-in shower is conveniently located between both bedrooms. At the back of the apartment, you'll find the primary suite that features a plush king size bed, large armoire, and a smart television.

Luxe on Liberty is just steps to many well-known restaurants, coffee shops, and attractions here in Savannah. Whether you are coming for a quick getaway, or an extended stay, you'll be centrally located to everything in town. 

Sleeping Configuration:
Bedroom 1: King Size Bed
Bedroom 2: Queen Size Bed

Luxe on Liberty offers two private tandem parking spaces directly behind the home.
