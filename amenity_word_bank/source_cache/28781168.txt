=== FULL DESCRIPTION ===
Live like a Savannahian in this stately two-story apartment, walking distance to the Fountain at Forsyth Park, Mrs. Wilkes, and Crystal Beer Parlor.

The space
In true Savannah fashion, the first level starts a half flight of stairs from the street. Enter into the foyer, adorned with the owner's collection of antiques and fine China. The dining room is just off the foyer and sits up to six guests. The kitchen sits just off of the dining room and has all the essentials. Towards the back of the home you'll find the bright and well-decorated living area. A plush sofa anchors the room with plenty of places to sit and sip! Guests can access their favorite streaming channels on the flat screen TV, or enjoy basic cable channels. A half bath is just off the living room.

Upstairs, three more beds and a full bath. The daybed is in a private nook at the top of the stairs, with the first bedroom just around the corner. This bedroom features a Queen bed and a beautiful collection of the owner's private art. Guests also have the convenience of a flat screen TV mounted across the bed. Unpack your suitcase in the antique chest of drawers and enjoy crawling into the hotel-grade linens at the end of a long day.

Next door, the second bedroom with King size bed and a walk-in closet. The full bath - shared by both bedrooms - is at the end of the hall. The bathroom features a walk-in shower and a wide vanity making getting ready a breeze. Guests are also able to enjoy the private courtyard, shared with the garden-level tenants.

What is parking like for this home? 
Parking for this home is on-street and metered, which is enforced by the City of Savannah from Monday through Friday, 8am to 5pm. Guests may conveniently pay using the ParkSavannah app, feed the meter or parking kiosks directly, or park on a nearby non-metered street. Please take note of posted Sweep Zone signs when parking on the street.

Sleeping Configuration:?
Bedroom 1: Queen Bed
Bedroom 2: King Bed
Bedroom 3: Twin Daybed (open to hall)

We can welcome your dog companions at this pet friendly home, but ask that you find other arrangements for cats, birds, and other furry friends. There is a $130 pet fee (non-refundable) with a 35 lb. maximum weight limit per dog and a limit of 2 dogs per reservation. Some homes may allow larger dogs, please inquire for more information.
