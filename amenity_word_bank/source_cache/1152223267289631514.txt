The space
Welcome to Bohemian Bliss at Bird Baldwin! Nestled in the heart of Savannah's historic district within the famed "Bird Baldwin House," this chic apartment promises an unforgettable Savannah experience.

What sets Bohemian Bliss apart?

-Ground Floor Access: Offering convenience for all guests, this stunning apartment boasts ground floor accessibility without compromising on natural light or charm.

- Prime Location: Immerse yourself in Savannah's vibrant atmosphere with unbeatable proximity to local gems. Just steps away from Savannah Coffee Roasters, indulge in the city's finest brews. Explore nearby Pulaski Square or take a leisurely stroll to Forsyth Park. Plus, for concert enthusiasts, the Savannah Civic Center is conveniently located just across the street.

- Tasteful Amenities: Experience the perfect blend of style and comfort within our thoughtfully furnished space. Relax in the vibrant ambiance of the living area or step outside to the patio and savor the gentle Savannah breeze.

The bedroom offers ample space and luxurious comfort, featuring a plush king bed, a dresser for storage, a large flatscreen smart television, and a desk—ideal for both relaxation and productivity.

Whether you're here for work or leisure, Bohemian Bliss at Bird Baldwin provides the ultimate Savannah retreat.
