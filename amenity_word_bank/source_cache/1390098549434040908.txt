=== FULL DESCRIPTION ===
Embrace the timeless beauty and rich history of Savannah. Perfect for a cozy getaway, you'll fall in love with the Southern charm and vibrant culture all around you. From picturesque streets to historic landmarks, every corner of Savannah tells a story. Whether you're strolling through beautiful parks or savoring delicious cuisine, this enchanting city will capture your heart and soul. Come experience the magic that makes Savannah unforgettable!

The space
Welcome to this beautifully appointed townhouse nestled in the heart of Savannah’s Historic District! This warm and inviting home perfectly blends classic Southern charm with modern comforts, making it an ideal choice for couples, friends, families, or small reunions looking to experience Savannah like a local. 

Step inside and feel instantly at ease in this thoughtfully designed space where every detail has been chosen to enhance your stay. The entire second-floor unit comfortably sleeps up to four guests with two private bedrooms — the first with a luxurious king-size bed and the second with a queen bed — each boasting its own spacious full bathroom with marble walk-in showers. There’s also a convenient half bath on the main floor and a comfy king sofa bed in the living room for extra flexibility. 

Relax in the stylish living area with a smart TV, whip up your favorite meals in the fully equipped kitchen complete with stainless steel appliances, granite countertops, a coffee bar, and a dishwasher, or take care of laundry with the in-unit washer and dryer. Step outside to enjoy your morning coffee or evening wine in the shared courtyard, a peaceful oasis right in the Historic District. 

Located just blocks from Savannah’s most beloved sights, you’re within easy stroll of iconic squares, scenic parks, the Downtown Design District’s local shops, and a variety of celebrated restaurants and cafés. Wander cobblestone streets, soak up the rich history around every corner, or simply savor the gentle Southern breeze from your courtyard retreat. 

Whether you’re here for a girls’ weekend, family getaway, or a romantic escape, this home is the perfect blend of comfort, walkability, and historic charm. Book today and start making unforgettable memories in enchanting Savannah!

Permit License Number: SVR-03118

Guest access
Guests will have exclusive access to the entire private apartment, along with shared access to the peaceful courtyard located on the property. The courtyard is a lovely spot to enjoy morning coffee or unwind in the evening. Entry is via self check-in with a secure keypad, allowing for a smooth and flexible arrival. Street parking is available nearby and is free, though guests should be mindful of posted street-sweeping signage and metered streets on neighboring streets.

Other things to note
We are a smoke free property. This includes No vaping. Smoking and vaping is not allowed in the Courtyard. All renters must be over the age of 25, unless it is a child accompanied by a parent. No Pets are allowed. We are a smoke free property. This includes No vaping. Smoking and vaping is not allowed in the Courtyard. As this home is located in the heart of Savannah’s Historic District, guests may hear typical city sounds, including occasional street noise, pedestrians, nearby restaurants, or horse-drawn carriages during the day and early evening. While the apartment is designed to be a peaceful retreat we want guest to be aware there may be city noises they may not be accustomed to. Many guests appreciate the lively atmosphere—it’s all part of the charm of staying in the city center.
