Neighborhood highlights
Located in the heart of Savannah’s Historic District, one of the city’s most charming and walkable areas. Step outside and you’ll find tree-lined streets, Spanish moss, and centuries-old architecture that make Savannah one of the most beautiful destinations in the South.

Stroll just minutes to Forsyth Park, the city’s iconic green space, or explore nearby restaurants, boutique shops, cafés, and local art galleries. You’ll also be close to River Street, City Market, and the SCAD Museum of Art, making it easy to enjoy Savannah’s best attractions without driving far.

The area is safe, scenic, and ideal for morning walks, photography, and experiencing true Southern charm. Whether you’re here for a weekend getaway, SCAD event, or business trip, this location offers the perfect balance of history, culture, and convenience.
