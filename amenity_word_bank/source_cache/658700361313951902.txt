=== FULL DESCRIPTION ===
Drayton Place 2 is a premier property for anyone searching for Savannah vacation rental getaway for up to four guests. This one-bedroom, one-bathroom charmer dazzles with a fully updated interior, quaint outdoor courtyard, and entertainment features. The home's enviable location in Savannah's famed Thomas Square neighborhood places guests next door the vibrant Starland District.

The space
Every day is a dream in Drayton Place 2! Experience the excitement of staying at one of the charming Savannah vacation homes located in the enviable Starland District. A standout property among Savannah vacation rentals, Drayton Place 2 comfortably fits up to four guests in a setting that can only be described as luxuriously Southern. This one-bedroom, one-bathroom Savannah getaway home is a perfect pick for a couple seeking a spot that feels like it's filled with stories. While this home is full of old charm, it certainly offers all of the modern amenities one could ever want from Savannah vacation homes. Of course, it's the location of Drayton Place 2 that places it on the list of the best vacation rentals Savannah GA visitors can use to see the city! This home is positioned in Savannah's famous Thomas Square zone. This highly walkable neighborhood is full of vibrant galleries, bars, restaurants, coffee shops, boutiques, and spots for live music.



There's no need to worry about long halls or corridors when reaching your door at Drayton Place 2. The home offers a private entry in a secure building. This unit is located on the residence's first floor. While this home is part of a larger complex, it is truly easy to feel totally secluded while tucked away in a spacious apartment full of old-world touches.



Step inside a beautiful living room with "parlor room" vibes. The living room in Drayton Place 2 features beautiful couches positioned by a stately all-white fireplace. A smart television provides streaming access. Next, head to the home's modern kitchen to enjoy a layout peppered with new cabinets, a refrigerator, stove, dishwasher, microwave, toaster, and coffee maker. A bistro table with seating for two located in an alcove within the kitchen offers a cozy place for coffee, snacks, and meals.



The spacious bedroom in Drayton Place 2 features a king bed. In the living room you will find additional sleeping arrangements for guests with a queen size sofa bed. In addition, there's a full-sized bathroom with a tub/shower combination. This is also one of the rare Savannah vacation rentals to feature its own washer and dryer. The highlight of this property is a private porch with gorgeous curtains. There is also a shared courtyard that offers a gas grill, quaint café lights, and plenty of seating.  Another perk of this home is that it provides shared off-street parking for one vehicle. Additional free parking is available on the street. The list of amenities included with Drayton Place 2 includes air conditioning, heating, Wi-Fi, streaming, cable, and household essentials.



Guests booked at Drayton Place enjoy free access to a private resort-style swimming pool located in nearby Tybee Island, Georgia. Located just 27 miles from Savannah, Tybee Island is one of America's premier beach destinations. A day trip to this beloved coastal destination is very doable when booked at a Savannah vacation rental.



As one of the standout properties located in Savannah's Starland District, Drayton Place 2 truly opens the door for an exciting vacation full of many different attractions. When throwing together a Savannah vacation itinerary, be sure to include strolls that take you through Forsyth Park, the Savannah Historic District, Bonaventure Cemetery, and Chippewa Square to get a feel for the lush, manicured aesthetic that makes this historic city so beloved. The list of other must-see attractions to hit while visiting Savannah includes the Cathedral of Saint John the Baptist, the American Prohibition Museum, Olde Pink House, and the Historic Savannah Theatre. When looking for a place for an afternoon of shopping and dining in Savannah, there's no better spot than City Market. This pedestrian-only zone provides a perfect spot for "taking it easy."



When you're ready to "liven things up" in Savannah, River Street is the place to find amazing bars, restaurants, and live music. In fact, River Street is often considered the hottest spot for experiencing Savannah's nightlife. Consider taking a ferry to see views of the city passing by from the water. Savannah is also an incredible place for walking tours. The city's reputation for being America's most haunted city creates great opportunities for haunted tours after dark. In fact, many local agencies bring guests through the historic cemeteries of Savannah with only lanterns to guide their way. In addition, guests can enjoy everything from guided house tours to pub crawls while exploring the charms of the city! Finally, Drayton Place 2 is a natural choice for people seeking vacation rentals Savannah GA visitors can use to enjoy the city's many festivals and events. The Savannah Bluegrass Festival, the Savannah Jazz Festival, the Savannah Craft Brew Fest, the Savannah Film Festival, and the Savannah Music Festival are all more than worth your while.



Drayton Place 2 is an exceptional property when looking for a Savannah vacation rental for a couple. This home comfortably sleeps two guests in a setting that feels whimsically formal. The amenities included with the home make the experience of staying here much grander than its square footage would let on. The home's location in the middle of the Starland District makes it a top choice for anyone seeking to be able to view many of Savannah's best attractions on foot. Don't miss this rental!
