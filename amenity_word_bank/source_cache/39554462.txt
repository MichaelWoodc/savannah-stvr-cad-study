=== FULL DESCRIPTION ===
This charming carriage house is in the heart of the downtown, historic district. Lovely courtyard provides quiet outdoor space to enjoy our Southern weather and our location is perfect...just a short walk to all of the best spots to explore in the city! We hope you absolutely love the modern updates and amenities we provide while still maintaining all of the old world feel that makes Savannah, "The Hostess City," so wonderful!

SVR 00299

The space
Your private carriage house apartment is located on the second story of the detached carriage house, behind our home. Once inside, you will find the master bedroom with brand new, KING bed, large and newly renovated bathroom with a fantastic walk-in shower, and a comfortable and open living/dining space! There are flat screen smart TVs with YoutubeTV in both the living room and bedroom as well as super fast Wifi! Have a third guest tagging along? The sofa also pulls out into a comfortable queen bed, and of course, all the linens are provided! We also hope you get to enjoy some time outside in our adorable courtyard, equipped with gas grill for your use and plenty of seating! Need something extra, like a pack n play for your little one or beach towels for a trip to Tybee? Just ask and we can try our best to have it waiting for you!

Guest access
The entire carriage house apartment is YOURS! Completely private, detached from the main house. You will travel down the path alongside the main house, through the shared courtyard, and up to your apartment on the second floor of the carriage house.

Other things to note
Don't be afraid to reach out if you need anything!  We live super close-by and are always happy to help out, even mid-stay.
