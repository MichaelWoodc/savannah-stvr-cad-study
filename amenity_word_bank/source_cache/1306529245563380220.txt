=== FULL DESCRIPTION ===
Welcome to this stunning 3-story estate in the heart of downtown Savannah! This listing is for the upper unit, which spans the 2nd and 3rd floors of the home, offering privacy, ample space, and modern amenities. Each unit has a private entrance for your convenience.
Accommodations:
This home includes 4 bedrooms, 5 bathrooms, and comfortably sleeps up to 10 guests, making it perfect for larger groups or families.
Short-Term Vacation Rental # SVR-00391

Guest access
The Upper unit has its own private entrance. You will be provided a unique access code for your stay.

Other things to note
Second Floor Highlights:

Spacious living room and dining area, perfect for gathering.
A bedroom featuring two queen beds and a flatscreen TV – ideal for families or groups.
A charming hall bathroom with a clawfoot tub and a separate full bathroom with a standing shower.
Fully equipped kitchen with granite countertops and stainless steel appliances.
Dining area with seating for six, opening onto a serene screened porch to enjoy Savannah’s breezy evenings.

Third Floor Comforts:

Bedroom 1: A cozy queen bed and a private full bathroom.
Bedroom 2: Another queen bed with its own full bathroom.
Master Suite: A luxurious retreat with a king bed, flatscreen TV, and a spa-like en-suite. The master bath features a clear shower, twin pedestal sinks, a clawfoot tub, a chaise lounge, wet bar, and large mirrors. Step out onto the private porch for morning coffee or evening relaxation.
Washer/dryer conveniently located in the hallway.

Regarding parking: Two off-street parking spots are available at the back of the house, and there is free on-street parking on W Bolton St and Barnard St.
