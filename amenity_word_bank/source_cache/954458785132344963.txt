=== FULL DESCRIPTION ===
Make your Savannah vacay easy peasy lemon squeezy 🍋 with a stay at The Lemon Drop! This bright & beautifully renovated 2BR/1BA condo blends historic Savannah charm with colorful, modern comfort. Enjoy soaring ceilings, a spacious living + dining area, a fully equipped kitchen & plenty of room to unwind. Located in the Victorian District just a few blocks from iconic Forsyth Park 🌳, it’s the perfect home base for exploring Savannah on foot!

SVR 02490

The space
Welcome to The Lemon Drop! 🍋 Step inside and make yourself at home in this bright and spacious second-floor condo in Savannah's beautiful Victorian District. With two bedrooms, a full kitchen, in-unit laundry, and plenty of room to spread out, relax, and recharge, it's a colorful and comfortable home base for your Savannah adventure!

🛋️ LIVING + DINING

The light-filled living and dining space is perfect for hanging out with friends and family, enjoying a meal together, planning the day's adventures, or kicking back after exploring Savannah.

Settle into the spacious living room and stream your favorite shows on the Smart TV using our high-speed WiFi.

🍋 FULL KITCHEN

Our fully equipped kitchen makes everything from a quick breakfast to dinner at home easy. You'll find a large basin sink, dishwasher, range, refrigerator, cookware, dishes, and the essentials for cooking during your stay.

Start your morning with a fresh cup of coffee from the Keurig—we provide a few starter K-Cups to get you going before a day of Savannah exploring! ☕️

Please note: The refrigerator in this unit does not have an ice maker.

🛏️ BEDROOM ONE – KING + TWIN

The spacious primary bedroom features a plush king-sized bed with crisp white linens, plus a twin daybed for an additional guest.

There's also a Smart TV, making this a great spot to sneak away, get cozy, and relax at the end of the day.

🛏️ BEDROOM TWO – QUEEN

The second bedroom features a comfy queen-sized bed with fresh, crisp bedding and its own Smart TV—a cozy space to settle in and recharge for another day in Savannah.

🚿 MODERN BATHROOM

The beautifully renovated bathroom is bright, fresh, and modern, with a gorgeous walk-in shower that adds a little spa-like luxury to your stay.

🧺 WASHER + DRYER

Need to freshen up your clothes during your trip? We've got you covered! A private washer and dryer are located right inside the condo and available for guest use.  Detergent provided.

🌳 WALKABLE + CLOSE TO FORSYTH PARK

The Lemon Drop is tucked into Savannah's beautiful Victorian District, a quieter, more residential part of the city that still puts you close to all the fun.

You'll be just a few blocks from iconic Forsyth Park, making it easy to start your morning with a walk beneath the live oaks, visit the famous fountain, grab a coffee, or enjoy one of the many events happening in the park.

You'll also have neighborhood restaurants within walking distance! Grab a slice at Pizza Party 🍕 or head to Tacos + Tequila for tacos and margaritas. 🌮🍹

Ready to explore downtown? Savannah's downtown nightlife, restaurants, historic squares, and River Street are about a 20-minute walk away, or just a very quick Uber ride. You can also hop aboard Savannah's FREE DOT shuttle, which picks up at Forsyth Park and makes stops throughout the Historic District—an easy way to explore without worrying about driving or parking! 🚎

We love that this location gives you the best of both worlds: a peaceful neighborhood to come home to with so much of Savannah easily accessible on foot, by free shuttle, or with a quick ride.

🚗 FREE STREET PARKING

This condo does not have a dedicated off-street parking space, but parking is typically easy in our neighborhood! You'll find free, non-metered street parking around The Lemon Drop and on the surrounding streets.

That means no daily parking fees or parking garage to deal with—park nearby and start enjoying Savannah! 🚗✨

💛 LOCALLY OWNED + MANAGED

We're Megan & Justin, Savannah locals, and we love getting to share our city with our guests! The Lemon Drop isn't managed by a big, far-away vacation rental company—you'll have local hosts who know Savannah and are nearby if you need us.

We're always happy to share our favorite restaurants, bars, coffee shops, sightseeing spots, hidden gems, and things to do. Looking for the perfect brunch? A great cocktail? Kid-friendly activities? Something fun to do on a rainy day? Just ask! We love helping our guests experience Savannah like a local.

🍋 COMING WITH A BIGGER GROUP?

The Lemon Drop has additional vacation rentals right here in the same building! Consider booking The Lemon Drop Simply the Zest or Lemon Lane in the back along with this condo. It's a great setup for larger families, friend groups, wedding parties, or anyone traveling together who wants to stay close while still having their own space.

Historic charm, modern conveniences, plenty of room, a peaceful neighborhood, and a sweet location—your Savannah stay at The Lemon Drop is easy peasy lemon squeezy! 🍋✨

Guest access
The entire second floor condo is all yours! You'll enter using your own coded front door and there is no shared space whatsoever. We do ask that you please review the photos to see the stairs you will have to climb to access the condo, as we do not have an elevator or other way to get to the second floor.

Other things to note
Parking is free and easy on the street right out front! Just be careful for the once per week posted sweep zone times! We provide a handy little sign right on the inside of the condo door to help you with parking which reminds you where you can and cannot park each night!

Neighborhood highlights
We are located in the Victorian District of Savannah, which is the area immediately bordering the Landmark Historic District to the South. We are four blocks off of Forsyth Park. While the Victorian District has its far share of awesome new spots to check out and explore, we are also pretty close to the downtown, better known tourist locations as well! We are 1.5 miles to the riverfront area and City Market. If you want to get downtown quickly and don't want to drive, walk through the gorgeous scenic squares, or take an Uber, you can just hop on the FREE dot shuttle express trolley, with a nearby stop right on Forsyth Park!
