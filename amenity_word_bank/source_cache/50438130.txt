=== FULL DESCRIPTION ===
Our historic tiny carriage house is situated in the heart of downtown and makes for an authentically Savannah home base for your stay! Step back in time as you ascend the wrought iron spiral staircase up to your private hideaway! Filled with modern conveniences and updates, including tons of storage space for those extended stays, small but mighty kitchenette,  and charming Juliette balcony overlooking the gorgeous shared courtyard space! Walking distance to all Savannah has to offer!

SVR 01794

The space
This private carriage house retreat is ALL YOURS! Enter the space through your private lane-side entrance into a first floor foyer and closet space. There you will find lots of space to store all of your luggage and unpack...no need to bring your bags upstairs! Then you can climb up the charming wrought iron spiral staircase up to the main living area. Once at the top of the stairs, you will see an open studio apartment, complete with queen sized bed (and comfy memory foam mattress!), small but mighty kitchenette (complete with Keurig and k-cups, snacks, filtered water, and lots of cookware!), wall mounted TV with full cable programming, large desk that makes for the perfect work-from-home space, washer dryer, and full bathroom with tub/shower combo. We hope you appreciate the light drenched space and historic charm, including original wide panel hardwood floors, Savannah gray brick walls, and even a sweet little Juliette balcony where you can overlook the ultra modern shared courtyard space!

Guest access
The entire apartment is yours! We are situated in the heart of the historic district, so getting around on foot is best! There is ample street parking all around our home, both paid metered as well as free! We are happy to provide more detailed parking instructions to help guide you when you book with us.

Other things to note
Don't be afraid to reach out if you need anything!  We live super close-by and are always happy to help out, even mid-stay.
