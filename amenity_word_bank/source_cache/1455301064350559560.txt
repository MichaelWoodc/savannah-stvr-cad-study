=== FULL DESCRIPTION ===
Oglethorpe Square, Savannah, 1856. Carriages still pass daily.

The Garden Suite, ground floor: two bedrooms, a living room of its own, and the coolest, quietest sleep in the house.

Skip the car: River Street one way, Broughton Street shops the other, Michelin-worthy tables between. Out back: a sauna, a cold plunge, and the best part of your morning.

Loved like a home, because it is one.

Bosch Huis began as a dream. Guests like you make it real. Tap the heart, see my Host Profile, or book it.

The space
THE GARDEN SUITE
The ground floor, where the house keeps its cool: two bedrooms, each with a queen bed, plush mattress, and premium linens, plus a living room of your own with a fireplace waiting on its restoration. The second bedroom opens through the first, the kind of layout made for families and close friends. Low light for sleeping in, an ensuite bath with a walk-in shower and double vanity, fresh towels already waiting. 

The sauna, cold plunge, and Peloton sit just steps from your door, closer to this suite than any other. Same for the laundry, and the parking is right on your side of the house. Car to bed in under a minute, bed to cold plunge in less. A Nespresso machine, mini-fridge, microwave, and 55-inch smart TV with every streaming service mean the suite runs on its own schedule.

MAIN LEVEL
One flight up, two ways to get there: the front staircase, or the back one by the parking lot. A long hallway leads to the kitchen, lined with art collected over the years. Local artists, European finds, antique market treasures. Free snacks along the way, and a photo wall of past guests. Add yourselves.

The kitchen is chef-grade: gas range, All-Clad, Vitamix, KitchenAid, a Samsung refrigerator with filtered water and ice. It's shared, but every suite has its own essentials, so barely one in a hundred guests use it. Private slots on request. Guests call it "beautiful" and "private."

OUT FRONT
All of Savannah, one minute at a time: Broughton Street's shops in one, the squares, galleries, and bars in the same, the Riverfront in five, Forsyth Park in ten.

Smoke detectors throughout. First aid stocked. Cameras outside only — never inside. The same small cleaning team, every stay. If maintenance ever touches your dates, you'll hear it from us first.

ALWAYS IMPROVING
