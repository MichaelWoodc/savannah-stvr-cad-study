=== FULL DESCRIPTION ===
Josephine's Carriage House, Apt. B, is a unique and charmingly renovated historic building located in the heart of Savannah's Victorian District. This beautiful space seamlessly blends modern convenience with old-world charm, providing you with a comfortable and stylish stay in close proximity to some of the city's most popular attractions, including the famous Savannah Victorian District and only a couple miles away from the famous River Street.

The space
Step inside this triplex house, part of a beautifully restored historic triplex in one of Savannah’s oldest neighborhoods, dating back to the 1830s. Artistic touches and eclectic décor make this space truly unique, blending historic charm with modern comfort. 

The open-concept living and dining area is perfect for relaxing, while the fully equipped kitchen is ideal for light cooking or microwaving. The bedroom features a plush queen-sized bed and a private deck where you can soak up the sun, a cozy retreat after a day of exploring. Please note, there are stairs required to access the home.

Located just a short walk from the sprawling 22-acre Forsyth Park, Apt. B puts you in the heart of Savannah’s historic Victorian District. Wander charming streets lined with storybook cottages and grand 19th-century homes, discover local shops, restaurants, and nightlife, or explore the vibrant energy of the neighborhood.

Don’t miss the seasonal Forsyth Farmers’ Market on Saturday mornings for fresh, locally grown and handmade foods. For day trips, I-16 provides quick access to the Outlet Mall or Tybee Island Beach, with historic Fort Pulaski along the way.

Whether it’s your first visit or a return trip, this home offers a historic, comfortable, and centrally located home base to experience the best of Savannah living.

Guest access
This is a private triplex, and guests have full access to their unit and the shared exterior property. For added security, exterior cameras are in place around the building. 

 Free parking is available on the side of the property, with one designated space per unit.

Other things to note
Please note that parking is available on a first-come, first-served basis, with one space per home. On-site parking is located immediately to the left of the building. Free street parking is also available on W. Park Ave. and Montgomery St. Guests are responsible for adhering to all posted parking signage and street-cleaning restrictions.

Neighborhood highlights
We’re located in Savannah’s celebrated Victorian District, just west of Bull Street and only 3.5 blocks from Forsyth Park. The property sits roughly midway between River Street to the north and the Starland Art District to the south, making it a convenient and central location for sightseeing.

Many of Savannah’s most popular attractions are walkable for some guests, including the Railroad Museum, SCAD Art Museum, the Design District, and Broughton Street shopping. Restaurants, shops, and bars are just a few blocks away, and a Kroger grocery store is located across Forsyth Park.

The Victorian District is known for its historic charm and ongoing revitalization. You’ll find a mix of beautifully restored late-1800s homes, grand Italianate and Queen Anne–style mansions, and classic Savannah streetscapes. Walking to and around Forsyth Park is a highlight for many visitors.

We are situated on a traditional Savannah lane, adding to the neighborhood’s authentic feel. The surrounding area blends residential homes, local businesses, SCAD facilities, and short-term rentals, creating a lively but established neighborhood atmosphere.
