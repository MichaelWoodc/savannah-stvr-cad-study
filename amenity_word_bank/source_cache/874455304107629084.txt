The space
Step back in time and experience Savannah's rich history with a stay in your very own historic guest house - built in 1790! 

This one-of-a-kind space has been lovingly restored to preserve many of its original details, from the exposed brick walls to the original fireplace and hardwood floors.

This gorgeous guest house features one bedroom and one bathroom and is filled with the character and charm that will make your stay unforgettable. You'll love the unique layout and original details that make this space truly special.

Don't forget the fully equipped kitchen, complete with all the amenities you need to whip up your favorite dish.  The smart layout gives you plenty of comfort if you choose to skip the restaurant and stay home for a meal or two.

The bathroom is beautifully appointed with modern amenities, while still maintaining its historic charm. The bedroom features a comfortable queen-size bed, ensuring a restful night's sleep.

Located in the heart of downtown Savannah, the guest house is just steps away from some of the city's best restaurants and attractions. And when you're ready to unwind, our private courtyard offers a peaceful oasis away from the hustle and bustle of the city.

Guests can enjoy high-speed WiFi, and metered parking is available on the street. You'll also appreciate the convenience of keyless entry and security systems, ensuring your safety and peace of mind during your stay.

Experience the magic of Savannah's rich history and Southern charm with a stay in our historic guest house. Book now to experience a truly unforgettable stay in one of the city's most unique and iconic properties.

*Of note, this guest house is accessible through the courtyard, as well as the service lane in the back of the property (East York Lane).
 
*Additionally, the entrance to the courtyard (from East Oglethorpe Ave.), as well as the courtyard itself, are shared with other guests.  

*Nevertheless, we ensure that all of our guests are here to relax and have a great time, so the shared spaces should create great opportunities to meet and mingle with similarly-minded people - which is what the Hostess City is all about!

Guest access
Guests will have access to the guest house, the shared courtyard, and the metered street parking across the building.  Please refer to the check-in guide on the app for more details.

*Note:  The guest house is in a property that houses other guests much like an Inn, so we ask that the space is treated with dignity and respect at all times.  Please keep noise to a minimum between 8pm and 8am as per city ordinance.  Parties and events are strictly prohibited.

Neighborhood highlights
When you stay in this guest house, you will be living in some of Savannah's most desirable real estate - right in the heart of Downtown! Some of the best restaurants and sights are only steps from your rich and luxurious property.

Having a car is unnecessary when living here - you can walk, ride, or bike anywhere in this part of town. Once you have booked, we will send you plenty of recommendations on what to do, see, eat, and drink while in Savannah - this list should kickstart your planning.

Convenience and grocery stores are just a few minutes walk from the historic guest house - without mentioning the plethora of cafes, bars, and restaurants. Walk through each square to shop local retailers, grab a coffee or quick bite, or park yourself on a bench and just enjoy the Savannah scenery.

Those in the mood for a true Southern meal will love being close to Mrs. Wilke's Boarding Room, with other local favorites such as 700 Drayton or Crystal Beer Parlor just a short walk or Uber ride from the property.

Popular tourist areas such as River Street and City Market are just a 5-minute walk away, while Savannah's local green space - Forsyth Park - is about 20 minutes away on foot or five minutes by car.

Once you're here, you will not want to move out!
