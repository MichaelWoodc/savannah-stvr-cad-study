Licensed occupancy and rental-property calls

Recorded-address CAD calls, not confirmed rental-guest incidents or verified 911-origin calls. January 1–September 20, 2024. Current complete license inventory is used; historical license activity in 2024 is unknown. These are descriptive counts over the supplied period, with no occupancy or annual adjustment.

Single-license parcels support comparison by individual licensed occupancy. The second set includes all APN-matched licensed parcels and sums their license limits, a parcel capacity measure rather than an individual rental limit. Each parcel appears once; zero-call parcels remain included. Calls to shared parcels cannot be attributed to individual units. Different occupancy groups can differ in property use, number of units and other characteristics.

Exact normalized license or digest situs addresses generate candidates via license APNs. A unique coordinate parcel corroborates the address or excludes it if conflicting; address-only matches are retained when no coordinate parcel is found. Coordinate-only, intersection, block and ranged-address calls are not attributed. No nearby/neighbor calls are added. Duplicate call numbers count once; conflicting repetitions are excluded. No fuzzy matching. Unmatched license APNs and ambiguous calls remain in audit files.

The six subplots are the most frequent citizen-initiated call types across matched rentals, selected from this log rather than judged to be guest-related. Overall plots include all initiation types and a separate citizen-initiated series. Small groups can be unstable. Lines connect group means, not fitted relationships.
