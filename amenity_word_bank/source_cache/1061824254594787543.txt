=== FULL DESCRIPTION ===
Unwind in our 1890 Historic District townhouse steeped in Southern charm. Enter the lantern-lit courtyard & step into soaring 13’ ceilings, crown molding, antique furnishings, luxe kitchen & two welcoming king bedrooms with 2.5 baths. Enjoy free parking in front & only steps from Troup Square, the Cathedral & favorite local eateries. Sip morning coffee or evening wine in the serene courtyard, then stroll cobblestone streets & savor Savannah’s best Southern flavors—all from charming Harris Haven.

The space
Please take a moment to add our Haven on Harris townhouse to your wishlist by clicking the ❤ in the upper-right corner so you can easily find us again. 

Our family townhouse is a true Southern haven—tucked away on a quiet residential street, hidden from the crowds, yet just steps from Savannah’s most beloved sights and experiences.

This downtown gem offers two off-street parking spaces (a rare and valuable treat in Savannah), making it easy to explore the city on foot and return home effortlessly. Step outside and you’re immediately immersed in the rhythm of historic Savannah—tree-lined streets, classic townhomes, and the gentle hush of a true residential neighborhood.

If you love strolling beneath moss-draped oaks, admiring 18th- and 19th-century architecture, or lingering at an outdoor café, you’re right where you should be. Just around the corner, stop by Urban Deli for fresh bites, gourmet sandwiches, and excellent lattes—perfect fuel before a day of exploring. Along the way, enjoy a peaceful walk through Troup Square, home to the elegant Armillary Sphere, a favorite photo stop and one of the city’s most charming squares.

Wander a few blocks to famed Jones Street, often called the most beautiful street in America, then stop in at Clary’s Café, a beloved Southern institution featured in Midnight in the Garden of Good and Evil. From there, continue south to Forsyth Park, where you’ll find the iconic white fountain, shaded walking paths, and the lively Saturday farmers market filled with local vendors, flowers, and fresh coastal flavors.

History lovers will appreciate the short walk to the breathtaking Cathedral Basilica of St. John the Baptist, one of Savannah’s most awe-inspiring landmarks. Nearby Broughton Street invites you to shop, dine, and explore—home to boutiques, art galleries, restaurants, and classic Savannah squares that reveal something new with every visit.

From this location, Savannah unfolds at an easy, unhurried pace. Slip on your walking shoes, follow the sound of church bells and carriage wheels, and let the beauty, history, and warm hospitality of the Hostess City carry you from one charming moment to the next 🌸
