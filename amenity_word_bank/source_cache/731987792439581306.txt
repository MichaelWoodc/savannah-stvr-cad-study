=== FULL DESCRIPTION ===
A charming 1915 Victorian filled with amenities- just a 15 minute walk to Forsyth Park with a free trolley to the historic district around the corner. Located in the heart of Savannah’s hip, artsy Starland District - this is where the locals come for the best restaurants.

The 2,100 sf space combines old-world elegance with modern comforts and amenities: a fun shuffleboard table, luxurious king-sized beds, large stocked kitchen, private patio, ultra-fast wifi & TVs in all bedrooms.

The space
Located on a city block lined with colorful 1915 Victorians, this private home is walkable to Forsyth Park, the Historic District and the best restaurants the city has to offer. Upscale amenities include all cotton bedding and towels, a fully stocked kitchen w/Nespresso, coffee, condiments & cooking supplies, a comfy down sofa and chair, and full size washer and dryer. A unique, bank-shot shuffleboard table provides pre-game entertainment before hitting neighborhood hot spots. Tennis rackets, yoga mats and board games galore provide for a fun time. Sweet, private backyard for enjoying your morning coffee. Gated parking for one vehicle or free street parking. Beach chairs and towels.

Neighborhood highlights
Starland is an artsy, vibrant area with arguably the best restaurants, music venues and coffee shops in Savannah. This is where the locals go, yet you are close to all the tourist spots. We are a diverse neighborhood, authentically Savannah. Antique stores, a bookstore, unique art and vintage finds are around the corner. Forsyth Park is only 3/4 miles away. A FREE SHUTTLE runs from our street up to the River/Historic District.

Tybee Island and the airport are both within a half hour drive. For SCAD parents, we are very close to Victory Village.

Our quaint and colorful street reminds us of San Francisco’s famous “painted ladies.” Diverse, urban and hip, Starland is seeing tremendous investment and revitalization. As in most cities, you will encounter people experiencing homelessness in our neighborhood and throughout Savannah.

Of Note: Our street is very quiet, but be aware that you will hear Savannah’s well-known train as it makes a quick, but loud, pass by the house most mornings on its way through town. You can find more information at the Starland District website
