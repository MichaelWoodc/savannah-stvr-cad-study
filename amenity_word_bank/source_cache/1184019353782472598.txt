=== FULL DESCRIPTION ===
Situated on majestic Troup Square, this manor offers an unforgettable experience in the heart of Savannah. Boasting four bedrooms, impeccable outdoor spaces, and an unbeatable location - This is the perfect retreat for families and friends alike!

The space
Nestled off the majestic, tree-lined Troup Square, Troup Square Manor offers an unforgettable vacation experience in the heart of Savannah. This beautiful manor boasts four bedrooms, impeccable outdoor spaces, and an unbeatable location, making it the perfect retreat for families and friends alike.

What Sets This Home Apart?

- High-End Finishes: Defining southern luxury, Troup Square Manor features a beautiful chef's kitchen with high-end finishes and exquisite architectural details that showcase the home's historic charm. Every corner of this home reflects meticulous attention to detail and superior craftsmanship.

- Spacious and Inviting: The incredible floor plan is designed for gatherings and entertaining. The parlor level is an entertainer's dream, featuring a large gathering room, a formal dining room, a gourmet kitchen, and an outdoor veranda—all on one floor, ensuring everyone can be together. Just added: a courtyard plunge pool, a rare find in the Historic District and the perfect way to cool off after sightseeing.

- Prime Location: Facing Troup Square, guests are just a 10-minute walk from Forsyth Park, a 10-minute walk to Broughton Street, and a 15-minute walk to the river. Situated on a quiet street, the manor is also close to delightful cafes and restaurants, offering the perfect blend of tranquility and convenience.

The bedrooms are thoughtfully arranged across three levels to provide privacy and comfort for all guests. The garden level is self-contained, and offers a full living space, a fully stocked kitchen, a king bed, a queen sleeper sofa in the living room, and a luxurious bathroom. In the main house, the primary bedroom features a king bed, a spacious sitting room, a private balcony overlooking the courtyard and a spa-like ensuite bathroom. Down the hall, the third bedroom is equipped with a queen bed, a dedicated workspace, and a full bathroom with a tub-shower combination across the hall. Making your way to the top bedroom,  this charming loft features a skylight, a king bed, and plenty of space to ensure privacy and relaxation.

What is parking like for this home?
Guests will have access to one private parking space directly behind the home that can accommodate a full-size sedan or SUV.
