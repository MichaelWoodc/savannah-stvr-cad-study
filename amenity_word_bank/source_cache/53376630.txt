=== FULL DESCRIPTION ===
Featured on HGTV Magazine's "A Local's Guide to Savannah"! Spend a little time wrapped in the luxury and beauty of Savannah Nest. Standing out among Savannah vacation rentals for its sprawling layout, Savannah Nest sleeps four guests comfortably. The home pampers guests with a large kitchen, two-sided hearth, 2.5 bathrooms, generous bedrooms, private courtyard and oversized front porch made of the stuff of Southern-style dreams!

The space
Take a look inside one of the Savannah vacation homes perfect for making life in the city feel like a dream! The open-concept first-floor layout of Savannah Nest takes everything that's great about classic estates to the next level by appealing to modern sensibilities. Note the beautiful columns, wood moldings and gleaming hardwood that define every corner of this beautiful home from the second you walk through its charming entryway. The standout feature of the home is the double-sided brick hearth in the center of the living room. The home's main living area is peppered with windows on every wall to fill the space with natural light! Touches like ceiling fans, recessed lighting and pendant lighting keep this home feeling alive with soft light and flowing air.



Something that's noticeable about why this home is special among Savannah vacation rentals from the moment you walk in the door is that it's decorated with fresh, funky furniture. A blue velvet couch, armchairs oozing in art deco and splashy artwork on the walls all create an elite space that combines the prestige of a well-preserved estate with the fun of an art gallery. Of course, the vivid living room also offers the simple comfort of a flat-panel television for your viewing pleasure.



Next, the kitchen in this home impresses on every level. It offers a sprawling, fully open layout with wide cabinets topped with glowing granite. This top-shelf kitchen features new appliance in stainless steel. Guests can make use of a full-sized fridge, oven, dishwasher and microwave. Guests can also choose between drip and pod coffee in the morning! A beautiful dining table set beside the home's oversized brick hearth offers room for six with plenty of room for stretching out!



Take a step outside before going upstairs to check out the bedrooms! Savannah Nest is one of the few Savannah vacation rentals featuring a fully private courtyard! Enjoy a small back porch that steps down to a brick courtyard with a lovely outdoor dining table with umbrella.



This home technically has 2.5 bedrooms. The first is a large suite with a king-sized bed set before a grand brick hearth! This bedroom also connects to its own full en-suite bathroom. Enjoy Roku TV on a wall-mounted television in this ultra-comfortable bedroom. The home's second bedroom also features a king bed, brick fireplace and Roku-connected television. It also has its own full en-suite bathroom. Finally, a fun bedroom with a Harry Potter theme has been magically placed just beyond the home's second bedroom to create a convenient sleeping space that's especially perfect for kids! This sleeping area features built-in twin bunks, a reading nook, a large dresser and a Roku-connected television.



The bathrooms in Savannah Nest are pampered with touches like double sinks, marble countertops, oversized walk-in showers, huge mirrors and vibrant lighting. These are truly showcase bathrooms that elevate the luxury of the home beyond compare! This home also offers the perk of being one of the Savannah vacation homes to provide a washer and dryer right at home! The home's laundry nook is located in the half bathroom. It features a prep area, folding area and storage cabinets.



This home delights with special amenities like Wi-Fi, air conditioning, laundry, ample outdoor lighting, private parking, private entrances, a beautiful front porch, and a private courtyard. Guests of Savannah Nest also enjoy access to a private heated pool located just a short drive away in beautiful Tybee Island! Savannah Nest is the ultimate choice among vacation rentals Savannah GA visitors can choose for privacy. For added safety and peace of mind, security cameras are located on the exterior of the property. While this home makes you feel like you're in your "own little world" within the city, it is situated on a beautiful oak-lined street located just two blocks from famous Forsyth Park.



Savannah Nest puts city guests so close to the best that Savannah has to offer! Staying in this beautiful Savannah rental house for up to six guests makes it easy to get to places like the Savannah Historic District, River Street, Market Street, Chippewa Square and pedestrian-only City Market. Attractions like the Mercer Williams House Museum, Sorrell Weed House and Bonaventure Cemetery are also close! This is also one of the best vacation rentals Savannah GA visitors can pick if they want easy access to excursions that will bring them to beautiful places like the Wormsloe Historic Site, Old Fort Jackson and Skidaway Island State Park. While spooky walking tours, fun pub crawls and walks across the campus of the Savannah College of Art and Design will keep anyone busy, there's always the option to make a 30-minute drive to "American paradise" with a trip to the beaches of Tybee Island. Make Savannah Nest your nest away from home!
