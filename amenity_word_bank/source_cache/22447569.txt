=== FULL DESCRIPTION ===
A charming ground level apartment in Downtown Savannah's Historic District on famous Jones Street.

The space
This charming, one-bedroom, one bath ground level apartment is tucked away on Historic Jones Street. You’ll wake up each morning to walk the cobblestones of what Southern Living Magazine dubbed America’s prettiest street. 

You’ll find plenty of comfortable seating in the den, including a small dining table and chairs. Enjoy a little “me time” curled up on the couch with a one of the provided books. The adjoining living room has more seating and a large flat screen TV. A large island countertop that doubles as a breakfast bar, opens and connects the living room into the galley style kitchen. When it’s time to get some shut-eye, get cozy in the comfortable Queen sized bed. The bedroom has plenty of storage space for longer stays, so unpack and relax on your private, enclosed porch.  A second living room includes a Queen sleeper sofa, and can be closed to allow for 2 private sleeping rooms each separate from the Living Room.
 
Start your day of exploration at Clary’s Cafe, the setting for several breakfast scenes in Midnight in the Garden of Good and Evil. You’ll be a short walk from the Cathedral of St. John the Baptist, which underwent a major restoration to preserve their beautiful stained glass windows and architectural integrity. A few blocks away at the corner of Bull Street and Liberty, you’ll find local shops featuring everything from handmade handbags and leather goods at Satchel, to Celtic crosses at Saints & Shamrocks.
 
Why Lucky Savannah Loves this Property:
- This apartment can be rented with the main house to sleep up to 12 Lucky guests. 
- The exposed beams and brick fireplace in the bedroom add true Savannah style
- The bathroom has a convenient pocket door to separate the shower. 

What is parking like for this home?
Parking for this home is on-street and metered, which is enforced by the City of Savannah from Monday through Friday, 8am to 5pm. Guests may conveniently pay using the ParkSavannah app, feed the meter or parking kiosks directly, or park on a nearby non-metered street. Please take note of posted Sweep Zone signs when parking on the street.
