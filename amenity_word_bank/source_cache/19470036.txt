=== FULL DESCRIPTION ===
This two bedroom, two bath apartment is centrally located on Whitaker Street, a perfect starting point to explore everything Savannah has to offer.

The space
This two bedroom, two bath apartment is centrally located on Whitaker Street. No matter which direction you set out in the morning, you’ll be just blocks away from all that Savannah has to offer. 

When you open the front door, you're immediately greeted by a large and comfortable living area. You shouldn’t need a vacation from your vacation, so don’t feel bad about spending an evening in, curled up on this comfortable couch watching a show (or two!). Whether you’re enjoying coffee and breakfast at the kitchen counter breakfast bar, or dinner at the cozy dining table, the Eleanor has plenty of room for quality family time. At the end of the hall you’ll find a full bath and a Queen bed in the first bedroom. The Primary Suite has a king bed and renovated full bath. 

When you’re ready to start your day, just cross the street to tour St. John’s Episcopal Church, known for its melodious chimes and magnificent stained glass windows. In 1942, St. John’s the Green-Meldrim House, one of Savannah’s finest examples of Gothic Revival architecture. During the Civil War, General Sherman occupied the Green-Meldrim House and penned his famous telegram to President Lincoln, presenting the city of Savannah as a Christmas present. 

Why Lucky Savannah Loves this Property:
- Each bedroom in this beautiful home has it’s own bathroom, so everyone will have space to get ready to explore Savannah! 
- Only half block from Mrs. Wilkes Restaurant, close enough to beat the long lines that form at this popular lunch spot

What is parking like for this home?
Parking for this home is on-street and metered, which is enforced by the City of Savannah from Monday through Friday, 8am to 5pm. Guests may conveniently pay using the ParkSavannah app, feed the meter or parking kiosks directly, or park on a nearby non-metered street. Please take note of posted Sweep Zone signs when parking on the street.
