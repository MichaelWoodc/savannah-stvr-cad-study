=== FULL DESCRIPTION ===
Experience Savannah's bustling downtown with this two-bedroom condo. Soaring 15-foot ceilings and a full kitchen provide the perfect crash pad for a busy getaway.

The space
Experience the heart of Savannah's historic downtown in this contemporary two-bedroom condo. Located at the corner of Broughton and Whitaker Street, you're only steps away from the city's most sought-after attractions, restaurants, and shopping.

The soaring fifteen foot ceilings greet you at the entry, leading you into the open kitchen and living area. Feel at home with granite countertops and stainless steel appliances, stocked with every essential you'd need for a quick bite before exploring the town.

Open to the kitchen is the spacious living area, which boasts stunning views of Savannah's "Main Street", Broughton Street. Kick your feet up after a long day of exploring and watch your favorite shows on the flat screen TV. Classic board games are found in the console for rainy afternoons.

Off the living room are the two bedrooms; the first facing Broughton Street with its own en suite bath. Another full bath in the hallway divides the apartment from the second bedroom, tucked away from bustling Broughton for those who prefer a darker space to rest their eyes. 

From the prime location to the contemporary decor, Broughton Central is perfect for a couples' getaway or girls' weekend. Experience the city's animated nightlife and rich food (and drink) culture only seconds away from your perfect Savannah HQ.

Bedroom 2: King bed
