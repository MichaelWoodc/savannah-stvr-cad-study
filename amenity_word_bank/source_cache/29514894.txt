=== FULL DESCRIPTION ===
Leave the bustle for the birds and experience the quiet, charming side of downtown Savannah. This spacious getaway hosts up to eight Lucky guests.

The space
Situated just West of the famous Fountain at Forsyth Park, Huntingdon Getaway is the ideal vacation home for a large family looking for a local experience. 

Park off-street in the designated parking areas and walk into the Savannah home of your dreams. The first level boasts high ceilings and ample natural light. Guests will enjoy kicking back and relaxing in the living room while their favorite show plays on the flat screen TV. An adjoining dining room comfortably seats eight and connects to an open kitchen with stainless steel appliances.

Upstairs, three bedrooms with two full baths accommodate an additional six guests. The first bedroom has a Queen size bed with en suite bath, and the second and third bedrooms have King and Queen size beds, respectively.

Head East towards Savannah's largest green space, Forsyth Park, and grab a bite at a few local favorites. On Saturdays, the Forsyth Farmer's Market gives guests a taste of the local agriculture. Head North along Bull Street for a promenade through the squares. Most of Savannah's main attractions such as Broughton Street, City Market, and the riverfront are just a short 15 minute walk away.

What is parking like for this home?
Huntingdon Getaway includes two private parking spaces off-street: one in the driveway and another in the attached garage, which can accommodate a full-size sedan, or mid-size SUV. Trucks and large SUV's should have no trouble finding a non-metered spot near the home.
