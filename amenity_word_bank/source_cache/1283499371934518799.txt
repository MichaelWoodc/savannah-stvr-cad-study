=== FULL DESCRIPTION ===
*Please note that there are currently main house and courtyard renovations taking place that affect use of the courtyard space.  These renovations are scheduled to be completed by September 4, 2026.*

A large carriage house located in the perfect center of Downtown Savannah. Walking distance to everything, but quiet enough for a solid night of sleep on the king size bed. With access to a courtyard oasis.

The space
The carriage house opens into a common courtyard lounge chairs on a bricked patio. There is a 10 person dining table to accommodate any family meal. The courtyard features a propane grill and a plunge pool. The 10 foot brick walls with gated entrance secure your privacy.

The carriage house has a full suite of shampoo, conditioner, body wash, etc. provided by Public Goods which is a vegan, fair trade, harsh chemical free company.

The carriage house has a TV and the gigabit speed internet keeps everything running nice and smooth.

STVR Certificate: SVR-03243

Guest access
The courtyard and plunge pool is shared between all the guests. 

Should you need it, there is a courtyard bathroom on the back of the property as well with a shower in case you need to rinse before going back to your room.

The courtyard should be cleared by 9PM  out of respect for your neighbors. There is a hefty fine for noise violations from the city.
