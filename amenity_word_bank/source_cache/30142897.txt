=== FULL DESCRIPTION ===
A renovated ground level apartment in Downtown Savannah's Historic District on famous Gaston Street, off of Forsyth Park!

The space
This charming, one-bedroom, one-bath ground level apartment is just a stones-throw from Forsyth Park! Comfortable furnishings with a modern flair will have you “living like a local” on the Historic District’s famous Gaston Street. Located just a block away from beautiful Forsyth Park, this updated apartment is the perfect retreat for two or four Lucky guests. 

Upon entering, you'll be greeted by a open space that offers a beautifully renovated galley kitchen, modern furnishings and a decorative fireplace. The kitchen is fully equipped, so you can enjoy breakfast from the comfort of your home-away-from-home. The dining area is open to the living area, which offers a comfortable couch, and a large flat-screen TV. The bedroom has a Queen bed, and lots of closet space so you can settle in during your stay. The bathroom has a large tiled walk-in shower,  and a lovely vanity where you can get ready for a night out on the town.

Savannah’s top eateries, including 700 Drayton and Clary’s, are all within a five-minute walk from..... Spend some time browsing the antiques at Alex Raskin’s, or tour the famous Mercer-Williams House on nearby Monterey Square. Get a taste of eating local by picking up fresh ingredients at the Farmer’s Market on Saturdays in Forsyth Park.

What is parking like for this home?
You have one assigned private parking space directly behind your apartment. Any other parking for this home is on-street and metered, which is enforced by the City of Savannah from Monday to Friday, 8am to 5pm. Guests are encouraged to either purchase a pre-paid parking pass from the Savannah Visitor Center, or park on a nearby non-metered street. Guests should be wary of the posted Sweep Zone signs.
