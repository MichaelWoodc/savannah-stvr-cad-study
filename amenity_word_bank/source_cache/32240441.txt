=== FULL DESCRIPTION ===
The Cohen House achieves that perfect blend of historical character and everyday comfort. This home is perfect for family and/or friend reunions.

The space
The Cohen House Main + Garden achieves a perfect blend of historical character and everyday comfort. Located on Jones Street, you'll wake up to some of the prettiest views in town and be within a short walk from several historic landmarks and points of interest. 

The first floor of the main home is up one flight of stairs, over the garden apartment. You’ll enter in the foyer, where you can walk straight back to the kitchen, go upstairs, or enter the living room. Two large rooms combined, the living space has plenty of comfortable seating and a large collection of books. The formal dining room has seating for six, with an additional table and chairs in the eat-in kitchen with an additional seating area and TV. 

The first of four bedrooms has a king sized bed, couch, and french doors to a private deck overlooking the courtyard.With two closets, you’ll have plenty of room to unpack and settle in. The second bedroom has a queen sized bed and ensuite bath with freestanding tub. On the third floor are the last two bedrooms. These connected bedrooms are perfect for guests with children! The main room features a queen sized bed and writing desk, and the adjoining room has two twin beds.

In the garden level apartment, you’ll find plenty of comfortable seating in the den. Enjoy a little “me time” curled up on the couch with a one of the provided books. The adjoining living room has more seating and a large flat screen TV. A large island countertop that doubles as a breakfast bar, opens and connects the living room into the galley style kitchen. A Keurig is provided, so you can enjoy a cup of coffee while you plan out your adventure each day. When it’s time to get some shut-eye, get cozy in the comfortable Queen sized bed. 

What is parking like for this home?
Parking for this home is on-street and metered, which is enforced by the City of Savannah from Monday through Friday, 8am to 5pm. Guests may conveniently pay using the ParkSavannah app, feed the meter or parking kiosks directly, or park on a nearby non-metered street. Please take note of posted Sweep Zone signs when parking on the street.
