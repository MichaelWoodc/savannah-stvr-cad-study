=== ABOUT THIS SPACE ===
Gorgeous 2 bedroom condo with luxury kitchen and bathrooms. Open living area has a wall of windows overlooking Wright Square. This location is steps from all the best fine dining restaurants Savannah has to offer, as well as museums, casual restaurants, boutiques, specialty stores, bars, day spas and historic landmarks. 

City of Savannah SVR-00808

The space
The condo has a large gourmet kitchen with marble counter tops which is open to a great dining room and living room with leather sectional sofa. The back bedroom has a Queen bed and nice bathroom with combo tub/shower next door off the hallway. The master bedroom at the front of the unit has a King bed, a lovely en suite bathroom with large custom shower and walk in closet. 

Conveniently, you will have a private laundry room with Whirlpool front loader washer and dryer.

Guest access
The building has coded entry from street level into the lobby stairwell area. This unit is on floor 2 of a 2 story building and there is no elevator so this is unsuitable for anyone who cannot walk up stairs.

Other things to note
We include parking for 1 car!! The owner's "monthly customer" card for Robinson Memorial Garage will be in the condo and must be returned at checkout. This garage is 2 blocks away. Parking for extra cars is on the street or in public garages. We suggest downloading the ParkSavannah app to pay for your numbered spot with a credit card on your phone. 

Events, holidays and stays during March & April may require 3 night minimums and/or have higher rates than the standard ones published.

This is a traditional vacation rental home, not a “bed and breakfast”. We do not provide food or drinks but we do provide basic cooking and serving utensils, a coffee maker, and dish soap. Downtown Savannah has a major supermarket (Kroger) and a 24 hour market (Parkers on Drayton) so there is no need to pack everything from home if shopping when you get here makes more sense. 

What to bring with you:

A typical grocery run prior to arriving at your vacation home would include coffee, beer/wine, milk, eggs, butter, bread and any other basic groceries you would need for the meals you eat in the home. 

When it comes to coffee, bring your own ground coffee or k-cups. The home has a 12-cup drip coffee maker and a Keurig.  We do not typically provide coffee but if you find coffee in the home please feel free to use it.

You should bring or buy your own napkins, laundry detergent, your own beach towels and beach gear, and your own cooler if you need one.  

What we provide:

We provide toilet paper (2 extras per bathroom), liquid hand washing dish soap, bathroom soap, sheets on each bed, and at least one bath towel for each guest. We do not permit our towels to be taken out of the house which is why you must bring your own towels if you visit the beach. 

If you find we have left seasonings, oil, dishwasher pellets, or any unopened food or drinks then of course feel free to use what is there. We occasionally leave items but just cannot promise them all the time. 

Please also note that in Savannah, sugar ants can smell cookies, spilled sugar or syrup, or an open sugar container from a mile away and will find a way in through a windowsill or door frame. It can be somewhat like a cartoon picnic where they march in a line to find the food. To avoid this try to keep your snacks contained in air tight baggies, the refrigerator, or buy single serve packets and put the trash in the outdoor trash cans as soon as possible. 

The following is standard Savannah Luxury Rentals information: We provide sheets, toilet paper and soap. If you prefer thick cushioned toilet paper bring it along but use it sparingly in our older plumbing and never flush "flushable" wipes or other products that are likely to cause a clog.

Children do not count in capacity. We do allow children but only for groups who understand this is a very "adult" decorated and furnished condo, the kids cannot be left to do what they want even for a minute they must be very closely monitored and we strongly recommend damage insurance. Guests must request to purchase damage insurance voluntarily it is $49 paid separately for CSA "Property Damage Protection" policy.
