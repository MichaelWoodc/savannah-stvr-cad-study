=== FULL DESCRIPTION ===
Tucked behind a historic home on W. Anderson St., this modern new-build carriage house blends Savannah charm with the comfort of newer construction. Enjoy a bright, thoughtfully designed space perfect for solo travelers or couples. You will be close to Forsyth Park, the Starland district and all that downtown Savannah has to offer!

The space
Welcome to your private Savannah escape, tucked behind a stunning new build home on West Anderson Street, this carriage house apartment offers the perfect blend of modern comfort and a convenient location. 

Thoughtfully designed for a elevated stay, the space features an open-concept kitchen, dining and living room.  The apartment offers lots of natural light, and carefully curated details throughout to ensure you have everything you need. Whether you're here for a weekend getaway or a longer stay, we hope you’ll feel instantly at home.

The living area flows seamlessly into a well-equipped kitchen, making it easy to unwind, enjoy a quiet morning coffee, or settle in after a day exploring the city. The bedroom offers a comfortable queen bed retreat designed for restful nights, while the overall layout maximizes both space and functionality. There is a full bathroom with a tub/shower combo as well as en suite washer and dryer.

Step outside and you’re just minutes from all of Historic Savannah’s most loved attractions. Enjoy a short stroll to Forsyth Park, or explore nearby coffee shops, restaurants, and boutiques.

Tucked away yet incredibly walkable, this carriage house is ideal for guests looking to experience Savannah like a local. There is not designated parking, but there is unmetered street parking on all the neighboring streets.

We can’t wait to host you!

SVR-03107

Guest access
Guests will have full access to the carriage house apartment. The apartment is on the second floor, above the garage. There is an exterior set of stairs that you will need to go up to reach the entrance door to the apartment.

Other things to note
Please note this is a carriage house located behind the main home, offering a private and tucked-away stay. As with many homes in Savannah’s Historic District, you may occasionally experience normal city sounds (road noise, emergency vehicles, sirens, trains, ship horns, trash trucks, neighbors, etc). Street parking is available nearby but not guaranteed. We recommend reviewing all listing details prior to booking and reaching out with any questions you may have.
