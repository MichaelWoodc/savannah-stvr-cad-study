The space
Welcome to Secret Savannah Collection – a combination of three separate units (that do not internally connect) creating a 4BR/3BA home, perfect for large groups, families, and likeminded travelers. The property is made up of three unique spaces all on the same grounds: Unit A, a spacious 2BR/1BA; Unit B directly next door, a cozy 1BR/1BA; and Unit C, the carriage house just steps away, another private 1BR/1BA.

What makes this home special?

Convenient split living: You’ll find a cozy living spaces, Smart TV's throughout each unit, and an eat-in kitchen in all three spaces; a delight for home cooks! Each kitchen features black stainless-steel appliances, granite countertops, and everything you need to prepare meals with ease.

Walkable location: Step outside and immerse yourself in the rich history and vibrant culture of Savannah’s South Historic District. Wander cobblestone streets shaded by moss-draped oaks, explore Forsyth Park, or tour iconic landmarks like the Mercer-Williams House Museum. For a change of scenery, Tybee Island’s sandy beaches and coastal charm are just a short drive away.

Whether you’re meandering through historic squares or basking in the sea breeze, Secret Savannah Collection is the perfect home base for your Savannah adventure!
