=== FULL DESCRIPTION ===
If one of your favorite parts of Savannah is the historic architecture, this is definitely the vacation home for you! Originally built in 1852, this beautiful townhome has been lovingly restored with modern conveniences. Despite having plenty of high-tech perks like keyless entry, full-home Wifi, and central HVAC, you can still see lovely historic touches like exposed brick walls, multiple fireplaces, and soaring ceilings.

The space
Enjoy a Stunningly Restored Historic Home

Inside of our Savannah vacation homes, designer furnishings provide a lovely backdrop for your Savannah vacation. When you walk in, you'll immediately be greeted by a cheerful living room decorated in warm shades of cream, blue, and orange. The kitchen has stunning marble countertops with a mosaic backsplash, while the dry bar has a fun jungle print wallpaper. In the bathroom, vintage-style tile complements a full-size tub and shower. Meanwhile, the bedrooms pair crisp, white bedding with restful hues of aqua and navy.

A Charming Spot for Mid-Sized Groups

Serenity on Jones is one of our Savannah vacation homes that fits groups of four. This makes it ideal for small family getaways or trips with a couple of friends. Two people can sleep comfortably on the queen-size bed in the first bedroom which also contains a comfortable armchair for relaxing and a desk for getting a little work done. The second bedroom also has a queen-sized bed, and its spot towards the back of the house makes it a quiet location to sleep in.

Our Savannah vacation rentals also contain plenty of space for the whole group to relax together. On the private patio, a table with four chairs is perfect for dining al fresco. The indoor dining table has an expandable leaf to seat four, while the living room contains spacious couches and plenty of other seating. There's even a dry bar to provide extra space for preparing drinks, appetizers, and other treats.

Our Amenities Make It Easy to Relax

We want our guests to feel completely carefree on their vacation, so we work hard to outfit our Savannah vacation rentals with a wide range of luxurious amenities. When you stay at Serenity on Jones, you get all the comforts of home. We provide everything you need to be comfortable.

Our amenities include a home fully equipped with essential appliances. A washer and a dryer let you clean your clothes, so you don't have to pack quite as much. The kitchen has a full-size stove, oven, and refrigerator, so you can cook delicious meals with your loved ones. It also has plenty of standard cooking supplies including pots, pans, utensils, serveware, and drinkware. When you want to plan incredible dinner parties or just grab a quick snack at home, the kitchen is ready to use. There are even smaller appliances, including a microwave, coffee maker, and toaster, to make your cooking easier.

The bedrooms and bathrooms are also filled with helpful amenities. We provide a hairdryer, ironing board, soap, shampoo, and hangers. Fresh linens and towels are provided, and we even have some extra pillows if desired. 

Quickly Access All Savannah's Attractions from a Central Location

Serenity on Jones has one of the most coveted addresses of Savannah. This quiet little street manages to be right next to some of the best sites of Savannah, yet it's still a peaceful and calm place to relax. From Serenity on Jones, you're just a short walk away from lovely Forsyth Square. This vacation home is also situated less than a mile away from attractions like the SCAD Museum of Art and the Massie Heritage Center. You can also easily hop onto Savannah's trolley system to explore attractions closer to the river or near the Victorian district.

Savannah has plenty of fun attractions for those who enjoy history, art, and fine shopping. It's also one of the best towns to visit if you love good food. Serenity on Jones is right near a few local favorites like Clary's Cafe and Yaupon Teahouse. While in town, you might want to try some upscale options like Noble Fare, Husk Savannah, and The Olde Pink House. After enjoying a great dinner, explore Savannah's nightlife. The town has everything from dance clubs like Club 51 to elegant cocktail bars like Congress Street Up.

When you want to enjoy the beautiful nature surrounding Savannah, consider a trip to nearby Tybee Island. This oceanside town is just a thirty-minute drive away from our Savannah vacation home. You can go on dolphin watching tours, kayak through stunning wetlands, or paddleboard in the ocean. All of our Savannah vacation home guests also have free access to our heated, resort-style swimming pool.
