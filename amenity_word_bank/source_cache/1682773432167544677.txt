=== FULL DESCRIPTION ===
Stay above the treetops on iconic Jones Street in this eclectic mariner cottage retreat. This private, second-floor condo features two bedrooms (one with king bed and a second, lofted third-floor bedroom with two twin beds), a cozy den with pullout sofa, full kitchen, large dining room, and charming balcony overlooking the shared courtyard below, perfect for those warm summer evenings! Parking on our block is free but our location is incredibly walkable to all downtown has to offer!

SVR 03180

The space
Welcome to your private retreat on iconic Jones Street in the Landmark Historic District of Savannah. This unique and character-filled condo occupies the entire second and third floors of a historic mariner cottage duplex, offering guests an authentic Savannah experience surrounded by timeless architectural charm.

Accessed through your private entrance at the top of the interior stairs, the condo combines historic details with comfortable modern living in one of Savannah’s most desirable downtown locations. Original architectural elements and old-world character have been thoughtfully preserved throughout the home, creating a warm and inviting atmosphere that feels distinctly Savannah.

The second floor features a spacious primary bedroom with a king bed (and TV), a full bathroom, washer/dryer laundry facilities, a fully stocked kitchen, cozy den (with pull-out sofa, perfect for that extra guest), and a beautiful large dining room with an oversized table perfect for gathering with family and friends, sharing meals, or working remotely. Just off the kitchen, guests can enjoy a private balcony — an ideal spot for morning coffee or evening cocktails beneath the Spanish moss.

Upstairs on the third floor, the loft-style bedroom includes two twin beds tucked beneath the historic roofline, creating a charming and cozy hideaway full of personality. This spacious room also has a small reading nook area to get away from it all, as well as a TV, just in case the kids want to watch their own thing!

Descend the outdoor spiral staircase and you'll find the shared courtyard patio featuring seating areas, a picnic table, and grill — a lovely outdoor space to unwind and enjoy Savannah’s relaxed pace.

Located in the heart of downtown Savannah, you’ll be just steps from historic squares, restaurants, shops, parks, and the riverfront while staying on one of the city’s most famous and picturesque streets.

Guest access
The entire second floor condo (and third floor loft) of this duplex is yours! Once you enter the front door, guests will find their private entrance for condo B immediately up the interior stairs. The courtyard is a shared space with guests who may be staying in the unit below.

Other things to note
This mariner cottage row home has two units - ask about booking both for larger parties! Check our profile for pictures of the other listing.

You are staying in the second floor condo of this historic duplex. As such, we always ask guests to be mindful and respectful of those staying in the first floor. 

Please note! We tried to maintain many of the original architectural features, including the two fireplace hearths. However, these are NON-OPERATIONAL (thus, not listed as a fireplace amenity), and are just for aesthetic appeal and a bit of old-world charm!
