=== FULL DESCRIPTION ===
A Perfect Romantic Getaway in one of the countries most magical cities!! This captivating 1869 Carriage House on Monterey Square offers it , Luxury, Romance and Parking!

The space
A Perfect Romantic Getaway in one of the countries most magical cities!! 
The 1869 original Carriage House is a free standing cottage behind the main house with its own private entrance off the lane/alley. 

It is located on the 2nd floor over the garage like all original carriage houses in Savannah, so there is a flight of stairs to get to the 2nd floor home.  
The carriage house is where the livery driver lived in the 1800s above the horses and carriages he cared for. The Wedding Cake Carriage House comes with a highly coveted garage parking space. 

The unusual design is evocative of a “Manhattan loft” set in a historic 1869 carriage house. This surprise blending of original 1920 - 1940 art deco furnishings and many antique elements creates a fun, casual ambiance.
The open and airy open concept living space showcases an entire wall of frosted artist windows, thus flooding the house with natural light.

The bedroom with king bed and TV is adjacent to the alluring bathroom with luxurious features such as 6 foot air tub and separate marble shower. 

The complete kitchen provides everything you need to whip up a Southern style meal or just grab toast and coffee on the way out your door to take in the cities historical sights that you are just steps out your door!

This reservation includes one private, garage parking space.

Guest may use the walled courtyard although it is shared with guests in the Main House.

NOTES:
There is a washer/dryer in the garage for you to use during your stay.

Maximum occupancy is 2 people, including infants & children.

Minimum age to reserve is 25 years old, no exceptions.

No pets or smoking.

We meticulously restore them back to the shining jewels they were in the 19th century. We have curated special homes for the enjoyment of vacation and business travelers of all generations. Our goal is simple - to create a unique, historic atmosphere for our guests to experience the "True Savannah" and "Live like a local". On nearby Tybee Island we are focused on providing ocean views & fun amenities with a variety of beach houses, town homes and condos, all with amazing views of the Atlantic Ocean. Our stylish and comfortable properties provide a perfect home away from home for families, romantic getaways or solo travelers.
