Welcome to The Darling House (est. 1900), a newly remodeled historic Victorian retreat in Savannah’s sought-after Victorian District, perfect for families + groups. Just blocks from dog-friendly, Forsyth Park and surrounded by local diners, cafes, restaurants, and bars, this spacious home puts the city at your doorstep. Relax in the private hot tub to elevate your stay! Hop on the free DOT trolley for a quick ride to River Street and Plant Riverside.  Welcome home to a Savannah Classic

The space
Welcome to The Darling House (est. 1900) — a beautifully restored Victorian retreat nestled in Savannah’s iconic Victorian District, where historic charm meets modern luxury. Designed with families, groups, and memory-making in mind, this spacious home offers the perfect blend of timeless architecture and elevated comfort just moments from the heart of the city.

Boasting a stunning all-oak bunk room and dramatic ceiling-to-floor historic windows, the home is filled with natural light and architectural beauty. Each spacious bedroom is thoughtfully designed and can easily be treated as a private master retreat, offering comfort, style, and flexibility for every guest. The fully renovated bathrooms introduce a clean, modern flair that perfectly complements the home’s historic character—bridging old Savannah charm with a fresh, elevated aesthetic.

Step outside and you’re within a short stroll of Forsyth Park, Savannah’s most beloved landmark, known for its famous fountain, shaded walking paths, weekend farmers markets, and dog-friendly green space. From morning coffee walks beneath moss-draped oaks to sunset strolls through historic squares, the location simply can’t be beat.

Inside, you’ll find thoughtfully updated interiors that preserve the home’s original character while delivering a true 5-star experience. High ceilings, classic details, and curated design elements create a warm, inviting atmosphere, while modern upgrades ensure comfort for every guest. Whether you're getting ready for a wedding, hosting a girls’ getaway, or traveling with family, this home is designed to accommodate and impress.

After a day exploring Savannah, unwind in your private hot tub, the perfect way to relax and recharge. This added luxury sets The Darling House apart—giving you a true retreat experience right at home.  Here we treat our neighbors like family so if you feel like floating back in time, you are welcome to continue your evening inside as you unwind.

Food lovers will appreciate being surrounded by some of Savannah’s most sought-after dining destinations. Enjoy brunch at Collins Quarter at Forsyth, grab Southern comfort food at Mrs. Wilkes Dining Room, sip craft cocktails at Artillery Bar, or explore the vibrant food scene in the nearby Starland District, home to favorites like Starland Yard, Two Tides Brewing Co., and eclectic local eateries. Coffee shops, bakeries, and late-night spots are all within easy reach, making every craving convenient.

For sightseeing, you’re just minutes from Historic Downtown Savannah, River Street, and Plant Riverside District, all easily accessible via the free DOT trolley, giving you effortless access to shopping, nightlife, rooftop bars, live music, and waterfront views without needing a car.

This location is ideal for guests looking to fully immerse themselves in Savannah’s charm—whether you're here for a wedding, SCAD visit, bachelorette trip, family vacation, or simply a relaxing escape.

At The Darling House, every detail has been considered to create a seamless, elevated stay—from the historic setting to the modern comforts and premium amenities.

Welcome home to a classic Savannah stay—reimagined.
