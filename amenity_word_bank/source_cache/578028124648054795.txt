Brand New Listing! SVR-02506

Welcome to historic downtown Savannah, and to the Huntingdon Pink House! 

Highlights include a gourmet kitchen with a massive island, 4 finely furnished bedrooms, 3 full baths, beautiful custom art, and 2 desk areas for remote working.

The space
KITCHEN & DINING
Prepare dishes in the gourmet kitchen, equipped with granite countertops, a deep sink, and a full suite of stainless-steel appliances. Gather for meals around the dining table for eight. Enjoy cocktails in the evenings around the huge kitchen island. 

BEDS & BATHS
This two-story home sleeps 8 guests between four bedrooms. 
Downstairs you’ll find a sun-drenched bedroom furnished with an upholstered full-sized daybed with an antique wardrobe, modern-chic armchairs, plantation shutters, 10-foot pocket doors and access to the full bath downstairs. 
Upstairs, you’ll find an additional three bedrooms and two full baths. Find supreme comfort in the primary bedroom, complete with a king bed, sitting chair, a 55-inch smart TV, and an en-suite bathroom with a custom antique vanity.
The third bedroom has a queen bed with tasteful art and furnishings.
Finally, a second primary bedroom features huge bay windows, a 55-inch smart TV, desk area and direct access to the guest bathroom. 
The guest bathroom comes with a tub/shower combination and floor to ceiling window.
