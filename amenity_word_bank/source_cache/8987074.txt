Experience Savannah like a local from this storybook cottage on Greene Square. Relax in a peaceful historic setting while being just minutes from River Street, shops, and restaurants. A cozy, unforgettable stay in the city’s most beautiful neighborhood

Excellent location - 2 bedrooms 1 king & 1 queen bed, 1.5 bathrooms. Enjoy the views of the square from the cozy front porch & unwind in the private courtyard.

Gingerbread Cottage on Greene Square FB

The Gingerbread House had everything we needed for a wonderful stay in Savannah. Nicole was a wonderful host and so incredibly responsive and proactive. The two bedrooms are really ample-sized and on the top floor, while the living room seats so many and has a huge television come on and this whole separate dining room and a kitchen has everything you need. There was a great little sitting area out back although we didn't use it much because of the weather. The best thing of all? THE FRONT PORCH. In fact, we spent our entire last day and the Airbnb sitting on the front porch all day long. The breeze from the fan, watching people enjoying Greene Square across the street, and listening to the many tour-goers on buses, Segways and bikes really made the trip special. We even got to sit through two or three thunderstorms, watch the Spanish moss sway the wind all around us, even learned all about our block just listening in on the tours going by the house! Would definitely stay here again.
