=== FULL DESCRIPTION ===
Beautiful garden apartment on world famous Jones Street in Historic Savanna, GA.  Live like a local, walk to shopping, pubs, and cafe's!  Enjoy Savannah in Style

The space
Gorgeous 1 bedroom, 1 bathroom apartment located on the ever-popular Jones Street. This charming apartment has beautiful hardwood flooring, two marble fireplaces and high ceilings. 

Located within walking distance of locally-owned shops and restaurants, such as Custard, Satchel, Public, and The Collins Quarter, this sweet little place is an ideal for enjoying Savannah as a local. This space is also within walking distance of beautiful Forsyth Park, Broughton Street, and City Market. This location in Historic Downtown Savannah means that you can relax and enjoy the serenity of this sweet little space, or you can put yourself right in the thick of the buzz of activity that is Savannah — the choice is yours.

This property is literally steps from downtown!  Located on famous Historic Jones Street, you can walk anywhere in the city with ease. Don't feel like walking? You can enjoy a free Downtowner or call a pedi-cab for a stroll around the city.  

This Property is perfect for Corporate & Monthly rentals.  Ask about special monthly rates and discounts!

You will be able to see a specific cleaning checklist that we have followed upon arrival or upon request.

This property features a king sized bed.
