=== FULL DESCRIPTION ===
Experience the charm of downtown Savannah at Soho Suite, a stylish retreat located right on Broughton Street in the heart of the Historic District. This cozy getaway features exposed brick walls, warm wood floors, a full kitchen, spacious living area, and large windows overlooking the city. Walk to Savannah’s famous squares, restaurants, shops, museums, and nightlife, then return home to relax in comfort with modern amenities including a washer and dryer.

The space
Memorable Georgia Vacations at the Soho Suite



Many travelers treat their vacation accommodations as a place to stow their luggage. The truth is, finding the right vacation homes Savannah will make a world of difference in enjoying your visit. Accommodations located within walking distance to interesting museums or live performances make it convenient to become part of the city, and live like the locals for a few days.



The best approach would be to find Savannah vacation rentals that provide every level of comfort. You can set up housekeeping and turn the place into your new home away from home. The Soho Suite on Broughton Street is one such place.



This newly renovated rental is a one-bedroom unit with one full bath. It is spacious enough for up to four guests. Located on the upper floors of a unique corner building, sitting amid a host of historic homes, this non-traditional Savannah vacation rental is the perfect spot to ponder the mystique of the city.



Floor to ceiling windows provide unobstructed views of Savannah’s historic district. Inspired by loft-style living, the high ceilings and open plan give this meticulously furnished rental an airy feel. Wood floors and exposed brick on the walls recreate the city’s cachet as an antebellum paradise.



The eat-in kitchen is full-size and is fully equipped for whipping up meals for you or a small party should you have the energy for it. Otherwise, a cozy living area is part of the great room furnished with a leather couch, TV and a reading chair by the voluminous windows. Sit and relax as you watch the world go by in your perfect vacation homes Savannah.



The bedroom picks up the warm and laid-back aesthetic with wood floors and exposed brick walls. Luxury bedding in cheerful colors give the room a welcoming feel. This room is set off behind heavy drapes, adding to the romantic yet casual atmosphere.



The bathroom is spacious with a shower and tub combination. The double capacity bathroom sink is set into smooth-surface counter top for a modern look befitting the suite’s newly renovated design. As a bonus, a full size washer and dryer are located just off the bathroom ensuring that you have everything you need for either quick or extended Georgia vacations.



Downtown Savannah is the Place to Be

The downtown area is one of the country's most extensive National Historic Landmark Districts, which is expected given the area's long and colorful history as a bastion of commerce and culture.



A number of Savannah's famous town squares are within walking distance from the Soho suite namely Franklin, Telfair and the recently restored Ellis Square. Dating back to 1733 and based on the Oglethorpe Plan, the squares feature monuments, fountains and green space for pedestrians to enjoy.



Georgia vacations are not complete without experiencing some of the historic spots that defined the area's role in the story of America. Christ Church and the Independent Presbyterian Church are among the longest surviving congregations with parts of the existing structure dating back to the 1700s. Historic cemeteries are part of the allure of the city. Prominent residents who made Savannah famous are buried in Colonial Park Cemetery established during English Colonial times and the Bonaventure Cemetery.



Savannah is synonymous with grand mansions, some of which have been lovingly maintained as public spaces. These homes include the Davenport House Museum, Judith Gordon Low's home and the Mercer William's House made famous by the movie "Midnight in the Garden of Good and Evil," based on a John Berendt novel.



After all that, be part of modern Savannah by exploring the art installations or watching cultural performances at the Savannah College of Art and Design. The Savannah Civic Center and the Jepson Center for the Arts are within a few blocks from Savannah vacation rentals on Broughton Street.



This charming city is best discovered in parts. Plan on staying at vacation homes Savannah where accommodations are homey and relaxed.
