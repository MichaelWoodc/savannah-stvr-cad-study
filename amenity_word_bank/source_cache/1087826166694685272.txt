=== FULL DESCRIPTION ===
Allure on Jones achieves the perfect blend of historical character and modern comfort.

The space
This townhouse on East Jones Street will have you feeling like a local in no time! With nothing but a key code between you and the cobblestones of “America’s prettiest street” - dubbed so by Southern Living Magazine - you’ll have all the convenience of home without having to lug around your keys.

The living room has a large couch and TV, so you can kick off your shoes and get started on the best part of a vacation - relaxing. On the other side of the original brick fireplace is the dining room, with enough space and seating to accommodate six Lucky guests. A half-bath is conveniently tucked away under the staircase. The large kitchen has been completely remodeled to have stainless steel appliances and everything you need to cook a family meal at “home.” The door off the kitchen leads to the porch and private courtyard. With plenty of seating and even a fire pit, this outdoor space is great for entertaining. 

Upstairs you’ll find a full bath a shower/tub combo shared by two of the bedrooms. The first bedroom has a twin bed with trundle, perfect for guests traveling with children. The second bedroom has a queen sized bed, TV, and wardrobe, so you can unpack and settle in. The primary bedroom is complete with a TV, closet, dresser, and en suite bath with a sleek, glass shower. Go ahead, take a long shower:  A tankless water heater ensures you won’t run out of hot water!

Why Lucky Savannah Loves this Property:
- Located in the residential area of the Historic District you’ll live like a local, and be only minutes from any tourist point of interest you desire. 
- Free parking: No meters on the street and 1 private space in the lane mean no hassles.
