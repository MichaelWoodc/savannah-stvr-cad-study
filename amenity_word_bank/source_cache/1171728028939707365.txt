=== FULL DESCRIPTION ===
Welcome to this beautifully renovated 4-bedroom, 2-bathroom Victorian condo in Savannah's historic Victorian District, designed for groups of up to 14 guests. Located a 2-minute walk from Forsyth Park, this second floor unit features 12-foot ceilings, heart pine floors, and access to a shared outdoor pool and dining area, making it an ideal retreat for groups exploring the best of Savannah.

The space
This condo is located in a building with 2 main house units and a carriage house in the back. This listing is for the second floor unit of the main house. The entire property has 3 units. Please inquire if you are interested in renting these units together.

🏠 1 Unit | 🛏️ 4 Bedrooms | 🛁 2 Full Bathrooms

FEATURES
🍳 Fully stocked kitchen with essential cookware
🏊 Shared heated outdoor pool and spa with loungers and dining area
⚡ High-speed WiFi
🛏️ Tempurpedic-quality mattresses and premium high thread count linens
🧴 High-end essential toiletries
📺 1 65-inch Smart TV in the living room
🚗 Free on-street parking available

ACCESSIBILITY & LAYOUT
A few steps are required to access the front door of the building. An additional 12 to 15 steps are required to reach this second floor unit. Once inside, the unit is entirely on one level with no interior stairs. The unit features 12-foot ceilings, plaster moldings, and heart pine floors. To access the shared outdoor space, guests walk out the front door and approximately 10 yards down the sidewalk to a locked gate. Gate codes will be provided at check-in.

Located a quick 2 min walk away from Forsyth Park, our home places you at the doorstep of Savannah’s acclaimed food and art scenes. There are a few homes in the neighborhood that need to be renovated, so while this could still be considered a transitional neighborhood, we have never had any safety issues. As far as safety is concerned, common sense is the general rule of thumb, just like in any major city.
