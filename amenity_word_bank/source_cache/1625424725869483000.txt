=== FULL DESCRIPTION ===
Experience Historic Savannah just steps from Forsyth Park at this spacious duplex retreat perfect for families, wedding groups, and friends. Combining both upper and lower units, it sleeps up to 8 with 2 full kitchens, multiple living areas, and private ensuite bedrooms. Walk to oak-lined squares, top restaurants, cafés, and boutiques from picturesque Lincoln Street. 
SVR-02340 + SVR-02341

The space
This combined listing includes both the upper and lower residences, thoughtfully arranged for larger groups who want to stay together while still enjoying the comfort of separate living spaces.
Upper Residence — Bright, Airy, and Spacious
The upstairs level serves as the heart of Hunter’s Knoll, featuring abundant natural light throughout, enhanced by large windows that create an open, airy atmosphere across the main living areas. The spacious modern kitchen is beautifully designed with updated finishes, generous counter space, and full appliances — perfect for preparing group meals or gathering over coffee before exploring Savannah. The expansive living and dining areas feel bright and welcoming throughout the day, making this level ideal for relaxing, entertaining, and spending time together. Two comfortable bedrooms one king and one queen, each with private ensuite bathrooms, provide privacy while maintaining the home’s bright and elevated feel. There is a small futon that can be used in the middle living room.
Lower Residence — Comfortable and Private
The lower level offers a private one-bedroom suite with a queen bed, a comfortable living room featuring a queen sleeper sofa, a full kitchen, and its own lounge space — ideal for additional guests who appreciate a little extra separation while still being part of the overall group stay.
Together, Hunter’s Knoll provides sun-filled gathering spaces upstairs, two full kitchens, multiple living areas, and flexible sleeping arrangements, creating the perfect blend of brightness, comfort, and convenience just steps from Forsyth Park.

Guest access
The entire home is setup for your use. We have two parking spaces in the rear parking lot dedicated for you. More details will be sent in the check-in instructions.

Neighborhood highlights
Located just a short stroll from the iconic Forsyth Park, Hunter’s Knoll places you in one of the most desirable and walkable areas of Savannah’s Historic District. Start your mornings with coffee or brunch at The Collins Quarter at Forsyth, located directly inside the park and known for its Australian-inspired café menu and scenic outdoor seating. Another neighborhood favorite, Clary’s Cafe, offers classic Southern breakfasts just minutes away, while guests looking for an elevated dining experience love the seasonal Southern cuisine at The Collins Quarter. For a true Savannah tradition, enjoy unforgettable Southern comfort food at Mrs. Wilkes' Dining Room, also nearby.
Art, history, and culture are all within easy reach. Spend the afternoon exploring the renowned Telfair Museums, one of the oldest public art institutions in the South, or step into the city’s past at the Savannah History Museum. A short ride brings you to the lively waterfront along River Street and the vibrant shops, galleries, and entertainment venues of City Market.
From peaceful morning walks beneath Savannah’s famous oak-lined squares to evenings dining at award-winning local restaurants, the surrounding neighborhood offers the perfect blend of historic charm, culinary experiences, cultural attractions, and effortless walkability — making Hunter’s Knoll an ideal home base for weddings, group getaways, SCAD visits, and unforgettable Savannah vacations.
