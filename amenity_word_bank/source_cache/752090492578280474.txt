The space
Looking for the ultimate Savannah Escape? Look no further! This incredible property is located just minutes from all Savannah has to offer. Private parking, pet-friendly, and an endless list of amenities one could wish for!

Built in 1899, this adorned and lovingly restored home offers the best of both worlds; Charming Historic details, such as original wood floors, ornate crown molding, while featuring modern, comfortable furnishings and finishes. Whether you're traveling with family or friends, everyone is welcome at Savannah Escape, including your sweet pups! Once you park your vehicle in one of the multiple private, off-street parking spaces, you'll enter via an electronic lock into your sweet home away from home to begin your vacation.

The first level is made for entertaining, which consists of the main gathering space, formal dining room, the completely remodeled kitchen, and a full-size bathroom. Boasting with natural light from the floor to ceiling windows, the gathering room offers an ample amount of plush, comfortable seating for everyone. The formal dining room conjoins, but can be closed off with a set of ornate, historic pocket doors, if you're looking for a more intimate dinner. Easy for serving, the kitchen is just at the back of the home and is truly equipped with all essentials needed to prepare home-cooked meals, cocktails, and so much more! 

You'll find all three bedrooms on the second level, where no detail has been spared. Two of the bedrooms offer King size beds, with plenty of space to store your personal belongings, and smart televisions. The quaint, yet charming third bedroom offers a Queen size bed. The luxurious full bathroom at the front of the home is the perfect place to reset by taking a hot, steamy shower, in the large walk-in shower.

Out back, your TRUE oasis awaits! A rarity here in Savannah, this large backyard is the perfect space to let your pups, children, or even adults play. At dusk, relax under the magical cafe lights, or join together on the back screened porch for an evening round of drinks. You'll never want to leave this Savannah Escape, but when you do decide to venture out, you'll be a short walk from amazing restaurants, coffee shops, and Forsyth Park!

Sleeping Configuration:

What is parking like for this home?
Private Parking: There is an ample amount of off-street, private parking, next to the home where guests can park side-by-side and tandem.

We can welcome your dog companions at this pet friendly home, but ask that you find other arrangements for cats, birds, and other furry friends. There is a $130 pet fee (non-refundable) with a 35 lb. maximum weight limit per dog and a limit of 2 dogs per reservation. Some homes may allow larger dogs, please inquire for more information.
