=== FULL DESCRIPTION ===
Welcome to The Habersham Loft, an elegantly renovated condo in the heart of Savannah’s vibrant downtown. This condo provides ample space to spread out, featuring three floors with two bedrooms and two and a half bathrooms. Enjoy custom touches throughout that create a modern yet cozy environment to relax in after a day of exploring. Close to everything, you will be just three minutes to Forsyth Park and surrounded by amazing dining, pubs, and attractions. 18 min to SAV International.
SVR-02781

The space
Tucked among the beautiful neighborhoods of downtown Savannah, this freshly renovated condominium features high-end finishes and stylish interior design from Plum West Designs.
Once home to the nefarious John Deering, this condo is one of a kind in Savannah.
Enter the three-level condo through a contemporary front door that sets the mood for the entire space.  To the right of the foyer is a spacious galley kitchen featuring a deep blue backsplash, solid surface countertops, and a brand-new appliance package. The kitchen is fully equipped and provides everything you need to cook, including pots, pans, utensils, dishware, and small appliances. A peninsula island provides dining space for three, while a dining table directly behind provides room for six.
Enjoy a beautiful sitting area on the first floor that opens directly to your private balcony. A small sofa and two chairs are great places to curl up and read your favorite book.   
Take the staircase to the top floor where two bedrooms are found. Enjoy a new king mattress wrapped in fresh linens and plush pillows in the primary bedroom. A large dresser provides ample storage for extended stays. In the second bedroom, find a new queen mattress with fresh linens, an eclectic dresser, and nightstands.
The second floor boasts clean lines, custom tile work, and a modern design. It is outfitted with a single vanity, toilet, low-entry shower, and all of the essentials, including towels, toilet paper, shampoo, body wash, and a hair dryer.
The bottom floor features a comfortable living space with an oversized sectional that pulls out into a queen-size bed. There is also a smart TV for streaming your favorite shows.
Working remotely during your stay? Our high-speed Wi-Fi supports video conferencing and multitasking.

Guest access
The entire condo is for your use! We have a balcony off of the back of the living room AND a rear patio off of the bottom level that you can sit and enjoy a morning coffee or before-dinner cocktail ;)
Parking is street-only and FREE.

Neighborhood highlights
The downtown districts of Savannah, filled with genuine Southern hospitality, offer the quintessential Southern experience rooted in the city’s colonial past. Situated on the edge of Savannah’s historical district south, you will be minutes away from everything the city offers.
You can walk to the iconic Forsyth Park in 12 minutes or enjoy Troup Square, a beautiful green space in your neighborhood.
Are you looking for breakfast? Clary’s Café is a classic perennial place two blocks from the condo. It serves Southern-style diner classics for breakfast and lunch. For a hip, modern setting, check out the Fox and Fig Café, which offers creative plant-based meals and espresso.
Treat your taste buds by grabbing dinner at one of the many excellent Southern-inspired restaurants, Hitch, Mrs. Wilkes Dining Room, The Olde House, and Vic’s On the River. All provide incredible dining experiences that offer the destination Southern fine dining you want.
A trip downtown would only be complete with a pint in one of the many historic and famous pubs. Stop into Six Pence Pub for a British-inspired scene, or let out your inner Irish at O’Connell’s Irish Publ. Get your karaoke at McDonough’s Restaurant & Lounge, or check out a sleek gastropub for a drink or weekend brunch at The Ordinary Pub.
Visit one of Savannah's many museums to learn about the city's history. The Beach Institute of African American Culture Center, Mercer-Williams House Museum, and the Telfair Museums are just minutes away. If you want to explore the city’s art scene, stop by the SCAD Museum of Art.
Want to see it all at once while riding or biking? We are minutes from Old Town Trolley Tours of Savannah and Savannah Bike Tours.
Getting around is easy with walkable streets, bike rentals, and Uber service.
