=== FULL DESCRIPTION ===
Charming and elegant 2 bedroom/2 bath victorian home with modern decor and appointments. Short walk to Forsyth Park, coffee shops and world-class dining. Perfect for a couple's weekend, girls' getaway, or families!

City license #SVR-01564

Guest access
The home is in the Victorian District and is divided into three private units. You would have the entire main floor unit to yourself (parlor level), which has 2 bedrooms, 2 bathrooms and front and rear secured entrance. Both bedrooms have queen size beds. There is no assigned parking space, however, we rarely have trouble finding parking right in front of the house or less than a block away. There is one set of stairs on the front exterior of the house that you would have to go up to access the front door. There is also a fenced courtyard in the rear of the unit, which is a shared space among the three units, so please be respectful of other tenants.

Other things to note
The unit is equipped with high speed wi-fi, cable, full-size washer/dryer, iron/ironing board and hair dryer. We also provide basic items like coffee pot and filters, dishes, pots/pans, etc. Our minimum stay is typically 3 nights but feel free to inquire about shorter stays as we may be able to make an exception depending on our schedule.

Neighborhood highlights
Please note there is a gas station/convenience store directly across the street from the house. While it is a bit of an eye-sore from the rest of the street, we've found it to be very safe and quiet and is actually pretty handy if you need to grab any last minute essentials or re-fuel the rental car. The rest of the street/neighborhood is very scenic and beautiful. There is a Victorian Market which is about a 2 minute walk away and sells grocery items, sundries, toiletries and beer/wine. Less than 5 min walk to to the Sentient Bean coffee shop (just at the entrance of Forsyth Park) so you can grab a cup of coffee or tea for your stroll through the park. There are also tons of top-notch restaurants that are just a short walk away!
