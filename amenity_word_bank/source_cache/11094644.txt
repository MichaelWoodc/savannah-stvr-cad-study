One Bedroom apartment suite Victorian/Savannah charmer  built is 1884 is located above Savannah Rum Runners Bakery and Café. The space
The Space
One Bedroom Victorian/Savannah charmer built in 1884, located above Savannah Rum Runners Bakery and Café. Our upper level row house apartment has been newly painted and furnished in a comfortable style, but still has the charm of a home built in 1884. The bedroom has a king-sized bed and there is a full-sized bathroom with shower. The living room has a queen sleeper sofa. Bedding for the sleeper sofa is located in the laundry area. Equipped kitchen and breakfast area.
