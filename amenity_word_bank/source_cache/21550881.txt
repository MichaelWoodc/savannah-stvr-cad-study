=== FULL DESCRIPTION ===
This adorable fully-updated apartment is in the heart of the Victorian District.  Relax on the front porch or take a stroll just a few steps away in Forsyth Park.  Built in 1902, you'll find original hardwood floors accenting the fully equipped kitchen with stainless appliances. Amenities include free wi-fi, cable,  and an HD TV.   Great food and drink are within walking distance with downtown Savannah a scenic twenty minute walk or quick Uber ride away.  

SVR-01175

The space
Situated in the Victorian District of Savannah, this house was built in 1902 as a two unit bungalow.   Freshly renovated, this apartment features new cabinets, quartz counter tops and stainless appliances in the kitchen.  The bathroom boasts an over sized shower along with a private toilet for extra convenience.  The heart pine flooring has been refinished with the original planks dated from saplings planted in the 1500's!  High speed internet and cable is provided along with a flat screen HD TV for viewing in the tastefully decorated living area.

Guest access
Guests have full access to the unit through a private entrance.  Amenities include in house washer/dryer, Keurig coffee maker with pods, and front porch with rockers.

Other things to note
Don't be afraid to reach out if you need anything!  We live super close-by and are always happy to help out, even mid-stay.

Neighborhood highlights
Savannah neighborhoods have experienced a renaissance over the last few years. Turn of the century homes are being restored to their original grandeur inviting visitors to Savannah to experience a touch of the past.

The home is close to all things in the Historic District. We are two blocks from Forsyth Park, with any easy stroll to their Saturday farmers market as well as other historic parks, and restored turn of the century homes. The Victorian District is an inclusive neighborhood of SCAD students, vacationers and permanent residence. Expect to find everyone enjoying the area well into the evening. Access to downtown restaurants, nightlife, and sightseeing is a short 20 minute walk or Uber/Lyft ride away. The local surrounding area offers many gems as well. Finish your day and relax on one of our rocking chairs on the over sized front porch

We are one of many rentals in a vibrant neighborhood. The year round residents go about their daily routine as vacationers enjoy the surroundings. Please be mindful that your neighbors keep normal schedules between 9 PM and 8 AM.
