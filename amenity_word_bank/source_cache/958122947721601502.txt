=== FULL DESCRIPTION ===
One bedroom apartment half a block from Forsyth Park 

Gorgeous decor and soaring ceilings await you in this totally renovated historic apartment steps from Forsyth Park, Starland District, SCAD etc. 

This is just over 1 mile from River Street. Parking is free on the street. Wifi is free. Linens are provided for the King bed. One extra person is allowed to sleep on the sofa which does not pull out. 

​​​​​​​City of Savannah SVR-01610

The space
This is a very large one bedroom apartment with a spacious entry foyer, living room, kitchen, bathroom and bedroom. There is a shared front porch. The building has 4 total apartments in it each with their own separate locking entry doors and nobody shares any of their indoor private dwelling space area.

Other things to note
Savannah is a tropical environment and certain bugs do reside in our city mostly outdoors but occasionally they wander in any home in town. If you see any native bugs dead or alive in the apartment we do not refund guests for typical native bug sightings.
