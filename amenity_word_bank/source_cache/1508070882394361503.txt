The space
Welcome to Secret Savannah Sanctuary A – a charming two-story retreat tucked away in Savannah’s South Historic District. This pet-friendly getaway offers two bedrooms, one full bath, and space for up to four guests, making it an ideal choice for a family trip or romantic escape.

What makes this home special?

Spacious split living: On the main level, you’ll find a cozy living room with a plush sectional and Smart TV—perfect for relaxing after a day of exploring. The eat-in kitchen is a delight for home cooks, featuring black stainless-steel appliances, granite countertops, and everything you need to prepare meals with ease. Enjoy the convenience of a shared washer and dryer available on site during your stay.

Walkable location: Step outside and immerse yourself in the rich history and vibrant culture of Savannah’s South Historic District. Wander cobblestone streets shaded by moss-draped oaks, explore Forsyth Park, or tour iconic landmarks like the Mercer-Williams House Museum. For a change of scenery, Tybee Island’s sandy beaches and coastal charm are just a short drive away.

Upstairs, two beautifully appointed bedrooms each feature a queen bed and Smart TV, with convenient shared access to the luxurious full bathroom.

Whether you’re meandering through historic squares or basking in the sea breeze, Secret Savannah Sanctuary A is the perfect home base for your Savannah adventure!

Sleeping Configuration:
Bedroom 1: Queen Bed
Bedroom 2: Queen Bed

Parking: Non-metered Parking
This unit has free, on-street parking right outside the front door. Availability will depend on time of day and season. Our guests have not reported trouble parking near the home, even on the busiest of weekends.

We can welcome your dog companions at this pet friendly home, but ask that you find other arrangements for cats, birds, and other furry friends. There is a $130 pet fee (non-refundable) with a 35 lb. maximum weight limit per dog and a limit of 2 dogs per reservation.

Other things to note
AGE RESTRICTION: All renters must be over the age of 25, unless it is a child accompanied by a parent. If a guest is found to have falsified their reservation in any way we reserve the right to evict the guest, cancel their reservation and retain ALL DEPOSITS PAID TO THAT POINT.
