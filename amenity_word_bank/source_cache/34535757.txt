=== FULL DESCRIPTION ===
Located only three blocks from Forsyth Park, this eclectic row-home is an ideal spot for those looking to be in Savannah's quieter residential area of Downtown Savannah.

The space
This bright and spacious three-bedroom home is located on Habersham Street and is nestled amongst the tree-lined residential area of Savannah. Just East of Forsyth Park, Savannah’s largest green space. This spacious home has been updated to suit the needs of the modern traveler, however built in 1900, The Queen Anne-style architectural home was one of a number of popular architectural styles to emerge during the Victorian era. 

Enter into the foyer where you'll be greeted by tall ceilings and framed artwork. Just off the foyer you will enter the formal living room facing the front of the home. Adjacent you'll find the dining room, with seating for four Lucky guests and a bar-cart, perfect for evening cocktails! The kitchen offers stainless steel appliances and everything you'd need to cook a delicious meal. Off of the kitchen is your private courtyard.

Located on the second floor of the home you will find 3 bedrooms, and 2 full bathrooms. The primary bedroom offers a queen size bed, with ensuite bathroom. Just down the hall you'll find a room with Queen bed and Queen sleeper sofa. In the front of the home, a third bedroom with Twin bed.

Just a short walking distance to the heart of downtown, this home is ideal for travelers looking to stay in a tree-lined, quieter residential part of town. Head West and dine at some of the city's best restaurants, like 700 Drayton, Fox & Fig, and Hitch. Due North toward Broughton Street, guests can shop til they drop inside various local retailers, like The Paris Market & Brocante, 24e, and shopSCAD to name a few. Guests are about a 15-20 minute walk to the River front, and a quick 8 minute walk to Forsyth Park.

We can welcome your dog companions at this pet friendly home, but ask that you find other arrangements for cats, birds, and other furry friends. There is a $130 pet fee (non-refundable) with a 35 lb. maximum weight limit per dog and a limit of 2 dogs per reservation. Some homes may allow larger dogs, please inquire for more information.

Non-metered Parking
This unit has free, on-street parking right outside the front door. Availability will depend on time of day and season. Our guests have not reported trouble parking near the home, even on the busiest of weekends.
