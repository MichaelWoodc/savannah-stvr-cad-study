=== FULL DESCRIPTION ===
Charming two bedroom, one bath Carriage House filled with local art and walking distance to all of Savannah's Historic District attractions.

The space
Our charming upstairs carriage house is new construction and has two bedrooms, kitchen living area, and a full bath. Features include hardwood floors, high ceilings, gourmet kitchen, and charming décor featuring antique furnishings and artwork by local artists. Enjoy Savannah's amazing weather on the balcony. The kitchen offers stainless appliances, stone countertops, seating for six, and everything you need to cook a meal for family and friends. Each bedroom offers a queen size bed and beautiful antique furnishings. A queen size sleeper sofa with memory foam mattress is in the living area. 

ALSO AT THIS LOCATION: 
Mimosa House 
Fernwood Cottage 
The Mimosa House can be combined with the two Carriage Houses to create a 5 or 7 bedroom property that can accommodate up to 14 people. Contact us with any questions or to check availability of all three units.

Parking
One compact garage parking space available. Additionally, ample unmetered street parking is conveniently available within a stone's throw from the carriage house.

Guest access
Guests have sole access to the whole carriage house and a balcony overlooking the fenced in garden.

Neighborhood highlights
We are in a wonderful area of mainly historical residential houses mixed with a few businesses, including a large grocery store on the next block! Located one block from the twenty-plus acre Forsyth Park with playgrounds, tennis and basketball courts, and many year round festivals including the start of Savannah's renowned St. Patrick's Day Parade.

The main house was built in the 1890s. Our quaint street is safe with light foot traffic and friendly neighbors. We have several tasty restaurants within a few blocks and a large grocery store right around the corner. A number of coffee shops, bakeries and a health food stores are all within 1-5 blocks too.
