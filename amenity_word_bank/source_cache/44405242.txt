=== FULL DESCRIPTION ===
This stunning Victorian just down the block from the beautiful Whitefield Square dates back to 1888. This is revealed by its gorgeous original hardwood floors, high ceilings, pronounced trim, and unique fireplaces. When you arrive you are immediately drawn in by the warm and inviting interior, professionally designed by Robin Michie. This downtown home right in the heart of Savannah's Historic District is within walking distance of everything!
SVR-02177

The space
Very generous in size, the house has plenty of living space inside and out. We offer a master king room with walk-in tile shower and 2 queen rooms with a shared hall bath. Each room has plenty of closet space. There is a screened-in-porch on the east side of the building and a huge outdoor rear patio equipped with a propane grill and plenty of seating room. We have a fully equipped kitchen with lots of counter top space perfect for those who like to cook. The main living room has plenty of space to relax and unwind in. There is a large smart TV with a booming soundbar if you're here while your favorite sports team is playing! If you are one of the unfortunate people who has work to do while on vacation, do not fear, we have a great WiFi connection. There are also plenty of wine glasses in the dining room kitchen for when you're done. We also offer a full washer/dryer (free) in the laundry room by the kitchen.

Guest access
You are welcome to use every inch of the house. There is a utility/housekeeping closet by the kitchen that is locked up and off-limits, but other than that it is all yours. Please be gentle with our furniture as the owners have put a lot of time and energy into making this place somewhere you can call your home away from home. Again, there is a full laundry room with a washer/dryer should you need it while you are here.

Other things to note
Please be respectful of your neighbors and keep noise/volume down outside in the patio after 10pm. We share the City parking area so just try to be courteous when parking and leave enough space for the neighbors.
