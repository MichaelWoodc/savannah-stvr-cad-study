=== FULL DESCRIPTION ===
Featuring a bevy of hotel style amenities with the unparalleled privacy and comfort of a private home, our vacation rentals Savannah GA will take your vacation to the next level. Larger parties will especially love our estate-style Savannah vacation rentals that feature plenty of sleeping accommodations, full-size kitchens and more. One such property is the Change of Place Estate off of West Henry Street, which is not far from the Savannah Victorian Historic District.

The space
Relax in Style with Savannah Vacation Homes 



This recently renovated property has two parts in the main house and garden level. The main house features a king bedroom with a private bathroom, a queen bedroom with a shared bathroom, plus a sitting room with sleeping arrangements for four guests and a shared bathroom. The living room and dining room both have seating for 10 people. There are four flatscreen TVs located throughout as well as a washer and dryer. The house features wireless internet and cable with HD channels for your convenience. Parking is available for one vehicle off-street, and then there is on-street parking for more vehicles. Make meals in-house with the gourmet kitchen, and stay comfortable with central AC and ceiling fans throughout. The garden level is similarly appointed with a king bedroom, one bathroom, a living room with a sofa bed, gourmet kitchen, dining room with seating for two, plus a washer and dryer. In total, the estate sleeps 12 guests and has four bedrooms and four bathrooms. 



Our vacation rentals Savannah GA are always amazingly outfitted with creature comforts and necessities. The Change of Place Estate features a classicly elegant decor theme with neutral colors and a combination of modern and nostalgic furnishings. The gourmet kitchen is expansive with polished wood cabinets and smooth marble countertops. It includes an extra large sink, plenty of food prep area and then an island with a range. If you love to cook, you won't want to leave this kitchen. The formal dining room is sure to upgrade your meal times with a decorative fireplace, long dining table and a curio hutch in the corner. Bedrooms are also individually decorated with their own design schemes. 



Getting Around Downtown From Vacation Rentals Savannah GA 



When you're ready to leave the comfortable confines of our Savannah vacation rentals, the entirety of the historic downtown is at your fingertips. The best way to enjoy Savannah is on foot, but you can also take the Old Town Trolley or Chatham Area Transit around the Historic District. The location of this particular property is just to the south of Forsyth Park, which is easily one of the biggest draws in Savannah. Head north to find the south end of the park and once inside, you'll find the Fragrant Garden, visitor center and fountain more towards the central-north part of the park. You'll also find a number of other popular spots to the north of the park, including the Mercer-Williams House, Calhoun Square, the Massie Heritage Center and the Ralph Mark Gilbert Civil Rights Museum. At the very north end of the Savannah Historic District is River Street. This long concourse along the Savannah River boasts a variety of places to eat and shop. 



Explore Nearby Tybee Island From Savannah Vacation Rentals 



Our Savannah vacation homes guests often enjoy taking a break from exploring downtown Savannah to take a trip over to Tybee Island. This little barrier island is big on beach charm. Enjoy warm Atlantic beaches and sumptuous waterfront dining for a relaxing day trip. The island also features plenty of shopping opportunities, from gift shops to specialty boutiques. The top attractions on Tybee include the Tybee Lighthouse on the north end and the Marine Science Center on the south end. There are also numerous opportunities for active guests, including biking around the island, water activities, fishing and walking. Civil War buffs will enjoy exploring Fort Screven and nearby Fort Pulaski. Tybee Island also has its share of special events and some of the favorites include: 



• Mardi Gras Tybee in February 

• Tybee Irish Heritage Celebration Parade in March 

• Fourth of July Fireworks on the Beach in July 

• Tybee Pirate Fest in October 

• Tybee Island New Year's Eve Fireworks in December
