=== FULL DESCRIPTION ===
Stay just one block from Forsyth Park in a brand-new, thoughtfully designed 2-bedroom apartment in Savannah's Victorian District. Completed in August of 2026, the former property was rebuilt from the ground up. Same longtime host, same fantastic location, and an entirely new stay.  Apartment A combines the walkability of one of Savannah's favorite neighborhoods with the comfort of a new space.

SVR-02345

The space
Welcome to Unit A —a brand-new, second-floor apartment designed to be an easy, comfortable home base for experiencing Savannah.

SLEEP
Two private bedrooms feature:

Queen-size beds
Blackout curtains
Ceiling Fans
Private closet
45" television

LIVING + DINING
The open living area includes comfortable seating, a 55" television and dining space for four. Large windows overlook the neighborhood and make the apartment feel bright and airy.

The living room features a full-size sleeper sofa, giving you extra flexibility for relaxing or accommodating different sleeping arrangements within your group.

KITCHEN
The kitchen includes a full-size refrigerator, dishwasher, microwave, two-burner electric cooktop, Keurig coffee maker, cookware, dishes and everyday essentials—everything you need for morning coffee, takeout nights or preparing a simple meal at home.

BATH
The modern bathroom features marble flooring, a spacious walk-in shower and built-in shelving with fresh towels and bath essentials.

NEIGHBORHOOD

Located in the Victorian District just one block from Forsyth Park, the apartment puts you between Savannah’s famous Historic District and the restaurants, coffee shops and creative energy of the Starland area. Step outside and explore on foot, or catch the nearby free DOT shuttle for an easy ride through downtown.

You’ll have the comforts of a completely new space while staying in one of Savannah’s most character-filled, walkable neighborhoods.

Guest access
Enjoy easy, contactless check-in. An entry code will be sent to you the morning of your arrival and you are welcome to check in anytime after 3:00 pm. 

Please note, the unit is located on the second floor of the building and we do not have an elevator.

Other things to note
Parking at this location is free, unmetered street parking.  There is no designated parking space.  Please be aware of the street sweeping signs!
