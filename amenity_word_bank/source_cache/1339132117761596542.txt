=== FULL DESCRIPTION ===
9 Bedrooms, 7 bathrooms next to Oglethorpe Square. You won't find a more walkable location in Savannah's Historic District. Two fully stocked kitchens await you along with four king size beds, all ensuite. This home is perfect for large families or small groups traveling together. No car is needed on E York St.

The space
Welcome to The Merchant on York St, a grand 9-bedroom, 7-bathroom luxury home nestled in the heart of Savannah’s Historic District. This extraordinary property, once a single-family estate, has been carefully transformed into two beautifully appointed apartments that retain the charm and elegance of the 1853 building. Named after its original owner, Captain Henry James Dickerson, a pioneering stevedore and prominent local figure, the home stands as a testament to Savannah’s rich maritime history.

With its spacious layout, this home offers an ideal retreat for large groups or families looking for both comfort and sophistication. The home features stunning period architecture, with high ceilings, intricate woodwork, and large windows that fill the space with natural light. Its seven luxurious bathrooms and nine well-appointed bedrooms provide ample space for guests to relax and unwind. There are 5 king bedrooms, all ensuite, and 4 queen bedrooms.

Located on East York Street, the home is perfectly positioned to take full advantage of Savannah’s charm. It’s just steps away from vibrant shops, historic landmarks, and world-class dining, and only a short stroll from the iconic St. Patrick’s Day parade route. The city’s walkable streets make it easy to explore all that this beautiful southern city has to offer, while paid parking is conveniently accessible for guests arriving by car.

Whether you're here for a special event, a family reunion, or simply a luxurious getaway, The Merchant on York St promises a memorable and opulent experience in the heart of Savannah’s Historic District.
