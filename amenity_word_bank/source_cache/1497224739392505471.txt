=== FULL DESCRIPTION ===
Set just off historic Lafayette Square, this stunning first-floor flat is perfect for your Savannah stay. Located in the heart of the Savannah Historic District, you're just a short stroll from the Cathedral of St. John the Baptist, Forsyth Park, River Street, and many of the city's best restaurants, shops, and landmarks.

This beautiful home is filled with charm and character, from ornate architectural details to cozy corners bathed in sunlight, the space is designed to make you feel at home.

The space
This elegant first-floor flat blends historic Savannah charm with modern comforts. Featuring a sunlit bay window, ornate columns, and a grand fireplace, the open living space is perfect for relaxing after a day of exploring. The fully equipped kitchen boasts custom cream cabinetry, stainless steel appliances, and a spacious island. With three comfortable bedrooms (1 king, 2 queen, 1 sleeper) and two classic bathrooms, the home comfortably accommodates up to six guests. Located just off Lafayette Square in the heart of the Historic District, you’ll be steps from Savannah’s most iconic landmarks.

Guest access
Guests have a private access and will receive a key code prior to arrival to enter the home. This keycode is changed after every guest for your safety.
