=== FULL DESCRIPTION ===
Step into Parlor on Lafayette and immediately feel at home. This charming two-bedroom vacation rental is situated in a wonderful location to walk everywhere in the Savannah Historic District. As soon as you enter through the front door, you step into a beautiful living space accented with gleaming hardwood floors and designer furnishings.

The space
Welcome to the Parlor on Lafayette! Here you'll discover a gorgeous, well-appointed vacation rental that offers designer furniture and hardwood floors. Between each set of guests, we have the rental fully sanitized and cleaned. Therefore, your trip will start off on the right foot. You'll be able to begin relaxing the second you arrive.



Picture Yourself Staying in Luxury



As one of the top Savannah vacation rentals, the Parlor on Lafayette is nestled between the Historic District - North and the Historic District - South. Simply walk outside and you'll discover several things to do within walking distance. For instance, you can check out the Andrew Low House, which is the historic home of the Girl Scouts Founder. Additionally, you can hang out with ghosts at the reportedly haunted Mercer-Williams House Museum or check out The Cathedral Basilica of St. John the Baptist.



Once you head back indoors, you'll find a sophisticated, classic home with every modern convenience you could need. Unwind on your bed as you watch a large screen TV or sit in one of the elegant side chairs and read your favorite book. You can also take in some history from within your rental home, which contains several framed prints of what the city used to look like.



Parlor on Lafayette



As far as Savannah vacation homes go, the Parlor on Lafayette has some of the best amenities available! In fact, there's one major thing that sets it apart from many similar rentals, and that's the free parking space for one vehicle. The parking space is located off the alleyway, and larger vehicles may have difficulty making the turn into the spaces. Mid-size and smaller cars are recommended for easier access.



You'll get a lot of other things out of your home base, too. For example, you can use the included washer and dryer to keep your clothes fresh throughout your stay. Whenever you feel like swimming, you're welcome to use our heated pool on Tybee Island. Safety really matters to us, so be sure to engage your deadbolt lock and enjoy the peace of mind that outdoor lighting can provide.



In the morning, take some time for yourself to enjoy a cup of coffee and freshly toasted bread. Once you're fully awake, it'll be time to go explore the local neighborhood. All you need to do is simply walk outside to find history dating back to 1733. At the same time, you'll also meet the hustle and bustle of a town with more than 147,000 residents.



Get to Know the Neighborhood



When you start to feel hungry, go into the neighborhood for several unique choices. Fox and Fig Cafe serves delicious vegan food, Clary's Cafe is a top-notch local diner and the Yaupon Teahouse + Apothecary is a great place to get a cup of tea. Other nearby choices include Mirabelle Savannah for coffee and the American-themed Gryphon Restaurant.



Once you're satiated, it's time to go shopping! E Shaver Booksellers is a wonderful place to begin. You'll definitely want to add it to your list if you love cats because there are several that call the bookstore home. Next, there's the ShopSCAD Savannah gift shop where you can grab souvenirs for your friends back home. If you fail to see something that catches your eye at ShopSCAD Savannah, be sure to visit the One-Fish Two-Fish gift shop. You can also spend your day antiquing at shops such as Arthur Smith Antiques and V&J Duncan Antique Maps.



Want to learn even more? Visit Tour with the Teacher to sign up for a fact-filled tour of the Savannah Historic District.



Vacation Rentals Savannah GA



No matter what grabs your attention, you're sure to need a relaxing environment to unwind after. Head back to the Parlor on Lafayette, turn on the air conditioning and take it easy.



Alternatively, if you find yourself itching to get some more history, you can go two doors down to the Flannery O'Connor Childhood Home. This unusual museum's name may sound familiar, and that's due to the fact that Flannery O'Connor won the O. Henry Award three times. Inside its walls, you can enjoy perusing the rare books at The Bruckheimer Library.



There's also lots of special events that take place in the area, including the Savannah Music Festival, Club Car Championship at the Landings Club, Annual Nogs Hidden Garden Tour and the Publix Women's Half Marathon & 5K. As you can see, Savannah is a town that likes to stay busy!



Savannah Vacation Rentals Make Seeing the Town a Breeze



Savannah vacation homes provide the best way to see the town. Not only will you be in the heart of the action but you'll have all the comforts of home. With two bedrooms and space for four occupants, the Parlor on Lafayette has to be seen to be believed! Unlike mass produced hotel rooms, it provides a truly Savannah, GA, experience.
