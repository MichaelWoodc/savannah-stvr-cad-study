=== FULL DESCRIPTION ===
Beautifully renovated and designed by the world-famous Lisa Garriss of Plum West Designs, this contemporary condo provides the perfect stay for four guests. Featuring two beds and two baths, you will enjoy comfort and style throughout, all with that modern flare you desire while traveling. Located on the edge of the historic district south, you will be right in the heart of everything Savannah offers. Three minutes to Forsyth Park. 18 min to SAV International. 

SVR-02780

The space
Experience the charm of Historic Savannah and the luxury of a modernized vacation home. 
This space is located on one of the twenty-two historic squares. Located just off of the famed Jones Street, it shares a corner with one of Downtown Savannah’s iconic homes. 
The two-story interior has been completely renovated and fashionably styled by an international design firm. 
Walk down the entryway and find the dining and kitchen area with panoramic views of the square, allowing in the glow of natural sunlight. Draw the custom window shades to allow for a more private dining experience. 
This kitchen, adorned with butcher block countertops and glossy white backsplash, is ideal for creating home-cooked meals. You’ll find an array of instruments at your fingertips, including a premium knife set and a display of pots, pans, and cooking utensils. Sleek appliances like the oven, dishwasher, toaster, and microwave will make your culinary experience effortless. 
Gather at the airy dining space with four, plush chairs. There is a collection of plates, bowls, and glassware to choose from, including stemmed wine and champagne glasses 
Take note of the Ninja Coffee Bar that allows you to choose the desired richness of your brew. Coffee is included in your stay. The addition of an electric kettle is there for those who prefer tea. 
Regardless of your preferred drink, you can enjoy it in the chic sitting room and or enjoy the surrounding display of local artwork. 
The living room welcomes you with a soft, spacious couch and coffee table crafted with solid wood. A large Smart T.V. allows you to stream your favorite shows and movies on any platform. 
Along with a contemporary powder room, the first floor of this home is complete with a private balcony holding an intimate, al fresco dining set. 
Make your way to the second story. On one end of the hallway, you have the first bedroom with gold accents, floating nightstands, and a floor-length mirror. The second bedroom features views of sunsets that match the pink throw pillows. In either bedroom, you’ll have your own queen-sized bed with a set of crisp linens, a closet, and a large dresser. A washer and dryer is provided on the first floor for your convenience. 
In the hall, you’ll find another bathroom lavished with fresh towels, shampoo, and bodywash. The spacious vanity is well-lit and has a provided blow dryer. The shower is enhanced with a
sliding glass door, refreshing white tile, and a rainfall showerhead.

Guest access
The entire condo is for your use! We have a balcony off of the back of the living room that you can sit and enjoy a morning coffee or before-dinner cocktail ;)

Parking is street-only and FREE.

Neighborhood highlights
This property is located on a canopy-covered, residential street that is expertly hidden from tourist crowds while remaining steps from Savannah’s most sought-after attractions. Plus, this street has free parking - a rare commodity downtown.
Not looking to venture far? From the porch you can see Fox and Fig, a trendy cafe serving gorgeous, colorful dishes and espresso drinks all day long. On your way, you’ll pass through Troup Square, home to the Armillary Sphere and one of the must-see Savannah churches.
Hop onto Jones Street, said to be the most beautiful street in the United States, and stroll a couple blocks to find Clary’s Cafe. This diner-style restaurant is famous for being featured in the film, Midnight in the Garden of Good and Evil.
From there, you can turn left and find Forsyth Park. This is Savannah’s oldest and largest park with enough grassy space for hours of fun. Take a picture with the iconic fountain and trees draped in Spanish moss.
Or turning right, you can explore the endless variety of restaurants, cafes, and shopping. A trip downtown is not complete until you’ve dined at one of the many excellent southern-inspired restaurants such as Hitch, Mrs. Wilkes Dining Room, The Olde House, and Vic’s On the River.
If you are looking for some nightlife, stop into McDonough’s Restaurant and Lounge for karaoke or drift to River Street for rooftop bars and views of the passing ships.
Take in all the history of Savannah by stopping in one of the many museums. The Beach Institute of African American Culture center, Mercer-William’s House Museum, and the Telfair Museums are just minutes away. Looking to explore Savannah’s art scene? Stop into the SCAD Museum of Art.
Want to take it all in at once while riding or biking? We are just minutes away from Old Town Trolley Tours of Savannah and Savannah Bike Tours.
Getting around is easy with walkable streets, bike rentals, and uber service. Jump on Savannah’s public transit system with the Price & Jones stop just a 3-minute walk from the condo. Try out the Savannah Belles Ferries to see the beautiful skyline of Savannah from the riverfront.
