Your Savannah Itinerary



Historic Savannah is just around the corner when you stay at Crawford Coastal. Centrally located as one the district’s 22 squares, Crawford dates to 1841. It is named after William Harris Crawford, who acted as Secretary of the Treasury in the early 19th century.



Crawford Square is small in area compared to other squares, but it is distinctive in a number of ways. It is the only square still bordered with a fence, although all were fenced in the distant past. It boasts a lovely gazebo perfect for picnicking and live music events. It also has a basketball court and a children’s playground. It’s a great destination for a stroll around the neighborhood, a bit of recreation or some relaxing alone time.



On an interesting historical note, Crawford Square was the only integrated place where African Americans were welcome during the South’s Jim Crow era. It also retains a cistern, which hearkens back to firefighting’s early days. All the 22 town squares once had one, but only Crawford’s remains.



Crawford Coastal is a comfortable walk from Colonial Park Cemetery, which has been a city park since the late 1890s. At that point, no new burials had occurred in the cemetery for several decades. For that reason, it does not include any Confederate soldiers, but has around 700 victims of the yellow fever outbreak of the 1820s.



Colonial Park Cemetery is an important stop on several Savannah ghost tours. If you like a good scare, consider signing up. You’ll learn more about the haunted history of the place.



Your accommodation is also within walking distance of other historic sites, making it easy to take a self-guided tour of the neighborhood. Some points of interest for Savannah vacation rentals guests include:

The Cathedral of St. John the Baptist

Andrew Lowe House, site of the founding of the Girl Scouts

Sorrel Weed House Museum, another stop on many haunted tours

Juliet Gordon Lowe’s birthplace, the founder of the Girl Scouts

Owens-Thomas House with slave quarters

Davenport House Museum

Webb Military Museum



Crawford Coastal is also convenient to many of the city’s best-loved restaurants. For example, the 17Hundred90 restaurant offers upscale fare at 307 E. President St., just north of Crawford Coastal. Cha Bella, on Broughton St., specializes in farm-to-table dishes. Collins Quarter at 151 Bull St. serves up a popular brunch and dinner menu.
