=== FULL DESCRIPTION ===
Stay steps from Forsyth Park in this charming upstairs 2BR/1BA Savannah apartment. With one king bedroom and one queen bedroom, this historic duplex unit is perfect for couples, friends, or a small family. Walk to shops, restaurants, bars, historic squares, the Forsyth Fountain, and Savannah’s favorite attractions from this unbeatable location.

The space
The home is a top/down duplex, and this listing covers the rooms in the upper-level apartment. The larger bedroom features a king-sized bed, while the other bedroom has a queen-sized bed. 

There is one full bathroom. 

The cozy kitchen is equipped with all the essential appliances for your stay. The spacious living room features a smart TV and comfortable seating for up to eight guests. For your convenience, a washer and dryer are also provided. The walls are adorned with the owner’s original artwork, and the windows offer charming views of the streets in Savannah’s historic district.

Enjoy the convenience of free off-street parking, then unwind in the double courtyard adorned with string lights—the perfect setting for morning coffee or evening cocktails. Whether you’re visiting for a weekend getaway or an extended stay, you’ll feel right at home.

Guest access
The guest has full access to the upper unit.

Other things to note
Hot Tub – Please note that the patio area is shared between both units. Each unit has its own private hot tub for guest use; however, the patio itself is a shared outdoor space.

Parking is on the side of the house (Huntingdon St.) There is enough space for 2 cars, take only 1 space.

There is an outdoor security camera in the parking area.

Neighborhood highlights
Savanah is the oldest city in Georgia. It was designed around 26 squares, the only city of its kind. Each square has its own name and history.

The South Historic District of Savannah, GA is one of the oldest neighborhoods in the city. This traditional neighborhood is located in the heart of downtown Savannah and is home to a variety of architecture, some of which dates back to the early 1800s. Many of the original Georgian and Federal-style homes are still standing, and they are a great representation of the city’s historic past. Additionally, the district is known for its grand, cobblestone streets, which are perfect for walking or biking, as well as its many parks and squares. With its unique character and charm, the South Historic District is a wonderful area to experience and explore Savannah’s amazing history.
