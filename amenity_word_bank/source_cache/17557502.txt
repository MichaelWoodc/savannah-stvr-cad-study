=== FULL DESCRIPTION ===
Sink into the inky velvet sofa with a coffee at this elegant, spacious home with four original fireplaces (non-working), hardwood floors, white paneling, and intricate wainscoting. Lounge on the bedroom day bed off the master bedroom, or gather for dinner under the chandelier. This Historic District townhouse (Hostess Nest) built in 1899 also has a convenient downstairs bedroom with an en-suite bathroom and 2 Off-street parking spaces. Close to everything in downtown Savannah!!

SVR00702

The space
This is a unique floor plan with:
A downstairs bedroom with an en-suite bathroom. 
An upstairs master bedroom that has a sitting room with a daybed (twin) for one additional guest for a total of 7 guests. 
Large updated kitchen. 
An upstairs laundry room with a sink.
A private fenced courtyard.
2 Off-street parking spaces which is a rarity for vacation rentals in Savannah!! 
Excellent location, walk to everything Downtown has to offer!  

“Hostess Nest ".....We truly meant it when we named our home, our goal is to receive and welcome you, and you make it a home away from home.  From the minute you check-in, we expect you to relax and indulge in the great hospitality Savannah has to offer. Our home has a KING bed in upstairs master bedroom, Queen bed in the secondary bedrooms (one upstairs and the other downstairs), daybed(twin) in the master sitting room, sleeps up to 7. 

BEDROOMS
Unwind after a day exploring Savannah in the spacious master bedroom which offers an en-suite bathroom. Submerge into comfort at night on the comfortable KING size bed and rise into your next day of adventure and play.  If movie in bed is your thing, a TV is available for your convenience.  A large closet is available to hang and store all your belongings. The master bathroom features a walk in shower and premium hotel collection towels and toiletries.
The 2 secondary bedrooms (one on the main level and the other on the second level) are also decorated elegantly which truly engages your visual senses.  Sleep comfortably with ultra-plush beddings. Movie in bed, enjoy yet another TV in each bedroom! The secondary bathroom on the second floor is conveniently located across the hall from the bedroom while the bedroom downstairs has an en-suite bathroom, both have a tub/shower combination and of course, some more premium grade towels and toiletries.
Lastly, the master bedroom sitting (AKA a half bedroom) has an antique gray Indonesian Daybed with an architectural backrest and beautiful curved arms, perfect space for one additional guest.

LIVING ROOM/DINING AREA
Inside the living room, modern touches mixed with vintage charms provide every necessity for gathering and relaxation including a wall mounted 42" smart TV. There is plenty of comfortable seating and fine décor.
The full dining room is large enough to comfortably seat 7 people for a festive dinner and is located off the kitchen.

KITCHEN
The kitchen is truly a chef's dream with all new small appliances. Everything is provided......coffeemaker, toaster, blender, kettle and the list goes on and on. We even provide the Tupperware for your yummy leftovers.  You need to bring absolutely nothing but your food to this fully stocked kitchen.

PRIVATE COURTYARD
The fenced and private courtyard is a perfect spot with a table to seat 6; to enjoy with friends and family, dining, or just relaxing.  

LAUNDRY
Need to wash clothes during your stay or right before heading home? That's covered too with the in-house laundry room with a washer and dryer. Iron and ironing board also provided for your convenience.

Guest access
Guest have access to the entire space in our house.

Other things to note
**Please be mindful of the City of Savannah Noise Ordinance. As you are staying in a residential area, we would like to remind you to observe neighborhood quiet times between the hours of 9PM – 8AM. Remember that sound carries very well throughout Savannah, most especially in courtyards, porches and patios. Our next door neighbors are full time residents and we want to respect them. Please try to avoid a situation where your vacation is interrupted or cut short because of a noise compliant by the neighbors to the police**

Our house was built in 1899, which makes it historic and unique in several ways. Those imperfections AKA “old charm” are the reasons we fell in love with the house. It is NOT a new house even after renovations, we were very mindful in keeping the character and soul of the house intact to match up with the city. We hope you enjoy!
