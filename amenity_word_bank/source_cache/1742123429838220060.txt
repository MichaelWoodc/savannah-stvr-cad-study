=== FULL DESCRIPTION ===
Gordon Studio is a bright and inviting second‑floor carriage house offering comfortable open studio living in Savannah’s National Historic Landmark District. This thoughtfully restored space blends historic character with modern comfort, all within steps of the city’s most iconic green spaces.

The space
Studio Layout and Interior Details
- Open‑concept studio with original hardwood floors  
- Skylight positioned over the bed, filling the space with soft natural light during the day and fitted with louvered shutters for when you want to sleep in 
- Historic windows throughout, each fitted with interior louvered shutters  
- Private exterior staircase entrance  
- Fast wifi
- 50" smart TV
- Convenient fold out desk 
-Washer and dryer conveniently located downstairs from the carriage house

Newly Remodeled Kitchen
- Stainless steel appliances  
- Full‑size refrigerator, oven, stovetop, and microwave  
- Updated cabinetry and counters  
- Suitable for extended stays or guests who enjoy cooking and want to have all of the basics on hand

Shared Courtyard 
Guests enjoy access to a fully enclosed courtyard.
- Outdoor dining table with seating  
- Historic brick surroundings and greenery  
- A peaceful spot for morning coffee or relaxed evenings outdoors.

Prime Walkable Location
- 1‑minute walk to Taylor Square  
- 2‑minute walk to the northeast corner of Forsyth Park  
- 5‑minute walk to the nearest north–south DOT Fare Free Shuttle stop, providing easy access to the riverfront, City Market, Broughton Street, downtown museums, and the Starland District  
- 10‑minute walk to the nearest east–west DOT Fare Free Shuttle stop for convenient access to the Savannah Visitor’s Center, Coastal Heritage Society museum sites, and Eastern Wharf  

Perfect For
Solo travelers, couples, business visitors, and anyone seeking a peaceful, walkable home base in Savannah’s National Landmark Historic District.

SVR-03292

Guest access
Guests have access to all spaces within the studio apartment and use of the shared courtyard.  Access to the property is through the garage and the washer and dryer for  studio guests is in the garage, but you will not have use of the garage for parking.
