=== FULL DESCRIPTION ===
Welcome to our whimsical top-floor condo overlooking Bay St! This spacious 1BR/1BA retreat in an 1857 building that feels like you've been pulled from the pages of a fairytale! Enjoy the romantic views of the massive live oaks below sprinkled with Spanish moss, a fully equipped kitchen, and a comfy and fresh living room (with pull-out sofa for an extra guest!). The huge bedroom features a luxurious king bed. This space will be your home base for a most memorable trip to Savannah!

SVR-02997

The space
Welcome to our enchanting condo, a whimsical top-floor hideaway located in the heart of downtown historic Savannah in an architectural gem...built in 1857! This bright and light-filled retreat offers sweeping views of Bay Street through its floor-to-ceiling windows, immersing you in the vibrant atmosphere of the city and making it feel like you've stepped into a fairytale.

The living room is designed for comfort and relaxation, featuring a cozy yet stylish design that exudes charm. The sofa pulls out into a queen-sized bed, perfect for accommodating an extra guest. Thoughtful touches throughout the space create a welcoming ambiance. Enjoy your favorite shows or movies on the flat-screen TV with streaming capabilities, available in both the living room and the bedroom. Stay connected with fast WiFi throughout the condo.

The bedroom is a tranquil haven with a luxurious king-sized bed and a spacious closet for all your storage needs. The full bathroom boasts a spacious sink vanity and a large, walk-in shower, creating a spa-like experience that invites you to unwind and rejuvenate.

The kitchen is equipped with all modern conveniences and amenities, ensuring you have everything you need for a perfect vacation. It is fully stocked and includes a Keurig coffee maker with complimentary K-cups, creamers, and sugar. The eat-in counter with seating for four makes dining in convenient and enjoyable.

Original features, including beautiful heart pine floors and the incredible architecture blended with modern upgrades, add character and comfort to this exceptional space. For your convenience, an in-unit washer and dryer are available, making it easy to take care of laundry during your stay.

One parking pass for the nearby Congress Street iParkit garage, located just a few blocks away, is included with your stay, ensuring you have a hassle-free parking experience.

With its perfect location in the heart of everything, our condo offers easy access to all the best attractions, dining, and entertainment that historic Savannah has to offer. Whether you're here to explore the city's rich history, enjoy its vibrant dining scene, or simply relax and take in the views, this whimsical hideaway provides an exceptional home base for your Savannah adventure.

Guest access
The entire condo is all yours from check-in at 3pm until check-out at 10am! The condo is completely private. To access the condo, you must climb an interior staircase that is shared with one neighboring condo. At the top of the staircase, there is a small landing with two coded entries -- one for your condo and another for the neighboring condo.

Other things to note
- The best thing about our condo, in our opinion, is the location! We are in the heart of downtown Savannah central to all the amazing places that folks come to the city to visit! HOWEVER, with our incredible location comes noise! Guests should expect noise from the street below, tourists, adjoining and nearby businesses, and nearby traffic. Noise should be expected at all hours. If you don't feel as though you would be comfortable with urban, city noise throughout your stay, we do not recommend this space for your Savannah travels. 

- We include one parking pass for the nearby Congress Street iParkit garage. The garage is located three blocks away. Unfortunately, Airbnb does not provide the option to check off "off-site, free parking" in the amenities list, so we like to clarify that we do provide one pass for this nearby garage, however it is three blocks away from the condo and not "on-site." You can, however, pull up to the condo to unload your luggage while idling your vehicle, then proceed to the parking garage, to avoid having to tote your belongings.

- This condo is located up one tall flight of interior stairs. There is not an elevator or alternative means to access the space.
