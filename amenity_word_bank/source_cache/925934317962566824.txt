The space
Located in the Victorian District, this extremely charming, stand-alone 2nd story home is on a quiet lane within walking distance to Forsyth Park, the Historic District, and much more. Two Lucky guests can call Happy Times on Park Carriage their new home away from home, where they'll make memories that will last a lifetime!

Upon entering the courtyard off of the lane, you'll find a shared courtyard and a staircase that leads up to your charming 2nd story carriage house! Inside, you'll find that the space boasts with natural light, warm tones, and comfortable furnishings. The open floor plan allows guests to never feel separated, as the kitchen sits just off of the entrance, and overlooks the living room.The kitchen is fully stocked with everything you'll need, including all of the modern conveniences one could ask for.

 The bedroom features a plush King size bed, graciously sized walk-in closet, and an ensuite bathroom. The bathroom features a tub-shower combination, and a stackable washer and dryer, perfect for extended stays!

The shared courtyard is perfect for sipping sweet tea while watching the clouds roll by, while you and your guest reminisce on your favorite memories. 

Head South a few blocks and find yourself immersed in Savannah's culture - a wine bar, vegan cafe, and the Forsyth Park Farmer's Market. A few minutes' walk in the other direction gets you closer to the bustling city. Take the scenic route through downtown's beautiful squares and end at the well-known River Walk - a great place to grab a beer and people watch.

What Is Parking Like For This Home?
Parking for this home is on-street, non-metered.

We can welcome your dog companions at this pet friendly home, but ask that you find other arrangements for cats, birds, and other furry friends. There is a $130 pet fee (non-refundable) with a 35 lb. maximum weight limit per dog and a limit of 2 dogs per reservation. Some homes may allow larger dogs, please inquire for more information.
