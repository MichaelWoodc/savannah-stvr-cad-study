=== FULL DESCRIPTION ===
This absolutely stunning 1869 townhouse will transport you back in time with its quintessential Southern charm and grandeur. This Savannah beauty has been tastefully restored, mixing thoughtfully preserved original details with all the modern amenities one might need, including a dedicated parking spot! Perfect for a family getaway or a gathering with friends! Steps from magnificent Forsyth Park and walking distance to all that downtown Savannah has to offer!

SVR 02132 and 02133

The space
The entire townhouse is all yours! Two of the three bedrooms boast king sized beds and en suite bathrooms. The third bedroom has a cozy queen sized bed and can access one of the full bathrooms from the main hallway. Downstairs you will find a spacious living room, large formal dining room with impressive gold chandelier, half bathroom, and bright and sunny kitchen with modern appliances including a gas range, farmhouse sink, and all the cookware you might need for a quick breakfast or a 5 course dinner! Off the kitchen, travel down the exterior staircase to the charming courtyard garden...the perfect spot to enjoy a glass of wine or sip some sweet tea! Wifi and full cable programming? Of course, y'all! This impressive historic 1869 townhome is the epitome of Southern charm, grace, and class with all the modern conveniences a traveler could ask for!

Guest access
The entire 3-bedroom, 2.5 bathroom townhome is yours! The back courtyard is the only shared space with our tenant who lives below, in the street level garden unit.

Other things to note
Don't be afraid to reach out if you need anything!  We live super close-by and are always happy to help out, even mid-stay.

Neighborhood highlights
This beautifully restored historic home is located less than two blocks from the west side of Forsyth Park, where there is always something fun going on! From festivals, to the Saturday Farmers Market, to the NEW Collins Quarter location in the center of the park (perfect for a picnic!), to just walking the perimeter and enjoying the splendor of Savannah's picturesque live oaks and Spanish moss, we feel that we are situated in the perfect area for exploration and fun! We are also a scenic 10-15 minute walk to the downtown area (or a 2 minute Uber ride), which includes Broughton Street, River Street, City Market, and all 22 of Savannah's charmingly historic squares!
