=== FULL DESCRIPTION ===
On the 200 East block of Jones Street, this historic one bedroom home is steps away from everything including the legendary Clary's Restaurant. With a garden beside the home, to unwind after a busy day on the town.

The space
Tucked away on Historic Jones Street is a cozy, ground-floor apartment you’ll enjoy calling home during your stay in Savannah. With nothing but a key code between you and the cobblestones of “America’s prettiest street” - dubbed so by Southern Living Magazine - you’ll have all the convenience of home without having to lug around your keys.
 
The beautifully renovated kitchen has stainless steel appliances, and is fully equipped to handle everything from your morning coffee needs, to enjoying a home cooked meal. Enjoy a little “me time” curled up on the couch with a good book, or catching up on your favorite shows. When it’s time to get some shut-eye, slip between the sheets of a comfortable Queen sized bed. Come morning, you’ll be ready for a whole new day of explore Historic Savannah.
 
Start your day of exploration at Clary’s Cafe, the setting for several breakfast scenes in Midnight in the Garden of Good and Evil. Gem on Jones is just mere steps away from the Cathedral of St. John the Baptist, which has been beautifully preserved. A few blocks away at the corner of Bull Street and Liberty, you’ll find local shops featuring everything from handmade handbags and leather goods at Satchel, to Celtic crosses at Saints & Shamrocks.
 
Why Lucky Savannah Loves this Property:
-The shared courtyard makes for a picturesque spot for your morning cup of coffee or evening glass of wine.
-An office nook provides a workspace for the digital nomad.
-The walk-in closet in the bedroom provides plenty of space to unpack.

Non-metered Parking
This unit has free, on-street parking right outside the front door. Availability will depend on time of day and season. Our guests have not reported trouble parking near the home, even on the busiest of weekends.
