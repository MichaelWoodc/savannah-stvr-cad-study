=== FULL DESCRIPTION ===
Nestled in a serene location of Savannah's Historic District, this charming southern retreat offers a tranquil escape with modern comforts. With 3 bedrooms, 2.5 bathrooms, and ample porch space, this home exudes Southern living at its finest.

The space
Introducing The Blushing Belle, nestled in a serene location of Savannah's Historic District, this charming southern retreat offers a tranquil escape with modern comforts. With 3 bedrooms, 2.5 bathrooms, and ample porch space, this home exudes Southern living at its finest.

What sets The Blushing Belle apart?

- Secluded Serenity: Situated in a peaceful residential area, yet within walking distance of Savannah's vibrant attractions like trolley tour stops and eclectic dining spots, our abode provides a quiet oasis away from the downtown hustle.

- Enchanting Outdoor Haven: Immerse yourself in the beauty of Savannah's outdoors in our expansive courtyard, complete with a gas grill, corn hole set and cozy patio set. As the sun sets, bask in the warm glow of cafe lights, creating a magical ambiance for outdoor gatherings.

- Welcoming Interiors: Step inside to discover a thoughtfully furnished interior designed for comfort and convenience, ensuring a delightful stay for all guests.

Each bedroom is thoughtfully appointed for ultimate comfort and privacy. The first-floor guest room features a bunk bed with a full and twin mattress, ideal for children or solo travelers. A convenient powder room is also located on this level. Ascend to the second floor to find the primary suite boasting a spacious porch overlooking a picturesque tree-lined street. Relax in the plush queen-size bed and pamper yourself in the ensuite bathroom with a luxurious tub-shower combination and dual walk-in closets. The third bedroom, nestled at the back of the home, offers a tranquil retreat with a queen bed and a charming bay window that floods the room with natural light. Adjacent, a generously sized full bathroom with a tub-shower combo awaits.

What is parking like for this home? 
Guests will have access to two large private parking spaces directly behind the home.
