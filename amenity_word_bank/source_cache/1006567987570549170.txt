The space
Welcome to Squareside Serenity, a charming townhome nestled in the heart of Savannah's Historic District, overlooking the picturesque Greene Square. This meticulously furnished residence is designed to warmly embrace families and friends, ensuring that everyone will find comfort and joy in their stay.

Why do we love Squareside Serenity?

- Prime location, one block from Broughton Street and three blocks from the River front.
- Open and inviting floorplan that bathes in natural light, with high ceilings and beautifully refinished original wood floors. 
- Beautiful exposed brick fireplaces, a historic relic dating all the way back to 1809.
- Spacious living room with an ample amount of seating for all guests.
- Luxurious, spa-like bathrooms with spacious walk-in showers
- Well-appointed kitchen with granite countertops and all the modern conveniences you need to prepare delicious home-cooked meals. 

As you ascend the stairs to the upper level, two generously-sized bedrooms await you. Each bedroom is outfitted with a king-size bed and spacious closets to accommodate your belongings. The second full bathroom on this level mirrors the luxurious design of the downstairs bathroom, featuring another stunning walk-in shower for your convenience.

For a memorable stay in the heart of Savannah's Historic District, book your visit to Squareside Serenity, where history, comfort, and modern convenience seamlessly blend to create an unforgettable vacation experience.
