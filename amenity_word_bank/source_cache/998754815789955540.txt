=== FULL DESCRIPTION ===
Welcome to the Eleanor Loft, ideally situated on Whitaker St., North of Forsyth Park and in the heart of Savannah’s best dining, pubs, and attractions. The apartment has been beautifully remodeled from top to bottom, featuring two bedrooms and one bathroom while preserving the historical touches. Boasting original hardwood floors, custom cabinets, high-end appliances, and more, you will have everything you need while visiting Savannah—steps from Pulaski and Madison Square.
SVR-02418

The space
Located on the second floor of the beautiful Elanor apartments complex, the Eleanor Loft is a lovely and spacious retreat in the heart of the city.
Enter the apartment to a long hallway that runs front to back of the unit. Along the right side of the apartment is an open-concept chef’s kitchen and living room, while the two bedrooms and shared bathroom run along the left side of the hallway.
Stepping right into the well-appointed kitchen, you will immediately appreciate the custom cabinetry, tile work, and ample quartz countertops—new stainless-steel appliances, including a smart refrigerator cap off the beautiful kitchen. The kitchen provides everything you need to cook, including pots, pans, cooking utensils, dishware, and more. Find multiple small appliances, including a coffee maker and Keurig. Coffee provided.
Flowing directly off the kitchen is a spacious den and dining space. The den features a comfortable oversized pull-out queen sofa, two sitting chairs, a custom-tiled fireplace, and a smart TV ready to stream your favorite shows. Large windows across the open space fill the room with abundant natural light.
Across the hall, find the two bedrooms and shared bathroom. The primary suite features a new king-size mattress wrapped in fresh linens. Nightstands, a large dresser, and a closet provide ample storage space for extended stays. Find another smart TV above the custom-built fireplace.
The second bedroom provides a new queen mattress with crisp linens. High ceilings and a large window make this smaller room live large.
Finally, enjoy a perfectly renovated large bathroom with complete custom tile work, a hardwood vanity, and a low-entry shower. The bathroom has all the essentials, including toilet paper, towels, shampoo, body wash, and a hair dryer.

Guest access
The entire condo is for your use! You may use the pull-out sofa couch for kids, third-wheelers, or maybe for your snoring partner! :)
We allow one (1) dog with a max weight of 50 pounds, no cats. The one-time pet fee for one dog is $100 and must be paid separately before checking in.

Neighborhood highlights
Located just a seven-minute walk north of Forsyth Park, The Eleanor Loft puts you in the heart of Savannah. Enjoy your morning coffee from Savannah Coffee Roasters in Madison or Pulaski Square, one of Savannah’s many beautiful green spaces just steps from the apartment.
Continue to explore all of the flavors Savannah has to offer, all just minutes from the apartment. Enjoy French-inspired dishes at Circa 1875, or grab a traditional southern dish at The Lady & Sons or Mrs. Wilkes's Dining Room. Crack a few oysters at Sorry Charlie’s Oyster Bar. Breakfast is easy and delicious by checking out Treylor Park Diner or the Little Duck Diner.
Enjoy Savannah's incredible nightlife with a stop in the Crystal Beer Parlor Totally Awesome Bar, Jens’s and Friends, Moon River Brewing, and many more. Transport to London by stopping into Six Pence Pub, a British-inspired pub, or bring out your inner Irish with a stop into O’Connell’s Irish Pub.
A trip to Savannah is complete with exploring a little of its historic colonial past. Fill your schedule by checking out the Telfair Academy, a beautiful art museum, or stroll through the Webb Military Museum. Relive the prohibition area by heading to the American Prohibition Museum. Don’t forget to grab a drink at their cocktail bar to celebrate freedom. Or fancy exploring Savannah’s ocean ties? Check out the Ships of the Sea Maritime Museum.
Not enough time to walk all the sights of Savannah? Take it all in at once while riding or biking. We are just minutes from Old Town Trolley Tours of Savannah and Savannah Bike Tours. Check out the city’s skyline from the water by jumping on one of the Savannah Belle’s ferries, with multiple stops all along Savannah’s riverfront.
