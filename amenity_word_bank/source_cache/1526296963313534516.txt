Located in Savannah's beloved Historic District, you'll enjoy the perfect balance of residential charm and easy access to the city's most iconic attractions.

Take an easy 8-minute walk to Forsyth Park, where you'll find beautiful walking paths, the famous fountain, weekend farmers markets, festivals, and live music. One of the best parts of staying here is how walkable everything is. Leave the car behind and experience Savannah the way locals do—on foot, by bicycle, aboard the free DOT shuttle, or by pedicab. Most of the city's best attractions are just minutes away, making it easy to explore at your own pace.
