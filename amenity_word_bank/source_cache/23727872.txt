=== FULL DESCRIPTION ===
The Chelsea House is where Savannah meets city living, and history meets today. From the blue velvet couch, traditional antique -4 poster bed, to the Pergola outside, that is a perfect for morning coffee and that afternoon glass of wine. You’re on a Savannah Vacation at The Chelsea House. It’s a very private property in the heart of the Historic District.

Newly restored and redecorated,  it is now a Jewel Box, 5-Star, Super Host property and it’s our pleasure to serve you.

The space
The Victorian neighborhood was built to be The Beautiful part of the City. Its Park and gardens were planted with 350 varieties of flowering trees and bushes.  

It was described in the Savannah Morning News in the 1800s as  “Cottages and townhomes of moderate size which will not be too large for a small family or too small for a moderate-sized one: houses that will suit a pecuniary class of people who are not by any means wealthy, but who are in point of respectability and intelligence ranked with the best.”

The Chelsea House is newly restored and redecorated,  it is now a Jewel Box, 5-Star, Super Host property and it’s our pleasure to 'Welcome You'. 

We have always been known for our Cleanliness Reviews.  You can rest easy here!,  Miss Eva, the housekeeper, makes sure your stay is safe and enjoyable.

There is free street parking at The Chelsea.  When you arrive you are greeted by an Oak tree and a Tulip tree both are hundreds of years old.  The shade at The Chelsea makes her outdoor spaces wonderful. The property is entirely fenced which makes it the best vacation spot in Savannah if you have a  child that likes the outdoors, and or a four-legged family member can also vacation with you. 

The people, who built this to be their home, were our first Americans. Space was thought of differently then, and a whole family lived in this modest space.  Today this space is a perfect and efficient place to stay for 2 people or 2 people with a child of 4 to 10. It is easy to make food or you can concentrate on spoiling yourself touring and enjoying some of Savannah’s new great eating experiences while you are out. Or order in!

We are right on the Bike Path, you can bring your bikes or rent them.  The Park is only 2 and 1/2 blocks away, where there is a great playground for children.  A  Cafe in the Park and on Saturday mornings there is a Farmers Market.  If you love to walk, the entire historic district of Savannah is about 2 miles square - and everywhere you walk is beautiful.

Come stay with us, you are very welcome here.

Neighborhood highlights
Located in the Historic District, in the Victorian Neighborhood. The entire historic district is 2 sq miles, so you can walk to the River in about 25 minutes, and you are in the most beautiful neighborhood in Savannah.

The Historic Chelsea is wonderful for FAMILY TRAVEL. Perfect for a family of 2 adults and up to 2 children who are old enough to be comfortable in a house with stairs.

Savannah has amazing American history everywhere. Most museums have special areas for children. For outdoor play there is Forsyth Park, 3 blocks away. This majestic 30-acre park named for Georgia’s 33rd governor has it all! The city’s most famous fountain—installed in 1858 and modeled after the fountains at the Place de la Concorde in Paris—crowns the northern edge of the park, inspiring contemplation, selfies, marriage proposals, and weddings. Sometimes the stroll to the fountain is dotted with local artisans working to the sounds of a lively trumpet. On quieter days it’s just the locals out with their dogs, avid readers nestled deep in their comfy hammocks, and art students sketching their surroundings from a nearby bench.

The Playground. Thanks to the Rotary Club of Savannah, we now have a fun, all-accessible playground in Forsyth Park. The design includes nine slides, 16 climbers, and eight swings. The equipment can support a capacity of up to 300 children. The ground is covered with rubber matting and mulch. Oh & that’s just one of the two Forsyth Park playgrounds. Yes, two. The second one for older kids is also popular (just not newly redone) and includes swings, climbing walls, slides. The two Forsyth Park playgrounds include restrooms, café, spray pool, paved sidewalks for walking, and biking. Pack a lunch (or eat at the café) & spend the day in this landmark, historic park. Check out the gated fragrant garden for the blind if it’s unlocked & the park fountains. Kids can dance to their heart’s content on the bandshell’s canopied stage.
