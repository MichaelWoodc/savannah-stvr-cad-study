=== FULL DESCRIPTION ===
Embark on a journey through time at this Savannah vacation rental! Just around the corner from Leopold's Ice Cream shop, this 1-bedroom, 1-bathroom condo is perfect for couples and solo travelers with a love of history. With a charming balcony, modern decor, and a well-equipped kitchen, the unit will make you feel at home the moment you walk in. Spend time at Oglethorpe Square or head to City Market - Savannah's 18th-century open-air market chock full of shops, restaurants, and art galleries!
