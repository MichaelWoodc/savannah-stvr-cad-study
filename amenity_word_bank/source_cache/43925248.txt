=== FULL DESCRIPTION ===
Southern Comfort Manor - Experience a true retreat located on the charming, historic side of Huntingdon Street in Savannah. Up to eight Lucky guests will call this bright and airy three bedroom, two-story home, "home sweet home".

The space
Experience a true retreat located on the charming, historic side of Huntingdon Street in Savannah. Up to eight Lucky guests will call this bright and airy three bedroom, two-story home, "home sweet home". On the first floor, the large, modern living room has two French doors that let in plenty of natural light, and lead to the front porch. Pocket doors lead into the eat-in kitchen. Just off the kitchen is the renovated full bath, which has a large vanity and a shower with a built-in seat. The King size bed is perfect for curling up in after a long day of exploring Savannah. 

Upstairs, you are greeted by bohemian decor and chic furnishings. The main living space offers an ample amount of seating, and a large Flatscreen TV. The dining room that is open to the main living space, can sit up to six  guests. The kitchen sits just off of the back of the home complete with marble countertops, dishwasher, fridge, and stovetop. A designer's dream bathroom is situated conveniently between the main living space and kitchen, accented with hand-laid marble tile, a generous sized walk-in shower, and a high-end double vanity.

Off of the kitchen in the back of the home, you will find a large private porch with a flatscreen television and large daybed. Right off of the courtyard space, you will conveniently find your two private parking spaces. Facing Huntingdon Street, you'll find the two well-situated bedrooms both with spacious closets. The King bedroom offers a desk, large dresser, two closets and a flatscreen TV.The Queen bedroom has plenty of flare, with a solid wood dresser and generous wall mirror.

There are two designated parking spaces directly behind the home off of the lane.
