=== FULL DESCRIPTION ===
Located on Chatham Square and one block from Forsyth Park, guest are able to walk to many historic sites, tons of shopping and dining options. Relax in the lush courtyard at the Gordon Street Suites Carriage House. The home features a cozy living area, fully-equipped kitchen, loft bedroom with queen bed and en-suite bathroom.

The space
Located on Chatham Square and one block from Forsyth Park, guest are able to walk to many historic sites, tons of shopping and dining options. The neighborhood also features one of the city’s best blocks of historic row houses.

The carriage house is accessed from the LANE/alley that runs parallel to Gordon Street. There is a locked iron gate that enters into the courtyard and the free-standing carriage house has a private entrance. 

The home is 2-stories with a small but fully equipped kitchen & living room on the ground floor. Up the stairs is the loft-bedroom with queen bed, smart TV & private bathroom (walk-in shower). Also a separate closet & stackable laundry set. 

The walled courtyard is shared with other guests in the building (private to outsiders) and there is a bistro table at your front door just for you to have an evening cocktail or an alfresco dinner.

NOTES:
We do not have cable but free Wifi so please bring your passwords!

On nearby Tybee Island we are focused on providing ocean views & fun amenities with a variety of beach houses, town homes and condos, all with amazing views of the Atlantic Ocean.
