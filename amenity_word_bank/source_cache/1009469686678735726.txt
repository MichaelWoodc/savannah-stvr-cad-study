The space
Welcome to Harris House, a charming home nestled in the heart of the Historic District, where tree-lined streets and the convenience of downtown await. Within walking distance to everything downtown has to offer, this three-story freestanding home exudes a timeless appeal with a touch of history. With three full bedrooms, all featuring ensuite bathrooms, this delightful home is perfect for couples or families. Harris House offers an open and airy ambiance, thoughtfully adorned with mid-century furnishings that radiate warmth and invite you to relax.

Upon entering, you'll be greeted by an open living room, perfect for hosting gatherings with friends and family. It’s an ideal space to unwind, catch up on your favorite shows after a day of exploring the town, engage in a lively game night with your fellow travelers, or curl up by the decorative fireplace with a selection from the reading library. Adjacent to the living room is a formal dining room, conveniently situated next to a well-appointed wet bar, making it an excellent spot for intimate dinners or even a productive workspace. The fully equipped kitchen features stainless steel appliances and ensures you have all you need to prepare delicious home-cooked meals. A convenient powder room and year-round sunroom complete the first floor.

Venture up to the second floor, where the primary suite awaits with a king-sized bed, decorative fireplace, ceiling fan, plenty of storage for your belongings, a smart television, and a spacious ensuite bathroom complete with double vanity. Down the hall, the second suite boasts a queen-sized bed, ceiling fan, two double-wide closets, smart television and an ensuite bathroom with a tub-shower combination. Conveniently located between both rooms, guests will have access to a full size washer and dryer.

Ascend to the third floor to discover the final suite, where guests will enjoy views of the spires of The Cathedral Basilica of St. John the Baptist. On this floor, another comfortable king-sized bed, ceiling fan, large closet, smart television and an ensuite bathroom with skylight, ensuring every guest enjoys their own space and privacy.

Out back, you'll find a large, brick courtyard with privacy fence that provides a delightful escape. Equipped with a gas grill and ample seating, it's the perfect setting for intimate outdoor meals or a relaxing evening glass of wine.

Harris House is not just a vacation rental; it's a retreat that combines modern comforts with old-world charm. Make this your home away from home as you explore the vibrant surroundings of the Historic District and enjoy the rich tapestry of experiences that await you in beautiful Savannah.
