=== FULL DESCRIPTION ===
Incredible location on historic and famous Jones Street!  This spacious apartment on the "most beautiful street in the USA" is in the heart of the Historic District of Savannah!  Enjoy being close enough to walk to many downtown restaurants, sites, and the river, while still being able to take advantage of a quiet, tucked away retreat in the heart of the city!

SVR-00230

The space
Our adorable residence has everything you might need for a vacation away from it all!  The apartment features a king bed and full bath.  Our kitchen has all the essentials including silverware, basic cookware, coffee, cream and sugar.  In the living room, the couch pulls out to a sofa bed for an extra guest or two kiddos.  There is also a dining area with space for four!

Guest access
The entire top level of the carriage house is yours!  The courtyard is a shared space with guests who may be staying in the unit below.

Other things to note
This carriage house has two units - ask about booking both for larger parties! Check our profile for pictures of the other listing.
