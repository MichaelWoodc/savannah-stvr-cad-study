Welcome to your private Savannah home, a 4 bedroom, 3.5 bath retreat for up to 8 guests within walking distance of Savannah's historic district. Featuring a heated cocktail pool, open-concept living spaces, and a fully stocked kitchen, this professionally designed home is the perfect Savannah base for groups. The pool area is shared exclusively with the carriage house on the property. Guests may be placed in one of two similar units on the property. Additional units available upon inquiry.

Three-story main house in Savannah, situated on a lot that also includes a carriage house. This listing is for the main house only. Another group may be occupying the carriage house during your stay; both homes share access to the heated cocktail pool and outdoor area. Guests are sometimes placed in one of two similar homes on this property depending on availability. If you are interested in securing both homes exclusively, or the carriage house separately, additional units are available upon inquiry.
