The space
SVR-03204

Looking for the perfect Savannah stay that blends historic charm with modern comfort? This beautifully updated garden-level apartment offers just that — a serene retreat tucked in the heart of the Historic District.

Situated beside picturesque Crawford Square, this one-bedroom, one-bath unit is ideal for couples or solo travelers who want to relax in style while staying steps away from Savannah’s best dining, shopping, and historic landmarks. You’re just a short walk from River Street and all the charm that downtown has to offer.

Inside, the living area feels bright and inviting, featuring a comfortable sofa, a charming bistro table, and a dedicated workspace — perfect for those who like to mix a little business with leisure.

The bedroom offers a plush queen bed with a memory-foam mattress, luxury linens, and a cozy seating area for unwinding after a day of exploring. The bathroom includes a stylish shower/tub combination with updated designer touches.

The kitchen is fully equipped with modern appliances and everything you need to prepare a delicious meal or a simple breakfast before heading out. For convenience, a washer and dryer are available in the unit, and metered parking can be found directly in front of the property.

Step outside to enjoy your morning coffee or evening wine in the shared courtyard, accessible through your private back entry — a peaceful space framed by classic Savannah charm.

Please note: this unit is part of a historic multi-unit complex. You’ll have a private first-floor entry access to your apartment and courtyard.

Whether you’re visiting for a quiet weekend or an extended stay, this elegant garden-level retreat places you right where you want to be — surrounded by history, comfort, and Savannah’s timeless beauty.

Guest access
Guests will have access to the garden-level apartment (ground floor), the shared courtyard, and the street parking in front and behind the building on East McDonough Street. Please refer to the check-in guide on the Guest App for more details.

*Note: This apartment is in a property that houses other guests, so we ask that the space and neighboring property are treated with dignity and respect at all times. Please keep noise to a minimum between 8pm and 8am as per city ordinance. Pets, parties, and events are strictly prohibited, and failure to observe these and all other house rules risks the termination of the reservation without the possibility of a refund.

Other things to note
We ask that the space, other guests, and our neighbors be treated with dignity and respect. Please respect the home and its rules. This will help us maintain peace and ensure that all of our guests have a wonderful Savannah Stay!

Metered street parking is available on the front and back of the property on East McDonough Street. This area is shared with other residents, and it is on a first-come, first-served basis with no assigned spots.

Please be aware that even though we spray for bugs on a quarterly basis, you might see palmetto bugs, wasps, and other insects during your stay. They usually come out to die, but please do your best to keep them out of the house by keeping the windows and doors shut at all times, only bringing food items to the dining room, and keeping trash in the trash can.
