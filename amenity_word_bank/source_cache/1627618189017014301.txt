=== FULL DESCRIPTION ===
Minutes from Forsyth Park, this charming single-story home blends classic Savannah architecture with modern comfort, offering the perfect retreat for families, couples, or small groups looking to experience the Hostess City like a local.

The space
Welcome to Barnard Row 1404 by Lucky Savannah, a beautifully renovated three-bedroom vacation rental in Savannah’s vibrant Victorian District–West neighborhood. Just minutes from Forsyth Park and the Historic District, this charming single-story home blends classic Savannah architecture with modern comfort, offering the perfect retreat for families, couples, or small groups looking to experience the Hostess City like a local.

Step inside to find bright, thoughtfully designed interiors with updated furnishings and warm hardwood floors. The inviting living room is ideal for relaxing after a day exploring downtown Savannah, complete with comfortable seating and a Smart TV for cozy movie nights. The eat-in kitchen features stainless steel appliances, ample counter space, and a charming dining area perfect for morning coffee or shared meals before heading out to explore the city.

Each of the three bedrooms is designed with comfort in mind, offering plush bedding, private TVs, and closet space for longer stays. The full bathroom includes a tub and shower combination, making it convenient for families or groups traveling together.

Outside, enjoy your private backyard space with a gas grill — perfect for a relaxed evening cookout under Savannah’s live oaks. The home’s single-level layout makes it easy and comfortable for guests of all ages.

Located in the Victorian District–West, Barnard Row 1404 places you in one of Savannah’s most desirable residential neighborhoods. You’re just a short walk or bike ride to Forsyth Park, home to the iconic fountain, Saturday Farmers Market, tennis courts, and year-round events. Stroll north into the Historic District to explore Savannah’s picturesque squares, River Street, City Market, and renowned restaurants like The Olde Pink House and Husk. Coffee shops, local boutiques, and neighborhood favorites are all within easy reach.

What is parking like for this home?
Guests will have access to one dedicated parking space directly behind the home. All other parking is on-street and non-metered.

We can welcome your dog companions at this pet friendly home, but ask that you find other arrangements for cats, birds, and other furry friends. There is a $130 pet fee (non-refundable) with a 35 lb. maximum weight limit per dog and a limit of 2 dogs per reservation. Some homes may allow larger dogs, please inquire for more information.
