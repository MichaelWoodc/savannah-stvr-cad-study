=== FULL DESCRIPTION ===
Enjoy a quintessential Savannah experience at our centrally-located downtown condo! This 1BR/1BA retreat in an 1857 building features floor-to-ceiling windows with sweeping Bay St. views. Enjoy all the comforts of home after exploring everything our Southern city has to offer! The open concept living/kitchen/dining space is freshly renovated and features impeccable design and a comfy king bed! One parking pass for a nearby garage is included. Unbeatable central location!

SVR-02995

The space
Welcome to our exquisite condo in the heart of historic downtown Savannah! Located in a beautifully preserved 1857 building, this bright and light-filled retreat offers sweeping views of Bay Street through floor-to-ceiling windows, providing a picturesque setting for your stay, with all the fun our Hostess City has to offer just steps out your front door!

Step into the open concept kitchen, dining, and living space. The kitchen, small but mighty, is fully stocked, equipped with all modern conveniences and amenities to whip up a quick breakfast before heading out into the city or a full dinner after a long day of exploration! Enjoy a fresh cup of coffee from the Keurig, complete with complimentary K-cups, creamers, and sugar.

The living room is designed for comfort and relaxation, featuring chic décor and a soothing atmosphere. The sofa pulls out into a queen-sized bed, perfect for accommodating an extra guest. Both the living room and the bedroom are equipped with flat-screen TVs with streaming capabilities, ensuring you have plenty of entertainment options. Stay connected with fast WiFi throughout the condo.

The bedroom is a tranquil oasis with a king-sized bed made up with crisp white linens atop a memory foam mattress for your best night's sleep! The full bathroom boasts a spacious vanity and a large, walk-in shower, creating a spa-like experience that invites you to unwind and rejuvenate. For your convenience, an in-unit washer and dryer are available, making it easy to take care of laundry during your stay.

One parking pass for the nearby Congress Street iParkit garage, just a few blocks away, is included with your stay, ensuring you have a hassle-free parking experience.

With its perfect location in the heart of everything, our condo offers easy access to all the best attractions, dining, and entertainment that historic Savannah has to offer. Whether you're here to explore the rich history, enjoy the vibrant city life, or simply relax by the river, this condo provides an exceptional home base for your Savannah adventure.

Guest access
The entire condo is all yours from check-in at 3pm until check-out at 10am! The condo is completely private. To access the condo, you will enter the building, passing through a small common foyer and hallway. Your coded entry is right off the foyer area. At the end of the hallway there is a second coded entry, which belongs to the neighboring condo.

Other things to note
- The best thing about our condo, in our opinion, is the location! We are in the heart of downtown Savannah, overlooking the river, and central to all the amazing places that folks come to the city to visit! HOWEVER, with our incredible location comes noise! Guests should expect noise from the river side tourists, adjoining and nearby businesses, and nearby traffic. Noise should be expected at all hours. If you don't feel as though you would be comfortable with urban, city noise throughout your stay, we do not recommend this space for your Savannah travels. 

- We include one parking pass for the nearby Congress Street iParkit garage. The garage is located three blocks away. Unfortunately, Airbnb does not provide the option to check off "off-site, free parking" in the amenities list, so we like to clarify that we do provide one pass for this nearby garage, however it is three blocks away from the condo and not "on-site." You can, however, pull up to the condo to unload your luggage while idling your vehicle, then proceed to the parking garage, to avoid having to tote your belongings.
