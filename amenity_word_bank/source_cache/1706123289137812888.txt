=== FULL DESCRIPTION ===
Experience timeless Southern elegance at Elsewhere on Madison Estate, a luxury Savannah vacation rental designed for wedding parties, family gatherings, corporate retreats, and large group getaways.

The space
Experience timeless Southern elegance at Elsewhere on Madison Estate, a luxury Savannah vacation rental designed for wedding parties, family gatherings, corporate retreats, and large group getaways in the heart of Savannah’s Historic District. Just steps from the city’s iconic squares, celebrated restaurants, boutique shopping, and historic attractions, this expansive estate blends classic Savannah charm with upscale modern comfort for an unforgettable stay.

Perfect for groups of up to 16 guests, Elsewhere on Madison Estate offers exclusive access to the main residence, garden apartment, and private carriage house — providing multiple gathering spaces and private retreats ideal for bridal parties, reunion weekends, and multi-family vacations.

Inside the beautifully preserved main home, guests are welcomed by soaring ceilings, hardwood floors, ornate fireplaces, elegant chandeliers, and timeless historic details throughout. The spacious layout includes four full bedrooms, one partial bedroom, four full bathrooms, a large chef’s kitchen with modern appliances, and an additional third-floor suite complete with its own living space, dining area, and secondary kitchen for added convenience and flexibility.

The private garden apartment comfortably sleeps four guests and includes a queen bedroom, two full bathrooms, and a partial bedroom with a Murphy bed — ideal for guests seeking additional privacy while remaining connected to the group.

Outside, enjoy Savannah living at its finest in the secluded brick courtyard featuring a built-in grilling station and outdoor dining area perfect for evening gatherings, celebratory dinners, or relaxing after a day exploring the Historic District.

The estate also includes a charming private carriage house with a full kitchen and two stories of inviting living space. Warm lighting, earthy tones, and comfortable furnishings create a relaxed atmosphere perfect for unwinding. Vintage-style interior stairs lead to the upper-level living room, where modern luxury blends seamlessly with historic character. Please note the staircase is steep and narrow and may not be suitable for all guests.

Whether you’re planning a Savannah wedding weekend, bachelorette celebration, holiday gathering, or luxury group escape, Elsewhere on Madison Estate offers the perfect combination of historic beauty, spacious accommodations, and an unbeatable downtown location.
