=== FULL DESCRIPTION ===
Experience Casa d’Arte, an authentic 1896 historic Savannah home near Forsyth Park. This charming 4-bed, 3-bath vacation rental offers 1,900 sq ft for up to 8 guests, blending classic elegance with modern comfort. Enjoy a walkable, historic neighborhood, unique architecture, and vibrant local cuisine. Perfect for families or groups seeking a memorable Savannah experience.

The space
Welcome to Casa d’Arte, a charming and authentic 1896 historic home offering 1,900 square feet of inviting space. This four-bedroom, three-bathroom vacation rental comfortably accommodates up to eight guests, showcasing preserved architectural details that blend classic elegance with modern comfort for a truly unique Savannah experience.

The space features:

> Sitting Room: Bright, well-lit with tall windows, a beautiful chandelier, and a cozy indoor fireplace, perfect for relaxation.
 
>Dining Area: Formal dining space with seating for six, ideal for memorable meals.

>Kitchen: Modern and fully equipped with appliances and ample counter space for all your culinary needs.

> Bedrooms: Four distinct bedrooms, each designed as a restful retreat with large windows, ceiling fans, and comfortable furnishings to ensure a peaceful night's sleep.

Guest access
Guests will have full and exclusive access to the entire Casa d’Arte home and all its amenities.

Key amenities available for your use include:
   
>High-speed WiFi
> Convenient laundry facilities
> TV with Roku livestreaming
> Complimentary towels
>  Essential bathroom supplies: soap, shampoo, and makeup wipes
> Initial supply of three rolls of toilet paper per bathroom and one roll of paper towel for the kitchen.

Other things to note
Please be considerate of the neighborhood and our neighbors. City of Savannah Noise Ordinance for vacation rentals is between the hours of 9pm- 8am. Please remember that sound carries very well throughout Savannah, most especially in courtyards, porches, patios, and verandas.

Please note the following important details for your stay:

> Our home is in compliance with all Short-Term Vacation Rental laws in Savannah, Georgia. Our STVR permit number is SVR-01379.
 
> While we provide an initial supply of three rolls of toilet paper per bathroom and one roll of paper towels, guests planning a longer stay or extensive cooking may wish to purchase additional supplies.

Speaks English
We've lived in Savannah for over 20 years each. We still live downtown but decided to rent our homes as vacation rentals because we want you to enjoy Downtown Savannah as much as we do. All of our homes are located in the Historic / Victorian district and in walking distance to Forsyth Park where we love to go on Saturday mornings for the Farmers Market or have a picnic on Sunday afternoon. Our kids love the playground and the grand yard. Our properties were decorated to be comfortable for you and your family/friends. We have a large sectional sofa in the living rooms. We want you to relax when you're at our homes and if that means lying on the couch and watching a movie then this is the right place for you! We are both Realtors for Salt Marsh Realty. So if you decide that you want to buy a home while visiting Savannah, please let us know.
