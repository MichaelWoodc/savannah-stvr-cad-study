=== FULL DESCRIPTION ===
Welcome to Sweet Retreat Savannah! This charming, comfortable one-bedroom apartment is thoughtfully designed for travelers seeking convenience, relaxation, and a great location. Whether you’re visiting for work or leisure, you’ll find everything you need for a seamless stay.

This condo is conveniently located on Oglethorpe Square, near shops, restaurants and local attractions. You’ll have easy access to everything Savannah has to offer while still enjoying a peaceful place to unwind. 
SVR-02338

The space
Charming, comfortable and stylish, Sweet Retreat Savannah, is located in the heart of the Historic District on Oglethorpe Square. This condo is just steps away from Broughton Street and just a short walk to City Market, River Street and Forsyth Park. You've been warned--so pack your walking shoes! 

The apartment is located on the third floor of the building and is reached by stairs. Unfortunately, an elevator is not available. On the third floor, though, you will find a shared balcony featuring the best views from the building! From there, start your morning with a cup of coffee or end the day watching the sun set with a cocktail in hand. If you're lucky enough to visit on St. Patrick's Day, you're in luck! Oglethorpe Square is on the parade route so take advantage of your front row seat to the show!

The apartment features a bright living room with comfortable seating, a fully equipped kitchen for home-cooked meals and a private bedroom with a plush queen bed  outfitted with quality linens to ensure a restful night’s sleep. Additionally, a pull-out sleeper sofa in the living room accommodates two more guests. The bathroom is clean, cool, modern and stocked with fresh towels and a starter set of essential toiletries.

Guest access
This apartment is a private space and the balcony is shared with other residents of the building.

Other things to note
Children
Please note that the apartment is not childproofed, and guests will accept responsibility for the safety and supervision of their children in the condo. We do not provide gear for children, such as Pack N Plays or high chairs, but they can be rented. 

Parking 
Parking in downtown Savannah can be challenging and luckily there is a parking garage very close to the apartment. The State Street Parking Garage is west of the apartment, on State between Abercorn and Drayton Streets. Open 24 hours a day, seven days a week, the parking spaces are not assigned or guaranteed. All parking is based on availability at the owner's expense. 

Most street parking is metered, and payment is due Monday-Saturday from 8 am-8 pm. Sundays and holidays are free. When parking overnight, please pay attention to the signs advising of the day and time for weekly street sweeping. During those hours parked cars will be ticketed.

If you see a “No Parking or You Will Be Towed” sign anywhere in Savannah–believe it!

Do not leave anything of value visible in your car.

Housekeeping During Longer Stays
For reservations exceeding eight (8) days, we require a weekly professional cleaning for the duration of the reservation. The dates and times will be coordinated after your reservation is made and payment will be requested through Airbnb's Resolution Center. By confirming your booking you acknowledge and agree to this requirement. 

Cancellation Policy
Before booking, please acknowledge that   
(1) any reservation cancelled at least 30 days before check-in will receive a full refund, 
(2) any reservation cancelled 7-30 days before check-in will receive a partial refund, and 
(3) any reservation cancelled 7 days or less of check-in will forfeit their payment.

Travel Insurance
Travel Insurance is strongly recommended to help you recover your loss if your trip must be cancelled.

Hurricane/Storm policy
During hurricane season (June 1-November 30), we closely monitor all storm activity. Guests are encouraged to monitor as well using reliable weather sources. If local authorities issue a mandatory evacuation order, guests must vacate the premises immediately. Guests are responsible for securing alternate living accommodations, if an evacuation order is issued. A full refund will be issued for any unused nights only if a mandatory evacuation order is issued. If guests choose to leave early (voluntarily/without a mandatory evacuation), due to changes in travel plans because of uncertainty weather conditions, standard cancellation policies will apply.
