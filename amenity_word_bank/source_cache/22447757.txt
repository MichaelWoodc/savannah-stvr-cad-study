=== FULL DESCRIPTION ===
The upscale-yet-relaxed style of our Price Street retreat will make the perfect getaway spot for a couple or small family. The one bedroom, one bath apartment will easily accommodate two Lucky guests.

The space
Just steps away from some of the best that Savannah has to offer in dining, entertainment, and nightlife, this second floor apartment is perfect for a romantic weekend getaway. Enjoy all the comforts of a hotel room with experiencing Savannah like a local in this charming apartment. 

The bright, open floor plan of this property flows from the combined living space with dining nook into the fully equipped kitchen. White washed cabinets and appliances lend an airy feel to the space, while offsetting the stunning honey-stained hardwood floors. You’ll be hard pressed to leave the comforts of this home-away-from-home, including the queen-sized bedroom with its soothing, neutral palette and whimsical artwork. The beautiful wood floors continue into the en suite bath, which has a shower/tub combo and vanity. 

Get your day started at Java Burrito, an affair of brunch, coffee and tex-mex cuisine. After venturing out for lunch, take in the shopping on nearby Broughton or enjoy the cafes and local music venues on River Street. You'll be less than a 15 minute walk from every destination you wish to reach in the Historic district! 

Why Lucky Savannah Loves this Property:
- Central air conditioning, a washer and dryer and private parking spot add to the conveniences of this live like a local vacation experience.
- Savannah’s bustling riverfront is just steps away.
- A short walk from Trustees Theatre, this is prime spot for attendees of the Savannah Film Festival. 
 
Sleeping Configuration:
Bedroom 1: Queen Bed

Parking: Private Parking
This unit includes a small private parking space located at the rear of the property. Additional vehicles will need to be park on the street, which may be metered or non-metered. You may also park in a nearby garage, or purchase a parking pass at the Visitor Information Center located on MLK Jr., Blvd. This information and more is provided for our guests in our confirmation email upon booking. 

We can welcome your dog companions at this pet friendly home, but ask that you find other arrangements for cats, birds, and other furry friends. There is a $130 pet fee (non-refundable) with a 35 lb. maximum weight limit per dog and a limit of 2 dogs per reservation. Some homes may allow larger dogs, please inquire for more information.
