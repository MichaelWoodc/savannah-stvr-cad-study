=== FULL DESCRIPTION ===
Perfect spot for your Savannah getaway! This spacious, 3-bedroom, 2-bathroom condo is the ultimate retreat for you and your group! FABULOUS chef's kitchen with all the amenities, cozy and comfortable living space with flat screen SmartTV and fast WiFi, private porch with grill, and lovely shared courtyard space! Located steps from Forsyth Park!

SVR 02706 and 02707

The space
This 3-bedroom, 2-bathroom condo is the entire first floor of a historic Savannah mansion. The huge master bedroom features a king sized, temperpedic bed with an additional pull-out sofa tucked into the corner, perfect for just one more person or the kiddo that wants to stay in Mom and Dad's room! The master also has a wall-mounted, flat screen TV, spa-like en suite bathroom with double vanity, huge walk-in shower, and TONS of storage in the massive walk-in closet. The second bedroom also features a king sized bed, plenty of storage space, a cozy reading nook, and a wall-mounted flat-screen TV. And last, but never least, is the whimsical third bedroom, bursting with pops of color, queen sized bed, plenty of storage, fun hanging egg chair, and private desk area (just in case you need to squeeze in a little work during your stay)! Off the hallway, between the two bedrooms, is the second full bathroom, complete with laundry facilities and jetted bathtub!
In the living room, you'll be able to kick your feet up and relax on the luxe velvet sectional, tune into your favorite shows on the SmartTV, or take that insta-worthy shot in front of our fun plate wall art! And finally...that kitchen! Hopefully the pictures speak for themselves, but our kitchen is quite the installation! TONS of prep space, incredible gas range, massive refrigerator, TWO sinks, wine fridge with wet bar area, counter seating...what more could a chef need? And if you want to take that meal outside...feel free! Off the kitchen you will find your private porch area with plenty of seating, or take a few steps down into the courtyard where you'll be able to use the gas grill and maybe mingle with our other guests!

Guest access
The entire condo is all yours!

Other things to note
Don't be afraid to reach out if you need anything!  We live super close-by and are always happy to help out, even mid-stay.
