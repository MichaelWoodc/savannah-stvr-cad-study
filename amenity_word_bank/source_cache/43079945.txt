=== FULL DESCRIPTION ===
Welcome to Rosé All Day 💕—Savannah’s perfectly pink Victorian getaway! Often called the “Barbie Dream House,” our insta-worthy 1885 home blends historic charm with cheerful, modern luxury. 🥂 This beautifully restored first-floor condo features 2 bedrooms, 1 bath & sleeps up to 6. Best of all? You’re just steps from Forsyth Park 🌳 and an easy walk to downtown Savannah’s restaurants, shops, bars & historic sights. Come sip, stay & Rosé All Day! 🍷✨

SVR-02119

The space
Welcome to your perfectly pink home away from home! 💕 Rosé All Day is a spacious, bright first-floor condo designed with equal parts style, comfort, and fun. From the historic details of our 1885 Victorian to playful pops of pink throughout, this is a Savannah stay with plenty of personality.

🛏️ ROOM TO RELAX
The primary bedroom features a plush pink velvet king bed and a fabulous makeup vanity—perfect for getting ready before brunch, a night out, or a Savannah celebration! The second bedroom has another comfy king bed, plus a twin bed with a twin trundle tucked underneath, giving your group plenty of room to spread out.

🥂 MADE FOR GOOD TIMES
Snap a selfie with your crew at our champagne-inspired photo wall, gather for drinks before heading downtown, or cook up something delicious (with a glass of rosé in hand, of course!) in our fully equipped kitchen.

When it’s time to wind down, curl up under our bubbly chandelier and stream your favorites on the Smart TV with super-fast WiFi—or grab a book from our rainbow color-coded bookshelf and settle in for a cozy night at home.

🌳 STEPS FROM FORSYTH PARK
One of the best parts of Rosé All Day is the location! We’re just steps from beautiful Forsyth Park, one of Savannah’s most iconic spots. Start your morning with a walk beneath the live oaks, grab a coffee nearby, visit the Saturday farmers market, have a picnic by the fountain, or simply enjoy one of the prettiest parts of the city right outside your door.

From here, you can explore Savannah on foot, wandering through the Victorian and Historic Districts to discover gorgeous squares, historic homes, restaurants, bars, shops, and plenty of that famous Savannah charm. 🌿✨

🚗 EASY & FREE PARKING
Rosé All Day does not have dedicated off-street parking, but free street parking is available around the house and is typically easy to find. No expensive parking garage or nightly parking fee! And with Forsyth Park just steps away and so much of Savannah within walking distance, you may find you hardly need your car at all.

💌 YOUR LOCAL HOSTS
We’re Megan & Justin, Savannah locals who truly love our city and love sharing it with our guests! Looking for the best brunch, a great cocktail, a special dinner, things to do, or a hidden gem away from the usual tourist spots? Just ask! We’re always happy to share our favorite Savannah spots and recommendations to help you make the most of your stay.

Whether you’re visiting for a girls’ getaway, a Savannah weekend, a celebration, or just because you deserve a little more pink in your life, Rosé All Day is ready to welcome you. Grab your favorite people, pour a glass, and stay awhile! 🍷💕

Guest access
The entire first floor is yours! The spacious floor plan includes two bedrooms, living space, full bathroom, and complete kitchen.

Other things to note
City of Savannah hotel tax is included in your nightly rate.
