=== FULL DESCRIPTION ===
The most premier location on River Street with two private balconies overlooking the Savannah River! Enjoy stunning views of the river while watching mega ships and super yachts sail into port. Located in the heart of the Historic District, explore all the city has to offer as the most popular sites attractions are within walking distance.

The space
This historic building offers all the modern amenities while showcasing the city's rich history through carefully preserved details. The house has plenty of games, books, and TV's  to keep everyone entertained and with 5 beds, there's enough room for the whole family or group of friends. The kitchen is fully equipped for cooking and the home offers plenty of space, creating the perfect home away from home. The listing includes one private parking space at the front door. There's an additional parking space reserved for the owner which can be leased upon request. Don't miss out on the opportunity to make unforgettable memories at Lowden Suites on Historic River Street.
