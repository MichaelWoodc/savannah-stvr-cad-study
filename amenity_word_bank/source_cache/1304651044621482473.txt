=== FULL DESCRIPTION ===
Welcome to the Luxe Retreat, the elegant west-side townhouse of Savannah's historic Silas Fulton House. Built in 1860 for Silas Fulton — one of Savannah's early freedmen and entrepreneurs — this beautifully preserved post-and-beam home has been thoughtfully restored to offer guests an experience that's authentically Savannah and unmistakably luxurious.

The space
With 3 bedrooms, 2 baths, and room for up to 6 guests, the Luxe Retreat is the perfect size for couples traveling together, small family trips, girls' weekends, anniversaries, and romantic Southern getaways. Step inside and you're greeted by original architectural details — exposed beams, soaring ceilings, and timeless Southern character — paired with the kind of refined modern finishes you'd expect from a high-end boutique stay.

Each of the three elegantly appointed bedrooms is designed for restorative rest, with luxury mattresses (frequently called out by guests as some of the most comfortable they've ever slept in), high-end linens, and thoughtfully curated decor. Wake up refreshed, brew coffee in the fully equipped chef's kitchen, and settle in for a day of exploring — or stay in and enjoy the home's beautifully designed living spaces, smart TV, and high-speed Wi-Fi.

When the sun sets, head out to the shared courtyard, where a gas fire pit sets the scene for slow evenings under the Savannah sky. And in a city famously short on parking, you'll appreciate the home's rare and welcome perk: a reserved off-street parking spot for guests of the Luxe Retreat.

Step outside the front door and you're immediately immersed in the magic of Savannah's Historic District. Stroll Jones Street — often called one of the most beautiful streets in America — wander the moss-draped squares, sip cocktails on lantern-lit patios, browse boutiques, and dine at award-winning restaurants. Forsyth Park is just a short walk away, and River Street's waterfront views, shops, and nightlife are an easy stroll from the home.

Planning a larger gathering? The Luxe Retreat shares a building with our Haint Blue Haven townhouse, and the two can be booked together to create a 6-bedroom, 3.5-bath estate that sleeps 12 — ideal for family reunions, bachelorette weekends, weddings, and milestone celebrations.

Whether you're here for the history, the food, the romance, or simply to slow down and enjoy the Lowcountry, the Luxe Retreat at Silas Fulton House offers a stay as memorable as Savannah itself. courtyard, exploring the nearby attractions, or relaxing in the beautiful living areas, Silas Fulton Historic House promises a unique blend of history and luxury.
