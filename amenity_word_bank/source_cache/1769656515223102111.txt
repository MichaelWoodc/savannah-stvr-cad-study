=== FULL DESCRIPTION ===
Welcome to our grand Federalist home in Savannah's Landmark Historic District! This home boasts 4 king and 1 queen bedrooms, 4.5 bathrooms, soaring ceilings, spacious dining areas, 2 well-equipped kitchens, and a ton of comfortable living spaces. We hope our thoughtful design has seamlessly blended old-world charm with modern conveniences! We can't wait to host you in this gorgeous Southern estate!

SVR 02754 and 02755

The space
The White House on President is perfectly situated in downtown, Historic Savannah, making an ideal homebase for your Savannah travels! We even have 2 private, off-street parking spaces...quite the commodity in the busy city! Outside in the small garden, you'll find a little seating area and a propane grill, which offers opportunities for relaxation and al fresco dining.

As you step inside, you'll be captivated by the seamless blend of original architecture and modern amenities. The first floor condo welcomes you with a spacious living room featuring a flat-screen TV, speedy fast WiFi, and ample seating.  If you want some home cooked meals, a full kitchen and dining area awaits.  Get some rest in the two king primary suites too!

Upstairs, you will find another living room and another large galley style kitchen.  The adjacent dining room is a grand space, ideal for entertaining friends and family. And for the mornings where you need a quick bite before you're off to explore all that Savannah has to offer...there's a cozy breakfast area just off the kitchen! Step towards the back of the house and you'll find the large sunroom, filled with natural light and perfect for indulging in a good book with a cup of coffee. Our powder room is adorned with a gorgeous, hand painted, mural wallpaper by a local artist!

Climbing the magnificent central staircase, which the house was designed around, you'll discover three beautifully appointed bedrooms (two kings and one queen) along with two freshly renovated and meticulously designed full bathrooms. Abundant storage space ensures your comfort, and there's ample room to retreat and unwind.

We hope that you enjoy the classic, Southern sophistication our home has to offer! We like to think of it as a place where history meets comfort, where old-world character meets modern design, and where unforgettable Savannah memories are made!

Guest access
This building is split into the large main parlor level house and a separate garden level condo (the small condo at street level). Both are part of this listing and connected via an internal staircase.  Yes, you have two fully appointed living spaces!

Other things to note
Don't be afraid to reach out if you need anything!  We live super close-by and are always happy to help out, even mid-stay.
