=== FULL DESCRIPTION ===
Private garden apt near Forsythe park.   1 block from Krogers,  in the most gracious part of the historic district.   Homey, artsy and glamorous, with the exquisite hand knotted, vegetable died oriental carpets and the  fine art, and extensive use of hand carved wood panels.  There is also a private  patio, which is exclusively yours.   The fountain in the patio, surrounded by potted plants. 
SVR-02555

The space
The living room is large, luxurious and warm with a fireplace (closed for insurance reasons,  and plushy newer furniture. The bedroom opens into a private patio garden, is oversized with brilliant hand knotted oriental carpeting, private full bath with travertine stone floor and hand carved decorative  panels. Security camera outside by front door.

Guest access
Extra towels are in the antique small cabinet to the right of the bedroom glass door and extra linens are in the closet by front door. Please reuse towels like you would at home.

Other things to note
Equipped with High speed wireless internet and amazing Roku 4K TVs.  Both the king size bed in the bedroom and the pull out sofa bed has the newest high-end self-cooling mattresses which are extremely comfortable!

There are security cameras outside the front and back doors

Very private home with no shared spaces or entry ways.
