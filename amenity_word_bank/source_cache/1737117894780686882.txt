Gordon Street Retreat

The space
Walk everywhere from this historic downtown flat. Showcasing vintage Victorian charm, this gorgeous retreat boasts an unbeatable walking distance to all the sights of Savannah, including Broughton Street, City Market, Forsyth Park, and River Street. This home is ideal for traveling professionals, students, or film industry workers with extended lodging needs.



Original antique floors, exposed brick walls, and strong natural lighting are on full display inside, along with a suite of modern home essentials furnished for convenience and comfort. The updated kitchen features all-new counters, cabinets, and stainless steel appliances to easily handle home cooking. Comfortable seating and a cable-equipped smart TV set the stage for casual downtime and home entertainment, with free WiFi provided for remote working or streaming. A breezy private balcony in the rear of the flat overlooking the enclosed courtyard, together with a stunning view of Chatham Park from huge front windows, offers a picturesque view for morning coffee or a nightcap. The private washer/dryer adds an extra layer of convenience during your stay.



Things to Know:

-Streaming services available with guests’ own account(s).
