Neighborhood highlights
Larry and Joan's garden apartment is right in the heart of Savannah's historic district! Walk two blocks to Forsyth Park and then head straight north on Bull Street past a host of shops, restaurants, historic homes and museums. Five of Savannah's 22 squares are on Bull Street, lovely oases of live oaks, monuments, flowering shrubs, and park benches to rest weary feet. The Savannah River and lively River Street are just a mile away. Closer to home are Kroger's Supermarket (with a Starbucks inside) and the quirky but yummy Mate Factor for coffee, teas, to-die-for muffins and breads as well as sandwiches. SCAD's Chatham Towers and Welcome Center are a block and half away. Location, location, location!
