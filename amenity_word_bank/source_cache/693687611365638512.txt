=== FULL DESCRIPTION ===
The most perfect location on iconic Jones Street. A historic home built in 1851. Close to restaurants, stylish shops, free downtown shuttle at the corner,  across the street from the famous Mrs. Wilkes and a quick stroll to Forsyth Park. Stylishly designed by its owner a local interior designer. Great "work from home" location. Host is always reachable directly. No crazy check out requirements, no added fees (just cleaning and taxes). NO PETS. For parking pls see "parking info" SVR-02191

The space
Entry is at ground level. No stairs just a small step up at entry. Spacious bedroom with a queen bed. Bathroom has a walk in shower. Ample living room with a dedicated work space (desk). Large screen smart TV. Kitchen is fully equipped, electric stove,  refrigerator, freezer, microwave, blender, etc. Central heat and AC with Nest Thermostat. Laundry room with a washer/ Dryer, ironing board and iron.

Other things to note
There is a pullout sofa. If there are only 2 guests on the reservation and the pullout is used a one time $25 linen fee will be charged upon check out. If there is a third guest staying in the apartment (even if partially) it must be disclosed  in the reservation request.  There is a 3 guests maximum, $25fee applies per guest (after 2) per night.
