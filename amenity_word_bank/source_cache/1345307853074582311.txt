Really enjoyed our time in Savannah! The house itself was comfortable, well-stocked, and had everything we needed for a great trip. The host was responsive and check-in was smooth.
The location is worth noting — it’s not walking distance to the historic district, so you’ll want a car or be prepared to use rideshare to get to the squares and main attractions. Not a dealbreaker at all, just something to plan around.
The one thing that caught us off guard was the noise. Not sure if it was street traffic or neighbors, but there were a few moments — especially at night — where it was noticeable. Light sleepers may want to bring earplugs.
Overall though, we had a genuinely good stay and would recommend it for anyone visiting Savannah. Great value for what you get.
