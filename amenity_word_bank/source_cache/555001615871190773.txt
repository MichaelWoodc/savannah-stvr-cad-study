=== FULL DESCRIPTION ===
This impeccable Mansion is your own private slice of heaven in Savannah’s National Historic Landmark District. Built in 1854, this lovingly restored home is nestled on quiet Pulaski Square.

The space
Bell Mansion Main + Garden on Pulaski Square is your own private slice of heaven in Savannah’s National Historic Landmark District. Built in 1854, this impeccably restored mansion is nestled on quiet Pulaski Square. Featuring floor to ceiling windows that boast with natural light, impressive 14-foot ceilings, original moldings and wood floors, you will appreciate the lovingly restored interiors that blend historic charm with modern luxury appointments. 

The grand exterior staircase leads up to the second level entrance, which is deemed the parlor level here in the south. You'll find that the open floor plan on this level is an entertainers dream! The open gathering room and dining room create an inviting and intimate space, perfect for entertaining while enjoying mesmerizing views of Pulaski Square where you'll be transported through time. The kitchen is situated just at the back of the home, with high-end finished and appliances, such as marble countertops, a gas-range, and everything you need to prepare meals.

Seven of the bedrooms are spread out amongst the four ravishing levels of the main home. The third floor has two king bedrooms, each with luxurious ensuite bathrooms, an ample amount of closet space, and tasteful furnishings. The primary suite faces charming Pulaski Square, with a plush king size bed. Also on this floor, you'll find a small sitting room with a flat screen television and a wet bar with a wine cooler - a great space to hangout! Heading up to the fourth floor, four additional bedrooms can be found, where guests will enjoy soaring views of the treelined streets, and two shared, full bathrooms.

Heading down to the garden level apartment, this private retreat is the perfect place for family members with mobility issues as it has ground floor access. Packed with history and charm, the original historic beams and decorative brick fireplace, bring so much character to this quaint space. The thoughtfully chosen furnishings create a comfortable place to relax, and catch-up after a long day exploring the beautiful city. No detail was sparred while remodeling the kitchen - you'll have all of the modern conveniences one could ask for! The garden level apartment additionally features a cozy bedroom, with a queen size bed and full-size bathroom. 

Whether you're here for a formal event, or family reunion, Bell Mansion Main + Garden on Pulaski Square is the perfect home for your party. Start your day of exploration through the squares as you venture down Barnard Street, that leads all the way to the river front. You’ll be a short walk from the Cathedral of St. John the Baptist, Forsyth Park, and so many other well known monuments and venues in Savannah.

Parking:
Parking for this home is on-street and metered, which is enforced by the City of Savannah from Monday through Friday, 8am to 5pm. Guests may conveniently pay using the ParkSavannah app, feed the meter or parking kiosks directly, or park on a nearby non-metered street. Please take note of posted Sweep Zone signs when parking on the street.
