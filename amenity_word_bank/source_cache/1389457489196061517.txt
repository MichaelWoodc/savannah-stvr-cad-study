=== FULL DESCRIPTION ===
Welcome to Bay Street Bliss! This elegant 2-bedroom, 2.5-bath condo is perfect for couples, small families, or friends seeking a luxurious Savannah getaway. Located in the heart of the Landmark Historic District, our sixth-floor condo offers unmatched views of the Savannah River and the iconic gold-domed City Hall. With stylish interiors, a private balcony, and room for up to 4 guests, this is the perfect home base for your Lowcountry escape. No pets or smoking on premises per HOA.

The space
KEY FEATURES:

☀ Prime Location + Views: Stunning sixth-floor river and City Hall views from your private double balcony

☀ Comfortable Living: Open-concept layout with a cozy seating area and elegant decor

☀ Well-Equipped Kitchen: Full kitchen with an extra-long breakfast bar and dining table for four

☀ Two En Suite Bedrooms:

• Primary bedroom with a plush king bed and ensuite full bath

• Second bedroom with a queen bed and ensuite bathroom

☀ Modern and well-equipped kitchen with coffee bar w/ Keurig 

☀ WiFi throughout the home

☀ Board games and books for enjoyment

☀ Convenient location that is walkable to attractions in Historic Savannah

☀ Convenient Amenities: Central AC, full-size washer & dryer, and elevator access



Expect a Five Star stay with us. 



☆☆ BEDROOMS ☆☆

This gorgeous apartment accommodates 4 guests in 2 bedrooms

Primary Bedroom – The primary bedroom at Bay Street Bliss exudes elegance and comfort, featuring a luxurious king-size bed adorned with extra sleeping pillows, accent pillows and a cozy blanket. The room also features a Roku smart TV, fast Wi-Fi, bedside tables with reading lamps and a cozy seating area, perfect for relaxation

Second Bedroom – The second bedroom features a queen bed with extra pillows.

☆☆BATHROOMS☆☆

Bay Street Bliss offers two private en-suite bathrooms—one with a walk-in shower in the primary suite and another with a tub/shower combo in the second bedroom—providing comfort and privacy for every guest. A convenient half bath off the kitchen adds extra ease for entertaining and day-to-day living.  The bathrooms are stocked with shampoo, conditioner and body wash.



☆☆ KITCHEN☆☆

The bright and airy kitchen is fully equipped with all the appliances, utensils and cookware you need to feel at home.



☆☆ OUTSIDE SPACE ☆☆

The exterior space is all about the view. Step out onto the private double balcony and take in breathtaking vistas of the Savannah River and the iconic gold-domed City Hall. Perched on the sixth floor, this covered terrace is the perfect place to sip your morning coffee, enjoy a sunset cocktail, or snap stunning photos of the city below. 



Located just one block from River Street, Bay Street Bliss places you in the heart of Savannah's vibrant downtown. You'll be within walking distance of the city's best cafes, bars, and restaurants, making it easy to explore the local culinary scene. Whether you're here for a quick getaway or an extended stay, Bay Street Bliss provides a perfect blend of comfort, style, and prime location, making it an ideal choice for families, couples, and solo travelers alike.



We provide a starter pack of K-cups, trash bags, paper towels and toilet paper for your convenience.

Primary renter must be 25 years old to rent and signer of agreement must stay on premises for entire booking.



No smoking or vaping on premises.  This includes the outdoor balcony.

Guest access

Other things to note
Bay Street Bliss boasts a range of features designed to ensure a luxurious and comfortable stay. This 2-bedroom, 2.5-bathroom condo has been recently renovated to blend historic charm with modern amenities. Each bedroom is luxuriously appointed with high-end bedding, extra pillows, cozy throws, and coordinating accent pillows, providing guests with a restful and indulgent experience.



The condo's large windows allow natural light to flood the space, highlighting the tall ceilings and historic details that add to its charm. Modern conveniences include TV, fast Wi-Fi, and a well-equipped coffee bar. The private porch offers a serene spot for morning coffee or evening tea. An in-condo laundry room adds to the convenience, making it easy for guests to keep their clothes fresh during their stay.



Located just one block from River Street, Bay Street Bliss provides easy access to Savannah's top attractions, cafes, bars, and restaurants. The condo is situated in a safe environment with security cameras for peace of mind. These thoughtful features make Bay Street Bliss an ideal choice for families, couples, and solo travelers seeking a memorable stay in Savannah.
