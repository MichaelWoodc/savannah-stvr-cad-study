=== FULL DESCRIPTION ===
The Lafayette Duo offers the rare opportunity to rent two full floors of a historic townhouse in the heart of Savannah’s Landmark District. Just one block from the iconic Cathedral Basilica of St. John the Baptist, this elegant property includes two private units, each with 3 bedrooms, 2 bathrooms, a full kitchen, and dining space. Perfect for groups, the home sleeps up to 16 guests and places you steps from Lafayette Square, Forsyth Park, and Savannah’s most beloved sights.

The space
Step inside The Lafayette Duo, a rare rental featuring two private units stacked across the first and second floors of a historic townhouse. Each unit offers its own kitchen, living area, 3 bedrooms, and 2 bathrooms, creating the perfect balance of shared space and personal privacy. With room for up to 16 guests, this beautifully designed property offers stylish comfort, flexibility, and unmatched access to Savannah’s most iconic sites—all just a block from Lafayette Square and the Cathedral.

Guest access
Guests have a private access and will receive a key code prior to arrival to enter the home. This keycode is changed after every guest for your safety.

Other things to note
★ Key Features:

• Prime location near Lafayette Square

• 6 bedrooms (2 king, 4 queens, 2 sleeper sofas)

• 4 full bathrooms

• Sunlit bay windows & grand fireplaces

• 2 full kitchens w/ stainless steel appliances

• Ceiling fans in bedrooms & living rooms

• 2 in-unit washer & dryers

• High-speed WiFi & smart TVs

• Elegant historic details throughout

• Walk to Forsyth Park, shops & dining
