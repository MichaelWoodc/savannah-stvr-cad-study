Bring the whole crew to The Lemon Drop! 🍋 This listing includes THREE private 2BR/1BA condos in the same bright & cheerful Savannah home—perfect for families and friend groups who want to stay together while still having their own space! Each condo has a full kitchen, living + dining area, and one features a fabulous outdoor patio. Enjoy 1 private parking space + a fantastic location just a few blocks from Forsyth Park! 🌳💛

SVR-02490, 02491, 02492

The space
Welcome to The Lemon Drop! 🍋💛 This listing gives your group THREE completely private 2-bedroom, 1-bathroom condos in the same colorful Savannah home—perfect for large families, groups of friends, wedding weekends, girls' trips, or anyone who wants to stay together while still having plenty of room to spread out!

Each condo has its own private entrance with no shared indoor spaces, plus its own full kitchen, living room, dining area, washer + dryer, and all the comforts of home. Your group can gather together when you want and retreat to your own space when you don't!

And when it's time to wind down, there's no fighting over the TV—every bedroom has its own Smart TV! 📺 Each condo also has a Keurig, and we'll have a few K-Cups waiting to help get your first Savannah mornings started. ☕️

Here's what you'll have:

🍋 LEMON TWIST – Upstairs Front | 2BR/1BA

Bright, spacious, and full of personality, Lemon Twist is our second-floor, street-facing condo.

Bedroom 1: King bed + twin daybed
Bedroom 2: Queen bed

The modern bathroom features a walk-in shower. You'll also have a spacious living room, dining area, full kitchen, washer + dryer, Keurig, and private entrance. The refrigerator does not have an ice maker.

💛 MAIN SQUEEZE – Downstairs Front | 2BR/1BA

Main Squeeze might just have the coolest history of the bunch! This first-floor, street-facing condo originally served as a storefront around 1910. Today, the space is bright and welcoming with tons of natural light, while still retaining the historic character that makes Savannah homes so special. ✨

Bedroom 1: King bed + twin trundle with two twin beds
Bedroom 2: King bed

The bathroom features a shower, and the full kitchen includes a refrigerator. You'll also have your own spacious living room, dining area, washer + dryer, Keurig, and private entrance.

🌿 LEMON LANE – Lane-Facing | 2BR/1BA

Tucked toward the back of the property, Lemon Lane offers a little extra privacy and its own fantastic outdoor space.

Bedroom 1: King bed
Bedroom 2: Queen bed
Living Room: Queen pull-out sofa

The bathroom features a walk-in shower with a sliding glass door, and the full kitchen includes a refrigerator with an ice maker. Lemon Lane also has its own living room, dining area, washer + dryer, Keurig, and private entrance.

But the real bonus at Lemon Lane is the private fenced courtyard! 🌿✨ Enjoy outdoor seating, a BBQ grill, and plenty of ambiance for hanging out together after a day exploring Savannah.

🏡 THREE CONDOS. ONE GREAT GROUP TRIP.

One of the best things about this setup is the flexibility! You're not squeezing everyone into one giant house. Instead, you'll have three separate, fully equipped 2BR/1BA condos under one roof.

Make breakfast in your own kitchen, put the kids to bed in one unit while the adults hang out in another, or give different families and couples their own space while keeping the whole group just steps away.

With six bedrooms, three bathrooms, three kitchens, three living spaces, three dining areas, three washers + dryers, and three private entrances, there's plenty of room for everyone to settle in comfortably. 🍋

🚗 PARKING

Your reservation includes one private off-street parking space behind the building. There is also plenty of free street parking around the property for additional vehicles.

🌳 A QUIET SPOT CLOSE TO THE FUN

The Lemon Drop is located in Savannah's beautiful Victorian District, just a few blocks from iconic Forsyth Park. It's one of our favorite parts of Savannah because you get the best of both worlds—a quieter, residential neighborhood to come home to while still being close to everything that makes Savannah such a fun place to visit.

Take a morning stroll beneath the live oaks, walk over to Forsyth Park, or grab something to eat at nearby neighborhood favorites like Pizza Party and Tacos + Tequila. 🍕🌮🍹 From here, you can continue exploring Savannah's historic squares, restaurants, bars, shopping, and sightseeing.

💛 HOSTED BY LOCALS

We're Megan & Justin, Savannah locals who love this city and love sharing it with our guests! We're always happy to recommend our favorite restaurants, bars, coffee shops, sightseeing spots, hidden gems, and things to do.

Looking for the perfect brunch? A great cocktail? Somewhere the locals actually go? Just ask! We love helping our guests experience Savannah beyond the usual tourist stops.

🍋 NEED EVEN MORE SPACE?

Believe it or not, there's one more Lemon Drop! If your group needs additional accommodations, consider also booking Lemon Loft, our fourth private condo in the building.

Book all four and you've got your own little Lemon Drop compound in Savannah! 🍋💛

Whether you're planning a family getaway, reunion, wedding weekend, girls' trip, or vacation with a big group of friends, The Lemon Drop makes it easy to stay together—with enough space to actually enjoy it!
