=== FULL DESCRIPTION ===
Victorian Villa is a charming 3-bedroom, 2-bath townhome located in the historic Victorian district of downtown Savannah. Accommodating up to 8 guests, this beautifully preserved property features elegant period details and modern amenities for a comfortable stay. Each room is thoughtfully decorated to reflect the timeless charm of the Victorian era. Perfect for families or groups, Victorian Villa offers an unforgettable blend of history and luxury in the heart of Savannah.

The space
Nestled in the heart of the Savannah Historic Landmark District, this Victorian villa offers a delightful blend of historic charm and modern amenities. Built in 1900 and meticulously renovated in 2024, the house boasts three elegantly appointed bedrooms, each designed for ultimate comfort with high-end bedding, cozy throws, and coordinating accent pillows. The open floor plan seamlessly connects the family room, dining room, and kitchen, creating a spacious and inviting environment perfect for socializing and entertaining. The kitchen, equipped with stainless steel appliances and a gas range, features a kitchen island that seats two, while the adjacent dining room accommodates eight, making it ideal for family dinners and gatherings.



The villa's outdoor spaces are equally charming. The front porch, with its comfortable seating, invites you to relax and enjoy a glass of wine as you watch the world go by. At the back, the rear deck features a propane grill and a dining table for eight, perfect for alfresco meals. Beyond the deck, a small fenced-in area provides a safe and convenient space for your dog to play. Additional conveniences include two designated parking spots in the rear and free street parking in the front. Located less than three blocks from Forsyth Park, this villa places you within easy reach of Savannah's most iconic attractions, making it an ideal home base for exploring the city's rich history and vibrant culture.

SVR-03004

Guest access
Guests have a private access and will receive a key code prior to arrival to enter the home. This keycode is changed after every guest for your safety.

Other things to note
The villa's prime location is a significant draw for guests. Being less than three blocks from Forsyth Park, visitors have easy access to one of Savannah's most iconic and picturesque spots. The surrounding Historic Landmark District is a treasure trove of cultural and historical attractions, charming boutiques, art galleries, and over 100 restaurants. After a day of exploring the city's vibrant scene, guests can unwind on the inviting front porch, enjoy alfresco dining on the rear deck, or let their pets play in the fenced-in area. With its ideal mix of historic charm, modern amenities, and convenient location, the Victorian villa offers a truly unique and memorable Savannah experience.
