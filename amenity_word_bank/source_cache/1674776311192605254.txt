=== FULL DESCRIPTION ===
Perfectly positioned just one block from Forsyth Park, Abercorn Manor is ideal for larger groups, family gatherings, or friend getaways, this combined offering allows everyone to spread out comfortably while still staying connected.

The space
Perfectly positioned just one block from Forsyth Park, Abercorn Manor House offers the rare opportunity to enjoy both the main residence and garden-level apartment together, creating a spacious, design-forward retreat in the heart of Savannah’s Historic District. Ideal for larger groups, family gatherings, or friend getaways, this combined offering allows everyone to spread out comfortably while still staying connected.

What do we love about this home? It begins with the thoughtfully designed interiors. The main residence, professionally curated by a local interior designer, features four light-filled bedrooms, a full kitchen, and expansive living and dining spaces, along with a secondary upstairs gathering room, perfect for relaxing, entertaining, or enjoying separate spaces when desired.

Just below, the garden-level apartment adds two additional bedrooms, a full kitchen, and a comfortable living area, creating a private and easily accessible retreat. This separation is ideal for larger groups or guests who prefer minimal stairs, offering flexibility without sacrificing comfort.

Together, the home offers multiple gathering areas, six well-appointed bedrooms, and beautifully curated furnishings throughout, blending comfort, style, and functionality for a seamless group stay.

Step outside and you’re immersed in the very best of Savannah, from morning walks through Forsyth Park to afternoons spent exploring nearby cafés, boutiques, and historic streets, all just moments from your door.

Abercorn Manor House is the perfect choice for guests seeking a beautifully designed, walkable home with generous space to gather, reconnect, and experience Savannah at its finest.
