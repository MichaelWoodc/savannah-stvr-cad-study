=== FULL DESCRIPTION ===
Step into a world of vibrant charm and old-world details! This 2-bed, 1-bath condo in Savannah's Victorian district is filled with unique touches and thoughtful details. The fully stocked kitchen is perfect for a quick breakfast or a full dinner in!  Sip some sweet tea on the large front porch and enjoy the huge live oaks on this quiet side street after a day of exploring! Only five blocks from Forsyth Park, this sweet first floor condo is the perfect homebase for your Savannah stay!

SVR 02796

The space
Welcome to our charming 2-bedroom, 1-bathroom condo nestled on the first floor of a meticulously restored turn-of-the-century Victorian home in the heart of Savannah's picturesque Victorian district. Each bedroom offers a haven of comfort, with a king bed in one and a queen bed in the other, both equipped with flat screen, smart TVs.

If you're looking to unplug and unwind, our cozy sitting room provides the perfect setting. Gather with friends, share a meal, and create lasting memories in this inviting space. Check out the original architectural features, from elegant pocket doors to the heartpine floors and fireplace hearths, all seamlessly blended with contemporary, thoughtful, and fun design elements. Need to connect? No worries! Fast WiFi throughout the condo makes it easy!

The fully stocked kitchen makes staying in easy -- whether you're whipping up a quick breakfast before a day of exploring the city or staying in for a homemade dinner for friends and family. And when you're ready for a breath of fresh air, step onto the spacious front porch. Here, amid the graceful boughs of ancient live oak trees, you'll find a tranquil spot to relax, chat, and take in the quiet charm of the street.

Come experience the perfect blend of old-world elegance and modern comfort in this adorable Savannah retreat!

Guest access
The entire first floor condo is all yours! The front porch is a shared space, though you will likely only see the folks that live in the second floor condo coming and going, as they have their own private outdoor space as well!

Other things to note
Reach out if you need anything!  We want you to have a 5 star stay!
